# Wording review: Lesson 35 (Spot It)

Every child-facing sentence was read against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1 to 25 and the Primary 1 paper patterns.

- Commands: Circle (one word or letter); Write the number; Draw. Same command for the same job (rules 2, 14). Choice words are printed on the answer line, so questions stay short ('Tile 1:', 'Different in:', 'Odd because of:').
- Fixed question forms: 'What is different?'; 'Circle the odd one out.'; 'There are [] differences.'; 'Shelf 2 has [] more apple than shelf 1.'; 'Tile [] and tile [] are the same.' Numbers are digits (rules 15, 16).
- Five feature words (shape, number, size, pattern, turn) are taught in the warm-up, Explore and the Mini-lesson and used the same way everywhere. 'Colour' is not a feature because printed books are black and white; 'pattern' means plain, stripes or dots.
- 'Shadow' is a taught word in the named puzzle only; the question says what to do ('Circle A, B, C or D').
- Wrong working belongs to 'a child in another class' (Talk). Cast name (Ali, Lily) appears only in home stories with correct working.
- No teaching sentence inside a question (rule 24); hints carry the method.
- Rule 25: the odd one out with a hidden shared feature, the missing piece, the 11th tile and Shadow Match are Think harder and Go further; Start here is two pictures of at most 4 tiles.
- Changed during review: Think harder question 3 (two tiles the same, turned) was dropped for space; labels 'Circle shape, number, size, pattern or turn' moved from the stem to the printed choices; the Investigation has no drawing box.
- Open: no Primary 1 paper uses this topic; wording marked unverified. Closest P1 forms: 'Circle the shape that comes next', 'grouped by shape, size or colour'.
