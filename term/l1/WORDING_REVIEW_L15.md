## Changes made during review (old -> new, reason)
1. Multiple-choice distractor: "4 + 4 + 4" was offered next to "3 + 3 + 3 + 3" for "the same as 4 groups of 3", but both total 12 (ambiguous). Now the options are 4 + 3, 3 + 3 + 3 + 3, 3 + 3 + 3 and 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 (exit E2 and online c5). Rule 6, one right answer.
2. T3: "Kumar says 3 groups of 4 is 7. Look at the picture." became a story first ("Kumar has 3 plates with 4 apples on each plate. He says he has 7 apples.") then (a) "Is Kumar correct? Circle Yes or No." and (b) the frame. Rules 11, 19. No wrong reasoning is stated.
3. S2, S3, S6, E3: frames end with the noun ("There are [] plates.", "Aisha has [] pencils in all."); a box for single numbers, [2] boxes for two digits; no mixing of lines and boxes in one question (rule 16). The sign is a circle in the number sentence (rule 17).
4. T1: the two arrays side by side were replaced by one array and "Turn the picture on its side." (rule 24: the turning is a command, not a taught sentence); (c) uses Yes or No with printed words.
5. T4: "in four different ways" shortened to "in different ways"; the table has missing numbers in boxes, the child counts in jumps (the method sits in the hint, rule 18).
6. G1 to G3: one question per part (a), (b), (c); numerals for every quantity; "in all" for totals, "How many more ... " for the gap; "Many answers are right." in G3 (rule 6).
7. Names: Kumar (error-spotter), Mei, Aisha, Jenny, Jamie, Wei, Ali, Ben, Siti (puzzle only). No Siti error-spotter.
8. Words: "shares" and the sharing picture are kept out of the lesson (Lesson 17); "groups of", "each", "equal", "in all" are the fixed words; "greater" and "more" only, never "bigger".
9. Tutor lines: no answers in Mi's lines; READ lines say "times" aloud for the sign and "groups of" first.
