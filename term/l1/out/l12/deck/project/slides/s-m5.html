THINK HARDER PREVIEW: Start here never needs this idea. Ali and Mei have 40 altogether and Ali has 8 more. Take the extra 8 away from the total: 32 is left, which is two equal strips. Each strip is 16. Mei has 16, and Ali has 16 and 8 more, which is 24. Check: 16 + 24 = 40 and 24 is 8 more than 16. Take one answer to what happens to the strips when the 8 is taken away.
