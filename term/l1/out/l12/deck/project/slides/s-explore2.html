A story with more than

Mei has 5 stickers. Ali has 3 more stickers than Mei.

Ali's strip: Mei's strip and 3 more cells.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 6 minutes. Children make Mei's strip of 5 cells, then Ali's strip: 5 cells and 3 more cells in a second colour. Count all of Ali's strip: 8. Then change the story: Mei has 9 and Ali has 4 more, so Ali has 13. A child who says 3 for Ali has counted only the extra part.
