Fewer than: take cells off

Ali has 13 stickers. Mei has 4 fewer stickers than Ali.

Mei's strip: Ali's strip with 4 cells taken off.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 4, about 4 minutes. Fewer than means a shorter strip. Children make Ali's strip of 13 cells and take 4 cells off for Mei: 9 left. Then they draw two strips for a new story on their own paper: Ravi has 6 marbles and Siti has 5 more, so Siti has 11. Nobody holds paper to the camera: answers are said on three. A child who writes 4 has given the gap, not Mei's number.
