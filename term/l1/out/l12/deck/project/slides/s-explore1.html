Explore: two strips

1Take a strip of 12 cells and a strip of 8 cells.

2Lay them side by side. Line up the left ends.

3Touch the extra cells on the long strip.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 4 minutes. Objects first, no writing. Children find or cut a 12-cell strip and an 8-cell strip (a grown-up cuts them at the dotted lines before the lesson), lay them side by side with the left ends together, and touch the extra cells. Answer: 4 extra cells. The extra part is the gap. Say: 12 is 4 more than 8.
