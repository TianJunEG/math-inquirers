Altogether: join the strips

ALTOGETHER

Push the strips end to end.

14 and 7 make 21.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

To find altogether, join the strips end to end: 14 and 7 make 21. Ask: when do we join the strips and when do we find the gap? Join for altogether, side by side for how many more. Take answers on three. A child who confuses the two usually reads the question word first: altogether means the whole line, how many more means the gap.
