3–9 MIN

A strip is a number

Make this strip with your kit strips: three strips of 10 and one piece of 6. How many cells in all?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The picture is a strip of three tens and a six; the total is not printed, so the children find it (36). Children make 36 from their kit strips (or draw boxes in a row), then count in tens: 10, 20, 30, 36. This lets a child who has never seen a strip read one before the lesson uses them to show a story. A child with no strips draws a long box for ten.
