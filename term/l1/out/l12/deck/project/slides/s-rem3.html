3–9 MIN

A strip is a number

Make 36 with your strips: three strips of 10 and one strip of 6.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The picture is a strip of 36 cells: three tens and a six. Children make 36 from their kit strips (or draw boxes in a row), then count in tens: 10, 20, 30, 36. This lets a child who has never seen a strip read one before the lesson uses them to show a story. A child with no strips draws a long box for ten.
