THE GAP

Siti has 12. Dev has 7.

How many more? 12 take away 7.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Strips are drawn to scale: 12 gets a longer strip than 7. The red bracket marks the extra part, the gap. To find the gap, take the short strip away from the long strip: 12 take away 7 is 5. Or count up from 7 to 12. Do not reveal the 5 before children answer. A child who adds 12 and 7 has joined the strips instead of finding the gap.
