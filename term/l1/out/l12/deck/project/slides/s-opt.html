Go further: ten stickers

Ali and Mei have 10 stickers altogether.

Ali has 2 more stickers than Mei. How many stickers does each child have?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional, only if you are on time. Answer: Ali 6 and Mei 4. Take the extra 2 away from the total: 8 is left, shared between two equal strips, 4 each. Mei has 4 and Ali has 4 and 2 more, which is 6. Take two answers and move on. A child who says 5 and 5 has shared the total but forgotten the gap.
