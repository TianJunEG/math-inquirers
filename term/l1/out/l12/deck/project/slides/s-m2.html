More than: add the extra

DEV HAS 14. SITI HAS 6 MORE.

Siti's strip is Dev's strip and 6 more.

14 and 6 more is 20.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Siti has 6 more beads than Dev, so Siti's strip is Dev's strip and 6 more cells. Ask: how many beads does Siti have? Add the extra part: 14 + 6 = 20. When the extra part is known, we add. When the gap is unknown, we take away.
