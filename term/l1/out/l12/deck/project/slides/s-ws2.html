Read the four questions aloud, one at a time, and show where the picture or the drawing box is. Question 4 asks the child to draw two strips in the box. Question 7 has no picture: it is a choose-the-number-sentence question: write the number of the right sentence in the brackets. Wait 10 seconds before helping.
