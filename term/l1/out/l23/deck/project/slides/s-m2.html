Half an hour later

+ half an hour

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example: 10 o'clock and half an hour later is half past 10 (10:30): the long hand goes half way round, 30 minutes. On the time line each jump is half an hour (30 minutes). A second half an hour from 10:30 lands on 11 o'clock (11:00): two half hours make 1 hour, and the hour changes from 10 to 11. The last tick is an empty box while you ask: what is half an hour after half past 10 (11:00)? Children answer aloud. Say that half an hour is the same as 30 minutes.
