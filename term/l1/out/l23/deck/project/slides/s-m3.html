Going back

The film ended at 6 o'clock. It lasted 1 hour. When did it start?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Counting back to find a start. The film ended at 6 o'clock and lasted 1 hour, so jump back 1 hour on the time line: 5 o'clock (5:00). Ask: when did it start? Say the answer after the children have tried; the box on the line is empty until then. The short hand goes back one number and the long hand stays. This is the move for Think harder: the end and how long are known, the start is the missing number. Answer: 5:00.
