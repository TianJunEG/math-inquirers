Your turn: going back

The class ended at 10 o'clock. It lasted half an hour. When did it start?

Write the start time on your paper.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Your turn, one try at going back. The class ended at 10 o'clock and lasted half an hour, so jump back half an hour (one tick on this line) to 9:30. Children write the start time on their own paper; wait 10 seconds before helping. Read the answer aloud afterwards: 9:30. Nobody holds paper up. A child who writes 9:00 went back 1 hour; a child who writes 10:30 added instead of going back: show it on the clock. Answer: 9:30.
