Jump on a line

Start at 34. Jump 10. Where do you land?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A tens jump on a number line (Term 1 Lesson 6, taught again). Ask: where do I land from 34 with a jump of 10 (44); from 52 with a jump of 10 (62). Then say: on a clock, one hour is one jump too; today we jump on a time line. A child who is unsure counts ten ticks along the line with a finger, or counts on in tens: 34, 44. Children answer aloud. No writing.
