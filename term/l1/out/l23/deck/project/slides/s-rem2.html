Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Count in 5s round the clock, pointing to the numbers 1 to 12 on the face: 5 minutes at 1, 10 at 2, 15 at 3, and so on to 60 at 12. Ask: how many fives to get all the way round (12); how many minutes is half way round (30, at the 6). This is Term 1 Lesson 4 (skip counting in 5s) taught again. A child who has not counted in 5s counts along the number row on the slide. Twelve steps of 5 minutes make 1 hour; six steps make half an hour.
