34–38 MIN

Clock arms

Stand up. Make a clock with your arms.

One arm points up for the 12. The other arm is the short hand.

Sip some water.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera with a little clear space. Call times and children make the hands with their arms: one arm is the long hand, one is the short hand. Call 12 o'clock (both arms up), 3 o'clock (long arm up, short arm out to the side), 6 o'clock (long arm up, short arm down), 9 o'clock (long arm up, short arm out the other side), then half past 6 (long arm down, short arm half way between 6 and 7). Then call one hour later from 3 o'clock. No equipment, no running. A child may stay seated and use fingers. Do not give prizes or compare children.
