TWO CHILDREN SAY

One child: half an hour after 7:30 is 8:00.

Another child: half an hour after 7:30 is 8:30.

Who is right? How could we check on the clock?

I think ... because ...

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

One minute of talk to the grown-up next to you, or to your screen. Children test it on the clock: set 7:30, move the long hand half an hour on (half way round, from 6 to 12) and see where the short hand lands. Then volunteers first; a pass is always fine; never call a child by name to answer. Model answer: the first child is right. Half an hour is 30 minutes. From 7:30 the long hand goes from the 6 to the 12 and the short hand moves on to the 8, so it is 8:00. 8:30 is a whole hour after 7:30. Repeat each child answer in your own words for the recording. The two children are in another class: no cast name is given the wrong answer.
