Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Three minutes. Stand up on camera. Call each move; children take small steps forward and back, or lean forward and back, in the space they have: add 3, take away 2, add 4, take away 5. Count aloud together. After the four moves, everyone is back at the chair: 3, 1, 5, 0. No equipment; keep it gentle for small spaces.
