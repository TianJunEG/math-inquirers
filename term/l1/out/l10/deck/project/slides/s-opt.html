Ali has 20 marbles. He wins 6 and loses 4.

Does it matter which comes first?

Win first
20 + 6 - 4
Lose first
20 - 4 + 6

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional, only if you are on time. Win first: 20 + 6 = 26, 26 - 4 = 22. Lose first: 20 - 4 = 16, 16 + 6 = 22. Same answer. Take two answers and move on.
