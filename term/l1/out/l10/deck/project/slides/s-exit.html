Exit ticket

Optional. On your own. No looking back. About 3 minutes

E1

A bus has 25 children. 10 children get off. Then 3 children get on. How many children are on the bus now?

The bus now has ______ children.

E2

Ben has 6 stickers. Mei has 5 more stickers than Ben. How many stickers does Mei have?

Mei has ______ stickers.

E3

Siti has 20 beads. Ali has 7 fewer beads than Siti. How many beads does Ali have?

Ali has ______ beads.
