Mei's stickers

STORY

Mei has 26 stickers. Ali gives her 10 more stickers. Then she gives 5 stickers to Ben. How many stickers does Mei have now?

Step 1
26 + 10 = 36
Step 2
36 - 5 = 31
Answer
Mei has 31 stickers.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Think aloud. First read: Mei, stickers, Ali gives, she gives to Ben. Second read: 26, 10 more, 5 to Ben. Step 1: gets more, so add, 26 + 10 = 36 (one more bundle of ten). Step 2: gives away, so take away, 36 - 5 = 31 (no renaming: 6 - 5 = 1). Write the answer in a sentence. Ask: where did 36 come from? Answer: from Step 1.
