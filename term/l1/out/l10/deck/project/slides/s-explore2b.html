Who has more?

STORY C

Ben has 9 balls. Mei has 4 more balls than Ben.

How many balls do they have altogether?

Find Mei first. Then put them together.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 3 minutes. Read story C twice: Ben has 9 balls. Mei has 4 more balls than Ben. How many balls do they have altogether? Children act it out with counters: a pile of 9 for Ben, then a pile for Mei that is 4 more (13), then put both piles together (22). Answer for the tutor: Mei 13, together 22. Say that Mei has more, so her pile is the greater one. This sets up question 5 in the workbook.
