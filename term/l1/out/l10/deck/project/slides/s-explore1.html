STORY A

Mei has 8 buttons. She gets 5 more buttons. Then she gives away 6 buttons.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 3 minutes, objects first and no writing. Read story A twice: Mei has 8 buttons. She gets 5 more buttons. Then she gives away 6 buttons. How many buttons does Mei have now? Children act it out with counters or the kit squares: 8 and 5 more is 13, then 6 away leaves 7. Ask for a thumbs up when each child has finished. Answer for the tutor: 7.
