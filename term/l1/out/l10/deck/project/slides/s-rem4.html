Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Read each short story twice and ask which number sentence fits, then the answer. Ben has 6 cards and gets 4 more: 6 + 4 = 10. Siti has 9 beads and loses 3: 9 - 3 = 6. Ali has 12 marbles and wins 5: 12 + 5 = 17. Children answer aloud or show a thumb up for the first sentence and down for the second. No writing yet.
