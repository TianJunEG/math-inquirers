Who is in the group?

STORY

Ravi is in a line. There are 5 children in front of Ravi and 2 behind him. How many children are in the line?

Count Ravi too!

5 + 2 + 1 = 8

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A reading trap. Show the line on a shared whiteboard, a blank slide or with counters: 5 children, then Ravi, then 2 children. 5 + 2 = 7 leaves Ravi out, so add 1 more: 8 children. Ask: who is in the group? Always check you counted everyone, including the person in the story. This is the taught idea for the Think harder page.
