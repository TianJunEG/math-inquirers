Round 2, about 3 minutes. Read story B twice, with a first read for who and what, then a second read for the numbers and the question: Ravi has 12 stickers. He gives 5 to Siti. Then he finds 3. How many stickers has Ravi now? Children act it out: 12 - 5 = 7, then 7 + 3 = 10. Answer for the tutor: 10. Praise the two reads, not the speed.
