Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A second step. Ali has 4 boxes with 5 pens in each box, and 6 loose pens. Draw 4 equal boxes of 5 and one more box of 6 in a different colour. Step 1: the pens in boxes, 4 groups of 5 is 20. Step 2: add the loose pens, 20 + 6 = 26. Ask: which step comes first? What does the different colour show? The table has question marks: let children say each total before you say it. Say: one operation in each step (Lesson 10). If the loose pens were given away, we would take away in step 2.
