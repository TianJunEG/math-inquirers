8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

Ali has 3 bags.

Each bag has 4 sweets.

How many sweets does Ali have in all?

You may draw to help.

PUZZLE 2: think

Mei has 5 stickers.

Ben has twice as many stickers as Mei.

How many stickers do they have together?

Then write or draw how you know.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 (3 bags with 4 sweets in each bag; 12 sweets) is an access question: any child can draw it or count it out; the empty bags are there to draw in. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: twice as many as 5 is 10, and 5 and 10 together are 15. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera. There is no private chat. Answers: 12; 15. Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
