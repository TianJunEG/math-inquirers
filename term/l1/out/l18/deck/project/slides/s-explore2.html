Which strip is the story?

Ben has 4 bags. Each bag has 3 sweets. Show 1, 2 or 3 fingers.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 4 minutes. Read the story: Ben has 4 bags with 3 sweets in each bag. Children show 1, 2 or 3 fingers for the strip that matches. Strip 1 is right: 4 boxes with 3 in each box. Strip 2 is 3 boxes of 4 (the numbers swapped). Strip 3 has 4 boxes but the last box is 2, so the boxes are not all the same. Do not name a child with a wrong answer. Then ask: what is the total (12)? Then give a second story with the same strips: Mei has 3 bags with 4 sweets in each bag (strip 2, 12).
