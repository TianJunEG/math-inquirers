Twice as many is two equal strips. It is not 2 more.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Beyond Primary 1 on purpose. Mei has 8 stickers. Ali has twice as many: two strips the same as Mei’s, 8 and 8, which is 16. Together they have 8 and 16, which is 24. Ask: how many strips for Mei (1)? For Ali (2)? How many for both together (3 strips of 8)? Say it plainly: twice as many is two equal strips, not 2 more. Three times as many would be three equal strips (Go further). A child who says 10 has added 2: compare Ali’s strip with Mei’s. The strips and the table come on the answer slide, after the children have answered. (answer shown after the children have answered)
