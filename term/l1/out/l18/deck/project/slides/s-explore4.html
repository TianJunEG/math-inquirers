Twice as many

1Make Mei a strip of 6: a row of 6 counters.

2Ali has twice as many. Make Ali a strip the same as Mei’s, and then another the same.

3How many counters has Ali? How many are there together?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 4, about 3 minutes. Children make Mei a row of 6 counters (or use a pre-cut paper strip of 6 cells: a grown-up cut these before the lesson, nobody cuts during the lesson). Mei has 1 strip. Ali has twice as many: two rows the same as Mei’s, 6 and 6, which is 12. Together they have 18. Ask: is twice as many the same as 2 more? Mei has 6, and 2 more would be 8; twice as many is 12. Key idea: twice as many is two equal strips. A child who says 8 has added 2: put Ali’s two strips next to Mei’s one. The strips come on the answer slide, after the children have answered.
