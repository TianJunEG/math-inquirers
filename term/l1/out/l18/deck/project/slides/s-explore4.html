Twice as many

1Make a strip of 6 cells for Mei.

2Ali has twice as many. Make Ali a strip the same as Mei, and then another the same.

3How many cells has Ali? How many are there together?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 4, about 3 minutes. Children cut or fold paper strips of 6 cells (a grown-up cut them at the dotted lines; or use counters in rows of 6). Mei has 1 strip. Ali has twice as many: two strips the same as Mei, 6 and 6, which is 12. Together they have 18. Ask: is twice as many the same as 2 more? Mei has 6, and 2 more would be 8; twice as many is 12. Key idea: twice as many is two equal strips. A child who says 8 has added 2: put Ali’s two strips next to Mei’s one.
