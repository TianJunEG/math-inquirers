Explore: cups and a strip

1Put 3 cups on the table.

2Put 5 counters in each cup.

3Draw 3 boxes in a row. Write 5 in each box.

4How many counters in all?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 4 minutes. Objects first. Children put 5 counters in each of 3 cups, then draw one strip of 3 boxes on their own paper: each box is one cup. They write 5 in each box and find the total: 15 (count in fives). Say the words once: each cup is a box; every box is the same size, so it is a strip of equal boxes. The picture shows the strip; the question mark is the total. A child with fewer counters uses 2 cups of 4. Do not ask for a number sentence yet.
