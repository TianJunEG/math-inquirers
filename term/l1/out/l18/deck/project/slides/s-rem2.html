Same-size groups

4 groups of 5. Every bag has the same number.

How many groups?How many in each?How many in all?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

This is the equal-groups idea from earlier in the term, taught again from scratch: 4 bags with 5 pencils in each bag is 4 groups of 5, and there are 20 pencils in all. Ask the three questions on the slide: 4 groups, 5 in each, 20 in all. Children answer aloud or with fingers. A child who is unsure counts the pencils in one bag, then counts in fives: 5, 10, 15, 20.
