Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The four steps of a times story. Read it (3 bags with 5 sweets in each bag). Draw equal boxes: one box for each bag, 3 boxes. Write the number in each box: 5. Find the total: count in fives, 5, 10, 15; 5 + 5 + 5 = 15, or 3 × 5 = 15. Ask: how many boxes, what is in each box, what is the total? Say the unit once: every box is the same size. A child who draws 5 boxes with 3 in each has swapped the numbers: re-read the story. The strip comes on the answer slide, after the children have answered.
