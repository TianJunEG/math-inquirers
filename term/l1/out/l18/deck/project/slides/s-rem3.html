This is Lesson 10 again: one operation in each step. Step 1: 6 and 4 more is 10. Step 2: 10 take away 3 is 7. Ask for step 1 first (10), then step 2 (7). Children answer aloud or with fingers. A child who is unsure uses counters: 6 and 4 more, then push 3 away. Say: we do one step at a time, and we write each step.
