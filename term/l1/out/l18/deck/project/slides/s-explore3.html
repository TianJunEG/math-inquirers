A total and some loose ones

1Put 3 cups on the table with 5 counters in each cup.

2Put 2 more counters on the table. They are loose: not in a cup.

3How many counters are there in all?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 3 minutes. Children keep the 3 cups of 5 and add 2 loose counters (loose means not in a cup). They count the cups first: 15, then the loose ones: 17. The strip shows 3 boxes of 5 and one smaller box of 2 in a different colour. Say: first the boxes, then the loose ones, one step at a time. Ask: what did we do first (3 groups of 5 is 15)? What next (15 and 2 more is 17)? A child who writes 5 + 3 + 2 has not drawn equal boxes: ask them to build it with cups.
