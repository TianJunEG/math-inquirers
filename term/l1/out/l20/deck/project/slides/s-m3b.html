A dozen

A dozen cookies is 12 cookies.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Count the cookies together: 12. A dozen is 12, whatever it is 12 of. A dozen eggs, a dozen pens. Ask: how many pens are in a dozen (12). This slide is optional to shorten: cut it first if the Mini-lesson runs over.
