Find one, then many

Find 1 pen first: share $20 into 4 equal parts.

Then take 3 parts.

How much do 3 pens cost?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 3.5 minutes. 4 pens cost $20. How much do 3 pens cost? Children share 20 tokens onto 4 pens (5 each) and then take 3 pens (15). The strip shows 4 equal parts for the 4 pens, and 3 parts are the 3 pens. Ask: how much does 1 pen cost (5); how much do 3 pens cost (15). Say: find one first, then find many. Answers: $5 and $15.
