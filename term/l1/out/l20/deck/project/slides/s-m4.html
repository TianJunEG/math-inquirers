How much does 1 pencil cost? How much do 4 pencils cost?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Each child works on their own paper with tokens if they like. 6 pencils cost $18. Step 1: 1 pencil costs $3. Step 2: 4 pencils cost 4 groups of $3, which is $12. Walk the cameras; do not ask children to hold papers up. Wait 10 seconds before helping. Answers: $3 and $12.
