2–8 MIN

A strip of equal parts

4 equal parts. The whole strip is 20.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A strip is cut into 4 equal parts, and the whole strip is 20. Ask: how big is each part (5); 3 parts of 5 (15); 6 shared into 2 equal parts (3). This is Lesson 18 taught again: a strip shows equal groups. A child who is unsure counts 5, 10, 15, 20 on fingers and counts the fingers.
