AISHA SAYS

3 books cost $12.

So 6 books cost $24.

Is Aisha right? How could we check?

I think ... because ...

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

One minute of talk to the grown-up next to you, or to your screen. Children test Aisha with tokens: 12 tokens on 3 books is 4 each, and 6 books at 4 each is 24; or 6 books are 2 lots of 3 books, so 2 lots of $12. Then volunteers first; a pass is always fine; never call a child by name to answer. Model answer: yes. 1 book costs $4, so 6 books cost 6 groups of $4, which is $24. Repeat each child answer in your own words for the recording.
