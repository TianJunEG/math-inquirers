Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Three words for word problems. Each means for one. A dozen is 12. Such means of the same kind, so 6 such cups are 6 cups the same, with the same price. Ask: what is a dozen (12); a dozen eggs is how many eggs (12); if 3 such cups cost $6, does each cup cost the same (yes). A dozen and such are used in Think harder.
