Go further: do we always find one first?

2 pens cost $6. How much do 4 pens cost?

Do we need to find 1 pen first?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional, only if you are on time. 2 pens cost $6. 4 pens are 2 lots of 2 pens, so 4 pens cost 2 lots of $6, which is $12. We do not need to find 1 pen first when the new number is a whole number of lots. Finding one first always works; doubling is a quick way here. Take two answers and move on.
