Each and in all

Each bag has the same number.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Three bags with 4 counters in each bag. We say each bag has 4, and in all means every bag together. Ask: how many counters in all (12); 5 bags with 2 in each bag (10); 4 groups of 5 (20). This is Term 2 Lessons 5 and 8 taught again. A child who is unsure adds one bag at a time and says the total each time.
