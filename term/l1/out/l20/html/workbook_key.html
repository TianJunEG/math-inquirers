Answer key: Lesson 10, Find One First

For the tutor and grown-ups. Keep this page away from the child until they have finished and checked their own work. Ask how do you know? before saying right or wrong.

Start here (pages 1 and 2)
S1. 4 pens cost $20. How much does 1 pen cost?
Answer: $5
$20 shared equally among 4 pens is $5 each. Check: 4 groups of $5 make $20.
Watch for: Writing 24 (adding 20 and 4).; Writing 80 (multiplying).; Writing 20 (the total).
S2. 1 pen costs $3. How much do 4 pens cost? Fill in the blanks.
Answer: 12; $12
4 groups of $3 is 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12, so 4 pens cost $12.
Watch for: Writing 7 (adding 3 and 4).; Writing 3 (the cost of 1 pen).
S3. 6 buns cost $12. How much does 1 bun cost? Look at the strip.
Answer: $2
$12 shared equally among 6 buns is $2 each. Check: 6 groups of $2 make $12.
Watch for: Writing 6 (the number of buns).; Writing 72 (multiplying).
S4. 3 books cost $15. Fill in the blanks. Look at the strip.
Answer: $5; $10
$15 shared equally among 3 books is $5 each. 2 books cost 2 groups of $5, which is $10. Check: 3 groups of $5 make $15.
Watch for: Writing 15 for 2 books.; Writing 7 (adding 5 and 2).; Stopping after the first part.
S5. 4 pens cost $12. Lily says 1 pen costs $4. Look at the picture.
Answer: No; $3
4 groups of $4 make $16, not $12. So Lily is not correct. $12 shared equally among 4 pens is $3 each. Check: 4 groups of $3 make $12.
Watch for: Saying Yes because 4 is the number of pens.; Writing 12 for 1 pen.
S6. Each book costs $3. Kumar buys 5 books. How much do the 5 books cost?
Answer: $15
5 groups of $3: 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 15, so the 5 books cost $15.
Watch for: Writing 8 (adding 3 and 5).; Writing 3.
Think harder (page 3)
T1. 3 books cost $12. Fill in the blanks. Look at the strip.
Answer: $4; $28
$12 shared equally among 3 books is $4 each. 7 books cost 7 groups of $4, which is $28. Check: 3 groups of $4 make $12.
Watch for: Writing 84 (12 × 7).; Writing 19 (12 + 7).; Stopping after the first part.
T2. A dozen buns cost $24. Fill in the blanks.
Answer: $2; $6
A dozen is 12, so 12 buns cost $24. $24 shared equally among 12 buns is $2 each. 3 buns cost 3 groups of $2, which is $6.
Watch for: Using 24 shared among 2 buns.; Writing 24 for 3 buns.
T3. Wei pays $18 for 6 such cups. Fill in the blanks.
Answer: $3; $12
$18 shared equally among 6 cups is $3 each. 4 such cups cost 4 groups of $3, which is $12.
Watch for: Writing 18 for 4 cups.; Writing 7 (3 + 4).
T4. 5 pens cost $15. Ben has $12. Fill in the blanks.
Answer: $3; 4 pens
$15 shared equally among 5 pens is $3 each. $12 holds 4 groups of $3, so Ben can buy 4 pens.
Watch for: Writing 5 pens (the number in the story).; Writing 36 (12 × 3).
Go further (page 4)
G1. 2 hats cost $10. 3 scarves cost $18. Jenny buys 1 hat and 2 scarves. Fill in the blanks.
Answer: $5; $6; $17
$10 shared equally among 2 hats is $5 each. $18 shared equally among 3 scarves is $6 each. 2 scarves cost $12. 1 hat and 2 scarves cost $5 + $12 = $17.
Watch for: Adding 10 and 18.; Counting 1 scarf instead of 2.
G2. 6 pens cost $18. Mei has $20. She buys 4 pens. Fill in the blanks.
Answer: $3; $12; $8
$18 shared equally among 6 pens is $3 each. 4 pens cost $12. $20 - $12 = $8 is left.
Watch for: Taking 18 away from 20.; Forgetting to take the cost away from $20.
G3. Shop A sells 4 pens for $12. Shop B sells 5 pens for $20. Which shop sells 1 pen for less?
Answer: $3; $4; Shop A
Shop A: $12 shared among 4 pens is $3. Shop B: $20 shared among 5 pens is $4. $3 is less than $4, so Shop A sells 1 pen for less.
Watch for: Choosing Shop B because 20 is more.; Comparing $12 and $20 without finding 1 pen.
Investigation (page 5): Which pack costs less?
No. In both packs 1 pen costs $3 ($6 shared between 2 pens, and $15 shared among 5 pens), so 1 pen does not cost less in the bigger pack. Finding the price of one lets us compare packs of different sizes.
Look for: A child who finds $3 for both packs, or who says 1 pen costs the same in both. Credit any clear reason. A child who says the bigger pack costs more is right about the total but has not compared one pen.
Exit ticket (page 5)
E1. 3 pencils cost $9. How much does 1 pencil cost?
Answer: $3
$9 shared equally among 3 pencils is $3 each.
Watch for: Writing 12 (adding 9 and 3).; Writing 27 (multiplying).
E2. Each sticker costs $2. How much do 5 stickers cost? Write its number in the bracket.
Answer: (2) $10
Each sticker costs $2, so 5 stickers cost 5 groups of $2, which is $10.
Watch for: Choosing (1) $7 by adding 2 and 5.; Choosing (3) $3 by taking 2 from 5.
E3. 4 buns cost $12. Fill in the blanks.
Answer: $3; $9
$12 shared equally among 4 buns is $3 each. 3 buns cost 3 groups of $3, which is $9.
Watch for: Writing 12 for 3 buns.; Writing 7 (adding 3 and 4).
