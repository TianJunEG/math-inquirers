The tally

5 balls, 3 cars and 4 bikes. 5 + 3 + 4 = 12 toys in all.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The answer for the example on the slide before. Balls 5 (one group of five), cars 3, bikes 4. Add them: 5, 3 and 4 make 12, the same as the toys on the tray, so none was missed. Read it aloud so that children can tick their own work. Say: the fifth stroke crosses the other four, so a group of five looks like a gate.
