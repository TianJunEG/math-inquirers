Explore: draw your graph

1Find your graph mat.

2Draw one small picture for each thing.

3Use one column for each kind.

4Write the kind under the column.

Make the pictures the same size. No gaps.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 5 minutes. The picture shows how Mi drew its own finds (a different set from yours): one column for each kind, one picture in each square, the same size, no gaps, the kind written at the bottom. Children draw their own graph on the graph mat from page 6, in pencil, one small picture in each square. If a child prefers, say: turn the mat on its side and the columns become rows. Same graph. Then ask: which kind has the most things; which has the fewest; how many more does the biggest pile have than the smallest. Children answer aloud from their own mat. Nobody holds the mat up to the camera. A child with no mat rules 5 columns and 8 rows on paper. Mi's example is kept off the screen for the questions: nobody copies its numbers.
