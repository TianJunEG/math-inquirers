8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

How many cups are on the tray?

Write the number.

PUZZLE 2: think

Draw a picture that shows how many cups, buttons and socks there are.

Many answers are right.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 is an access question: count the cups on the mixed tray (4). Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: draw a picture that shows how many of each kind there are. There are 4 cups, 3 buttons and 2 socks. Any clear picture is good: a row of small pictures for each kind, or tally marks, or three groups. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera. There is no private chat. Answers: 4 cups; any clear picture. Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
