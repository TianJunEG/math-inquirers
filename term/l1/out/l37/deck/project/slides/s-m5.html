The missing row

There are 14 pieces of fruit in all. How many pears? Draw them.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. The bowl has 14 pieces of fruit in all. The plums row has 6 pictures and the figs row has 5. The pears row is empty. Children find how many pears and draw that many pictures on their own paper. Ask: how many plums and figs together (11); how many are left for the pears (3). The answer is on the next slide. Say: we work backwards from the total. A child who draws 7 pictures has used all the empty boxes: the boxes are more than we need.
