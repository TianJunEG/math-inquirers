Explore: sort your things

1Take 12 small things from your pile of things.

2Put the same kinds together. Make one pile for each kind.

3Count each pile.

How many kinds are there? Which pile is the biggest?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 5 minutes. Each child has about 12 small things from home in 3 or 4 kinds (spoons, socks, shoes, buttons; not food). No more than 8 of one kind: extras go back in the bag. Read the three steps one at a time. Children sort, then count each pile. Ask: how many kinds do you have; which pile is the biggest and how many things are in it. Do not give any count: every child has different things. Nobody holds things up to the camera: children say the numbers aloud or show fingers. A child with nothing to sort draws 12 things on scrap paper and sorts the drawings, or uses the tray pictures from the earlier slides.
