2–8 MIN

Warm-up: read a graph

Each picture is 1 book. Who read the most books? How many more books did Mei read than Ben?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Reading a picture graph (taught again with a picture). Mei read 6 books, Ben 4 and Siti 5. Ask: who read the most (Mei); how many more books did Mei read than Ben (2). A child who is unsure matches Mei's pictures with Ben's one by one and counts the pictures that are left. Your turn, no new picture: how many books did Ben and Siti read in all (9). Children answer aloud. No writing.
