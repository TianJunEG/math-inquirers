Sort and move break

Stand up. Sock: reach up high.

Shoe: touch your toes. Button: clap.

Sip some water.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera with a little clear space. Call three kinds of things in a mixed order, for example: sock, button, shoe, sock, sock, button. Children do the movement for each kind: sock is reach up, shoe is touch your toes, button is clap. Then ask: how many times did you clap? Call a short list and let children keep a tally with fingers or on their own paper. No equipment, no running. A child may stay seated and move one arm. Do not give prizes or compare children.
