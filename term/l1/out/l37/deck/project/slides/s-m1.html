Sort and tally

Sort the toys into kinds. Make 1 tally stroke for each toy. How many balls, cars and bikes?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. The tray shows 12 toys mixed up: 5 balls, 3 cars and 4 bikes. Children sort in their heads, or count one kind at a time on their own paper. Say: a tally is one stroke for each thing; the fifth stroke goes across the first four, so groups of 5 are easy to see. Children make tally marks on their own paper for balls, cars and bikes. Ask: how many of each (5, 3, 4). The answer is on the next slide. A child who counts a toy twice crosses each toy out as they count it (on paper copies).
