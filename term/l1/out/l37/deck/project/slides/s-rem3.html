Sort the things

Put the same things together. Count each pile. How many socks? How many shoes?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Sorting into piles by kind (taught again with a picture). The tray has 8 things: 5 socks and 3 shoes, mixed up. Ask: how many socks (5); how many shoes (3). A child who is unsure touches each sock once and says a number. Your turn: how many things on the tray in all (8). Children answer aloud. No writing. Say: a kind is a group of things that are alike. We make one pile for each kind.
