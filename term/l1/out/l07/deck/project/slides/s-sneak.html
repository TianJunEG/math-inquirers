8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

There are 15 stars. Cross out 4 of them.

How many stars are left?

PUZZLE 2: think

Ali has 58 cards.

Ben has 62 cards.

How many more cards does Ben have?

Write the answer. Write how you found it.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 (15 stars, cross out 4) is an access question: any child can try it by crossing out and counting. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea, the gap between two numbers. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Everyone holds the paper to the camera once, at the same moment, after puzzle 2; there is no private chat and no board in front of the group. Answers: 11; 4 more. Do not reveal Puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores.
