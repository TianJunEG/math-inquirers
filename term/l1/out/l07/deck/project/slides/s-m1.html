Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Think aloud: 57 is 5 tens and 7 ones. Take away 2 tens first: 37. Then take away 4 ones: 33. The picture on the right is what is left. Ask: which digit changes when we take away tens? Answer: the tens digit. Keep it to about a minute; children have just done this with the kit.
