Round 1, about 6 minutes. Objects first, no writing yet except the final number. Children build 57, take away 2 bars (37), then 4 squares (33). Then they build 76 and take away 2 bars and 5 squares (51). Ask how they took away. Key idea: tens first, then ones. A child with few squares can swap ten squares for a bar; the kit page has enough.
