Fingers to ten

I show 7. How many more fingers make 10?

7492

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say each number; children show on their fingers how many more make ten. Answers: 7 needs 3, 4 needs 6, 9 needs 1, 2 needs 8. Hands up on camera is fine. No writing, no marks. This is the number-bond idea you will use to hop to the next ten.
