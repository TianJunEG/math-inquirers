Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Think aloud: from 38 hop 2 to reach 40; jump 2 tens to reach 60 (that is 20); hop 2 to reach 62. Add the hops and the jump: 2 + 20 + 2 = 24. Then ask the new question: how far is it from 29 to 54? Answer: 25 (hop 1 to 30, jump 20 to 50, hop 4 to 54). This is the same count as the explore with the bars and squares.
