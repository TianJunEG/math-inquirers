Subtraction and addition are friends.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Show that the answer plus the number taken away gives back the start. 33 + 24 = 57, so 57 - 24 = 33 is right, and also 57 - 33 = 24. Ask: if 33 + 24 makes 57, what is 57 take away 33? Answer: 24. This is the relation between addition and subtraction, with no new vocabulary.
