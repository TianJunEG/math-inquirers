How does it grow?

+2+2+2+2

The same number more each time.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Look at how the sticks grow: 3 to 5 is 2 more, 5 to 7 is 2 more, 7 to 9 is 2 more, 9 to 11 is 2 more. The sticks grow by 2 each time. Ask: how many more sticks each time (2); why only 2 and not 3 (the new triangle shares one stick with the one before). Children trace the plus 2 arrows with a finger. The number of pictures goes up by 1, and the number of sticks goes up by 2. Say once: 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 are odd numbers, every time (odd and even are from Term 2 Lesson 14); this is used again at Think harder.
