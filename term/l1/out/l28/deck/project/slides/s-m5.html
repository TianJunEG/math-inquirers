A rule in words

THE RULE

Double the number of triangles.

Then add 1.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A rule in words, with no letters. Look at the table: double 1 is 2, and 2 and 1 more is 3; double 2 is 4, and 1 more is 5; double 3 is 6, and 1 more is 7; double 4 is 8, and 1 more is 9. So the sticks are double the triangles, and 1 more. Ask: how many sticks for 6 triangles using the rule (double 6 is 12, and 1 is 13); does it match the table (yes). Say: now we can find any number without drawing. Do not use the word formula; say the rule in words.
