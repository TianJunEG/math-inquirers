Explore: guess, then build

1You have 1, 2 and 3 triangles in a row.

2Guess: how many strips for 4 triangles?

3Build 4 triangles. Count. Was your guess right?

A guess is a great start.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 3 minutes, main room. Children guess first (take volunteers: say your guess and why), then build the fourth triangle and count: 9 strips. Ask: how many more strips did the fourth triangle need (2); how many more did the third need (2). Do not say the rule before the children do. Take a different guess without comment: 12 (3 for each triangle) and 9 are both worth hearing. Strips used: 9 of the 14 in the kit. A child who has run out of strips draws the last triangle.
