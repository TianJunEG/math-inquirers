How many strips would 5 triangles need?

Use what you have found. Tell your grown-up how you know.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 3 minutes. Children do not build: they use what they found (3, 5, 7, 9, always 2 more) to guess for 5 triangles: 11. Ask: how many strips for 5 triangles; how did you work it out. Some will say 15 (5 times 3): do not say it is wrong; ask them to look back at the number for 4 triangles. Confirm 11 on the next slide, not here. The answer is not on the slide.
