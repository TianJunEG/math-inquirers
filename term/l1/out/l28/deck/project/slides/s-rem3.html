2–8 MIN

Read a table

Mei sticks 2 stickers in her book each day.

How many stickers on day 3?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A table has a row of days and a row of stickers. Day 1 has 2 stickers and day 2 has 4. Ask: how many stickers on day 3 (6). Ask: how do you know (2 more each day). Say: a table is a neat way to write numbers in rows. The slide shows an empty box for the answer. A child who is unsure counts on 2 more from 4 on fingers. Joining cold: the table teaches itself.
