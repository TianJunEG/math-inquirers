Hop in twos

Stand up. I call a number of pictures.

Hop twice for each picture and count in 2s.

Sip some water.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera with a little clear space. Call a number of pictures (3, 5, 2, 4) and children hop twice for each picture, counting aloud in 2s: for 4 pictures that is 8 hops. Then call the number of triangles (2, 3) and hop the sticks: 3 hops, then 2 more. No equipment, no running; a child may stay seated and tap the table twice for each picture. Do not give prizes or compare children.
