8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

Each box has 4 pencils. Fill in the table.

PUZZLE 2: think

Picture 1 has 2 dots. Picture 2 has 5 dots. Picture 3 has 8 dots. How many dots in picture 5? I know because ___.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 (4, 8, then 12 pencils) is an access question: any child can add 4 again. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: each picture has 3 more dots, so picture 4 has 11 and picture 5 has 14; a child who writes 11 has found the next one but not the fifth. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera. There is no private chat. Answers: 12; 14. Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
