Explore: build triangles

1Make 1 triangle with 3 strips.

2Count the strips.

3Make a second triangle that joins the first and shares one strip. Count all your strips.

4Make a third. Count all your strips.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 4 minutes. Children build with paper strips on the table: one triangle uses 3 strips. Say aloud that the second triangle shares one strip with the first. A second triangle joined to the first shares one strip, so only 2 more strips are needed: 5 strips in all. A third triangle needs 2 more: 7 strips. Children say each total aloud. The picture on the slide shows two joined triangles only to show what joined means; it never shows a count. Do not write the numbers on the slide: children write them on their own paper as a list. A child who counts 3 more each time is asked to look at the shared strip. A child without strips draws the sticks as lines.
