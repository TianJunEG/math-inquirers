Doubles

Double means the same number twice.

Double 6 is 6 + 6.

Double 7 is 7 + 7.

What are double 6 and double 7?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Term 2 Lesson 13 taught again. Double 6 is 6 and 6, which is 12; double 7 is 7 and 7, which is 14. A child who does not know holds up 6 fingers on each hand for 6 and 6, and counts. Ask: how do you know (6 and 6 is 12). The answers are not on the slide. This is the pairs idea that the rule double and add 1 uses later. No writing.
