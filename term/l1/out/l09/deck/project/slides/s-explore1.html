14–28 MIN

Explore: guess, then measure

1Find 3 things: a pencil, a spoon, a rubber or a toy.

2Guess how many cm long each one is.

3Put one end at the 0 mark. Read the mark at the other end.

4Say: my ___ is about ___ cm.

WHAT IF?

The end is between two marks. Which mark is closer?

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 6 minutes. Objects first, little writing. Children guess, then measure three things from home. Ask a few children to hold the thing and the ruler to the camera: look at where the thing starts on the ruler (the 0 mark, not the edge). The what-if card is open to everyone from the start and no child is named for using it: it previews the nearest-centimetre idea in the mini-lesson. A child with a 15 cm ruler measures shorter things.
