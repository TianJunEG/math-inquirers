Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 5 minutes. Call each length and let children draw it. Children look at their own lines and show a thumbs up; nobody holds paper to the camera. Then ask about Ravi, who started at the edge of the ruler and not at the 0 mark: his line is a little longer than 8 cm. Answers: any line drawn with the 0 mark at the start and the end mark at 3, 8 and 12.
