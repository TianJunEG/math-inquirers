Say the numbers with me

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Every child puts a finger on the 0 mark of their own ruler and counts along with you: 0 to 10, then on to 12. The strip on screen shows the numbers only, the real ruler is the one the child holds. Quick questions: the mark after 4 is 5, the mark before 10 is 9, 12 comes after 10. Rulers with a gap before the 0 are normal: we start at the 0 mark, not at the edge.
