NEAREST CENTIMETRE

Choose the closer mark. This one is about 9 cm.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use a real pencil whose tip falls between two marks. When the tip is clearly closer to one mark, choose that mark: that is the nearest centimetre. Ask the second example: between 12 and 13, closer to 12: answer 12 cm. Today every example is clearly closer to one mark; do not set one exactly halfway.
