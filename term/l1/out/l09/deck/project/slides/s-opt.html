Go further: how many cuts?

A strip of paper is 10 cm long. You may cut it at the 1 mark, the 2 mark and so on.

How many different places can you cut? What if the strip is 20 cm long?

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional, only if you are on time. Answer: 9 places for the 10 cm strip (the marks 1 to 9), and 19 places for the 20 cm strip. We cannot cut at the 0 mark or the 10 mark, because that is the end of the strip. Take two answers and move on.
