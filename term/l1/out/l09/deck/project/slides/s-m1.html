The parts of a ruler

REMEMBER

0 is where we start.

The gap from one mark to the next is 1 cm.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Hold your real ruler to the camera next to this slide. The numbers sit on the marks; the centimetre is the gap between two marks. Ask: how many centimetres from the 0 mark to the 6 mark? Answer: 6. Say the word mark every time, and say gap or jump for the centimetre.
