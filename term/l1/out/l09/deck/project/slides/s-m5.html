A line that starts at 3

The line starts at 3 and ends at 8. Count the jumps. 5 jumps. The line is 5 cm long.

8 take away 3 is 5

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A line from the 3 mark to the 8 mark: the numbers show six marks, but only five jumps between them. Count the jumps aloud: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. The length is the end mark take away the start mark: 8 take away 3 is 5. It is not 8, because the first 3 cm of the ruler are not part of the line. Ask: why is it 5 cm and not 8 cm?
