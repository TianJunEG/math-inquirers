Measure: three steps

1Put one end of the thing at the 0 mark.

2Keep the ruler straight along the thing.

3Read the mark at the other end. That is the length in cm.

Look from straight above

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Point to the pencil on the ruler picture: one end exactly on the 0 mark, the tip on the 7 mark. Then say the three steps as you show them with a real pencil if you wish. Say each step as you do it. Read the mark at the tip aloud, with the unit: 14 cm, for example. Looking from straight above matters: from the side the marks seem to shift. Do not ask a child to demonstrate yet.
