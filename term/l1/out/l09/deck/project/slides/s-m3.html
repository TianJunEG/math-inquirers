Draw a line 7 cm long

1Put a dot where the line starts.

2Put the 0 mark on the dot.

3Make a small mark at the 7.

4Join them with a straight line.

5Check: 0 at the start, 7 at the end.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Draw the line on paper under your camera while you say the five steps. Hold the ruler still with one hand and draw along the edge with the other. At the end, check aloud: the 0 mark was on the dot and the 7 mark is at the other end. A line that is a little long is the most common slip: it usually means the line started at the edge of the ruler.
