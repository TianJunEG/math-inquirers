Workbook page 1: Start here

Questions 1 to 3. About 9 minutes. Use your ruler

S1

In the box, draw a line 7 cm long. Start at the 0 mark.

Your line ends at the mark.

S2

In the box, draw a line 9 cm long. Ali's line is 4 cm long.

Your line is ______ cm longer than Ali's line.

S3

Measure something short from your table, such as a crayon or a rubber. Write your guess first. Then measure it in whole cm.

(a) My guess: ______ cm

(b) I measured: ______ cm

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Read questions 1 to 3 aloud. Children work on their own; the tutor looks around the gallery and asks how do you know. Wait 10 seconds before helping. For question 3 a child may measure any short thing that fits on the ruler: the answer depends on the thing, so a grown-up checks it. After question 3 any child may choose to carry on or try Think harder.
