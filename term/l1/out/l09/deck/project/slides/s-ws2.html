Questions 4 to 6. About 8 minutes. Use your ruler

S4

Look at the ruler. Ali's pencil and Mei's pencil both start at the 0 mark.

(a) Ali's pencil is ______ cm long.

(b) Mei's pencil is ______ cm long.

(c) Ali's pencil is ______ cm longer than Mei's.

S5

Mei's line is 5 cm long. In the box, draw a line 3 cm longer than Mei's line.

Your line is ______ cm long.

S6

Look at Mei's line on the ruler. Mei says it is 8 cm long.

(a) Is Mei correct? Circle Yes or No.

(b) Her line is ______ cm long.
