8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

How many pictures are in Ravi's row?

Write the number.

PUZZLE 2: think

Each picture stands for 2 stickers.

How many stickers does Ravi have?

I know because ___.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 is an access question: count the pictures in Ravi's row (3). Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: each picture stands for 2 stickers, so Ravi has 6 stickers; count in 2s. A child who writes 3 has counted pictures, not stickers: that is the idea of the lesson, so do not correct it now. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera. There is no private chat. Answers: 3 pictures; 6 stickers. Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
