Explore: make pairs

1Draw 8 dots in a row on your paper.

2Draw a loop around every 2 dots.

3Each loop is 1 picture. How many pictures?

Now try 12 dots. How many pictures?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 5 minutes. Read the three steps one at a time. Children draw 8 small dots in a row on their own paper and draw a loop around each pair. Each loop is 1 picture that stands for 2 dots. Ask: how many pictures. Then children try 12 dots and ask again. Answers are on the next slide: 8 dots make 4 pictures; 12 dots make 6 pictures. Nobody holds paper to the camera: children say the number aloud or show fingers. A child who loops 3 dots at a time has made a different picture: ask, how many dots does each loop hold?
