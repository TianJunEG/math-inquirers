Explore: two graphs

Both rows have 4 pictures. Which row shows more things? How do you know?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 5 minutes. Two graphs have rows of the same length: 4 pictures. Graph A has a key of 1 (each picture stands for 1 pen) and Graph B has a key of 2 (each picture stands for 2 rulers). Ask: which row shows more things; how do you know. Take guesses first, then ask children to look at the keys. The answer is on the next slide. Do not give the rule before the children say it: the mini-lesson makes it exact. A child who says the same because the rows are the same length has not looked at the key: ask, what does one picture stand for in each graph?
