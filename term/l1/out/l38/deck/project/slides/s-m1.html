Read the key first

Read the key first. How many apples did Ali pick? How many more apples did Ali pick than Mei?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. The key says each picture stands for 2 apples. Mei has 2 pictures, Ali 6 and Siti 4. Ask: how many apples did Ali pick (12: count in 2s along the row); how many more apples did Ali pick than Mei (8). Say: match the rows first: Ali has 4 more pictures than Mei, and each picture is 2 apples, so 4 more pictures are 8 more apples. The answer is on the next slide. A child who says 6 has counted pictures, not apples: ask, what does one picture stand for?
