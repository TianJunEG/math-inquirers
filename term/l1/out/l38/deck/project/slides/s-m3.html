The missing bar

Each picture stands for 1. There are 16 things in all. There are 3 fewer plates than cups. Draw the plates and the bowls.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. Each picture stands for 1 thing. The cups row has 7 pictures. There are 3 fewer plates than cups. There are 16 things in all. Children work out how many plates and how many bowls and draw them on their own paper. Ask: how many plates (4); how many cups and plates together (11); how many bowls (5). The answer is on the next slide. Say: first the clue, then the total: we work backwards from the total. A child who draws 9 bowls has used all the empty boxes. Check by adding: 7 + 4 + 5 = 16.
