4 pictures and 6 pictures

8 dots make 4 pictures. 12 dots make 6 pictures. Each picture stands for 2 dots.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The answer for round 1. 8 dots in pairs make 4 loops, so 4 pictures. 12 dots make 6 loops, so 6 pictures. Say: when each picture stands for 2, the row is half as long as the number of things. Children check their own paper with a tick. Do not name any child.
