Pictures that stand for 2

Each picture stands for 2. Count in 2s along the row. How many shells are there?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A row where each picture stands for 2 (taught with a picture). The key says each picture stands for 2 shells. The row has 4 pictures. Count in 2s along the row: 2, 4, 6, 8. Ask: how many shells (8). A child who is unsure touches each picture and says the next number in 2s. Your turn: 3 pictures, each standing for 2: how many shells (6). Children answer aloud. No writing. Say: always look at the key first.
