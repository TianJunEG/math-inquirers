Draw with a key

Ben picked 8 apples. Each picture stands for 2. Draw Ben's row.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. Ben picked 8 apples. Each picture stands for 2 apples. Children work out how many pictures and draw them on their own paper. Ask: how many pictures does Ben's row need (4). Say: count in 2s until you reach 8: 2, 4, 6, 8; that is 4 pictures. The answer is on the next slide. A child who draws 8 pictures has forgotten the key: ask, what does one picture stand for? A child who draws 6 pictures has used all the empty boxes: the boxes are more than we need.
