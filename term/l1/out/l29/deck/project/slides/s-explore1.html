Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 5 minutes. Each child has the 8 mistake cards and the RIGHT and WRONG mat. The slide shows the same 8 sums, without saying which are right. Children check each card a different way (adding back for a take away, taking away for an add, or counting on) and place it on RIGHT or WRONG. Four are right: 70 take away 30, 18 + 4, 62 take away 8 and 38 + 24. Four are wrong, each in exactly one way: 45 + 28 = 63 (the answer is 73), 84 take away 6 = 82 (78), 53 take away 20 = 73 (33) and 17 + 5 = 21 (22). The sorted answer is on the next slide. Ask: how did you check the first one? A child with no cards writes the 8 sums on paper and makes two lists. Nobody holds a card up to the camera: children say the sum and show a thumb for right or a flat hand for wrong.
