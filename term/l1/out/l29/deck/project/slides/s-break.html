Tick-tock break

Stand up. Swing your arms like the hands of a clock.

I say a sum. Say the right answer in a big voice.

Sip some water.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera with a little clear space. Swing both arms slowly like the hands of a clock: one hour is a big swing, half an hour is a half swing. Then call quick facts for children to answer aloud together: 10 plus 5, 20 plus 30, 50 take away 20, 7 and 3, 40 take away 10. Ask: is 6 plus 6 equals 13 right? Wrong! Say the right answer: 12. No equipment, no running. A child may stay seated and swing one arm. Do not give prizes or compare children.
