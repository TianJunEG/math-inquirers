Round 2, about 4 minutes. Children take the 4 WRONG cards and say what went wrong and what the right answer is, then write the four right answers on paper. Right answers: 45 + 28 = 73 (5 and 8 make 13, and the ten was forgotten, so the answer is 10 too small); 84 take away 6 = 78 (4 is less than 6, so 6 take away 4 was done instead of 4 take away 6: 6 take away 4 is 2); 53 take away 20 = 33 (the answer 73 is more than 53, so a plus sign was used); 17 + 5 = 22 (one short, a counting slip).
