A child adds 56 + 27 in three steps. Which step is wrong?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. A child works out 56 plus 27 in three steps: 56 plus 20 is 76 (right); 76 plus 4 is 80 (right); 80 plus 3 is 82 (wrong, it is 83). Only step 3 is wrong. Ask: check each step in turn; which one is wrong (3). What is the right step (80 + 3 = 83). Then check the answer: 83 take away 27 is 56. Say: we find the one wrong step, we fix it, and the steps before it were fine. The answer is on the next slide. Your-turn question, children write: 92 take away 35 in two steps: step 1 92 take away 30 is 72; step 2 72 take away 5 is 67. Which step is wrong? Step 1 (92 take away 30 is 62), and step 2 is right working from the wrong number: fix step 1 and then do step 2 again: 62 take away 5 is 57. The answer is 57, check 57 + 35 = 92.
