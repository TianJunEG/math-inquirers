A wrong total

A pen costs $4 and a book costs $9. A child says they cost $15 altogether. Is that right?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A wrong total. A pen costs 4 dollars and a book costs 9 dollars. A child says they cost 15 dollars altogether. Ask: is that right (no). Add 4 and 9: 9 and 1 make 10, and 3 more make 13. Check the child's total by taking the book away: 15 take away 9 is 6, but the pen costs 4, so 15 is not right. Say: we add prices to find the total, and we can check the total by taking one price away. The answer is on the next slide. Children answer aloud. A child who gives 15 may have added 4 and 9 and a slip of 2: ask them to count on from 9.
