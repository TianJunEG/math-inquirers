Check with a different way

A child in another class writes 29 + 35 = 54.

Jump on the line. Do you land on 54?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A different way to check an add. The child wrote 29 plus 35 equals 54. On the number line jump 30 to land on 59, then 5 more to land on 64. The line says 64, but the child wrote 54, so the two ways disagree and there is a mistake. Check the new answer by taking away: 64 take away 35 is 29. Ask: where do you land (64); does it match 54 (no). Say: two different ways must agree. If they do not, look for the mistake. Children answer aloud. The answer is on the next slide. A child who gets 54 again may have repeated the same slip: ask them to count the ones, 9 and 5 make 14.
