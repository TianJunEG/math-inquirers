Jump a ten

46 + 30. Jump three tens. Where do you land?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A tens jump (Term 1 Lesson 6, taught again): from 46, three jumps of ten reach 56, 66, 76. The ones stay the same. Ask: where do you land (76). Say: tens change, ones stay. A child who is unsure counts three jumps of ten on fingers. Children answer aloud. No writing.
