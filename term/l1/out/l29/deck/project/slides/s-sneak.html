Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

A child in another class writes

40 + 20 = 70.

Is the answer right?

Circle Yes or No.

PUZZLE 2: think

A child in another class writes

27 + 16 = 33.

Is the answer right?

I know because ___.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 (40 + 20 = 70 is wrong, it is 60) is an access question. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: 27 + 16 = 33 is wrong (43) because the ten from 7 + 6 = 13 was forgotten. A child who says 'it is not right because 7 and 6 make 13' or who checks by taking away has done well. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera. There is no private chat. Answers: No; No (27 + 16 = 43). Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
