A quarter

A QUARTER

One of 4 equal parts.

4 quarters make 1 whole.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A quarter is one of 4 equal parts. The square on the slide is cut into 4 equal parts with one coloured. Children count the parts and say: 4 equal parts, so one part is a quarter. Say: 4 quarters make 1 whole square. Ask: how is a quarter different from a half (4 parts instead of 2, so each part is smaller). The word quarter will return when we read the clock in Term 3; today it means one of 4 equal parts.
