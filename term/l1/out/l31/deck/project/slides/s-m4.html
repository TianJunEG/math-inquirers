Half circle and quarter circle

A half circle is half of a circle. A quarter circle is a quarter of a circle.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Two new shape names. The half circle has 1 straight side and 1 curved side. The quarter circle has 2 straight sides that meet at a corner and 1 curved side. Ask: how many half circles make a whole circle (2); how many quarter circles (4). Children can hold up their folded paper: a folded circle shows a half circle, and folded twice shows a quarter circle. Say the names together twice. This is the Primary 1 shape work in the lesson (2D Shapes 1.1).
