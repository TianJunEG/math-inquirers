In which picture is half coloured?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A check that works without folding: count. Each picture is 6 small squares. Picture 1 has 3 coloured and 3 white, so the 2 parts match and half is coloured. Picture 2 has 4 coloured and 2 white, so they do not match and half is not coloured. Ask: how many coloured; how many white; do they match. The answer appears on the next slide, after the children have answered. Say: half means the two parts are the same size. Answers: Picture 1; 3 and 3; 4 and 2.
