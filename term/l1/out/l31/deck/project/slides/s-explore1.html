Explore: fold a circle

1Take one paper circle from your kit.

2Fold it so the edges meet.

3Open it. Trace the fold with a pencil.

4Lay one part on top of the other. Do they match?

Fold only. Nobody cuts.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 4 minutes. Each child has a paper circle from the kit page (or a circle they traced round a cup). Fold so the edges meet exactly, open, trace the fold with a pencil, then lay one part on top of the other: they match, so the 2 parts are equal. Say the words: equal parts; each part is a half; 2 halves make 1 whole. Ask: how many parts; do they match; how do you know. The answer picture is on the next slide, after the children have answered. Answers: 2 parts, they match, a half. A child whose edges do not meet exactly tries again with a slower fold.
