Explore: fold again

1Fold your circle in half again.

2Open it. Trace the new fold. How many parts now?

3Lay the parts on top of each other. Do they all match?

4Now take a long rectangle. Fold it in half, then in half again. Open it.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 5 minutes. Fold the same circle in half again so the fold lands across the first one; open it: 4 parts that all match, so they are quarters. Ask a child to lay 2 quarters on 1 half: they cover it exactly, so 2 quarters make 1 half. Then the long rectangle from the kit: it has 3 faint lines. Fold it in half, then in half again so the folds land on the faint lines; open it. 4 equal strips, so each strip is a quarter of the rectangle. Answers: 4 parts; yes they match; a quarter; the folds land on the faint lines. A child with no rectangle folds a long strip of paper twice.
