A CHILD SAYS

Any 2 parts of a shape are halves.

Is the child right? Test it with your circle.

I think ... because ...

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

One minute of talk to the grown-up next to you, or to your screen. Children test it with their second circle from Explore round 3 (the crooked fold), or fold a fresh one crookedly. Then volunteers first; a pass is always fine; never call a child by name to answer. Model answer: no. Halves must be equal, so they must match when one is laid on the other. A crooked fold makes 2 parts that do not match. Repeat each child answer in your own words for the recording. The child who is wrong is a child in another class: no cast name is given the wrong answer.
