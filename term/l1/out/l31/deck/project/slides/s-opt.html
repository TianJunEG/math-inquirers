Optional: corner to corner

Mei folds a paper square corner to corner.

Each part is a half. What shape is each part?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional, only if you are on time. A square folded corner to corner makes 2 halves that are each a triangle. The picture on the slide shows only an up-and-down fold line, so children imagine the corner fold or try it with a spare square. Take two answers and move on. This links to Build the Figure next lesson. Answer: a triangle. Children check by folding a square corner to corner.
