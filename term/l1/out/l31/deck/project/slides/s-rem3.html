Warm-up: share 7

Share 7 dots between 2 plates.

Can both plates match?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Children try to share 7 dots between 2 plates, one at a time. One dot is left over, so the plates cannot match. Ask: can both plates have the same (no); what is left over (1 dot). Say: when the plates match we say the parts are equal. The word equal is the word for today. The answer is not on the slide. A child who counted 3 and 4 says that the plates are not the same, and that is the right idea.
