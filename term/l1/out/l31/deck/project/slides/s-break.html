Turn like a clock

Stand up. Face the screen.

A half turn. A quarter turn.

Sip some water.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up with a little clear space, in socks or soft shoes, no running. Call the turns slowly: a half turn (you face the back), then turn back; a quarter turn (you face the side), then turn back; a whole turn once, very slowly, all the way round. Say: a whole turn is 4 quarter turns and 2 half turns. A child may stay seated and turn their hands or head. Finish with a sip of water. No equipment. Do not give prizes or compare children.
