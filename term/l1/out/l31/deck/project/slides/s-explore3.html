Two parts, but are they halves?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 3 minutes. Each child folds a second circle crookedly: the edges must not meet, so the fold does not go through the middle. Open it: 2 parts, one big and one small. Lay one on the other: they do not match. Say: two parts are not always halves. Halves must be equal. Ask: are these two parts halves (no); how do you know (they do not match). The answer is on the next slide. A child whose crooked fold turned out to be a good half is asked to fold again a different way, and the good half is praised as a good fold.
