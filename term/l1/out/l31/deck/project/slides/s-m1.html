A half

A HALF

One of 2 equal parts.

2 halves make 1 whole.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Name the idea in words. A half is one of 2 equal parts. Point to the coloured part and the white part of the circle on the slide: they are the same size, so each is a half. Say: 2 halves make 1 whole circle. Children say it with you. Ask: what makes a part a half (there are 2 parts and they match). There is no fraction notation in this lesson: the word half only. Keep the picture on the screen while you speak.
