Two ways to see 12

Use the same 12 counters both times.

3 groups of 4

4 groups of 3

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 4 minutes. Take 12 counters. First make 3 plates with 4 on each (3 groups of 4). Then put the same 12 counters on 4 plates, 3 on each (4 groups of 3). Ask what children notice: the total is the same, 12, but the groups are different. Do not say 3 × 4 = 4 × 3 yet: we see it again with an array in the mini-lesson. Do not say share: the word sharing belongs to Lesson 17.
