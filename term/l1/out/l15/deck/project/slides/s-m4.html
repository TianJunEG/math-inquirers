Rows and columns

3 rows of 4: 3 × 4 = 12

Turn it: 4 rows of 3: 4 × 3 = 12

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

An array is equal groups in rows. 3 rows with 4 balls in each row is 3 × 4 = 12. Turn the page on its side: now there are 4 rows with 3 in each row, 4 × 3 = 12. The balls are the same, so the total is the same: 3 × 4 = 4 × 3. Ask: does the picture work for 2 rows of 5 and 5 rows of 2 (both 10)? Link back to the sneak-peek: 4 bags of 3 and 3 bags of 4.
