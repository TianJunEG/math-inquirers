Explore: equal groups

1Put 3 counters on each of 4 plates.

2Every plate has the same number. The groups are equal.

3Count all the counters in jumps of 3.

4Now try 5 counters on each of 3 plates.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 4 minutes. Objects first, no writing. Children put 3 counters on each of 4 plates (or on the groups mat), then count them in jumps of 3: 3, 6, 9, 12. Say the words once: group, each, equal, 4 groups of 3. Then 3 plates with 5 counters each (15). Ask: how many plates, how many on each plate, how many altogether. A child with fewer counters uses 3 plates of 3. The picture shows 4 groups of 3.
