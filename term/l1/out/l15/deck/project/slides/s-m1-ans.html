Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Look at 4 plates with 3 cookies on each. We say 4 groups of 3. We can add the same number again and again: 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12. Point to each plate as you add. Ask: how many groups, how many in each, how many altogether. Say that 4 is the number of groups and 3 is the number in each group. (answer shown after the children have answered)
