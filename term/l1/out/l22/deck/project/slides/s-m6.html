Each child works with their own clock. 2:20: the long hand on the 4 (count 5, 10, 15, 20) and the short hand just past the 2. 5:40: the long hand on the 8 (40 minutes) and the short hand about two thirds of the way from the 5 to the 6. Walk the cameras; do not ask children to hold their clock up. Wait 10 seconds before helping. The pictures appear on the next slide. Answers: 2:20 and 5:40. Children tick their own work.
