am and pm

AM

From the middle of the night to noon.

Wake up, breakfast, school.

PM

From noon to the middle of the night.

Lunch after noon, tea, bedtime.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

am is the morning: from the middle of the night (12 at night) to noon. pm is the afternoon and evening: from noon to the middle of the night. We write the time, a space, then am or pm, without full stops: 7:30 am, 6:15 pm. Ask: is breakfast at 7:30 am or pm (am); is bedtime at 8:30 am or pm (pm). Careful: 12:30 is just after noon, so it is pm; pm is not only night. Children answer on three. Answers: am, pm.
