Explore: the short hand waits

1Set your clock to 9 o'clock.

2Move the long hand to the 10. Count in 5s.

3Where is the short hand? Is it 9 or 10?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 3 minutes. Children set 9 o'clock, then move the long hand round to the 10: count 5 ... 50. The short hand has crept almost to the 10 but it is not there yet. Ask: what is the time (9:50); is it 9 or 10 (still 9: the short hand has not reached the 10; the hour changes when the long hand is back on the 12). Say: the short hand waits at the hour until the long hand has been all the way round. The picture appears on the next slide. Answer: 9:50, not 10:50. This is the hour-hand trap that the Think harder page asks about again with a different time.
