Warm-up: pairs that make 60

Look at the number line. What goes with each number to make 60?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The number line shows 50 and a jump of 10 to 60. Ask: 50 and what make 60 (10); 40 and what make 60 (20); 55 and what make 60 (5). This is make a ten from Term 1 Lesson 5, taught again: later in the lesson we move to the next hour in the same way (from 3:50 to 4:00 is 10 minutes). Children answer with a whisper or fingers. A child who is unsure counts up from the first number on the line. Answers: 10, 20, 5.
