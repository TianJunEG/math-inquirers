Hours and minutes

H AND MIN

h is hour. min is minutes.

1 h 20 min is 1 hour and 20 minutes.

A swim lasts 1 h 40 min.

How long is 2 h 10 min? Say it in words.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

h stands for hour and min stands for minutes: 1 h 20 min is 1 hour and 20 minutes. Ask: say 2 h 10 min in words (2 hours and 10 minutes); how many minutes are in 1 hour (60). We only read hours and minutes this way, and we do not change them into minutes today, and we do not find how long between two times (that is the Go further page). Answer: 2 hours and 10 minutes.
