Explore: set the minutes

1Set your clock to 4 o'clock.

2Move the long hand to the 2.

3Count in 5s from the 12. What time is it?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 4 minutes. Children set their cut-out clock (or a real clock) to 4 o'clock, then move the long hand to the 2 and count 5, 10: 10 minutes past 4, written 4:10. Then the same for 7 o'clock and the long hand on the 5: count 5, 10, 15, 20, 25: 7:25. Ask each time: where is the short hand (just past the hour); what time is it. The picture appears on the next slide, after the children have answered. Answers: 4:10 and 7:25. A child who reads the 2 as 2 minutes counts in 5s with a finger on each number.
