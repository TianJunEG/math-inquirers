Explore: morning or afternoon?

1Take your 8 event cards and the mat.

2Put each card in the morning pocket (am) or the afternoon and evening pocket (pm).

3Which cards were hard to place?

AM AND PM

am is from the middle of the night to noon.

pm is from noon to the middle of the night.

We write 7:30 am and 6:15 pm.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 4 minutes. Each child has the 8 event cards (wake up, have breakfast, go to school, morning break; come home, play ball, have a bath, go to bed) and the morning and afternoon mat from the kit page. They sort the cards into am (morning) and pm (afternoon and evening). Ask: which event is the first in the day; which is the last. Nobody holds cards to the camera: children say which pocket and show a thumb. (answer slide: shown after the children have answered)
