Explore: morning or afternoon?

1Take your 8 event cards and the mat.

2Put each card in the morning box (am) or the afternoon and evening box (pm).

3Read the time on each card to help you decide.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 4 minutes. Each child has the 8 event cards, each with a time (wake up 6:10, breakfast 6:50, school 7:20, break 10:40; come home 1:15, play ball 4:45, night bath 7:05, go to bed 8:05), and the morning and afternoon mat from the kit page. They sort the cards into am (morning) and pm (afternoon and evening). Ask: which event is the first in the day; which is the last. Nobody holds cards to the camera: children say which box and show a thumb. The card with the rule appears on the next slide, after the children have sorted. Answers: wake up, breakfast, school and break are am; come home, play ball, night bath and go to bed are pm. If a child says they have a bath in the morning, say: this card is the bath before bed, at night.
