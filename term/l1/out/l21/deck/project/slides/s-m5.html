Which has more sticks? How do you know?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

26 is 2 bundles and 6 singles. 62 is 6 bundles and 2 singles. 62 has more because the tens digit is worth more. The order of the digits matters. The Talk time question uses the zero case instead, so this idea is not repeated.
