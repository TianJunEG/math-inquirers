Half is two equal parts

Share 6 counters between 2 plates.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Half means two equal parts. Share six counters between two plates, one at a time: three each. Ask: how many on each plate (3); half of 10 (5); half of 12 (6). Children use their own small things or fingers. This is the sharing in two from Term 1 Lesson 8 and Term 2 taught again, because half past means half way round the clock. Answers: 3, 5, 6.
