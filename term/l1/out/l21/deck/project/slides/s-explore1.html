Explore: two hands

1Take one clock face and two hands.

2Find the short, wide hand and the long, thin hand.

3Put the long hand on the 12.

4Put the short hand on the 3. What time is it?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 5 minutes. Each child has the cut-out clock face and two hands from the kit page (or a real clock). Say: the short, wide hand is the hour hand; the long, thin hand is the minute hand. Children lay the long hand on the 12 and the short hand on the 3 and say the time: 3 o'clock, 3:00. Then the same with the short hand on the 10: 10 o'clock, 10:00. Ask what stays the same (the long hand stays on the 12). The picture with the hands appears on the next slide, after the children have answered. Answers: 3:00 and 10:00. If a family has no cut-out hands, a real clock or a paper plate with two card strips works.
