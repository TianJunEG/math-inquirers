Count the minutes

1Put the long hand on the 12.

2Move it to the 1. That is 5 minutes.

3Keep going and count in 5s.

4How many minutes at the 3? At the 6? At the 9?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 4 minutes. The long hand counts the minutes: each number it passes is 5 more minutes. Children move the long hand from the 12 one number at a time, counting 5, 10, 15, and say the minutes at the 3 (15), at the 6 (30), at the 9 (45), and all the way back to the 12 (60 minutes, one whole hour). Say: half way round is 30 minutes, which is half an hour: that is why we say half past. The table appears on the next slide, after the children have answered. Answers: 15, 30, 45 and 60.
