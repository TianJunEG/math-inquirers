Both hands start at 12

1Put both hands on the 12. It is 12 o'clock.

2Move the long hand slowly round to the 6.

3Watch the short hand. Where is it now?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 4 minutes. Both hands start together on the 12: 12 o'clock, 12:00. Children move the long hand slowly round to the 6, which is half way round the face, and watch the short hand creep: it ends up half way between the 12 and the 1. Ask: where is the short hand now (between the 12 and the 1); what time is it (half past 12, 12:30). Say: when the long hand has gone half way round, the short hand has gone half way to the next number. Answer: 12:30. The picture appears on the next slide. A child who loses the short hand while moving the long one holds the short hand still with a finger and then looks again.
