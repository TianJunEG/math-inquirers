Half past

The long hand is on the 6. Where is the short hand?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Half past: the long hand is on the 6, half way round, so 30 minutes have gone. The short hand has gone half way from the 11 to the 12, so the hour is still 11: half past 11, written 11:30. Ask: is it half past 11 or half past 12 (half past 11); why (the short hand has not reached the 12 yet). The card appears on the next slide, after the children have answered. The trap: reading the number the short hand is nearest to. Answer: 11:30.
