Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Circle + circle + square = 16 and the square is 4. Take the square away: 16 - 4 = 12 for the two circles. The two circles are equal, so each is 6. Check by putting 6 back into the line: 6 + 6 + 4 = 16. Reveal one step at a time and let children say each number first. Ask: what is left for the two circles (12); what is one circle (6). Always end with the check.
