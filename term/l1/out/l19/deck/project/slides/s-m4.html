A times line

STEPS

heart × circle means heart groups of the circle.

How many groups of the circle make the total?

Check: put the number back into the line.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A times line: heart × circle = 12 and the circle is 3. Read it as heart groups of 3 make 12. Ask: how many groups of 3 make 12 (4). Children may count in 3s on fingers or put counters in groups of 3. Check: 4 × 3 = 12. Either reading order is accepted in a child answer. Times lines come back in Think harder and Go further.
