8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

A cup hides some counters.

The cup and 4 loose counters make 9 in all.

How many counters are in the cup?

PUZZLE 2: think

Each shape is a number.

How many is the heart?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 (a cup and 4 counters make 9; the cup holds 5) is an access question: any child can count out 9 and take 4 away. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: two stars make 14, so a star is 7, and the heart and a star make 10, so the heart is 3. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera. There is no private chat. Answers: 5; 3. Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
