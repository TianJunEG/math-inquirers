Three rules

1Each shape is a number.

2The same shape is the same number every time.

3Different shapes are different numbers.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the three rules once, slowly. Rule 3 is only for our puzzles, so that each puzzle has one answer. Ask: can a circle be 6 in one line and 4 in another line of the same puzzle (no, rule 2). Children answer with a thumb. Never use letters; the shapes are the unknown numbers.
