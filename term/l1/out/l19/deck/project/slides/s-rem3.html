4 groups of 5

4 × 5 means 4 groups of 5.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four bags with five counters in each bag. We add one bag at a time: 5, 10, 15, 20. Ask: how many counters in 4 bags of 5 (20); 3 groups of 4 (12); 2 groups of 10 (20). This is Term 2 Lesson 5 taught again: the × sign says groups of. A child who is unsure counts the counters one bag at a time, saying the total each time.
