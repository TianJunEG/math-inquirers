Take one line from another

STEPS

Line 1 has one more star than line 2.

The totals show how much that extra star is.

Then put the star into line 2 to find the triangle.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Two lines about the same shapes: star + star + triangle = 17 and star + triangle = 12. Line 1 has one more star than line 2, and 17 is 5 more than 12, so one star is 5. Then star + triangle = 12, so 5 + triangle = 12 and the triangle is 7. Ask: how much more is 17 than 12 (5); what is the triangle (7). The steps appear on the next slide, after the children have answered. This is the new idea of the lesson and goes beyond Primary 1: say it was found by taking one line from the other.
