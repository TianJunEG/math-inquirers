Two lines, same shapes

Line 1 tells you the circle.

The circle is the same number in line 2.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 4 minutes. Two lines: circle + circle = 14 and circle + triangle = 10. Children use the first line to find the circle (7), then the same circle in the second line to find the triangle (3). They may use counters: 14 on two circle cards, then 10 on one circle card and one triangle card. Ask: what number is the circle (7), the triangle (3). Say: the circle is 7 in both lines. Answers: circle 7, triangle 3. A child who gets a different circle in line 2 is asked: is it the same shape?
