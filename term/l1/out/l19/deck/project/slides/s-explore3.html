Two lines, same shapes

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 4 minutes. Two lines: circle + circle = 6 and circle + triangle = 9. Children use the first line to find the circle (3), then the same circle in the second line to find the triangle (6). They may use counters: 6 on two circle cards, then 9 on one circle card and one triangle card. Ask: what number is the circle (3), the triangle (6). Say: the circle is 3 in both lines. The two lines on the slide are shown first; the two sentences about the circle appear after the children have tried (the next slide). Answers: circle 3, triangle 6. A child who gets a different circle in line 2 is asked: is it the same shape? The numbers are not the sneak-peek numbers: do not tell the sneak-peek answers here.
