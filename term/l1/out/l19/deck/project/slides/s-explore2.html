The same shape twice

1Count out 12 counters.

2Share them onto 2 heart cards, one at a time.

3The piles must match. How many on one heart?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 5 minutes. Heart + heart = 12. Both hearts are the same number, so the piles must match. Children deal 12 counters one at a time onto two heart cards. Ask: how many counters are on one heart card (6). Say: both hearts are 6, because the same shape is always the same number. Then check: put the piles together and count (12). Answer: 6. A child who deals unevenly is asked: are the piles the same?
