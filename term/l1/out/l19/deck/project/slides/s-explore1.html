Explore: a shape and a pile

1Count out 10 counters.

2Put 4 on the square card.

3The rest go on the circle card. How many?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 5 minutes. Children use their shape cards (small papers with a circle, a square, a triangle and a heart drawn on them) and counters (the kit page counters or small things). The picture says circle + square = 10, and the square is 4. They count out 10 counters, put 4 on the square card and the rest on the circle card, then count the circle card (6). Say: the circle is 6. Write circle = 6 on the screen, then check: 6 + 4 = 10. If a family has no cut-out cards, a cup or a piece of paper with the shape drawn on it works. Answer: 6. Do not name a child with a wrong answer.
