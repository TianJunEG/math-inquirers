Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 5 minutes. Ask: look at the last digit of each yellow number (the 5s). What do you notice? (5, 0, 5, 0). The 10s end in 0. The 2s end in 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8. Let children say the pattern themselves. Then ask: is 35 in the 5s? is 28? Children answer with a thumb up or down. The picture here shows the 5s as an example only. Accept any correct pattern a child finds, such as the 5s are every second number of the 10s.
