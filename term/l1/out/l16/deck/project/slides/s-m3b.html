A common mix-up is 6 + 5 and 6 × 5. 6 + 5 means 6 and 5 more: 11. 6 × 5 means 6 jumps of 5: 30. Ask: what is 4 + 3 and what is 4 × 3 (7 and 12)? Link to the pictures: 4 groups of 3 is 4 × 3.
