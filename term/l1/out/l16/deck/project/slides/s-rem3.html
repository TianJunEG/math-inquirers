5 groups of 6

6 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 6: add one group at a time.

12 + 6

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Five bags with 6 counters in each bag. We add one bag at a time: 6 + 6 = 12, then 12 + 6 = 18, and so on. Say 6 + 6 = 12 yourself, then ask: 12 + 6 (18). Children answer aloud. This is Term 2 Lesson 5 taught again: groups of is repeated addition, and a table is the same idea with the jump size fixed.
