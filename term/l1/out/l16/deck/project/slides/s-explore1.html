Explore: jumps of 5

1Put a counter just left of the 1. That is 0.

2Jump 5 at a time. Colour each number you land on.

3Say it: 1 five is 5, 2 fives is 10, 3 fives is 15 ...

4How many jumps to land on 30?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 5 minutes. Children use the 100 square on the kit page (page 6) and a counter as a frog. Start on 0: the counter sits just to the left of the 1, off the square. Each jump of 5 lands on 5, 10, 15 ... up to 40; children colour each landing square lightly and say 1 five is 5, 2 fives is 10. Fingers count the jumps. Ask: how many jumps to land on 30 (6), on 25 (5), on 40 (8). Then say 6 fives is 30 and write 6 × 5 = 30 on the screen. The picture shows the numbers 1 to 40 with nothing coloured, so children find the landing numbers themselves.
