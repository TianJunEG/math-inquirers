Double it

The top 2 rows are in the box.

What is 2 × 6? What is 4 × 6?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The 4s are the 2s doubled. The top 2 rows are 2 × 6 = 12. All 4 rows are two lots of the boxed rows, so 4 × 6 is 12 + 12 = 24. Ask for 2 × 6 first, then 4 × 6, and let children say the doubling themselves. The answer comes on the next slide. Workbook T1 uses the same idea with new numbers.
