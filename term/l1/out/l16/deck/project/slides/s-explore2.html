Jumps of 2 and of 10

Use a different colour for each table.

Jump 2 at a time: how many jumps to land on 12?

Jump 10 at a time: how many jumps to land on 40?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 4 minutes. Children colour the 2s in a second colour and put a ring round the 10s. Ask: how many jumps of 2 to land on 12 (6; 6 twos is 12) and how many jumps of 10 to land on 40 (4; 4 tens is 40). Say each fact with the sign: 6 × 2 = 12, 4 × 10 = 40. A child who loses count uses fingers for the jumps. Do not name a child with a wrong answer.
