Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A table is a list of jumps. The first number is how many jumps and the second is the size of each jump: 6 × 5 is 6 jumps of 5, which lands on 30. The table stops at 6 fives; 7 × 5 is asked aloud and the answer comes on the next slide. Link back to Term 2 Lesson 5: 6 groups of 5 and 6 jumps of 5 are the same.
