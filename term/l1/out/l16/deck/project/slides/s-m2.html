The 2s and the 10s

The 2s

1 × 2 = 2

2 × 2 = 4

3 × 2 = 6

The 10s

1 × 10 = 10

2 × 10 = 20

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The 2s are jumps of 2: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12. The 10s are jumps of 10: 10, 20, 30, 40. Say each fact. Ask: what is 4 × 2 (8), what is 3 × 10 (30); the answers come on the next slide. Children who know the facts say them aloud; others look at the card. Note the 10s are every second number of the 5s.
