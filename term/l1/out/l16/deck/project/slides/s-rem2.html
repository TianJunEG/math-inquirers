Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The frog jumps 5 each time and has made 3 jumps. Ask: where does the next jump land (20), where do 3 jumps of 5 land (15), how many jumps of 2 does it take to reach 8 (4). Children answer aloud or with fingers. A child who is unsure counts the jumps on fingers while saying the numbers.
