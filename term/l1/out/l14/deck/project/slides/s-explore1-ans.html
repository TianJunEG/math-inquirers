Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 4 minutes. Objects first, no writing. Children pair 6 counters (none left over) and 7 counters (one left over). Name the words once: none left over is even, one left over is odd. Then 8 (even), 9 (odd), 10 (even). Children answer with a flat hand for even and one finger for odd. A child with few counters can draw dots two by two.
