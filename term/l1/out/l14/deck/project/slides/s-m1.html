Odd and even

6: even

7: odd

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Even numbers make pairs with none left over; odd numbers have one left over. The last digit tells us at once: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 are even; 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 are odd. Ask: is 57 odd or even (odd, last digit 7)? Also ask how do you know. Take one or two volunteers. The tens are made of pairs (ten is five pairs), so only the ones digit can leave one over.
