Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Test the third claim with three sums: 4 + 6 = 10, 8 + 2 = 10, 10 + 10 = 20. All are even. Ask: does the claim work for all three? Yes, for these three. It is good evidence, and the picture of pairs on the next slides will show why. Reveal the table row by row after children have worked out each total.
