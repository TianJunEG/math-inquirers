Test a claim: even + even

CLAIM

An even number plus an even number is always even.

4 + 68 + 210 + 10

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Test the third claim with three sums: 4 + 6 = 10, 8 + 2 = 10, 10 + 10 = 20. All are even. Ask: does the claim work for all three? Yes, for these three. It is good evidence, and the picture of pairs on the next slides will show why. The totals (10, 10, 20) are not shown: children work them out and say odd or even.
