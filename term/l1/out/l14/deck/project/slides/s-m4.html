Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Each odd number has one counter left over. When we put two odd numbers together, the two left-over counters make a new pair. So there is none left over: the total is even. This is true for any two odd numbers, not just 3 and 5. Ask: does the picture work for 7 + 9? Yes: 7 has one left over and 9 has one left over. The total 16 is 8 pairs.
