Number Words and the Number Line: practice at home
NameDate

About 15 minutes, a few days after the lesson. Use your number cards, a line you draw, or a toy frog. A grown-up may read the questions aloud. Wrong answers are fine: they show what to try next.

1Start here
Fill in the missing numbers: 46 | 47 | ___ | 49 | ___
2Start here
Write the numbers in digits: thirteen, thirty, nineteen, ninety.
3Start here
Fill in the blanks: 83 is between ___ and ___.
4Think harder
Is 56 nearer to 50 or to 60? Circle your answer. Show how you know.
5Go further
At home: draw 5 boxes in a row. The jumps are equal. Write numbers in 2 boxes and leave 3 empty. Ask a grown-up to solve it. Write the answer on the back.
Answers for grown-ups

Please let your child try first, then check together. Ask how do you know? If your child is stuck, wait 10 seconds, then suggest drawing it.

1. 48 and 50
Count on: 47, 48, 49, 50.
2. 13, 30, 19, 90
Teen means ten and some more: 13 and 19. Ty means tens: 30 and 90.
3. 80 and 90
83 is 8 tens and 3 more, so it is past 80 and not yet 90.
4. 60 (4 hops to 60, 6 hops to 50)
56 to 60 is 4 hops. 56 to 50 is 6 hops. 4 is fewer, so 60 is nearer.
5. Any correct puzzle, for example 20 | ___ | ___ | ___ | 40 (jump 5: 25, 30, 35).
A grown-up checks that the jump is the same between every two boxes and that only one number fits each empty box.
