Tens and ones, and counting in tens

34 is 3 tens and 4 ones

Count in tens to one hundred:

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Show 34 as 3 tens and 4 ones, then count in tens to 100 together. Say that ten tens is called one hundred. Questions: 52 is 5 tens 2 ones; 80 is 8 tens 0 ones; 6 tens and 1 one is 61. This is a spiral review of skip counting in tens (holiday topic) and the tens and ones idea from Lesson 1, taught again with a picture for any child who was not there.
