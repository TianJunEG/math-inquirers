One before, one after

After 59 the ones are used up. A new ten begins: 60.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. One before 59 is 58 and one after is 60. Ask: what is one before 70? Answer: 69. A common slip is writing 510 after 59, or 50. Say the numbers out loud in order and tap the line.
