9–13 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

Fill in the missing numbers in the boxes.

PUZZLE 2: think

A frog sits on 59.

It hops 1 number forward. Where does it land?

Write the number. I know because ___.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 (20 and 23) is an access question: any child who can count on can do it. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the key idea of the lesson (after 59 a new ten begins). Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera: children keep their papers and check their own thinking at Talk time. There is no private chat and no board in front of the group. Answers: 20 and 23; 60 (after 59 the ones are used up). Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
