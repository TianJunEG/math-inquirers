Marks that are missing

Equal jumps. Count the jumps first.

4 jumps. 20 to 60 is 40. 40 shared by 4 is 10 each.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. The boxes are equal jumps. Count the jumps (4). The whole gap, 20 to 60, is 40, shared between 4 jumps: 10 each. So the marks are 30, 40, 50. Then ask about 0, 5, 10, a missing mark, 20: the jump is 5 and the missing mark is 15. Only the jump idea is taught; skip counting in 5s comes in Lesson 4.
