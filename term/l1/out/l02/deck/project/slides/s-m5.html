Which ten is nearer?

Nearer to 50.

Halfway. No ten is nearer.

Count the hops each way. The smaller number of hops wins.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Count the hops each way and pick the smaller. 47 is nearer 50. 45 is exactly halfway: 5 hops each way, so no ten is nearer; do not teach a rounding rule. Ask: is 43 nearer to 40 or 50 (40, 3 hops against 7)? Which ten is 68 nearer to (70, 2 hops against 8)? The 4 in 47 does not make it nearer to 40: that is the common slip. This idea is beyond the Primary 1 syllabus and is thinking enrichment, never a rule to memorise.
