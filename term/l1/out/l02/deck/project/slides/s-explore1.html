Explore: build a number line

1Find your ones cards, 0 to 10.

2Put them in a row, in order.

3Put your frog on 0. Hop where I say.

WHAT IF?

What if every card was worth ten? Try the tens cards next.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 5 minutes. Objects first, little writing. Children put the 11 ones cards (square, from the kit page) in order left to right and hop a small toy (the frog) where you say: hop to 7, hop on 3 more (10), hop back 4 (6). They check their row against the slide. The what-if card previews round 2 and no child is named for trying it. A child without cards writes 0 to 10 on 11 small pieces of paper. Ask: 11 cards, how many hops? 10: the hops are the gaps.
