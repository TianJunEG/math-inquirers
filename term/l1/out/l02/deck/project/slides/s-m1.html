Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teen numbers say the ones first (fourteen is four and ten). Ty numbers say the tens (forty is four tens). Two-part names say the tens first then the ones (twenty-four). Ask: which is greater, thirteen or thirty? Answer: thirty, it is 3 tens. Spelling: forty has no u; fifteen is not fifty. Children read the words; they do not need to spell them from memory.
