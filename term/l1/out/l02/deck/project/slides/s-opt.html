What is halfway between 0 and 50? How do you know?

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional, only if you are on time. Answer: 25. The gap from 0 to 50 is 50 hops, and half of 50 is 25 hops. 25 sits halfway between 20 and 30, so we use two tens marks. Take two answers and move on.
