Same line, new job

Find your tens cards. Put 0, 10, 20 up to 100 in a row.

There is no card for 47. Where does it live?

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 6 minutes. The same 11 places become a line in tens: this is the idea of a scale, the same line counted in ones or in tens. Children lay the 11 tens cards (wide, from the kit page) in order, hop the frog in tens, then place it on 60, 30 and 90. Then hop in small steps from 40 to 50 through the gap. Then ask for 47: it lives between 40 and 50. Count the hops: 3 to 50, 7 to 40, so it is nearer 50. Children keep their line on the table until Talk time.
