TEEN OR TY?

Teen means ten and some more.

Ty means tens.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 4 minutes. Say each name, children put the frog where the number lives, then you show the word. Twenty-six is between 20 and 30; sixteen is between 10 and 20; sixty is on the 60 paper; eighty-one is just past 80. Ask how to tell sixteen from sixty. Teen means ten and some more, so sixteen is 16. Ty means tens, so sixty is 6 tens, 60. Keep spelling small: children say and read the words today; writing is only copying a few tens words in the workbook.
