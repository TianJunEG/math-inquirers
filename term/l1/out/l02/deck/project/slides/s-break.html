Be the number line

Arms wide. Left hand is 0. Right hand is 100.

I CALL

50, 10, 90, 25, 75

Point to where each number lives. Sip some water.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera and stretch arms wide: left hand is 0, right hand is 100, and the nose is the middle. Call 50 (middle), 10 (near the left hand), 90 (near the right), 25 and 75 (halfway to each end). Children point or lean. Then 5 small hops forward and 3 back on the spot, counting aloud. No equipment. Keep it gentle for small spaces.
