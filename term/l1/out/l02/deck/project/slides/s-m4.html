Zoom in between two tens

47 lives between 40 and 50. It is 7 hops after 40.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Zoom in on the line between 40 and 50. 47 is 7 hops after 40. Ask: how many hops from 47 to 50? Answer: 3. Every number lives between two tens, or sits on one. To find the two tens, look at the tens digit: 47 has 4 tens, so it is past 40 and not yet 50.
