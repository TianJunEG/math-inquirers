Two quick worked examples. Along the row: triangle, ?, square, so the circle is missing. Down the column: ?, circle, triangle, so the square is missing. Ask each question first; children answer aloud or touch the tile. Then say: a column is just a row turned to stand up. Say the sentence frame: the row has a triangle and a square, so the box must be a circle. Children say the frame with you. The slide never shows the answers.
