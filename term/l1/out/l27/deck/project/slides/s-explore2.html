Explore: one of each in every column

1A column goes down.

2Look down each column. Is there one of each?

3Move tiles until every row and every column has one of each.

Take your time. Many ways are right.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 4 minutes, main room. Now the same rule goes down: a column is a line of boxes from the top to the bottom. Children check each column, find a repeat (two circles in one column), and swap tiles. Give one hint only when asked: leave the top row as it is and look at where the circle can go in the middle row. Take volunteers: how did you fix it. Several grids are right. Children say the rule in their own words before moving on: one of each in every row and every column. A child with no tiles uses the three letters on paper.
