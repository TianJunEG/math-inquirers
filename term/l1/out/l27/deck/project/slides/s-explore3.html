Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 3 minutes. Everyone keeps the top row circle, square, triangle on mat 1. How many different grids can they fill? There are exactly 2: the middle row can start with a square (square, triangle, circle) or with a triangle (triangle, circle, square), and then the bottom row is fixed. Children find one, then look for another. Ask: how many different grids did you find; how do you know there is not a third. The answer 2 is not on the slide and is confirmed on the next slide. A child who finds a grid that breaks the rule is asked to check each column.
