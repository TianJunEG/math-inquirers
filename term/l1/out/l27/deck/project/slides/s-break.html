Be the shapes

Stand up. I call a shape.

Make it with your arms or your body.

Sip some water.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera with a little clear space. Call the shapes one at a time: a circle (arms in a big ring above the head), a square (arms out to the sides and elbows bent at right angles), a triangle (hands together above the head like a roof). Then call a row of three, for example circle, square, triangle, and children make the three shapes one after another. Call one more row with a different order. No equipment, no running; a child may stay seated and make the shapes with their hands. Do not give prizes or compare children.
