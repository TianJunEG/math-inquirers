Go further: three in a row

Mei has a 3 by 3 grid with the digits 1, 2 and 3.

Every row and every column has 1, 2 and 3 once. Can you fill it?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional, only if you are on time. A 3 by 3 grid of the digits 1, 2 and 3 with 1, 2 and 3 on the long line from the top left to the bottom right. There is exactly one way to fill it: the top row must be 1, 3, 2 (a 2 in the top row would sit above the 2 in the middle), then 3, 2, 1 and 2, 1, 3. Take two answers: children say the first row, then check the rows and the columns together. Ask: how do you know the top row cannot be 1, 2, 3? Take the idea, not the speed.
