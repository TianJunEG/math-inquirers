Share or group?

1 finger: share. 2 fingers: group.

Story
What happens
A
Mei puts 12 cookies equally on 4 plates.
B
Ali packs 12 cookies, 6 in each box.
C
Ravi gives 15 stickers equally to 3 friends.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 3 minutes. Read each story aloud. Children show 1 finger for share (the cookies or stickers are dealt out equally, so the number of plates or friends is known) and 2 fingers for group (the number in each box is known). A: share, 3 on each plate. B: group, 2 boxes. C: share, 5 each. Do not name a child with a wrong answer. If time allows, children lay out story A or B with counters and find the answer.
