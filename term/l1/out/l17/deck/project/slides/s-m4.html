Left over

14 counters in groups of 4: 3 full groups, and 2 left over.

Left over means not enough to make another group.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Not every total comes out equal. 14 counters in groups of 4 make 3 full groups, and 2 counters are left over, because 2 is not enough for another group of 4. The fourth plate shows the left over. Count: 4, 8, 12, then 2 more. Ask: if we had 15 counters in groups of 4, how many groups and how many left over (3 groups, 3 left over)? Say left over, never remainder.
