Put into groups

12 sweets put into bags of 4.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The same 12 sweets, a different question. We want 4 sweets in each bag. We make a group of 4, then another, until the sweets are gone: 3 bags. We know how many in each bag (4) and we find how many bags (3). Count in fours to check: 4, 8, 12. Ask: how many bags do we need? Point out that the picture looks the same, but the question is different. The answer 3 bags comes on the answer slide, after the children have answered.
