Four minutes. Stand up on camera. Pretend to deal cards, one at a time, to players on each side. Call: 8 cards to 2 players (children deal and say 4 each), 6 cards to 3 players (2 each), 12 cards to 4 players (3 each), 10 cards to 5 players (2 each). Children move one arm to deal and say the number together. No equipment, no running, a little clear space. A child may stay seated and tap the table.
