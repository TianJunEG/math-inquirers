MEI SHARES 12 AND PUTS 12 INTO GROUPS

12 sweets shared equally among 3 friends: 4 each.

12 sweets put into bags of 3: 4 bags.

Both answers are 4. Is it always the same number? How could we check?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

One minute of talk to the grown-up next to you, or to your screen. Children try 12 shared among 4 and 12 in groups of 4 with counters, then another pair such as 10 and 5, or 16 and 2. Then volunteers first; a pass is always fine; never call a child by name to answer. Model answer: the number is the same each time (3 and 3, 2 and 2, 8 and 8), but it means something different (3 each, or 3 groups); turning the rows of counters shows why (3 rows of 4 and 4 rows of 3 use the same counters). Repeat each child’s answer in your own words for the recording.
