Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Both stories check the same way: 3 bags with 4 sweets in each bag is 3 × 4 = 12, and that matches the 12 sweets we began with. If it does not match, we check again. Say the grown-up sign once: grown-ups write both questions with the same sign, 12 divided by 3 is 4 and 12 divided by 4 is 3; you do not need it today and nobody has to use it. Beyond-Primary-1 remark for grown-ups: the sign is a Primary 2 idea. Ask: 4 plates with 5 counters on each plate: what is 4 times 5, and does it match 20?
