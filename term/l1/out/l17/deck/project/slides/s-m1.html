Share equally

Say it
We find
12 shared equally among 3 bags
4 in each bag

We know the number of bags. We find how many in each.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Look at 12 sweets shared equally among 3 bags. We deal one at a time and each bag gets 4. Point to each bag and count the sweets. We know how many bags (3) and we find how many in each bag (4). Ask: what do we know, and what do we find? Do not use the words divided by yet.
