8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

Mei has 6 sweets.

She gives the same number of sweets to each of her 2 friends.

How many sweets does each friend get?

You may draw to help.

PUZZLE 2: think

Ali puts 12 sweets into bags. He puts 3 sweets in each bag.

Mei shares 12 sweets equally among 3 bags.

Whose bags have more sweets in each bag?

Circle Ali, Mei or Same. Then write or draw how you know.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 (6 sweets, 2 friends; 3 each) is an access question: any child can draw it or count it out. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: Ali puts 3 in each bag, so he makes 4 bags; Mei shares among 3 bags, so each bag gets 4. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera. There is no private chat. Answers: 3; Mei (4 in each bag, against Ali with 3). Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
