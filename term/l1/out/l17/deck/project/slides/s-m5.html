Who is sharing?

Ben shares 8 sweets equally with 3 friends.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A reading trap. Ben shares 8 sweets equally with 3 friends. Who gets sweets? Ben and his 3 friends: 4 children. So 8 sweets shared among 4 children is 2 each. Ask: how many children share the sweets (4)? How many sweets does each child get (2)? A child who answers 3 children: point to Ben at the table. The word with means Ben is one of the people sharing. Say it plainly and do not make it a trick: always count who is sharing. The table and the words count Ben too come on the answer slide, after the children have answered.
