12–26 MIN

Put into groups

1Take 12 counters.

2Make groups of 4: put 4 counters in each group.

3Keep going until the counters are gone.

4How many groups of 4? Now try groups of 3.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 4 minutes. The same 12 counters, a different question. Children make groups of 4 and count the groups: 3. Then they push the groups back together and make groups of 3: 4 groups. Count in jumps to check: 4, 8, 12 (three jumps) and 3, 6, 9, 12 (four jumps). Say the words once: we put 12 counters into groups of 4; there are 3 groups. Ask: how many groups, and how many in each group. A child with fewer counters uses 8 counters in groups of 2.
