Explore: share equally

1Put 3 plates on the table.

2Deal 12 counters, one at a time: one for each plate, round and round.

3Stop when the counters are all gone.

4Is every plate the same? How many on each plate?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 5 minutes. Objects first, no writing. Children deal 12 counters, one at a time, onto 3 plates (or onto the groups mat) and stop when they run out. Say the words once: we shared 12 counters equally among 3 plates; each plate has 4. Then share 10 counters equally among 2 plates (5 each) and 12 counters equally among 4 plates (3 each). A child with fewer counters uses 6 counters among 3 plates. The picture shows the plates before dealing; do not show the answer. Use the words equally, each and among.
