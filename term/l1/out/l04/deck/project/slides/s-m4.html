28–38 MIN

Stripes and last digits

10s: one column

5s: two columns

2s: stripes

THE LAST DIGIT

10s end in 0.

5s end in 0 or 5.

2s end in 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Show the three pictures side by side. Counting in 10s is one column; counting in 5s is two columns; counting in 2s is stripes, every other square. The last digit tells you which list a number is in. Ask: would 45 be coloured counting in 5s? Yes, it ends in 5. Would 45 be coloured counting in 2s? No. Do not use the words multiple, odd or even yet; say numbers you say counting in 2s.
