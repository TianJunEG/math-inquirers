Explore: move the counter

WHAT DO YOU NOTICE?

Which way adds 1?

Which way adds 10?

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 4, about 3 minutes. Everyone puts one counter on 34 and moves it as you call. Right 1 gives 35, down 1 gives 45, down again 55, left 1 gives 54, up 1 gives 44. After each move children say the new number. Then ask what they notice: right adds 1 and left takes 1; down adds 10 and up takes 10. This is the key idea of the lesson; the mini-lesson names it. Main room only so you can see every camera.
