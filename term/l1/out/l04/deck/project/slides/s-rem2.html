Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Fives: show one hand, then two hands, then three hands (one hand and the next), saying five, ten, fifteen. Twos: hold up two fingers for each pair of socks. For a child who only counts to 20, this is the bridge: the numbers are the same, only the jump is bigger. Everyone says the numbers out loud; nobody writes.
