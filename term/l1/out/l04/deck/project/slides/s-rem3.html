Answers: 40, 20, 8, 30, 40. The last two questions show that after a 9 the next ten begins (29 then 30): a new ten, in the same row of the 100 square. Say the tens word slowly. If a child hesitates, count up from 20 together. Hands up on camera is fine; no marks.
