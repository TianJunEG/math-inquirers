Across: 1 more, 1 less

Right: 1 more. Left: 1 less.

40 is the last number in its row. The next number, 41, starts the next row.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Show rows three to five of the square (21 to 50). Moving right adds 1 and moving left takes 1. 40 is the last number of its row; the count carries on at the start of the next row: 41. Ask: what is 1 more than 40? Answer: 41 (still just 1 more, even though it starts the next row). Keep it to about a minute.
