Explore: cover the tens

1Count in tens: 10, 20, 30, ...

2Put a counter on each number you say.

3What do you see?

SAY IT

The counters make a line that goes ___ (across or down).

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 4 minutes. Children count in tens to 100 and put a counter on every number they say. Ten counters make one straight line down the last column. Ask what the numbers under the counters end in: every one ends in 0. Repeat each child answer in your own words so the recording picks it up. Counters can be buttons, coins or beans; a child with fewer can cover and uncover.
