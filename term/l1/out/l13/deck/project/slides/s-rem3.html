Move 1 counter across. The total stays the same.

6 + 2 = 5 + ___7 + 1 = 6 + ___

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Two plates. Moving one counter from the left plate to the right plate does not change how many counters there are, so 5 + 3 and 4 + 4 are the same total. Ask: 6 + 2 = 5 + 3 (the missing number is 3), 7 + 1 = 6 + 2 (2), then 5 + 3 = 4 + 4 (4). This is the equals-means-the-same idea that near doubles use. Show the plates picture if a child is unsure.
