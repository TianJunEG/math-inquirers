Stomp the double

I say a number. Stomp that many times on each foot.

Then say the double. Try 2, then 3, then 4.

Stand up. Sip some water.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera. Call 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 (answers 4, 6, 8, 10, 12). Children stomp that many times with the left foot, then the same with the right foot, softly, or tiptoe or tap their fingers in a small space, and say the double aloud together. No equipment.
