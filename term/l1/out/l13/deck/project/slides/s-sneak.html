8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

Mei puts 4 red counters in one frame and 4 yellow counters in another frame.

How many counters are there altogether?

You may draw to help.

PUZZLE 2: think

Ben knows that 6 + 6 = 12.

He says that 6 + 7 is 1 more than 12.

Is Ben right? Circle Yes or No. Then write or draw how you know.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 (8 counters, 4 red and 4 yellow in two ten-frames) is an access question: any child can try it by counting or drawing. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson's key idea: one more counter makes one more in the total. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera: children keep their papers and check their own thinking at Talk time. There is no private chat. Answers: 8; yes (6 + 7 is 6 + 6 and one more, so 13). Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
