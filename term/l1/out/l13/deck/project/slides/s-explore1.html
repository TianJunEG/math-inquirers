Explore: build a double

1Lay the ten-frame page in front of you.

2Put 3 red counters in one frame.

3Put 3 yellow counters in the other frame.

4How many altogether? Then try 4 and 4, and 5 and 5.

Two of the same number is a double.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 4 minutes. Objects first, no writing. Children put 3 red counters in one frame and 3 yellow counters in the other, then count 6. Name the idea once: two of the same number is a double, so double 3 is 6. Then double 4 (8) and double 5 (10; the red frame is full). A child without the kit page uses two kinds of small things on a drawn ten-frame, or kit squares and pencil dots.
