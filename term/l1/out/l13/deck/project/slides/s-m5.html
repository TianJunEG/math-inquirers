The 9 is 1 more than 8. The 7 is 1 less than 8.

8 + 9 + 7 = 8 + 8 + 8 = 24

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A worked example for Go further. The 9 is one more than 8 and the 7 is one less than 8, so they make up for each other: the sum is three 8s, which is 24. Check: 8 + 9 = 17 and 17 + 7 = 24. Keep it to about a minute and do not rush: ask which number is in the middle. Children who are not ready can skip this idea; it is only needed for Go further.
