Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A tens bar is a ten. Three bars and three more bars make six bars: sixty. Ask each pair: three tens and three tens (6 tens, 60), two tens and two tens (4 tens, 40), four tens and four tens (8 tens, 80). Say the tens word slowly. A child who has not met tens only needs to hear and say the number words; show the bars on the slide and count them in tens.
