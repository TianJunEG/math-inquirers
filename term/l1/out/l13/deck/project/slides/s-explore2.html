I move ..., so it is ten and ...

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 4 minutes, main room. Each child builds the double with red and yellow counters, then moves yellow counters into the first frame until it is full. The picture shows double 7: the first frame has 7 red and 3 yellow, and 4 yellow are left, so ten and 4. Answers: double 6 is 12 (ten and 2), double 7 is 14 (ten and 4), double 8 is 16 (ten and 6), double 9 is 18 (ten and 8). This links to making a ten. Do not name children.
