One more, one less

Double 7

1 more yellow counter

Build 7 + 8, then 7 + 6. Then build 8 + 9.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 5 minutes. Children build double 7, add one yellow counter (7 + 8 = 15), then take two yellow away (7 + 6 = 13) so they see it is one less than the double. Then they build 8 + 9: double 8 is 16 and one more is 17. Ask: is it more or less than the double? How do you know? Children count the extra counter, not the whole frame again.
