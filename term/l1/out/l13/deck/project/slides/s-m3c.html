A near double has two numbers that are next to each other.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A near double has two numbers that are next to each other, one step apart. 6 and 7: yes. 5 and 8: no, it is three steps (5, 6, 7, 8), so it is not a near double. 9 and 10: yes. Ask each pair before showing the table answers, and let children count the steps on their fingers. This prepares question T3 on page 3.
