Make 85¢ with coins. What is the fewest number of coins?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional, only if you are on time. 85 cents with the fewest coins: 50, 20, 10 and 5, which is 4 coins. Many children will try 20 and 20 and 20 and 20 and 5 (5 coins) first. Ask: can anyone beat 5 coins? The biggest coin that fits goes first, then the next biggest that fits. Take two answers and move on. This links to Go further and the Investigation.
