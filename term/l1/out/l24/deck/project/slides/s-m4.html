Change: count up

The ball costs $8. You pay with a $10 note. How much change?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked with a question. The ball costs $8 and you pay with a $10 note. Count up from $8 to $10 on the line: $9, $10, which is 2 steps, so the change is $2. Ask first: how much change? Children count up on their fingers or with their coins. Say the answer after they have tried. Change is what the shop gives back: the price plus the change makes the money you paid. A child who subtracts is welcome to: 10 minus 8 is 2.
