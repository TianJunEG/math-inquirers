Coins and notes

Read the number. The number is the value.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Name the pieces: coins are 5, 10, 20 and 50 cents and 1 dollar; notes are 2, 5, 10 and 50 dollars. Say it both ways: fifty-cent coin and 50¢; five-dollar note and $5. We write cents with the cent sign after the number (50¢) and dollars with the dollar sign before it ($5). We never mix them in one amount. The value is the number on the piece, not its size. These are PLAY MONEY: stylised and marked, not real. Ask: which piece is worth the most (the $50 note); which coin is worth the least (5¢).
