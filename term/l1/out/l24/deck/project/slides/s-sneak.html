8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

Count the coins. How many cents? Write the number.

PUZZLE 2: think

Mei has a one-dollar coin.

Ben has 10 ten-cent coins.

Does Mei have more money than Ben? Circle Yes or No. I know because ___.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 (3 ten-cent coins and 2 five-cent coins: 40 cents) is an access question: any child can count in 10s then 5s, or add on. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: 10 ten-cent coins make 100 cents, and a one-dollar coin is 100 cents, so they have the same. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera. There is no private chat. Answers: 40 cents; No, the same. Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
