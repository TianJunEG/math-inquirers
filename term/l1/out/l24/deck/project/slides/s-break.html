Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera with a little clear space. Call an amount in cents (20¢, 35¢, 50¢, 10¢) and children hop once for every 5¢, counting aloud in 5s: for 35¢ that is 7 hops. Then call two amounts in a row so children keep counting on from the last number. No equipment, no running; a child may stay seated and tap the table. Do not give prizes or compare children.
