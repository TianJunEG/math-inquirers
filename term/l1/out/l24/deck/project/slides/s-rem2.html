Put the coins in order

Read the number on each coin. Put your coins in order. Least first.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four coins on the slide: 20, 5, 50 and 10 cents. Children take the same four coins from their kit and put them in a row from least to most, reading the number on each coin. Ask: which coin is worth the least (5¢); which is worth the most (50¢); what is the order (5¢, 10¢, 20¢, 50¢). Say the cent sign: the number tells the value. A child with no kit coins writes the four numbers on paper and puts them in order. No writing is needed, no marks.
