Explore: change

A sweet costs 40¢. You pay with a 50¢ coin.

A ruler costs $6. You pay with a $10 note.

Count up from the price to the money you paid.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 3 minutes. Change is the money the shop gives back. We count up from the price to the money we paid, putting down a coin or note at each step. Start at 40 cents and put down coins until you reach 50 cents: a 10-cent coin is the change. Then the ruler: start at $6 and put down two-dollar notes or dollar tokens until you reach $10 (two $2 notes, or four $1 tokens): the change is $4. Ask: how much change for the sweet (10 cents); for the ruler ($4). The tags show the prices only; the changes are never on the slide.
