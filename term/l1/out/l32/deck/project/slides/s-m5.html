ITEM 1

A picture has 3 circles and 2 squares. How many shapes are there?

ITEM 2

Can 2 squares make a triangle? Say Yes or No.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Two quick items, answered on the children's own paper or said quietly on three (say so aloud). Item 1: 3 circles and 2 squares make 5 shapes in all. Item 2: 2 squares cannot make a triangle: put 2 squares side by side and you get a rectangle. Walk the cameras and wait 10 seconds before helping. Nobody holds paper up. The next slide shows the answers and children tick their own work. Answers: 5; No.
