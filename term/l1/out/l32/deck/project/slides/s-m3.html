Count each kind. Which kind is there most of?

How many triangles? How many circles? How many squares?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A row of shapes to count by kind. Count the triangles one at a time, touching each (children point to their screen), then the circles, then the squares. Say a method: count one kind at a time, and make a mark so that you never count one twice. Ask: how many triangles, how many circles, how many squares, how many shapes in all, which kind is most. The answer appears on the next slide. Answers: 4 triangles, 1 circle, 1 square: 6 in all; the triangles are the most.
