12–24 MIN

Explore: your own picture

1Make a picture with your pieces: a robot, a rocket or a boat.

2Use at least 3 different kinds of shape.

3Count how many of each shape you used.

4Say it: My picture has ... squares and ... triangles.

Keep it on the table. Say it. Nobody holds it up.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 3 minutes. Children make any picture they like with up to 8 pieces. They count each kind and say it aloud to a grown-up or to the screen: my picture has 2 squares, 3 triangles and a circle. Volunteers say their count; nobody holds a figure up to the camera and nobody is compared. Ask: which kind did you use most; how many pieces in all. A child who needs a start builds a robot: a rectangle body, a square head and 2 triangle feet. Praise any picture; the point is counting by kind. No answers on the slide.
