Shape moves

Stand up. Make each shape I call.

Triangle. Rectangle. Circle.

Sip some water.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up with a little clear space. Call the shapes one at a time and children make them with their arms and body: a triangle (hands together above the head like a roof), a rectangle (stand tall and then stretch the arms out wide), a circle (arms in a big ring in front), a square (elbows out). Then call 2 shapes at once: make a triangle with one arm and a circle with the other. A child may stay seated and make the shapes with their hands. No equipment, no running. Do not give prizes or compare children.
