Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 5 minutes. The house is drawn on dots; each square on the dot grid is one square piece and each slanted half-square is one triangle. Children build the house on the table with their pieces, check it matches the picture, and say which pieces they used: 2 squares for the bottom and 2 triangles for the roof, 4 pieces in all. Ask: which pieces did you use; how many of each; how did you know the roof needs triangles (the slanted sides). The answer is on the next slide. Some children use 1 rectangle for the bottom: that works and is a good idea, so ask whether it uses more or fewer pieces; the fewest-pieces question comes back at Go further. Answers: 2 squares and 2 triangles, 4 pieces.
