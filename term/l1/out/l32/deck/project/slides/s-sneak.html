8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

Look at the picture. How many triangles are there?

PUZZLE 2: think

Put 2 triangles from your kit together so that whole sides touch. Name one shape you can make. Draw it.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 is an access question: any child can count the triangles in a row of 5 pictures (the 2 triangles are different colours and one has stripes, so the count does not depend on colour). Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: 2 triangles put together make a square, a bigger triangle or a slanted four-sided shape, depending on which sides touch. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write and draw on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera. There is no private chat. Answers: 2; many answers are right. Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
