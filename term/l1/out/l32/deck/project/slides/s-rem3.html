Count by rows

How many squares make the rectangle? Count by rows.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A rectangle made of 2 rows of 3 squares. Children count the squares row by row: 3, then 3 more, 6 in all. This is the array idea from Term 2 Lesson 5, taught again so that counting squares by rows is easy later. Ask: how many in each row, how many rows, how many in all. The slide never shows the count. A child who is unsure points to each square and counts in ones. Answer: 6.
