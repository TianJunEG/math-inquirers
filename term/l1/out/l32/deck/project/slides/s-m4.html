A rectangle can replace 2 squares

4 SQUARES

make a long rectangle.

That is 4 pieces.

2 RECTANGLES

make the same long rectangle.

That is 2 pieces.

A rectangle is as long as 2 squares.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The same figure can be made with different pieces. Show 4 squares in a row, and then the same row made from 2 rectangles of your kit, each 2 squares long. Ask: how many pieces with squares (4); how many pieces with rectangles (2); which uses fewer pieces (the rectangles). Say: a rectangle in our kit is as long as 2 squares. This is the idea of the fewest pieces, which comes back at Go further. Children lay their pieces on the table to check. Answers: 4 pieces; 2 pieces.
