Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Three ways that 2 pieces make 1 shape, with whole sides touching. 2 squares side by side make a rectangle: show it with 2 kit squares. 2 half circles with their straight sides together make a circle: show it with the 2 half circles. 2 triangles make a square or a bigger triangle, as in Explore. Children try each with their pieces. Ask: what do 2 squares make (a rectangle); what do 2 half circles make (a circle); what do 2 triangles make (it depends on which sides touch). Say: the shapes must touch with whole sides. The pictures show the made shape.
