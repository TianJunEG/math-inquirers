Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Now we look inside a figure. The house is made of shapes: the roof is a triangle, the wall and the door are rectangles, the window is a square. Ask each question, then wait: how many triangles (1), how many rectangles (2), how many squares (1). Children point to each shape on their own screen and count aloud on three. The answer appears on the next slide, after the children have answered. A child who says 2 squares is asked whether the window is as tall as it is wide. Answers: 1 triangle; 2 rectangles; 1 square. Do not name a child with a wrong answer.
