8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

Ravi has 6 red beads and 4 blue beads.

Siti has 5 red beads and 5 blue beads.

Do they have the same number of beads? Draw dots to help.

PUZZLE 2: think

8 + 5 = 9 + ___

What number goes in the blank? Write how you know.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 is an access question: any child can try it by drawing dots and counting. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson's key idea. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Everyone holds the paper to the camera once, at the same moment, after puzzle 2; there is no private chat and no board in front of the group. Answers: yes, 10 each; the blank is 4. Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list of who found it easy.
