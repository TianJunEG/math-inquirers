Bigger numbers, same idea

25 + 18 = 27 + ___

25 went up to 27: 2 more.

So the blank is 2 less than 18.

The blank is 16.

Check: 25 + 18 = 43 and 27 + 16 = 43.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example before the Start here page. Do not add first: look at how the number moved. 25 became 27, so 2 more. The other number must go down by 2: 18 becomes 16. Then check by adding both sides if the group is ready: both are 43. Ask: what number is two less than eighteen? Answer: sixteen.
