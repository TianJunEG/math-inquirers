12–28 MIN

Explore: two plates

1Put your two plates side by side: LEFT and RIGHT.

2Left plate: a pile of 6 and a pile of 4.

3Right plate: a pile of 5, and a second pile.

4Make the plates match. How many in the second pile?

WHAT IF?

What if the left plate has 7 and 3? What if it has 9 and 2?

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 5 minutes. Objects first, no writing yet. Children build the left plate, count it, then build the right plate so that it matches. Ask a few children to show their plates to the camera. Answers: 5 (6 + 4 = 5 + 5), then 2 (7 + 3 = 8 + 2), then 3 (9 + 2 = 8 + 3). The what-if card is available from the start so nobody waits, and no child is named for choosing it. A child with too few things can use the kit squares.
