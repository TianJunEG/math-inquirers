Equals means balance

LEFT SIDE

5 + 3

RIGHT SIDE

4 + 4

5 + 3 = 4 + 4. Both sides make 8.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Show the two plates as a balanced see-saw. Both sides hold the same, so we write an equals sign between them. Ask: what does the equals sign mean? Answer: the two sides are the same amount. Say it is not a sign that the answer comes next.
