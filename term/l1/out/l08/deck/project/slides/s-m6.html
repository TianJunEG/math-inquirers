Quick-fire: is it balanced?

6 + 5 = 7 + 48 + 3 = 9 + 320 + 14 = 22 + 12

Thumbs up: balanced. Thumbs down: not balanced.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: yes (6 up 1 to 7 and 5 down 1 to 4), no (8 up 1 to 9 but 3 stays the same, so the right side is 1 too big), yes (20 up 2 to 22 and 14 down 2 to 12). Children answer with thumbs; nobody writes. Ask how do you know after each one, and repeat a volunteer's reason in your own words.
