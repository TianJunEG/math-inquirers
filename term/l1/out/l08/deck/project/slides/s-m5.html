Same total? Do not add yet

SAME TOTAL

47 + 18 and 45 + 20

47 goes down 2. 18 goes up 2.

NOT THE SAME

29 + 14 and 30 + 15

29 goes up 1. 14 goes up 1.

One number goes down and one goes up by the same amount. The total stays the same.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example for Think harder. 47 to 45 is 2 less, and 18 to 20 is 2 more: the 2 moved across, so the totals are the same (65 each). 29 to 30 is 1 more and 14 to 15 is 1 more: both went up, so the total went up (43 and 45). Ask: same total or different, and how do you know? Keep to about two minutes; it is a worked example, not a skill to drill.
