Different routes

A route goes only right or up. This route is R, U, U. Find another route.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. The counter goes from S to E and may only move right (R) or up (U). The grid is 1 step across and 2 steps up, so every route has one R and two U. The picture shows R U U. Ask: is there another route; how can we be sure we have found them all. Give time, then show the three routes on the next slide. The way to be sure: the single R can be first, second or third. Children draw the routes on route grids; they may rub out and use the same grid again. A child may write the letters instead of drawing.
