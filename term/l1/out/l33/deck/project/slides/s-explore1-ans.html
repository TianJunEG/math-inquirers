Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Children check their own copy against the picture. Both pictures are 2 steps across and 3 steps up, with the point of the roof on the middle dot of the top row. Say: put your finger on the bottom left corner of the house and count the steps along the bottom, then up the side. Children who were one step too wide counted the dots, not the steps: say that is what we will look at in the mini-lesson. Do not name any child. Ticks only, no marks.
