Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Take one or two answers from volunteers. Key ideas: we counted steps between the dots (not the dots); the dots are the places and the steps are the jumps; the directions are right and up. If a child says we counted dots, ask: how many dots are in the bottom row of the house, and how many steps long is it (3 dots, 2 steps). Repeat each child answer in your own words so the recording picks it up. Do not give the rule before the children say it; the mini-lesson makes it exact.
