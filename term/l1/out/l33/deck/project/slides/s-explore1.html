Explore: copy it exactly

Copy the house on your copy grid. Make it exactly the same.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 5 minutes. Each child has the grid page (page 6 of the book) and a pencil. The slide shows a house on a dot grid, 2 steps across and 3 steps up. Children copy it on the right-hand side of a copy grid (the grid with the dashed line); they may use a ruler or the edge of a card for straight lines. Walk the cameras. The picture is not shown with its copy until the next slide. Ask: how did you copy it exactly; what did you count. Listen for counting the dots (a copy that is a step too wide) and for counting the steps. Do not give the rule yet. A child with no printed grid draws rows of dots on paper: 4 dots across, 4 dots down. Nobody holds a drawing up to the camera: children check their own copy on the next slide.
