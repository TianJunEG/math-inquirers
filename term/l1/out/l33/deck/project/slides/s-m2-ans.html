The answer for the example on the slide before. A is the start. B is 3 steps right of A. C is 2 steps up from A. Then join A to B, B to C and C to A. Read it aloud so that children can tick their own copy, and say what we counted. The line from C to B slants: it goes from dot to dot like the others.
