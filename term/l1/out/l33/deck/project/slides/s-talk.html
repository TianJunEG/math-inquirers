TWO CHILDREN WALK FROM S

Ali goes 3 right, then 2 up.

Mei goes 2 up, then 3 right.

Do they stop at the same dot? Do they walk the same number of steps?

I think ... because ...

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

One minute of talk to the grown-up next to you, or to your screen. Volunteers first; a pass is always fine; never call a child by name to answer. Model answer: yes, they stop at the same dot, and both walk 5 steps. Both go 3 right and 2 up; only the order is different. The routes are different but the start and the end are the same. Link to Explore and the Investigation: different routes, the same number of steps. Repeat each child answer in your own words for the recording. The names in the story are for the story only: nobody in the group is a mover.
