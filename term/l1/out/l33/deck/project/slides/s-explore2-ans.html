The line your button walked

Right, up, right, up. The button is on the top right dot.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Children check their own line. The path goes 2 right, then 2 up, then 1 right, then 1 up and ends on the top right dot E. In all that is 3 right and 3 up. Say: we said the moves in order, and the order matters for the line, but look at where the button stopped. Do not ask about other routes yet. Ticks only.
