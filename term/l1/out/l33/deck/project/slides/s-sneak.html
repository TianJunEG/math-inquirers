8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

The counter is on S.

It must reach E.

Write: ___ right, ___ up.

PUZZLE 2: think

The counter goes from S to E.

It moves only right or up.

How many different routes? I know because ___.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 is an access question: S is at the bottom left, E is 3 dots to the right and 1 up, so the answer is 3 right, 1 up. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: on a grid 1 step across and 1 step up the counter can go right then up, or up then right: 2 routes. A child who writes 'right then up' and 'up then right' has done well. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera. There is no private chat. Answers: 3 right, 1 up; 2 routes. Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
