Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional, only if you are on time. The boxes stand for the corners of a grid 1 step across and 2 steps up, with S in the bottom left box. Each box says how many routes reach that corner. The bottom row and the left column are 1 (only one way to get there). The empty top right box is the box on its left plus the box below it: 1 + 2 = 3 routes, the same 3 routes as in the mini-lesson. Ask: what goes in the empty box (3). Take two answers and move on. This links to counting the routes: adding the two corners before it gives the number of routes to a corner.
