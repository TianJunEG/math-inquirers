Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera with a little clear space. Call directions for the body: two small steps to the right, one big reach up, one small step to the left, crouch down, and so on. Then call a short route with the body: right, right, up, up and ask children to say where they are on an imaginary grid. Say right and up clearly, and use the screen as the front so left and right match what children see on camera. No equipment, no running. A child may stay seated and move one arm. Do not give prizes or compare children.
