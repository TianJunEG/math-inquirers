Explore: walk your button

Put your button on the bottom left dot of a route grid.

1Go 2 right.

2Go 2 up.

3Go 1 right.

4Go 1 up.

Walk the button and draw the line as you go.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 4 minutes. Each child has a route grid on the grid page and a button (or small coin) as the counter. Read the four directions one at a time, slowly, and wait for children to move and draw before the next: go 2 right, go 2 up, go 1 right, go 1 up. Then ask where the button is now. The button ends on the top right dot (3 right and 3 up in all). Walk the cameras: you can see the counters on the table. The line is drawn on the next slide. Nobody holds a drawing up to the camera. A child without a button puts a finger on the dot, or makes a small cross with a pencil.
