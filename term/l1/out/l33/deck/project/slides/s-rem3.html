Right and up

The counter is in the yellow square. Move it 1 right, then 1 up. Which square?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Right and up on a grid of squares (Term 1 Lesson 11, position words, taught again). The counter is in the bottom left square (yellow). Right is along the row, up is up the column. Move 1 right and then 1 up: the counter is in the middle square. Ask: which square is it in (the middle square). A child who is unsure moves a finger along the picture. Your turn: from the middle square move 1 more right; which square (the middle square of the right-hand column). Children answer aloud by pointing or saying. No writing.
