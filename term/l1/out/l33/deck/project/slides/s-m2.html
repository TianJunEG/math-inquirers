Copy corner by corner

Corner A is the start, at the bottom left. How far right is corner B? How far up is corner C?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. A triangle with three corners. Start at the bottom left corner A. Corner B is on the same row, 3 steps to the right. Corner C is straight above A, 2 steps up. Ask: how many steps right is B (3); how many steps up is C (2). The answer is on the next slide. Say: to copy a figure, mark each corner by counting steps from the start, then join the corners. Then the your-turn: copy the triangle on your copy grid: mark A, mark B, mark C, join.
