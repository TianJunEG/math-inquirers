Build from the top view

Build the stack with your blocks. How many cubes are there in all?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. The top view says: the back left stack has 3 cubes, the other three stacks have 1 cube each. Children build it with blocks, or put buttons on a drawn 2 by 2 grid, and count: 3 and 1 and 1 and 1 make 6 cubes. The answer is on the next slide. Say: a top view tells us how many cubes without seeing the side. If a child builds a stack of 3 and forgets the others, ask: what does each number say?
