Count every cube

The top view says how many cubes are in each stack. How many cubes can you see in the picture? How many are there in all?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. The slide shows a stack on a 2 by 2 base and its top view. The top view numbers add to 7 cubes; one cube is hidden behind the front right tower, so 6 can be seen in the picture. Ask: how many cubes can you see (6); how many cubes are there in all (7). The answer is on the next slide. Say: a cube we cannot see is still a cube. Children may build it with blocks to check.
