Explore: fold and open

1Fold your paper square in half. Make the edges meet.

2Make one pencil dot on the folded paper, away from the fold.

3How many dots will there be when you open it?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 4 minutes. Each child has a paper square from the kit page (cut out by a grown-up beforehand). Read the three steps one at a time. Children fold the square in half so two edges meet, press the fold, and make one pencil dot on the folded paper, away from the fold edge. No punching: pencil only. Ask: how many dots will you see when you open it. Take guesses, then children open the paper. Nobody holds the paper up to the camera: children say what they see. A child with no square folds any sheet of paper.
