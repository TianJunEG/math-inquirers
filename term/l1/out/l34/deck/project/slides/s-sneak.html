8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

How many cubes are in the picture?

Write the number.

PUZZLE 2: think

The stack has 5 cubes.

The picture shows only 4.

Where is the 5th cube? I know because ___.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 is an access question: 2 cubes side by side and 2 on top, a wall of 4 cubes: the answer is 4. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: the stack has 5 cubes but the picture shows only 4, because one cube is hidden behind the front tower. A child who says 'it is behind the tall tower' has done well. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera. There is no private chat. Answers: 4; the 5th cube is hidden behind the front tower. Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
