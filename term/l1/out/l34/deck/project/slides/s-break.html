Fold and unfold break

Stand up. Fold your arms in half. Unfold them.

Make a tall stack with your arms. Make a flat one.

Sip some water.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera with a little clear space. Call movements: fold your arms in half, open them, stand tall like a stack of 3, crouch like a stack of 1, make a square with your arms. Then fold in half again and again, with the body bending, and say how many layers: 2, then 4 (each fold doubles the layers). No equipment, no running. A child may stay seated. Do not give prizes or compare children.
