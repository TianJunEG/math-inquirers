Fold once, punch once

A paper is folded in half. The fold is on the left. One hole is punched. How many holes when it is opened?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. The picture shows the folded paper with one hole punched through both layers. The folded edge is on the left. Ask: how many holes will there be when it is opened (2). Where will they be (one on each side of the middle line, the same distance from it). The answer is on the next slide. Children use their paper square: fold, make one pencil dot, open it, check. No real punching.
