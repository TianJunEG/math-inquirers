Same distance from the fold

The paper is folded along the dashed line. One hole is ringed. Where is the other hole?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. A dot grid stands for the opened paper, and the dashed line is the fold. The ringed hole is 2 steps to the left of the dashed line. Ask: how many steps from the dashed line is the ringed hole (2); where is the other hole (2 steps to the right of the dashed line, on the same row). The answer is on the next slide.
