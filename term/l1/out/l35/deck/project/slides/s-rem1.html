Warm-up: two lines

Two lines made of squares. Which line is longer? How many squares longer?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Comparing two lengths with squares (holiday measuring, taught again). The top line is 5 squares and the bottom line is 7 squares: the bottom line is longer, by 2 squares. Ask: which line is longer (the bottom one), how many squares longer (2). A child who is unsure counts the squares in each line with a finger. Your turn, no picture: a line of 4 squares and a line of 9 squares; which is longer, and by how many (the second, by 5). Children answer aloud. No writing.
