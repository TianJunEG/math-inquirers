Copy and change break

Stand up. Copy my move.

Now copy it, with one thing different.

Sip some water.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera with a little clear space. Make a slow move (arms up, arms out, one foot forward). Children copy it. Then change one thing (for example one arm lower) and say: what is different? Children say it aloud, then copy the new move. Do three or four moves. Say the five things: how many (arms), how big (a big step or a small step), which way (turn to face the side). No equipment, no running. A child may stay seated and move one arm. Do not give prizes or compare children.
