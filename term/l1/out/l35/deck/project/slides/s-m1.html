Five things to check

LOOK AT ONE THING AT A TIME

What shape is it?

How many are there?

How big is it?

Which pattern: plain, stripes or dots?

Which way does it face?

Two triangles. What is the same? What is different?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. The five things to check are shape, number, size, pattern and turn (which way it faces). The slide shows two triangles, A and B. They are the same in shape, number, size and pattern. Triangle B faces another way: it is turned. Ask: what is the same (shape, number, size, pattern); what is different (which way it faces). The answer is on the next slide. Say: a shape turned is still the same shape; it only faces another way.
