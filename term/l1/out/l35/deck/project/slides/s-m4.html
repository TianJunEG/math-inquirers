A pattern that repeats

The pattern keeps going. Which shape is tile 9? Count in threes: 3, 6, 9.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. The pattern repeats every 3 tiles: square, square, circle. The circles are tiles 3, 6, 9 (counting in threes), so tile 9 is a circle. Ask: what repeats (square, square, circle); which tiles are circles (3 and 6, then 9); which shape is tile 9 (circle). The answer is on the next slide. Say: a pattern that repeats has a part that comes again and again; find the part first.
