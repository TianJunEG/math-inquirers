Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 4 minutes. Two pictures of three tiles each. Picture 2 is like Picture 1 except for three things, one in each tile: tile 1 is bigger (size), tile 2 has 4 stars instead of 3 (number), tile 3 has dots instead of stripes (pattern). Children look at one tile at a time: say aloud what is different, using the words size, number, pattern. Answers on the next slide. Nobody points on screen: children say it aloud or show fingers for the tile number.
