Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

Count the fish in each row.

Write the two numbers.

PUZZLE 2: think

Three of the four triangles are alike.

One is different. Which one is it?

I know because ___.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 is an access question: 4 fish in the top row and 5 in the bottom row. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: three triangles point up and one (C) points down, so it is turned the other way. A child who says it is upside down or that it points the other way has done well. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera. There is no private chat. Answers: 4 and 5; C, it points the other way. Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
