Kit page: pair cards

A grown-up cuts out the 12 cards before the lesson. Each row of 4 is one set: three cards are alike and one is different. Keep each row together in a pile. No cutting? Draw 4 small pictures on paper: 3 the same and 1 different.

Print this page on its own sheet, or on one side only, so cutting never spoils another page. A grown-up cuts; children do not use scissors. Keep the pieces in an envelope with your name. We use them again.
