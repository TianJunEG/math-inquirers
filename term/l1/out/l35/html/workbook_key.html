Answer key: Lesson 5, Spot It

For the tutor and grown-ups. Keep this page away from the child until they have finished and checked their own work. Ask how do you know? before saying right or wrong.

Start here (pages 1 and 2)
S1. Look at Picture 1 and Picture 2. What is different in each tile? Circle one word.
Answer: size; number; turn
Tile 1 is a star in both pictures, but it is bigger in Picture 2, so the difference is size. Tile 2 has 3 squares and then 4 squares, so the difference is number. Tile 3 is the same triangle, but it faces down in Picture 2, so the difference is turn.
Watch for: Saying the shape is different when it is only turned.; Saying the pattern is different when it is the same.
S2. Are the two pictures the same?
Answer: Yes; No
Picture A and Picture B have the same tiles: 3 stars and one big striped circle, so they are the same. Picture C has 4 triangles and Picture D has 3 triangles, so they are not the same.
Watch for: Saying No because the pictures are side by side.; Not checking the number of triangles.
S3. Three tiles are alike. One is the odd one out.
Answer: C; pattern
A, B and D are big squares with stripes. Tile C is a big square with dots. It has the same shape, number and size, so the pattern is different.
Watch for: Choosing the tile that is in a different place.; Saying size when the squares are the same size.
S4. Shelf 1 is on the left. Shelf 2 is on the right.
Answer: 1 more apple
Shelf 1 has 3 cookies and 2 apples. Shelf 2 has 3 cookies and 3 apples. The cookies are the same. Shelf 2 has 1 more apple.
Watch for: Saying 3 for the number of apples.; Saying the cookies are different.
S5. Look at Picture 1 and Picture 2.
Answer: 3; 1
Tile 1 is the same. Tile 2 has 3 stars and then 4 stars (number). Tile 3 is smaller in Picture 2 (size). Tile 4 faces another way (turn). That makes 3 differences.
Watch for: Counting 4 differences because there are 4 tiles.; Missing the turned triangle.
S6. Two of the four tiles are the same picture.
Answer: A and D
A and D are both stars with dots. B is a star with stripes (the pattern is different). C is a triangle (the shape is different). So A and D are the same.
Watch for: Choosing A and B because both are stars.; Not checking the pattern.
Think harder (page 3)
T1. Four of the five tiles have one thing the same. One tile is the odd one out.
Answer: C; number
Look at how many shapes are in each tile. A, B, D and E each have 3. Tile C has only 1. So C is the odd one out and the difference is number. The shapes, sizes and patterns are all mixed, so they cannot be what makes a tile odd.
Watch for: Choosing the tile with the different shape.; Choosing the biggest tile.
T2. Look across, then look down. Which tile is the missing piece?
Answer: C
Across, 1 shape becomes 3 shapes. Down, a square becomes a circle. So the missing tile is 3 circles, the same size. That is tile C. A has only 1 circle, B has squares, D has small circles.
Watch for: Choosing A because the circle is on the left.; Choosing D because it looks neat.
T3. Two of the four tiles are the same picture, turned. Do not choose a tile that is bigger or has another pattern.
Answer: A and C
A and C are big triangles with no pattern. C is A turned upside down. B has dots, so the pattern is different. D is small, so the size is different. So the answer is A and C.
Watch for: Choosing A and B because both are big.; Choosing A and D because both look plain.
T4. A pattern of tiles repeats again and again.
Answer: circle; 10
The part that repeats is square, circle, circle. The squares are tiles 1, 4, 7 and 10, so the 4th square is tile 10. Tile 11 comes after tile 10, so it is the first circle of the next part: a circle.
Watch for: Saying tile 11 is a square.; Saying the 4th square is tile 4.
Go further (page 4)
G1. Shadow Match. Look at the shadow on top. One of the four shadows below it is the same shape, turned.
Answer: B
The top shadow is a tall L. Turn it a quarter turn to the right and the long part lies flat along the top, with the foot hanging under the left end. That is shadow B. Shadow A is the L flipped like a mirror: its foot points the other way, and a turn cannot do that. Shadow C has 5 squares and shadow D is a different shape.
Watch for: Choosing the flipped shadow because it looks nearly the same.; Choosing the shadow with more squares.
G2. Look at the five tiles. Check one thing at a time.
Answer: D; B
Look only at the pattern: A, B, C and E have stripes and D has dots, so D is the odd one out. Look only at the size: A, C, D and E are medium and B is big, so B is the odd one out. A row can have a different odd one out for each thing you check.
Watch for: Choosing the same tile for both.; Looking at the pattern and the size together.
G3. Make your own odd one out for a partner. Draw 4 tiles in a row. Three tiles are alike. One is different in just one thing. Many answers are right.
Answer: Many answers are right: three tiles alike and one different in exactly one thing
For example, draw 3 small striped circles and 1 big striped circle. The odd one is different in its size. Check that it differs in just one thing, and that the other three are the same in everything.
Watch for: Changing two things on the odd tile.; Making the three tiles different from each other.
Investigation (page 5): Change just one thing
The bigger circle is different in size only. A tile with two things different, for example a big circle with dots, is different in size and pattern. Many answers are right.
Look for: A child who names one thing for the first tile (size) and two different things for the second tile, such as size and pattern. Credit any clear drawing with the right count of differences. A child who changes the shape and the number has found two things too.
Exit ticket (page 5)
E1. Look at the two tiles.
Answer: pattern
Both tiles have 3 circles of the same size. One has dots and one has stripes, so the pattern is different.
Watch for: Saying number because there are 3 circles.; Saying shape.
E2. Three tiles are alike. One is the odd one out.
Answer: C
A, B and D are circles. Tile C is a star. So C is the odd one out.
Watch for: Choosing D because it is last.; Saying there is no odd one out.
E3. Look at Picture 1 and Picture 2.
Answer: 2
Tile 1 faces another way (turn). Tile 3 is smaller (size). Tile 2 is the same. So there are 2 differences.
Watch for: Counting 3 differences.; Missing the turned triangle.
