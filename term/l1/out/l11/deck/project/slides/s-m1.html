REMEMBER

Say first, second, third, fourth ... tenth.

Only 1st, 2nd and 3rd end differently.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Read the places with the children: first, second, third, fourth, fifth, sixth, seventh, eighth, ninth, tenth. The short forms end in st, nd, rd or th. Most end in th. Watch for 2th and 3th written for 2nd and 3rd. Ask: what is after 7th (8th)? What is before 10th (9th)? Stop at 10th today: 11th and 12th are not needed.
