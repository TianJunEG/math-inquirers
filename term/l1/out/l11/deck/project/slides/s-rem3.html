Front, back, behind, between

The door is on the left. The front is next to the door.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Point to the door on the picture. The child nearest the door (Ali) is at the front. The child farthest from the door (Jenny) is at the back. Wei is behind Kumar. Wei is between Kumar and Jenny. Then each child lines up four small things the same way on the table. The four words front, back, behind and between are taught here once with a picture and once with real things.
