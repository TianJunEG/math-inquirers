Ten and some more

10 and 3 more is ___

10 + 620 + 430 + 7

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 13, 16, 24, 37. Say the tens word slowly, then the ones: twenty... four. This is the bridge to bundles of ten and singles. Show 20 and 30 as two or three full ten-frames if a child is unsure.
