Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Read the four questions aloud, one at a time, and show where the picture is. Remind children that right behind means the very next place. Question 5 asks for the place counted from the back; children may line up six things and count from the other end. Question 7 asks how many children are in front, not the place. Wait 10 seconds before helping.
