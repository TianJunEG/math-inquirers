How many are in front?

BEN IS 5TH

The 4 children in the rings are in front of Ben.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Ben is 5th. The four children in the rings are in front of him: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th. The place is 5th but the number in front is 4. Ask: if Ben is 6th, how many are in front? 5. Say the rule in words: the number in front is one less than the place. This is the idea behind sneak-peek puzzle 2.
