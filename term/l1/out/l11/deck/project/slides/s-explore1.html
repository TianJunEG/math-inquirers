14–28 MIN

Explore: grab and bundle

1Grab a handful of small things. Not the whole pile.

2Make bundles of ten: a hair tie, a cup, or a ring on paper.

3Count the bundles. Count the singles left over.

4Say: I have ___ tens and ___ ones.

WHAT IF?

What if you open a bundle? Can you show your number a different way?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 4 minutes. Objects first, no writing yet. Children bundle in tens and count the singles. A child who wants to may show their bundles to the camera. The what-if card is available from the start so nobody waits: it previews the Think harder idea (opening a bundle), and no child is named for choosing it. A child with too few things can use the kit bars and squares.
