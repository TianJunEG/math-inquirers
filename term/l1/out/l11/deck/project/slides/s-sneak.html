10–14 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

Draw 5 dots in a line.

The door is on the left.

Colour the 2nd dot.

PUZZLE 2: think

Mei is 4th in the line.

Ben stands right behind Mei.

How many children are in front of Ben? Write the number.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 is an access question: any child can draw dots and colour one. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: the number in front is one less than the place. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper and keep it. Nobody holds paper to the camera and there is no private chat. Answers: the dot after the one next to the door; 4. Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
