3 tens and 4 ones

30 and 4 more is 34. The left digit counts the tens. The right digit counts the ones.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Think aloud: count the bundles in tens (10, 20, 30) then the singles (31, 32, 33, 34). Write 3 in the tens box and 4 in the ones box. Ask: which digit tells me how many bundles? Answer: the left one, 3.
