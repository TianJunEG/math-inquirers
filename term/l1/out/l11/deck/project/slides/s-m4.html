CLUES

Aisha is 2nd.

Right behind means the very next place.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Ask: who is 3rd? Kumar. Then say Wei stands right behind Kumar and ask the place of Wei: 4th. Right behind means the very next place after. Keep to one clue at a time. The answers are not on the slide: children answer on three or whisper to a grown-up first.
