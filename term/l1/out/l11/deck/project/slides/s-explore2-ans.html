How many? Which place?

Look at your row. How many things? Which place is the last one?

HOW MANY?

There are 6 children.

The number 6 says how many.

WHICH PLACE?

The last child is 6th.

The word 6th says which one.

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 5 minutes. Children put the place cards 1st to 6th under their six things (the top two rows of the kit page) and the number card 6 at the end of the row (the bottom two rows). Ask: how many things? 6. What place is the last thing? 6th. Then: touch the thing with the 4th card. What place is it, and how many things are in the whole row? 4th, and 6. The number says how many; the place says which one. The cards on the answer slide stay hidden until the children have answered. In a row the place of the last thing is the same as the number of things, but the two words do different jobs. (answer shown after the children have answered)
