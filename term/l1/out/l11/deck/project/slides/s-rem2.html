Just before, just after

Which numbers go in the empty boxes?

Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The 6 is marked. The two boxes beside it are the number just before and the number just after. Answers: 5 and 7, then 10 for just after 9. Children answer with fingers or a whispered number on three. Nobody writes. This reminds children that a place in a line is next to the places on both sides.
