Math Inquirers Term 2 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 3 minutes, main room. Call each number aloud and show it on the slide. Children build with the kit bars (tens) and squares (ones) and give a thumbs-up. Read the check line aloud so every child can check their own build; nobody holds anything up to the camera. Keep the builds to three: 17, 30 (the zero case) and 58. Answers: 17 is 1 bar and 7 squares; 30 is 3 bars; 58 is 5 bars and 8 squares. The picture of 23 is only an example. Camera tip: a child may tilt the screen so the table can be seen, but this is not required.
