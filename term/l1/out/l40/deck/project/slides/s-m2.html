A puzzle with a rule

MI'S PUZZLE

Two numbers add up to 9.

One number is 3 more than the other.

What are the two numbers?

List every pair that makes 9. Then find the pair that fits.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example, a puzzle with a rule. Children list every pair of whole numbers that make 9 on their own paper, starting with 0 and 9, and find the pair whose difference is 3. Ask: what are the two numbers (3 and 6). The answer is on the next slide. Say: listing every pair shows that no other pair works. That is a proof. A child who writes 6 and 3 and stops has the answer: ask, how do you know there is not another pair?
