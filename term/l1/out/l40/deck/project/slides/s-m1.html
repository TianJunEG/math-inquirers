MY IDEA

My favourite idea is counting by rows.

It works like this: count one row, then count on by rows.

Here is an example: 3 rows of 4 squares. 4, 8, 12.

Check: 3 rows of 4 is 4 + 4 + 4 = 12.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Modelled teaching, about 90 seconds. The frame has four lines: my favourite idea is; it works like this; here is an example; check. Read the model aloud, pointing to the rows of the grid: 4, 8, 12. Say: you will use these four lines in the Showcase; you may use your favourite page to help you. A pass is always fine and you may teach your grown-up instead. Say: your voice is recorded if you are not muted. Do not say best or better about any idea.
