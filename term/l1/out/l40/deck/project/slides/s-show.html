Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The Showcase, about 11 minutes. Volunteers teach one favourite idea from the year in about a minute with the frame on the slide; they may hold a page up to themselves, not to the camera, and may read from their page. Rules, said aloud before anyone starts: a pass is always fine; you may teach your grown-up instead (microphone muted) and tell us I taught my grown-up; your voice is recorded if you are not muted; everyone else listens, and afterwards may say one thing they learned; no voting, no best, no ranking, no counting of claps. Take up to five volunteers, one at a time, volunteers first; repeat each idea in your own words so the recording picks it up. Puzzles are shared inside the group only. Nobody collects a puzzle.
