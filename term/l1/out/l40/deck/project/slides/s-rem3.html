A STORY WITH TWO STEPS

Mei has 7 stickers.

She gets 5 more stickers.

Then she gives 4 stickers away.

How many stickers does Mei have now?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A story with two steps (Term 1 Lesson 10, taught again). Step 1: 7 and 5 make 12. Step 2: 12 take away 4 is 8. Ask: how many stickers does Mei have now (8). A child who is unsure acts it out with counters or fingers. Your turn, no new picture: Ben has 9 sweets, eats 3, then buys 6 more: how many now (12). Children answer aloud. No writing. Say: that was three ideas we know. Today we teach one.
