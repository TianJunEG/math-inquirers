A good puzzle

A has no question. B has many answers. C is good: a story, numbers, one question and one answer.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The answer for round 1. Puzzle A has numbers but no question. Puzzle B has no single answer: any even number from 2 to 18 fits. Puzzle C has a story, numbers and one question, and exactly one answer (9). Say: a good puzzle has a story, numbers, one question and one answer. Children tick their own thinking with a tick. Do not name any child.
