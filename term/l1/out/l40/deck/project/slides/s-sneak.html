8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Your paper is not collected.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

Ali has 6 pens.

Mei has 4 pens.

Write a question for this puzzle.

PUZZLE 2: think

Write a number bigger than 3 and smaller than 8.

Is this a good puzzle? Circle Yes or No.

I know because ___.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 is an access question: Ali has 6 pens and Mei has 4 pens, and the child writes a question (how many pens in all, how many more pens does Ali have than Mei: many questions are right). Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: a good puzzle has exactly one answer, and this one has four (4, 5, 6 and 7). A child who writes 5 has answered it: that is a good start, do not correct it now. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; your paper is not collected. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera. There is no private chat. Answers: many questions are right; No, many numbers fit. Do not reveal puzzle 2 until the Showcase. Do not write scores or keep a list.
