I remember: a pattern

Count in 3s. What are the next two numbers?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A pattern (Term 1 Lesson 4 and Term 2 Lesson 16, taught again with a picture). The numbers go 3, 6, 9, 12 and then two empty boxes. Ask: what are the next two numbers (15 and 18). Then ask for volunteers: who remembers a pattern from this year? A pass is always fine. A child who is unsure counts on fingers, three at a time. Your turn, no new picture: count in 3s from 18 (21, 24). Children answer aloud. No writing.
