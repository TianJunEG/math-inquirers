Before you share a puzzle

1It has a story, numbers and one question.

2It has exactly one answer.

3You checked it a second way.

4It is your own puzzle: not copied.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Read the four checks aloud. Say: these are the four checks for the puzzle in the workbook and for the Showcase. Say: a puzzle is shared inside our group only, unless your grown-up says yes. Take one answer to: which check would you do first. Do not collect puzzles and do not compile them. About 1 minute.
