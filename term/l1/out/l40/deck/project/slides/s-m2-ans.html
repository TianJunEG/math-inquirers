Only one pair fits

Only 3 and 6 differ by 3. The answer is 3 and 6, and no other pair works.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The answer for the example on the slide before. The five pairs are 0 and 9, 1 and 8, 2 and 7, 3 and 6, 4 and 5; the differences are 9, 7, 5, 3 and 1. Only 3 and 6 differ by 3. Read it aloud so that children can tick their own work. Say: we listed every case, so the answer must be right and it is the only answer.
