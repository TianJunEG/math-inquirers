Explore: write your own puzzle

1Pick two numbers up to 20.

2Write a short story with a name.

3Write one question.

4Solve it.

5Check it a second way.

Use your own paper. Then tell your quest partner your puzzle.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 5 minutes. Read the five steps one at a time. Children write their own puzzle on their own paper: two numbers up to 20, a short story with a name, one question. They solve it, then check it a second way (a different method or working backwards). The quest partner may solve it quietly. Walk the cameras and ask how do you know. A child who needs help starts with two numbers and a name: Mei has 6 and Ben has 3. A child who writes a puzzle with two answers: ask, how many answers does it have? Puzzles stay inside the group: nobody shares a puzzle outside the group unless the grown-up agrees. Nobody holds paper to the camera.
