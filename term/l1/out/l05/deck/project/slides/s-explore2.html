Your turn: make a ten

Use your ten-frame. For each sum, say how many you move, then ten and ___.

9 + 47 + 56 + 89 + 7

I move ..., so it is ten and ...

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 5 minutes, main room. Call each sum aloud and show it on the slide. Children build it on their frame and say how many they moved. Answers: 9 + 4: move 1, ten and 3 (13). 7 + 5: move 3, ten and 2 (12). 6 + 8: fill the 8, move 2, ten and 4 (14). 9 + 7: move 1, ten and 6 (16). On 6 + 8 some children fill the 6 (move 4): that is fine and works too; it is just more moves. Do not name children.
