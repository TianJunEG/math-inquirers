Spot the pair: 3 + 8 + 7

387

Which two numbers make 10? They do not have to be next to each other.

3 + 7 = 10, then 10 + 8 = 18

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A worked example for Think harder. In a sum with three numbers, look for two that make ten, even if they are not side by side. 3 + 7 = 10 and 10 + 8 = 18. Check: 3 + 8 = 11 and 11 + 7 = 18, the same. The order does not change the total. Keep it to about a minute and do not teach written column addition.
