Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Take one or two answers from volunteers. Key ideas: we fill up the number that is nearly full; the number we move is the number of empty spaces; after the ten we say ten and whatever is left; moving does not change the total. Repeat a child's answer in your own words so the recording picks it up.
