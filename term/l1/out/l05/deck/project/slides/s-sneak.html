8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

Count all the counters.

Write how many.

You may draw rings round groups.

PUZZLE 2: think

Ben has 9 red counters and 5 blue counters.

He moves 1 blue counter over to the red ones.

Now he has 10 red and 4 blue.

Is the total the same as before? Write yes or no, and why.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 (12 counters, 7 and 5 in two ten-frames) is an access question: any child can try it by counting or drawing rings. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson's key idea: moving does not change the total. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Everyone holds the paper to the camera once, at the same moment, after puzzle 2; there is no private chat and no board in front of the group. Answers: 12; yes (9 + 5 and 10 + 4 are both 14). Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list of who found it easy.
