Tens and ones together

30 + 640 + 250 + 9

Say the tens first, then the ones.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 36, 42, 59. Say the tens word slowly, then the ones: thirty... six. This is the bridge to adding to a tens number: after we make a ten we say, for example, thirty and four. A child who has not met tens and ones only needs to hear and say the number words. Show 30 as three tens bars if a child is unsure.
