Round 3, about 4 minutes. Children build 28 with 2 tens bars and 8 ones squares, and put 6 squares beside it. Two squares fill the 8 up to a ten: that makes a third bar. Now there are 3 bars and 4 squares: 34. Then 37 + 5 (move 3: 4 bars and 2 squares, 42) and 46 + 7 (move 4: 5 bars and 3 squares, 53). Each child has 20 ones squares, enough for all of these
