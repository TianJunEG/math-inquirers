How far to the next ten?

18 is one ten and 8 ones

18 needs ___ more to make 20.

18142639

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A bar is a ten and each square is a one. 18 is one ten and 8 ones, so 2 more ones make another ten: 20. Then ask 14 (6 more to make 20), 26 (4 more to make 30) and 39 (1 more to make 40). Answers: 2, 6, 4, 1. These are number bonds to 20 and to the next ten. Show the kit squares filling the next bar if a child is unsure.
