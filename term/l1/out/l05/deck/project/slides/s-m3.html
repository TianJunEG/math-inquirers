Add to a tens number: 28 + 6

28

28 needs 2 more to make 30. Split 6 into 2 and 4.

28 + 6 = 28 + 2 + 4 = 30 + 4 = 34

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Think aloud: 28 is 2 tens and 8 ones. 8 and 2 make a ten, so 28 and 2 make 30. 6 is 2 and 4. 30 and 4 more is 34. Say the tens first. Ask: which number did we fill up? Why? Answer: 28, because it is nearly at the next ten.
