Days later

Today is Thursday. What day is it 4 days later?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. Today is Thursday. Count 4 days later from tomorrow: Friday is 1, Saturday 2, Sunday 3, Monday 4. Children count with the cards and say the day. Ask: what day (Monday). Say: we never count today, and after Sunday comes Monday. A child who says Sunday counted Thursday as day 1. The answer is on the next slide.
