Explore: the days in a row

1Take your 7 day cards.

2Put them in a row. Start with Monday.

3What comes after Sunday?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 2 minutes. Each child puts the 7 day cards in order from Monday. Ask: what comes after Sunday. Children answer aloud: Monday. Then ask them to move the Monday card to the end of the row and say: the days go round, like a clock. A child with no cards writes the 7 days on small papers. The row is on the next slide.
