Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The days in order (taught again with the 7 day cards). Say them together: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday. Ask: what comes after Sunday (Monday: the week starts again). Do not chant the days in a song; say them calmly. A child who is unsure points along the cards. Children answer aloud. No writing.
