A fortnight

A fortnight is 2 weeks. The yellow day is the 4th. What date is a fortnight later?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. A fortnight is 2 weeks, which is 14 days. A fortnight after the 4th: one week is the 11th, two weeks is the 18th. Check: 4 and 14 make 18. Ask: what date (18). Say: a fortnight is 2 weeks or 14 days. Children answer aloud. The answer is on the next slide. A child who adds 2 gave the number of weeks, not days.
