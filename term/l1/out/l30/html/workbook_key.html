Answer key: Lesson 10, Calendar Detective

For the tutor and grown-ups. Keep this page away from the child until they have finished and checked their own work. Ask how do you know? before saying right or wrong.

Start here (pages 1 and 2)
S1. Write the missing days of the week.
Answer: Wednesday; Saturday
The days in order are Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday. The missing days are Wednesday and Saturday.
Watch for: Writing a day twice.; Writing Sunday before Saturday.
S2. Fill in the blanks.
Answer: Tuesday; Sunday
One step back from Wednesday is Tuesday. One step on from Saturday is Sunday.
Watch for: Writing Thursday for before.; Writing Monday after Saturday.
S3. Today is Tuesday. What day is it 3 days later?
Answer: Friday; Sunday
Wednesday is 1, Thursday is 2, Friday is 3. It is Friday. Five days later: Saturday is 4, Sunday is 5. It is Sunday.
Watch for: Counting Tuesday as day 1 and writing Thursday.; Writing Wednesday.; (b) Writing Saturday (counting Tuesday as day 1).
S4. Today is Saturday. What day is it 2 days later?
Answer: Monday
Sunday is 1. After Sunday the week starts again, so Monday is 2. It is Monday.
Watch for: Writing Sunday.; Writing Tuesday.
S5. Look at the month page. The yellow day is the 6th.
Answer: 7; 13
A week has 7 days. 6 and 7 make 13. The 13th is a week after the 6th, straight below it.
Watch for: Writing 12 or 14.; Writing 7 for part b.
S6. Tick True or False for each statement.
Answer: True; False; True; True
Monday comes after Sunday: true. Friday is the day after Thursday, so Friday is not the day before Thursday: false. Wednesday is between Tuesday and Thursday: true. Saturday is the day before Sunday: true.
Watch for: Ticking True for every statement.; Mixing up before and after.
Think harder (page 3)
T1. Today is Thursday.
Answer: Thursday; Wednesday
Yesterday was Wednesday, so the day before yesterday was Tuesday. Two days after Tuesday is Thursday, which is today. Tomorrow is Friday. Two days before Friday is Wednesday.
Watch for: Counting forward from today only.; Writing Friday for part b.
T2. Mei starts a swimming course on Monday. Monday is day 1.
Answer: Sunday; Wednesday
Day 1 Monday to day 7 Sunday. Day 8 is Monday again, day 9 is Tuesday and day 10 is Wednesday.
Watch for: Writing Tuesday for day 10 by counting Monday as day 0.; Writing Thursday.
T3. A holiday lasts 3 weeks. A fortnight is 2 weeks.
Answer: 21; 14
3 weeks is 7 + 7 + 7, which is 21 days. A fortnight is 2 weeks: 7 + 7, which is 14 days.
Watch for: Writing 3 times 3 = 9.; Writing 14 for 3 weeks.
T4. Look at the month page. Mei's party is on the 18th.
Answer: Saturday; 25
Wednesdays are the 1st, 8th and 15th. The 18th is 3 days after the 15th: Thursday 16th, Friday 17th, Saturday 18th. A week later is 18 and 7, which is the 25th.
Watch for: Writing Wednesday for the 18th.; Writing 24 or 26.
Go further (page 4)
G1. Today is Monday.
Answer: 14 weeks and 2 days; Wednesday
14 weeks is 14 times 7, which is 98 days. 98 days later it is Monday again. 100 is 2 more days, so it is Wednesday.
Watch for: Writing Monday (forgetting the 2 days over).; Counting 100 days one at a time.
G2. A month has 31 days. The 1st is a Wednesday. Look at the month page.
Answer: 8, 15, 22, 29; Friday
1 + 7 = 8, 8 + 7 = 15, 15 + 7 = 22, 22 + 7 = 29. After the 29th, Wednesday, the 30th is Thursday and the 31st is Friday.
Watch for: Counting in 6s or 8s.; Writing Wednesday for the 31st.
G3. Today is the 6th. It is a Monday. Mei's trip is 3 weeks later.
Answer: 21; 27; Monday
3 weeks is 21 days. 6 and 21 make 27. Whole weeks later is the same day, so the 27th is a Monday.
Watch for: Writing 9 (6 + 3).; Writing Tuesday.
Investigation (page 5): Which days come 5 times?
First: 1, 8, 15, 22 and 29, which is 5 Wednesdays. Thursdays are 2, 9, 16, 23 and 30, and Fridays are 3, 10, 17, 24 and 31, so Wednesday, Thursday and Friday come 5 times. The other days (Saturday, Sunday, Monday and Tuesday) come 4 times. 31 days is 4 weeks and 3 days over, and the 3 days over are the first 3 days of the month.
Look for: A child who writes the dates of each day in rows of 7 and counts them, or who says that the 3 days over are Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. Credit any clear method. A child who finds Thursday only is partly there: ask about Friday.
Exit ticket (page 5)
E1. Today is Sunday. What day is it 2 days later?
Answer: Tuesday
Monday is 1 and Tuesday is 2. After Sunday the week starts again.
Watch for: Writing Monday.; Counting Sunday as day 1.
E2. Look at the month page. A week after the 11th is which date? Write its number in the bracket.
Answer: (2) the 18th
A week is 7 days. 11 and 7 make 18.
Watch for: Choosing (1) the 17th.; Choosing (4) the 25th (two weeks).
E3. Ben swims every 7 days. He swims on the 4th.
Answer: 11
Every 7 days means a week later. 4 and 7 make 11.
Watch for: Writing 5 or 7.; Writing 14.
