10–14 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

Count the stars. Write how many.

You may draw rings round groups.

PUZZLE 2: think

Mei has 3 bundles of ten sticks and 4 single sticks.

Ben has 34 sticks.

Could they have the same number? Write yes or no, and why.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 (24 stars) is an access question: any child can try it by counting or drawing rings. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson's key idea. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Everyone holds the paper to the camera once, at the same moment, after puzzle 2; there is no private chat and no board in front of the group. Answers: 24; yes (3 tens and 4 ones is 34). Do not reveal Puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list of who found it easy.
