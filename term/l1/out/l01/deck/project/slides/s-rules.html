Four inquirer rules

1Notice it. Look closely.

2Test it. Try it with real things.

3Explain why. Say how you know.

4Mistakes help us. A guess is fine. You can pass.

RECORDING

We record the tutor, the screen and any voice that is unmuted. You may mute and keep your camera off.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Read the four rules together. Say the recording notice in your own words: the platform records the tutor, the shared screen and any voice that is unmuted; children's video is excluded; names are kept off the screen. A child or parent who does not wish to be heard can stay muted, and a camera can stay off. Then tell the shopkeeper story (47 pencils) and ask the group what a shopkeeper could do.
