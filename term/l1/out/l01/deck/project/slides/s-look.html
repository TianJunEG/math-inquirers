Three minutes. Children tick or write the look-back box on page 5. A pass is always allowed. It is never marked, never collected in front of the group and never read out. If a child ticks I am still stuck, check in during the open session next lesson (a quick visit to the shared room or a hand signal, parent nearby); never a direct message. The Next time I will try row is optional; at the start of the next lesson you may say to the whole group: today you could try drawing it, using objects or checking your answer.
