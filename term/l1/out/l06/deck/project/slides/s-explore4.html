Draw the jumps and hops

1Draw a dot. Write the start number.

2Draw a big arc for the jump. Write +20.

3Write where you land.

4Draw a small arc for the hop. Write +5. Write where you land.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 4, about 4 minutes. Draw on a shared whiteboard or blank slide as the children draw on paper: dot, arc, landing number. A big arc is a jump of tens, a small arc is a hop of ones. Draw 34 to 59 first, then 68 back to 44 with arcs going back. The paper line is the picture children use in question 7 and in Think harder. The landing numbers are hidden on this slide: children find them. They appear on the next slide.
