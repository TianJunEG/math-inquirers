Jump, then hop: take away

59-2039-534

25 is 2 tens and 5 ones. Jump back the tens first. Then hop back the ones.

59 - 25 = 34

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. 59 - 25: jump back 20 to 39, then hop back 5 to 34. The arcs go backwards, so the numbers get smaller. Ask: what is 87 - 32? Answer: jump back 3 tens to 57, hop back 2 to 55. Stay inside a ten (9 - 5 = 4, 7 - 2 = 5).
