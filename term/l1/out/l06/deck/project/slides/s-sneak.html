8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

Mei has these sticks.

Then she gets another bundle of 10 sticks.

How many sticks does Mei have now?

PUZZLE 2: think

Ravi says 43 + 20 = 45.

Is Ravi right? Write Yes or No. Then write how you know.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 (26 sticks and one more bundle) is an access question: any child can try it by counting or drawing. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson's key idea: 20 is 2 tens. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper up to the camera: children keep their own answer and check it at Talk time. No private chat and no board in front of the group. Answers: 36; no, 63. Do not reveal Puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list of who found it easy.
