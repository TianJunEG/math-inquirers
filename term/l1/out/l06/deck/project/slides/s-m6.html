Jump or hop?

306502

Tens are a jump. Ones are a hop.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Quick-fire. Say a number and ask: jump or hop? Answers: 30 jump, 6 hop, 50 jump, 2 hop. A multiple of ten is a jump because it is whole bars. Keep it quick: this is the decision children make on the workbook pages.
