Tens and ones

4 tens and 6 ones

A bar is a ten. A square is a one. How many tens? How many ones?

527083

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say each number; children say how many tens and how many ones, with fingers or a whispered answer. Answers: 52 is 5 tens and 2 ones, 70 is 7 tens and 0 ones, 83 is 8 tens and 3 ones. Point to the bars and then the squares in the picture. No writing, no marks.
