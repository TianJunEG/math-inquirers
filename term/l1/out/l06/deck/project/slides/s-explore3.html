Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 5 minutes. 34 + 25: 2 bars then 5 squares gives 59. 41 + 38: 3 bars then 8 squares gives 79. 68 - 24: take away 2 bars then 4 squares gives 44. Check each build as the children hold it to the camera. Say each answer aloud for the recording. A child who adds the 2 as ones is mixing up jump and hop: ask how many squares one bar is worth.
