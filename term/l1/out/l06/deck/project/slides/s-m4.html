25 is 2 tens and 5 ones. Jump the tens first. Then hop the ones.

34 + 25 = 59

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. Split 25 into 20 and 5. Jump 20 from 34 to 54, then hop 5 to 59. Ask: how many tens and how many ones in 36? (3 tens, 6 ones) to check children can split a number. The example stays inside a ten (4 + 5 = 9). Do not mention carrying or renaming.
