Warm-up: count by rows

Count the dots by rows. How many rows? How many dots in each row? How many in all?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Counting by rows (Term 2 Lesson 5, taught again). 3 rows of 4 dots: 4 and 4 and 4 make 12. Ask: how many rows (3), how many in each row (4), how many in all (12). A child who is unsure points along each row. Your turn, no picture: 2 rows of 6; how many in all (12). Children answer aloud. No writing.
