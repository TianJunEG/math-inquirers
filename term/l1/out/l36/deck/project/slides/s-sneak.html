8–12 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Nobody is marked.

PUZZLE 1: anyone can try

How many squares are in the picture?

Write the number.

PUZZLE 2: think

Ali has 2 hats and 3 scarves.

He wears one hat and one scarf.

How many different ways can he do it?

Write or draw them.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 is an access question: a grid of 2 rows of 4 squares, 8 in all. Puzzle 2 is a thinking question on the lesson idea: 2 hats and 3 scarves make 6 ways, because each hat goes with each scarf. A child who writes pairs such as hat 1 with scarf 1, hat 1 with scarf 2 and so on has done well. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Nobody holds paper to the camera. There is no private chat. Answers: 8; 6 ways. Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list.
