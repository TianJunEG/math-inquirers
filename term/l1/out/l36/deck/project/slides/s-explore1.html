Explore: the handshakes

1Stand 3 paper people up in a ring.

2Every two people shake hands once.

3List the handshakes. No repeats. None missed.

How many handshakes are there?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 6 minutes. Each child has 3 paper people (or 3 coins). They name them A, B and C and list every pair that shakes hands, for example A with B. Rule: each pair shakes hands once, and A with B is the same handshake as B with A. Ask: how did you make sure you missed none; how did you make sure you counted none twice. Many children will start with A: A with B, A with C. Then B with C. Do not tell the answer. Nobody holds the people up to the camera: children say the pairs aloud and show fingers for how many. A child with no people draws 3 dots and joins them. Answers on the next slide.
