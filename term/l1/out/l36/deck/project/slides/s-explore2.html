Explore: find every square

How many squares can you find? Look for small squares and a big one.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 4 minutes. A grid of 2 by 2 small squares. Children count the small squares first (4), then look for bigger squares: the whole grid is one big square, 5 in all. Children draw the grid on paper and trace each square with a pencil in turn if they like. Ask: how many small squares (4); is there a bigger square (yes, the whole grid); how many squares in all (5). Do not tell the answer. Nobody holds a drawing up to the camera: children say the number aloud. Answer on the next slide.
