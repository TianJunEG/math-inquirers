Warm-up: make tens

7 + 3 + 6 + 4

Find the pairs that make 10 first. What is the total?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Adding four numbers by making tens (Term 1 Lesson 5, taught again). 7 and 3 make 10. 6 and 4 make 10. 10 and 10 make 20. Ask: which two make 10 (7 and 3), which two more (6 and 4), what is the total (20). A child who is unsure holds up 7 fingers, then 3 more. Your turn, no picture: 8 + 2 + 5 + 5 (20). Children answer aloud. No writing.
