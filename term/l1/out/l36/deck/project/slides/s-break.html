34–38 MIN

Every move with every move

Stand up. Arms up or arms out.

Legs: jump or squat.

Try every arm move with every leg move.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera with a little clear space. 2 arm moves (arms up, arms out) and 2 leg moves (jump, squat). Children try every arm move with every leg move: arms up and jump, arms up and squat, arms out and jump, arms out and squat. Ask: how many moves (4); did we miss any. Then add a third arm move (arms down): 6 moves in all. No equipment, no running. A child may stay seated and do the arm moves only. Do not give prizes or compare children.
