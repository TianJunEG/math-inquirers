Spot the repeat

A CHILD IN ANOTHER CLASS LISTS HANDSHAKES

A with B

A with C

B with A

B with C

She says: 4 handshakes. Is she right? What went wrong?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. The child lists 4 handshakes but B with A is the same handshake as A with B, so there are 3. Ask: is she right (no); which one is a repeat (B with A); how many handshakes are there (3). The answer is on the next slide. The child in the story is in another class: no cast name is given the wrong answer. Say: a list with a rule has no repeats.
