A list that stops

Every pair of numbers that make 8.

Start with 1. 1 + 7 = 8. What is next? When do we stop?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. Start at 1 and go up: 1 and 7; 2 and 6; 3 and 5; 4 and 4. After 4 and 4 the pairs repeat: 5 and 3 is the same pair as 3 and 5. So there are 4 pairs. Ask: what is the next pair after 1 and 7 (2 and 6); and next (3 and 5); and next (4 and 4); when do we stop (when the pairs repeat). The answer is on the next slide. Say: a list with a rule, starting at the smallest, shows when to stop.
