Shopper moves

Stand up. Be a shopper.

Big price: reach up high. Small price: crouch down low.

Sip some water.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera with a little clear space. Call prices and children show them with their bodies: a small price ($2) means crouch low, a middle price ($10) means hands at the waist, a big price ($50) means reach up high. Call pairs and ask which one is higher: $3 or $8, $12 or $9, $20 or $15. Then call: how much higher? Show with fingers for small gaps. No equipment, no running. A child may stay seated and use arms only. Do not give prizes or compare children.
