Explore: how much more?

1Take the cup tag and the ball tag.

2Which costs more?

3Count up from the cup price to the ball price. Use your fingers.

4How many more dollars is the ball?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 5 minutes. Children take the cup tag ($8) and the ball tag ($14). Ask: which costs more (the ball). Then: how much more? Count up from 8 to 14 with fingers or on the line: 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, six fingers. Some children go to 10 first (2 steps) and then to 14 (4 steps): 2 and 4 make 6. Both ways are right; ask a child to say how they counted. The line has no jumps drawn, so the answer is not shown. A child with no tags writes 8 and 14 on paper. Answer: $6 more. Say the answer in a full sentence: the ball costs $6 more than the cup.
