Change: count up to what was paid

16 up to 20 is 4. The change is $4. For the cap: 11 up to 20 is $9.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The answers: the car's change is $4 (16 up to 20), the cap's change is $9 (11 up to 20). Read them aloud and children tick their own work. Say: to find the change, count up from the price to the money that was paid.
