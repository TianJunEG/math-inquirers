Change: count up

Mei pays with two ten-dollar notes. Count up from 16 to 20. How much change?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Change is the money the cashier gives back. The toy car costs $16 and Mei pays with two ten-dollar notes, which is $20 (there is no twenty-dollar note). Count up from the price to what she paid: 16 up to 20 is 4 steps. She gets $4 change. Ask: how much change; how do you know. Children answer aloud, then try one on paper: a cap costs $11 and the cashier gets $20; how much change (9: 11 up to 20 is 9). Say 'cashier' and 'change' in full sentences. The answer for the car is on the next slide. A child who adds 16 and 20 is finding a total: ask what the cashier gives back.
