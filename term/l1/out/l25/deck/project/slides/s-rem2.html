Count up to find the gap

From 28 up to 35. How many steps in all?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A gap by counting up on a number line (Term 1 Lesson 7, taught again). From 28 count up to the next ten (30): 2 steps. Then from 30 up to 35: 5 steps. 2 and 5 make 7, so the gap between 28 and 35 is 7. Ask: how many steps from 28 to 30 (2); from 30 to 35 (5); how many in all (7). The jumps are drawn on the slide; cover them with your hand or the ask comes first, and read the line aloud as you ask. A child who is unsure counts the ticks with a finger. Children answer aloud. No writing.
