Explore: put the tags in order

1Take the pencil tag, the glue tag and the ruler tag.

2Line them up. Put the tag that costs the least first.

3Which tag costs the most?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 4 minutes. Each child takes three tags from the price-tag page: pencil, glue and ruler. They line them up with the one that costs the least first. Ask: which costs the most; which costs the least; how do you know (the biggest number of dollars). The prices are on the tags, so the slide shows no price. The picture appears on the next slide, after the children have answered. A child with no tags writes the three prices on paper (3, 5, 7) and orders them. Do not name a child with a wrong answer. Answer: pencil $3, glue $5, ruler $7, so the ruler costs the most and the pencil the least.
