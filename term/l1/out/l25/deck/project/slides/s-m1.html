How much more?

How much more does the car cost than the ball?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. The toy car costs $11 and the ball costs $4. Ask: which costs more (the car). How much more? The strips show the gap as an empty box. Count up from 4 to 10 (6 steps), then from 10 to 11 (1 step): 6 and 1 make 7. The car costs $7 more than the ball. Children say the answer in a full sentence after they have tried. The next slide shows the number line and the answer. Fixed question words every time: How much more does ... cost than ...? A child who subtracts 11 take away 4 gets the same: 7.
