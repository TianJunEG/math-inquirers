A pen and a rubber cost $11 together. The pen costs $9.

How much does the rubber cost?

Optional, only if you are on time. The pen and the rubber cost $11 together and the pen costs $9, so the rubber costs 11 take away 9, which is $2. Count up from 9 to 11 to check. Take two answers and move on. This links to the Hidden Price in Go further.
