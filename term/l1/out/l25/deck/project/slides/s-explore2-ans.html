The gap is how much more

2 and 4 make 6. The jar costs $6 more than the cup.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The answer for round 2, shown on the line: from 8 up to 10 is 2 steps and from 10 up to 14 is 4 steps, so the gap is 6. Another way is to count 6 single steps. Say: the gap is how much more. Children check their own count with a tick.
