Altogether

Ben buys the shoe and the button. How much does he pay altogether?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Altogether means add. The shoe costs $10 and the button costs $2. Ask: how much does Ben pay altogether (12). Say it as a number sentence: 10 and 2 make 12. The total is on the next slide. Then a second one, read aloud without a picture: a sticker book costs $3 and a toy bird costs $13; how much altogether (16). Children write the second total on their own paper. Fixed question words: How much ... altogether? A child who writes 8 found the gap instead of the total: ask which word is in the question, more or altogether.
