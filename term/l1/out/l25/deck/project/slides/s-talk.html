TWO CHILDREN SAY

A kite costs $17. A top costs $12.

One child: the top costs $5 more than the kite.

Another child: the kite costs $5 more than the top.

Who is right? How could we show it on a number line?

I think ... because ...

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

One minute of talk to the grown-up next to you, or to your screen. Children test it on a number line or with fingers: count up from 12 to 17. Then volunteers first; a pass is always fine; never call a child by name to answer. Model answer: the second child is right. The kite costs more because 17 is greater than 12, and the gap is 5, so the kite costs $5 more than the top. The first child swapped them: the top costs $5 less than the kite. Repeat each child answer in your own words for the recording. The two children are in another class: no cast name is given the wrong answer.
