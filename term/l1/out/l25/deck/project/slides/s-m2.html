Your turn: how much more?

How much more does the bike cost than the spoon? Write the number on your paper.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Your turn, one try. The bike costs $13 and the spoon costs $5. Children write how much more on their own paper; wait 10 seconds before helping. Read the answer aloud afterwards: 8 (5 up to 10 is 5, 10 up to 13 is 3, and 5 and 3 make 8). Nobody holds paper up. A child who writes 18 added the prices: ask which is the question, the total or the gap. A child who writes 5 or 13 gave a price, not a gap. Answer: $8 more.
