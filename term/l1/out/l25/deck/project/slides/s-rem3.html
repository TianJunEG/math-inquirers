Two strips and the gap

Ali has 8 stickers. Mei has 3. How many more does Ali have?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Two strips drawn to scale (Term 1 Lesson 12, taught again): Ali 8 and Mei 3, with the gap shown and an empty box. Ask: whose strip is longer (Ali's); how many more stickers does Ali have (5). Say: the gap is how many more. Today a price is a strip too: the more it costs, the longer the strip. A child who is unsure counts the squares of the gap or counts up from 3 to 8 on fingers. Answers: Ali; 5. Children answer aloud. No writing.
