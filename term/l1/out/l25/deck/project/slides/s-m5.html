Which two cost exactly $15?

Ravi buys two things. They cost exactly $15. There are two answers.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Guess and check, with the tags the children hold: pencil $3, glue $5, ruler $7, cup $8 and book $12. Ask: which two of these five tags cost exactly $15? There are two answers. Model a way to be sure: start with the tag that costs the least, $3, and ask what partner it needs (12: the book, yes). Then $5 needs 10 (no tag). Then $7 needs 8 (the cup, yes). Then $8 and $12 have been used. So the two answers are $3 and $12, and $7 and $8. Children try with their own tags or on paper first; wait 15 seconds. Do not say the starting rule until after they have tried; then say: start with the tag that costs the least and look for its partner, so we do not miss one. The answer is on the next slide.
