Total, then change

Mei buys the cup and the pen. She gives the cashier two ten-dollar notes. How much change?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Two steps. First the total: the cup costs $8 and the pen costs $3, so they cost $11. Then the change from $20: count up from 11 to 20, which is 9. Ask: how much altogether (11); how much change (9). Children answer aloud. The answer is on the next slide. A child who gives 12 took away only the cup: ask what else she bought.
