Three of the same

Ravi buys 3 glue sticks. How much do they cost altogether?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Three glue sticks at $5 each. Ask: how much altogether (15): 5 and 5 make 10, and 5 more make 15. Say it as a number sentence: $5 + $5 + $5 = $15. The answer is on the next slide. Then ask a second one aloud: a rubber costs $2 and Siti buys 5; how much (10). Children write the second on their own paper. A child who writes 5 gave one price: ask how many glue sticks.
