Two ways to make $12

$4 and $8. $5 and $7. Start with the cheapest tag and ask what partner it needs.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The answers for round 1: $4 and $8 (pen and cup), and $5 and $7 (glue and ruler). Say: starting with the cheapest tag and asking what it needs means we do not miss one. Children check against their own tags and tick. Nobody holds tags up to the camera: children say the pair and show a thumb when they agree.
