Clap and stamp

Stand up. Clap for a two-dollar note. Stamp for a five-dollar note.

Make each amount: $7 is stamp, clap.

Sip some water.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera with a little clear space. Call an amount and children make it with claps ($2 each) and stamps ($5 each): $7 is one stamp and one clap, $9 is a stamp and two claps, $4 is two claps, $10 is two stamps (or five claps). Then call $6, $11 and $14. No equipment, no running. A child may stay seated and tap the desk softly. Do not give prizes or compare children.
