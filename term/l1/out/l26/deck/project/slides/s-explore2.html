Explore: pay exactly $10

1Take your $2 notes and your $5 notes.

2Pay exactly $10 using only $2 notes and $5 notes.

3How many different ways can you find?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 5 minutes. Children use the play-money notes: five $2 notes and four $5 notes (they need at most five of one and two of the other). They find every way to pay exactly $10 with $2 notes and $5 notes. There are two ways: five $2 notes, or two $5 notes. Ask for each way, then: how do you know there are only two? Try one $5 note: $5 is left, and $2 notes cannot make $5 exactly. Children say the way and show a thumb; nobody holds notes up. A child with no notes draws or writes 2s and 5s on paper.
