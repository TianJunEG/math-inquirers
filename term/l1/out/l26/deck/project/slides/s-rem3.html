List every pair that makes 6

Start at 1 and go up. Stop when the numbers swap round.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Listing in order (Term 2 Lesson 17, taught again). Start at 1: 1 and 5. Then 2 and 4. Then 3 and 3. After that the pairs swap round (4 and 2 is the same pair as 2 and 4), so we stop. Say: starting at the smallest and going up in order means we do not miss one. Ask for the second and third rows. Answers: 4; 3 and 3. A child who is unsure counts on fingers from the first number to 6. Children answer aloud. No writing.
