Three of the same

3 pens at $4 each. How much do they cost altogether?

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Three of the same price (Term 2 Lesson 15, taught again as adding again and again): 4 and 4 and 4. Ask: how much do 3 pens cost altogether (12). Add two at a time: 4 and 4 make 8, 8 and 4 make 12. A child who is unsure uses three groups of 4 fingers. Children answer aloud. No writing.
