TWO CHILDREN SAY

Ben wants a pen and a book.

One child: buying the pen then the book is a different way from buying the book then the pen.

Another child: it is the same way.

Who is right? How do you know?

I think ... because ...

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

One minute of talk to the grown-up next to you, or to your screen. Volunteers first; a pass is always fine; never call a child by name to answer. Model answer: the second child is right. A way is what you buy, not the order you pick it up: a pen and a book cost the same either way, and listing both would count one way twice. Link to Explore: five $2 notes then nothing, or nothing then five $2 notes, is still one way. Repeat each child answer in your own words for the recording. The two children are in another class: no cast name is given the wrong answer.
