Every way to pay $12

Pay exactly $12 with $5 notes and $2 notes. Fill in the last column. Write X if it cannot be done.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The table lists every way in order. Start with no $5 notes: $12 is left, and 12 is 6 two-dollar notes (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12). With one $5 note: $7 is left, and $2 notes cannot make 7 exactly (X). With two $5 notes: $2 is left, which is 1 two-dollar note. A third $5 note would be $15, more than $12, so we stop. Children try the last column on paper first; wait 15 seconds. Answers: 6; X; 1. So there are 2 ways: six $2 notes, or two $5 notes and one $2 note. Six $2 notes is more than the five in the kit: write it, do not lay it out. The filled table is on the next slide.
