Is the money enough?

Mei has $15. Does she have enough for the toy car and the ball? Circle Yes or No on your paper.

Math Inquirers Term 3 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. Mei has $15. The toy car costs $11 and the ball costs $6. Ask: how much do they cost altogether (17); is $17 more than $15 (yes); so is $15 enough (No). How much more money does she need (2)? Say: a budget is the money you have. Enough means the total is the same as the budget or less. Children answer aloud. The answer is on the next slide. A child who says Yes because both prices are less than 15 forgot to add: ask what altogether means.
