Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Puzzle 1 (box A has 15 stars, box B has 18) is an access question: any child can try it by counting or drawing rings. Say: two sneak-peek puzzles, ideas we have not learnt yet; a guess is a great start and I do not know is welcome; nobody is marked. Children write on their own paper. Answer: box B, 18 stars. Do not reveal it until Talk time. Do not write scores or keep a list of who found it easy.
