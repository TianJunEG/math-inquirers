Which group has more squares?

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Group A is a full bar of ten and 3 more squares, which is 13. Group B is 9 squares. Group A has more. Then ask by voice: 16 or 19 (19), 20 or 12 (20). Children point or answer with fingers. This is comparing two sets by matching and counting, to 20. Tens first comes in the mini-lesson.
