Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach digit, tens and ones with the kit: 3 bars and 4 squares make 34. Then say each number on the slide and children say how many tens and how many ones (25 is 2 tens and 5 ones, 40 is 4 tens and 0 ones, 17 is 1 ten and 7 ones, 63 is 6 tens and 3 ones). Show with fingers or kit. No writing, no marks.
