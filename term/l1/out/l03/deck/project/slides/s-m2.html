Which is greater? Tens first

61

59

THE RULE

Look at the tens first.

Same tens? Then look at the ones.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Think aloud: 6 bars is more than 5 bars, so 61 is greater than 59, even though the ones digit 9 is greater than 1. Many children look at the ones first. Then a same-tens pair: 47 and 43 have 4 tens each, so look at the ones: 7 is more than 3. Ask: which is greater, 52 or 58? Answer: 58.
