Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera. Call each pair of numbers: children reach up tall if the first is greater, curl small if the first is smaller, hands on hips if they are the same. Pause after each pair and ask one child to say how they know. Keep it gentle for small spaces. No equipment.
