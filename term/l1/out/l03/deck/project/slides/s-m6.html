Smallest first: 39, 40, 45, 54. Think aloud: 3 tens is the fewest, so 39 is first. 40 has 4 tens and 0 ones, 45 has 4 tens and 5 ones, so 40 comes before 45. 54 has 5 tens, so it is last. Children may say the order or write it.
