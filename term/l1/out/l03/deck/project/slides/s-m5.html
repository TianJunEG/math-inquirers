6 then 0 is 60. A two-digit number.

0 then 6 is 06. That is just 6.

A number does not start with 0.

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The zero rule in one minute. With the cards 0 and 6 the only two-digit number is 60. 06 is just 6: a zero on the left is empty. So when we make the smallest two-digit number and one card is 0, the 0 goes on the right. Ask: why is 06 not a two-digit number? Do not use the word place value.
