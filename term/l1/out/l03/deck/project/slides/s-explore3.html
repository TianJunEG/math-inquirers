Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 3, about 4 minutes. Say each pair aloud and keep this table on screen. Children make the number and give a thumbs-up, then check their own against your line; nobody holds anything up to the camera. Answers: 7 and 2 make 72; 3 and 9 make 93; 0 and 5 make 50 (05 is just 5, settled on the next slide); 6 and 1 make 16. Leave the zero case open and let the children argue it.
