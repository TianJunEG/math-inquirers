13–28 MIN

Explore: two cards, two numbers

1Put your digit cards face up.

2Pick two cards. Put them side by side.

3Read your number. Say the tens first.

4Swap the cards. Read the new number.

WHAT IF?

What if one of your cards is 0?

Math Inquirers Term 1 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 4 minutes. Objects first, no writing. Children make three pairs, swap, and say both numbers aloud, tens first. A child who wants to may show a pair to the camera. The what-if card is open from the start so nobody waits; it previews the zero rule and nobody is named for choosing it. Expect some children to read 47 as seven-four: ask them to say the tens first.
