Strip: more and fewer

Jamie has 14 cards. Lily has 6 fewer cards than Jamie. How many cards do they have in all?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example, strip. Jamie has 14 cards. Lily has 6 fewer cards than Jamie. Children work out how many cards Lily has and then how many cards in all, on their own paper. Ask: how many cards does Lily have (8); how many in all (22). The answer is on the next slide. Say: draw the long strip first, then a shorter one, and the gap is the 6 fewer. A child who answers 20 has added 14 and 6: the 6 is how many fewer, not how many cards. Check: 14 take away 8 is 6.
