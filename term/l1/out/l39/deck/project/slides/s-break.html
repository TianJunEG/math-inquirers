Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Four minutes. Stand up on camera with a little clear space. Mime the tools together: strip (draw a long line in the air with both hands), table (tap a table with a finger), list (write a list in the air), draw it (draw a circle), work backwards (turn your arms backwards, like a slow wheel; nobody walks backwards), find one first (hold up one finger), steps (climb three small steps with your hands). Then say a tool name and let children do its move. A child may stay seated and move one arm. No equipment, no running. Do not give prizes or compare children.
