Work backwards

I think of a number. I add 9. Then I take away 4. I get 20. What is my number?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example, work backwards. The boxes show the story: start, + 9 (add 9), a middle number, − 4 (take away 4), the end 20. Children work back from the end on their own paper: undo the take away 4 (20 and 4 make 24), then undo the add 9 (24 take away 9 is 15). Ask: what is my number (15). The answer is on the next slide. Say: undo the last step first. A child who writes 25 has added 9 again instead of taking it away.
