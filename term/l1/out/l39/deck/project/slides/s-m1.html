Seven tools, seven clues

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

One clue for each tool, read aloud in the order of the table. Strip: compare two amounts, or find a total. Table: the numbers grow in a pattern. List: find every way. Draw it: a line, a place or a shape. Work backwards: you know the end, not the start. Find one first: equal groups, or the price of one. Steps: one thing happens, then the next. Say: a clue is a help, not a rule: look at the whole story. Say: after the answer, check it a second way. About 90 seconds.
