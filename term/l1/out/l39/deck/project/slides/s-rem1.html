Warm-up: draw two strips

Ali has 8 stickers. Mei has 3 stickers. How many more stickers does Ali have than Mei?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Strips (Term 2 Lesson 2, taught again with a picture). The two strips show 8 and 3. The part that sticks out is how many more. Ask: how many more stickers does Ali have than Mei (5). A child who is unsure lays 3 counters against 8 counters and counts the extra ones. Your turn, no new picture: how many stickers do they have in all (11). Children answer aloud. No writing.
