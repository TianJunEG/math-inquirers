A CHILD IN ANOTHER CLASS SAYS

Amelia has twice as many marbles as Wei.

They have 24 marbles in all.

24 shared into 2 is 12, so Wei has 12 marbles.

Do you agree? How could we check a second way?

I think ... because ...

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

One minute of talk to the grown-up next to you, or to your screen. Volunteers first; a pass is always fine; never call a child by name to answer. Model answer: no, we do not agree. Check forwards: if Wei has 12 then Amelia has twice as many, 24, and together they have 36, not 24. Check with a strip: Wei has 1 part and Amelia has 2 parts, so there are 3 equal parts, and 24 shared into 3 is 8. Wei has 8 and Amelia has 16. The child shared into 2 parts but there are 3. Link to the mini-lesson: two roads, one answer. Repeat each child answer in your own words for the recording. The child in the story is in another class: no cast name is given the wrong answer.
