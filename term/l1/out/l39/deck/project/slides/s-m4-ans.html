My number is 15

Start at 20 and undo each step: 20 + 4 = 24, then 24 take away 9 is 15. Check: 15 + 9 = 24 and 24 take away 4 is 20.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The answer for the example on the slide before. Undo the last step first: 20 and 4 make 24. Then undo the first step: 24 take away 9 is 15. Check forwards: 15 and 9 make 24, and 24 take away 4 is 20. Read it aloud so that children can tick their own work. Say: checking forwards is a second way.
