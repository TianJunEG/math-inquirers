Explore: problems 1 to 4

Card
Problem
1
Ali has 9 pens. Mei has 4 fewer pens than Ali. How many pens do they have in all?
2
Rows of blocks have 2, 4, 6 blocks, and so on. Each row has 2 more than the row before. How many blocks are in the 6th row?
3
Mei picks 2 fruits from an apple, a pear and a plum. List every pair she can pick.
4
Ravi is 3rd in a line of 7 children. How many children are behind Ravi?

Put each problem card under a tool you would use. Say why.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 2, about 4 minutes. Read problem 1 aloud with its numbers, then give 20 seconds for children to find card 1 and lay it under a tool card. Ask one or two volunteers: which tool, and why. Then problem 2, 3 and 4 the same way. There is no need to solve the problems: we only choose a tool. Answers (a tool that fits) are on the next slide. Say: more than one tool can fit; the reason matters more than the name. A child who chooses by one word ("more" so add) has not yet looked at the whole story: ask, what do we know and what do we want to find? Nobody holds a card up to the camera.
