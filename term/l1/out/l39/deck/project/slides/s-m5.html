Two roads, one answer

Amelia has twice as many marbles as Wei. They have 18 marbles in all. How many marbles does Wei have?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example, two roads. Amelia has twice as many marbles as Wei and they have 18 marbles in all. Road 1: the strip. Wei has 1 part and Amelia has 2 parts, so there are 3 equal parts, and 18 shared into 3 parts is 6 marbles for Wei. Children work out how many marbles Wei has on their own paper. Ask: how many marbles does Wei have (6). The answer is on the next slide, with a second road: a table of tries. Say: twice as many is a Primary 2 and later idea at school; we try it because the strip shows it. A child who writes 9 has shared 18 into 2 equal parts: ask, how many parts does Amelia have?
