The answers for round 3. Problem 5: work backwards (start at the end and undo). Problem 6: find one first (5 buns cost $15, so 1 bun costs $3, then 8 buns). Problem 7: steps (take away 8, then add 5). Problem 8: table (2 red with 3 blue, 4 red with 6 blue, 6 red with 9 blue, 8 red with 12 blue). Problems 2 and 8 share the table tool.
