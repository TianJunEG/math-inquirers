Road 2: a table of tries

Road 1, the strip: 3 equal parts, 18 shared into 3 is 6. Road 2, the table: Wei 6 and Amelia 12 make 18. Both roads say Wei has 6 marbles.

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The answer for the example on the slide before. Road 1, the strip: 3 equal parts, so Wei has 6 and Amelia has 12. Road 2, a table of tries: try Wei 4 (Amelia 8, in all 12, too few), Wei 5 (Amelia 10, in all 15, still too few), Wei 6 (Amelia 12, in all 18, just right). Both roads agree. Ask: why do the two roads agree (they use the same story, so a right answer must fit every part of it). Say: when two tools agree, we can trust the answer. A child who tries numbers at random is guessing; a table of tries goes up in order and gets closer.
