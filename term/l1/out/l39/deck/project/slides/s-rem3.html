Work backwards

I think of a number. I add 5. I get 12. What is my number?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Working backwards (Term 1 Lesson 10, taught again on a number line). The number line shows a jump of 5 that ends at 12, and the start is an empty box. Ask: what is my number (7). Say: start at the end and undo the jump: 12 take away 5. A child who is unsure counts back 5 steps on fingers from 12. Your turn, no new picture: I think of a number, take away 3, get 9: what is my number (12). Children answer aloud. No writing.
