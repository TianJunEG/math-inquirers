Explore: your seven tools

Lay your 7 tool cards in a row. Which tool have you used before?

Math Inquirers Term 4 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Round 1, about 2 minutes. Children lay the 7 tool cards in a row on the table (a grown-up cut them out beforehand). Read the seven names: strip, table, list, draw it, work backwards, find one first, steps. Ask: which tool have you used before, and what did you use it for? Take two or three answers from volunteers; a pass is fine. Say: a tool is a way to solve a problem. Today we practise picking one. Nobody holds a card up to the camera: children keep the cards on the table. A child with no cards writes the seven names on scraps of paper.
