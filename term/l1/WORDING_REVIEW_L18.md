# Wording review, Lesson 18 (Times Stories)

Every child-facing sentence (sneak-peek, slides, workbook S1-S6, T1-T4, G1-G3, I1, exit E1-E3, home H1-H5, talk, online practice, parent page) was read aloud against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1-25 and the school-paper patterns (Henry Park P1 "[] groups of [] = []", number-sentence frames, MCQ with a bracket; Rosyth P2 two-step stories with "in all" and "left"). The tutor lines that are read to children (READ lines) were checked the same way. Maths and answers were not changed by the review; 120 answer checks and 144 practice variants pass; 0 FAIL.

## Changes made during review (old -> new, reason)
1. Answer frames in S4, S6 and T1 were fragments ("The pens in the boxes: [2]") -> full sentences that end with the noun ("The boxes have [2] pens.", "The bags have [2] marbles."). Rule 16.
2. G1 frames became "The first 4 cards have [2] stickers." and "The other 5 cards have [2] stickers." Rule 16; "left over" is kept in the story because Lesson 17 taught it.
3. T3: the error-spotter is Jenny and the claim is only a number ("Jenny says Ravi has 8 stickers."), no wrong reasoning in the stem; (a) is "Is Jenny correct? Circle Yes or No." and (b) asks for the number. Rules 11, 19, and Siti is not the error-spotter.
4. S2 first showed a small picture of boxes of pencils that could not be read at print size -> a plain story with the frame "[] groups of [] = [2]". Rule 5 (words and pictures match): no picture is better than a picture that cannot be counted.
5. E1 asks "Which number sentence finds how many sweets Ali has in all?" with four options in the paper style ((1) to (4), a bracket); the options are 4 + 5, 4 × 5, 5 - 4 and 4 + 4 + 4, so only one fits. Rule 23. Twice as many is not tested in the exit ticket (rule 25).
6. Mini-lesson step tables show "4 × 5 = ?" and "? + 6 = ?" instead of the answers, so the slide asks and the child says each total. Rule 24 (the question is not answered on screen).
7. The lesson says "twice as many" and "3 times as many" in full, never "double" or "x2", and always with "as Mei" or "as Siti" so the comparison is clear. The words "loose", "strip", "box" and "each" are taught in Explore before they appear on a page, and "loose" is explained as "not in a box or a bag" on the parent page.
8. Equal groups of Lesson 15 are called "same-size groups" or "groups of the same size" in slides and guide text; the child words (each, in all, groups of) are the same as Lesson 15.
9. Online practice c1 repeated a field (a and b twice in the frame) -> one frame "{a} groups of {b} = {c}". Rule 16.
10. H1: "[] × [] = [2]" printed with the sign so the home sheet uses the multiplication sentence frame (rule 17) and the glyph is checked in the PDF.
