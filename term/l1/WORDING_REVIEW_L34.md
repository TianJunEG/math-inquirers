# Wording review: Lesson 34 (Cubes and Views)

Every child-facing sentence was read against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1 to 25 and the Primary 1 paper patterns.

- Commands: Count; Write; Circle A, B or C; Circle first, second or third; Circle one; Draw the fold line; Fill in the missing number. Same command for the same job every time (rules 2, 14).
- Fixed question forms: 'There are [] cubes in the picture.'; 'You can see [] cubes. You cannot see [] cube.'; 'There are [] holes when it is opened.' Numbers are digits; a box comes before the unit (rules 15, 16).
- Words taught in Explore and the Mini-lesson: stack, top view, fold, layer. 'Layer' appears only at Think harder and Go further hints and the parent page.
- A picture cannot show every cube, so hidden-cube questions say 'The stack has 4 cubes in all' first. No question asks a child to guess a hidden cube from a picture alone.
- Wrong working belongs to 'a child in another class' (Talk question). Cast names (Lily) appear only in a story.
- No teaching sentence inside a question (rule 24); 'The fold is on the left.' is a fact the picture cannot show, not a method.
- Rule 25: fold and punch, the top view as numbers and the hidden cube are Think harder and Go further; Start here uses stacks of at most 5 cubes and one fold line.
- Changed during review: exit question 3 is a circle choice (the drawing square was too small); Question 3 on page 1 names the order of the pictures in words (A, B, C from left to right) because the cube pictures cannot carry letters.
- Open: no Primary 1 paper uses these topics; marked unverified.
