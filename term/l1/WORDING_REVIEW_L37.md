# Wording review: Lesson 37 (Make Your Own Graph)

Every child-facing sentence (slides, workbook, exit ticket, home sheet, online practice, parent page) was read aloud against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1 to 25 and `docs/books/p1_school_paper_patterns.md` section 5 (picture graphs).

- Commands: Count and write; Draw; Circle one; Fill in the table; Write its number in the brackets; Study the graph; Look at the graph. Same command for the same job every time (rules 2, 14, 23). 'Study the graph. ... Answer questions 5 and 6.' and 'Look at the graph in question 5.' follow the school-paper habit of one graph for two questions (rule 23).
- Fixed question forms from the papers: 'There are [] more cats than fish.'; 'There are [] pets in all.'; 'The fewest children play (circle one).'; 'Ali ate [] more biscuits than Mei.'; 'Each picture stands for 1 pet.' (rule 12; section 5.3 of the patterns file). 'more ... than', 'fewest', 'in all' and 'altogether' only; never 'bigger' or 'most pictures' for numbers (rule 13).
- Numbers are digits ('1 picture', '1 group of 5', 'half a picture'); a box comes before the unit ('[] cups') and the answer frames end with the noun (rules 15, 16, 17). Half a picture is written in words because it is not a count.
- Key: every graph where a child reads a number says 'Each picture stands for 1 ...' in the stem or shows the key. Where the stem says it, the drawn key is left out of the workbook picture to save room; where the key is 2 (Go further) the key is always drawn.
- Words taught in Remember, Explore and the Mini-lesson: kind, pile, tally, graph, title, key. 'Tally' appears in Start here only after the Mini-lesson has taught it (page 1 question 2); the grown-up page and the parent words list explain each word.
- Wrong working belongs to 'a child in another class' (Talk). Cast names appear only in stories and clues (Ali, Mei, Siti, Ben, Lily, Jamie, Ravi) and never with a wrong answer or a poll count.
- No teaching sentence inside a question (rule 24): 'Each picture stands for 2 sweets.' is a fact the key states, not a method; hints carry the method ('Count in 2s').
- Rule 25 and the Primary 1 papers: Start here keeps a key of 1, at most 8 pictures in a row, one or two steps; no tally or collecting appears in a paper, so Start here teaches them in class first. A key of 2, half a picture, choosing a question and the missing row from a total are in Think harder and Go further, and the missing row from a total is the school-level 'complete the graph from two clues' item (Ai Tong, Term 4).
- Singapore English and context (rules 3, 22): pets, fruit, books, games, stamps, biscuits, toys, sweets, stickers; 'rubber'; 'maths'.
- Changed during review: 'one picture' and 'one group' became '1 picture' and '1 group' (rule 15); the sneak-peek kinds changed from spoons and shells to cups and buttons and socks because the spoon and shell pictures were hard to tell apart on paper; the graph questions that repeated the same numbers as a class picture were changed; exit question 3 has no picture because the exit page had no room.
- Open: no Primary 1 paper collects, tallies or sorts for a graph, and none uses a key of 2, so those parts are marked unverified.
