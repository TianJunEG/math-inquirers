# Wording review, Term 3 Lesson 3 (Time Passes)

Every child-facing sentence (sneak-peek, workbook S1-S6, T1-T4, G1-G3, I1, exit E1-E3, home H1-H5, slides, talk, look-back, online practice, parent page) was read aloud against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1-25 and `docs/books/p1_school_paper_patterns.md` (which overrides the pre-flight on time wording). Maths and answers were not changed by the review; 200 answer checks and 91 practice variants pass; 0 FAIL, 0 WARN.

## Changes made during review (old -> new, reason)
1. Time words. The pre-flight spoke times as "ten past four" and "quarter to"; the P1 papers use only o'clock and half past in words and digits for everything else. Child pages write o'clock and half past in words with the digits beside them (7:30), and every other time as digits only (4:15, 9:45). Quarter past and quarter to appear in no child or parent text (rule 25 and the 23 Oct decision).
2. am and pm. Written "8:30 am", "6:30 pm" (lowercase, no full stops, a space before), as in the recent P1 papers. Answer frames put the word after the blank ("He stops at ____ am."), the unit-after-blank pattern of rule 16.
3. S1 to S3 and E1, E3: story first, then the question, then the answer frame ("Ali starts to swim at 8:30 am. He swims for 1 hour. What time does he stop?" then "He stops at ____ am."). One fixed question form for the end of something: "What time does it end?"; for the start: "What time did it start?"; for the length: "How long?" (rule 12). The same words are used on every page, in the exit ticket and in home practice.
4. S1: "Draw the hands to show when it ends." is the command (rule 2); part (a) is the drawing, part (b) the written time, so there is one question per part (rule 18). The clocks are labelled "start" and "end" on the picture instead of in the sentence.
5. S4: "Which clock shows half an hour later?" with the three clocks labelled A, B and C and "Circle A, B or C." as its own part (the choice words print on the page), not "Circle the correct clock" inside the stem (rule 19).
6. S5: "How long? Circle one." with "half an hour" and "1 hour" printed as the choices; the longer stem made the choice words wrap in two.
7. S6 and the Mini-lesson table: head "Job, Starts, Lasts, Ends", the same four words in every table; the child fills the Ends column only ("Fill in the time each job ends.").
8. T3 and G1 (find the start): "Find the start time of each swim." and "It started at ____ pm." state what is asked; the method (go back) is in the hint, never in the question (rule 24). The ends and the lengths are in the same sentence as the story so the child never has to guess which number is which (rule 5).
9. T2: the question says "Each child paints for half an hour." once, then (a) and (b) name the child; the clocks were dropped so the digits are the only form (the page was overfull) and no spoken quarter word is needed.
10. G2: "Write am or pm after each time." is the one instruction; "1 hour and half an hour" is written as the quantity the child will turn into 90 minutes (the tutor hint), not as "an hour and a half" (a phrase P1 papers never use).
11. G3: "Mei and Ali both finish their homework at 4:30 pm. Mei works for 1 hour. Ali works for half an hour." with (a) and (b) for the two starts and (c) "Who starts first? Circle one." so the comparison is its own part.
12. Talk and Mini: the wrong claim is given to "one child" and "another child", not a cast name (decision D18); the claims are stated, the reasoning is not.
13. Investigation: labels First: and Next:, a sentence frame of jobs ("swim 1 hour, painting 1 hour, ...") and "Fill the time exactly. Write each start and end." in three short sentences; the text was 48 words and is now under the reading-load limit.
14. E2: "It is 2 o'clock. What time is it half an hour later? Choose the correct answer. Write its number in the bracket." with options (1) to (4) and a bracket at the right, school style (rule 23). The wrong options are 2:05, 2:15 and 3:00 (5 minutes, 15 minutes and 1 hour).
15. Banned words checked: "stretch" and "challenge" appear on no child page (the arm break says "make a clock", the workbook movement line says "reach up"); the report line says "add an hour or half an hour to a time and count back to a start".
16. Mi's lines contain no numbers or answers.
17. Parent summary trimmed to two sentences plus the beyond sentence; the "Next lesson" panel carries the plain request to allow a week for the cutting.

## Checked and left as is
- Fixed words: "later", "lasts", "starts", "ends", "half an hour" (30 minutes), "Circle one.", "Fill in the time each job ends.", "How long?". "Hour hand" and "minute hand" are tutor words; children hear "short hand" and "long hand" (the words of the first lesson of Term 3).
- Numbers: every quantity in a sentence is a numeral ("1 hour", "8:30 am"); number words appear only in tutor SAY and READ lines, which are spoken (rule 15).
- Names: Ali, Mei, Ravi, Lily, Kumar, Siti, Ben; no brands; no money in this lesson.
- Beyond-P1 statement: the parent page, the guide opening, `moe.note`, tutor prep and `beyond.parent` say plainly that one hour or half an hour later from an o'clock or half past start is Primary 1 work, and that quarter-time starts, a trip in two stages and a time that crosses noon are beyond Primary 1 on purpose; the lesson is never called MOE-aligned.
- Wording unverified against a Primary 1 paper: the two-stage trip (G1) and the shared-end comparison (G3); the papers show one-stage find-the-start only.
