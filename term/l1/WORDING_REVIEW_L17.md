# Wording review, Lesson 17 (Sharing and Grouping)

Every child-facing sentence (sneak-peek, slides, workbook S1-S7, T1-T4, G1-G3, I1, exit E1-E3, home H1-H5, talk, online practice, parent page) was read aloud against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1-25 and the school-paper patterns (Henry Park P1 multiplication and division quiz: "shares equally with", "packs ... in a bag", "how many more to make equal groups"). The tutor lines that are read to children (READ lines) were checked the same way. Maths and answers were not changed by the review; 127 answer checks and 158 practice variants pass; 0 FAIL.

## Changes made during review (old -> new, reason)
1. S4: "Sweets are shared equally among friends. Fill in the table." -> "Share the sweets equally among the friends. Fill in the table." Rule 2 (command first) and rule 1 (no passive).
2. S6: the stem ended with the bare command "Check it." -> stem is two plain sentences, part (a) says "Fill in the number sentence to check." and prints the sign (the sign is known from Lesson 15), part (b) "Does it match the 12 stickers? Circle Yes or No." Rules 2, 17, 19.
3. T2: the reading trap was a bare question ("How many people share?") -> "Ben shares 20 biscuits equally with his 3 cousins. Aisha says 3 people share the biscuits." then (a) "Is Aisha correct? Circle Yes or No." and (b) the frame. Rules 11, 19 (no wrong reasoning in the stem, only a claim), and the error-spotter is Aisha, not Siti.
4. T3: four parts (a) to (d) so that no sentence asks two things: circle Share or Group, then the number, for each story. Rule 18.
5. G1: "Then answer (a)." -> "Then answer (a) and (b)." and (b) uses the frame "there are [] sweets left over." Rule 16 (frame ends in the noun).
6. E3: part (b) first said "Check: 6 groups of 3 = [2]", which printed the answer to (a). Now "Check: [] × 3 = 18". Rule 6 (one right answer, nothing given away).
7. E2: the four stories were shortened to one line each ("Mei puts 12 sweets into bags of 4.", "Mei has 12 sweets and eats 4.", ...); options are numbered (1) to (4) with a bracket, one right answer, no option that also makes groups of 4. Rule 23.
8. H2: "Fill in the blanks. [] × 3 = 12. Ravi needs [] pots." (one stem, two blanks in the paper style, full stops). Rule 16.
9. Slide s-m3: "You can ignore it." -> "You do not need it today." (a kinder grown-up-sign line; the sign is never required).
10. Online practice c1: "Each plate has [] ." -> "Each plate has [] counters." Rule 16 (noun after the blank). The frame also says "are shared equally" (tense and plural).
11. Slide title: the title slide says "Share or Group?" so the title does not run into Mi's bubble; the lesson title Sharing and Grouping stays in the subtitle, the guide and the workbook.
12. "equal groups" (the Lesson 15 title) is written "same-size groups" or "groups of the same size" where it would otherwise look like a link to another lesson's title; the child words are the same ones used in Lesson 15 elsewhere (equal, each, groups of).

## Checked and left as is
- Fixed question words: "How many sweets does each friend get?", "How many pots does Ben use?", "in all", "left over", "How many more ... to fill one more bag?". No "bigger", no "divide" in any child text (the word is a grown-up word on the parent page only).
- Quantities are numerals everywhere ("12 sweets", "3 friends"); number words only inside spoken lines where the tutor reads a number aloud.
- Names: Mei, Ali, Ben, Ravi, Jenny, Wei, Lily, Jamie, Amelia, Jason, Siti (sneak-peek and home only), Aisha as the error-spotter. Siti is never the error-spotter.
- School-paper frames used: "[] groups of [] = []", "[] × [] = []", circle Share or Group (like Yes or No), MCQ with a bracket, "Show ... equally" and "Is ... correct? Circle Yes or No." True/False is kept for Lesson 18 and later.
- Teaching sentences are in slides and the script, not in questions (rule 24): "count Ben too" is only a hint and a slide chip; "left over means not enough to make another group" is on a slide.
- Beyond-Primary-1 statement: present in the moe note, the guide opening and the parent page (left over, the grown-up sign, all the ways to share 12); no MOE, SASMO or GEP claim anywhere.
