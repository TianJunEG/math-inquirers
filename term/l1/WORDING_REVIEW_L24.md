# Wording review, Term 3 Lesson 4 (Dollars and Change)

Every child-facing sentence was read aloud against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1-25 and `docs/books/p1_school_paper_patterns.md` (money section). Maths unchanged by the review; 184 answer checks and 63 variants pass.

## Changes made during review (old -> new, reason)
1. Cents are written 50¢ (number then sign) and "fifty-cent coin" in words; never "50c" and never $1.50 (P1 papers, 23 Oct decision). Answer frames put the sign as the papers do: "____¢" and "$____".
2. No amount mixes dollars and cents; the only mix is T3 ($1 coin, 60¢ pencil, answer in cents).
3. Coins and notes are named with hyphens in stems ("five-dollar note", "ten-cent coins"); pictures show the sign and number.
4. Fixed question words: "Count the money. Fill in the blank.", "How much change does he get?", "Which is worth more? Circle one.", "Circle the coins that make 55¢.", "Which set has the most money?" (rules 2, 12).
5. T4 part (b) "Set A is worth ____¢ more than Set C." replaced the school-paper phrase to avoid copying.
6. Story first, then the question, then the frame (S6, T1, T3, G1, G3, E2, E3), one question per part.
7. No method in a question: "count up" and "biggest first" are in hints and slides, not stems (rule 24); G1 says only "You may use each one as many times as you like" and the workbook hint says there is no $20 note.
8. The wrong claim (Talk) is given to "a child", not a cast name.
9. "Stretch" and "challenge" appear on no child page; Mi's lines have no numbers.
10. Look-back option labels shortened so each row fits one line.
