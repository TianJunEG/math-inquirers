# Wording review, Term 3 Lesson 7 (Fill the Grid)

Every child-facing sentence was read aloud against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1-25 and `docs/books/p1_school_paper_patterns.md`. There is no Primary 1 paper evidence for grids, so every item is marked wording unverified; the Term 1 and 2 command words ("Circle one.", "Tick True or False.", "Fill in", "Which ... goes in ...?") are used throughout. Maths unchanged by the review; 188 answer checks and 18 variants pass.

## Changes made during review (old -> new, reason)
1. Boxes are named by place ("the top middle box", "the bottom left box") or by a letter under the box in class and in the 4 by 4 grid; never "cell" (rule 3, 7).
2. Fixed question words: "Which shape goes in the box with the ? mark?", "Is the grid correct? Circle Yes or No.", "How many different shapes could go in each box?" (rules 2, 12).
3. "One of each" is the only name for the rule and is said the same way in every slide, question and tutor line; "Latin square", "elimination" and "constraint" appear only in tutor notes (rule 1).
4. Reasons are chosen, not written: S3 offers "The column has a circle and a square." or "The row has a circle and a square." (rule 19, 25).
5. The wrong claim in Talk time and the swapped grid in T3 are given to "a child", not a cast name (rule 4, D18).
6. T2 and E3 are stated without a picture ("Circle in its row, circle in its column: [] shapes."), so the question is short and the shapes need no figure (rule 18).
7. "I know because ___" appears only in the sneak-peek, where nobody is marked.
8. Number words only in tutor lines; digits in every written quantity (rule 15). Digits 1 to 4 appear only in the warm-up and at Go further.
9. Teaching sentences removed from questions (rule 24): "look along the row" and "cross out" live in hints and slides.
