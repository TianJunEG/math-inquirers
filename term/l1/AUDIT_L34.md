## Judgement lines (author)

- Tier principle: PASS. Start here is counting cubes in a picture, the cube you cannot see when the total is given, matching and writing a top view, adding a top view and the fold line of one fold. Fold once and punch once, the missing number, the other hole and fold twice are Think harder and Go further (Fold Twice, Punch Once; owner decision 6).
- Age-appropriateness for 6 to 8: PASS. One idea (a view shows only part of the thing). Objects first: blocks and a paper square, pencil dots instead of punching. A picture cannot show every cube, so every hidden-cube item gives the total or the top view (found while checking: a stack of cubes can hide a whole stack, so reading a top view from a picture is only used with stacks where no cube is hidden).
- Joining cold: PASS. Rows, doubles and counting cubes are re-taught; fairly safe to miss, Quest gate noted.
- Safeguarding and privacy consistent: PASS. No real punching, small pieces on the table, nothing held to the camera, no private chat, WhatsApp number only in the grown-up pages.
- Pictures and pages viewed at readable size: PASS with limits. All 6 workbook pages, home sheet, parent page and slides viewed. Cube pictures are small in the workbook (the figure has fixed blank space for 3 levels); the exit ticket pictures are very small, so exit question 3 is a circle choice, not a drawing.
