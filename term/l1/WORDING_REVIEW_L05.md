Format: old -> new, then a one-line reason. Strings are exact; (xN) = text appears in N places (slide, script, key).

Circle Yes or No. Then write how you know." (x2). 'the red ones', '10 red and 4 blue' had no noun; yes/no and why were two commands in one.
