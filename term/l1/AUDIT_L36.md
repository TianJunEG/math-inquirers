## Instrument A (gifted-style, 0 to 2 each)

1 Low floor, high ceiling: 2 Start here is counting rows; Go further reaches handshakes and squares of every size.
2 Depth over speed: 2 a system, not a guess; 'how did you miss none'.
3 Generalise: 2 3 + 2 + 1, then 4 + 3 + 2 + 1, then 5 + ...; the new-ones rule.
4 Justify: 2 the talk question (4 times 3 counts twice) and spot the repeat.
5 Multiple paths: 1 list, table or rows give the same count.
6 Challenge ladder: 2 Start here, Think harder, Go further, Investigation.
7 Student-led inquiry: 1 Explore asks children to find their own way to list.
8 Complexity / context: 1 handshakes and outfits are simple contexts.
9 Problem posing: 2 the Investigation and spare-time puzzle.
10 Metacognition: 1 look-back and 'how did you know you had every one'.
11 Pacing / compacting: 2 skip order, choice time, optional slide and menu.
12 Extension, not acceleration: 2 stays with counting; nothing the next lesson needs.
