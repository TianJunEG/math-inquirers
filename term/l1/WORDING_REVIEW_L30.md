# Wording review: Lesson 30 (Calendar Detective, with Mi's Challenge Quest)

Every child-facing sentence (workbook, exit ticket, home sheet, Quest Log, slides, parent page) was read against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1 to 25 and the Primary 1 paper patterns.

- Commands: Write; Fill in the blanks; Tick True or False; Look at the month page; Circle Yes or No (Quest). Same command each time (rules 2, 14).
- Calendar words: day, week, fortnight (only at Think harder), date, 'the day before', 'N days later'. 'Day before yesterday' appears once, at Think harder (T1), with today named in the stem. 'Month page' is used throughout, never 'calendar grid'. No quarter or 'a.m.' wording on a family page except Quest gate 1.
- Time in the Quest: digits 4:35, 2:30, 3:00, 6:30 and 7:00, with am or pm; no 'quarter past' or 'ten past' (P1 paper patterns). Gate 2 uses half an hour and one hour only.
- Money in the Quest: 50, 20, 20 and 5 with the cent sign on PLAY MONEY coins; whole dollars ($10, $7, $6, $9, $14), no $1.50 style, no prices on pages beyond the made-up story prices (owner decision: prices on hold).
- Wrong working: gate 7 says 'a child in another class writes 49 + 38 = 77' (rule 4, D18); no cast name makes the mistake.
- Quest tone: 'game with Mi', 'swims round it', 'You may skip a gate'. No test, exam, score, mark, rank, timer or 'pass/fail' word on a child or parent page (checked in the built PDFs by b_quest.py).
- 'Later' always means count on from tomorrow; 'a week later' is 7 days later and the same weekday. The word 'inclusive' never appears.
- Rule 24: stems carry no method steps; hints carry 'Count on from tomorrow'.
- Rule 25: 'whole weeks' and 'days over' are used for 100 days (Go further); the word 'remainder' and a division sign never appear.
- Changed during review: exit ticket E2 said 'Look at the month page' twice (second removed); S3 gained 'part b: 5 days later' to fill page 1; Quest page names and the '3 pages' mentions were changed to 4 pages in the script, tutor notes, grown-up note and the Lesson 29 hand-off.
- Open: no Primary 1 paper in the sample asks for days of the week; wording follows the P1 pattern for 'what day is it N days later' only by analogy (marked unverified). Days of the week are not a listed outcome (D6).
