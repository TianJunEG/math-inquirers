# Wording review: Lesson 33 (Copy on the Grid)

Every child-facing sentence was read against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1 to 25 and the Primary 1 paper patterns.

- Commands: Copy; Draw; Circle A, B or C; Circle Yes or No; Tick True or False; Fill in the empty boxes; Write the other routes. Same command for the same job every time (rules 2, 14).
- Fixed question forms: 'The counter moves [] right and [] up.'; 'There are [] routes in all.'; 'How many different routes?'. Numbers are digits, a box or line comes before the unit (rules 15, 16).
- Figures are labelled 'A, B, C' (rule 23). The first picture in Question 4 is called 'the first picture'.
- 'Step', 'right', 'up', 'route' and 'exact copy' are taught in Explore and the Mini-lesson; 'route' and R and U letters appear only at Think harder and Go further; Start here uses 'path'.
- Wrong working belongs to 'a child'; the cast names (Mei, Ravi, Ali, Ben) appear only as movers in stories (rule 4, D18).
- No teaching sentences inside questions (rule 24). G1 gives the meaning of the boxes only; the rule is in the hint and the optional slide.
- Rule 25: counting routes, corner numbers and a route through a corner are Think harder and Go further only; Start here keeps to grids up to 5 steps across.
- Changed during review: removed the left and down move from Think harder; Question 4 now shows the original beside A, B and C; Question 6 is a row of dots.
- Open: no Primary 1 paper uses grid-copy wording; marked unverified.
