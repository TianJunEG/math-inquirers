Every child-facing sentence (sneak-peek, workbook S1-S6, T1-T3, G1-G3, I1, exit E1-E3, home H1-H5, slides, talk, online practice, parent page) was read aloud against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1-25 and the school-paper patterns. Maths and answers unchanged by the review; 101 answer checks and 73 practice variants pass; 0 FAIL.

## Changes made during review (old -> new, reason)
1. S1: "Count in 5s. Fill in the missing numbers." with a one-row table and boxes (the number tiles were unreadable in print). The command is first and the boxes sit in the picture (rules 2, 16).
2. S2: "A frog jumps along the number line. Fill in the blanks." with parts (a) jumps, (b) size of each jump, (c) [] x [] = [2]; one question per part (rule 18).
3. S3 and S4: story first ("Ravi has 4 bundles of 10 straws. ..."), question second, frame last; numerals for every quantity; "in all" for totals (rules 11, 12, 15).
4. S6: "Read each statement. Decide if it is True or False." with a tick table, as in the Henry Park pattern; no reason is asked.
5. T1: "The top 2 rows are shaded." replaces a teaching sentence ("4 x 6 is double 2 x 6" now only in the slide and hint) (rule 24).
6. T2: "Colour the 2s yellow. Ring the 5s. Then fill in the blanks." is split into the colouring commands and one answer line; many commands but each is one short sentence.
7. T3: one yes or no question per part with printed Yes and No; 45 is beyond the printed square on purpose so a child must use the last digit.
8. G1 to G3: "Ring each number ..." is a separate sentence from the count; G3 gives the jump sizes first and the three parts follow; "How many more jumps" is the fixed gap wording.
9. Error-spotter: Wei (Talk) and Ben (optional slide), not Siti; names Ravi, Jenny, Mei, Ali, Ben, Wei.
10. The word "stretch" is banned on child and parent pages: the report line now says "extras". "Challenge" is not used on any child page.
11. Mi's lines contain no numbers or answers.

## Checked and left as is
- Fixed words: "jumps", "in all", "How many more ... ", "Fill in the missing numbers.", "Circle Yes or No.", "True or False" tick table, options (1) to (4) with a bracket.
- Beyond-P1 statement: the parent page and the tutor guide say plainly that the 3 and 4 tables and numbers in two tables are beyond Primary 1; the 2, 5 and 10 tables within 40 are Primary 1.
- Reading order: "6 x 5" is 6 jumps of 5; either order is accepted in a child's own answer.
