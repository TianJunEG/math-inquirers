# Wording review, Lesson 1 (Bundles and Singles)

Changes in `lessons/l01.json` (maths and answers unchanged). Format: old -> new, reason.

| # | Where | Old | New | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | S5 text | "Ali has 47 straws. He ties them into bundles of ten." | adds "Fill in the blanks." | Command was missing |
| 2 | S5 a | "How many full bundles can he make? ___" | "He can make ___ full bundles." | Blank had no unit |
| 3 | S5 b | "How many straws are left over? ___" | "___ straws are left over." | Blank had no unit |
| 4 | S6 | "She writes 43. Is Siti right? Write the number she should write, and say what she mixed up." | "She writes 43. That is not right. Write the correct number." | Three jobs in one question, and no space to explain |
| 5 | S7 | "...no single sticks... no bundles. Draw what each child has in the box." | "Ali has 6 bundles of ten and 0 single sticks. Mei has 0 bundles and 6 single sticks. Draw Ali's sticks and Mei's sticks in the box." | Plainer, parallel sentences |

| 9 | T4 | "Who has more, or do they have the same?" | "Who has more, or are they the same?" | Grammar |
