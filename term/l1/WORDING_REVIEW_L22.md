# Wording review, Lesson 22 (Five Minutes at a Time)

Every child-facing sentence (sneak-peek, workbook S1 to S6, T1 to T4, G1 to G3, I1, exit E1 to E3, home H1 to H5, slides, talk, look-back, online practice, parent page) was read aloud against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1 to 25 and `docs/books/p1_school_paper_patterns.md`.

## Changes made during review (old -> new, reason)
1. "Ten past four", "twenty to ten" and the quarter words were dropped from every child page: the papers write 5-minute times as digits. The tutor says "four ten". The parent page says not to say 'ten past' yet.
2. am and pm are written without full stops, lowercase, after a space (7:30 am), as in the recent papers. The kit mat still prints a.m. and p.m. (shared figure); the lesson says they are the same.
3. S1: "Write the time for each clock." (same command as the first lesson of Term 3); minutes always have two digits (9:05). S4 is a four-option choice with a bracket; distractors are the school patterns.
4. S5 and T1: "Circle am or pm." with one event per part (rule 18). T1 (c) is 12:30 on purpose: just after noon, so pm.
5. S6: "Ben and Mei leave home at different times. Look at the clocks." then the frame "___ leaves first." (rule 16); both times are in the same hour so the comparison is of minutes.
6. T2 reads "A film lasts 1 h 20 min." then (a) "1 h 20 min is ___ hour and ___ minutes." and (b) a circle choice; h and min are explained in the slides, not in the question (rule 24).
7. T3: the wrong claim is "A child says" (decision D18), stated as a time, with no wrong reasoning.
8. T4: the table has Show and Starts; "Ravi waits ___ minutes for the next show." Answer frames end with the unit (rule 16).
9. G1: "Mi starts to swim at 3:10 and stops at 4:05. Fill in the blanks." with parts (a) to (c) as a worked chunk; G2 and G3 ask the same question with no blanks for the chunks.
10. Mi's lines have no numbers. "challenge", "core" and "stretch" appear on no child or parent page.
11. Home sheet: H5 is the rewind item (Term 1 Lesson 2, missing numbers) copied from that lesson's home sheet.

## Checked and left as is
- Numbers are numerals in sentences ("3:10", "50 min"); number words appear only in tutor SAY and READ lines, which are spoken.
- Singapore things: bus, swimming, film, shows; no brands.
- "Minutes past the hour" in S2 and the home sheet is a measure, not the spoken time form that the papers do not use.
- Wording for hours and minutes (1 h 20 min) and for durations is unverified against school papers: kept at Think harder and Go further.
