## Medium

1. **Recording and greeting order is inconsistent with the corrected wording, and the notice is said after the recording has started.** The script runs REC at 0:08, then the DO line at 0:19: "Greet the whole group warmly. Do not use children's first names on the recording." Then the notice at 1:00 ("This lesson is recorded ...") and the microphone routine at 1:18. But `tutor_prep` (and the guide "Before the lesson") says "Greet each child by first name as they arrive, then start the recording, then say the notice; avoid first names while it runs", which is the corrected Lesson 20 wording ("Greet each child by first name as they arrive. Then start the recording. While it runs, avoid names."). The script line is the older Term 2 first-lesson text (`l11.json`). So the same file tells the tutor two things; and in either reading the children are told they are being recorded after the recording began. `privacy.html` says only "We tell the group at the start of each lesson", so the fix is cheap: say the notice before pressing record. Same defect as L18 finding 5, L19 and L20 finding 3. Fix: replace the DO line with the L20 line, move the notice (1:00) and the microphone routine before the REC cue, and add "then start the recording".
2. **The Explore discovery cannot happen with the kit.** Round 2: "Now move the long hand slowly round to the six, and watch the short hand" and the child action "Children move the long hand slowly to the 6 and watch the short hand creep" (17:52, 90 s). The kit hands are loose paper pieces on a face with no fastener (a design choice, D4), so the short hand does not move at all. The answer slide says the short hand "went half way to the next number". A child who leaves the short hand on the 12 reads 12:30 correctly by accident and never meets the hour-hand idea that T2, the Investigation and the Think harder trap depend on. The tutor cannot check either, because small paper clocks on camera are unreadable (the NOTE says so: "You cannot see small clocks on tiles"). Fix: tutor demonstrates on a shared-screen clock, and children are told "move the short hand half way between the 12 and the 1 yourself"; or give the kit hands a printed notch so the hour hand can be pinned to the minute hand's turn.
3. **Quarter past and quarter to are taught to the whole class in the Mini-lesson, against the guide's own note.** s-m4 and s-m4-ans ask "How many minutes past five is the first clock? the second?" and then say "Five fifteen is quarter past five. Five forty-five is quarter to six" (the hour name changes at "to": the classic misconception, introduced in about 45 seconds). The same guide NOTE says "Quarter past and quarter to are for Think harder: the digits matter most", and D5 says quarter and ten past belong only at Think harder or beyond. It is class instruction, not a Start here item, so it is not a tier breach, but it spends a quarter of an 11-minute Mini-lesson on non-core content in a lesson whose Start here takes 18 minutes for a middle child. Fix: keep the counting in 5s to 15 and 45 minutes on s-m4, drop the quarter words from the whole-class slide and teach them only at the Think harder reveal or in the Spare-time menu.
4. **Start here minutes do not match the script.** The guide's timing note says "about 16 minutes" in the NOTE, 18 in section 9 (10.0 + 8.0), and the child minutes in the lesson file sum to 18. The script gives page 1 8:20 (500 s) and page 2 8:40 (520 s), so a middle child is short by 1:40 on page 1 and reaches the check slide before finishing S3; in the slow class the work part is 27:15 of 28:00, with 45 seconds of slack. The choice slide then has 2:30. Same defect as L20 finding 2 (smaller). Fix: 10:00 and 9:00, funded from Explore (9:34 of 13:00).
5. **Tutor cannot see what children set on their clocks, and the thumb is the only check.** Explore, Mini-lesson and the Talk test all rely on each child laying hands on a 46 mm cut-out clock. The gallery shows faces, not tables; the guide forbids holding things to the camera (default 9) and asks only for "a thumb when a child has set the time". A tutor running 8 children has no way to see the 3:00 versus 12:15 mistake or the swapped-hands mistake the guide lists. It is acceptable for a first lesson only if choral reading is used: add one rule such as "say the time on three, then fingers for the hour (1 to 10) and a fist for 11 and 12", and make the kit clock bigger (see the workbook audit finding 2).
6. **The parent page and the L20 hand-off do not tell families to cut the clock.** The guide's "Before the lesson" says "Lesson 20's goodbye ... is not enough" and tells the tutor to message families. That is a manual patch for a one-line edit in `l20.json` that the pre-flight (section 5) asked for. Until it is made, the printed Term 2 parent page says the clock is optional and the workbook "print it". See the workbook audit finding 1.

## Low

7. **Footer collides with the card on two workbook slides.** s-ws1 (the S2 card) and s-ws2 (the S6 card) reach the footer line ("Math Inquirers Term 3 . Notice it. Test it. Explain why." overlaps the card's edge). Nothing is hidden; trim the S2 clock or the card padding.
8. **Workbook slides carry small clocks.** On s-ws1 the four S1 clocks are about 100 px across on a 1000 px frame (about 190 px at 1080p), and on s-exit the E1 clocks are about 65 px (125 px at 1080p), with the 6:30 hands overlapping. The guide already says "the clocks on the slides are small, read each option aloud". Acceptable for a shared screen only if every question is read aloud; the exit ticket (E1) should have a larger slide.
9. **Optional slide mislabelled.** s-opt is titled "Go further: half past 12" but it repeats Explore round 2's answer (half past twelve, 20:05) and is Start here level. The guide says it "links to the Investigation". Retitle it or replace it with a different time such as half past 6.
10. **Skip order refers to something that is not there.** "Then: cut the Mirror Clock preview from Talk time (it is in Go further anyway)": the Talk script has no mirror preview (it tests the 12 o'clock claim and re-reveals the sneak-peek). The mirror idea is never shown in class; a 30-second demo would help the Go further page and make the skip rule real.
11. **Stale timing assumption.** Section 9 says "papers to the camera 30 s", which contradicts default 9 (no papers held to the camera). The script itself never holds paper up. Delete the phrase from the model.
12. **Mi's calculator** on the title, look-back and end slides. `assets/mi_dolphin_nocalc.png` was added at 03:08 and `common.py` uses it automatically; the deck was built at 02:21. Rebuild.
13. **Look-back slide says "Three minutes"; the part is 3 minutes but the script gives 2:06** (the L20 finding 10 pattern in reverse: here consistent with the window, so only a wording note).
14. **"Mic off, say it on three, unmute to share"** is spoken in full at 1:18 in this lesson; it is the same words as Term 2 (consistent) but long for minute 1:18 of a first lesson for new children who also get the routine slide at 1:41: about 40 seconds of rules before any maths.
