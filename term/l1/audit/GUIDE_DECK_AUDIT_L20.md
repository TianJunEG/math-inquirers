## Medium

1. **The end-of-term check item for this lesson is the Mini-lesson "Your turn", answered aloud seven minutes earlier, and the key says otherwise.** Check Q8 is "6 buns cost $18. 1 bun costs $__. 4 buns cost $__." The Mini-lesson s-m4 is "6 pencils cost $18. How much does 1 pencil cost? How much do 4 pencils cost?" and the tutor reads "One pencil costs 3 dollars. Four pencils cost 12 dollars" at 30:54 (s-m4a shows it too); the same numbers return as workbook T3 and G2. Likewise Q6 (4 bags of 5 sweets, then 20) is L18's exit ticket E1 (Ali has 4 bags of 5 sweets), and Q7 follows L19's S6 pattern with different numbers. The check key states "Each question is a fresh example ... (numbers chosen so that they do not repeat a lesson page)": untrue for Q8 and Q6. A correct Q8 shows recall of the last ten minutes, not that the child can find one first. Fix: Q8 "8 buns cost $16; 1 bun; 5 buns" ($2, $10) and Q6 new numbers; change the Your turn numbers instead if the check is kept.
2. **Think harder is unreachable and Start here overruns, after a 20-minute check.** Script: 5:20 for questions 1 to 3 and 5:20 for 4 to 6, 3:30 choice time. The guide's per-question middle-child times are S1 to S3 2.5 each (7.5) and S4 3, S5 3, S6 2.5 (8.5): 16 minutes against 10:40 allotted, so the middle child is about 5 minutes late and the choice slide is theirs only in name. The NOTE already says pages 3 to 5 are for home because "the check came first, so some children are tired". The guide's table flags workbook, talk and look-back OVER in the slow class (22:06, 4:27, 2:06) while the 5-minute flex absorbs it. Fix: allot 8:00 + 9:00 (take 3 minutes from the slack in Explore, 9:22 of 12:00) and drop S6 from the read-aloud, or say plainly that the workbook is Start here only.
3. **Recording and greeting order contradict each other.** REC cues run from 0:15 and the recording notice is said at 0:48, but the DO line (1:20) says "greet each child by first name before you start the recording. While it runs, avoid names" and the prep says "Start the recording after the greeting". If the recording starts after the greeting, the notice is not on it; if at 0:00, first names may be. `privacy.html` says names are not shown and that the group is told at the start of each lesson. Same defect as L18 finding 5 and L19. Fix: greet before recording, then start, then the cue and the notice.
4. **The recording carries the whole check, so a child who is absent can hear the questions before taking the make-up check; the make-up route is thin.** The tutor reads all eight questions and the optional one aloud (38:26 to 48:29) while recording; only the answers are never said. A child who misses the lesson "watches the recording" (guide, Joining cold) and "takes the check at the make-up slot", with no instruction to skip chapter 37:00 to 57:00 or the Mini-lesson 30:54 answers. The REC cue at 37:25 ("the private check is taken live or at the make-up slot. Ask your grown-up how to take it") tells a viewer nothing and invites them to pause and take it from the recording. The check key's checklist says "pause it [the recording] during silent work if the platform allows", which the lesson guide never says. Fix: tell make-up families to take the check first and watch the lesson after, or cut the check chapter from the shared link; say in the guide when to pause or mark the chapter.
5. **Two slides print the method before the discovery, and workbook thumbnails are unreadable.** s-explore3 ("Find one, then many") carries the strip and "Find 1 pen first: share $20 into 4 equal parts. Then take 3 parts" while the SAY (18:19) and the lesson's idea ("Find one first, then find many. That is our idea for today", 21:16) come after the children try; s-m1 prints "Step 1: find 1 ... Step 2: find many" alongside the strip before the tutor asks. On s-ws1 and s-ws2 the strips of S3, S4 (and T1 on its slide) are thumbnails about 130 px wide with numbers about 8 px high, unreadable at a distance; the READ lines do give the numbers. The speaker note says "Reveal one step at a time" but the deck is static. Fix: question first, strategy on a following slide; show the strips at the s-m1 size.
6. **Numbering clash.** Deck "LESSON 10 . TERM 2 . LEVEL 1"; guide "Lesson 10 of 10"; the same guide says "Lessons 15 to 18" and "Lesson 21: What Time Is It?" for next term, and the s-end slide says "the Lesson 21 workbook". The Term 3 workbook will say "Term 3 . Lesson 1". A tutor or family cannot tell which pages to print (same as L18 finding 6).

## Low

7. **Camera wording invites page-showing.** "Cameras on if you can, so I can see your page and hands" (0:26) and "Check that the page and hands can be seen" (1:20) conflict with owner default 9 (gallery view, no paper to the camera). Say "so I can see you".
8. **Materials list incomplete.** "Mark your own in another colour" (30:54, 63:47, 70:42, 81:00) needs a second pencil colour that is not listed; the check sheet needs "a pencil and a rubber" (listed) but also "a closed envelope" that the tutor must have sent a week before (prep line 86, not in the child list of the guide); the 20 tokens, 3 to 6 shop items and counters are listed.
9. **Optional exit ticket E2.** The four options ($7, $10, $3, $25) are neither on s-exit nor in the READ line at the flex time (the READ says "Write its number in the bracket" only). Same defect as L17 and L18; optional here.
10. **s-look says "Three minutes"** while the part is 2 minutes (83 to 85) and the script gives 0:45 of child time.
11. **Spare-time "Is it always true?"** ("if 1 pen costs $3, do 2 pens cost $6 and 3 pens $9") is a definition; the answer even mentions bulk prices, which the children never see. A genuine test: "Mei says the price of 1 gets bigger when we buy more pens. Is that true?" with a Shop A and Shop B pair.
12. **"Your child also took the end-of-term check"** (parent page) is untrue for a child who missed the lesson or whose family opted out (the key allows both). Say "may have taken".
13. **Check-sheet wording.** The instruction "Fill in the correct answers in the spaces provided" is school-paper language; the workbook says "Fill in the blanks". Blanks are dashed underlines in Q1 to Q3, Q6 and Q8 and boxes in Q4 and Q5: inconsistent for six-year-olds who learn "write in the box".
14. **Mi's calculator** on four slides (owner decision 6).
