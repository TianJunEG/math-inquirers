## Method

- Home sheet, answers and parent page rendered and read; margins measured (home sheet left 14.0 mm, right 14.0 mm, text ends 8.2 mm from the bottom; parent page left 15.1, right 15.5). All five home answers solved independently: H1 short; H2 5:00; H3 8:30 (long hand on the 6, short half way between 8 and 9); H4 45 minutes (the 9 is 9 fives); H5 29 (2 tens and 9 ones). All match the answer page.
- Online practice served over HTTP and driven in Playwright 1.56 (Chromium) at 390 x 844 (phone) and 1280 x 800 (laptop). Every tier and item opened (Start here 8, Think harder 3, Go further 3). For each item a wrong answer, a correct answer and the card after it were captured; for the ordered set r2 I tried the correct order `4,5,6` (accepted), spaces `4 5 6` (accepted), `6,5,4` (refused: "Those are the right numbers, but not in the right order. Check the order."), `4,5,6,7` ("Some of these fit, but one does not"), `4,5` ("Those fit. Are there more?"), words `four five six` ("Type or choose an answer first. It is fine to guess.") and `4-5-6` (treated as 4, -5, -6: "Some of these fit, but one does not"). Multi-box time items (c1, c5, t1): a right hour with a wrong minute gives "Look again at the boxes with a red edge", so wrong orders are refused box by box.
- Variants: all 8 templated items (c1, c2, c3, c5, t1, t2, t3, g1) were re-driven: for each, "Try a similar one" was pressed 30 times after a correct answer, the shown question and clock label read, the right answer computed independently (clock label to hour and minutes, 5 x n, 12 - k, mirror half past k as 12:00 minus k:30), typed into the boxes shown, and "Check" pressed. 240 of 240 variants were marked "Yes, that is right", 0 missing boxes, 0 console errors. Pictures: every clock variant has a drawn clock whose aria-label matches the question; t2 variants pair "A child says ... 9:30" with a clock labelled 8:30 (and equal clocks for the "yes" cases), as designed.
- Network: only `index.html`, `practice.js`, `fonts.css`, three font files and `data/t3l21.json`, all same origin; no external request, no cookies, no analytics. The page source holds no answers (they sit in `data/t3l21.json`, which any browser can fetch: shared with every lesson).

## C. Online rules

No timers, no scores, no streaks, no leaderboard; "There is no score and no timer. Try as many as you like, in any order. A grown-up can read along." on every card. Wrong answers get a hint, then the worked answer. Keyboard: Enter submits, focus goes to the first box, the question dots are buttons with aria labels; the dark-mode palette is defined. At 390 px width nothing overflows; the clocks and mirror clocks scale to about 270 px. The mirror faces carry no numerals and the label "mirror" in large type. Runner "rewind" items are absent in this lesson (noted, as in the brief).
