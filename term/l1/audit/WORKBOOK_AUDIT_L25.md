## E. Layout and usability for a 6-year-old (A4)

Pages 1 to 5 flow; every page starts with an instruction line; Name and Date on every page; Mi on page 1. Page 1 is full, page 2 about 75% full, page 3 and 4 about 85%, page 5 full and tight as in L23 and L24.

**Pictures at A4.** Price tags are drawn 20 to 24 mm wide on pages 1 and 2 (comfortable), about 17 mm on page 3 (T4: five tags in a row, the prices are legible, the item icons are small) and the item icons are clear except one (finding 3). The money in S5: the two $10 notes measure about 13 mm by 8 mm (at 250 dpi) and the words PLAY MONEY under each are about 1 mm tall, 43 mm to the right of the tag; the L24 kit notes are 25 to 29 mm. A child who has the kit note in hand sees a picture half its size (finding 6). The strips (S2, T2, G1) are drawn to scale (cup 8 is 47% of bag 17; glue 5 is 45% of cup 11) and the red gap bracket is easy to see.

**Kit page 6** (and `L25_Kit_Pages.pdf`): 12 tags in 3 columns by 4 rows, about 46 x 37 mm each; 9 with a picture and a price ($2, $3, $4, $5, $7, $8, $12, $14, $18), 3 blank. The dashed cut cells are 3 mm apart across rows and 4.5 mm across columns (the cut-out audit rule F10 asks 6 mm); the grid sits left of centre, leaving a 30 mm strip on the right and 15% of the page empty at the bottom, so the tags could be larger. The page says "A grown-up cuts out all 12", "Print this page on its own sheet" and where to keep them; the "no printer" alternative is in the materials list but not on the page (finding 8).

**Kit contract against tasks.** The contract (`kit_contract`) claims 9 priced tags supplied, at most 5 used by Explore, Mini and T4, and 0 blank tags used. In fact the lesson shows tags the kit does not supply: S3 pen $7 and ball $8 (the kit $7 tag is a ruler, $8 a cup), S4 $16, $11, $13, T1 $43, $26, $15, Investigation $11, and the slide `s-m5` five tags $5, $7, $8, $10, $13 (the kit has no $10 and no $13; its book is $12). The workbook page hints say "use your price tags" (page 1) and "Use your price tags or draw" (page 3), and the Investigation says "Pick the $3 tag"; a child cannot lay out S4, T1 or the Investigation's $11 with the supplied pieces (finding 2).

## F. Pedagogy

Progression: compare two, gap, total, cheapest of three, change, money needed; then three prices, total and gap with strip, total and change, exact amounts; then the hidden price, three prices, a $100 story. Exit ticket matches the lesson (gap, total as MCQ, change from $20). Timing: guide estimate for Start here 17.0 minutes (2 + 3 + 3.5 + 2.5 + 3 + 3) against 8:10 + 8:40 allotted: fits; Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are for home for most children, and the page says so on the choose slide, not on the pages (finding 9). Think harder and Go further are genuinely different from Start here; T2 and G1 are the same structure at different sizes, which is a deliberate ladder. Class numbers: Explore uses $3, $5, $7 and cup $8 with ball $14; the Mini uses $11 and $4, $13 and $5, $10 and $2, $3 and $13, $16 and $11; the workbook reuses the cup at $8 (S2), a ball at $13 (S5), E1 $14 and $5 (gap 9, against the Mini's $13 and $5), and the kite and top pair of the Talk question returns in G2 (Low, finding 10). Misconceptions are targeted: adding for a gap (S2, E1), bigger picture means dearer (S1), swapping "more" and "less" (talk and sneak-peek 2), stopping at one way (T4).
