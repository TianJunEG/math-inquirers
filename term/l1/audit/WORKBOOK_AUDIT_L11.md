## Verdict: READY AFTER MINOR FIXES

The maths is right throughout and the content is genuinely P1 at Start here. The weaknesses are practical: one ambiguous "between" on a round table (two defensible answers, and the key itself names the second), pictures drawn too small to write in or to read on A4 (the S2 answer boxes, the round-table labels), an Investigation with no room to draw, and a cut-out kit that is double what the lesson uses. Counts: 0 High, 5 Medium, 9 Low.

## B. Age and syllabus fit

Ordinals 1st to 10th and the position words are Primary 1 (the lesson's `moe.note` says so correctly). Start here is within that: places in a line of up to 10, one clue, "how many in front". The both-ends count (T1, H4), "between" (T2, T4, G2), a line facing the other way (T3), seating round a table (G1) and a race order from "beat" clues (G3) are contest-style and sit at Think harder or Go further only, which matches owner decision 6. Nothing beyond P1 is needed for Start here. The lines grow to 13 children at Go further only (G2): acceptable.

## C. Tier principle

Start here (S1 to S7) is approachable and explored in class (Explore rounds 1 to 3, the warm-up and the clue slides cover every item). Two partial cases: S5 (the place from the back in a line of 6) needs the "turn the line around" idea, which is done once in Explore round 3 with the child's own row, so it is borderline for a child who did not have six things to hand; and, at Think harder, T2's picture prints every ordinal 1st to 10th under the figures, so the child can read the answer places off the figure and the "between" idea is not really tested (finding 8; T2 is easier than S5). Think harder T1, T3, T4 are the taught idea in a new setting (T1 repeats the s-m6 slide with bigger numbers). Go further G1, G2, G3 are real extensions; G2 is T4 with a middle-child step. No contest-style item is in Start here. Exit ticket E1 to E3 are all Start here items (same kind as S7, S5, S4 plus a two-clue chain) and match the lesson objective.

## D. Wording against the guide

Mostly on target: commands first ("Study the picture", "Fill in", "Count"), numerals for quantities, "right behind" is used consistently, names are varied (the error-spotter is Kumar in T1, not Siti), no teaching inside a stem except one hint-like command in T3 ("Count the places from the door", Low, but it is needed to fix the direction). Rule 16 (do not mix box and blank in one question) is broken in S3: (a) is two boxes, (b) is a `___`; defensible because (a) is a number and (b) is a place word, but flagged Low (finding 12). T1(b) "There are ___ children in the line." prints with no gap between the blank and "children" on the page. "Right behind" is used in S4 and E3 without any definition on the page; it is taught on a slide only (home users of the workbook have only the hint to the tutor).

## E. Layout and usability for a 6-year-old

Page 1: S1 is clear (tick table is big and good). S2 and S3 have 5 mm figures. Page 2: one question per card, good white space, picture before answer line: this is the best page. Page 3: two drawing boxes (T1, T4) with room; T2 and T3 pictures are small (T2 labels are about 5 pt). Page 4: round table is small; labels collide with figures; the bottom fifth of the page is empty. Page 5: Investigation + exit ticket + look-back on one page is crowded. Page 6: kit page prints clearly, cards are large and the dotted cut lines are visible; half the page is empty. Footers sit 5.8 mm from the paper edge on every page (measured on pixels), below the usual 6 mm unprintable band: the page number may be clipped on some printers. Left and right margins are 14.0 and 14.5 mm, enough for a left binding.

## F. Pedagogy

The sequence is good: objects, then cards, then the other end; Start here moves from reading places, to clues, to turning the line round, to counting in front. The Think harder items turn the idea in four different ways. The Investigation (two places for one child add to one more than the line) is a real inquiry. Weak points: (1) the cardinal versus ordinal contrast, which objective 1 names ("tell 4 from 4th"), is only tested in S3 and E1 and the number cards that were made for it are never used (finding 4); (2) the time claim in `timing.notes` is optimistic (finding 10); (3) misconceptions are well targeted in the "Watch for" lines (4 for 4th, 2th, wrong end, counting a child twice); "first as 0" is not addressed.

## G. Safeguarding and inclusion

Private paper; no ranking; labels neutral (Start here, Think harder, Go further, Choose, Kit). The choice note says "everyone may choose". Names are mixed and inclusive (Aisha, Kumar, Wei, Jenny, Lily, Mei, Ali, Ben, Jason, Siti, Ravi). "I am still stuck (my grown-up will photograph this page for my tutor)" is on the look-back page, consistent with the WhatsApp-only photo route. The photographed page also carries the Investigation and the exit ticket, so the parent shares those too (fine, but the tutor guide should say that the whole of page 5 is photographed).

## H. Consistency with the other lessons

Box and blank style, header badge, the Mi mascot only on page 1, the look-back block and kit page match the Term 1 conventions. Term 2 footers and the "Term 2 . Lesson 1" badge are correct (no stray "Lesson 11 of 10").
