## What works

One idea at a time in a sensible order: sort cards by checking, name what went wrong, add back, check a different way, find one wrong step, a wrong time, a wrong total, then the "same way again" trap at Talk time. The warm-up re-teaches adding back, the tens jump and one hour later, so a child who joins cold follows (the guide says all that is needed is counting to 100 in tens). Explore gives every child the 8 cards and asks the tutor to scan; the answer slides follow each round and the question slides carry no answers (`s-explore1` lists the 8 sums without saying which are right; the Mini-lesson example slides ask, the next slide answers). The guide forbids "you made a mistake" and requires "the sum has a mistake"; wrong working belongs to "a child in another class", never a cast name or a child in the group, throughout the script, slides and workbook. The safeguarding block is consistent with the script (paper cards, nothing in mouth or ears, no matches or toothpicks, nobody cuts, main room only, no paper held to the camera, gallery view, no private chat, WhatsApp-only route, no names with answers, passes allowed, a safe movement break). The beyond-P1 statement is in the strand note, the "Plain statement" NOTE, `beyond`, tutor_prep and the "Before the lesson" box, and is honest; no MOE-aligned claim. The hand-offs to and from this lesson are true (Lesson 28's 8 mistake cards; the Quest Log, a partner and the 7 day cards for Lesson 30).

## Timing and tutor load

Script 67:25 of 90, slow class 79:37, last 5 minutes untouched (flex 0:34 of 5:00). One tutor can run it: main room only. Three realism points:

- **Explore round 1** gives 2:30 for children to check eight cards a different way and sort them (about 19 seconds a card) and 1:40 in round 2 to fix four cards and write four answers. For a six-year-old who has to add back each take away (a second sum per card) this is tight; the skip order only shortens round 3 (finding 6).
- **Workbook allotment.** The script allows 8:00 for page 1 and 8:40 for page 2, but the guide's own per-question times add to 9.5 and 9.0 minutes for a middle child, so a middle child cannot finish a page inside its slot; the guide's NOTE says 17 minutes and the question table 18.5 (finding 7). A slower child (the guide says 1.7 times as long) needs about 31 minutes for Start here against a slow-class work allowance of 29:21; the guide's remedy (the check slides cover every answer, pages 3 to 5 are for home) is stated.
- **Exit ticket** 3:10 for E1 to E3 (two two-digit sums and a bracket item): enough for a quick child, tight for a slow one.
