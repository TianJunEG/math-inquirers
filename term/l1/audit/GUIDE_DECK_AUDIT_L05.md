### Medium

**1. Sneak-peek puzzle 2 contradicts itself.**
- Location: slide 5 (s-sneak), guide Sneak-peek READ at 9:41 and section 4.
- Evidence: "Ben has 9 red counters and 5 blue counters. He moves 1 blue counter to the red counters. Now he has 10 red counters and 4 blue counters." A blue counter moved to the red group is still blue, so he has 9 red and 1 blue in that group, and the next sentence is false. The Back-to-sneak-peek slide then shows all-red frames (9 + 5 and 10 + 4) and the note says "moved 1 counter from the blue group to the red group". The question is the key idea of the lesson (moving does not change the total), and a literal child will say "No, the counter is blue".
- Fix: "Ben has 9 red counters in one frame and 5 in another. He moves 1 counter from the second frame to the first. Now the first frame has 10 and the second has 4. Is the total still the same?" (the colour goes).

**2. The recording statement is not in the spoken script, and it is incomplete. [ST]**
- Location: Opening (0:38 SAY, NOTE), Before the lesson, slide 1 note.
- Evidence: the SAY line is "Good morning, inquirers! Cameras on if you like, pencil ready ..." with no mention of recording. The statement exists only as a NOTE ("Remind the group once, in one line, that the lesson is recorded (you and the screen only)"), which a tutor can skip. Lesson 4 includes it as a SAY line. Both say "me and the screen only", but `website/privacy.html` says a child's voice may be heard if they speak and a parent can ask for the child to be muted; the tutor greets "each child by first name" (0:56) on the recording. The setting that excludes children's video is unconfirmed in the privacy notice itself ("[Confirm the platform setting ...]").
- Fix: make it a SAY line before the first child speaks, add the voice and mute sentence, and name the setting.

**3. Papers to the camera: false privacy claim and unworkable timing. [ST]**
- Location: Sneak-peek 11:00 (HOLD UP, 25 s), Exit 79:11 (25 s), slide 25 note ("so only the tutor sees it"), Exit NOTE (S, D, H mark for each child), slide 8 note ("Everyone holds their build to the camera on a count of three, if you wish").
- Evidence: in gallery view everyone sees every paper; 25 s for 8 papers is about 3 s each, then the exit answers are read from 80:01 while the tutor is also meant to mark 8 children S, D or H. A 6-year-old's handwriting in a tile is unreadable. No instruction on how to hold the paper. A child with the camera off has no route. The explore note adds a third hold-up that the safeguarding block ("once") does not allow.
- Fix: as Lesson 4 finding 3; delete the explore hold-up or make it the one allowed moment.

**4. "I am still stuck" cannot reach the tutor. [ST]**
- Location: Look-back (slide 27 note, guide 3 and 4, workbook page 5), safeguarding block.
- Evidence: the page promises "my tutor will come and help" but the look-back is never shown or collected; the check-in is "in the next lesson", a week later.
- Fix: give the child a route that works in the lesson, or remove the promise.

**5. Camera and desk logistics are assumed. [ST]**
- Location: Opening (0:38 "Cameras on if you like" against the DO line "check their camera shows their page and hands"), `tutor_prep`.
- Evidence: the opening both allows a camera to be off and requires the desk view; no set-up instruction for families; the routine and rules slides are skipped after Lesson 1, so a child whose first lesson is this one is never told how to show a paper, signal for help or mute. The explore asks the tutor to see 8 children build 4 sums on paper, which a face camera cannot show.
- Fix: a 20-second routine slide each lesson (mute, show paper, hand signal) and a one-line parent set-up; replace "tutor checks the building" with "show me with your fingers".

**6. Slides print the answer while the question is being asked; one warm-up slide does not match the questions.**
- Location: slide 4 (s-rem3), slide 10 (s-m1), slide 13 (s-m3b), slide 2 (s-rem1).
- Evidence: s-rem3 caption "30 and 6 more is 36" is visible while the tutor asks "Thirty and six more makes?" (first of three; the other two stay hidden). s-m1 prints "8 needs 2 more to make 10" under the question "How many more does eight need to make ten?" (28:38). s-m3b prints "Fill the 8: move 2", "Fill the 6: move 4" and "Fill the number that is nearly full" while the tutor asks "Which way moves fewer counters?" (31:40). s-rem1 keeps "There are 7 counters in the frame" and a picture of 7 while the tutor asks about 4, 9, 5, 2. This breaks the brief's "answers hidden until the reveal" and the author's own rule on a deck that otherwise follows it (Lesson 4 is clean).
- Fix: reveal on a click or a second slide; for s-rem1 use a picture-less version ("How many more make 10? 4"), or change the caption each time.

**7. The Investigation is solved for everyone before page 5.**
- Location: slide 13 (s-m3b), script 31:40 to 32:16, slide 6 (s-explore1) "WHAT IF? Fill the frame a different way", Investigation (guide section 4).
- Evidence: the Investigation asks "Which way moves fewer dots? Does the same way always move fewer?" The tutor says "Fill the number that is nearly full. The eight only needs two." (32:07) and the slide prints the rule; the slide note says "This is the seed of the Investigation, so do not push the rule: ask and listen", which the script does not follow. The WHAT IF card on slide 6 has no script line and no time.
- Fix: stop at "Which way moves fewer counters?" and take answers; leave the rule off the slide; give the what-if card one script line or delete it.

**8. Workbook page 2 is under-timed, with fixed check times.**

**9. Materials load and next-lesson notes are wrong in both directions.**
