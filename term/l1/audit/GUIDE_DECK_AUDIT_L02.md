## Verdict: READY AFTER MINOR FIXES

Maths is correct and the explore with a physical number line and a frog is the best hands-on design of the three lessons. The problems are an answer leaked on an optional slide, a Go further item that breaks owner decision 6, the lesson's own idea ("nearer ten") being heavier than the title says, and a materials set-up that the previous lesson told families they did not need.

## Findings

### Medium

**M12. Answer shown on the optional Go further slide.**
Location: deck slide 31 (s-opt "Go further: halfway"), notes and guide 85:00 optional line.
Evidence: the slide asks "What is halfway between 0 and 50? How do you know?" and the number line already has 25 highlighted in yellow on it. AUTHORING: answers hidden until the reveal slide.
Fix: remove the highlight (reveal with a click or a second slide).

**M13. Go further G2 is a "meet in the middle" contest move placed at Go further; the guide says Think harder only.**
Location: guide section 1 row "Contest-style move" ("numbers between two marks; meet in the middle; clue puzzle using halfway ... Used at Think harder only") against section 4, G2 ("Mei's frog is on 28. Ali's frog is on 52. They hop towards each other ... On which number do they meet?", Go further, page 4).
Evidence: owner decision 6 moves contest-style items to Think harder; the guide's own statement is false for G2. G1 (equal jumps with 3 boxes and a jump of 3) is also contest-flavoured.
Fix: move G2 to Think harder (swap with T4) or correct the guide statement and record an owner exception.

**M14. The lesson's declared idea is "nearer ten" but the title, objective ordering and Start here are about number words and the line.**
Location: guide 10:39 NOTE ("Many children will write 30 because of the 3 in 38: that is the idea of the lesson"); sneak-peek puzzle 2; Talk time (14 vs 40, about number words); mini-lesson slides s-m5 and Explore 20:08 (about 3 minutes on "nearer").
Evidence: the strand row says nearer ten is "informal enrichment ... never called rounding" and it is the syllabus Primary 4 idea; Start here has no "nearer" item at all (T1 only). So the sneak-peek reveal, which the whole Talk time returns to ("Back to the sneak-peek"), is practised by Think harder T1 only. For a joining-cold child the sneak-peek asks for an idea that is not in Start here.
Fix: either change the sneak-peek puzzle 2 to a number-name or one-before question (an idea Start here teaches), or add one "which ten" item to Start here with the frog.

**M15. Closing and parent page say "pencil and paper only" for Lesson 3, which needs digit cards and the kit.**
Location: guide 85:25 "Next lesson is Digit Cards. You need only a pencil and paper. Please keep your number line"; slide 29 (s-end); parent page "Lesson 3 ... only a pencil and paper".
Evidence: L3 guide "Before the lesson": print and cut both kit pages (digit cards, tens bars and ones squares), or write 0 to 9 on ten scraps. Common finding M7 in the L1 audit.
Fix: "pencil, paper, and your digit cards cut out (page 6 of next week's workbook)".

**M16. Joining cold: "Our rules are the same as before" with no rules slide.**
Location: guide 1:01 SAY; NOTE "Lessons after the first skip the routine and rules slides".
Evidence: the child who joins here never saw the four inquirer rules (slide 3 of Lesson 1). "Same as before" is meaningless to them, though the line does name the three rule words.
Fix: one-line say "Notice it. Test it. Explain why. Mistakes help us, you can pass." with no "same as before"; or add the rules slide to every Opening.

**M17. Frog placement cannot be checked online.**
Location: Explore 19:26-23:23 (place the frog "in the gap between 40 and 50", "a little past 20", "just past 80").
Evidence: the tutor needs to see a toy on a table in eight tiny camera tiles to judge "a little past". The guide says "Nobody is named for a wrong place" but also scans cameras; no camera set-up (common M11).
Fix: have children say the two tens aloud as the check ("between 40 and 50"), or draw the frog position on the paper line; do not rely on the camera.

**Common to all three lessons (see `GUIDE_DECK_AUDIT_L01.md` for evidence and fix):** M1 hold-up visible to all; M2 no audio/mute protocol; M3 "inquiry journal" and "tutor tracker" do not exist (guide 80:04 and the note at 80:04-80:05, slide note on s-exit); M4 recording statement, first names and children's voices (Opening 0:38); M5 "I am still stuck" route; M6 exit questions not read aloud and the slide E1 has no number (the word "seventy-three" is not on the slide at all); M8 Start here fills the slot (page 2 needs 12 min of 10:40); M9 tutor load and the tutor console does not know Term 1; M11 camera set-up.
