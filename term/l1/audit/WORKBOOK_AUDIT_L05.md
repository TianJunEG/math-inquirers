## B. Age and syllabus fit

Adding more than two 1-digit numbers and addition within 100 are P1. Mental addition crossing a ten (28 + 6, 47 + 6, 56 + 8, 38 + 5) is placed by the plan in Start here, but the MOE P1 mental outcome in `syllabus_by_level.md` is "2-digit plus ones without renaming", and school P1 quizzes stay about 40 (WORDING_GUIDE rule 25); here totals reach 64, 80 (G1) and 100 (G3). The lesson's own `moe.note` asks the owner to confirm this placement. Treat sums over about 40 as Think harder.

## C. Tier principle

Start here: S1-S3 are approachable and untaught-needed-free (ten-frame, dots, bonds). S4 and S5 are scaffolded by blanks ("28 needs ___ more to make 30"): low floor, acceptable. S6 c-d (47 + 6, 56 + 8, no scaffold) and S7 (spot the mistake in a method the child has just met) need the taught method: partial violations. Think harder T1-T4 are right (three or four addends, a reasoning item, a story). Go further G2 and G3 are real extensions; G1 is T4 with more numbers (pairs are visible), thin for Go further. No contest-style item sits in Start here.
