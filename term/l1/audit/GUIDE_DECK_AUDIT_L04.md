## 2. Findings

### High

**1. Wrong maths in the tutor notes: "29 is the end of its row".** Severity High (the tutor is told to say a false statement to children).
- Location: `L04_Tutor_Guide.pdf`, section 7 slide list: slide 5 (s-rem3) note, slide 11 (s-m1) note, slide 13 (s-m3) note, slide 14 (s-m3a) note.
- Evidence: s-rem3 note: "after a 9 the next ten begins (29 then 30): this is the move from the end of one row of the 100 square to the start of the next". s-m1 note: "1 more than 39? Answer: 40 (still just 1 more, even though it is on the next row start)". s-m3 note: "Question 3 is the row-end case: 29 then 30". s-m3a note: "say that 29 is the end of its row and 30 is the first number of the next row". On the 100 square the rows are 21 to 30 and 31 to 40, so 30 and 40 are the last numbers of their rows; 31 and 41 start the next. 29 to 30 is a tens-word change inside a row. The child-facing slide s-m1 ("At the end of a row, the next number starts the next row: 39, 40, 41") is ambiguous for the same reason.
- Fix: write "40 is the last number of its row; the next number, 41, starts the next row", and change "Your turn" Q3 to "1 more than 30" (or 40) if the row-end case is wanted; re-word the s-rem3 note to "29 then 30 is a new ten, in the same row". Update the s-m1 slide caption to "40 is the end of a row. 41 starts the next row."

### Medium

**2. The recording statement is incomplete and conflicts with the privacy notice. [ST]**
- Location: guide section 3 Opening SAY (0:36); Before the lesson ("exclude children video"); slide 1 note. Compare `website/privacy.html`, "How lessons are recorded".
- Evidence: the script says "We record me and the screen only." The privacy notice says a child's voice may be heard if they speak, that a parent can ask for the child to be muted, and that children's names are not shown. This lesson has children counting aloud (Remember, Brain break, Explore) and the tutor greets "each child by first name" (2:29), so voices and first names are on the recording. The notice itself still carries "[Confirm the platform setting before the first lesson]" about excluding children's video; Lessons are on Google Meet per the notice and the guide tells the tutor to "check the recording settings exclude children video" without saying where that setting is. I could not confirm that setting exists; test it before the trial.
- Fix: say "We record me and the screen. If you speak, your voice may be on the recording; a grown-up can ask me to mute you." Greet by first name only before the recording starts, or say "hello" without names. Put the confirmed platform setting (or the fact that it does not exist) into the guide.

**3. Papers to the camera: the privacy claim is false and the routine is not workable. [ST]**
- Location: guide Sneak-peek (12:55, HOLD UP 25 s), Exit (79:11, HOLD UP 25 s), slide 26 note ("so only the tutor sees it").
- Evidence: in gallery view every child and parent sees every other child's paper, so "only you see it" is not true. 8 papers in 25 s is about 3 s each; the tutor cannot read a 6-year-old's handwriting in a tile and then record S, D or H for 8 children (the exit NOTE) while also reading the answers from 80:01. No line tells children how to hold a paper (flat, close, steady, count of three). A child who has turned the camera off has no route, and the opening says "You may switch your camera off whenever you like".
- Fix: drop the "only you see it" wording; time the hold-up at 60 to 90 s; tell children how to hold the paper; decide the evidence the tutor actually needs (e.g. fingers for "how many right" after the check slide, read by the tutor in one sweep) and say what a camera-off child does; use a separate quiet signal rather than a per-child S, D or H mark, or let the parent report.

**4. "I am still stuck" cannot reach the tutor. [ST]**
- Location: Look-back (guide section 3 and 4, slide 28 note, workbook page 5); safeguarding block.
- Evidence: the page tells the child "I am still stuck (my tutor will come and help)" but the look-back is "never collected in front of the group or read out" and is not shown to the camera, so the tutor never sees it. The check-in is promised "in the next lesson", a week later.
- Fix: give the child a route that works in the lesson (a hand signal after the exit check, or the parent tells the tutor by message to the business WhatsApp) or remove the printed promise; say what happens the same day.

**5. Slide-versus-script-versus-model mismatch on workbook minutes.**
- Location: slides 19 and 21 ("Timer: 8 minutes", "Timer: 10 minutes"); script 42:56 [8:20] and 53:12 [8:40]; guide section 9 ("middle child needs about 6.5 min for page 1 and 11.0 min for page 2", slow class workbook 31:20 against 28:00 allocated, flag "ok").
- Evidence: three different numbers for page 2 (10, 8:40, 11.0). The "Check page" slide is read at fixed clock times (51:16, 61:52) whether or not children have finished. The tutor has no way to see progress: the guide says "Tutor walks the cameras" but a webcam shows the child's face, not the page. There is no timer in the deck (finding 20).
- Fix: use one number per page (page 1 about 7 min, page 2 about 11 min), re-balance with the 5 min choice slot, flag the part TIGHT, and tell the tutor to read the check slide when most children look up.

**6. Choral counting and answering with 8 unmuted children. [ST]**
- Location: Remember 4:45 ("Count in tens together ... whispering, shouting on 50"), 6:08, 6:56; Brain break; Explore round 1 to 3.
- Evidence: no mute or unmute rule appears in the guide or the slides. On a video call, eight children counting at once arrive as noise with lag; the tutor cannot hear a child who is wrong, and the audio is recorded (finding 2). "Shout on 50" adds volume.
- Fix: say "mute yourself, count with your fingers and counters, I count aloud", or unmute one child at a time (volunteer), and teach the rule on a routine slide.

**7. Hold-up and greeting times in the script are too short, so the "TIGHT" parts are hidden by the 5-minute buffer.**
- Location: guide section 3 timings, section 9 flag column.
- Evidence: greeting 8 children by name and checking the desk view in 40 s plus 60 s of "children find their things"; hold-ups 25 s; `timing_p1.md` models 30 s plus 8 s per child at the exit. Eight children at 8 s each is 64 s. The guide still calls every part "ok", including Look-back 2:28 of 3:00 and Brain break 3:35 of 4:00 where the slow-class figure is already above the allocation.
- Fix: model the hold-ups at the audit's own figure and let the flag column report TIGHT where slow-class exceeds allocation (Opening, Remember, Brain break, Workbook, Talk).

**8. Camera and desk logistics are assumed, not taught. [ST]**
- Location: Opening 1:29 ("Tutor checks that every camera shows the 100 square and the counters"), `tutor_prep`, parent page "Things to have ready".
- Evidence: a laptop webcam shows the face, not the desk; the guide asks for the desk view (for the counters) and also for papers held up. Nothing tells families how to set the camera (tilt, a phone on a stack of books, a mirror) and nothing says what to do if they cannot. A child who joins cold at this lesson (owner decision 1) gets no routine slide: the guide says "Later lessons of the term skip the routine and rules slides".
- Fix: a one-slide, 30-second "how to show your paper" and "mute and hand signals" routine that every lesson can show for 20 seconds, and a one-line set-up note on the parent page.

**9. 48 minutes seated with no movement after the break.**
- Location: timing plan (break 38 to 42; workbook 42 to 70; talk; exit; look-back to 90).
- Evidence: same pattern as `audit_p1.md` P-2. The class is 6 to 8 years old.
- Fix: a 60-second stand-and-stretch at the choice slide (about 63:00) or before Talk, using the counting moves again.

**10. Explore round 4 cannot be followed from the slide.**

- Evidence: the slide shows rows 11 to 50 only; the moves go 34, 35, 45, 55 (outside the picture). The children who rely on the slide (no printer) cannot do "Down one square again".

**11. Mi shows a calculator.**

The mascot image (never to be edited) holds a calculator in his hand on every appearance. Mi's lines themselves are fine ("Count along with me!", "Great thinking today!") and Mi appears on 4 slides, within the limit.

**12. The next-lesson note gives the wrong materials.**
- Location: slide 29 (s-end) and `L04_Parent_Summary.pdf` "Next lesson".
- Evidence: both say Lesson 5 needs "a pencil and paper" only. Lesson 5's own materials list about 20 small things of two kinds, paper ruled as a ten-frame, and the workbook kit page cut out by a grown-up. Families will arrive without them.

**13. "I will stamp your inquiry journal" has no referent.**
- Location: guide Exit (80:13), slide 26 note ("Stamp the inquiry journal").
- Evidence: no lesson, material list, workbook page or parent page defines an "inquiry journal" or stamps; an online tutor cannot stamp a child's page.

**14. REC cues are said live to children with no framing. [ST]**
- Location: every REC line (for example 4:25 "Recording: join in!", 10:25 "Recording: pause now and try both puzzles").
- Evidence: the cues are said to the camera while the live class is in progress (about 8 to 20 s each; `timing_p1.md`). A live 6-year-old told "pause now" and "press play" will be confused. The first cue is spoken before the tutor says hello, and the tutor never says "these lines are for friends watching the recording".
- Fix: one framing sentence at the start ("When I say 'Recording', that is for friends watching later; you keep going") and shorten the cues to the child's action.

**15. Contradictions inside the safeguarding block (L04 text).**
- Location: guide section "Safeguarding and recording", `lessons/l04.json` `safeguarding`.

"Never show a child page in the recording; record the tutor and the screen only" depends on the unverified platform setting (finding 2). The possessives are missing: "the child own paper", "a child name", "children video", "a child page" (the same lines in Lesson 5 are correct, so this is a Lesson 4 text slip).
- Fix: delete the pairs sentences, restore the apostrophes.
