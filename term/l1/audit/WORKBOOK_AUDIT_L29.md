## A. Maths (independent solutions; all agree with the key)

S1 24 + 17 = 41, Yes (41 - 17 = 24). S2 37 + 23 = 60, No; 50 - 23 = 27. S3 36 + 4 = 40, 40 + 20 = 60 (not 50), so step 2; 64 (64 - 28 = 36). S4 the clock is 2:30; one hour later 3:30; the child's 3:00 is wrong. S5 9 + 4 = 13, No. S6 25 + 30 = 55 true; 56 - 20 = 36 so "46" false; 24 + 9 = 33 true; 80 - 5 = 75 so "85" false: True, False, True, False. T1 step 2 and 45 (see finding 1). T2 75 - 27 = 48, 85 - 27 = 58; 85 (58 + 27 = 85). T3 Mei has fewer, so Aisha has 28 + 6 = 34. T4 7:30 plus half an hour is 8:00: clock B (A is 7:00, C is 8:30; hands checked at 200 dpi). G1 52 + 39 = 91, about 90, 191 cannot be right. G2 47 + 38 = 85; the tens digit 7 should be 8. G3 open (key example 36 + 27 = 53: right 63, the ten forgotten; add back 53 - 27 = 26). Investigation 34 - 19 = 15 not 25, so 25 + 19 = 44; 36 + 27 = 63 vs 53, 48 + 35 = 83 vs 73: each wrong answer is 10 too small; a new sum needs 10 or more in the ones (29 + 14 = 43). Exit E1 45 + 28 = 73, No (63 - 28 = 35); E2 (2) 15 - 2 = 13 not 12 (28 + 15 = 43); E3 20 - 12 = 8, No. All match the key. Sneak-peek 40 + 20 = 60 and 27 + 16 = 43 are right.

Ambiguity: **T1** prints "74 - 30 = 44" and "44 - 1 = 43" and asks which step is wrong. Both lines are correct arithmetic; the mistake is one of method (29 is 1 less than 30, so 1 must be added back, not taken away). A child who reasons "step 1 takes away 30 but the question says 29, so step 1 is wrong" has a defensible answer; the key lists that as an error ("Circling step 1 because 30 is not 29"). The key's "right" answer is the better reading but the stem never says that step 1 is a legitimate rounding step (finding 1).

Class numbers: the rule (preflight section 8, item 3) is that no Start here or Think harder number is used in Explore, the Mini-lesson or the sneak-peek. **S5 (a bag $9 and a cup $4, a child says $12) uses the same two prices as the Mini-lesson example (a pen $4 and a book $9, a child says $15)**; the same child-says-a-wrong-total structure, so the item is a repeat, not new (finding 2).
