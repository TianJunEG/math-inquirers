Findings: High 0, Medium 7, Low 8.

## Medium

1. **Explore round 3 is the sneak-peek puzzle, so the "ideas we have not learnt yet" puzzle is solved in class 50 minutes before it is revealed.** Sneak-peek puzzle 2 is "two stars make 14, the heart and a star make 10"; Explore round 3 (s-explore3, 19:38) is "circle + circle = 14, circle + triangle = 10". Same numbers, same structure, shapes renamed. The tutor says "the circle is 7... the triangle is 3" at 23:23, while the slide note for the sneak-peek says "Do not reveal puzzle 2 until Talk time" and Talk time (74:15) re-reveals it as a surprise ("Did anyone change their mind?"). A child who kept their paper can only change their mind because the answer was read out at 23:23. Fix: change the numbers of one (Explore round 3 "circle + circle = 16, circle + triangle = 11": 8 and 3).
2. **Think harder is barely reachable in the time the script gives.** The script allots 7:00 for questions 1 to 4 and 7:00 for questions 5 to 7 (the DO line says "questions 4 to 6": a typo) and 4:50 for choice time. The guide's own per-question middle-child times are S1 to S4 at 2 minutes each (page 1, 8.0) and S5 3, S6 3, S7 3.5 (page 2, 9.5): 17.5 minutes against 14 allotted. A middle child reaches the choice slide 3.5 minutes late and has about 1 minute for T1 (timed 4). The NOTE says "the workbook part needs about 30 minutes against 28". Fix: allot 9:00 + 9:00 (take 4 minutes from Explore, which scripts 11:30 of 14:00) and say that pages 3 to 5 are for home for the middle child.
3. **Slides and read-aloud lines do not give the numbers a child needs, so the page is the only source.** S3 ("find the number for the heart"), S4, S6 ("Ben says the circle is 7. Is Ben correct?") and E3 ("the star is 5; find the number for each circle") are read at 42:34, 51:14 and 76:41 without the total of the line (14, 15, 12, 19); the totals are only in the figure. On the slides s-ws1, s-ws2 and s-exit the figures are thumbnails about 70 px wide at 1440 (the numbers about 9 px high): unreadable at a distance, and a child who follows by ear, or watches the recording with the page temporarily away, cannot answer. E2's four options (5, 6, 7, 8) are neither on s-exit nor in the READ line, although the guide's rule is that the tutor reads every question aloud and the slide note says "the questions are on the slide" (same defect as L17 E2 and L18 E1). Fix: read the lines ("circle plus circle equals twelve") and the options, and show the figure at the size of s-explore1.
4. **The recording and greeting order contradict each other.** The REC cue runs from 0:15 and the recording notice is said at 0:48, but the DO line at 1:16 says "greet each child by first name before you start the recording. While it runs, avoid names", and the prep says "Start the recording after the greeting". If the recording starts after the greeting, the notice and the cue are not on it and a child watching later never hears the notice; if it starts at 0:00 names may be on it. `privacy.html` says children's names are not shown and that the group is told at the start of each lesson. Same defect as L18 finding 5. Fix: greet in an unrecorded pre-roll, then start the recording, then the cue and the notice.
5. **Two slides print the method before the discovery.** s-explore3 (Explore round 3, "Your hands are the teachers") carries "Line 1 tells you the circle. The circle is the same number in line 2.", which is the lesson's idea ("That is our idea for today", 23:23) stated on screen before the children have used their counters; s-m3 prints "Line 1 has one more star than line 2. The totals show how much that extra star is." together with the picture, so the rule is read before the child can try. The deck is static and the notes do not say to hide the text. Fix: put the question on the slide and the strategy on a following slide, or leave the strategy to the tutor's voice.
6. **Numbering clash.** The deck says "LESSON 9 . TERM 2 . LEVEL 1" and the guide "Lesson 9 of 10", while the guide says "This is Lesson 15 taught again", the s-end slide and parent page say "the Lesson 20 workbook" and "Lesson 20: Find One First". A tutor who tells families "Lesson 20" while the workbook says "Lesson 10" causes the wrong pages to be printed (same as L18 finding 6).

7. **"Fairly safe to miss: no later lesson needs picture equations" is untrue: the end-of-term check in Lesson 20 asks one.** C7 of the Term 2 check (lesson 19 row, page 3 of the student sheet) is "Each shape is a number. The heart + the heart = 18. Ben says the heart is 8. Is Ben correct? ... The heart is ___." The `joining_cold.note` and the guide's Joining cold row say a child who misses the lesson "watches the recording and does workbook pages 1 and 2" and that nothing later needs it; the check then asks a child who may not have met the idea to check a friend's number in a shape equation (the sheet has no pictures, so the question is in words only). The check is Start here by design, so the item is fair for a child who did pages 1 and 2, but the family is told the lesson is optional. Fix: change the note to "the Lesson 20 check has one picture-equation question; a child who misses this lesson should do workbook pages 1 and 2 first", and say so in the parent page.
## Low

8. **Camera wording invites page-showing.** "Cameras on if you can, so I can see your page and hands" (0:27) and "Check that the page and hands can be seen" (1:16) conflict with owner default 9 (gallery view, no paper to the camera). Say "so I can see you".
9. **Materials list incomplete and a star.** "Mark your own in another colour" (34:57, 50:30, 59:05, 80:30) needs a second pencil colour that is not listed; "Put a star by each one you got right" (80:38) conflicts with "tick your own work" everywhere else (and a star is also a shape in this lesson). The four shape cards listed (circle, square, triangle, heart) leave out the star that appears in the sneak-peek, s-m3, s-m5 and five workbook items; Explore never needs a star card, so nothing stops, but "shape cards" in the REC cue should say which. A grown-up cuts out 20 counters, finds 30 more, two cups and draws four cards.
10. **"Used at Think harder only" mislabels.** The contest-style row lists "a sum and a product together" (G3, Go further) and G2's add-the-two-lines move, which are at Go further. Owner decision 6 asks for contest-style items at Think harder; the lesson keeps G2 and G3 at Go further.
11. **Brain break slide and script disagree.** s-break says "I say a shape number. Clap for it. Whisper the numbers in between." but the script counts 1 to 20 clapping on 5, 10, 15 and 20, then "count back from 20 in fives"; the slide note says "count back from 20 in tens and twos". There are no shape numbers in the break and the REC cue says "Clap the shape numbers". Harmless, but a tutor following the slide does a different activity from the one timed.
12. **Spare-time "Is it always true?" is a definition, not a test.** "Circle + square = 10, so square + circle = 10 too" cannot fail, so a child cannot find a counterexample; it models testing a claim that cannot be false (as L18). The Talk-time claim (Aisha, circle + circle = 12 so three circles are 18) is also true; Ben's and Kumar's wrong claims are in the workbook (S6, T2), which is better. Better spare claim: "Ali says the heart is 5 in one line and 6 in another, and the lines are fine. Is that allowed?"
13. **Parent hand-off for Lesson 20 matches, but the load is high.** s-end: "the Lesson 20 workbook with its tokens page cut out, 3 to 6 small things, the check sheet in an envelope, a pencil and paper" matches the L20 materials list and parent page. The L19 box itself needs the check sheet "a week before" (prep) and the parent page says "3 pages, printed at 100% and closed in an envelope": the instruction to the tutor to send "the check sheet and the grown-up note with this lesson materials" is the only place the envelope is explained, and the child slide says "in an envelope" without saying to leave it closed.
14. **Small figures elsewhere and empty slide halves.** s-m1, s-m3b, s-m5a, s-sneak, s-break and the answer slides leave the lower half empty; text size is fine (about 22 to 28 px at 1440). The slide tier labels ("PUZZLE 1: anyone can try", "PUZZLE 2: think") are neutral.
15. **Mi's calculator** on the title, warm-up, look-back and end slides (owner decision 6; Mi says nothing about it).

## Checks that passed

Plausible except Start here (finding 2). Explore is three rounds in 11:30 of 14 and each has its own slide and fallback.
- **Tutor load for eight.** One idea at a time; answers by whisper, fingers, thumb (rule 2, rule 3) and a count of three; main room only; no breakouts. The tutor cannot see counters on small tiles (the guide admits it) and asks "how do you know?".
- **Recording cues.** 11 REC cues, short, each with a child action; the chapter table matches the script times (0:00, 2:00, 8:00, 12:00, 26:00, 38:00, 42:00, 70:00, 76:00, 82:00, 85:00). The tutor is told to read each question and answer aloud for a child watching alone (see finding 3 for where this is not done).
- **Safeguarding.** No paper to the camera, no private chat, no personal phones, no names with answers, look-back private and never read out, "I am still stuck" route through the grown-up's photo to the business WhatsApp only; the number is not on s-look or s-end (the parent page carries it); photos kept 90 days after the program ends matches `privacy.html` (the notice's "children's names are not shown" is the reason for finding 4); the recording statement (tutor, shared screen, unmuted voice; children may mute and keep cameras off) matches the notice; counters and cups stay on the table, nothing in the mouth; movement break has a seated fallback; a grown-up does the cutting.
- **Beyond-P1 honesty.** The strand cell, prep list, slide notes and parent page say it plainly: one line with a hidden number is P1; the same shape in two lines, taking one line from another and the times line go beyond P1 on purpose; "do not call it MOE-aligned"; "never use letters for unknowns"; "the counters are the method, not a rule to recite".
- **Reading order.** "Heart x circle means heart groups of the circle" (s-m4), "4 x 5 means 4 groups of 5" (s-rem3) and the key "4 x circle = 12 means 4 groups of 3" are one convention (owner default 1); the guide says either order is accepted.
- **x glyph, division honesty.** The multiplication sign renders in the deck and PDFs; no division sign anywhere; "share" and "how many groups of three make twelve" carry the idea; odd and even are not touched.
- **Picture-text match.** s-rem1 six counters in a ten-frame; s-rem2 two plates of six and "[] + [] = 12"; s-rem3 four bags of five; s-explore1 to s-explore3, s-m2 to s-m5a, s-talk, s-sneakans and s-opt all show the lines the script reads; the answer slides (s-ws1ans, s-ws2ans, s-exitans) agree with the key.
- **Think harder reachable (route) and Start here approachable.** The "after question 4 you may choose" line is in the script, the workbook banner and the choice slide; Start here is a cup, equal shapes and one known shape. (Time: finding 2.)
- **Joining cold.** The warm-up re-teaches a box, the same number twice and groups of with pictures; nothing from an earlier lesson is assumed; "fairly safe to miss: no later lesson needs picture equations" is not fully honest: see finding 7 (the Lesson 20 check has a picture-equation question).
- **Misconceptions anticipated.** Different numbers for one shape (ask "is it the same shape?"), adding every number in a line (ask "what is the total of the line?"), no shape cards (a cup or marked paper): tutor responses as IF STUCK lines and per-question hints.
- **Slides.** 29 slides (27 timed + 2 optional), one task per slide, text about 22 to 28 px at 1440, contrast fine, no overflow; the answer slides come after the working time; Mi on 4 slides with short friendly lines only.
