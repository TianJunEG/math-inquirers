## High

1. **"The short hand waits at the hour" is false, and it is the lesson's headline idea.** Explore round 2 slide title "Explore: the short hand waits"; the answer slide and script (20:00 area): "Nine fifty. The short hand waits for the long hand to come all the way round. The hour is still nine."; the Mini-lesson slide "The short hand waits" and "STILL 8: the short hand is almost on the 9, but it has not reached it"; the timing note ("find that the short hand is still on the hour at 9:50"); the parent quote ("The short hand waits at the hour"); objective 3 ("the short hand stays on the same hour until the long hand has been all the way round"). In fact the short hand never waits: it creeps round all the time, and at 9:50 it is 5 degrees short of the 10 and at 8:55 only 2.5 degrees short of the 9. What stays true is that the hour NUMBER is the one the short hand has passed until the long hand completes the circle. Lesson 21 taught the opposite picture ("at half past the short hand is half way between two numbers"; its Investigation page), so a child who believed Lesson 21 is told in Lesson 22 that the hand "waits". The error is repeated in the deck titles, the script, the key words and the parent page, so it will be rehearsed at home. Fix: say "the short hand creeps; at 8:55 it is nearly at the 9, but the hour is still 8 until the long hand is back on the 12" everywhere; retitle the slides "Nearly the next hour" (or similar); change the parent quote to "The long hand counts the minutes in 5s. The hour stays the same until the long hand has been all the way round."

## Medium

2. **The Explore discovery cannot happen with the kit, and the script contradicts the slide.** The script says "Children move the long hand to the 10 and watch the short hand creep" while the slide says "the short hand waits". The kit hands are loose paper (no fastener), so the short hand does not move at all when the long hand is moved. A child who sets 9 o'clock and moves only the long hand has the short hand on the 9 and answers "still nine" because nothing moved, not because they observed anything; the idea that matters ("the short hand is nearly at the 10") is never seen. As in L21 finding 2, the tutor cannot check it on camera. Fix: the tutor demonstrates on a shared-screen clock and children place the short hand "nearly at the 10" themselves; then ask "is the hour still nine?".
3. **Slide titles give the answer before the question.** s-explore2 is titled "Explore: the short hand waits" while the slide asks "Where is the short hand? Is it 9 or 10?"; s-m3 is titled "The short hand waits" while asking "The long hand is on the 11. What is the time?". The guide's own rule (tutor_prep: slides keep the picture or the answer off the screen while you ask) is broken by the title. Fix: neutral titles ("Explore: where is the short hand?", "Read the clock").
4. **A paper-hold-up instruction survived in the warm-up.** Remember WORK line: "Children find the short hand and the long hand on their own clock and hold each up in turn for you to see with a thumb." Holding paper hands up to the camera is the thing default 9 forbids ("no paper hold-ups to the camera at all"), and the Mini-lesson note says the opposite ("do not ask children to hold their clock up"). It is printed in the guide's script at 4:13. Fix: "point to the short hand, then the long hand, on your own clock, and give a thumb".
5. **Event-card sort: ambiguous cards, untestable, and the mat does not hold them.** Explore round 3 asks children to sort 8 cards "into the am pocket and the pm pocket" and "which card was the hardest to place?". The cards have no times; "play ball" and "have a bath" can happen in the morning, and the guide's answers ("come home, play ball, bath and bed are pm") are the author's convention; a child who says "I play ball before school" is right and the script has no response. The mat is two flat 70 x 55 mm boxes, not pockets, and four 34 x 33 mm cards do not fit in one (workbook audit finding 6); the tutor cannot see the sort (thumb only); the "hardest to place" question invites a child to name a card and be "wrong". Fix: put a time on each card (wake up 6:30, breakfast 7:15, go to school 7:40, morning break 10:20 and four pm times) and ask "morning or afternoon and evening for 4:30?".
6. **Recording notice after the recording has started.** The REC cue is at 0:08; the DO line (greet first by name, then start the recording, "avoid names while it runs") is the corrected Lesson 20 wording, and tutor_prep matches it, so the file is consistent; but the NOTE then says "Say the recording notice in the SAY line above in your own words after the recording has started" and the SAY at 0:40 runs after the REC cue. So children are told they are recorded after the recording began, and the REC cue sits before the greeting line in the script. `privacy.html` says only "at the start of each lesson", but the brief for these audits asks for the notice before recording starts. Same as L20 finding 3 (the Term 2 wording) and L21 finding 1. Fix: notice, then press record, then the REC cue.
7. **Start here minutes and the workbook slack.** The script allots 8:40 each to pages 1 and 2; the guide's own table says a middle child needs 9.5 and 8.5 minutes (18 in all, "about 17" in the NOTE) and the child minutes in the lesson file sum to 18. The slow class uses 29:48 of 30:00 in the workbook (12 seconds). Page 1 is 50 seconds short for a middle child and the choice slide (4:30) is the only buffer. In a slow class there is no room for the "how do you know" walk-through that the script promises. Fix: allot 9:30 and 8:30; remove the "17 minutes" figure.

## Low

8. **Deck footers collide with cards.** On s-ws2 (page 2) the S6 card reaches the footer text; s-ws1 and s-exit use small clocks (the E1 and E3 faces are about 65 px on the 1000 px frame, 125 px at 1080p). Read each option aloud (the guide says so).
9. **A whole-class duration example in the Mini-lesson.** s-m5 "A film from 4:00 to 5:20 lasts 1 h 20 min" teaches finding a duration, which the lesson's own `moe.note` calls Primary 3. It is a worked example, not a task, so acceptable; but it pre-empts the Go further Chunk the Gap and shows the answer 1 h 20 min before T2.
10. **Optional slide mislabelled.** s-opt is "Go further: the next hour" (5:50 until 6:00 = 10 minutes), a Start here level item reading two numbers; Go further's real task is G1 to G3. Retitle.
11. **Stale timing assumption.** Section 9: "papers to the camera 30 s" contradicts default 9; the script never asks for it (apart from finding 4).
12. **The Mini-lesson says "four ten", never "ten past four".** That follows D5 and the school papers, and the guide says so plainly; but the guide and parent page tell tutors and parents "do not say 'ten past' or 'quarter to' yet" while the previous lesson's Mini-lesson taught quarter past and quarter to to the whole class (L21 guide finding 3). Tutors will contradict themselves. Decide one rule for the term.
13. **Mi's calculator** on the title, look-back and end slides. `assets/mi_dolphin_nocalc.png` was added after the build; rebuild.
14. **The "Five at a time" brain break** ("make a big circle with one arm for each 5") is good and allows a seated option; the slide has no Mi or picture, which is fine.
