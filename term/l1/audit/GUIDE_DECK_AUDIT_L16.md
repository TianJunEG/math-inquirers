## Medium

1. **Answers on the slide while the question is asked (five slides).** s-m1 "The 5s table" prints the whole 5s table to 8 x 5 = 40 including "7 jumps | 7 fives is 35 | 7 x 5 = 35", while the script asks "What is seven times five? How do you know?" (26:49). s-m2 "The 2s and the 10s" prints 1 x 2 to 6 x 2 and 1 x 10 to 4 x 10 while it asks "What is four times two? What is three times ten?" (28:13). s-rem1 highlights every even number in yellow while the script asks "What comes after eighteen when we count in twos?" (the 20 is yellow). s-rem2 shows the three arcs ending on 15 while it asks "Where do three jumps of five land?" (4:30). s-opt prints "Yes: 2 fives / 4 fives / 6 fives" while it asks "Is Ben right? Try 10, 20, 30 and 40". The s-m1 speaker note says "Reveal the table one row at a time and let children say the total first", but the deck is a static page with no build steps. Fix: question slides, then answer slides; or build steps; or blank cells.
2. **The counter starts "on zero" on a square that has no zero.** The script (12:36), s-explore1 ("Put a counter on 0, just before the 1") and the workbook say so; the printed 100 square (kit page 6) and the slide chart both start at 1 in the corner cell with no 0 position. A child starts on the 1 and lands on 6, 11, 16 (or colours 5, 10, 15 and cannot say where the counter began). The guide's own IF STUCK (23:30: "a child who starts on 1 instead of 0") shows that the authors expect it. This is the first 14 minutes of the lesson. Fix: a start cell marked 0 on the kit page and on the slide chart.
3. **Timing under the project's 30 s rule, and the Start here pages.** The script waits "about 12 s" for the nine warm-up questions and 15 s for Explore; `timing_p1.md` uses 30 s. Re-timing the waits adds about 2:40 to Warm-up (quick about 7:00, slow about 8:20 against 6:00) and 1:10 to Explore (slow about 15:00 against 14:00). Total slow class about 87 of 90, so the buffer is almost spent before the workbook. The guide also models 8.0 and 8.5 middle-child minutes for the two Start here pages (16.5) but gives 7:00 and 7:00 (14): a child at the slow rate (1.7x) needs about 28 minutes for Start here alone, so Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are "for home" for most children. Fix: five warm-up questions; one fewer Start here item or 8:30 for each page.
4. **Think harder is partly the class and partly untaught.** Workbook T3(a) and (b) (35 and 28) are asked in Explore (23:06), in the Mini-lesson (29:42) and in the Talk time, and the rule is printed on s-m3; T1's "double the 2s to find the 4s" appears in the objectives and on the s-title speaker note ("use the 2s to find the 4s") but no slide or script line teaches it (the six Mini-lesson slides are the 5s table, the 2s and 10s, last digits, add or jump, Your turn, Check). The "Your turn" slide asks 3 x 5, 6 x 2, 4 x 10 and 8 x 5, all four of which were printed on s-m1 and s-m2 minutes earlier. Fix: add one slide for "4 x 6 is two lots of 2 x 6" or move T1; new numbers in T3; mix new facts into Your turn.
5. **Recording and greeting order contradict.** The REC cue at 0:15 and the notice at 0:47 come before the DO line at 1:16 ("greet each child by first name before you start the recording. While it runs, avoid names") and the "Before the lesson" line ("Start the recording after the greeting"). A tutor following the script greets by name in minute 1 on the recording. Same pattern as L14 and L15. The notice itself matches `privacy.html` (tutor, shared screen, unmuted voice; children may mute and keep the camera off). Fix: greet, say the notice, then start the recording.
6. **Lesson numbering is inconsistent across guide, deck, workbook and parent page.** Deck and workbook: "Lesson 6"; guide title "LESSON 6 OF 10" but body "Lessons 15 to 18 build on one another... try not to miss Lesson 16" and "Lesson 15" links; parent page: "LESSON 6" with "Next lesson: Lesson 17". Fix: one scheme.
7. **Model answers disagree with the script, and the answer-order rule is hidden.** Talk time tells children to test Wei's rule with "15, 40, 28 and 33" (70:47) but the model answer says "It works for 15, 40, 35 and 50" (35 and 50 are not tested; 28 and 33 are mentioned only as non-members). And the guide says either order is accepted (35:24 NOTE) while its own E3 key lists "Writing 5 x 6 = 11" as a common mistake. Fix: one set of numbers in the script and the model answer; reword the watch-for.

## Low

8. **Camera wording invites page-showing.** "Cameras on if you can, so I can see your page and hands" (0:26) and "Check that the page and hands can be seen" (1:16) while the same guide says "Nobody holds paper to the camera". Say "so I can see you".
9. **A "different colour" pencil is needed beyond the list.** "Use a different colour" (17:22) and "Mark your own in another colour" (35:24, 50:24, 59:00, 80:31) need a third pencil; the materials list says "2 or 3 coloured pencils", and the Explore needs one for the 5s, one for the 2s and a ring for the 10s plus a marking colour: four. Say "3 or 4 coloured pencils".
10. **Self-star.** "Put a star by each one you got right" (80:31): use a tick only.
11. **Brain break.** "Count 1 to 30 together, whispering the in-between numbers" with eight unmuted children on a video call cannot be done in time (audio lag), and the whispered numbers are inaudible on the recording; the tutor cannot tell who is whispering. The tap fallback is given. Fix: the tutor counts and children hold up fingers for every fifth.
12. **Workbook slides shrink the figures.** s-ws1 and s-ws2 show the S1 strip and the S5 table at about 5 px text: unreadable at a distance. The tutor reads the question and the printed page carries them, so Low.
13. **Hand-off to Lesson 17.** The goodbye, s-end and the parent page ask for "about 20 counters or buttons, 5 plates or cups, pencil and paper"; the Lesson 17 materials add "the printed workbook with the groups mat (page 6)" (an alternative to plates). Consistent apart from the missing workbook; say "your workbook" too.
14. **Mi's calculator** on the title and end slides (owner decision 6; Mi never mentions it).
15. **Parent report line.** "This week: the 2s, 5s and 10s, with the 4s and 3s as extras" is good; the term line that follows ("draw a story, use groups and sharing") is the term-level sentence, not this lesson's.
16. **Spare-time and optional slide.** s-opt prints its answers (finding 1). The menu activities are sound (answers correct, 3 to 4 minutes each, tutor line and recording cue given).

## Checks that passed

- **Beyond-P1 statement.** Present and plain in five places: the strand label, "Before the lesson", an opening NOTE, the title-slide speaker note and the parent page ("BEYOND PRIMARY 1 ON PURPOSE": 3s and 4s are Primary 2, numbers in two tables are Primary 4; "do not call the lesson MOE-aligned"; "do not teach tables by heart"). Owner default 3 is honoured. Common multiples are never named to children.
- **Multiplication reading order.** "6 jumps of 5 is 6 x 5 (jumps first, then the size of the jump); either order is accepted in an answer" (strand label and 35:24); consistent with Lesson 15's groups first.
- **x glyph.** A true multiplication sign on every slide and the workbook; no division sign anywhere; sharing is not introduced (Lesson 17).
- **Odd/even and division honesty.** Not applicable here; "every second number", "every fifth number" and "a number is in a table if its last digit is 5 or 0" are all true, and the guide keeps "tests do not prove a rule for every number" (s-m3 note, Talk time).
- **Tutor scripts.** One idea at a time; thumbs up and down for "is 35 in the 5s?" and fingers for jumps are checkable on a gallery tile; every question and answer is read aloud; SAY lines under 60 words; natural spoken English.
- **Logistics.** Colour lightly, pencil crayons rather than felt pens, counters on the table, nothing sharp or near mouths; no paper hold-ups; ticks against the answer slide.
- **Tutor load for eight.** Acceptable for the maths. The tutor cannot see the 100 squares being coloured (the guide admits it); a count of "how many jumps to 30?" at 16:13 and 16:33 is the only check. Flag for a tutor trial: how many children coloured the right cells by 16:13, and how long Explore round 1 (3:10 for colouring 8 fives) really takes.
- **Safeguarding.** No private chat; no names with answers; look-back private; "I am still stuck" via the grown-up's photo to the business WhatsApp (the number is only on the parent summary: slide s-look and the script checked); photos kept 90 days; consistent with `privacy.html`. Pass.
- **Inclusion and joining cold.** Names mixed; a printed square replaces any equipment; the warm-up counts in jumps on a number square and line and rebuilds "5 groups of 6" with a picture, so a child who missed Lesson 15 can follow; the guide says so honestly.
- **Misconceptions anticipated:** 6 + 5 for 6 x 5 (the "Add or jump?" slide), counting from 1, "ends in 5 so it is in the table", "close to 30". Tutor responses are given.
- **Deck legibility.** Text 28 px on a 1600 px slide; contrast good; tables clear; one task per slide. The lower half of most slides is empty; the chart on the Explore slides is a clear 40-cell block with large numerals (10 px cells at 40 dpi, readable on a laptop).
- **Mi use rules.** Mi appears on the title, warm-up, look-back and goodbye slides only, as designed.
