## 1. Verdict and scores

**Verdict: Ready after minor fixes.** Every answer, key line and slide computation is right (checked below). The explore sequence (two strips side by side, then end to end, then take cells off for "fewer than") is a good introduction, the mini-lesson says one idea at a time, the Talk question ("Aisha draws two strips of the same length: is her picture a good picture?") is a real reasoning task, the recording statement and mute routine are spoken, no paper is ever held up by a child, the hand-offs in and out are true, and the beyond-P1 statement is in the guide. The weak points are delivery: five slides print the answer while it is asked, one question cannot be answered the way the script asks (13 on ten fingers), the strip kit does not fit the strip handling the Explore needs, a public finger count of exit-ticket scores, and a promised "catch-up question" at the start of the next lesson that no script contains. Count: 0 High, 6 Medium, 7 Low.
