Findings: High 0, Medium 7, Low 6.

## Medium
1. **The slides never show the measuring skill.** s-m1 and s-rem2 are number lines (no ruler body, no 0 offset), s-m2 "Measure: three steps" has no picture at all, s-m4 "Between two marks" shows only tiles "8" and "9" and no object ending between marks. The key skills (object at 0, end between marks, draw from 0) depend on a live camera demonstration (DO 29:42, 31:00). The recording shows tutor and screen only, so a child watching later sees a small tutor tile. Fix: add figures with the existing `ruler` type (pencil on a ruler, end between 8 and 9, a line from 0 to 7).
2. **S3 and the Explore ask for "nearest cm" at Start here.** Real objects almost never end on a mark, so S3 ("measure in whole cm"), Explore round 1 and s-m4 teach and require rounding to the nearest mark in the core path, although the fix pass moved rounding to Think harder (WORDING_GUIDE rule 25). Fix: use drawn lines or objects chosen to end on a mark in Start here; keep "closer mark" for T3.
3. **S3 pencil is 17 to 19 cm but the guide allows a 15 cm ruler.** A child with a 15 cm ruler must slide the ruler, a skill never taught (only a IF STUCK). H2 also uses a 17 mark. Fix: ask for a shorter object (rubber, crayon piece) or require a 30 cm ruler.
4. **Opening asks everyone to hold the ruler to the camera for 0:55 (0:57).** Eight tiles cannot be checked in 55 s; and publicly shows who has no ruler, contradicting the note "do not ask the child in front of the group". Same for "hold thing and ruler to the camera: look at the 0 end" (15:08): webcam resolution cannot show a ruler end. Fix: whole-group check by fingers on the 0 ("show me your 0 with a finger"), no per-child camera inspection.
5. **Check slide s-ws1ans shows tutor-key text** ("OPEN ANSWER, accept any sensible measure (guess and measure within about 3 cm of each other); a new pencil is about 17 to 19 cm. A grown-up checks...") on the children's screen. Fix: child wording ("Any answer close to your guess").
6. **Webcam logistics for a table task.** Drawing and measuring happen below the laptop camera; no instruction on tilting camera or phone stand; tutor needs "a phone or camera that can look down" (tutor only). Same "paper to the camera so only you see it" false claim as L8 (sneak-peek 13:10, exit 79:15).
7. **Slow-class total 89:00 of 90.** Almost no margin; the 4-minute opening and 12 cm line (Explore) are the skip candidates and are listed, but the flex buffer is fully used. Also "Mi holds a calculator" on title, spot and goodbye slides (AUTHORING bans showing a calculator).

## Low
8. Brain break "A big jump is 10 cm" is physically odd (10 cm is a hand's width) and mixes units with tens/ones; use "10 more" language or drop cm.
9. s-ws2 ruler thumbnails (S4, S6) are about 170 px wide: illegible on screen; fine only for children holding the printed page.
10. s-end: the Mi speech bubble overlaps the "Next lesson" card corner (the deck check did not catch it).
11. Optional slide "How many cuts?" says "cut", while materials ban sharp things; wording "cut" should be "mark".
12. Talk time: "show your screen what you would do" is unclear; "show the camera".
13. Goodbye slide precedes optional slides (as L8); "I will stamp your inquiry journal" (80:20) undefined online.

## What works
Parts sum to 90, script 75:05; sneak-peek reveals at Talk time; ruler safety (flat, no metal edges, no compasses); ruler fallback privately arranged; print-at-100% instruction for the real-size ruler pictures; misconceptions (counting the 0, starting at the edge, answering the end mark) anticipated with tutor responses; warm-up with clips and finger counts is a good cold-join; safeguarding block and consent statement present; beyond-P1 statement honest ("do not describe as MOE-aligned").
