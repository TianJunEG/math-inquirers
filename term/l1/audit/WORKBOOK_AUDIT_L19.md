## F. Pedagogy

Progression: share to find one, count groups to find many, a strip, the two-step chain, a claim to test (S5), then the chain with a different count, a dozen, "how many", two prices, two shops. Sound, with each page opening with a one-line rule ("Find 1 first. Then find many. Each means for one."). Start here is feasible only slowly (finding 3). Think harder and Go further differ. The exit ticket matches the objectives (E1 find one, E2 find many, E3 both). Misconceptions are named in the key (adding the number and the price, multiplying 12 x 7, stopping after step 1, comparing totals $12 and $20 without finding one).
