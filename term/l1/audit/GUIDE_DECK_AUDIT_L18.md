Instruments: AUTHORING 16-point rubric (delivery items 3, 7, 8, 9, 15): 3 PARTIAL (materials: cutting), 7 PARTIAL (recording order), 8 PASS, 9 PASS, 15 PASS; instrument A re-score for delivery **17/24**; timing model of `timing_p1.md`: script 68:48, slow class 81:23 of 90, no part over (parts 2+6+4+14+12+4+28+6+6+3+5 = 90). Findings: High 0, Medium 6, Low 9.

## Medium

1. **Think harder is barely reachable in the time the script gives.** The script allots Start here 7:00 for questions 1 to 3 and 7:00 for questions 4 to 6, then 4:10 of choice time. The guide's own per-question middle-child times are S1 3, S2 3, S3 2, S4 3, S5 3, S6 3 = 17 minutes (page 1 8.0, page 2 9.0) against 14 allotted; a slower child needs 1.7 times as long. The middle child reaches the choice slide 3 minutes late and has about 1 minute for Think harder (T1 to T4 are timed at 5, 4, 3 and 5 minutes). The NOTE even says "the workbook part needs about 28 minutes against 28, so Start here may fill most of it". Fix: allot 9:00 + 9:00 (take the 2 minutes from Explore, which scripts 11:08 of 14:00) and say plainly that Think harder is normally for home for the middle child.
2. **The strips kit needs cutting, but children do not use scissors and the Explore needs strips of a stated length.** The kit page (page 6) says "A grown-up cuts out the strips before the lesson. Cut a strip to any length at a dotted line"; the guide says "ten strips, cut at the dotted lines when needed" and "children do not use scissors in the lesson". Explore round 4 (20:50) says "Make a strip of 6 cells for Mei ... Make Ali a strip the same as Mei's, and then another the same": that is three strips of 6 cells cut from ten-cell strips, in the lesson. A grown-up cannot know in advance which lengths (6 here, 8 and 5 elsewhere) and the guide's fallbacks ("fold a strip", "counters in rows") are the only workable route, so the kit does not serve its purpose. Also a family that has not cut the page before the lesson has no strips at all (the L17 s-end and parent box say "the strips page" and "a grown-up cuts out the strips" but not how many or how long). Fix: tell grown-ups to pre-cut three strips of 6 cells and two of 5 (or say "cut the page into ten strips of ten, then fold at a dotted line"), and make counters in rows the first instruction on s-explore4.
3. **The exit-ticket options are never read aloud and are not on the slide.** The READ line (76:41) is "E1. Ali has 4 bags. Each bag has 5 sweets. Which number sentence finds how many sweets Ali has in all? Write its number in the bracket." and the four sentences (4 + 5, 4 x 5, 5 - 4, 4 + 4 + 4) are neither read nor on the slide s-exit. The guide's own rule is that the tutor reads every question aloud; E1 is the exit check of the lesson's key objective. A child who reads slowly, or who is watching the recording, cannot answer. (Same defect as L17 E2.) Fix: add the four options to the READ line and to s-exit.
4. **Pictures on the slide give the structure of the answer before the question.** s-m3 shows Mei 8 and Ali 8 | 8 while the script asks "How many stickers does Ali have? How many do they have together?" (30:44); s-explore4 shows Mei 6 and Ali 6 | 6 while the children are told to build Ali's strips and asked "How many cells has Ali?" (22:04); s-explore1 and s-m1 show the strip 5 5 5 with the total "?" before "How many counters are there in all?" / "How many boxes? What is in each box?". The Explore rounds are meant to be built from hands, "do not show the answer" (the L17 Explore slides did this well). The speaker notes do not say to hide the strip; the deck is static. Fix: show the question first and the strip on a second slide (or a reveal), at least for s-explore4 and s-m3.
5. **The recording and greeting order contradict each other.** REC cues run from 0:15 and the recording notice is said at 0:47, but the DO line at 1:16 says "greet each child by first name before you start the recording. While it runs, avoid names", and the prep says "Start the recording after the greeting". A tutor cannot do both. If the recording starts after the greeting, the notice at 0:47 and the REC cue at 0:15 are not on the recording and a watching child never hears the notice; if it starts at 0:00, first names are on it. `privacy.html` says children's names are not shown and that the group is told at the start of each lesson. Fix: greet in a pre-roll (not recorded), then start the recording, then the cue and the notice.
6. **Numbering clash.** Deck: "LESSON 8 . TERM 2 . LEVEL 1"; guide "Lesson 8 of 10"; the same guide says "Lessons 15 to 18 build on one another" and "Lesson 20 uses strips"; the s-end slide and the parent page name the next lesson "Picture Equations" and "Lesson 19". A tutor who tells families "Lesson 19" while the workbook says "Lesson 9" causes the wrong pages to be printed. Fix: one scheme.

## Low

7. **Camera wording invites page-showing.** "Cameras on if you can, so I can see your page and hands" (0:26) and "Check that the page and hands can be seen" (1:16) conflict with owner default 9 (gallery view, no paper to the camera). Say "so I can see you".
8. **Materials list incomplete and a star.** "Mark your own in another colour" (35:16, 50:35, 59:25, 80:39) needs a second pencil colour that is not listed; "Put a star by each one you got right" (80:39) conflicts with "tick your own work" everywhere else.
9. **"Used at Think harder only" mislabels.** The contest-style row lists G3 (Go further) and the Investigation under "Used at Think harder only".
10. **Hand-off to Lesson 19.** s-end: "your workbook with its counters page, about 30 counters, 2 cups, 4 paper shape cards, pencil and paper". The L19 kit is a ten-frames and counters page (a grown-up cuts out 20 counters) and the "shape cards" are four pieces of paper with a circle, a square, a triangle and a heart that a grown-up must draw: neither the slide nor the SAY says who prepares them (the parent box does). Materials are accurate but the load rises from 20 to 30 counters and two items to prepare.
11. **"Half of the gap" in T4 is the first time "half" is used,** in the key and the online step ("Ali gives half of the gap") without a picture of halving. The T4 hint "share the gap equally" does carry it, but there is no slide. Acceptable at Think harder; make sure the tutor shows the gap in two equal parts.
12. **Spare-time "Is it always true?" is a definition, not a test.** "Is twice as many the same as the number plus itself? Try 5, then 7, then 9 ... Can you find a number where it is not true?" The answer says "No number breaks it: twice as many is two equal strips, which is the number added to itself". The children cannot find a counterexample because the sentence is the meaning of the words; it models testing a claim that cannot fail. Better: "Is twice as many ever the same as 2 more?" (only for 2; try 2, 3 and 8), which is a real test.
13. **Brain break.** "Clap in groups" with a clap total aloud: 3 groups of 2, 4 groups of 3, 2 of 5, 3 of 4 and then "clap 4 for Mei and twice as many for Ali" (8 claps). Noise in a flat and counting claps with a pause is hard to supervise on a gallery; the tap fallback is given and good. The final call is not listed with its answer.
14. **Small figures.** The workbook slides s-ws1 and s-ws2 show the strips as thumbnails about 100 px wide (S1, S3, S4, S6): unreadable at a distance. The printed page carries them (as L17). s-rem1, s-rem3 and several slides leave the lower half empty; text size is fine (about 24 px on 1440).
15. **Mi's calculator** on the title and end slides (owner decision 6).

## Checks that passed

- **Timing realism.** Parts sum to 90; script 68:48 and slow-class 81:23; all flags "ok". Plausible except Start here (finding 1). Explore is four rounds in 11:08 of 14: tight but each round has its own slide and a fallback.
- **Tutor load for eight.** One idea at a time; answers by whisper, fingers (1, 2 or 3 fingers for the strip choice) and a count of three; main room only; no breakouts. The one thing a tutor cannot supervise is whether each child built the strips (the guide admits "you cannot see builds on small tiles").
- **Recording cues.** 11 REC cues, short, each with a child action; chapter table consistent with the script times.
- **Safeguarding.** No paper to the camera, no private chat, no names with answers, look-back private and never read out, "I am still stuck" route through the grown-up's photo to the business WhatsApp only; the number is not on s-look or s-end; photos kept 90 days after the program ends matches `privacy.html`; the recording statement (tutor, shared screen, unmuted voice; children may mute and keep cameras off) matches the notice. Counters not food; nothing in the mouth; a movement break with a seated fallback; a grown-up does the cutting.
- **Beyond-P1 honesty.** The strand cell, prep list, slide notes and parent page say it plainly: equal-group stories are P1; strips (bar models) and "twice as many" / "3 times as many" go beyond P1 on purpose; "Do not call it MOE-aligned"; "say bar model only as the school word for grown-ups"; "twice as many is two equal strips, never 2 more".
- **Reading order.** "4 groups of 5 is 4 x 5" throughout the guide, slides ("Step 1: 4 x 5 = ?") and exit; the guide says either order is accepted but the key does not (see the workbook audit).
- **x glyph.** The multiplication sign renders in the deck and PDFs; no division sign anywhere (G3 never says divide; "half" only in T4 notes).
- **Picture-text match.** s-explore2 is the S3 workbook strips (strip 1 = 4 boxes of 3; strip 3 = 3-3-3-2, last box shorter); s-m2 shows 4 boxes of 5 and a different-colour box of 6; s-opt shows Siti 6 and Dev 6-6-6 with the 12 gap; s-sneakans shows Mei 5 and Ben 5-5.
- **Think harder reachable (route) and Start here approachable.** The "after question 3 you may choose" line is in the script, workbook and choice slide; Start here is cups, strips, one more step. (Time: finding 1.)
- **Joining cold.** The warm-up shows two strips and the gap, same-size groups and a two-step story with pictures; nothing from an earlier lesson is assumed; the safe-to-miss note is honest (Lessons 12 and 15 to 18 build on each other, and L20 uses strips).
- **Misconceptions anticipated.** "Twice as many is 2 more" (put the two strips beside Mei's one), 5 boxes of 3 for 3 bags of 5, forgetting the loose pens: tutor responses given as IF STUCK lines and per-question hints.
- **Hand-off from L17.** The L17 s-end and parent box ("the L18 workbook with its strips page, about 20 counters, 3 paper cups or plates") match this lesson's materials list.
- **Slides.** 28 slides, one task per slide, text about 22 to 28 px at 1440 px, contrast fine, no overflow; the answer slides come after the working time; the sneak-peek answer slide at Talk time.
