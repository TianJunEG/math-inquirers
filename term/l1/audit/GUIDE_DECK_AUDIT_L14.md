## Medium

1. **Answers on the slide while the question is asked.** s-explore3 "Be a detective: test the sums" shows "3 + 5 | 8: even, 3 + 4 | 7: odd, 4 + 6 | 10: even" with the caption "Build each sum with counters first", while the script asks "Three plus five. Is the total odd or even?" (20:10); s-m2 and s-m3b tables likewise print the totals the class has not yet worked out; s-explore1 prints "No: the number is even" and "Yes: the number is odd" before the children pair; s-m1 prints the last-digit rule that the Explore notes say "do not reveal: let children say it". The speaker notes say "hide the table answers until children have built each sum, then reveal one at a time" but the deck is a static PDF with no build steps. Fix: question and answer slides, or row reveal in the deck tool.
2. **The 1 to 20 cards are not asked for.** Explore round 2 needs "your number cards 1 to 20, cut out" (15:39), yet the opening SAY (0:26), the REC cue and the previous lesson's goodbye (L13 s-end) ask only for "counters and your workbook". The kit page is page 6 of the workbook; only the parent summary of L13 mentions cutting the cards. A child arriving with an uncut page loses the sort round (about 5 minutes of the lesson). Fix: opening line "kit page with the number cards cut out" and an L13 s-end line.
3. **Think harder repeats the class sums** (workbook T1 = slide s-m2 table; T3 = slide s-m3 and the Talk time). The slide answers are read aloud before the workbook begins, so those items cost no thought. Fix: new numbers in the workbook (see workbook audit).
4. **T3(c) key conflict is in the guide as well:** "odd + odd is Never odd (always even in our tests)" for a stem that says "is an even number. Circle Always, Sometimes or Never". Tutors will mark inconsistently. Also slide s-m3 asks "Always, sometimes or never?" about "an odd number plus an odd number is odd" (answer: never odd), the reverse polarity of the workbook stem. Fix: one polarity in guide, slides and workbook.
5. **Lesson numbering is inconsistent.** Deck and workbook: "Lesson 4 of 10"; guide text: "Lesson 14 is the safest lesson in Term 2 to skip", "Lessons 15 to 18 build on one another", "(Lesson 13)"; parent summary: "Lesson 15: Equal Groups" next. A tutor following the guide tells a parent "Lesson 15", the workbook says Lesson 5. Fix: one scheme.
6. **The "why it works for any odd number" step is asserted, not shown.** s-m4 shows 3 and 5 and says "So 3 + 5 has none left over: even" and the SAY adds "This works for any two odd numbers" (32:08), then asks "Does the picture work for 7 + 9?" with a 35 s wait and no picture. A 6-year-old can accept the claim on trust, which is the opposite of the lesson. Fix: show 7 + 9 on the next slide or have the children build it with counters (they hold 20), then let them say "any".

## Checks that passed

- **Timing realism.** Script 68:49 (quick), 81:29 (slow) of 90; all parts "ok" in the model. Concern: Explore round 2 has the children lay out and sort 10 cards in 1:30 (15:57 to 17:27), then 11 to 20 "if time" in a single sentence; sorting ten physical cards with a pencil and counters in 90 seconds is optimistic for 6-year-olds, but the slow-class figure already adds 20%. The mini-lesson carries five claims and a picture reason in 9:35 (slow 11:18, inside 12). Workbook 28 min covers Start here (model 14 min).
- **Tutor load for eight.** One idea at a time, answers by flat hand and fingers (checkable on a gallery), builds invisible (the guide admits "you cannot see builds on small tiles"). Acceptable; the clap/stomp break and the card sort are the two moments a tutor cannot supervise. Flag for a tutor trial.
- **Recording cues** short and correct; chapters time-stamped; every question read aloud.
- **Safeguarding.** No paper to camera, no private chat, no names with answers, look-back private, "I am still stuck" via the grown-up's photo to the business WhatsApp (number not on any slide: s-look checked), recording statement consistent with `privacy.html` (tutor, shared screen, unmuted voice; children may mute; photos 90 days).
- **Odd/even honesty.** The lesson says odd and even are Primary 2 content (strand note, prep list, speaker note on slide 1, parent summary). It teaches the words by pairing, not the last digit first. It keeps "tests can show a claim is false, never prove it" in the guide, the slide s-m3 and the Talk time: good epistemics. It states "Do not teach division or halving", so "even = divisible by 2" never appears. It does not say "÷" or "x" anywhere. Pairs of counters are a legitimate model. The only overreach is the last-digit rule extended to two-digit numbers without a reason (workbook finding 4).
- **Think harder reachable, Start here approachable:** the "after question 3 choose" line is in the script, workbook and guide; Start here items are all pairing, a ringed picture, a chart or a bracket.
- **Beyond-P1 statement** present (strand note, prep, slide-1 note, parent page); "Do not call it MOE-aligned".
- **Joining cold:** warm-up rebuilds pairs, counts in twos and the last digit with pictures; nothing assumed; "safe to miss" is stated, and consistent with the plan.
- **Misconceptions anticipated:** "odd plus odd is odd because the numbers are odd" (build 3 and 5), "one test proves it" (try another pair), big number = even, first digit. Tutor responses given.
- **Hand-off to L15:** s-end, the SAY line and the parent page all say "4 plates or bowls, about 20 small things of one kind, pencil, paper": consistent.
- **Deck legibility:** text about 28 px on a 1440 px slide (about 20 pt at 1080p); contrast fine; pairs pictures large on s-explore1 and s-m1 (counters ~12 px at 72 dpi, which is small on the warm-up slide s-rem1, readable on a laptop); workbook slides s-ws1 and s-ws2 shrink the S1 and S5 pictures to about 6 px per counter, unreadable at a distance (the printed page carries them, so Low).
