## What works

One idea at a time on the whole: order tags, count up for a gap, add for altogether, count up for change, which two make an amount. The warm-up re-teaches the three things the lesson needs (compare with tens bars, count up on a line, two strips), so a June holiday graduate who joins cold can follow; the guide says so and says the lesson is not easy to miss (Quest gate 4 asks "which costs more and by how much"). Explore is kit-led and the IF STUCK lines are concrete ("count the steps, not the numbers"). Answers are held off the screen for the Mini-lesson (`s-m1`, `s-m2`, `s-m4` have the question only; `s-m1a`, `s-m2a`, `s-m4a` carry the line and answer). Check slides follow each workbook page; the exit ticket is read aloud with its four options. The safeguarding block is consistent with the script: no paper or tags to the camera, children say the order and show a thumb, small pieces on the table, nobody cuts, no private chat, WhatsApp-only route with no number on any slide, no child named with an answer ("volunteers first; never call a child by name"). The "two answers" Mini item teaches the listing rule (start with the cheapest and ask what partner it needs) that Lesson 26 builds on. The beyond-P1 statement is in the strand note, the opening NOTE and tutor_prep, and is honest (the gap, total and change within $20 are P1; the gap among three, change from $50, exact amounts and the total-and-gap story are beyond on purpose). No MOE-aligned claim. The hand-off lines are true against `l26.json`.
