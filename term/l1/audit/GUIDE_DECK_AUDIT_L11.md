## 1. Verdict and scores

**Verdict: Ready after minor fixes.** Every answer, key line and slide computation is right. The script is plain and one idea at a time, the recording statement and the mute routine are spoken in the Opening, there are no paper hold-ups, the hand-off to Lesson 12 is true, the beyond-P1 statement is in the guide, and the Term 1 failure patterns (no recording sentence, papers to the camera, WhatsApp number on a slide) are fixed. The remaining problems are delivery: five slides print the answer while it is being asked, the Explore depends on the tutor seeing things on children's tables that a gallery view cannot show, a "microphone off" rule that clashes with the choral answers, a lesson that has no real slack at the project's own 30-second response model, and a public finger count of exit-ticket scores. Count: 0 High, 7 Medium, 8 Low.

## 4. Check of the safeguarding and privacy items

No child is named with a wrong answer anywhere; the guide says never to read a name with an answer (Opening, Explore, Talk, Exit). Only first names of the lesson cast appear. No private chat, no private room, the still-stuck route is a tick on the child's own page that a parent photographs and sends to the business WhatsApp (no personal phones), and the tutor replies to the grown-up only. The recording sentence ("the tutor, the shared screen and any voice that is not muted") matches `privacy.html`, which also says children's names are not shown and the tutor repeats answers in their own words; both are in the script. One mismatch sits in the parent page (see `HOME_PARENT_PRACTICE_AUDIT_L11.md`, finding 4: "private to your family" versus "enrolled families of the same group").
