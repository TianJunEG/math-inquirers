Findings: High 0, Medium 5, Low 9.

## Medium

1. **Think harder is barely reachable in the time the script gives.** The script gives Start here 7:00 for questions 1 to 3 and 7:00 for questions 4 to 7 (CHILDREN lines at 43:09 and 51:58), then 4:25 of "choice time" (60:49). The guide's own per-question times for a middle child are 7.5 min for page 1 and 9.5 min for page 2 (S1 3, S2 2.5, S3 2, S4 2.5, S5 2.5, S6 3, S7 1.5) = 17 minutes against 14 scripted, and a slower child needs 1.7 times as long. So a middle child reaches the choice slide about 3 minutes late and has about 1.5 minutes left for Think harder, whose items are timed at 4 minutes each (T1 to T4 = 16 min). The banner "After question 3 you may choose" helps only a quick child. The guide admits "pages 3 to 5 are for home" for slow classes, but even the middle child is in that case. Fix: give Start here 9:00 + 9:00 in the script (shorten the Explore script by 2 minutes, it has 4 spare), keep choice time at 4 minutes, and state that Think harder is normally a home page for the middle child.
2. **Two sets of stories are never read aloud and are not on the slides.** The READ line for page 2 says "Question 7: read each story and circle Share or Group" (51:21) without the two sentences (Lily gives 12 beads equally to 3 friends; Jamie puts 12 beads into boxes of 6); the CHILDREN line for page 2 says "questions 4 to 6". The exit ticket READ (76:41) says "E2. Which story puts 12 sweets into groups of 4? Write its number in the bracket" and never reads the four stories; slide s-exit shows only the stem and the bracket. The guide's own rule is that "the tutor reads every question aloud". S7 is the share-or-group sort, the key assessment of the lesson, and E2 is the exit check of objective 2. A child who reads slowly (age 6) cannot do either without help, and the recording child cannot hear it. Fix: add the sentences to the READ lines and to the slides s-ws2 and s-exit, and correct the CHILDREN line to "questions 4 to 7".
3. **Answers are on the slide while the question is asked.** s-m2 ("Put into groups") prints "12 sweets put into bags of 4 | 3 bags" and shows three filled bags while the script ASKs "How many bags do we need?" (28:02, wait 20 s). s-m1 prints "4 in each bag" under "We find" while the script ASKs "What do we know, and what do we find out?" (26:48). s-m5 prints "Count Ben too." as a slide caption while the script asks "How many children share the sweets?" (31:49), which hands over the whole reading trap. s-m4 prints "3 full groups, and 2 left over" before the ASK about 15 counters (that one is a different number, so it is only a worked example). The speaker notes say nothing about hiding or revealing; the deck is static. The explore slides (s-explore1 with empty plates, s-explore3 with no answers) do this well. Fix: show the picture and the question first; add a reveal build (or a second slide) for the "We find" cell and the caption.
4. **The recording and greeting order contradict each other.** The script runs REC cues from 0:15 and says the recording notice at 0:46 ("This lesson is recorded ..."), then at 1:12 the DO line says "greet each child by first name before you start the recording. While it runs, avoid names", and the tutor prep says "Start the recording after the greeting". A tutor cannot do both: if the recording starts after the greeting the REC cue and the notice at 0:15 to 0:46 are not on it (the recorded child misses the notice), and if it starts at 0:00 the greeting by name is recorded. `privacy.html` says children's names are not shown and "we tell the group at the start of each lesson". Fix: greet first in a 1-minute pre-roll (not recorded), then start the recording, then the REC cue and the notice.
5. **The division sign is labelled as "the sign for sharing" only (division honesty).** s-m3 shows "A grown-up sign for sharing: 12 ÷ 3 = 4. You do not need it today." and the SAY at 28:50 says "Grown-ups have a short sign for sharing." The lesson's own big idea is that sharing and grouping are two kinds of division; the same sign also means "12 put into groups of 4 = 3". A parent who copies the parent page ("grown-up sign: 12 ÷ 3 = 4") will believe the sign means sharing only, and the Groups card on the same slide carries no sign. Fix: "A grown-up sign for both: 12 ÷ 3 = 4 (12 shared among 3) and 12 ÷ 4 = 3 (12 put into groups of 4)", or keep the sign off the slide and in the guide only.

## Low

6. **Camera wording invites page-showing.** "Cameras on if you can, so I can see your page and hands" (0:26) and "Check that the page and hands can be seen" (1:12) conflict with owner default 9 (no paper to the camera; a gallery view is enough). Say "so I can see you".
7. **Numbering clash.** Deck "LESSON 7 . TERM 2 . LEVEL 1", workbook "Term 2 . Lesson 7", guide "Lesson 7 of 10", but the guide's own text says "Lessons 15 to 18 build on one another" and the next-lesson line and parent page say "Lesson 18". Tutors will tell parents "Lesson 18" while the workbook says "Lesson 8".
8. **Materials list incomplete.** The marking routine ("Mark your own in another colour" at 35:50, 50:34, 59:23, 80:29) needs a second colour of pencil, not in the child materials. "Put a star by each one you got right" (80:29) conflicts with "tick your own work" everywhere else and invites comparison; say "tick".
9. **"Used at Think harder only" mislabels.** The guide's contest-style row says "Used at Think harder only" and lists G1 (Go further) and the Investigation. The contest tag items sit at Go further and in the Investigation, not at Think harder. Per owner decision 6 contest-style items belong at Think harder; "all the ways to share 12" is a plan row Go further item and arguably not contest-style, so the simplest fix is to correct the label ("Used at Think harder, Go further and the Investigation").
10. **Explore needs up to 5 plates and the guide says so but the opening asks for "your plates and counters" only;** the Explore script uses 3, 2 and 4 plates and keeps re-dealing, each time 40 to 50 seconds of child action that a tutor cannot see on a gallery of small tiles (the guide itself says "you cannot see builds on small tiles"). Acceptable; flag for a tutor trial.
11. **Small figures.** s-rem2 (Jumps of 3 and 4): two number lines with numerals about 12 px at 1440 px (about 9 pt at 1080p) and the lower half of the slide empty; the arcs stop at 9 and 8, so 12 is never reached. s-ws2: the S4 table and S5 line thumbnails are about 90 px wide and unreadable at a distance. The printed page carries them, so Low.
12. **Warm-up link to later lessons.** s-m6a speaker note: "16 shared among 2 is a half of 16, 8 and 8, which is a double from Lesson 13". A child who missed Lesson 13 has no "double" and the lesson claims to need nothing earlier; "half" is introduced by a note a tutor may say aloud. Remove from the SAY or define it with counters.
13. **Spare-time and Think harder timing in the flex buffer.** The optional slide, the spare menu and the look-back all draw from the final 5 minutes; with the script total of 68:39 there are 21 spare minutes by the model, but the slow-class total (81:14) leaves 8:46, and the workbook part alone is over by 3 minutes in practice (finding 1), so the buffer is partly spent before Talk time. The guide's skip order (cut the check slide, shorten Explore round 3) is sensible.
14. **Mi's calculator** on the title and end slides (owner decision 6; Mi never mentions it).

## Checks that passed

- **Timing realism.** Parts sum to 90; script 68:39 and slow-class 81:14, flags all "ok". The model is plausible except for Start here (finding 1). Brain break is 4:00 of dealing in the air with one arm and a sip of water: fine.
- **Tutor load for eight.** One idea at a time; answers by whisper, fingers (1 finger share, 2 fingers group) and a count of three; the main room only; no breakouts. The one thing a tutor cannot supervise is who has dealt correctly (as above).
- **Recording cues.** 11 REC cues, each short with a child action; chapter table at section 8 consistent with the script times.
- **Safeguarding.** No paper to the camera, no private chat, no names with answers, look-back private and never read out, "I am still stuck" route through the grown-up's photo to the business WhatsApp only, number not on any slide (s-look and s-end checked); photos 90 days after the program ends matches `privacy.html`; the "recording has the tutor, the shared screen and any unmuted voice; children may mute and keep cameras off" wording matches the notice (the notice says children's cameras are not recorded: consistent). Counters not food, nothing in the mouth, a movement break with a seated fallback.
- **Beyond-P1 honesty.** The guide's strand cell, prep list, slide notes and the parent page all say plainly: sharing and grouping within 20 are P1; the division sign (P2), left over (P3 remainder idea) and all the ways to share 12 go beyond on purpose; "Do not call it MOE-aligned"; "say left over, never remainder". No "times as many".
- **Think harder reachable (route) and Start here approachable.** The "after question 3 you may choose" line is in the script, the workbook and the choice slide; Start here items are dealing, ringing, a table, a jump line and a sort. (Time: finding 1.)
- **Reading order.** "3 bags with 4 sweets in each bag is 3 times 4"; the slide shows "3 x 4 = 12" for both cards; the guide says either order is accepted; the key does not (see workbook finding 3).
- **x and glyphs.** The multiplication sign and the division sign render correctly in the deck and the PDFs (no tofu).
- **Picture-text match.** s-m5 shows Ben plus three friends at a round table (4 children) for "Ben shares 8 sweets equally with 3 friends"; s-m1 and s-m2 show 3 bags of 4 sweets for both stories (intended: same picture, different question); s-explore1 empty plates; s-m4 three plates of 4 and a fourth plate of 2. The fourth "left over" plate looks like a fourth group, so a child may say "4 groups": the speaker note does address it.
- **Joining cold.** The warm-up deals counters, counts in jumps and shows same-size groups with pictures; "nothing from an earlier lesson is needed" is true; the safe-to-miss note says Lessons 15 to 18 build on one another (matches the plan).
- **Misconceptions anticipated.** Plates count instead of share, unequal shares, the number of friends instead of each, "3 children share" for Ben: tutor responses are given as IF STUCK lines and per-question hints.
- **Hand-offs.** L16 to L17: 20 counters and 5 plates, matches the L16 parent box and this lesson's materials. L17 to L18: s-end and the parent box say the L18 workbook with its strips page, about 20 counters, 3 paper cups or plates; consistent with each other (L18 has its own kit; see the L18 audit).
- **Slides.** 30 slides, one task per slide, text about 22 to 28 px at 1440 px (about 17 to 21 pt at 1080p), contrast fine, no overflow found; answer slides appear only after the working time; the sneak-peek answer slide is at Talk time.
