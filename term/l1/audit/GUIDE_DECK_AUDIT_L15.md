## Medium

1. **Answers on the slide while the question is asked.** s-m1 "Groups of" prints "4 groups of 3 | 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12" while the script asks "How many groups? How many in each group? How many altogether?" (26:48). s-m2 "The x sign" prints all three rows, including "5 groups of 2 | 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 10 | 5 x 2 = 10", while the script asks "Look at 5 groups of 2. What is the total? How would you write it with the sign?" (28:22). s-opt (the optional slide) prints "3 groups of 5: 15 | 5 groups of 3: 15" while it asks "Who has more sweets?". The s-m2 speaker note says "Reveal the table one row at a time and let children say the total first", but the deck is a static page with no build steps (checked: no step or reveal markup in `L15_Deck.html`). Fix: question slides then answer slides (as the deck does for workbook checks), or row reveals in the deck tool, or empty the answer cells.
2. **Warm-up and Explore under the project's 30 s rule.** The script waits "about 12 s" for the nine warm-up questions and 12 to 15 s for the Explore questions; `timing_p1.md` uses 30 s (10 thinking, 20 answering) because eight children answer. Re-timing only the waits adds about 2:25 to Warm-up (quick 4:22 becomes about 6:50, slow about 8:00 against 6:00 allotted) and about 1:40 to Explore (slow about 14:40 against 14:00). Total slow class about 87 of 90, so the 5-minute buffer is almost all used before the workbook starts. Fix: five warm-up questions instead of nine (drop "ten sticks in two bundles" and "6 + 8"), or lengthen Warm-up to 8 and cut the flex time.
3. **Mini-lesson has no slack and "Your turn" is optimistic.** Slow class 11:58 of 12:00. Within it, children write four items (5 groups of 2, 2 groups of 6, 3 groups of 5, 4 groups of 4), each as a sum, a x sentence and a total, in 2:30 (about 37 s each, 12 writings) at the point where they first write the sign. A slow 6-year-old will finish two. The check slide then reads all four answers. Fix: three items, or sum and total only for the last two.
4. **Recording and greeting order contradict.** The REC cue at 0:15 and the recording notice at 0:46 come before the DO line at 1:16 ("greet each child by first name before you start the recording. While it runs, avoid names") and the "Before the lesson" line ("Start the recording after the greeting"). A tutor following the script times the recording from 0:00 and greets by name in minute 1 on the recording. Same pattern as L14 (finding 7) and the Term 1 recording-statement failures. The wording of the notice itself matches `privacy.html` (tutor, shared screen, unmuted voice; children may mute and keep the camera off), which is good. Fix: greet by name first, say the notice, then start the recording; move the REC cue after it.
5. **Lesson numbering is inconsistent across guide, deck, workbook and parent page.** Deck and workbook: "Lesson 5"; guide title "LESSON 5 OF 10" but body "Lessons 15 to 18 build on one another... try not to miss Lesson 15" and "Lesson 14 / 13 / 16" links; parent page: "LESSON 5" with "Next lesson: Lesson 16" and "The Lesson 16 workbook". A tutor who says "Lesson 16" next week points to a sheet headed "Lesson 6". Fix: one scheme.
6. **The key picture-slide of Explore is small and mostly empty.** s-explore2 (Are the groups equal?) shows three pictures in the top 25% of the slide, each about 220 px wide on a 1600 px slide, with counters of about 7 px; two thirds of the slide is blank. This is the slide on which children must see 2 versus 3 counters and answer with one or two fingers. On a laptop it reads, on a phone or a small gallery tile it does not (the guide admits "you cannot see builds on small tiles"). s-explore1 and s-explore3 are similar; s-m1 is fine. Fix: one picture per slide, full width, or the three pictures stacked at about 50% slide width each.
7. **Think harder repeats the class example and the hand-off is not shown to be deliverable.** Workbook T1 is the array on s-m4 (3 rows of 4, turn to 4 rows of 3) with the same numbers, and the answers are read aloud twice before the page (31:10 and 70:32). Separately, the goodbye (85:25) and the parent page ask children to bring "the workbook with the 100 square page" for next lesson; the Lesson 16 workbook is not given to the family anywhere in the Lesson 15 materials (the guide says only "Tell parents a week before: print the workbook pages"). Fix: new numbers in T1 (see workbook audit); say in the goodbye and parent page how and when the next workbook arrives.

## Low

8. **Camera wording invites page-showing.** "Cameras on if you can, so I can see your page and hands" (0:26) and "Check that the page and hands can be seen" (1:16) while the same guide says "Nobody holds paper to the camera". Say "so I can see you".
9. **A "different colour" pencil is needed and not listed.** "Mark your own in another colour" is said four times (36:01, 50:22, 59:07, 80:39) and "check in a different colour" again; the materials list asks for a pencil, rubber and paper only (the 2 or 3 coloured pencils are requested for next lesson).
10. **Self-star.** "Put a star by each one you got right" (80:39): use a tick only, as the Term 1 fixes did, so that no child counts stars.
11. **Slide term "Multiply".** s-m2 column heading is "Multiply" while the script and notes say "Do not say that x means multiply by: say groups of" (36:01). Use "Times sentence" or "x sentence".
12. **Workbook slides shrink the S5 table.** s-ws2 shows the S5 table at about 8 px text: unreadable at a distance. The printed page carries it and the tutor reads the question, so Low.
13. **Plates for the extension steps.** The guide asks for 4 plates and about 20 counters, but Talk time asks children to try "3 groups of 6 and 6 groups of 3" (6 plates, 18 counters) and the spare-time activity "2 groups of 7 and 7 groups of 2" needs 7 plates. The printed mat has six plates, so 6 groups fits only by using the mat; 7 does not. Say "use the mat or rows" or keep to 5 groups.
14. **Brain break counts.** Four calls in 2:30 with a pause and the total said aloud in unison, from a gallery of eight, is tight (about 37 s a call). The tap fallback is given (good). A soft clap is not audible on the recording when children are muted, so the "say the total" is the only usable evidence; fine.
15. **Mi's calculator** on the title and end slides (owner decision 6; Mi never mentions it).
16. **Parent-line sentence.** The report line says "your child is learning to draw a story, to use groups and sharing, and to test a claim with an example" which is the term line, not this lesson's; "sharing" is Lesson 17 and not yet taught here. Use the lesson line only.

## Checks that passed

- **Timing as written.** Script 69:03 (quick), 81:46 (slow) of 90; all parts "ok" in the guide's model; the model's own waits are the problem (findings 2 and 3). Workbook slot 28 minutes against a Start here of 15.5 modelled middle-child minutes plus a 4:50 choice time is realistic.
- **Tutor scripts.** One idea at a time; "Show one finger for equal and two fingers for not equal" is checkable on a gallery tile; all answers are said aloud; the tutor reads every question. Natural spoken English; SAY lines under 60 words.
- **Camera, screen and materials logistics.** Counters and plates stay on the table; a printed mat replaces plates; nothing sharp, nothing near the mouth; no paper hold-ups: ticks against the answer slide, count-of-three answers and fingers. Items for next lesson are listed identically in the script (85:25), s-end and the parent page, and agree with the L16 materials list (100 square page 6, 2 or 3 coloured pencils, about 20 counters, pencil and paper).
- **Tutor load for eight.** Acceptable for the maths; the builds in Explore rounds 1 to 3 (all children lay out plates at the same time) cannot be inspected on small tiles. Flag for a tutor trial: how many children have counters on the plates at 12:46, and how the tutor learns who has none.
- **Recording cues.** Short (under 26 words), each gives a child action; chapters time-stamped; every question and answer is read aloud.
- **Safeguarding.** No private chat; no names with answers; look-back private; "I am still stuck" via the grown-up's photo to the business WhatsApp (the number is only on the parent summary: slide s-look and the script checked); photos kept 90 days; consistent with `privacy.html`. Pass.
- **Inclusion.** Names mixed (Mei, Ali, Ben, Siti, Kumar, Aisha); no stereotyping; a printed mat for a family without plates; "a pass is always fine".
- **Beyond-P1 statement.** Correctly absent as a claim: the strand note says multiplying within 40 and the x sign are in Primary 1 (p. 31) and "do not describe the lesson as MOE-aligned"; the parent page says so honestly.
- **Reading order and glyphs.** "Groups of" reads number of groups first, in every slide, the script and the notes; either order is accepted for the child's own work (guide, 36:01 note); the slide frames use groups first; x renders as a true multiplication sign on every slide; no division sign appears; sharing is left to Lesson 17.
- **Joining cold.** The warm-up builds on counting in jumps with pictures; groups, each, equal and the sign are taught in Explore and the Mini-lesson; "joins at Lesson 16 or later can follow" is stated and true for L16's warm-up.
- **Misconceptions anticipated:** adding the numbers (4 + 3 for 4 groups of 3), counting only the groups, calling unequal groups equal; tutor responses are given (count each plate aloud, build it).
- **Deck legibility.** Text 28 px on a 1600 px slide; contrast good; slide tables clear; one task per slide. The lower half of most slides is empty (finding 6).
