Class numbers: Explore uses $2 to $8 tags with $12 and $10; the Mini uses $15, $11, $6 and Ali $12 with $4 and $9, three $5 glue sticks, five $2 rubbers, $12 with $5 and $2 notes, cup $8 and pen $3 with $20; the workbook reuses cup/ball $8 and pen $3 (T3) and the same $12 / $4 / $9 shape (E2: Ben $12, pen $5, book $8) (Low, finding 7).
