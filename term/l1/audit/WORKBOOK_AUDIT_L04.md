## B. Age and syllabus fit

Counting in 2s, 5s, 10s to 100, 10 more one row down and reading the 100 square are P1 (Whole Numbers, number-sequence patterns). Content beyond P1 is correctly kept out of Start here: growing-gap sequence (T3), sum of the two before (G1), two interleaved rules (G2), counting in 3s (Investigation), the "always true" block claim (G3). The key's algebra (a + 11) is tutor-only. Ordinals (10th) are P1. Good.

## C. Tier principle

Start here S1-S5 are approachable and need no taught idea (S5 can be done from the neighbouring numbers). Think harder T1/T2 apply "down a row is 10 more" in new settings, T3 and T4 are properly contest-style moves placed at T. Go further is placed correctly. Items flagged: S6 (spot the mistake plus a written reason in Start here, and rule 25 says "say why" belongs in T/G), E3 (a "I know because" reason in the exit ticket, same rule), G1 (a single addition after reading the rule; as hard as T3, thin for Go further).

## D. Wording (rule list)

Commands lead and numbers are digits; sentences are mostly 6-12 words. Remaining problems: findings 5, 9 above; "Fill in the blanks" after a Colour instruction in S1/S2 is two commands (acceptable, but could be "(a) ... (b)" only); G2 has three instructions in two sentences.

## E. Layout and usability for a 6-year-old

Colour, contrast, font, grid paper and card style are clear and legible; the 100-square kit page is large, numbered and needs no cutting (good for counters). Problems: findings 1, 2, 3, 10, 11. Every page except page 2 starts with a header; only page 1 has an instruction line and the choice line. No text is cut off.

## F. Pedagogy

Progression is sound: colour 10s, 5s, 2s, extend patterns, use rows, then move by rows and squares, then change the rule. Start here time (modelled mins 3, 3.5, 3, 3, 3, 2.5 = 18 min) fits the 28-minute workbook slot. Think harder and Go further differ less than they should (finding 8). Misconceptions targeted in the key "Watch for" lines are realistic (adding 1 for down, 22/26 for the wrong last digit). Exit ticket matches objectives 1 and 2 but only partly 3 and 4. Weakness: no problem-posing or making a pattern anywhere in the workbook.

## G. Safeguarding and inclusion

Look-back is private ("Nobody reads it out", pass option), "I am still stuck (my tutor will come and help)" is in the open session, no ranking, no labels such as gifted or weak, tier names are neutral, mixed cast. No issues.

## H. Consistency with other lessons

Same page shell, tab numbering (No.S1...), choice line and dash blanks as Lesson 5 and others. Lesson 4 is the only one of the two audited with a pagination defect; its choice line is "after question 2" while S1-S2 are on different pages.

## Strengths

Correct maths; low-floor colouring start; clean separation of syllabus (2s, 5s, 10s, rows) from enrichment (growing gap, sum of two, interleaved, 3s); good one-page kit square; honest "You do not have to finish" wording.
