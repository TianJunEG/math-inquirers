## Medium
1. **Papers "to the camera so only you see it" (guide 0:00 sneak-peek 11:03, exit 79:31; deck notes slides 5, 24).** In a live group call every child sees every tile, so holding a paper up shows it to all, and 8 tiles at webcam resolution cannot be read by the tutor in 30 s. The claim "only you see it" is false. Fix: say how (tutor pins/spotlights nobody, children hold up for a count of three accepting the group sees it, or children send nothing and the tutor reads thumbs/fingers), or drop the claim and rely on self-marking; state the platform setting.
2. **One webcam must show page, two plates and hands (opening DO 1:12, explore).** Laptop cameras face the child. No mirror/phone-stand instruction for families. Fix: add a materials line (camera tilted down or a phone on a stack of books) or accept the face-only view and use fingers/answers.
3. **Mi holds a calculator on title and goodbye slides (and the small Mi spots).** AUTHORING says do not show or rely on the calculator. Known first-round finding 12, still open. Fix: new Mi art or crop.
4. **Workbook time vs Start here.** Guide section 9: mid child needs 8.0 + 11.0 min; script gives 8:00 + 8:40 (+ 4:30 choice). Page 2 slide says "Timer: 9 minutes" while script says [8:40]. The slow-class workbook (29:56 vs 28:00), mini (10:15) and talk (6:01) overrun but are flagged "ok"; only the 5-minute flex buffer absorbs it. Fix: flag overruns honestly or give page 2 about 11 min from Explore (16 min has 3:44 spare).
5. **Slide s-ws2 omits the numbers** (S4 "7 + 5 = 12. Fill in the missing numbers.", S5 and S7 "Fill in the missing numbers." only) while s-ws1 prints S1 to S3 in full. A child watching the recording, or a child who lost the page, cannot see what is asked; the tutor reads them aloud only once. Fix: print the items as on page 1.
6. **"I will stamp your inquiry journal" (80:41; slide 24 note "Stamp the inquiry journal").** No journal appears in any materials list and a tutor cannot stamp online. Fix: remove or define (e.g. "show your grown-up your page").
7. **Goodbye before the optional activities.** s-end ("Goodbye, and thank you") precedes optional slides s-opt and s-menu in the deck and script. If used, the lesson ends twice. Fix: move s-end last or say goodbye after the optional slide.
