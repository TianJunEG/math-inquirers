## E. Layout and usability (6-year-old, A4)

Answer boxes are large rounded squares with room for two digits where needed (S6 result, G1 12, G2c, G3a). Yes/No and Share/Group are bold words to circle, no printed buttons (same as Term 2 so far). Every page opens with an instruction line, and page 1 carries the "After question 3 you may choose" banner. Pictures are clear: cookies (S2) and sweets (S3) are recognisable, T1 counters are large and countable. Weak points: the S5 line (finding 4), the S1 plates (5), the Investigation space (9). Nothing is cut off. The kit page is a large six-plate mat, nothing to cut, printable and usable (page fill about 80%).

## F. Pedagogy

Progression is sound: deal, ring groups, share a total, table of shares, jumps on a line, check by multiplying, tell share from group. Start here is feasible: the guide's own middle-child figure is 7.5 + 9.5 = 17 minutes, against 28 allotted but see the guide audit on the script's 7 + 7 minutes. Think harder and Go further are different (left over and reading traps versus factors and two-step stories). The exit ticket matches objectives 1 to 3 (share, tell share from group, group count with a check). Misconceptions in the key are specific (writing the number of friends, 5 for the last row of S4, choosing by the number 3 or 6 in S7). The three-line "Watch for" entries are used. The "I think ... because" justification is only in the Talk, not in the workbook.
