## Method and the build audited

- Build state. While this audit ran, `out/l01` to `out/l05` were rebuilt (files timestamped 22 Oct 2026, 17:15 to 17:17; uncommitted; `lessons/l01.json` to `l05.json` unchanged from HEAD). The PDFs differ from HEAD only in blank-space widths (checked by diffing the extracted text). The practice data (`data/t1l0N.json`) is identical to HEAD. The runner changed: the rebuilt pages use the new, untracked `term/l1/runner/` (relative paths, shuffled options, input messages, 44 px targets); HEAD used `practice/public/`. I ran the whole test battery on the old runner first and again on the rebuilt one; the findings below are against the rebuilt build and say where HEAD differs.
- Printed pieces: both PDFs rendered with pdftoppm (60 and 100 dpi) and viewed; ink margins measured from the pixels; font sizes read with pdfplumber; text read with pdftotext; the five home answers recomputed by hand and in Python (brute-force enumeration for the card and calendar items).
- Online practice: `out/l05/practice/` served as a static site and driven with Playwright 1.56.0 (Chromium 141) at 375x667, 320x568 (dark mode) and 1280x800. Every item of every tier was opened; the authored answer was typed or chosen (all accepted); wrong-twice reveals the right answer (all match); each choice option was tried singly (only the right one passes); 14 typed formats per number item; every templated item was enumerated over all variable combinations (this lesson: see table) and compared with an independent oracle written from the question text; 45 variants per template were also drawn in the browser and the revealed answer compared; request log, cookies, `localStorage`, console, DOM before a check, tab order, focus after checking and 44 px targets were captured.
- AUDIT ONLY: no lesson, tool, template or output file was edited. Scripts live in the auditor scratchpad only.
