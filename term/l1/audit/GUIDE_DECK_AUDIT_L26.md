## What works

One idea at a time on the whole: exact pairs, enough or not, equal prices added again, a table of ways, total then change. The warm-up needs only counting to 20 (make 10 on a ten-frame, three equal prices, the pairs of 6 in order), so a child who joins cold can follow; the guide says so and marks the lesson "fairly safe to miss" with a Quest gate on it. The Explore is concrete and kit-led and the Mini slide `s-m3` puts the table on the screen with the "money left" column given, so the listing method (a row for each number of $5 notes, an X when the rest cannot be made) is modelled before the workbook uses it. Answers are held off the screen where the children are asked: `s-m1`, `s-m2`, `s-m4` have the question only and the answer arrives on `s-m1a`, `s-m2a`, `s-m4a`; `s-explore1` and `s-explore2` show the instruction and the answer slides follow. The Talk question teaches that a way is not an order, with "two children say" (no names). The safeguarding block agrees with the script: no paper, tag or note to the camera, thumbs and fingers, small pieces on the table, nobody cuts, no private chat, WhatsApp-only route with no number on any slide. The beyond-P1 statement is in the strand note, the opening NOTE and tutor_prep and is honest (exact pairs, total and change are P1; listing every way and three kinds of note are beyond on purpose). No MOE-aligned claim. The hand-off lines match `l27.json` (16 tiles cut, tags and money kept).
