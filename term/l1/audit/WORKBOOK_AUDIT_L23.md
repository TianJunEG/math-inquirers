## B. Age and syllabus fit

Start here (S1 to S6) is Primary 1 work as the school-paper pattern file describes it: draw the hands, choose the clock, a duration of 1 hour or half an hour, am and pm without full stops, times as digits. Think harder adds quarter-time starts (4:15, 9:45), a half-hour step that crosses the hour, a one-stage find-the-start and a timetable: placed correctly. Go further (two-stage trip backwards, noon crossing) is beyond P1 and is never needed for Start here. The beyond item is named (`beyond.name` "Find the Start"). One naming wrinkle (Low): the one-stage find-the-start is P1 work and sits in T3 and E3, so the puzzle called "Find the Start" is itself only partly beyond P1; the two-stage trip and the noon crossing are the parts that are.

## C. Tier principle

Start here approachable and class-explored: yes (the Explore round used 1:00 and 1:30; the Mini-lesson used 10:00, 12:00 and a table). No contest item at Start here. Non-routine types (preflight section 13) are all at T or G: BACK at T3, MULTI at G1, timetable at T4. T2b (9:45 plus half an hour is 10:15) is borderline: adding 30 minutes to a quarter-to time is not a new setting of the taught idea, it is a new step (the class only crossed the hour from half past) and the guide tells the tutor not to teach quarter times; the item is fine as a Think harder stretch but it is the one that is not "the taught idea in a new setting" (strict count 12/13).

## D. Wording (guide rules and `p1_school_paper_patterns.md`)

Plain, short, command first, (a)/(b), units, Singapore English. Time follows the patterns file: "o'clock" and "half past" in words with digits, times as digits only after that, am and pm lower case with a space and no full stops, no "quarter past/to" on any workbook page, no 24-hour clock. "1 hour and half an hour" (G2b) is natural speech but a child could read it as two separate durations; "1 hour 30 minutes" is not P1 wording, so the fix is a clause ("a long film: 1 hour, then half an hour more") rather than a change of notation. T4 stem "The table shows the shows at a zoo in the afternoon" repeats "shows" (noun, noun); "The table shows the times of the zoo shows" is smoother. No hints in stems. No wrong working attached to a cast name. Hints appear only in the key and guide.

## E. Layout and usability for a 6-year-old (A4, 80 to 300 dpi)

Page flow: five pages as designed, each starts with a clear line ("Use your clock...", "Read the clocks and the table...", "Write am or pm where it is asked..."). Name and date on every page. Mi on page 1 only. Page 3 is about 70% full and pages 1, 2 and 4 are full; no page is under 40% full (rubric 14 passes). Page 4 header line wraps to two lines, touching the first question label (Low).

Clock faces (the key figure of the lesson) are 28 to 32 mm across, numbers are readable, ticks are clear, hour and minute hands are both dark navy and differ only in thickness and length. Two real legibility defects (finding 2): at half past the hour hand is drawn almost on top of the minute hand (S4 clock B, S5 both clocks, G2), and every minute hand ends in a round tip that sits over the numeral it points to, so the 6 at half past and the 12 at o'clock lose half their glyph (visible in A, B and the S5 clocks at 150 dpi). The correct answer to S4 depends on telling those two hands apart.

Writing space: blanks are about 20 mm and sit on the sentence line, good. The S1 end clock is an empty face with a centre dot: drawing two hands on a 30 mm face is hard for a six-year-old and the key accepts "short hand on 9, long hand on 12"; acceptable. Page 5 (finding 3) has only four dotted lines for an Investigation that needs seven time entries, a first-step answer with no blank at all, and a look-back box with ragged wrapped check boxes ("something else", "I was not stuck", "asking a question" and "I would like to pass" each sit alone on a line).

The no-calculator Mi (finding 1) is not used yet: page 1 still shows the dolphin holding a calculator.

## F. Pedagogy

Progression is sound: read the clock, one hour later, half an hour later, two clocks, a table, then back, across the hour, across noon. The exit ticket matches the objective (E1 forward, E2 half an hour as a choice, E3 count back), although E3 tests the Think harder skill of finding a start for every child after only one worked example ("Your turn: going back"). Timing: Start here is 16.5 minutes for a middle child (guide: page 1 7.5, page 2 9) against 8:00 plus 9:00 allotted: it fits, and the guide says plainly that pages 3 to 5 are for home. The page itself does not say so (finding 5, Low). Think harder and Go further really are different (one-stage forward, then back, then two-stage back plus noon). Misconceptions targeted: adding 1 to the minutes, 5:60, mixing "half an hour" with "half past", am/pm at noon. Not targeted: the hour hand's position at half past (the very thing S4 asks).

Repeats of class numbers: none in Start here or Think harder (Explore used 1:00, 1:30; Mini used 10:00, 12:00, 6:00, 9:00 and 11:00; the workbook uses other times). The optional slide "both ways" (1 hour and half an hour, both ending at 6:00) is G3's structure, so it pre-teaches G3 if used.

## G. Safeguarding and inclusion

Names (Ravi, Lily, Kumar, Siti, Ali, Mei) spread across Chinese, Malay and Indian names; wrong working never attached to a name; no ranking or labels; the look-back says "Nobody reads it out" and "You can pass"; the "I am still stuck" box tells the child a grown-up will photograph the page for the tutor (WhatsApp route stated in the guide and parent page, not on the page), consistent with default 9. No paper is held to a camera anywhere.
