## F. Pedagogy

Progression: a cup, a pair of equal shapes, three equal shapes, a known shape plus two equal, a claim to check, a bag version, then two lines, a friend's claim, a times line, three shapes, two lines together. Sound, and "put the number back and check" is practised in S6 and T2. Start here is feasible only slowly (finding 3). Think harder and Go further differ (two lines and a times line versus three shapes and a sum with a product). The exit ticket matches objectives 1 to 3 (E1 same shape twice, E2 one hidden number, E3 two equal shapes plus one known); it does not test the Think harder idea, correctly. Misconceptions are named in the key: writing the total, adding the total twice, counting the shapes, checking only line 1, accepting 20 for 17 "because it is close".
