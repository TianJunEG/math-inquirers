## B. Age and syllabus fit

Days of the week and dates are not a listed Primary 1 outcome, as the moe note says; Start here (days in order, day before and after, 3 and 5 days later, a week on a month page, true or false) is approachable. Think harder (a fortnight, the 10th day, "two days after the day before yesterday") and Go further (100 days, every Wednesday) are beyond on purpose and the lesson names it ("Hundred Days Later" in `moe.note`, `beyond`, the guide and the parent summary). The lesson never writes a division and says "14 weeks and 2 days over"; the key says "14 times 7, which is 98 days" (multiplication in words at Go further, Low). Time reading in the Quest (a clock to 5 minutes, am or pm) is Term 3 Lessons 21 and 22 content (beyond Primary 1 on purpose) and is stated as Start here level for the term. No MOE-aligned wording.

## F. Pedagogy

Progression: days in order and the cycle (Explore: cards in a row, count on from tomorrow, a month page), then days later, the day before, a week and a fortnight (Mini-lesson), then the Quest, then the workbook. Start here is carried by counting on and the month page; Think harder adds the 10th day, a fortnight and a date's weekday; Go further adds 100 days and every Wednesday; the Investigation asks which days come five times. The exit ticket (days later; a week on the month page; every 7 days) matches the objective and is not done live. Timing: per-question estimates 2.5, 3, 3, 3, 3.5, 3 = 18 minutes against the JSON note "about 16" and the guide's "11.5 minutes for page 1 and 6.5 for page 2" (18); the script allows 6:40 for page 1 and 7:40 for page 2 (a total 14:20 with reading), so a middle child cannot finish Start here in the workbook slot after the Quest; pages 3 to 5 are for home, as the lesson says (finding 8).

Class numbers: **S3(b) "Today is Tuesday ... 5 days later it is Sunday" is the Explore round 2 example** ("Tuesday and five days later is Sunday", `s-explore2-ans`), so the answer was said in class (finding 1). Sneak-peek Thursday plus 5 is not reused. Mini numbers (the 9th, the 2nd, the 4th; Thursday plus 4) are not reused in the workbook. The Quest gates avoid lesson numbers apart from gate 8, which repeats the S3 structure (Friday plus 3). Talk (Monday plus 10 is Thursday) and T2 (day 10 from Monday is Wednesday) are deliberate neighbours.

Misconceptions targeted: counting today as day 1, Sunday to Monday not wrapping, "a week later" as 8 days, "fortnight", confusing date and weekday: all appear in the key's "watch for" lines and the guide.
