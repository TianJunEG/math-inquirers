## What is sound (checked and found fine)

- Maths: all guide, slide, key, exit and home answers recomputed (for example 62 - 38 = 24, 83 - 77 = 6, 100 - 64 = 36 both ways, 83 - 47 = 86 - 50 = 36, 71 - 46 = 25); Go further G3 and the spare-time M1 are true; gaps in the brain break (22, 15, 5, 35) are right.
- The two-tool idea (take away for what is left, count up for the gap) and "check by adding" are consistent between guide, deck, workbook, key and parent page; terms (take away, count up, difference, gap, tool) are used the same way everywhere.
- Answers are hidden until a separate reveal slide for the worksheets, the exit ticket and the sneak-peek (Back to the sneak-peek).
- Recording cues: 11 cues of 11 to 25 words, each with a child action and a chapter time; SAY lines are natural spoken English, one idea at a time.
- Safeguarding: no private chat, no breakout rooms, volunteers first and a pass always allowed, no name with a wrong answer, look-back private, nothing sharp, no purchase.
- Beyond-Primary-1 statement: present in the strand note and on the parent page; no MOE, GEP or SASMO claim.
- Mi: 4 slides, one short friendly line each, never about answers.
- Misconceptions are anticipated per question (taking 2 + 5 = 7, subtracting the smaller digit from the larger, adding instead of subtracting) with a hint and a common-mistakes line.
- Spare-time menu: three whole-class activities (3 to 4 min each), tutor line, REC cue and answer, optional slide, never in the 90-minute sum.
