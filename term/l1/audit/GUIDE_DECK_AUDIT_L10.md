# Guide and deck audit: Level 1 Term 1 Lesson 10 (Story Steps: Read It Twice) and the end-of-term check

Auditor: independent desk review. Read all 21 pages of `L10_Tutor_Guide.pdf`, viewed all 34 slides of `L10_Deck.pdf`, and read `L10_Check_Tutor_Key.pdf` (6 pp), `L10_Check_Student.pdf` (4 pp, viewed), `L10_Check_Parent_Note.pdf`, `lessons/l10.json` incl. `end_of_term_check`. All lesson, exit and check answers re-solved: correct.

Instruments: AUTHORING 16-point rubric (delivery): items 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 15 PASS; 3 (timing) FAIL for the check. Instrument A delivery re-score: **16/24**. Verdict: **Needs rework** (the check timing and the lesson plan around it); the maths, safeguarding wording and key are sound.

Findings: High 1, Medium 8, Low 7.

## High
1. **The 20-minute check does not fit, and the three documents disagree.**
   - Guide script: child work time 2:10 + 3:00 + 3:50 + 2:40 = **11:40** for 10 questions with about 22 answer slots (including drawing a 6 cm line and a number-line reading), about 32 s per answer.
   - Tutor key section 2.4: quiet work 3 + 4 + 5 + 4 = **16 min** (page 1 five answers, page 3 seven answers and a ruler drawing).
   - Plan, JSON `minutes` and parent note: **20 minutes**. Script from s-check1 (62:26) to pencils down (78:52) is 16:26 including intro, reading, stretch.
   - Evidence of realistic need: the lessons' own per-question "middle child" times for comparable Start here items are 2 to 3 min each (L8 S5 3 min, L9 S1 2.5 min); ten such questions need about 22 to 28 min plus reading aloud and a stretch. At the key's own pacing the check runs about 21:50, ending near 84:20, and with the slow-class factor (1.2 on child time) about 93 min, over the 90.
   - Lockstep "Turn to page 2" pacing means a slow child is cut off mid-page, contradicting "not a race" and "may use flex time" (which is 5 minutes already spoken for).
   Fix (any one, preferably the first two together): (a) drop the L10 exit ticket (it duplicates check Q10 and costs about 3.5 min) and start the check at 59:00 with 24 min; (b) let children work at their own pace across pages after the tutor has read all questions of a page, with a visible finish time; (c) cut the check to 8 questions or move Q9 and Q10 to the make-up slot; (d) state 25 minutes in the plan, note and key and re-time. Reconcile the key (16 min) and the guide (11:40) to one table.

## Medium
2. **Start here workbook time too short.** 35 to 55 gives 5:30 (page 1) + 4:30 (page 2) for work a mid child needs 7.5 + 8.0 min (guide section 9); choice time 2:30. The guide excuses this ("pages 3 to 5 may go home") but Start here itself is not finished. Slow-class work 20:07 vs 20:00 flagged ok.
3. **Exit ticket E1 to E3 (three story questions, one a two-step) in "Timer: 2 minutes" (script 1:40)** is too tight and is immediately followed by the check; E1 duplicates check Q10 (same structure). Slide says 2 minutes, guide 3 minutes in places.
4. **Fatigue and sequence.** The check starts at minute 62, after 60 minutes of live screen time; 6-year-olds meet a silent 16 to 20 minute test at the end. Consider the check first after the warm-up, or a separate make-up slot for the whole group.
5. **Privacy of returns (PDPA).** Photos of children's pages with names go to one business WhatsApp number (default pending owner change). Gaps: no named person or access list (WhatsApp Web may be open on several tutor devices); "delete from the chat" does not remove copies in the device gallery, cloud backup or Meta servers; the parent note does not say WhatsApp is a third-party service or how long the progress note and record sheet are kept; the number is printed on slides s-check1 and s-check2 and is therefore in every recording. Fix: name an owner of the number, disable media auto-save and backups, add retention and third-party wording to the note, consider an upload form instead, remove the number from the recorded slides (read it out on the live call or put it in the grown-up note only).
6. **"Hold the ticket to the camera so only you see it"** (61:24) is untrue in a gallery view (same as L8, L9). The check itself correctly keeps papers flat.
7. **Recording gap.** A child watching the recording cannot take the check ("ask your grown-up how to take it"); the make-up route is in the key but the recording chapter list has one chapter 59:00 to 83:00 covering about 24 minutes of silence. Add chapter 62:26 "End-of-term check" and say on the slide that the sheet is taken live or at the make-up slot.
8. **Plan and decisions out of step.** Plan section 5 still prescribes 4 Start here, 4 Think harder, 2 Go further; the build is 10 Start here plus one optional Think harder, justified in the JSON as "owner brief for this build". `docs/terms/owner_decisions.md` records only the 20-minute length, return route and keeping the optional item. Record the all-Start-here choice in the decisions file or restore the mix. Check items Q5 (37 + 5) and Q7 (74 - 31) are above the school Primary 1 ceiling at Start here, consistent with the 22 Oct owner decision but should be said in the parent note.
9. **Check Q9 marking from a parent's photo.** The key accepts 5.7 to 6.3 cm by comparing with the 10 cm bar in the photo (57 to 63%); perspective and print scale make this fragile; it also depends on a family that printed at 100%. Accept 5.5 to 6.5 or mark R/P on "drawn from the dot, about 6 cm".
