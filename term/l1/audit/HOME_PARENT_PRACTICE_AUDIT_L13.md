## C. Online practice behaviour (summary)

Typed answers: spaces, a leading zero and a unit are accepted; a word (`six`) is refused with a kind "number digits" message that does not use up a try; two numbers are refused with "just the answer number"; an empty box asks for an answer. A first wrong answer shows a slip line (base numbers only) plus a one-line hint; a second different wrong answer shows the answer with "Let us look at it together." (kind; no score, no timer, no streak); the same wrong answer twice gets "That is the same answer as before. Read the hint and try a different one." (kind). Multi-box marking is per box, ordered and right on the first view: swapped boxes are refused, empty boxes asked for. Keyboard: Enter checks, the first input has focus on load, every plain input has an aria-label. Phone width (390 px): one column, buttons large, the figure fills the card (316 px wide). No external requests, no tracking, no console errors. The fonts are local.

Whether a child who has just been refused "7, 14" understands "red edge" without a grown-up; whether the figure at 316 px on a phone (shapes about 34 px) is legible to a six-year-old; and, after the L19-1 fix, a pass of every `fields` item on a phone.
