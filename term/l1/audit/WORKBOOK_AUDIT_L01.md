# Independent workbook audit: Level 1 Term 1, Lesson 1 (Bundles and Singles)

Auditor: independent desk review by a model that did not write the lesson. Sources: `out/l01/L01_Workbook.pdf` (6 pages) and `L01_Workbook_Answer_Key.pdf` (3 pages), both rendered with pdftoppm and every page viewed; lesson data `lessons/l01.json` (hints, minutes, materials) read for context. No child used it; all judgements are from text and rendered pages. The author self-audit (`AUDIT_L01.md`, 19/24) was read after forming my own view.

Instruments used (same scales as the holiday-course audits): (1) gifted-style rubric, 12 criteria x 0/1/2, max 24 (`docs/gifted_research.md` section 2, `reaudit_p1.md` instrument A), scored on workbook evidence; (2) tier compliance, strict, per printed item (`gifted_brief_template.txt` step 2). The student-journey instrument (B) was not used: it needs slides and scripts, which this brief excludes.

## Verdict: Ready after minor fixes. Rubric 17/24. Tier compliance 14/14 (100%) with two caveats. 0 High, 4 Medium, 8 Low.

## (A) Maths correctness
I solved all 14 workbook items plus E1-E3 and the investigation independently and compared with the key: S1 26; S2 35; S3 42, 70, 19, 85; S4 5t8o, 4t0o, 7t1o; S5 4 bundles, 7 left; S6 No, 34; S7 60 and 6; T1 64; T2 11; T3 46 (36 fails "even tens"; unique); T4 same (72 = 72); G1 Siti, 11 more (74 vs 85); G2 58, needs 2; G3 4 ways; E1 48; E2 3 tens 7 ones; E3 70. All match the key. I counted the printed pictures: S1 shows 2 bundles and 6 singles, E1 shows 4 bundles and 8 singles; both agree with the key. No wrong, unanswerable or two-answer item found. G3 has one soft spot: "different ways" could be read as also allowing 10 + 10 + 10; the example "3 tens and 0 ones" steers it, but a sentence frame "_ tens and _ ones" would remove doubt.

## (B) Age and syllabus fit (MOE P1: numbers to 100, tens and ones, read and write numerals)
Core content (S1-S7, E1-E3) is squarely P1. Opening a bundle (5 tens 14 ones) is a standard place-value extension, correctly at Think harder. Beyond P1: "even" in T3 (odd and even numbers first appear in P2, syllabus 1.6, p. 33). It is not defined anywhere on the page. It is at Think harder, so placement is right, but the word is an unexplained barrier (finding 3).

## (D) Wording (WORDING_GUIDE rules)
Mostly good: commands first, units after blanks, short stems, Singapore names, "rubber" not used. Remaining problems: G1 stem has no command and its blank (a) starts with a name (rule 2, 16). G3 asks three things in one run ("Write ... Find all the ways. How many ways are there?"), rule 18 wants (a)(b). S5 says "straws", S1/S6 "sticks", G2 "pencils": three objects in one lesson (rule 5, consistency). "Circle Yes or No" with nothing printed to circle (finding 2). T4 "Circle one: Mei has more. Ali has more. They have the same." is a run-on option list; print three options as separate lines.

## (E) Layout and usability for a 6-year-old
Legible: 18 px question text, dashed answer lines, 36 px answer boxes, drawing boxes about 105 px, good contrast, Mi small in the page 1 corner, no clipping, page order Start here, Start here, Think harder, Go further, Investigation + exit, Kit. Fill is good (page 2 about 80%, others 90%+). Problems: drawing capacity (finding 1); Investigation has no writing area (finding 4); key page 1 ends with the orphan heading "Think harder (page 3)" with its items on the next page; the kit page intro line touches the yellow wavy underline and the right quarter of the page is empty grid; the kit is cuttable: 9 bars and 20 squares cover every item that needs objects (S5 needs 4+7, T1 5+14, G2 4+18).

## (F) Pedagogy
Progression is sound: count, draw, write, spot mistake, then open a bundle. Misconceptions are targeted well (S3d "5 ones and 8 tens" reversal, S6 reversed digits, S7 6 tens vs 6 ones, T4 "12 is greater than 2"). Time: modelled Start here is 17.5 minutes for a middle child, inside the 20-minute budget, but 7 items with three drawing tasks will run longer for a slow writer. Exit ticket matches objectives 1-3 but not objective 4 (opening a bundle); acceptable because that is Think harder. Variety is limited to counting/writing/drawing; the investigation is the only open task and G3 the only "find all".

## (G) Safeguarding and inclusion
No labels, no ranking, no "gifted/weak", private paper, look-back has a pass option. Casting: Siti is the child who makes the mistake in S6 here and again in L02 S8; Ali and Mei are never wrong. Across three lessons the wrong-answer child is Siti, Siti, Ravi. Rotate it (finding 12).

## (H) Consistency with L02-L03
Same header, tier pills, hint line, box and dash style, mascot only on page 1, Look-back block. Differences: L01 uses sticks pictures while L02 and L03 use bars; "Circle Yes or No" has no options in all three; the Investigation has no response space in all three.

## Instrument A (0-2)
1 low floor/high ceiling 2 (S1-S3 vs T and Investigation); 2 depth over speed 1 (14 items plus investigation in 28 minutes; "any order" helps); 3 generalise 2 (swap the digits); 4 justify 1 (S6, T4, but only one line or none); 5 multiple paths 1; 6 ladder 2; 7 student inquiry 1; 8 context 1; 9 problem posing 1 (no make-your-own in the workbook, only G3 find-all); 10 metacognition 2 (printed look-back, pass option; "what I would try next" present); 11 pacing 1 (choice line after Q3 only); 12 extension not acceleration 2. **Total 17/24** against the author's 19. I scored 9 and 4 lower because the printed workbook alone carries them; the lesson script may add more.
