## Verdict: READY AFTER MINOR FIXES

The maths is right throughout, the bar figures are drawn to scale and match the text, the tiers are sensibly built (compare, add the extra, join; then sum and difference, make equal, "fewer than"; then three people and a transfer with a total), and the Investigation (same total, different gaps) is a good one. The weaknesses: the Start here arithmetic needs two-digit addition with regrouping that nothing in the lesson scaffolds (and the lesson claims a child needs nothing from Term 1), the transfer arrow in T2 and G1 is unreadable (a thin line struck through its own label), the Investigation again has no room to draw, and the cut-out strip kit cannot make the numbers the questions use. Counts: 0 High, 4 Medium, 7 Low.

## B. Age and syllabus fit

Addition and subtraction stories within 100 are Primary 1 content; the strips (bar models) are not named in the syllabus and, on the guide's own note, schools use them from Primary 2; sum and difference and make-equal-by-transfer are contest-style. The lesson says so honestly and keeps those at Think harder and Go further, as owner decision 6 requires. Start here totals reach 85 (S3(b)) and sums with a regroup (34 + 17, 28 + 6, 28 + 34): above the school P1 quiz ceiling of about 40, kept on purpose by the owner's "stronger arithmetic" decision on condition the item is approachable with scaffolding. See finding 1 for why this one is only partly scaffolded.

## C. Tier principle

Start here S1 to S7: approachable for the strip idea (the figures are given, the child reads a gap, adds an extra part or joins). S4 (draw two strips) is the only item where the child produces the figure. Think harder T1 (total and difference), T2 (transfer) and T3 ("fewer than", reversed) are the previewed ideas in a new setting, with `bar_model` figures; T2 is a real transfer and is first met in the sneak-peek and the mini-lesson extras. Go further G1 (a transfer with a total), G2 (three people, a chain with a total), G3 (change in a gap) are genuine extensions. No contest-style item is in Start here. The partial violations: S3, S5 and exit items E2, E3 need regrouping (34 + 17 = 51, 28 + 6 = 34, 28 + 34 = 62, 27 + 15 = 42, 25 + 33 = 58) with only a strip picture and no tens scaffold; the exit ticket repeats that level, so a child who cannot regroup cannot show the strip idea even if they have it (finding 1).

## D. Wording against the guide

Good: command words, "How many more ... than ...?" fixed question frame, numerals for quantities, MCQ with (1) to (4) and a bracket (rule 23), "Write its number in the brackets", answer frames ending in the noun ("Ali has [ ] more stickers than Mei."). S7 asks for a number sentence from words, in the school paper style. Remaining: the name Dev is not in the cast of rule 21 (Low); the minus sign is a hyphen "40 - 24" (typographic, Low); "Draw a strip for Mei and a strip for Ravi." (S4) does not say how long a box is (Low). No hint is in a stem. The "Look at the strips. A longer strip means more." intro line on page 1 is a lesson sentence outside a question, which rule 24 permits as a page hint.

## E. Layout and usability for a 6-year-old

Page 1 and page 2: clean, one question per card, picture before answer line, good boxes (two-digit boxes about 8 mm wide, enough). The S4 drawing box is 40 mm tall and 150 mm wide, enough for two strips. Page 3: three questions with strips, good spacing. Page 4: the transfer arrows in G1 collide with their labels; the bottom quarter of the page is blank. Page 5: crowded (Investigation + exit ticket + look-back) and the Investigation has no drawing room. Page 6: strips are 10 cells in two colours (yellow, light blue), 150 mm long; printed at 100 percent the cells are about 15 mm; half the page is empty. Left and right margins are 14.0 and 14.5 mm and top 14.2 mm. The footer sits 5.8 mm from the bottom edge on every page (measured), below the usual 6 mm unprintable band.

## F. Pedagogy

A good sequence: lay strips, then a story, then the two ways to join, then "fewer than". The Investigation matches the lesson (pair, check the total, spot the shift). The exit ticket is the three Start here ideas (gap, add the extra, altogether) and matches the objective. Misconceptions are targeted (adding when asked how many more; writing the extra part only; the whole gap instead of half). Weaknesses: the title says "More and Fewer" but no Start here item uses "fewer than" (it first appears at T3, which is deliberate per the plan, but the title, the look-back ("more than or fewer than") and the parent line ("more than, fewer than and how many more") promise it for everyone, finding 5); "Draw one strip for each person" (objective 1) is tested in S4 only.

## G. Safeguarding and inclusion

Private paper; no ranking; neutral labels; "everyone may choose". Names are mixed and inclusive (Ali, Mei, Ben, Kumar, Siti, Dev, Ravi, Aisha, Lily, Wei, Jenny). The look-back "I am still stuck (my grown-up will photograph this page for my tutor)" is consistent with the WhatsApp-only route; the photographed page 5 also carries the exit ticket and Investigation.

## H. Consistency with the other lessons

Header badge "Term 2 . Lesson 2", footer "Term 2", box and blank style, look-back block and the Mi mascot on page 1 follow the Term 1 conventions and Lesson 11. The key heading wraps to two lines ("Answer key: Lesson 2, Bar Strips: More / and Fewer"), a cosmetic Low also seen in the key's "Watch for" lists, which print as "Adding 12 + 8 = 20.; Writing 12, the whole strip." with ".;".
