## Medium

1. **Answers are on the slide while the question is asked (s-m3c "Next to each other?", s-m3 "A near double: 7 + 8", s-explore2 "Bigger doubles").** The static slide shows "6 + 7: Yes, 6, 7 / 5 + 8: No, 5, 6, 7, 8 / 9 + 10: Yes" and "7 + 8 = 7 + 7 + 1 = 14 + 1 = 15" at the same moment the script asks the children (32:08 "Six and seven. Are they next to each other?"; 29:56 "How could we check 7 + 8?"). The notes say "ask each pair before showing the table answers" but a PDF slide cannot do that. s-explore2 also shows the finished picture (7 red + 3 yellow in one frame, 4 in the other) while the script asks "Double 7 is ten and what?". Fix: split each into a question slide and an answer slide, or build the table row by row in the deck tool.
2. **The "Which double do we use?" slide (s-m3b) does not show what it says.** Captions read "Double 7 and 1 more" and "Double 8 and 1 less" but both pictures show a plain double (7 + 7 and 8 + 8) with no extra or missing counter. The key teaching picture for "1 more / 1 less" is absent. Fix: show 7 red + 8 yellow with the 8th ringed, and 8 red + 8 yellow with one yellow dashed ("take away").
3. **Hand-off to Lesson 14 is incomplete on the slide, in the script and in the guide.** s-end, the 85:25 SAY line and the guide say "Please have 20 counters ... a pencil and paper". L14 needs the workbook with the number-card kit page (page 6) cut out, which only the parent summary says ("The Lesson 14 workbook, with its number cards cut out") and L14's own opening does not ask for them either (see that audit). A family that never reads the parent page arrives without cards for Explore round 2. Fix: add "your kit page of number cards, cut out" to s-end and the SAY line.
4. **Lesson numbering is inconsistent across the package.** Cover and slides say "Lesson 3 of 10" (Term 2), the guide text says "Lesson 13 is fairly safe to miss ... Lessons 14 or 15 ... Lesson 16", the parent summary says "Lesson 14: Odd + Odd" while its own heading shows "3". A tutor reading section 1 and a parent holding the printed workbook see different numbers. Fix: one scheme everywhere (recommended: "Lesson 3 of Term 2" in children's and parents' pages, "L13" only in file names), and fix the joining-cold paragraph.
5. **Recording and greeting order contradict.** The script says the recording notice at 0:48 ("This lesson is recorded ...") and the DO line at 1:17 says "greet each child by first name before you start the recording" and prep says "Start the recording after the greeting". If the notice is said before the recording starts, the recording has no statement; if it starts first, names are said on the recording. The wording matches `website/privacy.html` ("We tell the group at the start of each lesson") but not the order. Fix: put the greeting by name first (not recorded), then start recording, then say the notice, then no names.
6. **A "different colour" pencil is needed but not in the materials.** The check slides say "Mark your own in another colour" (35:10, 50:03, 58:36, 80:49) but the child materials list only a pencil and rubber. Fix: add "a second pencil or pen colour" or say "tick your own with a tick".

## Low

8. **Warm-up answer note does not match the script.** NOTE says "Answers: 3, 6, 1, 4, 2" (five) but only four asks are scripted (7, 4, 9, 6); slide chips show five (7, 4, 9, 6, 8).
9. **Brain-break slide and script differ.** Slide: "Try 2, then 3, then 4"; script: "Call 2, 3, 4, 5, 6". The slide also says "Stomp that many times on each foot" while the script gives a 2:30 timing; stomping in a flat is noisy (a tap or tiptoe fallback is given: good).
10. **Self-star.** "Put a star by each one you got right" (80:49) invites counting stars; Term 1 used "fingers for how many right". Use a tick only.
11. **Workbook slides show no question content for S3 and S6** (s-ws1 shows S1 and S2 only; s-ws2 shows S4 and S5 pictures, S6 no statements). A child watching the recording without a workbook sees no questions. The script reads them aloud (good); add the table of statements to the slide.
12. **Slow-class mini-lesson flagged OVER (11:12 against 11:00)** in the timing check, yet the guide says all is fine; nothing says what to cut first. The skip order starts in Explore, which is earlier in the lesson. Fix: add "cut s-m5 (Almost the same) first" to skip order (it is only needed for Go further).
13. **Slides are half empty** and the text is modest (about 28 px of a 1440 px slide, roughly 20 pt at 1080p). Legible but wasted space; Term 1 template issue.
14. **Mi holds a calculator on the title and end slides** (owner decision 6, keep; Mi never mentions it in this lesson: confirmed).

Workbook 28 min covers Start here (model 14 min) plus answer slides and the choice time. The five-minute flex is a real buffer. No part is over except the slow mini-lesson (finding 12).

Workable for 8 with a calm class; the Explore round 2 (four doubles in a row, 45 s each) has the tutor unable to verify any build. Acceptable; flagged for a tutor trial.

- **Beyond-P1 statement** is present in the strand note, the prep list, the tier note at the workbook slide and the parent page: double 35 and the five-number sum only; "Do not call it MOE-aligned".
- **Joining cold.** Warm-up rebuilds a ten-frame, tens bars and two plates; nothing from earlier lessons is assumed; Lesson safe to miss (a mild overstatement: Lesson 16 uses "double" for the 2 table, as the note says).

- **Term 2 risks checked:** no multiplication wording, no x or division glyphs, no odd/even words (correctly withheld until L14); one wording trap: "double 9 is 10 and 8" (s-opt) is a fine contrasting statement.
- **Maths on slides:** every table and picture count verified (ten-frame counters 7/8/9, tens bars 3 x 10, plates 5 + 3 and 4 + 4).
