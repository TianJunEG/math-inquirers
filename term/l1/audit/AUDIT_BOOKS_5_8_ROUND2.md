### Medium
M1. **Book 8 p28 (L39 G1), bar model misleading, colour and B&W.** "Jenny has 3 times as many stickers as Jason. They have 36 in all." The "36" bracket spans exactly the length of Jenny's bar (3 units). The total is Jason plus Jenny (4 units). A child who reads the bracket as a length reads Jenny = 36. Bars are to scale (1 : 3), so fix the bracket to span both bars or draw the bars end to end. Part (b) also gives away part (a): "Try Jason 9: Jenny has ___" prints the answer to (a).
M2. **Book 8 Quest Log p40 and p41, wrong page sub-headings.** Page 2 says "Gates 3, 4 and 5: keep swimming" but holds gates 3 and 4. Page 3 says "Gates 6 and 7: nearly home" but holds gates 5, 6 and 7. (Book 6 headings are correct.) A child told "gates 6 and 7" will look for the wrong gates.
M3. **Book 7 pattern fills, pp41-45 and kit p47 (L35).** The stripes and dots are still soft and uneven (round 1 item 2 only partly fixed). The dot pattern is clipped by the shape, so tiles that must be "the same" look different: p41 S1 tile 3 (up triangle has about 5 dots, the turned one about 3, so a child may answer "pattern" or "number" instead of "turn"); p41 S2 Picture C triangles show different dot counts (answer No is still right, for another reason); p41 S3 tile D has a visible white seam down the middle of the stripes (D looks different from A and B, the answer is C); p45 E3 tile 2 circles have dots in different places in Picture 1 and 2 (the key says 2 differences). The same soft fill shows in B&W (readable but rough; kit p47 row 3 stripes differ in moiré). Anchor the pattern to the shape or use a clean vector hatch.
M4. **Book 7 p47 (L35 kit), five-sun card.** The 5-sun card (top right) is crowded: the sun rays touch each other and run into the dashed cut line. Colour and B&W.
M5. **Exit-ticket and home clocks too small to use** (round 1 item 13 class, still open).
