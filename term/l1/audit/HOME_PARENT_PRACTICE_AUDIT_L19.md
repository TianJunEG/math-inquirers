- Page source: `index.html` and `practice.js` hold no answers and no external URL; the answers sit in `data/t2l20.json`, which any browser fetches (shared).

## C. Online practice behaviour (summary)

Typed answers: spaces, a unit and a dollar sign are accepted (`$6`); a word (`a dozen`) is refused with a kind "number digits" message that does not use up a try; two numbers are refused; an empty box asks for an answer. A first wrong answer shows a slip line (base numbers only) plus a one-line hint; a second different wrong answer shows the answer with "Let us look at it together." (no score, no timer, no streak); the same wrong answer twice gets "That is the same answer as before." Multi-box marking is per box, ordered and right on the first view of c3 (swapped boxes refused, blanks asked for); on t1 the extra box is ignored (L20-1). Keyboard: Enter checks. Phone width (390 px): one column, buttons large. No external requests, no tracking. Fonts are local.

## D. What a trial must watch

Whether a child can read "1 book costs $[ ]. [ ] books cost $[ ]" on a phone (after L20-1 is fixed); whether children confuse the optional exit ticket with the check; and, after L20-2, a pass of every `fields` item in Terms 1 and 2.
