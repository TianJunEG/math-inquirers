## B. Syllabus fit

Odd and even numbers are P2 (Whole Numbers 1.6) and the lesson says so plainly (strand note, prep, parent page: "beyond Primary 1 on purpose, always with counters"), per the owner decision on stronger arithmetic. Using the last-digit rule on 57, 80, 34, 91 (T2) extends the 1 to 20 pattern to two digits; this is stated as a rule in the mini-lesson but the reason (tens are made of pairs) is never given, so for a child it is a memorised rule (finding 4). No x, division sign or multiplication reading-order wording appears (checked). "Division honesty": halving is explicitly excluded by the guide ("Do not teach division or halving"); pairing is used instead, and nothing implies "÷ 2 = pairs".
