## E. Layout and usability (6-year-old, A4)

Margins 14 mm left and right, top 16.8 mm, footer 5.3 mm (shared template). Pages 1 to 4 have large type and clear instruction lines ("Use your clock. Count in 5s from the 12 to the long hand. Write the time with digits."). Clocks: S1, S2 about 27 to 30 mm (numerals 2 to 2.5 mm: "12" and "1" nearly touch, 10 to 12 touch the ticks), S3 and S4 40 to 45 mm. S4's clock has the two hands almost overlapping at 5:25 (the hour hand is 12 degrees from the minute hand): the short hand is hard to find on any printer. Boxes and the exit ticket clocks are too small (findings 2, 4). Nothing is cut off on pages 1 to 5; the kit page has cut-off headings (finding 6). The kit page is also in `L22_Kit_Pages.pdf`. The card outlines are 3.4 mm apart across and 3.9 mm apart down (kit rule F10: at least 6 mm), so a grown-up cutting along one dashed line risks the next card (finding 6).

## F. Pedagogy

The sequence read, count, draw, choose, order is right and Start here is one idea (count in 5s from the 12) in six forms. Think harder differs honestly (am and pm at 12:30, h and min, the trap, a timetable). Go further needs chunking. The exit ticket matches the objective, but E2 is S5(b) again and E1 is the trap, so the ticket tests recall and the hardest sub-skill rather than the lesson's main idea (count in 5s). The Investigation ("12 steps make an hour") is a good generalisation. Misconceptions targeted: the 2 read as 2 minutes (S2, Talk), 4:5, hour trap, 12:30 pm, 50 against 1 h.
