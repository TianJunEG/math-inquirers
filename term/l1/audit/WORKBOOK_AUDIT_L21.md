## E. Layout and usability (6-year-old, A4)

Margins 14 mm left and right, top 16.8 mm, footer 5.3 mm (shared template). Every page opens with an instruction line; p1 carries the "After question 3 you may choose" banner. Page 1 to 4 type is large and clear; S2 and S4 have good drawing and reading room. Faults are the small boxes and the small exit-ticket clocks (findings 3 and 4). Nothing is cut off on pages 1 to 5. The clock numerals are 2 to 2.5 mm tall on the 27 mm clocks, so 10, 11 and 12 touch the minute ticks and the "12" and "1" nearly merge ("12" reads like "1P"): readable by a teacher, hard for a six-year-old; a lighter minute-tick ring would help. The kit page is on its own sheet and also in `L21_Kit_Pages.pdf`; only half the page is used.

## F. Pedagogy

Progression is clean: read o'clock, read half past, draw, choose, order, then the hour-hand trap, then the mirror. Start here is one idea (long hand first, then the short hand) repeated six ways; Think harder really differs (count in 5s, a false claim); Go further needs a new idea. The exit ticket matches the objective (read, draw, choose o'clock or half past). Misconceptions targeted: swapped hands, "6 means 6 minutes", half past read as the next hour. Weak spot: the kit cannot show the hour hand moving (finding 6), so the key discovery is told, not found.
