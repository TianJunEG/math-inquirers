## Verdict: Ready after minor fixes

No High finding. The maths, the answers, the tier labels, the REC cues (all 11 are 25 words or fewer, each with a child action) and the spare-time menu are sound, and the beyond-Primary-1 statement is present in the guide and the parent page as the owner decided. ## Timing realism: how the adjusted estimate was made

Base is the guide's 74:38. Added (model estimates): extra answer-taking and repeat-for-the-recording on 27 group questions (about +5 min); two number lines 135 s -> about 4 min (+1:45); eight-child greeting (+0:20); sneak-peek hold-up reading (+0:45); exit hold-up and marking (+1:30); explore builds checked on camera (+0:30); setting up and resetting the kit for round 2 (+0:30); late joiners and one mute or camera problem (+2:00). Total about 12, giving 86 to 87. The slow-class scenario uses the guide's own factors (+10 percent speech, +20 percent child and slide time) on that base: about 97 to 100. The lesson has about 11 min of slack inside parts and 4:22 in Flex (15 min in all), which is the reason it still lands near 90 in the base case; a tutor who runs each part to its budget instead of its script will finish on time, one who follows the script minutes will end early.

## What is sound (checked and found fine)

- Maths: every guide answer, slide answer, Think harder, Go further, Investigation, exit and home-practice answer recomputed; G2 is 5 jumps (four 20s and one 10), G3 is 55, 45, 65 then a hop of 4, the optional slide is 95 after 9 jumps. Number-line pictures agree with the text (the "Jump, then hop: take away" slide draws -20 then -5 correctly).
- Explore keeps every example inside a ten (4 + 5 = 9, 1 + 8 = 9), and the guide says so; kit counts (9 bars, 20 squares) cover every build in the lesson.
- Answers are hidden until a separate reveal slide for the worksheet pages, the exit ticket and the sneak-peek (slides s-ws1ans, s-ws2ans, s-exitans, s-sneakans).
- Recording cues: 11 cues of 11 to 25 words, each with a clear child action and chapter time. The SAY lines are natural spoken English, one idea at a time (longest about 45 words).
- Safeguarding: no private chat, no breakout rooms, "volunteers first, a pass is always fine", no name with a wrong answer, no tallies, the look-back is private, a movement-break safety bullet and a seated alternative are present, nothing sharp, no purchase.
- Beyond-Primary-1 statement: present in the strand note and on the parent page; no MOE, GEP or SASMO claim anywhere in the guide, deck or parent page.
- Mi: 4 slides (title, one Remember, Look-back, goodbye), one short friendly line each, never about answers.
- Joining cold: the warm-up re-teaches counting in tens, tens and ones with bars and squares, and "ten and some more"; the guide states that Lesson 6 is among the hardest to miss and what a missed-lesson child should do.
- Misconceptions: the guide anticipates adding the tens to the ones digit, hopping the wrong way and 20 read as 2 ones, each with an IF STUCK or common-mistake line.
