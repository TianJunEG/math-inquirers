## What works

The order is right: put the days in a row (the day cards), what comes after Sunday, jump from tomorrow, a month page and a week, then days later, the day before, a week on the page and a fortnight, then the Quest, then the workbook. The warm-up (count in 7s, the first to the tenth, the day cards in order) is exactly what the lesson needs, so a child who joins cold follows. Explore is kit-led with the answer slides after each round; question slides carry no answers (`s-explore3` highlights the 5th and asks; `s-explore3-ans` adds the 12th). The guide says "we never count today" and the IF STUCK lines are concrete. The Quest is handled with care: it follows the L20 model (minutes 35 to 55 straight after the break; recording paused for the whole Quest and restarted for the workbook; no clock or timer shown, the tutor uses a watch out of sight; Quest Log opened only when told; paper flat, never held up; "I will not say any answers"; gates open for everyone; skip is allowed; no collection; photos only to the business WhatsApp; the line goes to the parent only; make-up before watching the recording; never alone with one child on a call). The safeguarding block and the privacy checklist in the tutor notes are consistent with `privacy.html` (90 days after the program ends). The recording and greeting wording is word for word that of Terms 1 and 2 and of L24 to L29. The beyond-P1 statement (days of the week not a listed outcome; "two days after the day before yesterday", a fortnight, every Wednesday and Hundred Days Later beyond on purpose) is in the strand note, the "Plain statement" NOTE, `beyond` and the "Before the lesson" box and is honest; no MOE-aligned claim.

## Timing and tutor load

Script 67:54 of 90, slow class 80:07, the Quest 16:38 of 20:00 (slow 19:31, so about 30 seconds of slack in a slow class where the Quest is never shortened), work 18:50 of 25:00 (slow 22:25), last 4 minutes untouched. One tutor can run it (main room, no breakout rooms). Realism points:

- **Workbook page and question numbering (finding 1).** The script and slides call page 1 "questions 1 to 3" and page 2 "questions 4 to 6", but the printed workbook has S1 to S4 on page 1 and S5 and S6 on page 2. The READ lines for "Page 2. Question 4: today is Saturday ..." (S4) are therefore read as page 2 while S4 is on page 1; `s-ws1` shows S1 to S3 and `s-ws2` shows S4 to S6 (as "Workbook page 1: Start here" and "Workbook page 2: Start here"); the answer slides follow the same split; the page 1 box says "After question 3 you may choose" on a page that has four questions. The slots are 6:40 for page 1 (four questions, about 11.5 minutes of estimated work) and 7:40 for page 2 (two questions, 6.5 minutes), so the slot for page 1 is badly short and page 2's is long.
- **Quest pace.** The quiet-working slots are 150 s for gates 1 and 2 (four answers, a clock to 5 minutes included), 190 s for gates 3 and 4 (six answers), 180 s for gates 5 to 7 (five answers) and 110 s for gate 8 and the cave (three answers). That is about 35 seconds an answer for children aged 6 to 8, including a clock reading, counting four coins and a grid; the notes say a middle child needs about 17 minutes, which the 16:38 script just covers, and a slow class has no slack (finding 4). The notes allow a child to finish in the first minutes of the workbook, which is the right remedy; the skip order forbids shortening the Quest.
- **Tutor cannot see the Quest.** The tutor scans the gallery and "notices how they start" but, with the recording paused and no papers shown, relies on the photos afterwards for the private notes; the notes say to write from "photos and what you saw on camera": realistic.
- **Remember (4:36 of 6:00)** has two "your turn" items (5, 12, 19; the day cards in a row) whose answers are stated; the NOTE lists "24; third; Monday": complete.

## Tutor scripts and the term checklist

- **Recording and camera wording** matches the corrected wording: "Greet each child by first name as they arrive. Then start the recording. While it runs, avoid names." then the notice "the recording has me, the shared screen and any voice that is unmuted. You may mute and keep your camera off." That agrees with `privacy.html`. The REC cue "If you are watching the recording: hello!" comes before the DO line that starts the recording and the notice is said a little over a minute in (carried over from Term 2; finding 10).
- **Recording pause for the Quest.** The DO line "Pause the recording now" is correct and matches L20. The announcement `rec_pause_say` ("the recording pauses now for Mi's Challenge Quest, and starts again for the workbook") appears only in the recording-cues section of the guide, not as a SAY line before the pause in the lesson script (the children and a watching family are told "the recording has me, the shared screen ..." but not that the Quest is not recorded until the Quest slide); add it (Low, finding 7).
- **Paper hold-ups:** none in the script; the Quest says "never held up". Guide section 9 still lists "papers to the camera 30 s" in the assumptions (finding 10).
- **"I am still stuck":** a mark on the look-back page, photographed by the grown-up to the business WhatsApp; the number only in the notes: correct. The tutor "helps in the open session at the start of next term": a long wait for a stuck child (Low).
- **Think harder reachable, Start here approachable:** yes (the choice slide, the page 1 box, "Not finished? Pages 3 to 5 are for home too").
- **Rounding and unfamiliar words:** "fortnight", "days later", "date", "the day before" are defined in Explore and the Mini-lesson; "round" is never used.
- **Multiplication reading order, division, x glyphs:** none printed; "14 times 7" is spoken in the key's G1 solution.
- **Odd and even:** the cave solution says "9 is left, so no" (no use of the words).
- **Hand-off chain:** from L29 true; to L31 cannot be checked (`l31.json` does not exist), but the text matches preflight section 5 (Book 7, the fold-shapes page, 10 shapes, a coloured pencil); **from L20 to L21 still half fixed** (finding 8).
- **Slow class:** no part OVER; the Quest has about 30 seconds of slack in a slow class.
- **Beyond-P1 statement:** present and honest in the guide; the parent-facing Quest note says something different and false (finding 6).
- **Misconceptions anticipated:** counting today as day 1, Sunday not wrapping, a week as 8 days, a fortnight; tutor responses are concrete (start from tomorrow; cards in a ring; count 7 days one at a time).
