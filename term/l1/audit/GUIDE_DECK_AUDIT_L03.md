## Findings

### Medium

**M18. The kit has 9 tens bars; a task in the script needs up to 17.**
Location: guide Explore 16:49-18:52 ("Take one of your pairs and build both numbers ... Which pile has more?"); slide s-explore2 ("Build both of your numbers with bars and squares"); kit page 1 in the workbook ("Tens bars: cut out all 9 ... Ones squares: cut out all 20").
Evidence: a pair such as 7 and 8 needs 78 and 87 = 15 bars; 6 and 5 needs 11; 7 and 3 needs 10. Only pairs with digit sum at most 9 (3 and 5: 8 bars; 6 and 2: 8; 4 and 5: 9) can be built as two piles. The slide's own examples (35 and 53 = 8 bars) fit, but children choose their own pairs ("Pick any two cards"). Also the sneak-peek reveal slide shows 84 (8 bars) and 48 (4 bars) together = 12 bars, fine on a slide, not buildable.
Fix: say "choose two cards that add up to 9 or less" for the build round, or build one number, record it, then rebuild; or print 18 bars.

**M19. Digit-card fallback gives one set, the lesson needs two.**
Location: guide "Child materials" ("cut out the two sets of 0 to 9 from the kit page, or write the digits 0 to 9 on ten small pieces of scrap paper"); Before the lesson ("ten scraps of paper with 0 to 9 written on them").
Evidence: the exercises that use two copies of a digit: spare-time and optional "33", G2b ("a card used twice, like 11"), Investigation "4 and 4", brain break "40 and 40" (spoken, not built). The scrap-paper option provides only one of each digit, so those cannot be built. The 20-second scrap writing at 1:30 (Timing realism) compounds it.
Fix: fallback = "write 0 to 9 twice on 20 scraps (a grown-up helps)" and move this to the parent page a week before, not to the Opening.

**M20. Lesson 2's closing and parent page tell families Lesson 3 needs only a pencil and paper.**
Location: L02 guide 85:25, L02 slide s-end, L02 parent page "Lesson 3: Digit Cards. Your child needs only a pencil and paper. Please keep the number cards".
Evidence: this guide's Before the lesson requires both kit pages printed and cut out, or scraps. A family following the L2 message arrives without cards.
Fix: same wording fix as L1/L2 (common M7).

**M21. Talk time question has two cases behind one Yes/No.**
Location: guide 70:32 READ "Is this always right? ... Try the cards 3 and 8. Then try 0 and 5. Circle Yes or No. I know because ___"; slide 25 (s-talk); model answer.
Evidence: for 3 and 8 the claim is right, for 0 and 5 it is not; one Yes/No hides a two-part question (WORDING_GUIDE rule 4 and 18 want parts a and b). The model answer starts "For 3 and 8 she is right": there is no "she" in the question or slide (the claim has no speaker).
Fix: (a) "Try 3 and 8. Does the smallest card on the left make the smallest number? Yes or No." (b) the same for 0 and 5; or keep one question "Always true? Yes or No" and delete "she". The mini-lesson (slide s-m5 at 34:28) already taught the 0 case, so the Talk answer is largely recall.
