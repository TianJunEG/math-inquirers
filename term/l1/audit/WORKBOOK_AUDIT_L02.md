Auditor: independent desk review by a model that did not write the lesson. Sources: `out/l02/L02_Workbook.pdf` (7 pages) and `L02_Workbook_Answer_Key.pdf` (3 pages), rendered with pdftoppm, every page viewed; `lessons/l02.json` (hints, materials, notes) read for context. Instruments: gifted-style rubric (12 criteria x 0-2, max 24, `reaudit_p1.md` instrument A) on workbook evidence; strict tier compliance per printed item. Student-journey instrument not used (slides and scripts are outside this brief).

## Verdict: Ready after minor fixes. Rubric 17/24. Tier compliance 14/15 (93%). 0 High, 5 Medium, 7 Low.
Do not use the exit ticket as printed: E2 has two defensible answers.

## (A) Maths correctness
Independent answers: S1 10, 12; S2 29; 39, 41; 70; S3 34/36, 59/61, 78/80; S4 15, 50, 24; S5 twenty, forty; S6 30, 50, 80; S7 20/30, 60/70 (see below); S8 No, 60; T1 50, 90; T2 6 (29-34); T3 56 (unique; clue 2 is redundant but consistent); T4 97, 98, 99, 100, 4 hops; G1 (a) 10, 20, jump 10 (b) 15, 20, 25, jump 5 (c) 53, 56, 59, jump 3; G2 meet on 40 (gap 24, 12 each); G3 open; E1 73; E2 70/80 (see below); E3 100. All agree with the key. **Ambiguity (Medium): S7 "23 is between [] and []", "68 is between [] and []", and E2 "76 is between [] and []" have two defensible answers.** The stem never says "tens"; 22 and 24 (or 75 and 77) are correct readings and are exactly what S3 (one before, one after) trains. Only the hint (tutor-side) says tens. E2 is the exit ticket, so a correct child can be marked wrong. T4 picture is fine (94, 96, 98, 100 with unlabelled ticks between).

## (B) Age and syllabus fit
Number words to 100 and counting to 100 are P1 (1.1-1.3 numerals and words). Number line as a scale is a sensible P1 representation. "Which ten is nearer" is the idea behind rounding (P4, syllabus p. 37); it appears only at T1, T3 and the Investigation, never at Start here and not in the exit ticket: correctly placed. Equal-jump lines in 3s (G1c) go beyond P1 skip counting (2s, 5s, 10s); acceptable at Go further. "Halfway" is not named in the syllabus but is informal and fine at Think harder.

## (D) Wording
Commands are clear and placed first. Problems: S7/E2 omit "tens" (finding 1). T3 chains three clues and "The number before it is halfway between 50 and 60" is hard grammar for a 6-year-old (31 words); T4 "hops 1 number at a time to 100" is fine. S5 "Write each number in words. Word bank: ..." is school-paper style. S4 and S2 use "Fill in" and "Write" consistently. "Circle Yes or No" has no printed options (S8a). The page 3 instruction reads "count the hops. Then write how you know" but no item has a line to write on. G2 gives no picture; "hop towards each other ... at the same time" is long (27 words) but acceptable for G.

## (E) Layout and usability
Good: large boxes (36 px) for single answers, number line pictures clear, nothing cut off, Mi only on page 1, each page starts with an instruction. Problems: the strand line in the header is too long and wraps to two lines on every page, crowding the tier pill (page 1 worst); page 3 has no writing lines although it asks for explanations; the Investigation has only a dividing dotted line, no writing area; page 1 about 80% full. Kit: two pages (ones cards 0-10 in rounded rectangles, tens cards 0-100 in circles). Circles are harder to cut than rectangles, the "100" card touches its circle edge, and neighbouring dashed circles touch; the T pages say "your number line" but the workbook prints none (the child must lay out the tens cards; the plan lists a printed number line kit type).

## (F) Pedagogy
Progression is good (count on, before/after, words, tens line, then nearest ten). It targets real misconceptions (510 after 59; 308 for 38 + 1; fifteen vs fifty). The Investigation "Halfway" is a strong low-floor, high-ceiling task. Weaknesses: T4 repeats Start here; the exit ticket (E1 words, E2 between, E3 frog to 100) matches the objective "find a number on a line" but E2 is ambiguous; no exit item tests nearer ten (acceptable). Timing: 19.0 minutes Start here is heavy.

## (G) Safeguarding and inclusion
No labels or ranking; look-back has a pass option. Siti is the mistaken child in S8 (also L01 S6): rotate. A small toy or coin as the frog is fine; no private chat in workbook text.

## (H) Consistency
Same style as L01 and L03. Differences: header wraps (L01 and L03 do not); circles on the kit (others rectangles); the T instruction line refers to a number line the other lessons never need.

## Instrument A (0-2)
