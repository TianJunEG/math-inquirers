## Timing realism

- Part budgets sum to 90: 4+6+4+14+10+4+28+6+6+3+5. Script need 74:20, slow class 88:18. Both within the AUTHORING limits (86 and 90). The 5-minute flex part is a real buffer (script fills 0:38 of it).
- Remember 4:36 of 6:00 is plausible: 5 + 4 "fingers/ten and more" questions at 12 s each with 6-8 year olds is realistic; the clap-and-whisper count to 100 and back in 45 s is realistic only if children are not chorusing over each other (M2).
- Opening is 4:00 and tight but fits: three slides, consent statement, shopkeeper ask with a 25 s wait.
- Explore 11:13 of 14:00: round 2 allows 35-45 s per number for children to build with kit pieces and the tutor to look at up to 8 builds. The tutor can glance, not check (see Tutor load).
- Workbook 26:37 of 28:00 as scripted. The guide's own "time for a middle child" figures: page 1 = 1.5+3+3 = 7.5 min against a 9:00 slot; page 2 = 2.5+2.5+2+3 = 10 min against a 9:00 slot. So a middle child finishes page 1 with 1.5 minutes to spare and page 2 slightly over; a slower child (the guide itself says 1.7 times) needs about 30 minutes for Start here against 18. See M8.
- Look-back 100 s for a five-row tick table (hard / helped / next time / still stuck / pass) plus "write or draw one thing" is short for 6 year olds (Low, L8).
- Slide timer text does not always match the script: page 1 slide says "Timer: 8 minutes", script allots 9:00 (Low, L6).

## Findings

Severity: High = wrong maths, unsafe or unusable; Medium = misleading, ambiguous, impractical or inconsistent; Low = polish.

### Medium

**M1. "Only you see it" is not true on a video call; contradicts the guide's own rule.**
Location: guide section 3, HOLD UP lines at 13:02 and 79:11, Explore round 2 (19:56 to 22:38), slide notes for s-sneak, s-explore2, s-exit; safeguarding block ("no board held up in front of the group").
Evidence: the exit line reads "holds their ticket to the camera at the same moment so only you see it". In gallery view every child sees every held-up paper and build. The same guide says "Never ask a child to show a wrong answer to the group" and "no boards held up in front of the group".
Fix: say plainly in the script "everyone can see your paper; we look only at our own"; or hold up in two groups of four; or use a spotlight/pin so non-speakers are off-screen; and delete "so only you see it". Keep the "never comment on another child's paper" rule.

**M2. No audio protocol anywhere (muted by default, how to answer).**
Location: Remember 4:46 (count in tens "together, clapping ... whispering, shouting on 50"), 36:18 ("Count together"), 24:07 "wait about 45 s for answers" (all lessons).
Evidence: searched all three guides for mute, unmute, microphone: no hits. Eight unmuted children chorusing over a video call arrive at different moments and the tutor hears noise; "shouting on 50" invites it.
Fix: add one standing rule to the Opening (and to Before the lesson): children are muted; they speak when invited or put a thumb up; chorus lines are said "to your own room" with the tutor modelling; "shout" becomes "say it big with your hands".

**M3. "Inquiry journal" and "tutor tracker" do not exist.**
Location: guide 80:11 "I will stamp your inquiry journal"; note after 80:11 "Mark S, D or H in the tutor tracker"; slide 27 speaker note "Stamp the inquiry journal"; flex note "(stamps, the look-back)".
Evidence: the workbook text has no journal or stamp (searched for "journal", "stamp"); `AUDIT_L01.md` itself says there is "no term cover or journal page yet"; no tracker template is in the repository outputs. The tutor is also expected to mark S/D/H for up to 8 children during a 25-second hold-up (79:11 to 79:36), which is not feasible.
Fix: delete the stamp sentence or create the journal page; supply a one-page tracker; mark S/D/H afterwards from the recording or from parent photos, not live.

**M4. Recording statement may overclaim; first names and children's voices are recorded.**
Location: guide Opening 0:36 ("We record me and the screen only"), Before the lesson ("Check the recording settings exclude children's video"), slide 3 note ("names are kept off the screen"), DO line "Greet each child by first name", note "Repeat a child's answer in your own words so the recording picks it up".
Evidence: only video is excluded. A platform recording normally includes the audio of any child who unmutes (answers, volunteers) and the tutor says first names aloud on the recording. The note "repeat so the recording picks it up" hints that children's audio is not captured, but no setting or check is given. The parent page says only "Every lesson is recorded; your tutor will share a private link with enrolled families": no retention, no who-sees, no voice statement.
Fix: owner to confirm what the platform captures. Then (a) say "the recording includes sound", (b) greet by first name before pressing record or avoid names while recording, (c) say who can watch it and for how long on the parent page.

**M5. "I am still stuck" has no working route.**
Location: guide Safeguarding block, 82:00 look-back note, slide 29 note.
Evidence: "Never collected ... or read out", yet "A child who ticks 'I am still stuck' gets a check-in in the open session next lesson". The tutor never sees the page, so cannot know who ticked. "A quick visit to the shared room" refers to a shared room that does not exist (this lesson has no breakout rooms). "Hand signal" is not defined.
Fix: define one route, for example the grown-up says "my child is stuck on X" in the parent chat/WhatsApp (business number only), or the child holds up a card "?" at the start of the next lesson; delete "shared room".

**M6. Exit ticket questions are not read aloud and are incomplete on the slide.**
Location: guide 76:33 SAY (only "Three questions ... write on page 5"); deck slide 27 (s-exit).
Evidence: slide shows "E1 How many sticks?" with a picture about 45 px high at 1080p, "E2 Fill in the blanks." (the number 37 is not on the slide), "E3 Write the number: 7 tens and 0 ones." AUTHORING item 7 requires questions read aloud. Compare L3's exit slide, which prints every stem in full.
Fix: add READ lines "E1 how many sticks ... E2 thirty-seven equals how many tens and ones ... E3 write seven tens and no ones" and print "37 =" on the slide; enlarge the E1 picture.

**M7. What the family must prepare for the next lesson contradicts the next lesson.**
Location: guide 85:25 ("Next lesson ... You need only a pencil and paper"), slide 30 (s-end), parent page "Lesson 2 ... pencil and paper only".
Evidence: Lesson 2 needs number cards cut from the kit pages (or 11 paper squares) and a toy frog (L2 guide Before the lesson; L2 parent page "Things to have ready").
Fix: change L1's goodbye, slide and parent line to "pencil, paper, number cards cut out, a small toy".

**M8. Start here fills the workbook slot; Think harder and Go further are not reachable in class.**
Location: guide timing table; 63:25 choice slide (5:00); `s-choice`.
Evidence: see Timing realism: page 1 needs 7.5 of 9, page 2 10 of 9, choice time 5 minutes, Think harder items are 3-5 minutes each (T3, T4 5 minutes, G items 5-6). The choice slide invites "Think harder, Go further or the Investigation" as if it could be done.
Fix: say in the guide and on the slide "if you finish early" and treat pages 3-5 as home work; or reduce Start here by one question; or move the choice time to the flex part.

**M9. Tutor load and tooling: Term 1 is not in the tutor console; speaker notes are not visible.**
Location: `tutor/README.md` (Levels 1 to 6 holiday build only; `build_data.py` has no Term 1 input); deck HTML `aside{display:none}`; the deck PDF has no notes.
Evidence: the tutor must run the 90 minutes from a 19-page A4 PDF on a second device, with the slide deck shared, a clock, up to 8 cameras, 11 recording cues, chapter markers (guide section 8 "Add a chapter marker at each time"; most platforms cannot add chapters live), hold-ups and answer reading. One tutor for 8 is possible only with very disciplined use of the guide.
Fix: wire `term/l1` lessons into the console (or export a one-page run sheet per lesson); say whether a co-host is required; make chapter markers a post-processing step.

**M10. Talk time repeats the mini-lesson and puts a wrong claim on the slide.**
Location: guide 33:00 ASK ("Which has more sticks, 26 or 62?") against Talk 70:32 ("Are 26 and 62 the same number?"); slide 25 (s-talk) text "26 and 62 are the same, because they use the same digits."
Evidence: slide 16 (s-m5) already answers the same question with pictures. WORDING_GUIDE rule 19 says do not state a child's wrong reasoning in the stem. The script ASK also reads "Two six and six two use the same digits", which is confusing when spoken aloud.
Fix: use a new talk prompt (for example "Is 40 the same as 4?" or the Investigation question); say "twenty-six and sixty-two"; drop the printed wrong claim or label it as "Ravi says".

**M11. Kit and pace of the second explore round (hold-up of builds).**
Location: guide 19:42-22:47.
Evidence: five hold-up builds in under 4 minutes (17, 30, 42, 58, plus the "Ravi is right?" check) where each build is held to a face camera; many children's cameras show faces, not the table. No camera set-up instruction (tilt screen, tablet at the side, hold things up) is in the guide or the parent page.
Fix: add a camera set-up line to Before the lesson and the parent page ("camera showing your paper and your hands"), and shorten to three builds.
