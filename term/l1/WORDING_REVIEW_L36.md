# Wording review: Lesson 36 (Count Everything)

Every child-facing sentence was read against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1 to 25 and the Primary 1 paper patterns.

- Commands: Count; Fill in the missing numbers; Write the number. Same command for the same job (rules 2, 14).
- Fixed question forms: 'There are [] squares.'; 'There are [] marbles in all.'; 'There are [] lines.'; 'There are [] handshakes in all.'; 'There are [] outfits in all.'; 'The missing pair is ___.' Numbers are digits; a box comes before the unit (rules 15, 16).
- Words taught in Explore and the Mini-lesson: system, handshake, list, repeat. 'A with B is the same as B with A' is said every time.
- Wrong working belongs to 'a child in another class' (Talk, spot the repeat). Cast names (Ravi, Ali, Siti, Mei, Ben, Kumar) appear only in a sticker table story with correct data.
- No teaching sentence inside a question (rule 24). T1 says 'A has already shaken hands with B, C and D' as a fact that scaffolds the count, not a method.
- Rule 25: handshakes, every pair, squares of every size and routes in two stages are Think harder and Go further; Start here is rows, groups, lines and a short list.
- Changed during review: the T4 table was removed (the stickers are in the sentence); T2 has no picture, the hint asks the child to draw a table.
- Open: no Primary 1 paper uses these topics; wording is unverified.
