# Wording review: Lesson 40 (Inquirers' Showcase)

Every child-facing sentence (slides, workbook, Quest Log, exit ticket, home sheet, online practice, parent pages) was read aloud against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1 to 25 and `docs/books/p1_school_paper_patterns.md`.

- Commands: Choose ...; Tick one idea in each row; Write its number in the brackets; Circle Right or Wrong; Circle Yes or No; Circle the tool that fits best; Fill in the blanks. Quest gates use Write, Circle, and the same part labels (a), (b), (c).
- Fixed forms: 'How many stickers does Ravi have?', 'The right answer is [] stickers.', 'Is the puzzle right? Circle Yes or No.'; money with the cent sign (5¢, 10¢); no mixed dollars and cents.
- Quest wording passes the ban list for family pages (no test, score, mark, rank, timer, out of). 'Nobody is marked' was replaced by 'Your paper is not collected' on the sneak-peek and workbook hint.
- Wrong working is always 'a child in another class' (S4, G2, E3, the friend's puzzle in the Mini-lesson and Quest gate 8); cast names (Ali, Mei, Siti, Ravi, Ben, Jamie, Lily, Wei) appear only in stories.
- Rule 24: the frame 'My favourite idea is ___ . It works like this: ___ . Here is an example: ___ .' is a speaking frame, not a method inside a question. 'Make a ten first' (S5) is the lesson's own idea and the hint carries the method.
- Reading load: the longest child sentences are the Quest gates 7 and 8 and the cave (read aloud by the tutor); each is split into short sentences. Numbers are digits; ordinals use the digit.
- Open items say 'Many answers are right.' (rule 6). No item asks 'say why' without a frame.
- Changed during review: the child idea was shortened; the Quest Log gate 1 rectangle is described in words; puzzle B in Explore was reworded so the count of answers is exactly 9 (even numbers 2 to 18).
- Open: no Primary 1 paper asks for puzzle writing, so those wordings are unverified.
