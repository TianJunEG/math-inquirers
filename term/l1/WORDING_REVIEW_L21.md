# Wording review, Lesson 21 (What Time Is It?)

Every child-facing sentence (sneak-peek, workbook S1 to S6, T1 to T3, G1 to G3, I1, exit E1 to E3, home H1 to H5, slides, talk, look-back, online practice, parent page) was read aloud against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1 to 25 and `docs/books/p1_school_paper_patterns.md` (which overrides the pre-flight on time). 154 answer checks and 140 practice variants pass.

## Changes made during review (old -> new, reason)
1. "Write the time shown on each clock." -> "Write the time for each clock." The old wording matched a quoted phrase in a reference file (7-word overlap check); the new one is also shorter (rule 1). One fixed command for the one job, used in S1, T1, E1 and the practice item (rule 2).
2. Quarter words: the pre-flight put "quarter past" and "quarter to" at Start here; the P1 papers use only o'clock and half past, so Start here has none (verify script asserts it). Every time on a Start here page is a digit time with minutes 00 or 30.
3. Times are written 7:30 with a colon; "am" and "pm" do not appear in this lesson (they start in Lesson 22, without full stops).
4. S3 "The bus leaves at half past 7. Circle the correct clock." The picture labels are (1) (2) (3) and the printed choices are Clock (1), (2), (3): options numbered, not A, B, C (rule 23). Distractors follow the school pattern: same hour in the other form and a different hour with the same long hand.
5. S4 and T-level choice questions use "Look at the clock. Choose the correct answer. Write its number in the bracket." with options (1) to (4) and a bracket at the right (rule 23).
6. S5 asks "Who wakes up first?" and "... last?" as parts (a) and (b), answer frames "___ wakes up first." (rules 16 and 18). Names Ben, Mei and Ali are only the people on the clocks; nobody is the error-spotter.
7. T2 and Talk: the wrong claim comes from "A child says" (rule 19 and decision D18), the claim is stated, the wrong reasoning is not.
8. G1: "A mirror swaps left and right." is a fact about the mirror, not a method (rule 24); the method (two faces, swap the short hand) is in the hint only.
9. Home sheet: "The long hand points to the 6" became "The long hand is on the 6" after a 7-word overlap with a reference file; same wording in the practice items.
10. Mi's lines contain no numbers or answers. "challenge", "core", "stretch" appear on no child or parent page.
11. Parent page: the words list defines short hand, long hand, o'clock, half past and the quarter words; a "do not teach clockwise as a rule" line is in when-stuck.

## Checked and left as is
- Numbers are numerals in sentences ("4 o'clock", "7:30"); number words appear only in tutor SAY and READ lines, which are spoken.
- Fixed question words: "What is the real time?", "Who wakes up first?", "Is the child correct? Circle Yes or No." Tick True or False tables use full sentences.
- Singapore spelling and things: bus, bathroom, mirror; no brands.
- Quarter past and quarter to are unverified against P1 papers: kept at Think harder only and always paired with digits.
