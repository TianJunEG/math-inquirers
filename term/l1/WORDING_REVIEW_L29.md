# Wording review: Lesson 29 (Spot the Mistake)

Every child-facing sentence was read against WORDING_GUIDE rules 1 to 25 and the Primary 1 paper patterns.

- Commands: Circle Yes or No; Check; Which step is wrong?; The right answer is; Tick True or False; Write a sum. Same commands every time (rules 2, 14).
- Wrong working is always 'a child' or 'a child in another class'; cast names (Mei, Aisha, Ravi, Lily) only appear in stories, never as the one who made the mistake (rule 4, D18).
- Time: o'clock and half past only, digits (2:30, 3:30, 7:30, 8:00). '7:60' appears only as a child's impossible answer in Think harder. No quarter words.
- Money: whole dollars ($9, $4, $13), no cents, no decimals, answer blank '$___'.
- Numbers as digits in stories; a blank takes a unit or a box; multi-part questions use (a), (b), (c) (rules 15 to 18).
- Rule 24: stems contain no method teaching. The check is a fill-in part ('Check: 24 + 17 = []'), not a method order. Hints and the tutor script carry the teaching.
- Rule 25: 'about' and 'near' appear only at Go further (G1); never 'round' or 'estimate' on a child page.
- Changed during review: removed a worked line from S1's picture that showed the answer; the look-back options were rewritten for this lesson; the parent page was shortened to one page.
- Open: no Primary 1 paper uses 'check by adding back' wording; marked unverified.
