## Changes made during review (old -> new, reason)
1. Money: whole dollars as $12, "$____" blanks with the sign first, dollar sign inside each number-sentence box (rule 17 and the paper pattern); no decimals; "fifty-dollar note" and "ten-dollar notes" in words in stems (hyphenated, as in the papers); there is no $20 note, so a $20 payment is "gives the cashier $20".
2. S1: "Ben has exactly $10. He buys two things. Look at the four tags." then the answer frame "The two tags are $____ and $____." (the paper form "which 2 items did he buy"); the method (start with the cheapest and ask what partner it needs) is in the hint, never in the question (rule 24).
3. S4: Yes or No and the amount needed are two parts (a), (b), not one question with two answers (rule 18); S3 uses "left" and S5 uses a number sentence with boxes then "altogether".
4. S6: the table has one instruction ("Fill in the blanks in the table."); column heads carry the unit, "Had ($)", so no "$" sits next to a box.
5. T1: "Exactly $20" with a stem of three short sentences and the table by number of books; "He may buy only pens." removes the hidden question whether zero books is allowed (rule 4). X is explained in the part line.
6. T2: the wrong list belongs to "a child in another class" (D18); "Is the list complete? Circle Yes or No." is its own part.
7. T4: True or False table; each statement is one short sentence with numerals.
8. G1: "Any order of the ways is fine." added so the key accepts either order (known failure pattern 7); G2 asks (a) stickers and (b) pads instead of one two-answer question.
9. H1 and the online c5 use "she"/"Aisha" so they do not repeat a Primary 1 paper phrase (7-word check).
