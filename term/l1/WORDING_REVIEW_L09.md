# Wording review, Lesson 9 (Draw and Measure)

Reviewed: workbook, key, exit ticket, investigation, look-back, slides, script lines read to children, home practice, parent page, online practice items and templates, spare-time menu. Maths and answers are unchanged (answer checks untouched, 175 pass, 230 practice variants pass). Build: 0 FAIL; workbook 5 pages (fill 88, 93, 100, 89, 96); home sheet 2 pages; parent page 1 page.

## Changes (old -> new, reason)

Workbook and exit
- S1 "Draw a line 7 cm long in the box. Put the 0 mark of your ruler at the start of the line." -> "In the box, draw a line 7 cm long. Start at the 0 mark." (shorter, one command; the sentence was quoted in the school-paper doc, so it also cleared the copy check)
- S1 frame "My line starts at ..." -> "The line starts at the ___ mark and ends at the ___ mark." (neutral, ends with the unit noun)
- S2b "It is ___ cm longer than the short line." -> "... than the shorter line." (grammar: comparing two)
- S3 "Measure your pencil. First write your guess. Then measure to the nearest cm." -> "Measure the length of your pencil. Write your guess first. Then measure to the nearest cm." (plain command order)
- S4c "Ali's pencil is ___ cm longer." -> "... cm longer than Mei's." (longer than what? was ambiguous)
- S5 "...draw a line that is 3 cm longer." -> "...draw a line 3 cm longer than the first line." (same reason)
- S6 "Mei wants a line 8 cm long. She puts the 0 mark at the start and draws to the 7 mark. She says: my line is 8 cm. Is she right?" with part "Yes or no? ___" -> "Mei's line goes from the 0 mark to the 7 mark. Mei says it is 8 cm long." with (a) "Is Mei correct? Circle Yes or No." (b) unchanged (rule 19: circle, not write; one clear mistake; no hidden second question)
- S6 hint "Where does her line end on the ruler? Look at the mark. What mark should it end at?" -> "Where does her line start and end on the ruler? Read the end mark." ; mistake "because she wanted 8" -> "because Mei said 8" (matches the new stem)
- T1 "Mei draws a path with 3 straight pieces: 5 cm, 3 cm and 4 cm. Draw the path in the box with your ruler. How long is the whole path?" -> "Use your ruler to draw a path with 3 straight pieces: 5 cm, 3 cm and 4 cm." (the part "The whole path is ___ cm long." asks the question; stem is shorter, which also kept page 3 on one page)
- T2 "A line starts at the 2 mark of a ruler and ends at the 9 mark. A second line starts at the 3 mark and is 6 cm long." -> "Line A starts at the 2 mark and ends at the 9 mark. Line B starts at the 3 mark and is 6 cm long." with parts "Line A is ___ cm long." / "Line B ends at the ___ mark." ("first/second line" was easy to mix up; hint and mistakes updated)
- T4 "To the nearest cm: Ali's tip is ..." with one three-blank part -> stem "Both pencils start at 0. Ali's tip is between 16 and 17, closer to 16. Mei's tip is between 18 and 19, closer to 19." with (a) "To the nearest cm, Ali's pencil is ___ cm and Mei's pencil is ___ cm long." (b) "Mei's pencil is ___ cm longer than Ali's." (three answers in one line with no units, two jobs; now a/b with units)
- G1 "Mei cuts it into three pieces." -> "Mei cuts all of it into 3 pieces: A, B and C." and a new frame "Piece C is ___ cm long." (a blank with a unit; "all of it" removes the doubt that some ribbon is left over)
- G3 "Every piece must be a different whole number of cm long. The whole path must be exactly 20 cm. Write the 3 lengths." -> "The pieces are all different lengths, in whole cm. The whole path is exactly 20 cm long. Many answers are right." (open question now says so; the 3 lengths are asked by the frame)
- I1 first step "Put the 2 mark of your ruler at its start. Which mark is at its end?" -> "Start it at the 2 mark of your ruler. Which mark does it end at?" ; "Try 3 start marks. Is the length always the same? Is there a rule?" -> "Try 3 more start marks. Each time, draw a line 6 cm long and write where it ends. What do you notice? Start with: I notice ..." (the old text asked if a length you had just fixed at 6 cm stays 6 cm; the new text says what to do and gives a sentence frame)
- E1 "Draw a line 6 cm long on your spare paper. Then write where it starts and ends on your ruler." -> "... Start it at the 0 mark of your ruler. Then fill in the marks." (two answers were possible: start at 2, end at 8)
- E2 "Siti's eraser ... Both start at 0." -> "Siti's rubber ... Both start at the 0 mark." and "longer than the rubber" (guide rule 3: rubber, not eraser; also in solution, explore script and slide)
- Workbook page 3 hint "Use your ruler and a drawing. Then write how you know. ..." -> "Use your ruler and drawings to help. Choose any question, in any order." (no question on that page asks for "how you know")

Sneak-peek, slides and script (read aloud to children)
- Sneak 1 "Draw a dot. Now draw a line from the dot that is exactly 3 cm long. Use your ruler." -> "Draw a dot. Use your ruler to draw a line from the dot, exactly 3 cm long." (slide card and script READ the same)
- Sneak 2 "Ali puts the end of a pencil at the 1 mark on his ruler. The tip is at the 9 mark. Ali says: it is 9 cm long. Is he right? Write yes or no, and why." -> "Ali puts one end of a pencil at the 1 mark of his ruler. The other end is at the 9 mark. Ali says it is 9 cm long. Is he right? Write yes or no. Then write why." ("the end" and "the tip" read as the same end; Talk-time slide "the tip was at the 9" -> "the other end was at the 9")
- Explore script and slide: "a pencil, a spoon, an eraser or a toy" -> "... a rubber or a toy"
- Explore script "Tell me one of your things. My pencil is about how many centimetres? How close was your guess?" -> "Tell me about one of your things. How long was it? How close was your guess?" (the tutor was asking about the tutor's pencil by mistake)
- Brain break "We measure with our bodies today. A big jump is ten centimetres. A small hop is one centimetre. Fourteen centimetres is one big jump and four small hops." -> "We move our bodies today. Let us pretend. A big jump is 10 cm. A small hop is 1 cm. So 14 cm is one big jump and four small hops." (a jump is not 10 cm; slide: "Pretend a big jump is 10 cm.")
- Mini-lesson turn slide, practice t1 and script: "A line starts at the 2 mark and ends at the 9 mark." -> "A line goes from the 2 mark to the 9 mark." (the old sentence is quoted in the school-paper doc; same meaning)
- Workbook script lines: Q5 "...then a line 3 cm longer." -> "... than the first." ; Q6 reading now matches the new Q6 and asks "Is she correct?"
- Talk time "What could have happened? How can we find out who is right?" -> "Why might their answers be different? How can we check?" (script, card, guide; "what could have happened" is vague)
- Optional slide and script "You may cut it at the 1 mark, the 2 mark and so on." -> "... but not at the ends." (the answer 9 (not 11) depended on a rule the text never stated)

Home practice
- H1 "Draw a line 8 cm long on spare paper. On your ruler, where does it start? Where does it end?" -> "... Start it at the 0 mark of your ruler. Which mark does it end at?"
- H2 "How much longer is the pencil?" -> "How many cm longer is the pencil?" (a unit for the gap)
- H3 "a spoon, a toy and a book ... Write the lengths from shortest to longest." -> "a spoon, a toy and a crayon ... Write the 3 lengths in order, shortest first." (a book is longer than most rulers)
- H4 "Show how you know." -> "Write: I know because ..." (sentence frame)
- H5 "At home: draw a path with 3 different straight pieces that is 25 cm long in all." -> "Draw a path with 3 straight pieces. The pieces are all different lengths. The whole path is 25 cm long. Write the 3 lengths." ("3 different straight pieces" could mean different shapes)

Online practice (items and templates)
- c2 "How much longer is Ali's line?" -> "How many cm longer is Ali's line?" (q and template)
- c3 "Where do you put the 0 mark of your ruler?" -> "You draw a line 6 cm long. Where does the 0 mark of your ruler go?"; slip text "...unless you take the start mark away" removed (confusing)
- c5 "The spoon starts at 0." -> "The spoon starts at the 0 mark." (q and template)
- t1 "A line starts at the {s} mark and ends at the {e} mark." -> "A line goes from the {s} mark to the {e} mark."
- t4 "Ben starts a line at the 2 mark and it ends at the 9 mark. He says: it is 9 cm long." -> "Ben draws a line from the 2 mark to the 9 mark. He says it is 9 cm long."
- g1 "...How long is piece C, the rest of the ribbon?" -> "It is cut into 3 pieces. ... How long is piece C?" (q and template)
- g2 "how far has the snail crawled?" -> "how many cm has the snail crawled?" (q and template)
- g3 "You may cut it at a mark: the 1 mark, the 2 mark and so on, but not at the two ends. How many different places can you cut?" -> "A paper strip is {n} cm long. It has marks at 0, 1, 2 and so on up to {n}. You cut it at one mark, but not at an end. How many places can you cut?" (q and template)
- g4 "...3 straight pieces that are all different and add up to exactly 20 cm." -> "Make your own path puzzle. Use 3 straight pieces, all different lengths. The whole path is exactly 20 cm."

Parent page
- "Start at the edge on purpose, for them to spot." -> "Start at the edge of the ruler on purpose, so your child can spot it."
