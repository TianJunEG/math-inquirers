2Ali, Ben and Siti each have a different pet: a cat, a dog and a fish. Ali’s pet has no fur. Ben does not have the dog. Who has which pet?
3Triangles are made in a row with sticks: 1 triangle uses 3 sticks, 2 triangles use 5 sticks, 3 triangles use 7 sticks. How many sticks are needed for 12 triangles?
4Use the digits 3, 0, 6 and 9 once each. Make the largest even 4-digit number and the smallest odd 4-digit number.
