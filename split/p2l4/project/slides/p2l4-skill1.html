Optional slide for pairs who finish early or ask for more. Show it only on request and never announce who is working on it. Children write the answer on their own paper and say it together on three; nothing is held up to the camera. Answers and tutor lines are for you only. Item 1 (Sequences: the gap grows by 1 each time; tool of the day idea: Make a table). SAY: "This pattern does not add the same number. Make a table and put the gaps in a row of their own." ASK: "What are the gaps? How does the gap change? What is the next gap?" LISTEN FOR: "The gap goes up by 1." ANSWER: a) 21 and 27 (the gaps are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6). b) No: the pattern goes 27, 34, 42 and jumps over 40. Item 2 (Sequences: add the two numbers before; tool of the day idea: Spot the pattern). SAY: "Try the gap row. Does it look like the numbers?" ASK: "What is 2 + 4? What is 4 + 6? What would the next number be?" LISTEN FOR: "Add the two numbers before." Ask them to write the whole list to the 9th number. ANSWER: a) 26 and 42 (6 + 10 = 16, 10 + 16 = 26, 16 + 26 = 42). b) 110: the numbers are 2, 4, 6, 10, 16, 26, 42, 68, 110.
