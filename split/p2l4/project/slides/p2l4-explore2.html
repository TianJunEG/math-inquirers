1. What colour is cube 20?

2. How did you know without building?

3. Which cubes are blue? What do you notice?

Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 14 to 20. Gather the pairs and take answers. Answers: 1) Red. 2) The blue cubes are at 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18; then 19 and 20 are red. 3) The blue cubes are the 3 times table, because each group of 3 ends with a blue. Push: "What colour is cube 30? Cube 31?" (blue; red). Say: "The group is 3 cubes long, so the 3 times table tells us where each group ends. Today we use that idea to jump far along a pattern."
