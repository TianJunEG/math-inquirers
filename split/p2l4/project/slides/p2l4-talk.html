A pattern repeats every 5 shapes. Is the 10th shape always the same as the 20th? What if it repeats every 3 shapes?

Sentence starters:

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 72 to 80. Pairs talk for 2 minutes, then share. Answer: Every 5: yes, 10 and 20 are both in the 5 times table, so both are the LAST shape of a group. Every 3: no. 9 ends a group, so 10 is the 1st shape; 18 ends a group, so 20 is the 2nd shape. Praise the reasons, not only the answers.
