Beat 9?

Beat 10?

Beat 20?

CLAP: 18 ends a group, then 19, 20

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 4 to 6 (a 2-minute game: one or two rounds). Pattern rhythm. Everyone stands. Do clap, clap, stomp together for a few rounds while counting the beats aloud. Freeze, then ask each question; take predictions before clicking. Answers: beat 9 is a stomp (stomps are on 3, 6, 9: the 3 times table); beat 10 is a clap (9 ends a group, so 10 is the first beat of the next group); beat 20 is a clap (18 ends a group, 19 is clap 1, 20 is clap 2). Ask: "Which beats are stomps?" (3, 6, 9, 12 …). "How did you find beat 20 without clapping 20 times?" Online: children clap and stomp in view of the camera.
