These are the SAME four questions as the printed Think harder page: the slide shows 1 to 3 and question 4 is on the page only. Never announce who is working on which page. Answers: 1) 12 circles (30 shapes is 6 groups of 5, with 2 circles in each group). 2) Mei is not right. The pattern repeats every 4 beads and 28 ends a group (4 × 7 = 28), so the 29th bead is red and the 30th bead is red. Green beads are the 4th bead of each group: places 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 and 28. 3) 12 yellow beads (18 beads is 6 groups of 3, with 2 yellow in each). 4) (Question: Houses are made of sticks in a row. 1 house uses 5 sticks, 2 houses use 9 sticks, 3 houses use 13 sticks. How many sticks are needed for 6 houses?) 25 sticks. The rule is add 4: 5, 9, 13, 17, 21, 25.
