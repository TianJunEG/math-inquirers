For early finishers

Stretch

1. A pattern goes 1, 2, 3, 1, 2, 3 … What is the total of the first 15 numbers?

2. Ben’s pattern is 2, 5, 8, 11 … Will 30 be in his pattern? Explain.

3. Square, circle, circle, square, circle, circle … Mei draws 20 shapes. How many squares?

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 30 (5 groups, each adds up to 6). 2) No: 2, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26, 29, 32, it jumps from 29 to 32 (every number is 1 less than a 3 times table number). 3) 7 squares, in places 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19 (18 shapes is 6 groups, then place 19 starts a 7th group with a square).
