Step 1. The rule: 3 legs for every stool. Legs = stools × 3.

Step 2. 10 stools: 10 × 3 = 30 legs.

24 legs? Count in 3s to 24: 8 stools.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4, flower petals). Click: the legs row, the rule, going forwards, going backwards. Think-aloud: "Each stool adds 3 legs, so the table is the 3 times table. To go forwards I multiply: 10 × 3 = 30. To go backwards from 24 legs I count in 3s until I reach 24: 3, 6, 9 … 24 is 8 threes, so 8 stools." Ask: "What about 20 legs?" (6 stools use 18, and 2 legs are left over: not enough for another stool.)
