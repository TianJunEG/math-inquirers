Far places: count in groups

What is in place 14?

Step 1. One group is sun, moon, star: 3 shapes. Each group ends with a star.

Step 2. Groups end at 3, 6, 9, 12 (count in 3s). Place 12 is a star.

13 starts again with a sun, so place 14 is a MOON.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Point to the dashed lines: "Each line marks the end of a group." Click to reveal each step. Think-aloud: "Sun, moon, star, sun, moon, star. The group is 3 shapes long. Every group ends with a star, at 3, 6, 9, 12: the 3 times table. Place 14? 12 is the end of a group, so 13 starts again with a sun, and 14 is a moon." Ask: "Is 12 the first shape of a group or the last?" (the last). Children often say "12 is in the 3 times table so it is a sun"; point at place 3, 6 and 9 to show they are stars.
