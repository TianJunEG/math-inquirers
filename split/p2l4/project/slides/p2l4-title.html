Pattern Machines

Patterns with Multiplication

TODAY WE WILL

Find far places in a pattern by counting in groups, and find the rule of a growing pattern with a table.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find far places in a repeating pattern by counting in groups (the ends of the groups are in a times table); count how many of one shape there are; describe a growing pattern with a table and a rule and use it forwards and backwards; find the numbers that two counting patterns share. Tables used: 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10 only. Thinking strategies taught today: LOOK FOR A PATTERN (group size, where groups end) and MAKE A TABLE / MAKE A LIST (growing patterns, two counting lists). Materials: Linking cubes in 3 colours (about 20 per pair), mini whiteboards, matchsticks or lolly sticks (about 25 per pair), counters. Online: Coloured pencils and squared paper (or Lego bricks) for the cube train; children draw sticks for the growing pattern and check their tables against the slide. Time plan: opening 0 to 1.5, sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 6, warm-up 6 to 10.5, explore 10.5 to 23.5, mini-lesson 23.5 to 35.5, brain break 35.5 to 38.5, worksheet page 1 38.5 to 52.5, check together, page 1 52.5 to 55, short teaching 55 to 57.5, worksheet page 2 57.5 to 69, check together, page 2 69 to 71.5, flex (spare time) 71.5 to 76.5, talk time 76.5 to 81.5, exit ticket 81.5 to 86.5, looking back 86.5 to 88.5, closing and stamps 88.5 to 90. The Think harder and Go further slides are optional, if you finish early (no clock time). Kit ready at minute 0: ask families to put every item on the desk before the lesson starts (a 30-second check at the start).
