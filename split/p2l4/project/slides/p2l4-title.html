Pattern Machines

Patterns with Multiplication

TODAY WE WILL

Find far places in a pattern by counting in groups, and find the rule of a growing pattern with a table.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find far places in a repeating pattern by counting in groups (the ends of the groups are in a times table); count how many of one shape there are; describe a growing pattern with a table and a rule and use it forwards and backwards; find the numbers that two counting patterns share. Tables used: 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10 only. Thinking strategies taught today: LOOK FOR A PATTERN (group size, where groups end) and MAKE A TABLE / MAKE A LIST (growing patterns, two counting lists). Materials: Linking cubes in 3 colours (about 20 per pair), mini whiteboards, matchsticks or lolly sticks (about 25 per pair), counters. Online: Coloured pencils and squared paper (or Lego bricks) for the cube train; children draw sticks for the growing pattern and hold up their tables. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 8, explore 8 to 20 (explore2 talk is 6 minutes), teaching 20 to 32 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 32 to 36, worksheet page 1 36 to 50 then check together 50 to 52, short teaching 52 to 57, worksheet page 2 57 to 69 then check together 69 to 72, talk time 72 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90. Stretch and Challenge slides are for early finishers.
