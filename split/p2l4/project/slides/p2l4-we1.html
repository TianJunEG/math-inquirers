Worked example: which place? how many?

Where is the 5th star?

How many moons in the first 21 shapes?

One star at the end of each group.

Stars: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15.

21 shapes: 3, 6, 9 … 21 is 7 groups.

One moon in each group.

Place 15 (5 groups of 3)

7 moons

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click to reveal the left steps and answer, then the right. Think-aloud: "Where is the 5th star? There is one star at the end of each group, so the 5th star ends the 5th group: 5 × 3 = 15." "How many moons in the first 21 shapes? 21 is in the 3 times table: 7 groups. One moon in each group, so 7 moons." Ask: "How many suns in the first 22 shapes?" (8: 7 groups, and place 22 starts the 8th group with a sun).
