Use after pairs have tried. Q1: each tower has 3 more cubes than the one before; keep the table going until 29. A strong child may notice each tower is 1 less than the 3 times table (tower 10: 30 − 1 = 29), which answers the extra questions: 50 + 1 = 51 is in the 3 times table (17 × 3), so tower 17 has 50 cubes; 100 + 1 = 101 is not (99 and 102 are), so no tower has 100. Q2: list each frog's landings in order up to 30 and circle the numbers in both lists. The next shared number would be 32 (they meet every 12 steps: 8, 20, 32).
