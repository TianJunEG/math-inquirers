Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5, tables and chairs). Build the squares with lolly sticks. Ask: "How many more sticks for the next square? Why not 4?" (it shares a side with the square before). Click: the sticks row, the note, the step, the answer. Think-aloud: "I make a table. 4, 7, 10: it goes up by 3 each time. I keep adding 3 to reach 10 squares: 31. To go backwards from 22 sticks, I keep adding 3 until I reach 22 and read the squares: 7." Watch for: 10 × 3 = 30 or 10 × 4 = 40. Point at the first square: it needs 4, not 3.
