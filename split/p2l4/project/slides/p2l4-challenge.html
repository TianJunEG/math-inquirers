1. Siti builds towers of cubes. Tower 1 has 2 cubes, tower 2 has 5 cubes, tower 3 has 8 cubes. They keep growing in the same way. Which tower has 29 cubes? Is there a tower with exactly 50 cubes? With exactly 100 cubes?

Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. These are Q6 and Q7 on the Think Harder page. Answers: 1) Tower 10 has 29 cubes. Tower 17 has 50 cubes (3 × 17 − 1). No tower has 100, because every tower is 1 less than a 3 times table number and 101 is not in the 3 times table. 2) 8 and 20.
