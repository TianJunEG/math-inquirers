Answers: 1) 6 (18 ÷ 3). 2) 60 (5 × 12: we can turn the mean back into a total). 3) 22.4 (112 ÷ 5): four of the five values are 3, yet the mean is over 20. Ask on 2: "How did you get a total from a mean?" This is the key move for today’s missing-value puzzles.
