Minutes 5 to 10. Read each aloud, give thinking time, take answers, then click to show. Answers: 1) 6 (18 ÷ 3). 2) 25 (100 ÷ 4; not one of the values!). 3) 60 (5 × 12: we can turn the mean back into a total). 4) 22.4 (112 ÷ 5): four of the five values are 3, yet the mean is over 20. 5) 3 (6 ÷ 2): means can be decimals, and decimals can have a whole-number mean. Ask on 3: "How did you get a total from a mean?" This is the key move for today’s missing-value puzzles.
