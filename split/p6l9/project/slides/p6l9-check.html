Sneak-peek puzzles

Two ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome. “I do not know” is welcome. Nobody is marked. When your tutor says, everyone says the answer together on three.

Puzzle 1: try it with objects, a drawing or counting

Four friends have 2, 4, 4 and 6 stickers. Use counters to share the stickers out so that every friend has the same number. How many does each friend get?

4 each. Total 16 stickers, shared between 4 friends. (This is the “fair share” idea behind the mean.)

Puzzle 2: have a think

Five friends have $2, $3, $3, $4 and $38. If they shared it all equally, each would get $10. Is $10 a good description of what a typical friend has? Why or why not?

No: four of the five friends have $4 or less. One very large amount pulls the equal share up. The middle value ($3) says more about a typical friend.
