Missing value: use the total

The mean of 4 test scores is 15. Three of them are 12, 18 and 14. What is the 4th score?

Step 1. Mean × how many = total. 4 × 15 = 60.

Step 2. The three we know: 12 + 18 + 14 = 44.

Step 3. The missing score: 60 − 44 = 16. Check: 60 ÷ 4 = 15 ✓

What 5th score would keep the mean at 15? 15 itself: it is already level.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click each step. Think-aloud: “The mean of 4 test scores is 15, so the total must be 4 × 15 = 60. The three I know make 12 + 18 + 14 = 44. The missing score is 60 − 44 = 16. I never subtract from the mean: I subtract from the total.” The last box: a new value equal to the mean changes nothing (5 × 15 = 75 = 60 + 15). Common mistake: 15 − 12 − 18 … (subtracting from the mean). Ask: "What number do we take the known values away FROM?" (the total).
