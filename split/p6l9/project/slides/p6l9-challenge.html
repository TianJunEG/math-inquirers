Challenge

1. Five numbers have a mean of 8. One number is removed, and the mean of the other four is 7. Which number was removed?

2. A basketball player’s mean score for 9 games is 30 points. How many points must she score in the 10th game to raise her mean to 32?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use as a pair challenge or in talk time. Ask for the reason every time. More are in the question bank. Answers: 1) 12 (5 × 8 = 40, 4 × 7 = 28, 40 − 28 = 12) 2) 50 (10 × 32 = 320, 9 × 30 = 270, 320 − 270 = 50)
