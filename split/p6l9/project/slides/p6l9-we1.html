Worked example: find it, then check it

Goals in 5 matches: 3, 0, 4, 1, 2. Find the mean.

Step 1. Total: 3 + 0 + 4 + 1 + 2 = 10. There are 5 values: the 0 is a match too.

Step 2. Mean = total ÷ how many = 10 ÷ 5 = 2.

Step 3. Balance check: above 2 → 3 is +1, 4 is +2 (+3). Below 2 → 0 is −2, 1 is −1 (−3). +3 and −3 balance ✓

Mean 2 goals. Above and below the mean always balance.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click to reveal each step. Think-aloud: “Goals in 5 matches: 3, 0, 4, 1, 2. The 0 is a real value: that match counts. Total 3 + 0 + 4 + 1 + 2 = 10. Five matches, so the mean is 10 ÷ 5 = 2. Check by balancing: 3 is 1 above, 4 is 2 above: 3 above in all. 0 is 2 below, 1 is 1 below: 3 below in all. They balance, so 2 is right.” Why the check works: levelling moves the extra cubes above the mean into the gaps below it, so "how much above" must equal "how much below". Common mistake: dividing by 4 because 0 "is nothing" (10 ÷ 4 = 2.5 is wrong).
