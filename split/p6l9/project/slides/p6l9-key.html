Key idea: average (mean) = total ÷ how many

The average (mean) = total ÷ how many values. Turn it round: total = mean × how many. The values above and below the mean always balance.

One extreme value can pull the mean a long way. Then the median (middle value) or mode (most common) may describe a typical value better.

What changes the mean? A new value equal to the mean: nothing. Add 1 to every value: up 1. Remove a value above the mean: down.

Worksheet Q3 is like this. Say the key idea together: "Mean = total ÷ how many. Total = mean × how many." Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Dividing by the wrong count: leaving out a value of 0, or forgetting the new value when one is added. (2) Finding a missing value from the MEAN instead of from the TOTAL (mean × how many) minus the known values. (3) Thinking the mean must be one of the values, or that it is always a typical value even when one value is extreme.
