EXIT TICKET

The mean of 3 numbers is 9. Two of them are 5 and 12. What is the third number? A fourth number, 13, is added. What is the new mean?

Write your answer on a slip of paper or in the chat. Show your working. Then collect your stamp.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 82 to 90. Answer: 10 (total 3 × 9 = 27; 27 − 5 − 12 = 10). New mean 10 (27 + 13 = 40, 40 ÷ 4 = 10). “Today you found means by levelling and by dividing, worked backwards from a mean to a total, and caught a misleading average.
