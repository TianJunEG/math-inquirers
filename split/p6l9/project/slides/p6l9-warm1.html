Five numbers, five seconds. No working: write your best guess of the mean.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Quick-eye mean. Hide the slide (or cover the numbers), show one round for 5 seconds, then hide it again. Children write a guess on their whiteboards and hold it up. Click to show the mean; the closest guess wins a point. Round 1: 8 + 12 + 10 + 9 + 11 = 50, 50 ÷ 5 = 10. Round 2: 150 ÷ 5 = 30 (the middle number, because the numbers are evenly spaced). Round 3: 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 37 = 55, 55 ÷ 5 = 11. Ask after round 3: "Most of you guessed about 5. Why is the mean 11?" (The 37 pulls the mean up: we come back to this today.) Ask: "How did you guess so fast?" Listen for: pick a middle number, then check the big ones and small ones balance.
