Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6.5 to 9.5. Quick-eye mean. Hide the slide (or cover the numbers), show one round for 5 seconds, then hide it again. Children write a guess on paper (no points, no ranking). Click to show the mean and ask how they guessed. Two rounds only. Round 1: 8 + 12 + 10 + 9 + 11 = 50, 50 ÷ 5 = 10. Round 2: 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 37 = 55, 55 ÷ 5 = 11. Ask after round 2: "Most of you guessed about 5. Why is the mean 11?" (The 37 pulls the mean up: we come back to this today.) Ask: "How did you guess so fast?" Listen for: pick a middle number, then check the big ones and small ones balance.
