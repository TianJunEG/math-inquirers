Math Inquirers Level 6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Let pairs keep testing (add other values: which new value keeps the mean exactly the same?), then gather to answer. Answers: 1) Pick a value in the middle, then check that the values above it and below it roughly balance. 2) It went up, a lot, because 30 is far above every other value. One big value pulls the mean towards it. 3) They should. If not, look for a value typed twice or missed, or dividing by the wrong number. Finisher answer: adding a value equal to the mean keeps the mean the same (it is already "level").
