Challenge: walk-through

Q1: compare the totals

Total of 5: 5 × 8 = 40

Total of 4: 4 × 7 = 28

Removed: 40 − 28 = 12

Check: 28 + 12 = 40, 40 ÷ 5 = 8 ✓

Q2: total needed, total so far

Needed after 10 games: 10 × 32 = 320

So far after 9 games: 9 × 30 = 270

Game 10: 320 − 270 = 50 points

Why so many? It must lift 9 games by 2 each (18) AND reach 32 itself: 32 + 18 = 50 ✓
