The big idea in both: work with TOTALS, not means. Q2 also has a levelling explanation: the 10th score must bring itself up to 32 and also give 2 extra to each of the 9 earlier games. Q1(b): Ali is wrong. Removing a number ABOVE the mean (like 12) lowers the mean; removing one BELOW the mean raises it. Example: 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 has mean 8; remove 4 and the mean of 6, 8, 10, 12 is 9. Check for (a): 28 + 12 = 40 and 40 ÷ 5 = 8.
