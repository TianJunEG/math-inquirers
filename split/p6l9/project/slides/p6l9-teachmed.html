When the mean misleads

Goals scored by 7 players in a season, in order:

Step 1. Mean: 42 ÷ 7 = 6. But 6 of the 7 players scored fewer than 6!

Step 2. Median: the middle value in order. 7 values, so the 4th: 3.

Step 3. Mode: the value that appears most often: 2.

One big value (25) pulls the mean up. The median does not move.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 1 (Q4). Think-aloud: “Goals by 7 players: 1, 2, 2, 3, 4, 5, 25. The mean is 42 ÷ 7 = 6, but 6 of the 7 players scored fewer than 6! The one big value pulled the mean up. The median, the middle value in order, is 3. That describes a typical player better.” Ask: "If the 25 became 250, what happens to the mean? To the median?" (Mean (17 + 250) ÷ 7 is about 38; the median is still 3.) Point out: for an even number of values the median is halfway between the middle two (we will not need that today). The mode is useful for things like shoe sizes: the shop wants the most common size.
