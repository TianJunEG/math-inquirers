Think harder

1. The mean of 6 numbers is 7. Five of them are 3, 5, 8, 9 and 10. What is the sixth?

2. Find ALL the sets of 3 different counting numbers with a mean of 5 and a median of 3.

3. A class of 30 has a mean score of 70. The 10 boys have a mean of 64. What is the mean of the 20 girls?

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 7 (6 × 7 = 42; 3 + 5 + 8 + 9 + 10 = 35; 42 − 35 = 7). 2) Two sets: 1, 3, 11 and 2, 3, 10 (total 15; the middle is 3; the smallest is 1 or 2, so the largest is 15 − 3 − 1 = 11 or 15 − 3 − 2 = 10). 3) 73 (30 × 70 = 2100; 10 × 64 = 640; 2100 − 640 = 1460; 1460 ÷ 20 = 73). Test any of them in code: put the numbers in a list and run the mean program.
