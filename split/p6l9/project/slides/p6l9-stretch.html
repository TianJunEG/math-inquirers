Think harder

1. The mean height of 4 children is 150 cm. A fifth child joins the group and the mean height becomes 152 cm. How tall is the fifth child?

2. Five whole numbers have a mean of 8, a median of 10 and a mode of 12 (12 appears more often than any other number). Find them. Is there more than one answer?

3. Write 5 whole numbers with a mean of 6 and a mode of 4.

4. A Scratch list holds 8 numbers with a mean of 12. Then the number 21 is added to the list. What will the mean program say now?

Math Inquirers Level 6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers (the same four questions as the printed Think harder page): 1) 160 cm (5 × 152 = 760, 4 × 150 = 600, 760 − 600 = 160) 2) Three answers: 0, 6, 10, 12, 12 or 1, 5, 10, 12, 12 or 2, 4, 10, 12, 12. Median 10 is the middle (3rd) number; the mode 12 must appear twice, so the 4th and 5th numbers are 12; the total is 5 × 8 = 40, so the first two add up to 40 − 10 − 24 = 6. (Using 3 and 3 would make 3 a second mode.) 3) Open, e.g. 4, 4, 5, 8, 9 (total 30, and 4 appears most often). Check the total is 30. 4) 13 (8 × 12 = 96, 96 + 21 = 117, 117 ÷ 9 = 13)
