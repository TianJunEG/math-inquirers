The mean: level it out

Four towers: 3, 7, 5 and 9 cubes. Move cubes until every tower is the same height.

Before

Levelled

Step 1. Total: 3 + 7 + 5 + 9 = 24. Moving cubes keeps the total.

Mean = 24 ÷ 4 = 6

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 26 to 27.5. Teach. Build the towers with real cubes on camera if you can. Think-aloud: "The 9-tower gives 3 cubes to the 3-tower; the 7-tower gives 1 cube to the 5-tower. Now they are all 6. That level is the mean." Click: the levelled towers, the total, then the mean. Say: "Levelling out and total ÷ how many give the same answer, because moving cubes keeps the total and sharing it equally is exactly dividing by how many towers." Ask: "Could the level ever be above the tallest tower?" (No: we only move cubes downhill.)
