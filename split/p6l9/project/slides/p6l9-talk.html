Second question (only if flex is left; otherwise the opener of the next lesson): “Can the mean be bigger than every value in the list? Why or why not?” (Only if flex is left; otherwise it is the opener of the next lesson.) Answer: Yes: the mean of 2 and 3 is 2.5, a number nobody in the group has. No: the mean is always between the smallest and the largest value. Levelling out moves cubes from taller towers to shorter ones, so the level can never be higher than the tallest tower or lower than the shortest. Praise the reasons, not only the answers.
