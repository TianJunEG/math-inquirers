Answer: Yes: the mean of 2 and 3 is 2.5, which is not in the list, and the mean of 3, 0, 4, 1, 2 is 2 (that one is in the list, but it does not have to be). No: the mean is always between the smallest and the largest value. Levelling out moves cubes from taller towers to shorter ones, so the level can never be higher than the tallest tower or lower than the shortest. Praise the reasons, not only the answers.
