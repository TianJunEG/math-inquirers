P6 LESSON 9 • WEEK 5

Data Detectives

Averages and data • lists, adding up, the average (mean)

TODAY WE WILL

Find the mean by levelling and by dividing, work back from a mean to a total, catch a misleading average, and make a program that finds the mean of a list.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find the mean as total ÷ number of values and as levelling out, use total = mean × number of values to find a missing value, predict how changing the data changes the mean, compare the mean with the median and mode when one value is extreme, and build a list program that adds up the data and finds the mean. Materials: Laptop or desktop with Scratch 3 (scratch.mit.edu; free, in the browser), workbook, pencil, mini whiteboard, about 25 linking cubes or counters for levelling towers. A calculator only for checking. Online: Data is collected in the chat. Children share their screen to show their code. The tutor shares a starter project as an .sb3 file (one sprite with an empty list called data; children use File, Load from your computer) or children build it from the slide in 5 minutes. Block colours on our slides are simplified; in Scratch, find blocks by the category name on the left, not by colour. The mean is familiar from primary school; the new parts are lists and loops, median and mode, and misleading averages. Time plan (the exact minutes of this lesson, with a standing flex buffer): opening 0 to 2, sneak-peek puzzles 2 to 6.5, warm-up 6.5 to 12 (quick-eye mean, then quick questions), explore at the computer 12 to 26 (class data in a Scratch list), mini-lesson 26 to 37.5 (click to reveal each step and answer; the mean program with a trace table), brain break 37.5 to 43, worksheet 43 to 75.5 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, flex 75.5 to 77 (unscripted: use it for Think harder or catch-up; it is lost first), talk time 77 to 82, exit slip 82 to 86, looking back 86 to 88, close and stamp 88 to 90.
