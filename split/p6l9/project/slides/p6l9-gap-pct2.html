ADDED ITEM pct2 (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Percentages from a table"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Make a table. SAY: Make a table of fruit, number of children and percentage. The total of the number column is 80. ASK: How can you find the percentage for each row? What should the percentage column add up to? LISTEN FOR: Percentage = children / 80 × 100, or 1 child is 1.25%. Apple 36 ÷ 80 = 45%. Banana 25% and orange 10% add to 35%. Listen for a check that the percentages add to 100%. Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) 8 children. (b) 45%. (c) 35%. WHY: Orange: 80 − 36 − 20 − 16 = 8. Apple: 36 ÷ 80 = 45 ÷ 100. Banana 20 ÷ 80 = 25%, orange 8 ÷ 80 = 10%, so 35%. Check: 45% + 25% + 20% + 10% = 100%.
