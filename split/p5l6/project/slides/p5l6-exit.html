Tap A fills 15 ℓ a minute and tap B fills 10 ℓ a minute. How long do both taps take to fill a 200 ℓ tank? How long if a crack also lets out 5 ℓ a minute?

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Minutes 82 to 90. Answers: 8 minutes (15 + 10 = 25 ℓ a minute; 200 / 25 = 8). With the crack, 10 minutes (25 - 5 = 20 ℓ a minute; 200 / 20 = 10). Extension: make a better-buy poster of three pack sizes where the biggest pack is NOT the best buy. Swap with a partner.
