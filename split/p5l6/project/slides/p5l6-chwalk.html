Q1: two stages

9.52 to 10.00 is 8 min: 8 × 50 = 400 cups

After that: 50 + 30 = 80 cups a minute

Still to make: 1600 − 400 = 1200 cups

1200 ÷ 80 = 15 min after 10.00: 10.15 a.m. ✓

Q2: pretend the bath holds 60 ℓ

Tap: 60 ÷ 12 = 5 ℓ in each minute

Plug out: 60 ÷ 20 = 3 ℓ out each minute

5 − 3 = 2 ℓ stay in each minute

60 ÷ 2 = 30 minutes ✓
