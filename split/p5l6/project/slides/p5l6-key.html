37–38.5 MIN

Key idea: find the rate for 1

Find how much for 1 unit (1 minute, 1 kg, 1 can). Multiply to scale up. Divide to find how many or how long.

Working together, the rates add. A leak or drain takes its rate away. Compare buys by the price of 1, counting every item you get.

Minutes 37 to 38.5. Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Adding or averaging the times when taps or printers work together (20 minutes and 30 minutes gives 50 or 25) instead of adding the rates. (2) Choosing the better buy by the pack price, or dividing a free-gift deal by the cans paid for instead of all the cans received. (3) Charges "for every half hour or part of it": dividing exactly instead of rounding up to the next half hour, or charging the first hour twice.
