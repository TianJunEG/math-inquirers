For early finishers

Challenge

1. A machine starts at 9.52 a.m. and makes 50 cups a minute. At 10.00 a.m. a second machine starts, making 30 cups a minute. At what time have they made 1600 cups altogether?

2. A tap fills an empty bath in 12 minutes. With the plug out, a full bath empties in 20 minutes. Ben turns on the tap but forgets the plug. How long does the bath take to fill?

3. Tap A fills a tank in 20 minutes. Tap B fills it in 30 minutes. Ben says together they take 50 minutes. Mei says 25 minutes. Who is right?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. These are in the question bank. Answers: 1) 10.15 a.m. (400 cups by 10.00; then 80 cups a minute; 1200 / 80 = 15 minutes more). 2) 30 minutes (pretend the bath holds 60 litres: 5 litres a minute in, 3 a minute out, 2 a minute stay in: 60 / 2 = 30). Many children say 8 minutes (20 - 12) or 32: pretending a number shows why they are wrong. 3) Neither. Together the taps must be faster than the faster tap alone, so the answer is less than 20 minutes. Pretend the tank holds 60 litres (both 20 and 30 go into 60): tap A fills 3 litres a minute and tap B fills 2, together 5 litres a minute, and 60 / 5 = 12 minutes.
