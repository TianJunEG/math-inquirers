Math Inquirers Level 5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Five minutes. Use a phone stopwatch. Round 1: 10 seconds of star jumps; children count and call the number. Predict 1 minute: multiply by 6. Round 2: 30 seconds; compare with the prediction (x 2 from 30 seconds to a minute). Ask: "Why is the rate usually lower for longer?" (we get tired: a rate stays the same only if the speed stays the same, which machines do and people do not).
