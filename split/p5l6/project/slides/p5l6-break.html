38.5–42.5 MIN

Brain break

Rate race! Do star jumps for 60 seconds and count them once. That number is your rate: star jumps a minute.

Then: a drink of water and back to our seats.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 38.5 to 42.5. Use a phone stopwatch. One round only: 60 seconds of star jumps; children count once and say the number. Ask: "Would your rate be the same for 10 seconds? For an hour?" (we get tired: a rate stays the same only if the speed stays the same, which machines do and people do not).
