2–6 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Guessing is welcome. Nobody is marked.

1. A tap puts 12 litres into a tank in 4 minutes. Draw 4 buckets and share the 12 litres. How many litres go in each minute? How many litres go in during 6 minutes?

3 litres a minute; 18 litres

2. Ali reads 12 pages in 4 minutes. Mei reads 20 pages in 5 minutes. Who reads faster? How can you be sure?

Mei: 4 pages a minute, Ali 3

Show your paper ONCE, after both puzzles: only to your tutor, or all hold paper to the camera together.

Q1: 3 litres each minute, so 18 litres in 6 minutes (6 × 3).; Q2: Mei. Ali reads 3 pages a minute (12 ÷ 4) and Mei reads 4 pages a minute (20 ÷ 5). Comparing the amount for 1 minute makes it fair.
