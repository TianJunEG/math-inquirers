Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 4. Better Buy Showdown. Show one row at a time. Children point left or right (online: hold up a left or right hand), then you click to show the proof. Pens: 3 for $2.40 is 80 cents each; 5 for $3.80 is 76 cents each, so the 5-pack. Juice: $3.00 / 1.5 = $2.00 a litre; $3.80 / 2 = $1.90 a litre, so the 2 litre bottle. Eggs: $3.20 / 10 = 32 cents each; $3.96 / 12 = 33 cents each, so the 10-pack (the bigger pack is NOT cheaper per egg). Ask: "Why is it fair to compare the price of ONE?" Watch for children who compare the pack prices ($2.40 and $3.80). Do not say "the bigger pack is always better": the eggs show it is not.
