Rate Racers

Rate

TODAY WE WILL

Find the rate for 1, compare better buys, add rates when taps and printers work together, and work out charges and price plans.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 2 (opening, inside the 90-minute clock). Objective: Find a rate for 1 unit and use it to scale up or work backwards in a rate table; compare buys by the price of one item (including a free-gift deal); add rates when taps or printers work together and take away a leak; work out charges "for every half hour or part of it"; and compare two price plans with a table. Thinking strategies taught today: FIND THE VALUE OF 1, MAKE A TABLE and PRETEND A NUMBER (taps that fill a tank in a stated time). Materials: mini whiteboards, a calculator for checking only, a stopwatch or phone, a few real price tags or supermarket flyers if you have them. Online: children hold whiteboards up to the camera; the tutor fills the rate tables on the slides. Pairs work in breakout rooms for the Explore. The brain break uses a phone stopwatch. Time plan (the exact minutes of this lesson; every lesson adds up to 90): opening 0 to 2, sneak-peek puzzles 2 to 6 (one hold-up for both puzzles, no volunteer round, nobody is marked), warm-up 6 to 12 (one game round, four quick questions), explore 12 to 26 (pair work 6 minutes at most, then two questions together; a Go further what-if is on the explore slide for any pair that finishes), teaching 26 to 38.5 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 38.5 to 42.5, workbook page 1 42.5 to 57 (the questions without a star; after question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder), check together 57 to 61, short teaching before page 2 61 to 62.5, workbook page 2 62.5 to 71.5 (alone: start with the questions named at the top, then choose; starred parts are take-home; nobody has to finish), check together 71.5 to 74.5, talk time 74.5 to 80 (you may say pass; volunteers first; the first step of the Investigation is take-home), exit ticket 80 to 85, reflection 85 to 87.5 (pass is always OK), stamp typed in the chat and goodbye 87.5 to 88.5, and the buffer 88.5 to 90 (floating time: spend it first if you run late, never fill it on purpose). Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child and have no clock time. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and never splits a pair unless both children agree. Invite the WHOLE group, never one child: "when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation."
