Rate Racers

Rate

TODAY WE WILL

Find the rate for 1, compare better buys, add rates when taps and printers work together, and work out charges and price plans.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find a rate for 1 unit and use it to scale up or work backwards in a rate table; compare buys by the price of one item (including a free-gift deal); add rates when taps or printers work together and take away a leak; work out charges "for every half hour or part of it"; and compare two price plans with a table. Thinking strategies taught today: FIND THE VALUE OF 1, MAKE A TABLE and PRETEND A NUMBER (taps that fill a tank in a stated time). Materials: mini whiteboards, a calculator for checking only, a stopwatch or phone, a few real price tags or supermarket flyers if you have them. Online: children hold whiteboards up to the camera; the tutor fills the rate tables on the slides. Pairs work in breakout rooms for the Explore. The brain break uses a phone stopwatch. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, stretch and challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
