Click each answer after a pair explains theirs. Q1: 48 / 3 = 16 bottles a minute; 5 minutes 80; 160 bottles take 10 minutes; 12 minutes 192. Q1b: 400 / 16 = 25 minutes, so 9.25 a.m. Q2a: $4.20 / 6 = 70 cents; $2.92 / 4 = 73 cents; shop C gives 12 cans for $7.80: 65 cents. Q2c: 20 cans exactly, any correct way is fine. One deal from C (12 cans, $7.80) and two packs from B (8 cans, $5.84) = $13.64 (the cheapest). Two packs from A and two from B = $8.40 + $5.84 = $14.24. Five packs from B = $14.60. Compare two ways. (The cheapest for at least 20 cans is on the Go further page: 22 cans cost $14.92 and 24 cans cost $15.60.) Q3: a) 240 / 12 = 20; b) 240 / 30 = 8; c) 30 - 6 = 24 a minute, 240 / 24 = 10; d) half is 120 litres, 120 / 6 = 20 minutes.
