Car park charges: “or part of”

First hour $3. After that $1 for every half hour or part of a half hour. How much for 1.30 p.m. to 4.10 p.m.?

Step 1. Time: 1.30 to 4.10 is 2 h 40 min = 160 minutes. After the first hour: 100 minutes.

Step 2. 100 min = 3 half hours + 10 min. The 10 min is part of a half hour: it counts as a whole $1.

3 + 4 × 1 = $7

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5). Ask first: "If you stay 10 minutes into a new half hour, do you pay for it?" (Yes: "part of" means we round UP.) Click through. Think-aloud: "The first hour has its own price, so I take it away first. Then I count half hours: 100 minutes is 3 full half hours and 10 minutes left over. The 10 minutes still starts a fourth half hour, so I pay 4 lots of $1." Two traps: charging the first hour twice, and dividing 100 by 30 and writing 3.33 lots. Check with a different time: 1.30 p.m. to 3.45 p.m. is 2 h 15 min; after the first hour 75 min = 2 half hours + 15 min, so 3 lots and $3 + $3 = $6.
