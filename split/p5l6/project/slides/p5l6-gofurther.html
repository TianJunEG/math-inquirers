These are the printed Go further questions B1 to B3 (the same as the workbook). Answers: B1) 3 cats (each cat eats 1 fish every 3 minutes, so the 3 cats eat 1 fish a minute: 100 fish in 100 minutes) B2) After 6 hours, both 8 cm tall (the gap of 6 cm closes by 1 cm each hour) B3) 30 minutes (pretend the bath holds 60 ℓ: 5 ℓ a minute in, 3 ℓ a minute out, 2 ℓ a minute stay: 60 ÷ 2) Make up your own (printed under Go further): Make up a better-buy puzzle with three packs of the same thing. Which pack is the best buy? Swap and check. Think for 30 seconds before anyone speaks. Open-ended: check that a partner can solve it and that exactly one answer fits.
