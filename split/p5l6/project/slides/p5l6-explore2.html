1. Which printer is faster? How do you know?

2. How long do both together take for 430 pages? How did you work it out?

3. Why is the rate for 1 minute so useful?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 14 to 21. Let pairs keep working, then gather them. Answers: 1) B is faster: 25 pages a minute against 18 pages a minute for A (or compare 4 minutes: A 72, B 100). 2) Together 18 + 25 = 43 pages a minute, and 430 / 43 = 10 minutes. Children who guessed "add the times" (4 + 2 = 6) or "half of the average" will need the teaching slides. 3) With the rate for 1 minute we can compare fairly, multiply to scale up and divide to find a time. Do not give the method if nobody finds it: the later slides teach it.
