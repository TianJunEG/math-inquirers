GO FURTHER: A SECOND WHAT-IF (OPTIONAL, IF YOU FINISH EARLY)

Printer A prints 18 pages a minute and printer B prints 25 pages a minute. How long do they take together to print 430 pages? Then: how many pages should each printer be given so that both finish at exactly the same time?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 22 to 26. Pencils down: gather the pairs. Answers: 1) B is faster: 25 pages a minute against 18 pages a minute for A (or compare 4 minutes: A 72, B 100). (Go further, not asked together: together 18 + 25 = 43 pages a minute, and 430 / 43 = 10 minutes. Children who guessed "add the times" (4 + 2 = 6) will need the teaching slides.) 2) With the rate for 1 minute we can compare fairly, multiply to scale up and divide to find a time. Do not give the method if nobody finds it: the later slides teach it. TWO questions together only (about a minute each), then move on. GO FURTHER what-if (open to every pair as soon as it has finished; nobody waits): 10 minutes (18 + 25 = 43 pages a minute; 430 ÷ 43 = 10). Printer A gets 180 pages (18 × 10) and printer B gets 250 pages (25 × 10); 180 + 250 = 430. If both work for the whole 10 minutes they finish together.
