Careful: “get 2 free!”

Shop A: 8 pencils for $4, and you get 2 more FREE. Shop B: 5 pencils for $2.25. Which is the better buy?

Step 1. Shop A gives 8 + 2 = 10 pencils for $4. Count ALL the pencils you receive.

Step 2. Shop A: $4.00 ÷ 10 = 40¢ each. Shop B: $2.25 ÷ 5 = 45¢ each.

Shop A is the better buy: 40¢ is less than 45¢.

Not this one: $4 ÷ 8 = 50¢ each. That forgets the 2 free pencils.

Math Inquirers Level 5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 1 (Q2, shop C has a free-gift deal). Ask first: "Do you pay for the free pencils?" (No, but you get them, so they share the price.) Click through. Think-aloud: "I pay $4 and I walk away with 10 pencils. $4 divided by 10 is 40 cents." The trap: dividing by 8 gives 50 cents and the wrong shop. Check: 20 pencils from shop A cost 2 x $4 = $8; 20 pencils from shop B cost 4 x $2.25 = $9.
