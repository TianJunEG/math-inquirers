c A tap puts in 10 ℓ a minute but a drain lets out 4 ℓ a minute. How long to fill 90 ℓ?

15 minutes

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) 8 + 12 = 20 ℓ a minute; 200 / 20 = 10. b) 25 + 15 = 40 pages a minute; 480 / 40 = 12. c) 10 - 4 = 6 ℓ stay in each minute; 90 / 6 = 15. Ask for c: "What if the drain was 10 ℓ a minute?" (the tank never fills).
