Worked example: the better buy

Shop A sells 3 pens for $1.95. Shop B sells 5 pens for $3.10. Which is the better buy?

Pack
3 pens
5 pens
Price

1 pen
65¢

Step 1. Find the price of 1 pen: $1.95 ÷ 3 = 65¢ $3.10 ÷ 5 = 62¢

Step 2. 62¢ is less than 65¢, so the 5-pack is the better buy. Check: 15 pens cost $9.75 or $9.30 ✓

But if Mei needs only 3 pens, the 3-pack costs less ($1.95 against $3.10). The better buy depends on how many you need.
