Minutes 74.5 to 80. Pairs talk for 2 minutes, then share. Answer: no. Compare the price of 1. Example: 3 pens for $1.95 is 65 cents a pen and 5 pens for $3.10 is 62 cents a pen, so here the bigger pack is better. But 4 pens for $2.80 is 70 cents a pen and 10 pens for $7.50 is 75 cents a pen, so the small pack is the better buy. The better buy also depends on how many you really need (if Mei needs only 3 pens, the 3-pack costs less). Second question if time: "Where did the eggs in the warm-up show this?"
