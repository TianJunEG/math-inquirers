TALK TIME

Tap A fills a tank in 20 minutes. Tap B fills it in 30 minutes. Ben says together they take 50 minutes. Mei says 25 minutes. Who is right?

Sentence starters:

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 70 to 80. Pairs talk for 2 minutes, then share. Answer: neither. Together the taps must be faster than the faster tap alone (30 minutes is slower than 20), so the answer must be less than 20 minutes. Pretend the tank holds 60 litres (both 20 and 30 go into 60): tap A fills 3 litres a minute and tap B fills 2 litres a minute, together 5 litres a minute, and 60 / 5 = 12 minutes. Second question if time: "Is the biggest pack always the best buy?" (No: the eggs in the warm-up.) Praise the reasons, not only the answers.
