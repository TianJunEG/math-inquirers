Think harder

A1. A tap fills a tank in 12 minutes at 15 ℓ a minute. When a second tap is also turned on, the tank fills in 9 minutes. How many litres a minute does the second tap fill?

A2. Eggs are sold in boxes of 10 for $3.30 and boxes of 6 for $2.10. Siti needs at least 28 eggs. What is the least she can pay?

A3. A car park charges $1.50 for the first hour and $1 for every half hour or part of a half hour after that. How much does it cost to park from 10.15 a.m. to 2.50 p.m.?

These are the printed Think harder questions A1 to A4 (the same as the workbook). Answers: A1) 5 ℓ a minute (the tank holds 180 ℓ; 180 ÷ 9 = 20 ℓ a minute together; 20 − 15 = 5) A2) $9.60 (one box of 10 and three boxes of 6 make 28 eggs; three boxes of 10 cost $9.90) A3) $9.50 (4 h 35 min: first hour, then 3 h 35 min is 8 half hours or parts) A4) 100 minutes (Plan B costs 15¢ more each minute; $15 ÷ 15¢ = 100; both cost $25)
