For early finishers

Stretch

1. A bath holds 168 ℓ. The tap fills 12 ℓ a minute. The plug is out and 4 ℓ drain away each minute. How long does the bath take to fill?

2. Cheese costs $2.40 for 200 g at shop X and $5.40 for 500 g at shop Y. Which shop is cheaper per 100 g, and by how much?

3. A taxi costs $4 for the first km and 60¢ for every 500 m or part of 500 m after that. How much does a 3.2 km trip cost?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 21 minutes (12 - 4 = 8 ℓ stay in each minute; 168 / 8 = 21). 2) Shop Y by 12 cents: X costs $1.20 per 100 g and Y costs $5.40 / 5 = $1.08 per 100 g. 3) $7.00 (after the first km: 2.2 km = 2200 m; 2200 / 500 = 4.4, so 5 lots of 60 cents = $3.00; $4 + $3 = $7).
