61–62.5 MIN

Car park charges: “or part of”

First hour $3. After that $1 for every half hour or part of a half hour. How much for 1.30 p.m. to 4.10 p.m.?

Step 1. Time: 1.30 to 4.10 is 2 h 40 min = 160 minutes. After the first hour: 100 minutes.

Step 2. 100 min = 3 half hours + 10 min. The 10 min is part of a half hour: it counts as a whole $1.

3 + 4 × 1 = $7

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 61 to 62.5. Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6). Ask first: "Which plan do you think is cheaper?" Take a show of hands, then test with a table. Click the rows. Think-aloud: "For each number of visits I work out both costs. At 4 visits Plan A costs 24 + 12 = 36 and B costs 20. At 12 visits they are both 60. After 12 visits B is dearer." Why 12? Each visit costs Plan B $2 more than Plan A, and Plan A starts $24 higher, so they meet after 24 / 2 = 12 visits. The table tells us WHEN the answer changes: it depends on how many visits you will make. Use a table, not algebra.
