Plan A: $24 + 4 × $3 = $36. Plan B: 4 × $5 = $20.

Same cost at 12 visits: $60 each.

After that Plan A is cheaper. Why? Plan B costs $2 more for every visit, and that catches up the $24 joining fee: 24 ÷ 2 = 12.

Math Inquirers Level 5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6). Ask first: "Which plan do you think is cheaper?" Take a show of hands, then test with a table. Click the rows. Think-aloud: "For each number of visits I work out both costs. At 4 visits Plan A costs 24 + 12 = 36 and B costs 20. At 12 visits they are both 60. After 12 visits B is dearer." Why 12? Each visit costs Plan B $2 more than Plan A, and Plan A starts $24 higher, so they meet after 24 / 2 = 12 visits. The table tells us WHEN the answer changes: it depends on how many visits you will make. Use a table, not algebra.
