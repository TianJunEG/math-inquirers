A rate tells us how much for 1

5 kg of rice cost $9.50. How much do 3 kg cost? How many kg can you buy for $13.30?

Step 1. Find the price of 1 kg first: $9.50 ÷ 5 = $1.90

Step 2. 3 kg: 3 × $1.90 = $5.70

Divide to get 1. Multiply to scale up. Divide again to find how many.

Step 3. Backwards: 1330¢ ÷ 190¢ = 7 kg

Math Inquirers Level 5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Fill the table with the children. Ask first: "What do we need to know to get 3 kg?" (the price of 1 kg). Click step 1: 9.50 / 5 = 1.90. Click step 2: 3 x 1.90 = 5.70. Check another way: 3 kg is 3/5 of 5 kg, 3/5 of 9.50 is 5.70. Click step 3: change dollars to cents first, 1330 / 190 = 133 / 19 = 7 kg. Children who prefer can test it: 7 x $1.90 = $13.30. Think-aloud: "A rate is how much for ONE. Once I know the rate for one, everything else is multiply or divide." Use a real price tag if you have one. The rate for 1 minute, 1 kg, 1 can are all the same idea.
