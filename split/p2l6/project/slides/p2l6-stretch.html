1. Ben spent half of his money on a kite. Then he spent $4 on a ball. He had $6 left. How much money did Ben have at first? Tip: draw his money as a bar cut in half.

2. How many ways can you pay exactly $10 using $5 notes, $2 notes and $1 coins?

3. Ali has 4 coins. They are worth $1.25 altogether. Find all the sets of 4 coins he could have.

Question 4 is on your Think harder page.

Answers: 1) $20 ($6 + $4 = $10 is the half he kept after the kite, so the other half is also $10: $10 + $10 = $20). 2) 10 ways (1 + 3 + 6) 3) 2 sets: $1 + 10¢ + 10¢ + 5¢, and 50¢ + 50¢ + 20¢ + 5¢ 4) (Question: How many different ways can you pay exactly 40¢ using 5¢, 10¢ and 20¢ coins? Make a list in order, starting with the most 20¢ coins.) 9 ways: two 20¢ (1 way); one 20¢ and 20¢ more (3 ways); no 20¢ (5 ways).
