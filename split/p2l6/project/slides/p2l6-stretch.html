For early finishers

Stretch

1. Find all the ways to pay exactly $9 using only $5 and $2 notes.

2. Ali has 3 coins worth 75¢ altogether. Which coins are they? Is there another way?

3. Ben’s mum gave him $5. Then he spent $12 on a game. Now he has $3. How much did he have at first?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) Only 1 way: $5 + $2 + $2 (without a $5 note, $2 notes make only even amounts, and two $5 notes is $10, too much). 2) 50¢ + 20¢ + 5¢, and there is no other way (we need a 5¢ coin to end in 5, then 70¢ from two coins is only 50¢ + 20¢). 3) $10 ($3 + $12 = $15 before the game; $15 − $5 = $10 before mum gave him money).
