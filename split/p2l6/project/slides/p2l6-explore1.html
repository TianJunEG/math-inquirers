Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. You have $10. Look at the toy stall.
  2. Choose some toys (at most one of each). What do they cost? What change do you get?
  3. Find every basket that costs EXACTLY $10.
  4. Make a list in order so you do not miss one or count one twice.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8 to 14. Each pair gets a $10 note and the price list: kite $6, ball $4, yo-yo $3, bubbles $2, sticker $1 (whole dollars, so children add in dollars only). First they choose any basket and find the change, then they hunt for every basket that costs exactly $10. Prediction first: "How many $10 baskets do you think there are?" Online: the stall is on the slide; children record baskets on whiteboards. Hint for stuck pairs: "Put the kite in first. How much is left to spend?"
