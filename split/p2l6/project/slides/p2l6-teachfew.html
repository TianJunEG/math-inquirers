Fewest notes and coins: dollars first

Pay exactly $27.85 with the fewest notes and coins.

Step 1. Dollars: $50 is too much. There is no $20 note. $10, $10, then $5, then $2: $27.

Step 2. Cents: biggest coin that fits each time: 50¢, 20¢, 10¢, 5¢: 85¢.

Step 3. $10 + $10 + $5 + $2 + 50¢ + 20¢ + 10¢ + 5¢: 8 pieces.

Count on to check: $10, $20, $25, $27, then $27.50, $27.70, $27.80, $27.85 ✓
