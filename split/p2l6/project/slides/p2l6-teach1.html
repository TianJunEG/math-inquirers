Dollars and cents

$3.45

the price tag

3 dollars 45 cents

what we say

345¢

all in cents

100¢ = $1. So $3 = 300¢, and $3.45 = 300¢ + 45¢ = 345¢.

$3.05 = 305¢

3 dollars and 5 cents

$3.50 = 350¢

3 dollars and 50 cents

The two digits after the dot are the cents. 5 cents is written .05

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Hold up $3 in notes and coins and 45¢ in coins: "This is 3 dollars and 45 cents. We write $3.45." Count it all in cents with the children: 100, 200, 300, then 320, 340, 345. Then the trap: $3.05 and $3.50 look alike but 5¢ and 50¢ are very different. Ask: "How do you write 2 dollars and 8 cents?" ($2.08).
