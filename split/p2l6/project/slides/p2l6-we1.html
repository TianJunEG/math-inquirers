Click to build each row and the side notes. Ask before each row: "What comes next if I use one fewer $2?" Think-aloud: "One $5 and one $1. Now no $5: three $2 makes $6. Two $2 makes $4, so two $1. One $2 and four $1. Six $1 coins. The $2 column counts down to 0, so I know I have them all: 5 ways." Point out $5 + $1 and $1 + $5 are the same way.
