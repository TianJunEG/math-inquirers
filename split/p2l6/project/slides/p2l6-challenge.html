Challenge

1. Ali has 10 coins. They are all 10¢ or 20¢ coins, worth $1.50 altogether. How many of each coin does he have?

2. Mei has $1. At the snack stall: chips 45¢, sweet 15¢, jelly 25¢, biscuit 30¢, lollipop 20¢. She wants as many DIFFERENT snacks as she can. Which should she buy?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then the "see it" slide, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) Five 10¢ and five 20¢ (50¢ + 100¢ = 150¢). 2) Sweet, lollipop, jelly and biscuit: 15 + 20 + 25 + 30 = 90¢, with 10¢ change. All five cost $1.35, too much, and any 4 snacks with the chips cost at least $1.05.
