Your turn: work backwards

Start from the end. Put back what was spent.

a Ali paid with a $10 note and got $4 change. What did the toy cost?

$6

b Mei spent 35¢. Now she has 40¢. How much did she have at first?

75¢

c Ravi spent half his money on a book, then $5 on a game. He has $3 left. How much at first?

$16

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) $10 − $4 = $6 (check: $6 + $4 = $10). b) 40¢ + 35¢ = 75¢. c) $3 + $5 = $8 is the half he kept after the book; the other half is also $8, so $16 (check: half of $16 is $8, $8 − $5 = $3).
