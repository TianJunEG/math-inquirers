Market Day

Money

TODAY WE WILL

Pay exactly with the fewest notes and coins, list every way in order, give change and work backwards with money.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read and write money in dollars and cents, pay exactly with the fewest notes and coins, make a list in order to find every way to pay, give change by counting on, decide whether there is enough money, and work backwards to find how much someone had at first. Materials: play money (coins 5¢, 10¢, 20¢, 50¢, $1; notes $2, $5, $10, $50), about 6 of each per pair, price tags, mini whiteboards. Online: print the cut-out coins and notes, or use real money at home; the shop and toy stall are on the slides and children show their money to the camera. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90. Level: P2 adds and subtracts money in dollars only or in cents only; mixed amounts like $3.45 are only counted out with notes and coins, never added in columns. Singapore money has no $20 note.
