Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6 to 8.5. The Market Day shop, two price tags only: the eraser (85¢) and the scooter ($57.20). The play money is already cut out and on the desk (kit at minute 0). Point to one item at a time: "The eraser costs 85 cents. Pay exactly. Can you use fewer pieces than your partner?" Children lay out their money; click to show the fewest way (clicks go Eraser, Scooter). Fewest: 85¢ = 50¢ + 20¢ + 10¢ + 5¢ (4). $57.20 = $50 + $5 + $2 + 20¢ (4). (Spare tags, not shown: $1.35 = $1 + 20¢ + 10¢ + 5¢ (4); $3.45 = $2 + $1 + 20¢ + 20¢ + 5¢ (5); $6.50 = $5 + $1 + 50¢ (3); $12.70 = $10 + $2 + 50¢ + 20¢ (4).) Strategy to draw out: pay the dollars first with the biggest note that is not too much, then the cents with the biggest coin that fits. Ask: "Someone paid 85¢ with eight 10¢ coins and a 5¢ coin. Is it exact? Is it the fewest?" (Exact, yes. Fewest, no: 9 pieces instead of 4.) Watch for 50¢ and $50 mixed up. A game of about 2.5 minutes: stop it on the clock even if the game is not finished.
