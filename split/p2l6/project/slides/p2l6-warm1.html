Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 4 to 6 (a 2-minute game: one or two rounds). The Market Day shop. Hand out play money. Point to one item at a time: "The sticker book costs $3.45: 3 dollars and 45 cents. Pay exactly. Can you use fewer pieces than your partner?" Children lay out their money; click to show the fewest way (clicks go Eraser, Pencil, Sticker book, Water bottle, Toy car, Scooter). Fewest: 85¢ = 50¢ + 20¢ + 10¢ + 5¢ (4). $1.35 = $1 + 20¢ + 10¢ + 5¢ (4). $3.45 = $2 + $1 + 20¢ + 20¢ + 5¢ (5). $6.50 = $5 + $1 + 50¢ (3). $12.70 = $10 + $2 + 50¢ + 20¢ (4). $57.20 = $50 + $5 + $2 + 20¢ (4). Strategy to draw out: pay the dollars first with the biggest note that is not too much, then the cents with the biggest coin that fits. Ask: "Someone paid $6.50 with three $2 notes and five 10¢ coins. Is it exact? Is it the fewest?" (Exact, yes. Fewest, no: 8 pieces instead of 3.) Watch for 50¢ and $50 mixed up.
