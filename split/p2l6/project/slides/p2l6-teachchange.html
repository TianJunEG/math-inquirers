Change: count on like a shopkeeper

A book costs $33. Ali pays with a $50 note. How much change?

Step 1. Count on from $33: $33 → $35 is $2.

Step 2. $35 → $40 is $5. $40 → $50 is $10.

Step 3. Change: $2 + $5 + $10 = $17. Fewest notes: $10 + $5 + $2.

Cents too: 45¢ from a $1 coin. 45¢ → 50¢ is 5¢, 50¢ → $1 is 50¢. Change 55¢.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q3). Act it out: one child is the shopkeeper and counts the change into Ali’s hand: "33 … 35, 40, 50." The notes given ARE the fewest notes. Check with take-away: $50 − $33 = $17. Watch for children who give $27 (counting the wrong way).
