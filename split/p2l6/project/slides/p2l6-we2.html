Work backwards with money

Mei bought a drink for 80¢. Then she bought a bun for 65¢. She has 55¢ left. How much money did she have at first?

Step 1. Start at the end: 55¢ is left.

Step 2. Put back the bun: 55¢ + 65¢ = 120¢

Step 3. Put back the drink: 120¢ + 80¢ = 200¢

200¢ = $2. Check: 200 − 80 = 120, 120 − 65 = 55 ✓
