Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 19.5 to 23.5. Let pairs keep working, then gather them. Answers: 1) Undo the LAST card first with its opposite, then undo the first card. 2) ÷ 3 undoes × 3; + 8 undoes − 8. (Does the order of the cards matter? That is the teachorder slide and worksheet Q5, not asked here.) Ask a pair whose machine could not be undone with a whole number (for example ÷ 3 first, then OUT 7 with + 2: 5 is not in the 3 table) to share: what went wrong? Keep it moving (about 4 minutes in all): ask "how do you know?" on question 2 only; about 15 seconds of talk on the other questions.
