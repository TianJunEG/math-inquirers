Before and after: draw the bars

Ben had some stamps. He gave 18 to Mei. Then he bought 25 more. Now he has 60. How many stamps did he have at first?

He had 53 stamps. Check: 53 − 18 = 35, 35 + 25 = 60 ✓

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click 1: the "Now" bar (bottom): 60 is the stamps he had before buying, plus the 25 he bought. Click 2: before buying he had 60 − 25 = 35. Click 3: before giving 18 away he had 35 + 18 = 53. Click 4: check forwards. Say: "We read the story forwards but we solve it backwards, from the bottom bar up." The bars stop children from adding 18 and 25 to 60 without thinking.
