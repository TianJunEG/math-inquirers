Warm-up: Guess my machine

WARM-UP GAME

I am a secret machine. Call out a number to put IN. I tell you what comes OUT. Can you guess what I do?

IN 4, OUT 12. Is it + 8? Or × 3? Test another number!

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 4 to 6 (shortened to two minutes because of the Warm-up check: one or two rounds). You are the machine. Pick a secret one-step rule (start with × 3). Children call IN numbers; you say the OUT. After two or three, ask for a guess. Point at the picture: IN 4, OUT 12 could be + 8 OR × 3, so test another IN (5 gives 15 for × 3 but 13 for + 8). Good rules to use: × 3, + 15, × 10, ÷ 2 (only give even IN numbers), − 20, × 5. Then flip it: give only an OUT ("My machine is × 5. OUT is 35. What went IN?" 7). Listen for children who already "do the opposite".
