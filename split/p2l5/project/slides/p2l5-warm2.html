Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8.5 to 10.5. Read each one aloud, give thinking time, whiteboards face down until 3, 2, 1 (then say the answer together on three; never read a board out), then click for the answer. Ask "How did you find it?" each time. 1) 18 ÷ 2 = 9. 2) 40 + 25 = 65. Spare questions in the guide table: I divide by 5 and get 4 (20); the opposite of × 4 is ÷ 4; I multiply by 3, then add 4, and get 19 (5: 19 − 4 = 15, 15 ÷ 3 = 5; do not teach it yet, see who tries 19 ÷ 3 first, that is today’s big idea). Ask quick questions 1 and 3 only (about 2 minutes): children answer by thumbs, hands, not by naming a child. The other questions are spare.
