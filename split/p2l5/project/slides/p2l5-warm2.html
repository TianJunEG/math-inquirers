Quick questions

1. I think of a number. I multiply it by 2. I get 18.

2. I think of a number. I divide it by 5. I get 4.

3. I think of a number. I take away 25. I get 40.

4. What is the opposite of × 4?

5. I multiply my number by 3, then add 4. I get 19.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6 to 8 (2 minutes: ask questions 1, 3 and 5; 2 and 4 are spare). Children answer by thumbs, hands, not by naming a child. Read each one aloud, give thinking time, whiteboards face down until 3, 2, 1 (or show only the tutor; never read a board out), then click for the answer. Ask "How did you find it?" each time. 1) 18 ÷ 2 = 9. 2) 4 × 5 = 20. 3) 40 + 25 = 65. 4) ÷ 4 undoes × 4. 5) Two steps: 19 − 4 = 15, 15 ÷ 3 = 5 (check: 5 × 3 = 15, 15 + 4 = 19). Do not teach question 5 yet: see who tries 19 ÷ 3 first; that is today’s big idea.
