Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find a mystery number in two-step puzzles with +, −, × and ÷ by working backwards (undo the LAST step first with the opposite operation), check by going forwards, and use before-and-after bar models for stories where an amount changes. Materials: mini whiteboards, counters, paper cups, number machine cards (× 2, × 3, × 4, × 5, × 10, and + or − cards from 1 to 20), paper strips for bars. Online: machine cards are shown on the slides; children write on whiteboards and say the answer together on three; for bars they draw on paper or fold a paper strip into equal parts. Time plan: opening 0 to 1.5, sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 6, warm-up 6 to 10.5, explore 10.5 to 23.5, mini-lesson 23.5 to 35.5, brain break 35.5 to 38.5, worksheet teach slide 38.5 to 41, worksheet page 1 41 to 52.5, check together, page 1 52.5 to 55, short teaching 55 to 59.5, worksheet page 2 59.5 to 71, check together, page 2 71 to 73.5, flex (spare time) 73.5 to 76.5, talk time 76.5 to 81.5, exit ticket 81.5 to 86.5, looking back 86.5 to 88.5, closing and stamps 88.5 to 90. The Think harder and Go further slides are optional, if you finish early (no clock time). Kit ready at minute 0: ask families to put every item on the desk before the lesson starts (a 30-second check at the start). SPINE: Today's tool is Work backwards. Start at the end and undo each step. Look for where you use it. Point at the card on page 1 of the workbook. HABIT: Focus habit: Check. Ask the child to test a second case before saying it works. Say: "Will it work with a different number? Test it." Do not give the answer first: ask the prompt, wait, and give a hint only if the child is stuck.
