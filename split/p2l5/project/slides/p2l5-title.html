Mystery Number Machines

Working Backwards

Find mystery numbers in two-step machines with + − × ÷ by working backwards, and draw before-and-after bars for stories.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find a mystery number in two-step puzzles with +, −, × and ÷ by working backwards (undo the LAST step first with the opposite operation), check by going forwards, and use before-and-after bar models for stories where an amount changes. Materials: mini whiteboards, counters, paper cups, number machine cards (× 2, × 3, × 4, × 5, × 10, and + or − cards from 1 to 20), paper strips for bars. Online: machine cards are shown on the slides; children write on whiteboards and hold them up; for bars they draw on paper or fold a paper strip into equal parts. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90. Level: × and ÷ only with the 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10 tables; all numbers within 1000.
