Three children: take away the extras

Tom, Mei and Ravi have 25 stickers altogether. Mei has 3 more than Tom. Ravi has 4 more than Tom. How many does each child have?

Step 1. Take away the extras: 25 − 3 − 4 = 18

Step 2. Now there are 3 equal bars: 18 ÷ 3 = 6. Tom has 6.

Step 3. Mei 6 + 3 = 9, Ravi 6 + 4 = 10. Check: 6 + 9 + 10 = 25 ✓

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach for Think Harder 4 (three children and 40 marbles), after page 2 is checked. Draw Tom’s bar first: the other two are "Tom’s bar and a bit more". Cover the yellow extras with your hand: "If nobody had the extras, there would be 25 − 3 − 4 = 18, in three equal bars." Then give the extras back. Common mistake: 25 ÷ 3 straight away (it does not even work).
