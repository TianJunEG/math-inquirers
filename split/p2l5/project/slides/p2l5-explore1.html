Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Pick one × card and one + or − card. Put them in a row: that is your machine.
  2. Secretly choose a start number. Run it through both cards. Say only the OUT.
  3. Your partner works backwards to find your start number. Check it together.

GO FURTHER (optional, any time you finish early): What if the machine adds 7 FIRST and then doubles? Put IN 5 into it. Is the OUT the same as before (17)?

Minutes 10.5 to 19.5. Pairs build a two-step machine from the cards (one × card from × 2, × 3, × 4, × 5, × 10, and one + or − card). Partner A chooses a start number (keep it from 1 to 10) and says only the OUT. Partner B works backwards, then they check by going forwards. Partners swap roles every round, so each child both chooses the secret number and works backwards (never one child always being the solver). (Swapping the order of the two cards is dropped from the explore: the teachorder slide and worksheet Q5 ask exactly that.) Online: the tutor runs the machine on the slide and children work back on whiteboards; then pairs in breakout rooms make their own. Go further (a second what-if, so nobody waits) is the box at the foot of this slide; the question is also posted in the chat when the pairs go to their breakout rooms. It is for ANY child who finishes the explore task or wants more: say it to the whole group as a choice, never as a prize, and never name who tries it. Pairs stay together unless both children agree to split. Answer: No: 5 + 7 = 12, doubled is 24. The order of the steps matters. Take it at the debrief only if a pair offers it.
