Opposites undo each other

+ 6 and − 6

Add 6, then take away 6: back where you started.

× 4 and ÷ 4

4 groups of 5 is 20. 20 shared into 4 groups is 5.

20 ÷ 4 = 5. Check: 5 × 4 = 20 ✓

Teach. Show 4 groups of 5 counters: "× 4 made 20." Now share the 20 back into 4 groups: 5 in each. "÷ 4 undid × 4." Do the same with + 6 and − 6.
