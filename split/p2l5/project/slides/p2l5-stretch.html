1. A machine multiplies by a number, then adds a number. IN 2 gives OUT 11 and IN 5 gives OUT 20. Find what the machine does (try × 2, × 3, × 4 … with IN 2, then test IN 5). Then: what OUT does IN 10 give? Which IN gives OUT 26?

2. Ben had some money. He spent $15 on a book. Then his dad gave him as much as he had left. Now he has $42. How much did Ben have at first? Draw bars.

3. Siti had some beads. She gave half of them to Mei. Then she bought 12 more. Now she has 30 beads. How many beads did Siti have at first?

These are the SAME four questions as the printed Think harder page: the slide shows 1 to 3 and question 4 is on the page only. Never announce who is working on which page. Answers: 1) × 3, then + 5. IN 10 gives OUT 35. OUT 26 comes from IN 7 (26 − 5 = 21, 21 ÷ 3 = 7). 2) $36. Undo the last step: dad gave him as much as he had left, so $42 is two equal bars of $21. So Ben had $21 after the book, and $21 + $15 = $36 at first. Check: 36 − 15 = 21, 21 + 21 = 42. 3) 36 (30 − 12 = 18 is half, 18 + 18 = 36) 4) (Question: Ali, Ben and Siti have 40 marbles altogether. Ben has 5 more marbles than Ali. Siti has 8 more marbles than Ali. How many marbles does each child have? Draw a bar for each child.) Ali 9, Ben 14, Siti 17 (40 − 5 − 8 = 27, 27 ÷ 3 = 9; check 9 + 14 + 17 = 40)
