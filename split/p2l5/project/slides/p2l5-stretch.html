Stretch

1. ? → + 7 → × 2 → 30. What went IN?

2. Mei had some stamps. She gave half to Ali, then 3 to Ben. She has 5 left. How many did she have at first?

3. Ben, Ravi and Tom have 31 cards. Ravi has 2 more than Ben. Tom has 5 more than Ben. How many does each have?

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) 8 (30 ÷ 2 = 15, 15 − 7 = 8; check 8 + 7 = 15, 15 × 2 = 30). 2) 16 (5 + 3 = 8 is the half she kept, 8 + 8 = 16; check: half of 16 is 8, 8 − 3 = 5). 3) Ben 8, Ravi 10, Tom 13 (31 − 2 − 5 = 24, 24 ÷ 3 = 8; check 8 + 10 + 13 = 31).
