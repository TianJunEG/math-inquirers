Challenge

1. A machine does × 2, then − 4. Ravi puts a number in, then puts the OUT straight back in again. The final OUT is 12. What number did he put in first?

2. Ben gives Ali 5 marbles. Now they both have 20 marbles. How many marbles did each boy have before? How many MORE did Ben have than Ali at first? Why is it not 5?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Ask for the reason every time. Answers: 1) 6 (go back twice: 12 + 4 = 16, 16 ÷ 2 = 8; 8 + 4 = 12, 12 ÷ 2 = 6; check 6 → 8 → 12). 2) Ben 25, Ali 15 (give the 5 back: Ben 20 + 5, Ali 20 − 5). Ben had 10 more than Ali, not 5, because Ben lost 5 AND Ali gained 5. Extension: if Ben had given 8 and they ended equal, he had 16 more at first (the gap is double the number given). Common wrong answer for 2: "both had 20" or "Ben 25, Ali 20", or "5 more".
