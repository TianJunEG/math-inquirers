Challenge

1. A machine does × 2, then − 4. Ravi puts a number in, then puts the OUT straight back in again. The final OUT is 12. What number did he put in first?

2. Ben gives Ali 5 marbles. Now they both have 20 marbles. How many marbles did each boy have before?

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Ask for the reason every time. Answers: 1) 6 (go back twice: 12 + 4 = 16, 16 ÷ 2 = 8; 8 + 4 = 12, 12 ÷ 2 = 6; check 6 → 8 → 12). 2) Ben 25, Ali 15 (give the 5 back: Ben 20 + 5, Ali 20 − 5). Common wrong answer for 2: "both had 20" or "Ben 25, Ali 20".
