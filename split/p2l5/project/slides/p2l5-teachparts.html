Equal parts: one part tells you the rest

Mei had some beads. She gave half of them to her friend. Then she used 5 beads to make a ring. She has 9 beads left. How many beads did she have at first?

Step 1. The half Mei kept is the part she used and the part left: 5 + 9 = 14

Step 2. The two halves are equal, so the other half is also 14.

Step 3. All her beads: 14 + 14 = 28. Check: half of 28 is 14, 14 − 5 = 9 ✓

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4 uses 3 equal bags instead of 2 halves; do not do Q4 for them). Fold a paper strip in half to show the two equal parts, then fold one half again unevenly for "used" and "left". Common mistake: answering 14 (one half) instead of 28. Ask: "Is 14 ALL her beads, or one part?"
