Find a two-step rule: guess and test

A machine does × a number, then + a number. IN 1 gives OUT 7. IN 4 gives OUT 13.

Guess
Make IN 1 give 7
Test IN 4: is it 13?
× 2, then + 5
1 × 2 + 5 = 7
4 × 2 + 5 = 13 yes
× 3, then + 4
1 × 3 + 4 = 7
4 × 3 + 4 = 16 not this one
× 4, then + 3
1 × 4 + 3 = 7
4 × 4 + 3 = 19 not this one

Only × 2, then + 5 works for BOTH pairs. Always test a second pair!

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 1 (Q2b asks for a rule like this). Think-aloud: "I guess the × part first. If it is × 2, then IN 1 makes 2, and I need + 5 to reach 7. Now I test it on the other pair: 4 × 2 = 8, 8 + 5 = 13. Yes!" Then show that × 3 and × 4 also give 7 for IN 1 but fail the test. Point out: with IN 1 every guess works, so the second pair is what tells them apart. Do not tell them the worksheet rule.
