57.5–59.5 MIN

Does the order matter?

Put 2 into each machine. Do they give the same OUT?

A: 2 + 6 = 8, 8 × 3 = 24

B: 2 × 3 = 6, 6 + 6 = 12

Step 1. Not the same! The order matters.

Step 2. What must go into B to give 24? Go backwards: 24 − 6 = 18, 18 ÷ 3 = 6
