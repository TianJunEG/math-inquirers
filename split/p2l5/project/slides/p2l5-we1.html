Worked example: two steps

I think of a number. I multiply it by 3, then add 4. I get 25.

Step 1. Start at the end. The LAST step was + 4. Undo it: 25 − 4 = 21

Step 2. Then undo × 3: 21 ÷ 3 = 7

Step 3. Check forwards: 7 × 3 = 21, 21 + 4 = 25 ✓ My number is 7.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click to reveal each step; ask the group to predict it first. Analogy: "In the morning you put on socks, then shoes. At night you take off the shoes first." The last step done is the first step undone. Watch for children who do 25 ÷ 3 first: it does not even give a whole number, which is a clue they went the wrong way.
