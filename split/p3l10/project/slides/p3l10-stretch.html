For early finishers

Stretch

1. A bar graph shows stamps: Ali 35 and Ben 20. Mei has 15 more than Ben. Siti has as many as Ali and Ben together. How many stamps altogether?

2. Ali, Ben and Siti stand in a line. Ali is not first. Ben is just behind Siti. Who is first?

3. A snail climbs a 12 m wall. Each day it climbs up 5 m; each night it slides down 2 m. On which day does it reach the top?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) 145 (Mei 20 + 15 = 35; Siti 35 + 20 = 55; 35 + 20 + 35 + 55 = 145). 2) Siti: if Ben is just behind Siti, the line is Siti, Ben, Ali or Ali, Siti, Ben; Ali is not first, so Siti, Ben, Ali. 3) Day 4: make a table: end of day 1: 5 m (night 3 m), day 2: 8 m (night 6 m), day 3: 11 m (night 9 m), day 4: 9 + 5 = 14 m, past the top. Many children say day 4 because 12 ÷ 3 = 4, which is the right day but the wrong reason: check the table.
