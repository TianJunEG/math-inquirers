List it: in order, so none are missed

Make 3-digit numbers with 3, 6 and 9. How many are even?

3 first: 369, 396

6 first: 639, 693

9 first: 936, 963

6 numbers (2 for each first digit). Even: 396 and 936.

Riddle: I am an even 2-digit number. My digits add up to 7. My tens digit is bigger than my ones digit. Find ALL.

Even: the ones digit is 0, 2, 4 or 6.

70 ✓ 52 ✓ 34: 3 is not bigger than 4 16: no

Answers: 70 and 52.

Even numbers end in an even digit: only 6 is even, so 396 and 936." Right: "For a find-ALL riddle, I list every case for one clue, then cross out the ones that break another clue."
