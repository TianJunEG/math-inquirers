TALK TIME

Pick one puzzle from today and teach it to the group: how did you START it, and how do you know you are right?

Sentence starters:

“I started by …” “I noticed that …” “I know because …”

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 70 to 80. Pairs talk for 2 minutes, then share. Ask each child: "Draw it, list it or make a table: which helped you most today? Why?" Also ask: "Why do we take away 1 in a queue puzzle?" (the person is counted from the front and from the back). Praise the reasons, not only the answers.
