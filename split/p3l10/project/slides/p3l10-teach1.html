How to start a puzzle

Stuck? Do not just stare. Put the puzzle on paper.

Draw it

Use it for: queues, bar models, people in order.

List it

Use it for: find ALL the ways: numbers, coins, shapes.

Make a table

Use it for: clues (a logic grid), patterns that grow.

A puzzle is hard to hold in your head. On paper you can see it, check it and explain it.
