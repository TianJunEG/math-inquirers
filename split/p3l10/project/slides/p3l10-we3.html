Reading a bar graph: the scale first

Step 1. Read the scale: 0, 2, 4, 6 … Each space stands for 2 books.

Step 2. Tuesday’s bar stops halfway between 6 and 8, so it shows 7.

Step 3. How many more on Wednesday than Tuesday? 15 − 7 = 8.

Altogether: 12 + 7 + 15 + 10 = 44 books

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example: a bar graph (books borrowed from the class library). Ask first: "How many books on Tuesday?" Children who count spaces say 3 or 4. Click step 1: "Before reading any bar, read the scale. The lines go up in 2s." Step 2: "Tuesday is halfway between 6 and 8: 7." Step 3: "How many more" means the difference. Answer: add all four. The worksheet graph goes up in 10s with halfway values (5s); do not read it for them.
