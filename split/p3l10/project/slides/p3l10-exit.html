EXIT TICKET

Ali is 4th from the front and 4th from the back of a queue. How many people are in the queue? How did you start?

Then: “My favourite lesson was ____ because ____.” Write both on a slip of paper and collect your last stamp.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 80 to 85. Answer: 7 people (3 in front, Ali, 3 behind; 4 + 4 − 1 = 7), started by drawing it. A child who writes 8 has counted Ali twice. The favourite-lesson line is open: use it to plan next steps. Extension: write a logic grid puzzle with 3 people and 3 things for a friend, and check it has exactly one answer.
