Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 10. One question from each part of our program. Read each aloud, give thinking time, then click. Ask "Which lesson does this remind you of?" Answers: 1) 700 (hundreds place). 2) 5/8 (add the eighths; the bottom number stays). 3) 9 remainder 2 (9 × 5 = 45, 2 left). 4) 20 cm (6 + 4 + 6 + 4). 5) 2050 g (not 250 g).
