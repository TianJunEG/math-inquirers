Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Relay chain. Name a child for each card; they answer and pass the number on to the next child, who uses it as the start of the next card. Click to show each answer only after the child has said it. Chain: 6 × 9 = 54 (tables), 54 + 46 = 100 (adding to the next hundred), 100 × 7 = 700, 700 − 250 = 450, 450 ÷ 5 = 90 (think 45 tens ÷ 5 = 9 tens), 90 minutes = 1 h 30 min (time). If a child is stuck, the group may give a hint, not the answer. Second round if time: start with 8 × 7 and change the steps.
