Math Inquirers Level 3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 8. Relay chain, made safe: the TUTOR reads each card; volunteers say the answer (a pass is always fine, and the tutor or the group gives it). The chain never depends on one child: if an answer is wrong the tutor says "Let us check together" and writes the correct number on screen, and the chain carries on from the correct number. No child is named as the one who broke the chain. Click to show each answer after it is said. Chain: 6 × 9 = 54 (tables), 54 + 46 = 100 (adding to the next hundred), 100 × 7 = 700, 700 − 250 = 450, 450 ÷ 5 = 90 (think 45 tens ÷ 5 = 9 tens), 90 minutes = 1 h 30 min (time). Second round if time: start with 8 × 7 and change the steps.
