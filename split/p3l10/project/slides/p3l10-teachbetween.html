Draw it: people in between

Mei is 2nd from the front. Ben is last. There are 3 people between them. How many in the queue?

Step 1. In front of Mei: 1 person. Then Mei, then 3 people, then Ben.

1 + 1 + 3 + 1 = 6 people. Count your drawing to check.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q3). Ask first: "Is it 2 + 3 = 5?" No: that forgets Ben. Click the drawing, the step and the answer. Think-aloud: "I draw Mei 2nd, so 1 person in front. Then the 3 between, then Ben. Count every person: 6." The worksheet Q3b has different names; let children draw it themselves.
