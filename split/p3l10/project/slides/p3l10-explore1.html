Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Two stations, about 4 minutes each: List, then Measure. The timer is a guide, not a race.
  2. Work in pairs. Read the puzzle card aloud. You may pass on a station.
  3. Before you solve it, decide: draw it, list it or make a table?
  4. Stuck on the ribbon? Draw 3 pieces: a short one, then 10 cm longer, then 10 cm longer again.

Go further, any time: A 120 cm ribbon is cut into 4 pieces, each 10 cm longer than the last. How long is each?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10.5 to 22.5. Two stations only, so that the rotation fits online: each station is 4 minutes (the List station has only 6 numbers and the ribbon answers are 30, 40 and 50 cm), and 9 minutes of pair work includes the switch. Share the List card first (4 minutes), then the Measure card (4 minutes), and switch with a timer on the slide. The timer is a guide, not a race: say so out loud, do not count down aloud, and do not use words like hurry or quickly. A pair may pass on a station, or write how far they got; praise the thinking, not the speed. The debrief on the next slide is question 1 only, plus the two answers. Visit every breakout room at least once during the two stations (about 1 minute per room). Ribbon scaffold (say it if a pair is stuck, do not tell the answer): draw the ribbon as 3 boxes, the short piece, then a piece with a 10 cm extra, then a piece with a 20 cm extra; take the extras (10 + 20) away from 120 cm. Pairs stay in their pre-assigned breakout room. List: make every 3-digit number from the cards 3, 6, 9 (there are 6). Measure: a 120 cm ribbon is cut into 3 pieces, each piece 10 cm longer than the one before. Draw it and find each piece (30 cm, 40 cm, 50 cm). The graph and logic puzzles come next on worksheet page 1. Online: one station card on screen at a time, then report back. GO FURTHER (on the slide from the start of explore, so nobody waits): A 120 cm ribbon is cut into 4 pieces, each 10 cm longer than the last. How long is each? Answer: 15 cm, 25 cm, 35 cm and 45 cm (take away 10 + 20 + 30 = 60, 60 cm left, 60 / 4 = 15). Any pair may choose it as soon as their first task is done; do not say who is ready for it. Online: pairs are in pre-assigned breakout rooms (set up before the lesson, auto-move in and auto-close with a timer, about 45 seconds per move). Visit each breakout room at least once while the pairs work (about 1 minute per room).
