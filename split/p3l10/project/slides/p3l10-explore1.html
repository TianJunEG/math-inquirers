Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Pick 2 of the 4 stations: Graph, Logic, List, Measure.
  2. Work in pairs. Read the puzzle card aloud.
  3. Before you solve it, decide: draw it, list it or make a table?
  4. Write the answer and how you started.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 25. Station cards: Graph: a bar graph of fruit sold; write 3 true facts (one "how many more", one "altogether"). Logic: a 3 by 3 clue card. List: make every 3-digit number from the cards 3, 6, 9 (there are 6). Measure: cut a 1 m 20 cm string into 3 pieces so each piece is 10 cm longer than the one before (30 cm, 40 cm, 50 cm). Pairs spend about 7 minutes at each of their 2 stations. Online: one station card per breakout pair, then report back.
