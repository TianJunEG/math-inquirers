Challenge

1. In a queue, Siti is 6th from the front and Ravi is 4th from the back. There are 3 children between them, and Siti is in front of Ravi. How many children are in the queue?

2. A bar graph shows paper collected by 4 classes. 3P1 collected 10 kg more than 3P2. 3P3 collected the same as 3P1. 3P4 collected 5 kg less than 3P2. Altogether: 135 kg. How much did each class collect?

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use as a pair challenge or in talk time. Ask for the reason every time. Answers: 1) 13 children (5 in front of Siti, Siti, 3 between, Ravi, 3 behind Ravi: 5 + 1 + 3 + 1 + 3 = 13). 2) 3P1 40 kg, 3P2 30 kg, 3P3 40 kg, 3P4 25 kg (compare everything with 3P2: the extras are +10, +10 and −5, so 135 − 15 = 120 kg is 4 equal parts of 30 kg).
