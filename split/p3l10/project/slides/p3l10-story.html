Story break: Raymond Smullyan

USA (born in Far Rockaway, New York) • 1919 to 2017

Raymond Smullyan was born in New York in 1919. He was a mathematician, a logician, a magician and a pianist. He studied mathematics and logic on his own, and in 1959 he earned a doctorate at Princeton. He loved puzzles, and wrote many puzzle books, such as What Is the Name of This Book?, in 1978. In his famous puzzles, a knight always tells the truth and a knave always lies. You must use the clues to work out who is who. He also used puzzles to introduce big ideas in logic to readers of all ages.

Ana says: 'Ben is a knave.' Ben says: 'We are both knights.' A knight always tells the truth and a knave always lies. Who is the knight and who is the knave?

ANSWER to the try-it (tutor only, do not show): Ana is a knight and Ben is a knave. If Ben were a knight, both would be knights, but then Ana's words 'Ben is a knave' would be a lie, which a knight never tells. So Ben is a knave, his words are false, and Ana's words are true, so she is a knight.
