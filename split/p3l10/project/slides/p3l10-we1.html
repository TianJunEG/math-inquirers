28.5–30 MIN

Draw it: a queue

Siti is 4th from the front and 6th from the back of a queue. How many people are in the queue?

Step 1. 4th from the front: 3 people in front of Siti.

Step 2. 6th from the back: 5 people behind Siti.

3 + 1 + 5 = 9 people. (4 + 6 = 10 counts Siti twice: 10 − 1 = 9)

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example: draw it. Ask first: "Is it 10?" Many children add 4 + 6. Click: the 3 people in front appear, then the 5 behind, then the steps and answer. (Click order: front people, back people, step 1, step 2, answer.) Think-aloud: "I draw Siti. 4th from the front means 3 people are in front of her. 6th from the back means 5 people behind her. Count: 3 + 1 + 5 = 9. If I add 4 + 6, I count Siti twice, so I take 1 away." Check by counting the drawing from both ends.
