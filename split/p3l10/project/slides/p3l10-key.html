Key idea: how to start a puzzle

Stuck? Draw it, list it in order, or make a table. Then check every clue.

Logic grid: a ✓ means crosses in the rest of its row and column. Bar graph: read the scale first.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Queues: adding the two places and counting the person twice. (2) Bar graphs: counting spaces as 1 instead of reading the scale; missing the halfway values 45 and 55. (3) Logic grids: a tick without crossing out the rest of the row and column. (4) Digit cards: missing a number because the list is not in order. (5) Matchstick pattern: 4 sticks for every square (shared sticks are counted twice).
