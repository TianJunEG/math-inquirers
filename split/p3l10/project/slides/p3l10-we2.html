Make a table: a logic grid

Ben, Siti and Ravi each like a different snack. Clue 1: Ben does not like curry puff or popiah. Clue 2: Ravi does not like curry puff.

Clue 1: cross out 2 boxes for Ben. Only kaya toast is left: ✓.

A ✓ means crosses in the rest of its column: nobody else has kaya toast.

Clue 2: Ravi is not curry puff, so popiah ✓. Siti: curry puff ✓.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example: make a table. Click order: Ben’s two crosses, Ben’s tick, the crosses in the kaya toast column, the last marks; the three side notes rise in between as you talk. Think-aloud: "A table keeps every clue in one place. Clue 1 tells me 2 things Ben does NOT like: 2 crosses. Only one box is left in his row, so a tick. A tick means nobody else can have kaya toast, so I cross out the rest of that column. Clue 2: Ravi is not curry puff, and kaya toast is gone, so Ravi likes popiah. Siti gets the last one: curry puff." Check each clue against the answer.
