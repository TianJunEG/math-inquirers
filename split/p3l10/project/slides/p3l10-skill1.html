Optional slide for pairs who finish early or ask for more. Show it only on request and never announce who is working on it. Children write the answer on their own paper and say it together on three; nothing is held up to the camera. Answers and tutor lines are for you only. Item 1 (Picture patterns: each feature has its own cycle; tool of the day idea: Make a table). SAY: "Name each feature. Make a table with a row for the eyes and a row for the mouth." ASK: "How often do the eyes repeat? The mouth? When do they both start again?" LISTEN FOR: "Two cycles at the same time." ANSWER: The eyes repeat every 2 faces (open, closed). The mouth repeats every 3 faces (smile, flat, open). a) Face 9: open eyes, open mouth. b) Face 14: closed eyes, flat mouth. c) Face 7 (6 is the first place where both cycles start again).
