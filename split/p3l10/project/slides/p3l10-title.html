Puzzle Day

Mixed puzzles: bar graphs and logic

TODAY'S TOOL

You choose

Focus habit: Look back

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Choose a way to start a puzzle (draw it, list it, make a table), solve a logic grid with ticks and crosses, read and complete a bar graph with a scale, and explain the thinking. Heuristics taught: (1) draw it, list it or make a table to start; (2) a logic grid: a tick means crosses in the rest of its row and column. Materials: Puzzle cards for 2 stations (list, measure), digit cards 0 to 9 (or paper cards), squared paper, mini whiteboards, certificates, stamps. Online: Two stations, about 4 minutes each, with a timer on the screen for the switch. The timer is a guide, not a race: there is no rush, and a pair may pass on a station. Pairs are in pre-assigned breakout rooms (auto-move in, auto-close with a timer), and the tutor visits every room at least once. The tutor shares the next station card when the 4 minutes are up. No physical materials beyond paper. Logic grids and graphs are shared on screen and annotated together. Time plan (90 minutes, with a named 3-minute float inside talk time): opening 0 to 1.5, sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 4.5 (own paper, nobody marked, one show-me for both), warm-up game 4.5 to 7.5 (two rounds) and three quick questions 7.5 to 10.5, explore 10.5 to 27 (a Go further what-if is on the explore slide from the start; pairs go to pre-assigned breakout rooms), teaching 27 to 36.5 (how to start a puzzle: draw it, make a table, read the scale; click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 36.5 to 41.5, worksheet 41.5 to 71 (page 1 in pairs, short teaching, page 2 on their own, a 60-second self-check slide after each page; after question 3 of page 1 any child may choose Think harder or the Investigation; from minute 68 everyone chooses), talk time 71 to 77 (volunteers first, pass is fine), exit ticket 77 to 82, look back 82 to 85, stamp and close 85 to 90. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child; the tutor never announces who is on which page and invites the whole group, never one child. If the class is behind, children finish the last question of each page at home; the check slides cover it. SPINE: Today you choose the tool. Point at the row of eight tools on page 1 of the workbook. HABIT: Focus habit: Look back. Ask what was learnt and what they would change, after the work is done. Say: "What did you learn? What would you do differently next time?" Do not give the answer first: ask the prompt, wait, and give a hint only if the child is stuck.
