LESSON 10 • WEEK 5

Challenge Day

Mixed puzzles: bar graphs and logic

TODAY WE WILL

Choose how to start a puzzle, solve a logic grid, read a bar graph with a scale, and explain our thinking.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Choose a way to start a puzzle (draw it, list it, make a table), solve a logic grid with ticks and crosses, read and complete a bar graph with a scale, and explain the thinking. Heuristics taught: (1) draw it, list it or make a table to start; (2) a logic grid: a tick means crosses in the rest of its row and column. Materials: Puzzle cards for 4 stations (graph, logic, list, measure), digit cards 0 to 9, a 1 m 20 cm string and a ruler, squared paper, mini whiteboards, certificates, stamps. Online: Each breakout pair gets one station card on screen and reports back; logic grids and graphs are shared on screen and annotated together. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (how to start a puzzle: draw it, make a table, read the scale; click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket and certificates 80 to 90.
