Math Inquirers Level 3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6, the party tables). Click to build each row while children say the numbers. Think-aloud: "1 bench: 3 + 4 = 7 children, but 4 on a bench holds only 4. Not the same. 2 benches: 6 + 4 = 10 and 8. Closer." Point out the pattern: each extra bench takes one more of the standing children, so the gap shrinks by 1 each row. 4 standing children means 4 benches. Check: 4 × 3 + 4 = 16 and 4 × 4 = 16.
