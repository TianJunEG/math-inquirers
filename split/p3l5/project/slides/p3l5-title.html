Remainder Riddles

Division

TODAY WE WILL

Divide with leftovers, decide what the leftover means in a story, work backwards, and crack “what number am I?” riddles with a list.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Divide with remainders, check that the remainder is smaller than the number we divide by, decide what the leftover means in a story (one more group, or leave it out), work backwards from groups and leftovers, and solve "what number am I?" riddles by making a list. Heuristics taught today: WORK BACKWARDS (start number = groups × size + leftover) and MAKE A LIST (list the numbers that fit one clue, then test each with the other clue). Materials: 25 counters per child (or beans or buttons), 6 small cups for groups, mini whiteboards and markers. Online: children use 25 small things at home (dried beans, buttons, LEGO bricks) and a few cups or bowls; whiteboards are held up to the camera. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90. Stretch and Challenge slides are for early finishers.
