LESSON 5 • WEEK 3 • 0–1.5 MIN

Remainder Riddles

Division

TODAY WE WILL

Divide with leftovers, decide what the leftover means in a story, work backwards, and crack “what number am I?” riddles with a list.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Divide with remainders, check that the remainder is smaller than the number we divide by, decide what the leftover means in a story (one more group, or leave it out), work backwards from groups and leftovers, and solve "what number am I?" riddles by making a list. Heuristics taught today: WORK BACKWARDS (start number = groups × size + leftover) and MAKE A LIST (list the numbers that fit one clue, then test each with the other clue). Materials: 25 counters per child (or beans or buttons), 6 small cups for groups, mini whiteboards and markers. Online: children use 25 small things at home (dried beans, buttons, LEGO bricks) and a few cups or bowls; children write on whiteboards. Time plan (90 minutes, with a named 3-minute float inside talk time): opening 0 to 1.5, sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 5 (own paper, nobody marked, one show-me for both), warm-up game 5 to 8 (two rounds) and three quick questions 8 to 11, explore 11 to 25.5 (a Go further what-if is on the explore slide from the start; pairs go to pre-assigned breakout rooms), teaching 25.5 to 36.5 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 36.5 to 41.5, worksheet 41.5 to 72.5 (page 1 in pairs, short teaching, page 2 on their own, a 60-second self-check slide after each page; after question 3 of page 1 any child may choose Think harder or the Investigation; from minute 69.5 everyone chooses), talk time 72.5 to 79.5 (volunteers first, pass is fine), exit ticket 79.5 to 85, look back 85 to 88, stamp and close 88 to 90. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child; the tutor never announces who is on which page and invites the whole group, never one child. If the class is behind, children finish the last question of each page at home; the check slides cover it.
