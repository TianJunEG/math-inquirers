41.5–43.5 MIN

Work backwards: groups × size + leftover

HEURISTIC: WORK BACKWARDS

Mei packs cookies into bags of 6. She fills 9 bags and has 4 cookies left. How many cookies does she have?

Step 1. 9 bags of 6: 9 × 6 = 54

Step 2. Add the leftover: 54 + 4 = 58 cookies

Step 3. Check forwards: 58 ÷ 6 = 9 r 4 ✓

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 1 (Q3 works backwards from bags and a leftover, then repacks). Heuristic: WORK BACKWARDS. Say: "Division splits the cookies into bags and a leftover. To go back, we put the bags together, groups × size, and add the leftover." Think-aloud: "9 bags of 6 is 54. Plus the 4 loose ones: 58. I check by dividing again: 58 ÷ 6 = 9 r 4. That matches the story." Ask: "Why must the leftover be less than 6?" (otherwise she could fill another bag).
