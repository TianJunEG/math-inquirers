EXPLORE

  1. Take 25 counters.
  2. Put them in groups of 2, then 3, 4, 5 and 6.
  3. Record the groups and the leftover in a table.
  4. Ask: what do you notice about the leftovers?

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Predict first: "Which group size will leave nothing over?" Pairs make the groups with real counters (cups help) and fill in the table on a whiteboard. Results: 2s: 12 groups, 1 left. 3s: 8 groups, 1 left. 4s: 6 groups, 1 left. 5s: 5 groups, 0 left. 6s: 4 groups, 1 left. Hints: "Use a times-table fact: 6 × 4 = 24." Finished early: "Try 49 counters. Which group sizes leave 1 over? Why?" (2, 3, 4, 6 and 8 all leave 1, because 48 is in all those tables; 7s leave 0.) Online: children use beans or buttons and hold the whiteboard to the camera.
