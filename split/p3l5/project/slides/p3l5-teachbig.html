The biggest remainder

Dividing by 6, the remainder can only be 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5.

A number between 30 and 40 is divided by 6. The remainder is as big as it can be. What is the number?

Step 1. The biggest remainder when ÷ 6 is 5 (one less than 6).

Step 2. Numbers in the 6 table near 30 to 40: 30 and 36. Add 5: 35 and 41.

Step 3. 41 is too big. The number is 35. Check: 35 ÷ 6 = 5 r 5 ✓
