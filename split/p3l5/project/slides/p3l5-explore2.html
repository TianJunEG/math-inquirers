Explore: talk about it

1. Which group size left nothing over?

2. Why did 2s, 3s, 4s and 6s all leave 1 over?

3. In groups of 4, can 4 or more be left over?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Let pairs keep working, then gather. Answers: 1) Groups of 5: 25 is in the 5 table. 2) 24 is in the 2, 3, 4 and 6 tables, and 25 is one more than 24. 3) No. If 4 were left over they would make another group. The leftover (remainder) is always smaller than the group size. This is the rule we use all lesson.
