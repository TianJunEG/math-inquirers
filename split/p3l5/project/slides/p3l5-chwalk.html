Q1: weeks are groups of 7

100 ÷ 7 = 14 r 2. After 14 weeks (98 days) it is Monday again.

Forward 2 more days: Tuesday, then Wednesday.

Backward: 14 weeks ago was Monday; 2 more days back: Sunday, then Saturday.

100 days from Monday is Wednesday. 100 days ago was Saturday ✓

Q2: compare the two shares

From 4 to 6 pens, each pupil gets 2 more.

That needs the 10 spare pens + 4 more = 14 pens.

14 ÷ 2 = 7 pupils.

Pens: 7 × 4 + 10 = 38. Check: 7 × 6 = 42 = 38 + 4 ✓

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use after pairs have tried. Q1: the remainder is what matters: full weeks bring us back to the same day. Many children count on 100 from Monday or say Tuesday; the table of days shows why it is 2 days on. For 100 days AGO, children often say Wednesday again: ask them to go backwards, so 2 days BEFORE Monday is Saturday. Q2: if children are stuck, act it out with 3 or 4 "pupils" and pens; a table (pupils 1, 2, 3 … with pens needed both ways) also works: 7 pupils is where 4 × 7 + 10 = 38 and 6 × 7 − 4 = 38 match.
