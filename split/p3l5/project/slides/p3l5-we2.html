Worked example: what does the leftover mean?

A van holds 8 people. 50 people go on a trip. How many vans are needed?

Step 1. 50 ÷ 8 = 6 r 2: 6 full vans, and 2 people are left over.

Step 2. The 2 people still need a ride, so we need one more van: 7 vans.

Now: 50 cakes are packed into boxes of 8. How many boxes are FULL?

Step 3. 50 ÷ 8 = 6 r 2 again, but only full boxes count: 6 boxes. Read the question!
