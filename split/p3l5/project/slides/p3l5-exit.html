EXIT TICKET

29 children go on a ride. Each car holds 4 children. How many cars are needed? Write the division and explain the leftover.

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 80 to 86. Answer: 29 ÷ 4 = 7 r 1. 8 cars are needed: the 1 child left over still needs a car. Extension: Find the smallest number bigger than 1 that leaves a remainder of 1 when divided by 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 (61). Children show the answer to the tutor on camera in the open group call, or keep it to share if they want (own paper). No photos and no private messages. Never mark or read out answers in front of the group. Reflection (look back) follows on the next slide, 2 minutes.
