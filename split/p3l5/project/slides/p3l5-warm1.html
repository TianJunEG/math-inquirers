Warm-up: Leftover Lucky Dip

WARM-UP GAME

I call some sweets and a group size. Show the number of groups on one hand and the leftover on the other.

17 in 3s

26 in 8s

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 8. Leftover Lucky Dip. Play two rounds only (the first two cards, then the last two). Call each card in turn: "23 sweets, groups of 4." Children show the number of groups on one hand and the leftover on the other (fists for zero). Then click to reveal. Always ask for the times-table fact: "4 × 5 = 20, and 3 more makes 23." Answers: 23 in 4s = 5 groups, 3 left; 17 in 3s = 5 groups, 2 left; 26 in 8s = 3 groups, 2 left; 41 in 6s = 6 groups, 5 left. Extra calls if time: 30 in 7s (4 groups, 2 left), 50 in 9s (5 groups, 5 left). Watch for children who say "41 in 6s is 5 groups and 11 left": ask "Can 11 make another group of 6?"
