Worked example: share with a leftover

Ali shares 38 sweets equally among 5 children. How many does each child get? How many are left over?

Step 1. Count in 5s: 5 × 7 = 35. 5 × 8 = 40 is too many. Each child gets 7.

Step 2. 38 − 35 = 3 left over. Is 3 smaller than 5? Yes. 38 ÷ 5 = 7 r 3

Step 3. Check: 7 × 5 + 3 = 35 + 3 = 38 ✓

Step 4. How many more sweets so that none are left over? 40 − 38 = 2 more.
