The remainder rule

14 counters in groups of 4. Which picture is right?

3 groups, 2 left over. 14 ÷ 4 = 3 r 2 ✓

2 groups, 6 left over: not this one. 4 of the 6 make another group!

The leftover must be SMALLER than the group size. If it is not, make another group.

Math Inquirers Level 3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Show 14 counters and make groups of 4 in front of the children. Click 1: the wrong picture (2 groups, 6 left). Ask: "What is wrong?" Click 2: the rule. Think-aloud: "When I divide, I keep making groups until I cannot make another one. So what is left must be smaller than the group size. Dividing by 4, the remainder can only be 0, 1, 2 or 3."
