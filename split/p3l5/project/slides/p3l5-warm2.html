Minutes 8 to 10. Write "r" for remainder. Whiteboards up together, then click. After each one ask: "How can we check?" (answer × divisor + remainder: 7 × 6 + 3 = 45). Answers: 1) 3 r 2 2) 4 r 2 3) 7 r 3 4) 6 r 2 5) 7 r 0, nothing left over because 63 is in the 9 table.
