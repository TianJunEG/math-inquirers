Think harder

1. Siti has between 40 and 50 beads. In groups of 6, 1 is left over. In groups of 4, 3 are left over. How many beads?

2. A number ÷ 9 = 8 r 7. What is the number ÷ 8?

3. Ali packs 60 cupcakes into boxes of 8. How many more cupcakes does he need to fill one more box?

For children who choose Think harder. Answers: 1) 43 (remainder 1 when ÷ 6: 43 and 49; 43 ÷ 4 = 10 r 3 ✓, 49 ÷ 4 = 12 r 1). 2) 9 r 7 (work backwards: 9 × 8 + 7 = 79; 79 ÷ 8 = 9 r 7). 3) 4 more (60 ÷ 8 = 7 r 4; the next box needs 64).
