What number am I? Make a list

HEURISTIC: MAKE A LIST

I am between 20 and 40. Divided by 3, my remainder is 2. Divided by 5, my remainder is 4. Who am I?

Step 1. List the clue with the BIGGER divisor first (a shorter list). Remainder 4 when ÷ 5: 24, 29, 34, 39

Step 2. Test each one with ÷ 3: 24 r 0, 29 r 2 ✓, 34 r 1, 39 r 0

Step 3. Only 29 fits both clues. For “find ALL”, keep every number that fits.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4). Heuristic: MAKE A LIST. Think-aloud: "Two clues. I list the numbers for one clue, then test them with the other. The 5 clue gives a shorter list: 5 × 4 = 20, plus 4 is 24, then every 5 more: 29, 34, 39. Now test with 3." Stress: write the list in order so nothing is missed. If a question says "find ALL", do not stop at the first one. Check 29: 3 × 9 = 27, remainder 2 ✓; 5 × 5 = 25, remainder 4 ✓.
