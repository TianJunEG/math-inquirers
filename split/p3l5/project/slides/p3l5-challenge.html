1. Today is Monday. What day of the week will it be 100 days from today?

2. Mr Lim gives pens to his pupils. If each pupil gets 4 pens, 10 pens are left over. If each pupil gets 6 pens, he is 4 pens short. How many pupils are there? How many pens does he have?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) Wednesday (100 ÷ 7 = 14 r 2). 2) 7 pupils and 38 pens.
