1. Today is Monday. What day of the week will it be 100 days from today? What day of the week was it 100 days ago?

2. Mr Lim gives pens to his pupils. If each pupil gets 4 pens, 10 pens are left over. If each pupil gets 6 pens, he is 4 pens short. How many pupils are there? How many pens does he have?

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) Wednesday in 100 days (100 ÷ 7 = 14 r 2, so 2 days after Monday). Saturday 100 days ago (14 weeks back is Monday, then 2 more days back: Sunday, Saturday). 2) 7 pupils and 38 pens.
