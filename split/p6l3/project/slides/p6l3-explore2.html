Minutes 16 to 26.5. Let pairs keep working, then gather them to answer. Answers: 1) x = −150 (100 − 250: from 100 back to 0 is 100, then 150 more). 2) −13 (5, then 2, −1, −4, −7, −10, −13: six jumps of 3 down). Many predict −12 or −18: ask them to count the jumps on a number line. 3) Change it by a negative number: "change temp by −3" moves it 3 to the left. Adding a negative is the same as taking away.
