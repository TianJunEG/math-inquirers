26.5–28.5 MIN

The number line: right is bigger

Numbers get bigger as you go right and smaller as you go left. Below zero, the further left, the colder.

Step 1. −6 is further left than −1, so −6 < −1. (Think: −6 °C is colder.)

Step 2. Which is colder, −2 °C or −8 °C? −8 is further left, so −8 °C is colder.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 26.5 to 28.5. Teach. Click 1: the three points. Ask: "Which is biggest? Which is smallest?" Then the steps. Think-aloud: "Six is bigger than one, but MINUS 6 is further below zero than minus 1. If I owe you $6, I am poorer than if I owe you $1." Use the signs: < means "is less than", pointing at the smaller number. Common slip: ordering by the digits only.
