Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Order and compare negative numbers on a number line; add and subtract with negative numbers by moving along the line (temperature, lifts, sea level); use "change by" a negative number in code and trace a variable that goes below 0. Materials: laptop or desktop with Scratch 3 open (scratch.mit.edu, no log-in needed), mini whiteboard and marker, workbook, pencil. Tutor: this deck, Scratch open for screen share. Online set-up: children keep Scratch in one browser tab and the video call in another; ask them to rename the sprite "Dot" so nobody is distracted by costumes. Children share their screen only when asked. Type the minus sign with the keyboard minus key (the hyphen); do not copy and paste a number from the slide or chat, because the slide uses a different minus character that Scratch will not accept. Block colours on our slides are simplified; in Scratch, find blocks by the category name on the left, not by colour. Time plan (the exact minutes of this lesson, with a standing flex buffer): opening 0 to 2, sneak-peek puzzles 2 to 5.5, warm-up 5.5 to 11.5, explore at the computer 11.5 to 26.5, mini-lesson 26.5 to 36.5 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 36.5 to 41.5, worksheet 41.5 to 74.5 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, flex 74.5 to 77.5 (unscripted: use it for Think harder or catch-up; it is lost first), talk time 77.5 to 83.5, exit slip 83.5 to 86, looking back 86 to 88, close and stamp 88 to 90. SPINE: Today's tool is Draw it. Sketch and label a model; let the picture show what stays the same. Look for where you use it. Point at the card on page 1 of the workbook. HABIT: Focus habit: Try. Ask the child to test one example, or a smaller case, before deciding. Say: "Pick one example and test it. What happens?" Do not give the answer first: ask the prompt, wait, and give a hint only if the child is stuck.
