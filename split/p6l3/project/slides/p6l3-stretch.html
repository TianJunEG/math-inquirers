For early finishers

Stretch

1. At 6 a.m. it was −4 °C. By 2 p.m. it was 7 °C. By 10 p.m. it had fallen 9 degrees. How much did it rise by 2 p.m.? What was it at 10 p.m.?

2. set n to 10, then repeat 5 times: change n by −4. Then say n. What does it say?

3. What number is exactly halfway between −9 and 3?

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) It rose 11 degrees (4 up to 0, then 7). At 10 p.m.: 7 − 9 = −2 °C. 2) −10 (10 − 4 × 5 = 10 − 20 = −10). 3) −3: the gap is 12, half is 6, and −9 + 6 = −3 (check: 3 − 6 = −3).
