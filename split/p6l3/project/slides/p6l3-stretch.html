Think harder

1. A sprite starts at x = a. It runs “change x by −8” and then “change x by 5”. It ends at x = −1. What was a? Work backwards from the end, and check by running it forwards.

2. A diver is at −14 m. A seagull is flying at 9 m. How far apart are they?

3. Use the numbers −2, 3 and 5 once each to fill the gaps in ____ − ____ − ____. What is the biggest answer you can make? What is the smallest?

4. City A is 5 degrees colder than city B. City B is 3 degrees warmer than city C. City C is at −4 °C. Find the temperature of B and of A.
