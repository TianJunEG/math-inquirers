3 MIN CHECK

Warm-up check

Two new puzzles. Whiteboard: your answer AND one reason. Hold up together.

Q1. A freezer is at −18 °C and a fridge is at 4 °C. How many degrees warmer is the fridge than the freezer?

22 degrees. From −18 up to 0 is 18, then 4 more: 18 + 4 = 22. A number line drawing shows it.

Q2. A sprite is at x = −40. It runs “change x by 25”, then “change x by −60”. Where does it end up?

x = −75. −40 + 25 = −15, then −15 − 60 = −75. The sprite is left of zero, and further left than where it started.
