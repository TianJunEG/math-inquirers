Key idea: move along the number line

Right is bigger. Adding a positive moves right. Adding a negative moves left. a − b is the jump from b to a.

In code, "change by −3" makes a variable go down by 3.

Math Inquirers Level 6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Ordering negatives by their digits (saying −9 > −4). (2) Crossing zero wrongly: 3 − 8 answered as 5 or −11 (split the jump at 0). (3) In code, using "change by 1" when the variable should go down, or counting the start value as one of the repeats when tracing.
