Minutes 5 to 10. Whiteboards: write, hold up together, then click. Answers: 1) −2 °C (3 down to 0, then 2 more). 2) −7 °C (further below 0). 3) −3, −6 (the pattern keeps going past 0). 4) −3. 5) B3.
