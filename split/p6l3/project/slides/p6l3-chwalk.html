Q1: zero magic square

A line through the middle pairs opposites: 3 and −3, so 0 goes in the middle.

4 can not go in a corner (a corner needs 3 pairs that make −4; there are only 2).

Put 4 and −4 in the middle of the left and right sides. Fill the rest: −3 2 1 / 4 0 −4 / −1 −2 3

Q2: two kinds of hop

Midnight to 7 a.m.: 7 hops of +2 = +14.

−6 + 14 = 8 °C at 7 a.m. (6 up to 0, then 8 more).

7 a.m. to 10 a.m.: 3 hops of +3 = +9. 8 + 9 = 17 °C ✓
