Subtracting: the jump from b to a

How much warmer is 7 °C than −3 °C? That is 7 − (−3).

Step 1. a − b asks: how far is it from b to a? Start at −3, finish at 7.

Step 2. −3 up to 0 is 3. 0 up to 7 is 7. That is 10 steps to the RIGHT, so the answer is +10.

7 − (−3) = 10. Check: −3 + 10 = 7 ✓

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click order: the jumps, step 1, step 2, the answer. Think-aloud: "Subtraction finds the difference. 10 − 4 = 6 because from 4 to 10 is 6. The same works below zero: from −3 to 7 is 10." Right means a positive answer, left means a negative answer: 2 − 5 is the jump from 5 to 2, 3 steps LEFT, so −3. Second example if time: −2 − (−8) is the jump from −8 to −2: 6 right, so 6. Do NOT teach "two minuses make a plus" as a rule today; let them see it.
