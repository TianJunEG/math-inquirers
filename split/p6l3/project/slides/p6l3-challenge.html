1. Put −4, −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 in a 3 by 3 grid so that every row, every column and both diagonals add up to 0.

2. At midnight it was −6 °C. It rose 2 degrees every hour until 7 a.m., then 3 degrees every hour. What was the temperature at 10 a.m.?

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) One answer: top row −3, 2, 1; middle row 4, 0, −4; bottom row −1, −2, 3 (0 must be in the middle; there are 8 answers, all turns and flips of this one). 2) 17 °C: midnight to 7 a.m. is 7 hours, −6 + 14 = 8 °C; 7 a.m. to 10 a.m. is 3 hours, 8 + 9 = 17 °C.
