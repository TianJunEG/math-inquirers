Warm-up: cold city line-up

7 a.m. on a December morning. Line the cities up from coldest to warmest.

Singapore

25 °C

Tokyo

3 °C

Seoul

−7 °C

Beijing

−9 °C

Harbin

−22 °C

Coldest to warmest: Harbin −22, Beijing −9, Seoul −7, Tokyo 3, Singapore 25

Then: How many degrees warmer is Tokyo than Seoul?

10 degrees

Math Inquirers Level 6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 8. Cold city line-up. Give each child a city (online: type the city names in the chat in order, coldest first, then reveal). Ask: "Which is colder, Beijing at −9 or Seoul at −7? Nine is bigger than seven, so why is −9 colder?" Let them argue before any click. Click 1 puts the cities on the number line (Har = Harbin, Bei = Beijing, Seo = Seoul, Tok = Tokyo, Sin = Singapore). Click 2 shows the order. Click 3 shows the gap Tokyo to Seoul: from −7 up to 0 is 7, then up to 3 is 3 more, 10 degrees. Extra: "How much warmer is Singapore than Harbin?" (22 up to 0, then 25 more: 47 degrees). Watch for children who say −9 is warmer than −7 because 9 > 7: we fix this with the number line in a moment.
