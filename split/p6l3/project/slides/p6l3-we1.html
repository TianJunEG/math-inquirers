Worked example: up and down the line

It is −4 °C at 6 a.m. It rises 9 degrees by noon. Then it falls 7 degrees by night.

Step 1. Rise 9: from −4, 4 up to 0, then 5 more. Noon: 5 °C. (−4 + 9 = 5)

Step 2. Fall 7: from 5, 5 down to 0, then 2 more. Night: −2 °C. (5 − 7 = −2)

Math Inquirers Level 6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click order: the +9 jump, the −7 jump, step 1, step 2. Ask before each jump: "Which way do we move? Where do we cross zero?" Think-aloud: "Rising means moving right. From −4 it takes 4 to reach 0. I still have 5 of my 9 left, so I land on 5. Falling means moving left: 5 to get down to 0, 2 more to −2." Splitting the jump at 0 is the key move.
