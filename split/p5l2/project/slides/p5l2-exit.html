EXIT TICKET

(a) 4 pizzas are shared equally among 5 children. How much does each child get?

(b) Ali had 60 marbles. He gave 1/3 of them to Mei and 1/2 of the remainder to Ben. How many marbles did Ali have left?

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Minutes 82 to 86. Answers: (a) 4/5 of a pizza. (b) 20 marbles: 1/3 of 60 = 20 to Mei, 40 left, half of 40 = 20 to Ben, 20 left. Extension: write 1 as the sum of three different unit fractions (1/2 + 1/3 + 1/6, the only way).
