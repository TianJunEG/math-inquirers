Explore: fair shares

EXPLORE

  1. Take 3 paper pizzas. Share them equally among 4 friends.
  2. How much does each friend get? Write it as a fraction.
  3. Can you cut so that each friend gets only 2 pieces?
  4. Now try 5 pizzas among 6 friends, with only 2 pieces each.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 9 to 14. Pairs with paper circles (online: each child cuts their own). Ask them to predict first: will each friend get more or less than half a pizza? Let them find any fair way, then push for "only 2 pieces each". Early finishers: 5 pizzas among 6 friends with only 2 pieces each.
