9–14 MIN

Explore: fair shares

EXPLORE

  1. Take 3 paper pizzas. Share them equally among 4 friends.
  2. How much does each friend get? Write it as a fraction.
  3. Can you cut so that each friend gets only 2 pieces?
  4. Now try 5 pizzas among 6 friends, with only 2 pieces each.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 9 to 14. Pairs with paper circles (online: each child cuts their own). Ask them to predict first: will each friend get more or less than half a pizza? Let them find any fair way, then push for "only 2 pieces each". Anyone who wants more (open to every pair): 5 pizzas among 6 friends with only 2 pieces each. PROTECTED THINK TIME: before the pairs touch anything, say "Think for 30 seconds before anyone speaks" and show the clock; then pairs talk and start. GO FURTHER (say it at minute 9): any pair that finishes step 4 early may try the what-if question on the next slide. It is open to every pair, not a reward. Answer: 4/5, 5/6 and 6/7: the share grows each time (0.8, 0.83, 0.86). Each child is 1/5, 1/6, 1/7 short of a whole pizza, a smaller piece each time, so the shares creep up towards 1 pizza but never reach it.
