Cards 1 2 3 5: make the SMALLEST sum of two fractions ▢▢ + ▢▢.

Step 1. Big digits on the bottom make small pieces. Try 1 and 2 on top: 13 + 25 = 515 + 615 = 1115

Step 2. But test the other way too: 15 + 23 = 315 + 1015 = 1315 (bigger)

Smallest sum: 13 + 25 = 1115. Largest sum: 51 + 32 = 6 12.

Put the big digits on the bottom for the smallest sum, and on the top for the largest.
