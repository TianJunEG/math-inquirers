Q4: 1/2 + 1/6 = 2/3, + 1/12 = 3/4, + 1/20 = 4/5; each sum is 1 take away the next unit fraction (1 − 1/3, 1 − 1/4, 1 − 1/5), because a unit fraction minus the next unit fraction is 1 over (the number times the next number), for example 1/9 − 1/10 = 1/90. So the sum up to 1/90 (9 × 10) is 1 − 1/10 = 9/10. Q5: 10 equal units; game 4, remainder 6, book 3, left 3 units = $36, so 1 unit is $12, total $120, book $36.
