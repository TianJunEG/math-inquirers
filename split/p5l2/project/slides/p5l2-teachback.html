Working backwards with a model

Ali spent 13 of his money on a book and 12 of the remainder on lunch. He had $15 left. How much did he have at first?

Step 1. Book: 1 of 3 parts. Remainder: 2 parts. Lunch: half of the remainder = 1 part. Left: 1 part.

Step 2. The part left is $15, so 1 part = $15. 3 parts = $45.

Check: book $15, lunch $15, left $15. Total $45 ✓

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5). Start from the END of the story: what do we know? The $15 is what is left, so we know the size of one unit. Ask: "Why is the lunch exactly the same size as what is left?" (half of the remainder is spent, so half is left). Common error: working with 1/3 + 1/2 = 5/6 as if both were fractions of the whole. Draw the model first, then work out one unit.
