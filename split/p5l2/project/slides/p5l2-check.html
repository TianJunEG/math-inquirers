1. Ali says 3 ÷ 4 and 4 ÷ 3 are the same. Is he right? Use 3 pizzas shared by 4 friends, and 4 pizzas shared by 3 friends, to explain.

No. 3 ÷ 4 = 3/4 of a pizza each (less than one pizza); 4 ÷ 3 = 4/3 = 1 1/3 pizzas each (more than one pizza).

2. Mei spends 1/3 of her money on a book and then 1/2 of the money she has LEFT on lunch. What fraction of her money has she spent altogether? Draw a bar.

2/3. Left after the book 2/3; half of that is 1/3 (lunch); 1/3 + 1/3 = 2/3.
