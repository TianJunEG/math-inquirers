Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 4 to 9. Read each aloud, whiteboards up together, then click to show. 1) The top is what is shared, the bottom is how many share. 2) 4/6 + 1/6 = 5/6. 3) 20 ÷ 4 = 5, × 3 = 15. 4) 6/20 = 3/10. 5) Cut each of the 4 parts in half: 4/10 = 2/5, or 4 ÷ 2 = 2 on top. Ask: "Who added the bottoms in question 2?" Answers: 3/4, 5/6, 15, 3/10, 2/5.
