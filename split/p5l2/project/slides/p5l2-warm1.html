6.5–9.5 MIN

Warm-up: Fraction Face-off

Which fraction is bigger? One finger for the first, two fingers for the second. Whisper a reason to your partner. Volunteers share.

Clues to use: same top number? Close to 1? More than a half? More than a whole?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6.5 to 9.5. Fraction Face-off. Point to one pair at a time; children show 1 or 2 fingers (nobody has to explain a finger), then pairs whisper a reason and ONLY volunteers share it; never ask a child because of the finger they showed. Answers and reasons: 3/5 is bigger than 3/7 (same top number: fewer, bigger pieces, fifths are bigger than sevenths). 6/7 is bigger than 5/6 (5/6 is 1/6 short of 1, 6/7 is only 1/7 short, and 1/7 is smaller than 1/6). 1/2 is bigger than 4/9 (4/9 is less than 4.5/9). 8/7 is bigger than 7/8 (8/7 is more than 1 and 7/8 is less than 1). Listen for the reasons that volunteers give. Praise "compare to a half" and "how far from 1" as strategies; these come back in the lesson.
