E2. 5 pizzas are shared equally among 12 children. Each child must get exactly 2 pieces, and each piece must be a unit fraction of a pizza. How can the pizzas be cut? (Find two ways.)

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional, if you finish early (no clock time): show it only while some pairs are working or have finished, never as a group slide at a fixed minute. Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) 5/14: each fraction is a difference ((1/2 − 1/3) + (1/3 − 1/4) + (1/4 − 1/5) + (1/5 − 1/6) + (1/6 − 1/7)), the middle parts cancel, leaving 1/2 − 1/7 = 5/14. 2) Each child gets 5/12. Two ways: 1/3 + 1/12 (cut 4 pizzas into thirds and 1 pizza into twelfths: 12 thirds and 12 twelfths) or 1/4 + 1/6 (cut 3 pizzas into quarters and 2 pizzas into sixths: 12 of each).
