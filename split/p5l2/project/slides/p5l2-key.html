Minutes 36.5 to 38. Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Taking the second fraction of the whole amount instead of the remainder. (2) Adding tops and bottoms, 1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5, or using the same unit fraction twice when the question says different. (3) Writing 3 ÷ 4 as 4/3, or multiplying instead of dividing when a fraction is shared.
