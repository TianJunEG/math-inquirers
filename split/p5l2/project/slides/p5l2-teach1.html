Fraction and division

2 pizzas shared among 5 friends: 2 ÷ 5 = 25. And 35 ÷ 2 = ?

Each friend gets 15 + 15 = 25.

Step 1. The top number is what is shared. The bottom number is how many share.

Step 2. 35 ÷ 2: cut each fifth in half. 3 fifths become 6 tenths, shared in 2 groups: 3 tenths. So 35 ÷ 2 = 310.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Draw the two pizzas cut in fifths. "Each friend takes one slice from each pizza: 1/5 + 1/5 = 2/5. So 2 ÷ 5 = 2/5." Then the second idea: a fraction divided by a whole number. Draw a bar of 5 parts with 3 shaded; cut every part in half so there are 10 small parts and 6 shaded; two friends share the 6 shaded parts: 3 each, 3/10. Say: "Dividing by 2 makes each piece twice as small, so the bottom number doubles: 3/5 ÷ 2 = 3/10." Check: 3/10 × 2 = 6/10 = 3/5. Ask: "What is 3/4 ÷ 3?" (1/4: shortcut, the top 3 can be shared in 3 groups of 1.) This is a P6 skill: children who have not met it may need the picture twice.
