Minutes 86 to 88. EXIT SLIP AND REFLECTION, 2 minutes. Children use the small box at the bottom of the Investigation and reflection page: Something I did well; The hardest part was; What helped me (tick boxes include "I asked for help" and "I am still stuck (we will look at it together next lesson, in class)"); Next time I will try. Pass is always OK. Never collected in public. It is never collected or shown in public. You may read it afterwards to plan the next lesson. Never read a child's reflection aloud; only a child who volunteers may share. FOLLOW-UP FOR "I AM STILL STUCK": it happens in the NEXT lesson, in the open session, at a fixed time: minutes 37 to 49 (page 1 pair work). Visit those children FIRST, in their shared breakout room with the camera on and a parent nearby (face to face: at their table, in the open room), and go through the idea they ticked with one worked example. Before that, in the warm-up (minutes 3 to 9) you may re-teach the most-ticked idea to the whole group. All contact with children stays in the open session. Praise strategy, not speed: "Drawing the bar again when it did not work is exactly right." At 88 stamp the inquiry journals and say goodbye.
