Worked example: fraction of a remainder

Mei had 180 beads. She used 13 of them for a bracelet and 34 of the remainder for a necklace. How many beads were left?

Step 1. Bar in 3 parts: bracelet 1 part, remainder 2 parts. Cut the remainder into 4 small units: the whole bar is 6 units.

Step 2. Bracelet 2 units. Necklace 34 of 4 units = 3 units. Left: 1 unit.

6 units = 180, so 1 unit = 30. 30 beads were left.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The fraction of WHAT? Draw the bar as you go (online: use the slide, then children draw their own). Think-aloud: "A third of the beads is 1 part out of 3. The remainder is the other 2 parts. The necklace uses 3/4 of THAT, so I cut the remainder into 4 equal small units, which means I must cut the bracelet part into 2 so that every unit is the same size. Now the whole bar is 6 units." Common wrong answer: 3/4 of 180 = 135. Ask: "Is 3/4 of the whole the same as 3/4 of the remainder? Who has more, the bracelet or the necklace?" Check: bracelet 60, remainder 120, necklace 90, left 30. Total 60 + 90 + 30 = 180.
