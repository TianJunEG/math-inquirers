Look for a pattern

13 − 14 = 112 and 14 − 15 = 120. What is 15 − 16?

Step 1. Bottom numbers: 12 = 3 × 4 and 20 = 4 × 5. So 15 − 16 = 130 (5 × 6).

Step 2. Now add: 130 + 142 = (15 − 16) + (16 − 17).

Step 3. The 16 cancels: 15 − 17 = 735 − 535 = 235.

Each fraction is a difference of two unit fractions, so the middle parts cancel.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4). Ask first: "How do you think the bottom numbers are made?" (two neighbouring numbers multiplied: 3 × 4, 4 × 5). Think-aloud: "1/30 + 1/42 looks hard, but each one is a difference. The 1/6 is added in one and taken away in the other, so it disappears." Check: 1/30 + 1/42 = 7/210 + 5/210 = 12/210 = 2/35. Say: "Next you will meet a different set of fractions with the same trick."
