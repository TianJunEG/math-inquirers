Minutes 74 to 79.5. Pairs talk for 2 minutes, then share. Answer: Ben is wrong. 1/2 of a pizza is already more than 2/5, so adding 1/3 more cannot give 2/5. Cut into sixths: 1/2 = 3/6 and 1/3 = 2/6, so the total is 5/6. We can only add the tops when the pieces are the same size.
