Stretch

1. Which is bigger, 5/6 or 6/7? Explain without finding common denominators.

2. Find a fraction between 2/5 and 1/2 with 20 as the bottom number.

3. Which is bigger, 7/8 of 24 or 5/6 of 30?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 6/7: 5/6 is 1/6 short of 1 and 6/7 is only 1/7 short, and 1/7 is smaller than 1/6. 2) 9/20: 2/5 = 8/20 and 1/2 = 10/20. 3) 5/6 of 30 is bigger: 7/8 of 24 = 21 and 5/6 of 30 = 25.
