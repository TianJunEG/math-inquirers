For early finishers

Stretch

1. Ben ate 1/3 of a pizza. Ali ate 1/4 of what was left. What fraction of the pizza is left?

2. Which is bigger, 5/6 or 6/7? Explain without finding common denominators.

3. 4 litres of juice are poured equally into 6 bottles. How much juice is in each bottle?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 1/2. After Ben, 2/3 is left; Ali ate 1/4 of 2/3 = 1/6; 2/3 − 1/6 = 1/2. (Or: 3/4 of 2/3 = 1/2 is left.) 2) 6/7: 5/6 is 1/6 short of 1 and 6/7 is only 1/7 short, and 1/7 is smaller than 1/6. 3) 4/6 = 2/3 of a litre.
