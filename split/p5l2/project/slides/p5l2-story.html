Story break: Eugenia Cheng

born 1976 • England and the United States • A mathematician who explains big ideas with cake and baking.

Eugenia Cheng is a mathematician who was born in England in 1976 and studied maths at Cambridge University. Her research is in an area called category theory. She is well known for explaining maths with food. Her book How to Bake Pi came out in 2015, and each chapter opens with a dessert recipe. One example in the book is about three brothers and sisters trying to share a cake. Today she teaches maths to art students in Chicago. Cooking and baking use fractions all the time, such as half a cup of milk, a quarter of a cake or a third of the dough. Cheng shows that maths is not only for tests: it is also in the kitchen.
