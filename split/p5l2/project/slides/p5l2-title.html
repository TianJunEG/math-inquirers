LESSON 2 • WEEK 1

Fraction Feats

Four operations of fractions, fraction and division

TODAY WE WILL

Share pizzas fairly, write fractions as unit fractions, take a fraction of a remainder with a model and find patterns in fraction sums.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 2 (opening, inside the 90-minute clock). Objective: Link fractions and division (3 ÷ 4 = 3/4) and divide a proper fraction by a whole number; write fractions as the sum of two different unit fractions by testing in order; solve fraction-of-a-remainder problems forwards and backwards with a bar model; and spot and explain patterns in fraction sums. Thinking strategies taught today: DRAW A MODEL (bar or pizza), TEST IN ORDER and know when to stop, find the REMAINDER first, LOOK FOR A PATTERN, and test both ways. Materials: paper circles (about 6 per child) and scissors, a paper strip for bar models, digit cards 1 to 5, mini whiteboards, workbook. Online: children cut out their paper circles before the lesson and fold or cut them on camera; the tutor shares the slides and draws the bar models live; pairs use breakout rooms for the explore and page 1. Time plan (the exact minutes of this lesson; every lesson adds up to 90): opening 0 to 2, sneak-peek puzzles 2 to 6.5 (one hold-up for both puzzles, no volunteer round, nobody is marked), warm-up 6.5 to 13 (one game round, four quick questions), explore 13 to 27 (pair work 6 minutes at most, then two questions together; a Go further what-if is on the explore slide for any pair that finishes), teaching 27 to 38 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 38 to 42, workbook page 1 42 to 57.5 (the questions without a star; after question 2 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder), check together 57.5 to 61.5, short teaching before page 2 61.5 to 63, workbook page 2 63 to 71 (alone: start with the questions named at the top, then choose; starred parts are take-home; nobody has to finish), check together 71 to 74, talk time 74 to 79.5 (you may say pass; volunteers first; the first step of the Investigation is take-home), exit ticket 79.5 to 84, reflection 84 to 86.5 (pass is always OK), stamp typed in the chat and goodbye 86.5 to 87.5, and the buffer 87.5 to 90 (floating time: spend it first if you run late, never fill it on purpose). Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child and have no clock time. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and never splits a pair unless both children agree. Invite the WHOLE group, never one child: "when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation."
