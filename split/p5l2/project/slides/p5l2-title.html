Fraction Feats

Four operations of fractions, fraction and division

TODAY WE WILL

Share pizzas fairly, write fractions as unit fractions, take a fraction of a remainder with a model and find patterns in fraction sums.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Link fractions and division (3 ÷ 4 = 3/4) and divide a proper fraction by a whole number; write fractions as the sum of two different unit fractions by testing in order; solve fraction-of-a-remainder problems forwards and backwards with a bar model; and spot and explain patterns in fraction sums. Thinking strategies taught today: DRAW A MODEL (bar or pizza), TEST IN ORDER and know when to stop, find the REMAINDER first, LOOK FOR A PATTERN, and test both ways. Materials: paper circles (about 6 per child) and scissors, a paper strip for bar models, digit cards 1 to 5, mini whiteboards, workbook. Online: children cut out their paper circles before the lesson and fold or cut them on camera; the tutor shares the slides and draws the bar models live; pairs use breakout rooms for the explore and page 1. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 9, explore 9 to 21, teaching 21 to 33 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 33 to 37, worksheet page 1 37 to 49, check together 49 to 51, short teaching before page 2 51 to 57, worksheet page 2 57 to 72 (alone), check together 72 to 76, talk time 76 to 82, exit ticket 82 to 90. Stretch and challenge are for children who finish early, or for home.
