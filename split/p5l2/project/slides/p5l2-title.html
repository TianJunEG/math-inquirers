Fraction Feats

Four operations of fractions, fraction and division

TODAY WE WILL

Share pizzas fairly, write fractions as unit fractions, take a fraction of a remainder with a model and find patterns in fraction sums.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Link fractions and division (3 ÷ 4 = 3/4) and divide a proper fraction by a whole number; write fractions as the sum of two different unit fractions by testing in order; solve fraction-of-a-remainder problems forwards and backwards with a bar model; and spot and explain patterns in fraction sums. Thinking strategies taught today: DRAW A MODEL (bar or pizza), TEST IN ORDER and know when to stop, find the REMAINDER first, LOOK FOR A PATTERN, and test both ways. Materials: paper circles (about 6 per child) and scissors, a paper strip for bar models, digit cards 1 to 5, mini whiteboards, workbook. Online: children cut out their paper circles before the lesson and fold or cut them on camera; the tutor shares the slides and draws the bar models live; pairs use breakout rooms for the explore and page 1. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
