These are the printed Go further questions B1 to B3 (the same as the workbook). Answers: B1) 5/14. Each fraction is a difference: (1/2 − 1/3) + (1/3 − 1/4) + (1/4 − 1/5) + (1/5 − 1/6) + (1/6 − 1/7). The middle parts cancel, leaving 1/2 − 1/7 = 5/14. B2) Each child gets 5/12. Two ways: 1/3 + 1/12 (cut 4 pizzas into thirds and 1 pizza into twelfths), or 1/4 + 1/6 (cut 3 pizzas into quarters and 2 pizzas into sixths). B3) (a) 54 litres: each hour 2/3 is left, 54 → 36 → 24 → 16. (b) 2 hours: 24 litres is less than half of 54 (27), but after 1 hour 36 litres is more than half. (c) 54 − 24 = 30 litres.
