Key idea: the 360° turn

Going once round a closed shape, the turtle turns 360° altogether. A regular polygon with n sides turns 360° ÷ n at each corner, and its inside angle is 180° − turn.

A star goes round more than once: turn = 360° × times round ÷ points.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Using the inside angle as the turn: turn 60° for a triangle draws half a hexagon, and turn 108° for a pentagon does not close. (2) Dividing 180 by the number of sides instead of 360, or forgetting that turn + inside angle = 180°. (3) Stars: thinking the turns always add up to 360°. A star goes round more than once, so its turns add up to 720°, 1080° and so on.
