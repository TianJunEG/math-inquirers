Challenge

1. Ali wants a regular pentagon. He types the inside angle by mistake: repeat 5 [move 100, turn ↻ 108]. Does his shape close? How many repeats would make it close, and what would it draw?

2. How many different 12-point stars can a turtle draw with equal moves and equal turns? Give the turn and how many times it goes round.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first (they may test ideas in Scratch), then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) No: 5 × 108° = 540° is not a whole number of turns. 108 × 10 = 1080 = 3 × 360, so 10 repeats close it: a 10-point star that goes round 3 times (each point 72°). 2) Only 1: turn 150°, going round 5 times (5 × 360 ÷ 12). Turns 60°, 90° and 120° (round 2, 3, 4 times) just redraw a hexagon, square or triangle, and 180° goes back and forth on a line.
