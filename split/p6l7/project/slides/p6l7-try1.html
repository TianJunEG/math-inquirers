18°

c 360 sides: the turn and the inside angle? What does it look like?

1° and 179°

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 31 to 32.5. Whiteboards up together, then click. a) 360 ÷ 20 = 18°. b) 180 − 18 = 162°. c) 360 ÷ 360 = 1°, inside 179°: with tiny steps (move 1) it looks just like a circle. That is how Scratch artists draw circles: repeat 360 [move 1, turn right 1]. Let a fast pair test it.
