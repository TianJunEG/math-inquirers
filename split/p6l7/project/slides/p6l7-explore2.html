Minutes 15 to 25. Let pairs finish, then gather. Answers: 1) 120°. Turn 60 draws only half a hexagon: 3 × 60 = 180°, so the turtle does not come home. 2) Pentagon 72°, hexagon 60°. 3) 360°: the turtle comes home facing the same way, so it has made one full turn. Collect the table on the board: 3 sides 120°, 4 sides 90°, 5 sides 72°, 6 sides 60°, and multiply each pair. Do not name the rule yet: the teaching slides do that.
