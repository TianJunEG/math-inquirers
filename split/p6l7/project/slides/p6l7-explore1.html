The Turtle start file (L7_Turtle_Start.sb3, sent to families with the workbook; the tutor also keeps a copy) already has the 4 set-up blocks (erase all, go to x: -50 y: 100, point in direction 90, pen down), so children open it in about 60 seconds instead of building them; a child without the file adds the Pen extension (blue button, bottom left) and builds the 4 blocks. Say the stage size aloud: 480 wide, 360 tall, shapes grow right and DOWN; start at x: -50, y: 100 so every shape stays on the stage (up to 10 sides move 80, 12 to 15 sides move 50, 24 sides move 30, 36 sides move 10).
