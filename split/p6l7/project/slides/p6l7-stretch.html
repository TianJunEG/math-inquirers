Think harder

1. Each inside angle of a regular polygon is 4 times its turn angle. How many sides does the polygon have?

2. Each inside angle of a regular polygon is 140°. What do all its inside angles add up to?

3. Mei’s turtle draws a shape with 4 sides. Its first three turns are 70°, 100° and 90°. It comes home facing the same way after going round once. What is its last turn? What is the inside angle at that corner?

4. Plan a 20-point star that goes round 3 times (move 100). Find the total turning, the turn angle and the point angle. Which has sharper points, this star or the 9-point star in Q4?

The same four questions as the printed Think harder page, for anyone who wants them. Answers: 1) 10 (turn + 4 × turn = 180°, so the turn is 36° and 360 ÷ 36 = 10; check: inside 144° = 4 × 36°) 2) 1260° (turn 40°, so 9 sides; 9 × 140° = 1260°; check (9 − 2) × 180° = 1260°) 3) Last turn 100° (360 − 70 − 100 − 90); inside angle 80° (180 − 100) 4) Total 3 × 360° = 1080°, turn 1080 ÷ 20 = 54°, point angle 180° − 54° = 126°. The 9-point star (point angle 20°) has sharper points.
