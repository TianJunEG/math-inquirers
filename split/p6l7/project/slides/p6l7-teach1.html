The turtle turns the OUTSIDE angle

Step 1. The turtle walks along a side. At the corner it turns away from “straight on”.

Step 2. The turn is the angle between straight on and the new side: 70°.

Step 3. The inside angle and the turn sit on one straight line: 110° + 70° = 180°.

Go once round a closed shape and come home facing the same way: the turns add up to one full turn, 360°.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Act it out: walk along a line on the floor, stop at the "corner" and point straight on, then turn to face the new side. "How far did I turn? Not the angle inside the corner: the angle from straight on." Click 1: the walk. Click 2: the turn (red). Click 3: the inside angle (teal): angles on a straight line make 180°. Click 4: the big idea.
