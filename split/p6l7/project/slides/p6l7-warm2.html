Quick questions

1. How many degrees in a full turn?

360°

2. Two angles of a triangle are 50° and 75°. What is the third?

55°

3. What is 360 ÷ 8?

45

Minutes 10.5 to 13. Three questions only. Read each aloud, give thinking time, take answers on whiteboards, then click. Ask "How do you know?" each time. 1) 360°. 2) 180 − 50 − 75 = 55°. 3) 45. These facts (straight line 180°, full turn 360°) are the two tools for today.
