Think harder: Perimeter of a staircase

Tool: Try something simpler

The figure is a staircase with 4 steps. Every step is 3 cm high and 3 cm wide. All the corners are right angles.

(a) Find the perimeter of the figure.

(b) A staircase has 10 steps. Every step is 2 cm high and 2 cm wide. Find its perimeter.

(a) 48 cm.

(b) 80 cm.

ADDED ITEM perim (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Perimeter of a staircase"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Try something simpler. SAY: Try something simpler: a staircase with 1 step, then 2 steps. Can you slide the steps to the edges to make a rectangle? ASK: What is the width of the whole figure, and the height? What stays the same when you slide a step sideways? LISTEN FOR: Sliding the horizontal pieces to the bottom and the vertical pieces to the left makes a rectangle: the perimeter is the same as the rectangle around the whole figure. (a) 12 + 12 + 12 + 12 = 48. (b) 20 + 20 + 20 + 20 = 80. Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) 48 cm. (b) 80 cm. WHY: The horizontal edges add up to the width of the figure (twice: top and bottom) and the vertical edges add up to its height (twice). (a) Width 4 × 3 = 12, height 12, perimeter 2 × (12 + 12) = 48. (b) 2 × (20 + 20) = 80. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): A staircase has 6 steps, each 5 cm high and 5 cm wide. Find the perimeter. Answer: 120 cm.
