Q1: when is the total a full number of turns?

108 × 5 = 540°: 1½ turns. Not home.

108, 216, 324, 432 … 1080 = 3 × 360

10 repeats: a 10-point star, round 3 times

Each point: 180° − 108° = 72°

Q2: turn = 30° × jump

Jump 1: 12-gon. Jump 2: hexagon, twice

Jump 3: square, 3 times. Jump 4: triangle, 4 times

Jump 5: a star! Turn 150°, round 5 times ✓

Jump 6: a line. So only ONE 12-point star.
