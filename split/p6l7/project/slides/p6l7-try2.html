Your turn: work backwards

Turn first. Then share 360.

a The turn is 4°. How many sides?

90

b Each inside angle is 168°. How many sides?

30

c Can a regular polygon have inside angles of 125°?

No: turn 55°

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards, then click. a) 360 ÷ 4 = 90. b) turn 180 − 168 = 12°, 360 ÷ 12 = 30. c) The turn would be 55°, and 360 ÷ 55 = 6.54…, not a whole number of sides. Ask a child to explain c in words.
