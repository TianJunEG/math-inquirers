32.5–34.5 MIN

Worked example: work backwards

Each inside angle of a regular polygon is 174°. How many sides does it have?

Step 1. Turn = 180° − 174° = 6°.

Step 2. The turns make 360°, so the number of sides is 360 ÷ 6 = 60.

Step 3. Check: 60 × 6° = 360° ✓ A 60-gon.

Inside angle 115°? Turn 65°, and 360 ÷ 65 = 5.53… Not a whole number of sides: impossible!
