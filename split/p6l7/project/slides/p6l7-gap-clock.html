Think harder: Angle between the clock hands

Tool: Draw it

In one minute the minute hand sweeps 6° and the hour hand sweeps 0.5°.

(a) At 3:20, how many degrees apart are the two hands?

(b) At 4:10, how many degrees apart are the two hands?

(a) 20°.

(b) 65°.

Optional, if you finish early (no clock time): show it while some pairs are working or have finished, or use it live as an extra warm-up or tutor-bank question; never at a fixed minute, and it never takes time from the plan. ADDED ITEM clock (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Angle between the clock hands"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Draw it. SAY: Measure both hands from 12 as angles. Draw a clock and mark where each hand points at the time. ASK: How far has the hour hand moved from the 3 by 3:20? How far from 12 is the minute hand? LISTEN FOR: At 3:20 the hour hand is at 3 × 30 + 20 × 0.5 = 100 degrees from 12. The minute hand is 20 × 6 = 120 degrees from 12. The difference is 20. At 4:10: 125 and 60, difference 65. Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) 20°. (b) 65°. WHY: (a) Hour hand: 90 + 10 = 100°. Minute hand: 120°. Angle 20°. (b) Hour hand: 120 + 5 = 125°. Minute hand: 60°. Angle 65°. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): At 2:40, how many degrees apart are the two hands? Answer: 160° (hour hand at 80°, minute hand at 240°).
