Warm-up: Turtle says

Stand up (or sit and use your arm as the turtle’s nose) and face the screen: that is north. Turn, then say which way you face.

1. Turn right 90°

East

2. Turn right 180°

West

3. Turn left 270°

North

4. Turn right 45°

North-east

5. Turn right 360°

Still north-east

6. A full turn in 3 equal turns?

120° each

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6.5 to 10.5. Turtle says (the game stays about 150 seconds; if you run late stop after call 4). Everyone stands (online: stand back from the camera) or sits and uses an arm as the turtle’s nose; nobody has to stand. The turns follow on from each other: each one starts where the last one ended. Call each turn, children turn their bodies, then ask "Which way are you facing?" and click to show it. 1) right 90 from north: east. 2) right 180 from east: west. 3) left 270 from west: north (left 270 is the same as right 90). 4) right 45: north-east. 5) right 360: a full turn, still north-east. 6) "Make one full turn in 3 equal turns. How big is each?" 360 ÷ 3 = 120°. Ask: "Which other turn would have done the same as left 270?" (right 90). Keep this picture in mind: a full turn is 360°.
