Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Turtle says. Everyone stands (online: stand back from the camera). The turns follow on from each other: each one starts where the last one ended. Call each turn, children turn their bodies, then ask "Which way are you facing?" and click to show it. 1) right 90 from north: east. 2) right 180 from east: west. 3) left 270 from west: north (left 270 is the same as right 90). 4) right 45: north-east. 5) right 360: a full turn, still north-east. 6) "Make one full turn in 3 equal turns. How big is each?" 360 ÷ 3 = 120°. Ask: "Which other turn would have done the same as left 270?" (right 90). Keep this picture in mind: a full turn is 360°.
