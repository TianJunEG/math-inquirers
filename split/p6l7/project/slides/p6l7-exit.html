EXIT TICKET

A turtle draws a regular polygon and turns 5° at every corner. How many sides does the polygon have? What is each inside angle?

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 82 to 90. Answer: 72 sides (360 ÷ 5 = 72); each inside angle is 175° (180 − 5). Children who write 175 sides and 5° have swapped the two steps: ask "Which angle does the turtle turn?" Extension: Draw a rosette: repeat 12 [draw a square, then turn ↻ 30]. Start this one with go to x: 0 y: 0 instead of x: -50 y: 100, so the rosette stays on the stage. How far does the turtle turn altogether? (12 × (360 + 30) = 4680° = 13 full turns.) Design your own rosette and predict its total turning before you run it.
