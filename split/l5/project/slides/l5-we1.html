Worked example: one step

I think of a number. I add 6. I get 14. What is my number?

Step 1. Draw the machine: ? → + 6 → 14

Step 2. Go backwards and do the opposite: 14 − 6 = 8

Step 3. Check by going forwards: 8 + 6 = 14 ✓ My number is 8.
