Think harder: ages

1. Ali is 6 years old. His sister is 9 years old.

a) How many years older is his sister?

b) In 3 years, Ali will be ___ years old. His sister will be ___ years old.

c) How many years older is his sister then?

Optional slide for pairs who finish early or ask for more. Show it only on request and never announce who is working on it. Children write the answer on their own paper and say it together on three; nothing is held up to the camera. Answers and tutor lines are for you only. Item 1 (Ages: the gap between ages stays the same; tool of the day idea: Draw it). SAY: "Draw two bars for their ages. Both bars grow by the same amount." ASK: "What stayed the same? What changed?" LISTEN FOR: "They both get 3 years older, so the gap stays 3." ANSWER: a) 3 years. b) Ali 9 years, sister 12 years. c) 3 years. The gap between their ages never changes.
