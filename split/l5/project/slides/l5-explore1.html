Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Hide a secret number of counters under a cup.
  2. Add 5 more counters beside the cup. Tell your partner the total.
  3. Your partner works backwards to find the secret number.
  4. Lift the cup to check. Then swap roles.

Go further: your choice, any time

What if you add 5 and then take away 2 before you tell your partner the total? Can they still find your number?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs work with the materials: Paper cups, counters, number machine cards. Pairs hide a secret number of counters under a cup, add 5 more, and tell their partner the total. The partner works backwards by removing 5. Online: Slide with a cup that hides counters; the tutor reveals the total and the children work back. Go further (what-if) is on this slide from the start: any pair may choose it at any time, so nobody waits. The tutor does not choose who and does not announce it. Answers: Yes: add 5 and take away 2 is the same as add 3, so undo it with take away 3. Open: accept any reasoning that undoes both steps.
