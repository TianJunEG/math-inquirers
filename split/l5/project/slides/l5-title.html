Solve “I think of a number” puzzles by working backwards and by using a number machine.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Solve "I think of a number" problems by working backwards, find a number machine rule, and solve more-than and fewer-than stories. Materials: Paper cups, counters, number machine cards. Online: Slide with a cup that hides counters; the tutor reveals the total and the children work back. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (worked examples with whiteboard practice; click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with a short teaching slide before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
