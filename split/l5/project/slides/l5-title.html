Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Solve "I think of a number" problems by working backwards, find a number machine rule, and solve more-than and fewer-than stories. Materials: Paper cups, counters, number machine cards. Online: Slide with a cup that hides counters; the tutor reveals the total and the children work back. Time plan: opening 0 to 2, warm-up 2 to 8 (game 2 to 6, quick questions 6 to 8; ask questions 1 to 3, and 4 and 5 only if you are on time), sneak-peek puzzles 8 to 12, explore 12 to 26, teaching 26 to 36 (worked examples with whiteboard practice; click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 36 to 40, worksheet 40 to 72 with a short teaching slide before each page and a check-together slide after it (a 30-second stand-and-stretch reset after the page-1 check, taken from that slot; the last 4 minutes check page 2, after a 1-minute stand-up stretch), talk time 72 to 79, exit ticket 79 to 85, look-back 85 to 88, then flex time and goodbye 88 to 90 (this is the buffer: if you are behind, use it; do not start anything new). Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are for every child to choose, any time or at home. Nobody is told which page they should be on. SPINE: Today's tool is Work backwards. Start at the end and undo each step. Look for where you use it. Point at the card on page 1 of the workbook. HABIT: Focus habit: Check. Ask the child to test a second case before saying it works. Say: "Will it work with a different number? Test it." Do not give the answer first: ask the prompt, wait, and give a hint only if the child is stuck.
