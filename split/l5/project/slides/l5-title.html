Solve “I think of a number” puzzles by working backwards and by using a number machine.

Math Inquirers Level 1 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Solve "I think of a number" problems by working backwards, find a number machine rule, and solve more-than and fewer-than stories. Materials: Paper cups, counters, number machine cards. Online: Slide with a cup that hides counters; the tutor reveals the total and the children work back. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10 (game 0 to 5, quick questions 5 to 7, sneak-peek puzzles 7 to 10), explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (worked examples with whiteboard practice; click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 72 with a short teaching slide before each page and a check-together slide after it (the last 4 minutes check page 2, after a 1-minute stand-up stretch), talk time 72 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 88, then a two-minute look-back 88 to 90. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are for every child to choose, any time or at home. Nobody is told which page they should be on.
