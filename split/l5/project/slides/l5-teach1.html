Undo it: do the opposite

Adding and taking away undo each other. If a number had 6 added, take 6 away to get back to it.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Show 8 counters, add 6 more in front of the children: 14. Ask: "How do I get back to 8?" Take the 6 away. Say: "Adding 6 and taking away 6 undo each other. They are opposites." Then point to the machine: going forwards we add 6; going backwards we do the opposite, take away 6. Think-aloud: "Mystery number plus 6 is 14. To find it I go backwards. The opposite of add 6 is take away 6."
