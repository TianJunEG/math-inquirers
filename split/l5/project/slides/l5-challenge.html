1. A number machine adds 3, then adds 4. IN 5 gives OUT 12. What goes IN to give OUT 20?

2. A snail climbs 3 cm each day and slides back 1 cm each night. It starts at the bottom of a 9 cm wall. On which day does it reach the top?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) 13 (20 − 4 = 16, 16 − 3 = 13; check 13 + 3 + 4 = 20). 2) Day 4 (end of day 1: 3, night: 2; day 2: 5, night: 4; day 3: 7, night: 6; day 4: 9, it is at the top before it can slide back).
