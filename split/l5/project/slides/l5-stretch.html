Optional: if you finish early

Stretch

1. Ali and Ben have 20 marbles together. Ali has 4 more than Ben. How many does each have?

2. Ben had some stickers. He gave away 6, got 4, then gave away 3. Now he has 10. How many did he have at first?

3. Tom is 5th from the front. There are 4 children between Tom and Sue, who is last. How many are in the queue?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) Ben 8, Ali 12 (take away the extra 4: 16, share equally: 8 each, then give Ali the 4). 2) 15 (work backwards: 10 + 3 = 13, 13 − 4 = 9, 9 + 6 = 15; check 15 − 6 + 4 − 3 = 10). 3) 10 (Tom is 5th, then 4 children, then Sue: 5 + 4 + 1).
