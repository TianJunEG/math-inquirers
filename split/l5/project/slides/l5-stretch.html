Answers: 1) 10 (Tom is 5th, then 4 children, then Sue: 5 + 4 + 1). 2) 7 (work backwards: 9 + 4 = 13, 13 − 6 = 7; check 7 + 6 − 4 = 9). 3) 11 (undo in reverse order: 14 − 8 = 6, 6 + 5 = 11).
