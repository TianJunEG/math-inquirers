Worked example: two steps

I think of a number. I add 5, then I take away 3. I get 9.

Step 1. Draw it: ? → + 5 → □ → − 3 → 9

Step 2. Undo the LAST step first: the opposite of − 3 is + 3. 9 + 3 = 12

Step 3. Then undo + 5: 12 − 5 = 7

Step 4. Check forwards: 7 + 5 = 12, 12 − 3 = 9 ✓ My number is 7.
