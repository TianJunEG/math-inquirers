Key idea: the number machine

Number machine rule: add 2. IN 3 gives OUT 5. OUT 9 came from IN 7.

To go from OUT back to IN, do the opposite.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 33 to 35. Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Adding again instead of undoing (12 + 5). (2) Two steps: undoing in the wrong order. (3) Queue question: giving 16 because Ali is counted twice.
