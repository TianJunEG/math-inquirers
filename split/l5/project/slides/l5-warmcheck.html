Warm-up check

Whiteboards: answer and say how you know

1. A bag has some marbles. Ali puts in 6 more. Now there are 15. How many were in the bag at first?

9 (15 − 6)

2. I think of a number. I take away 2, then I add 7. I get 12. What is my number?

7 (12 − 7 = 5, then 5 + 2 = 7)

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 7 to 10 (3 minutes). Two questions on the key idea in a new setting. Children write their answer and how they know on a whiteboard and show it on a count of three; click to show each answer. Ask one or two children "How do you know?". Quietly note who got both right and gave a reason: those children may start the worksheet on the Think Harder page (then Challenge and the Open investigation) while the group works on the core page. Tell them privately at minute 40; do not announce names or scores. Everyone still joins Explore and the Mini-lesson. Answers: 1) 9. Work backwards: 15 − 6 = 9. 2) 7. Undo the last step first: 12 − 7 = 5, then undo the first step: 5 + 2 = 7. Check: 7 − 2 = 5, 5 + 7 = 12.
