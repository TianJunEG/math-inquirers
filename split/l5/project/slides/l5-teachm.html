Step 1. Compare each IN with its OUT: 1 → 5 is 4 more. Check 4 → 8: 4 more ✓

Step 2. Rule: add 4. So IN 7 gives OUT 7 + 4 = 11

Step 3. OUT 20? Go backwards: 20 − 4 = 16. IN is 16.
