Worked example: make a table. Think-aloud: "Chickens and goats: 7 heads and 20 legs. I make a table. 0 goats: 7 chickens, 14 legs. 1 goat: 4 + 12 = 16. 2 goats: 8 + 10 = 18. 3 goats: 12 + 8 = 20 ✓. So 3 goats and 4 chickens. Each goat I swap in adds 2 legs, so I could even have jumped straight there: 20 − 14 = 6 more legs, 3 swaps." Your turn: tricycles 0 → 12 wheels, 1 → 13, 2 → 14, 3 → 15 ✓: 3 tricycles and 3 bicycles (9 + 6 = 15). This is the method for worksheet Q3 (coins).
