Think harder: posts and places

1. A straight path is 20 m long. A lamp post stands at the start, at the end, and every 5 m in between.

a) How many lamp posts are there?

b) A fence has 7 posts in a straight line, 3 m apart. How long is the fence?

2. Counting from the front of a queue, Ravi is number 5. Counting from the back, he is number 4.

Find the number of children in the queue.

Optional slide for pairs who finish early or ask for more. Show it only on request and never announce who is working on it. Children write the answer on their own paper and say it together on three; nothing is held up to the camera. Answers and tutor lines are for you only. Item 1 (Posts and gaps; tool of the day idea: Draw it). SAY: "Draw a dot for each post and a line for each gap." ASK: "Are there more posts or more gaps? Why?" LISTEN FOR: "One more post than gaps" in a line. ANSWER: a) 5 posts (4 gaps of 5 m). b) 18 m (6 gaps of 3 m). Item 2 (Places in a queue; tool of the day idea: Draw it). SAY: "Draw the queue and mark Ravi." ASK: "Who is in front of Ravi? Behind him? Did you count Ravi twice?" LISTEN FOR: "Ravi is counted in both numbers." ANSWER: 8 children. Ravi is counted twice (5 + 4 = 9), so take 1 away.
