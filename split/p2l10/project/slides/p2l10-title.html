PRIMARY 2 • LESSON 10 • WEEK 5

Challenge Day

Mixed puzzles: how to start a puzzle

TODAY WE WILL

Learn three ways to start a puzzle, then use them on mixed challenges.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Choose a way to start a puzzle (draw it, list it in order, make a table) and use it on mixed puzzles: picture graphs with a key of 5 or 10, a logic grid with exactly one answer, numbers to 1000, money, fractions, and × and ÷ with the 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10 tables. Check the answer and explain it. Materials: Puzzle cards for 4 stations, play coins, digit cards 0 to 9, paper strips for fractions, mini whiteboards, certificates, stamps. Online: Breakout pairs work on one station card each and report back to the group. Each child needs digit cards (cut from paper), a pencil and a mini whiteboard. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (how to start a puzzle: draw it, list it, make a table; click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 88, certificates 88 to 90.
