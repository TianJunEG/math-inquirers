EXPLORE

  1. Four station cards: a picture graph, a logic grid, a coin puzzle and a fraction strip.
  2. Work in pairs. Read the card aloud.
  3. Before you solve it, write how you will start: draw, list or table.
  4. Solve it, check it, and be ready to explain.

Minutes 10 to 15 (stations run until about minute 22). Station cards (print or show one per breakout pair): Graph: "Key ★ = 2 children. Red has ★★★★, blue has ★★, green has ★★★. How many children? How many more chose red than blue?" (18; 4 more). Logic: "Ali, Ben and Mei each have a different fruit: apple, banana, cherry. Ali does not have the apple. Mei has the cherry. Who has the banana?" (Ali; Ben has the apple). Coins: "Make 50¢ with exactly 3 coins (5¢, 10¢, 20¢, 50¢). Is there more than one way?" (20 + 20 + 10 only). Fraction strip: "Fold a strip into 5 equal parts. Colour 2/5. What fraction is not coloured?" (3/5). Online: give each breakout pair one card. Walk round and ask "How did you start?" before anything else.
