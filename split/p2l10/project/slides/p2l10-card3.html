Station card 3: coin puzzle

CARD 3

Make 70¢ with exactly 4 coins. You can use 5¢, 10¢, 20¢ and 50¢ coins. Find all the ways.

Before you solve it, write how you will start: draw it, list it or make a table. Then check your answer.

Answer: 2 ways: 50¢ + 10¢ + 5¢ + 5¢, and 20¢ + 20¢ + 20¢ + 10¢

Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Station card 3. List in order, biggest coin first. With 50¢: 20¢ left for 3 coins: 10 + 5 + 5 works (10 + 10 + 0 does not, 5 + 5 + 10 is the same set). Without 50¢: four coins of 20¢ or less: 20 + 20 + 20 + 10 = 70 works; 20 + 20 + 10 + 10 = 60 is too small and 20 + 20 + 20 + 20 = 80 is too big. Exactly 2 ways (checked by a program).
