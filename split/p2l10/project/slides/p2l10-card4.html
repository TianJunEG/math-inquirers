Station card 4: fraction strip

CARD 4

A strip is cut into 8 equal parts. 3 parts are red and 2 parts are blue. What fraction is NOT coloured?

Before you solve it, write how you will start: draw it, list it or make a table. Then check your answer.

Answer: 3/8

Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Station card 4. Draw it: 8 equal parts, 3 red, 2 blue, so 3 + 2 = 5 are coloured and 8 − 5 = 3 are not. The fraction not coloured is 3/8 (3 parts out of 8 equal parts). Common mistake: writing 5/8 (the coloured parts). Practises: draw it.
