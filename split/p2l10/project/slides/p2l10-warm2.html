Minutes 5 to 8. Read each aloud, give thinking time, take answers, then click to show. Answers: 1) 8 (5 × 8 = 40, count in 5s to 40: 8 jumps). 2) 11:05 a.m. (5 minutes to 11:00, 5 more). 3) 1/3: the cake is cut into fewer pieces, so each piece is bigger. 4) A semicircle. 5) 70 (work backwards: 100 − 30). Ask "How do you know?" for 3: draw two same bars, one cut in 3 and one cut in 5.
