Minutes 8.5 to 10.5. Read each aloud, give thinking time, take answers, then click to show. Answers: 1) 8 (5 × 8 = 40, count in 5s to 40: 8 jumps). 2) 1/3: the cake is cut into fewer pieces, so each piece is bigger (draw two same bars, one cut in 3 and one cut in 5). Spare questions in the guide table: 10:55 am plus 10 minutes is 11:05 am; half of a circle is a half circle; a number plus 30 makes 100: 70. Ask quick questions 1 and 3 only (about 2 minutes): children answer by thumbs, hands, not by naming a child. The other questions are spare.
