2. Mei saves $3 on day 1, $6 on day 2, $9 on day 3, and so on ($3 more each day). Ali saves $10 every day. Make a table of how much each child has saved altogether after each day. On which day does Mei FIRST have more than Ali?

Answers: 1) 1 picture (30 − 10 − 15 = 5 children) 2) Day 6. Mei’s totals: $3, $9, $18, $30, $45, $63. Ali’s totals: $10, $20, $30, $40, $50, $60. On day 6 Mei has $63 and Ali has $60. (On day 5 Mei still has less: $45 and $50.) 3) 16 (14 − 6 = 8 is half, 8 + 8 = 16). 4) (Question: 5, 9, 13, 17, … Will 49 be in this pattern? Will 50? How do you know?) 49 will be in the pattern and 50 will not. The pattern adds 4 each time, so every number in it is 1 more than a number in the 4 times table: 5 = 4 + 1, 9 = 8 + 1, 13 = 12 + 1. 49 = 48 + 1, but 50 is 2 more than 48.
