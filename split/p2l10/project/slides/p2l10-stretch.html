For early finishers

Stretch

1. Use three of the cards 4, 0, 6, 2. What is the greatest 3-digit number you can make that is less than 500?

2. A farm has ducks and rabbits. There are 10 heads and 26 legs. How many rabbits?

3. Siti, Ravi and Lin sit in a row of 3 chairs. Lin is not at either end. Siti is on the left of Ravi. Who sits on the right end?

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) 462 (the hundreds digit must be 4 or 2 to be less than 500; take 4, then the biggest tens and ones left: 6 and 2). 2) 3 rabbits and 7 ducks (table: 0 rabbits 20 legs, 1: 22, 2: 24, 3: 26 ✓). 3) Ravi (Lin is in the middle, so Siti and Ravi are at the ends; Siti is on the left, so Ravi is on the right end).
