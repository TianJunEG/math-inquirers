Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6 to 8.5. Relay review, three cards only (digits, change, fractions; the other three are spare). Read a card and ask for a volunteer; that child answers, then chooses a friend who has not had a turn yet. Any child may say “pass”. Never name a child to put them on the spot. Click to reveal each answer in order. A different answer gets “let us test that”, never a groan. Answers: 740 (biggest digit first; 0 last). 35¢ ($1 = 100¢; 65 + 35 = 100). 3/4 (4 quarters make the whole; 1 eaten, 3 left). Spare cards: missing digit 3[ ]5 + 214 = 579 is 6; 5 chairs have 20 legs; 3, 8, 13, 18, __ is 23. Ask after each: "Which lesson does this remind you of?" A game of about 2.5 minutes: stop it on the clock even if the game is not finished.
