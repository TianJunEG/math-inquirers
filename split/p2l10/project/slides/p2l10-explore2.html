Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Let pairs finish, then gather. What we want them to notice: every card got easier once it was on paper (a drawing, a list in order, or a table). Answers: 1) Open answers; collect one example of each start on the board. 2) Open answers. 3) Listen for: drawing it, going in order, trying small numbers first, checking with every clue. Follow-up: "How do you know there is only one answer to the coin card?" (we listed every 3-coin set in order).
