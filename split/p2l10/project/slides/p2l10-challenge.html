Challenge

1. Ali, Ben, Mei and Siti each have one coin: 10¢, 20¢, 50¢ or $1. Mei has more money than Ali but less than Ben. Ben does not have the $1 coin. Who has which coin?

2. At a hawker centre, chicken rice costs $4 a plate and noodles cost $3 a bowl. Ravi’s family buys 7 dishes for $25. How many plates of chicken rice do they buy?

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) Ali 10¢, Mei 20¢, Ben 50¢, Siti $1. 2) 4 plates of chicken rice (and 3 bowls of noodles). Both are on the workbook Think Harder page (Challenge 6 and 7). A program check confirms each has exactly one answer.
