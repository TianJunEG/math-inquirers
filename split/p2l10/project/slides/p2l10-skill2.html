Optional, if you finish early

Think harder: how many to be sure?

1. A bag has 6 red counters and 4 blue counters. You take counters out without looking.

a) How many must you take to be sure you have 2 red counters?

b) How many must you take to be sure you have one counter of each colour?

Optional slide for pairs who finish early or ask for more. Show it only on request and never announce who is working on it. Children write the answer on their own paper and say it together on three; nothing is held up to the camera. Answers and tutor lines are for you only. Item 1 (Worst case: how many to be sure?; tool of the day idea: Act it out). SAY: "Be unlucky on purpose. What is the worst that can happen?" ASK: "What is the unluckiest draw? What counter must come next?" LISTEN FOR: "The unlucky draw is all of the other colour first." ANSWER: a) 6 (the worst case is 4 blue first, then 2 red). b) 7 (the worst case is all 6 red first, then 1 blue).
