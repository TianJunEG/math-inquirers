Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6 tables in a row; Q5 fractions). Ask first: "How many people at 1 table? 2 tables? Why not 12 for 2 tables?" (the sides that touch lose their seats). Click step 1: the rule is add 4. Then the table fills in: 18, 22, 26. Step 2: "How many tables for 30 people? I keep the table going: 26, then 30 at 7 tables." Your turn (fractions, like Q5): Ali eats 3 slices, Ben eats 4 slices, 3 + 4 = 7 slices, so 1 slice of 8 is left: 1/8. Common mistake: Ben eats 1/8 (reading "1 slice more" as "1 slice"). The worksheet uses square tables: do not give the rule away.
