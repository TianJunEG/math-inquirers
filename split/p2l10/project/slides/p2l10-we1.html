Draw it: a before-and-after story

Mei had some stickers. She gave half of them to Ben. Then she gave 6 to Siti. She has 4 left. How many did she have at first?

Step 1. Draw a bar for all the stickers. Cut it into two equal halves.

Step 2. The half Mei kept is the 6 for Siti and the 4 left: 6 + 4 = 10.

Both halves are the same: 10 + 10 = 20.

Check: half of 20 is 10. 10 − 6 = 4 ✓

Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example: draw it. Ask first: "Can you hold all of this in your head?" Click order: the second half fills in (6 and 4), then the 10s, then step 1, step 2, the answer, the check. (If you prefer, say step 1 before the first click.) Think-aloud: "Mei had some stickers. She gave half to Ben. Then she gave 6 to Siti and had 4 left. I cannot hold that in my head, so I draw a bar cut into two halves. One half went to Ben. The other half is the 6 for Siti and the 4 left: 6 + 4 = 10. Both halves are the same, so Mei had 10 + 10 = 20 stickers. Check: half of 20 is 10, 10 − 6 = 4 ✓." Common mistake: 6 + 4 = 10 given as the answer (that is only half).
