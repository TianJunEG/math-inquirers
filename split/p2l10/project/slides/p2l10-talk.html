TALK TIME

Pick one puzzle and teach it to the group in two sentences: how you started it, and how you know your answer is right.

Sentence starters:

“I started by …” “I noticed that …” “I know because …” “Pass.”

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 72 to 80. Pairs talk for 2 minutes, then share. Also ask: "When is a table better than a drawing?" Listen for a clear start (I drew bars, I listed in order, I made a table) and a check (I put my answer back into the puzzle). A table is good when we try 0, 1, 2, 3 … and something grows each time; a drawing is good for before-and-after stories and more-than or fewer-than. Praise the reasons, not only the answers. Talk time rules: you choose volunteers first ("Who would like to share?"); never cold-call a child by name; any child may say pass; no public tallies, scores or rankings.
