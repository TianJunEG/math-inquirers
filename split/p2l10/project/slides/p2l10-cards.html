12–18.5 MIN

Four station cards

CARD 1: picture graph

Each picture stands for 5 books. Monday has 3 pictures, Tuesday 5, Wednesday 2. How many books were read on these 3 days?

50 books

CARD 2: logic grid

Ali, Ben and Cai each have one pet: a cat, a dog or a fish. Ben has the fish. Ali does not have the dog. Who has the cat?

Ali has the cat (Cai has the dog)

CARD 3: coin puzzle

Make 70¢ with exactly 4 coins. You can use 5¢, 10¢, 20¢ and 50¢ coins. Find all the ways.

2 ways: 50¢ + 10¢ + 5¢ + 5¢, and 20¢ + 20¢ + 20¢ + 10¢

CARD 4: fraction strip

A strip is cut into 8 equal parts. 3 parts are red and 2 parts are blue. What fraction is NOT coloured?

3/8

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 12 to 18.5. All four station cards are on this ONE slide (one slide change instead of four). Online, each breakout pair takes TWO cards (for example 1 and 3, or 2 and 4); paste the text of their two cards into the breakout room chat. About 2 minutes a card. Answers appear on the click. Card 1 (picture graph): read the key first: one picture is worth 5 books. 3 + 5 + 2 = 10 pictures, 10 × 5 = 50 books. Common mistake: 10 (counting the pictures). Card 2 (logic grid): tick Ben and fish, cross the rest of Ben’s row and the fish column. Ali is not dog and fish is taken, so Ali has the cat. Cai has the dog. Card 3 (coins): list in order, biggest coin first. With 50¢: 20¢ left for 3 coins: 10 + 5 + 5. Without 50¢: 20 + 20 + 20 + 10. Exactly 2 ways. Card 4 (fraction strip): 3 + 2 = 5 coloured, 8 − 5 = 3 not coloured: 3/8. Common mistake: 5/8.
