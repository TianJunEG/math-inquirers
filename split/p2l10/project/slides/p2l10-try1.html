Whiteboards. a) Draw 5 equal parts, shade 2: 3 parts of 5 are left, 3/5. b) Take away the extra 10 cm: 30 cm; share: 15 and 15; give the 10 back: 15 cm and 25 cm. Check 15 + 25 = 40 and 25 − 15 = 10. c) Take away Ali’s extra 12: 18; share: 9 and 9. Ben has 9, Ali 21. Check 9 + 21 = 30 ✓.
