CARD 2

Ali, Ben and Cai each have one pet: a cat, a dog or a fish. Ben has the fish. Ali does not have the dog. Who has the cat?

Before you solve it, write how you will start: draw it, list it or make a table. Then check your answer.

Answer: Ali has the cat (Cai has the dog)
