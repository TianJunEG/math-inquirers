List it: every number, in order

Use the cards 2, 0 and 5 once each. How many 3-digit numbers can you make?

Step 1. Go in order of the hundreds digit. 0 cannot go first: 025 is not a 3-digit number.

Step 2. Hundreds digit 2: 205, 250

Step 3. Hundreds digit 5: 502, 520

4 numbers. Greatest 520, smallest 205.

Going in order means you do not miss a number, and you know when to stop.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example: list it. Give out digit cards if you have them. Think-aloud: "Digit cards 2, 0 and 5. How many 3-digit numbers? I list them in order, smallest hundreds digit first. 0 cannot go first, because 025 is just 25. With 2 first, the other two cards can go 0 5 or 5 0: 205, 250. With 5 first: 502, 520. Four numbers, and I know I have them all because I went in order." Ask: "Why are there two numbers for each first card?" (the last two cards can swap).
