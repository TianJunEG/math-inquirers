Picture graphs: read the key first

The children read 90 books altogether. How many did Ravi read?

Step 1. Key: one star is 10 books. Ali: 10, 20, 30, 40. Mei: 20.

Step 2. Ravi: 90 − 40 − 20 = 30 books.

Step 3. 30 books is 3 stars. Draw them!

Your turn: Key: one picture = 5 children. A row has 4 pictures. How many children?

20

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 1 (Q1 is a picture graph with a key of 5). Ask first: "Ali has 4 stars. Did he read 4 books?" (No: read the key first.) Click step 1: count in 10s along each row. Step 2: "The total is 90. Ali and Mei read 40 + 20 = 60, so Ravi read the rest: 90 − 60 = 30." Step 3, then the stars appear: "30 books is 10, 20, 30: 3 stars." Your turn: count in 5s: 5, 10, 15, 20. Common mistake: writing the number of pictures (4) instead of using the key.
