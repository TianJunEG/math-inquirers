Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 88 to 90. What was hard? About 1 minute: children tick a face and finish the three sentences on the last workbook page. No marks and no wrong answers. Tutor script: SAY: One minute, no talking. Tick a face and finish the three sentences on your page: the hardest part, how you got unstuck, and what you will try next time. There are no marks for this and no wrong answers. DO: Ask one or two children to share ONE sentence (not always the fastest). Never grade or correct it. Praise the strategy, not the speed: say “You drew it when you got stuck, that is what mathematicians do”, not “you were so quick”. Glance at the pages as you stamp journals and use them to plan the next lesson.
