Go further: Dice and hidden faces

Tool: Act it out

A tower is made of 3 dice, one on top of another, standing on a table. Opposite faces of each die sum to 7. The upper face of the highest die shows 4.

Find the total number of dots on the hidden faces: the face touching the table, plus each pair of faces that touch each other.

17 dots.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional, if you finish early (no clock time): show it while some pairs are working or have finished, or use it live as an extra warm-up or tutor-bank question; never at a fixed minute, and it never takes time from the plan. ADDED ITEM dice (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Dice and hidden faces"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Act it out. SAY: Take a real die if you have one. Turn it and look: which faces are on opposite sides? What do two opposite faces always add up to? ASK: Which hidden faces form pairs that add up to 7? Which one hidden face is left over? LISTEN FOR: The top and bottom of the bottom die add to 7. The top and bottom of the middle die add to 7. The bottom of the top die is 7 − 4 = 3. Listen for "pairs of opposite faces". Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: 17 dots. WHY: Hidden faces: the bottom die's bottom and top (7), the middle die's bottom and top (7), and the top die's bottom face (7 − 4 = 3). 7 + 7 + 3 = 17. It does not matter how the lower dice are turned. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): The upper face of the highest die shows 2. Find the hidden total now. Answer: 19 dots.
