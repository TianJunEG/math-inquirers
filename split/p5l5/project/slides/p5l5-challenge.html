Challenge

1. Tank A: base 30 cm by 20 cm, water 30 cm deep. Tank B: base 40 cm by 30 cm, empty. Water is poured from A into B until the water in both tanks is equally deep. How deep is it?

2. A tank has a base 30 cm by 20 cm and water 10 cm deep. A block 10 cm by 10 cm by 15 cm is stood upright on the bottom. How deep is the water now?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. These are in the question bank and Think Harder. Answers: 1) 10 cm (the 18 000 cm3 of water is shared over bases of 600 + 1200 = 1800 cm2). 2) 12 cm (the block takes 10 x 10 = 100 cm2 of the floor, so the water spreads over 500 cm2: 6000 / 500 = 12 cm; the block is 15 cm tall so it sticks out of the water). Not 12.5 cm: 12.5 would come from adding the block as if it were fully under water.
