E1. A tank has a base 40 cm by 30 cm. A cube with edges of 10 cm sits on the bottom, and the water is 6 cm deep around it. How much water is in the tank?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional, if you finish early (no clock time): show it only while some pairs are working or have finished, never as a group slide at a fixed minute. Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. These are in the question bank and Think harder. Answers: 1) 6600 cm3 = 6.6 litres (40 x 30 x 6 = 7200 cm3 if there were no cube, but the cube takes up 10 x 10 x 6 = 600 cm3 of that space: the cube is taller than the water, so only 6 cm of it is under water. 7200 - 600 = 6600). 2) 12 cm (the block takes 10 x 10 = 100 cm2 of the floor, so the water spreads over 500 cm2: 6000 / 500 = 12 cm; the block is 15 cm tall so it sticks out of the water). Not 12.5 cm: 12.5 would come from adding the block as if it were fully under water.
