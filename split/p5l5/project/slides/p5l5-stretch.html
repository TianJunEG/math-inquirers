Stretch

1. A tank is 1/5 full of water. Ali pours in 12 ℓ and it becomes 3/5 full. How many litres does the tank hold when it is full?

2. A 6 cm cube is painted on the outside and cut into 1-cm cubes. How many small cubes have no paint at all?

3. Tank base 30 cm by 20 cm. The water is 5 cm deep. Ben drops in 3 identical stones and the water rises to 8 cm. What is the volume of ONE stone?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 30 litres (3/5 - 1/5 = 2/5 of the tank is 12 litres, so 1/5 is 6 litres and the whole tank is 5 x 6 = 30 litres). 2) 64 (inside block 4 x 4 x 4). 3) 600 cm3 (the rise is 3 cm, so the 3 stones take up 30 x 20 x 3 = 1800 cm3, and one stone is 1800 / 3 = 600 cm3).
