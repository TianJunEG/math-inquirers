Key idea: base area × height

A cuboid, or the water in a tank, has volume = base area × height. Anything that sinks raises the water by its volume ÷ base area.

1 ℓ = 1000 cm³ and 1 ml = 1 cm³. Change litres to cm³ before you divide by a base area.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Mixing units: dividing 8 litres by a base area in cm2 without changing 8 litres to 8000 cm3. (2) Using the height of the tank instead of the depth of the water, or giving the new level when the question asks for the rise (or the other way round). (3) Painted cubes: counting a corner cube again as an edge cube, or using n instead of n - 2 for the cubes along each edge.
