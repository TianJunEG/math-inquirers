Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Let pairs keep working, then gather them. Write their cuboids on the board in the order they say them, messy on purpose, then ask "Is anything missing? Is anything there twice?" Answers: 1) 6 cuboids: 1 x 1 x 24, 1 x 2 x 12, 1 x 3 x 8, 1 x 4 x 6, 2 x 2 x 6, 2 x 3 x 4. 2) List in order by the shortest edge. Shortest edge 1: the other two multiply to 24 (1 x 24, 2 x 12, 3 x 8, 4 x 6). Shortest edge 2: the other two multiply to 12 and are at least 2 (2 x 6, 3 x 4). Shortest edge 3 would need two edges of at least 3 that multiply to 8: impossible. 3) All 24 cm3, but the shapes look very different. Do not name the method if nobody finds it: the later slides teach it.
