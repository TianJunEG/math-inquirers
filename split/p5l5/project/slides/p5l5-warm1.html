Math Inquirers Level 5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 3 to 6 (play only 1 or 2 rounds). Missing Edge Match (not a race: nobody shouts, speed does not matter). Read one card at a time; teams write the third edge and the division on whiteboards, then show them together on three, two, one. Click to reveal the check (the card pops). Answers: 3, 4, 3, 4, 3, 6. Ask each time: "Which operation did you use?" (volume divided by the area of the base: one layer times the missing edge gives the volume). Watch for children who divide by only one edge. Extension: "A cuboid has volume 60 cm3. Its base is 5 cm by 4 cm. How many cm3 in one layer?" (20 cm3). If a child says 5 + 4 + 3, point out that a volume is multiplied, never added.
