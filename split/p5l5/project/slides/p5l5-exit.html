A tank has a base 30 cm by 25 cm and water 8 cm deep. A rock sinks and the water rises to 10 cm. (a) What is the volume of the rock? (b) How many litres of water are in the tank?

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 80 to 90. Answers: (a) 1500 cm3 (30 x 25 x 2). (b) 6 litres (30 x 25 x 8 = 6000 cm3). Extension: find every cuboid with whole-number edges and a volume of 60 cm3 (there are 10, from 1 x 1 x 60 to 3 x 4 x 5). Which one is most like a cube? (3 x 4 x 5).
