These are the printed Go further questions B1 to B3 (the same as the workbook). Answers: B1) 60 cm³ (edges 3 cm, 4 cm and 5 cm: 3 × 4 = 12, 3 × 5 = 15, 4 × 5 = 20) B2) 24 with 2 faces (4 × 1 + 4 × 2 + 4 × 3 along the edges); 22 with 1 face (60 − 8 corners − 24 − 6 inside) B3) 12 cm (the water now covers 600 − 100 = 500 cm² of floor: 6000 ÷ 500 = 12 cm; the block is 15 cm tall, so it sticks out.
