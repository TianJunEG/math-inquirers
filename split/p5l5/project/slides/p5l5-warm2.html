Math Inquirers Level 5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6 to 9. Read each aloud, whiteboards up together, then click to show. Answers: 70 cm3, 1000 cm3, 3200 ml, 5 cm, 4.5 litres. After 4 ask: "Which number multiplied by itself three times gives 125?" (5 x 5 x 5). After 2 ask: "How big is a 1000 cm3 box?" (10 cm by 10 cm by 10 cm, about the size of a small tissue box). 1 ml is the same as 1 cm3: a small sugar cube of water.
