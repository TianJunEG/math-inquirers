Your turn: whiteboards

Change litres to cm³ before you divide.

a Base 20 cm by 10 cm, water 6 cm deep. How many ℓ?

1.2 ℓ

b Base 30 cm by 20 cm. 3 ℓ of water. How deep?

5 cm

c Base 25 cm by 20 cm, water 4 cm deep. Add 1.5 ℓ. New depth?

7 cm

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards up together, then click. a) 20 x 10 x 6 = 1200 cm3 = 1.2 litres. b) 3 litres = 3000 cm3; base 600 cm2; 3000 / 600 = 5 cm. c) 1.5 litres = 1500 cm3; base 500 cm2; 1500 / 500 = 3 cm more, so 4 + 3 = 7 cm. Watch for children who write 1.5 / 500 or who answer c with 3 cm (the rise, not the new depth).
