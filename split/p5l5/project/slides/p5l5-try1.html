Your turn: whiteboards

Change litres to cm³ before you divide.

a Base 20 cm by 10 cm, water 6 cm deep. How many ℓ?

1.2 ℓ

b Base 30 cm by 20 cm. 3 ℓ of water. How deep?

5 cm

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 32.5 to 34.5. Write it, say the answer together on three, then click. a) 20 x 10 x 6 = 1200 cm3 = 1.2 litres. b) 3 litres = 3000 cm3; base 600 cm2; 3000 / 600 = 5 cm. Watch for children who write 3 / 600 without changing litres to cm3. (Two items only.)
