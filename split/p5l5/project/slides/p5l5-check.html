Warm-up check: 3 minutes

Whiteboards. Answer both, then be ready to say WHY.

1. A fish tank is 40 cm long, 20 cm wide and 25 cm tall. How many litres of water fill it to the top?

20 litres. 40 × 20 × 25 = 20 000 cm³ and 1000 cm³ = 1 litre.

2. A tank has a base 20 cm by 10 cm with water 6 cm deep. A 10-cm cube is dropped in and sinks. How far does the water rise? How do you know?

5 cm. The cube is 1000 cm³ and the base is 200 cm², so the rise is 1000 ÷ 200 = 5 cm.

Everyone does the check. Then some of us start on page 1 and some start on the Think Harder pages. Both are good choices, and you can swap.
