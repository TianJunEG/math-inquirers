Math Inquirers Level 5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5, a 4 x 4 x 4 cube). Ask first: "How many of the 125 cubes touch the outside? Which cubes have 3 painted faces? 2? 1? none?" Click row by row. Think-aloud: "The 8 corner cubes show 3 faces. Along each edge there are 5 cubes: the 2 ends are corners, so 3 are in the middle, with 2 painted faces. A cube has 12 edges: 12 x 3 = 36. Each face is a 5 by 5 square: take off the border, and the middle is 3 x 3 = 9 cubes with only 1 painted face. 6 faces: 54. What is left is a 3 x 3 x 3 block inside with no paint: 27. Check that they add up to 125." Why do we write 5 - 2? Because each edge loses its 2 corner cubes. For the worksheet cube the middle size will be 4 - 2.
