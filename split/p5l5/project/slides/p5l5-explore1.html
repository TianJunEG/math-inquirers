Explore: the 24-cube challenge

EXPLORE

  1. Take 24 one-centimetre cubes (or draw them on squared paper).
  2. Build a cuboid that uses ALL 24 cubes. Write its length, breadth and height.
  3. Build as many different cuboids as you can. Turning one round is not a new one.
  4. How do you know you have found them all?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs build or draw cuboids with exactly 24 cubes (online: dice, Lego or squared-paper drawings). Ask them to predict first: how many cuboids? Most guess 3 or 4. Let them record in any order at first; the next slide asks how they know they have all. Hint: start with a flat cuboid 1 cube high, then 2 cubes high. Early finishers: which one is most like a cube? Could any be a perfect cube? (No: 2 x 2 x 2 = 8 and 3 x 3 x 3 = 27, and 24 is between.)
