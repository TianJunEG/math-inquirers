Every cuboid from 30 cubes: list in order

Fix the SHORTEST edge. The other two edges must multiply to give the rest.

other two make 30

5 cuboids. Your turn: how many cuboids from 20 cubes?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach: the listing method from the Explore, with a new number. Click to reveal row by row; children say the pairs first. Think-aloud: "If the shortest edge is 1, the other two multiply to 30: 1 x 30, 2 x 15, 3 x 10, 5 x 6. I stop at 5 x 6 because the next pair would just be turned round. Shortest edge 2: the other two multiply to 15 and cannot be 1: 3 x 5. Shortest edge 3 needs two edges of at least 3 that multiply to 10: impossible. So I am finished: 5 cuboids." Your turn (20 cubes): 1x1x20, 1x2x10, 1x4x5, 2x2x5. The 20-cube answer is 4. Ask: "Why can the shortest edge never be bigger than the cube root?" only if time (3 x 3 x 3 = 27 is already more than 20).
