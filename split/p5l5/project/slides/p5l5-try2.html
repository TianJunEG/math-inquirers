Your turn: sinking objects

The object is completely under water.

a Base 30 cm by 10 cm. A stone makes the water rise 4 cm. Volume?

1200 cm³

b Base 25 cm by 20 cm. A 1000 cm³ block sinks. How far does the water rise?

2 cm

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 36.5 to 38.5. Whiteboards. a) 30 x 10 x 4 = 1200. b) base 500 cm2; 1000 / 500 = 2 cm. Ask for b: "Why is the rise small? (a wide base spreads the volume out)".
