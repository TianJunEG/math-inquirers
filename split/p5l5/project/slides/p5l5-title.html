LESSON 5 • WEEK 3

Volume Vault

Volume

TODAY WE WILL

Count cubes in layers, list every cuboid from a number of cubes, and solve tank puzzles: litres, water levels and stones that make the water rise.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find the volume of a cuboid by counting layers of 1-cm cubes and by length x breadth x height; list every cuboid that can be built from a number of cubes; solve water-tank problems (cm3 and litres, new water levels, the rise when an object sinks, pouring from one tank into another); and count the painted faces of a cube cut into small cubes. Thinking strategies taught today: MAKE A LIST IN ORDER, DRAW IT TO SCALE and WORK BACKWARDS from a water level. Materials: about 30 one-centimetre cubes (or dice, Lego bricks, sugar cubes) per pair, a ruler, squared paper, mini whiteboards. Tutor: a clear plastic box with a ruler taped to its side, water and a stone for the demonstration. Online: show the water rise live on camera (clear box, ruler on the side, drop in a stone). Children use dice or Lego for the Explore; with no cubes they draw layers on squared paper. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 9, explore 9 to 21, teaching 21 to 33 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 33 to 37, worksheet page 1 37 to 49, check together 49 to 51, short teaching before page 2 51 to 57, worksheet page 2 57 to 72 (alone), check together 72 to 76, talk time 76 to 82, exit ticket 82 to 90. Stretch and challenge are for children who finish early, or for home.
