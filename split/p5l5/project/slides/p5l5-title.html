LESSON 5 • WEEK 3

Volume Vault

Volume

TODAY WE WILL

Count cubes in layers, list every cuboid from a number of cubes, and solve tank puzzles: litres, water levels and stones that make the water rise.

TODAY'S TOOL

List every way

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 3.5 (opening, inside the 90-minute clock). Objective: Find the volume of a cuboid by counting layers of 1-cm cubes and by length x breadth x height; list every cuboid that can be built from a number of cubes; solve water-tank problems (cm3 and litres, new water levels, the rise when an object sinks, pouring from one tank into another); and count the painted faces of a cube cut into small cubes. Thinking strategies taught today: MAKE A LIST IN ORDER, DRAW IT TO SCALE and WORK BACKWARDS from a water level. Materials: about 30 one-centimetre cubes (or dice, Lego bricks, sugar cubes) per pair, a ruler, squared paper, mini whiteboards. Tutor: a clear plastic box with a ruler taped to its side, water and a stone for the demonstration. Online: show the water rise live on camera (clear box, ruler on the side, drop in a stone). Children use dice or Lego for the Explore; with no cubes they draw layers on squared paper. Time plan (the exact minutes of this lesson; every lesson adds up to 90): opening 0 to 3.5, sneak-peek puzzles 3.5 to 8 (one check for both puzzles, no volunteer round, nobody is marked), warm-up 8 to 14.5 (one game round, four quick questions), explore 14.5 to 28 (pair work 6 minutes at most, then two questions together; a Go further what-if is on the explore slide for any pair that finishes), teaching 28 to 40 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 40 to 44.5, workbook page 1 44.5 to 59.5 (the questions without a star; after question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder), check together 59.5 to 63, workbook page 2 63 to 70 (alone: start with the questions named at the top, then choose; starred parts are take-home; nobody has to finish), check together 70 to 72, talk time 72 to 77.5 (you may say pass; volunteers first; the first step of the Investigation is take-home), exit ticket 77.5 to 82.5, reflection 82.5 to 85 (pass is always OK), stamp typed in the chat and goodbye 85 to 86, and the buffer 86 to 90 (floating time: spend it first if you run late, never fill it on purpose). Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child and have no clock time. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and never splits a pair unless both children agree. Invite the WHOLE group, never one child: "when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation." SPINE: Today's tool is List every way. Count every case once with a systematic order; rule out repeats. Look for where you use it. Point at the card on page 1 of the workbook.
