E1: the cube takes up space

No cube: 40 × 30 × 6 = 7200 cm³

Cube under water: 10 × 10 × 6 = 600 cm³

7200 − 600 = 6600 cm³

6600 cm³ = 6.6 ℓ ✓

B3: the block takes up floor

Water: 30 × 20 × 10 = 6000 cm³

Floor left: 600 − 100 = 500 cm²

6000 ÷ 500 = 12 cm

12 is less than 15: the block sticks out ✓

Optional, if you finish early (no clock time): show it only while some pairs are working or have finished, never as a group slide at a fixed minute. Use after pairs have tried. E1: the cube is 10 cm tall but the water is only 6 cm deep, so only 6 cm of the cube is under water. Ask: what if the water were 12 cm deep? (Then the whole cube is under water: 40 x 30 x 12 - 1000 = 13 400 cm3.) B3: the water has the same volume as before, but the block takes away 10 x 10 = 100 cm2 of the floor, so the water is squeezed into 500 cm2 and rises higher. Always check that the block is taller than the water (15 > 12); if the water were deeper than the block, we would add the block as a solid instead.
