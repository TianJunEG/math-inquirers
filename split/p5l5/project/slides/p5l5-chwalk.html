Q1: the water is shared

Water: 30 × 20 × 30 = 18 000 cm³

Both floors: 600 + 1200 = 1800 cm²

18 000 ÷ 1800 = 10 cm

Check: 600 × 10 + 1200 × 10 = 18 000 ✓

Q2: the block takes up floor

Water: 30 × 20 × 10 = 6000 cm³

Floor left: 600 − 100 = 500 cm²

6000 ÷ 500 = 12 cm

12 is less than 15: the block sticks out ✓
