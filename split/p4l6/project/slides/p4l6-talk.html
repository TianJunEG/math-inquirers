Is turning 270° clockwise the same as turning 90° anticlockwise? How do you know?

Sentence starters:

“I noticed that …” “I tested it by …” “I know because …”

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 70 to 80. Pairs talk for 2 minutes, then test it standing up: everyone faces North and does both turns. Answer: yes, you end up facing the same way (both from N end at W), because 270° + 90° = 360°, a full turn. The turns are different sizes, but the direction you end up facing is the same. Second question if time: "Ben says the angle at 3 o’clock is smaller than at 9 o’clock because 3 is smaller than 9. Who agrees?" (both are 90°: 3 spaces the shorter way). Praise the reasons, not only the answers.
