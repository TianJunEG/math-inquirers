54–54.5 MIN

Corners of squares and rectangles: 90°

90° − 37° = 53°

90° − 20° − 45° = 25°

90° + 90° − 40° = 140°

Two squares: add both corners, then take away the overlap, which was counted twice.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5 has two parts; the third drawing, two squares, is an optional extension and is the idea behind Go further 9). Fold a square of paper to show the corner is a right angle. Click each answer after children try on whiteboards. Rectangle: the whole corner is 90°, so the missing part is 90 − 37 = 53°. Square cut in three: 90 − 20 − 45 = 25° (check 20 + 25 + 45 = 90). Two squares: the two right angles together make 180°, but the overlap of 40° is counted twice, so 180 − 40 = 140°. Act it out with two paper squares on top of each other.
