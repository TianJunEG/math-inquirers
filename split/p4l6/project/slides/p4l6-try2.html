c Smallest turn from E to NW?

135° anticl.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) 1 step: NE. b) A 3/4 turn is 270° = 6 steps clockwise from S: SW, W, NW, N, NE, E. Or think: 3/4 clockwise is the same as 1/4 anticlockwise, and 1/4 anticlockwise from S is E. c) Clockwise from E to NW is 5 steps (225°); anticlockwise is 3 steps (135°), so the smallest turn is 135° anticlockwise.
