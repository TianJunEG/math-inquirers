Challenge

1. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 3:30?

2. Five rays come out of one point and make 4 equal angles side by side. The angle between the two outside rays is 120°. How many angles can you find altogether? How many are right angles?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) 75°. At 3:30 the minute hand points to 6, but the hour hand is HALFWAY between 3 and 4 (half an hour has passed). From the hour hand to 4 is half a space (15°), from 4 to 6 is 2 spaces (60°): 75°. 2) 10 angles (4 small, 3 of two parts, 2 of three parts, 1 of four parts). Each small angle is 120 ÷ 4 = 30°, so the three-part angles are 90°: 2 right angles. More are in the question bank and on the Think Harder pages.
