Minutes 36 to 51.5. Pair task for Q1: each child estimates and measures two of the four angles, then checks the partner's two (about 4.5 minutes, not 8). Walk around and watch protractors: centre on the vertex, baseline on an arm, count from 0. Ask "Does your answer match your estimate?"
