LESSON 6 • WEEK 3 • 0–1.5 MIN

Angle Turns

Angles

TODAY WE WILL

Estimate and measure angles, find every angle in a fan, work out clock angles, turn on the 8-point compass and find missing angles in squares and rectangles.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Estimate and measure angles with a protractor, find every angle in a fan of rays, work out angles between clock hands (30° for each space) and turns of the minute hand (6° a minute), turn on the 8-point compass (45° steps) and work backwards from a turn, and find unknown angles in squares and rectangles. Thinking strategies today: ESTIMATE FIRST (compare with 90° and 180°), COUNT THE TURN (12 clock spaces, 8 compass steps), WORK IN ORDER (every angle in a fan), WORK BACKWARDS (undo a turn). Materials: A clear 180° protractor and a 30 cm ruler for each child (a plastic school protractor is fine, cheap to buy; families bring one from home; keep two or three spares), a paper clock with a split pin (or a real clock), a square of paper for checking right angles, mini whiteboards, a compass card on the floor or wall. Online: children use their own protractor and a paper clock at home; the screen is North for the turning games, and children stand and turn on camera. Time plan (90 minutes in all; every part has a budget and there is a 3-minute standing buffer inside the page-2 block). Opening 0 to 1.5. Sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 6.5 (puzzle 1 about 60 seconds, puzzle 2 about 90 seconds, then ONE hold-up moment for both papers; answers shown to the tutor only, on camera, nobody marked). Warm-up game 6.5 to 8.5 (one short round, about 90 seconds), quick questions 8.5 to 11.5 (three questions). Explore 11.5 to 23 (the only breakout of the first half: about 60 seconds each way, closed by a 60-second timer; a Go further what-if is on the slide from the start; two debrief questions). Teaching 23 to 30.5 (click to reveal each step and answer; the one "your turn" slide is optional: skip it if you are behind). Brain break 30.5 to 34 (water and toilet first, then the game; clock check at the end of the break: if you are more than 1.5 minutes behind, skip the next optional item, never a check, the exit ticket or Looking back). Worksheet 34 to 80: page 1 (Start here, in pairs) (the first 2 minutes are the protractor demo) 34 to 51.5, check together 51.5 to 53, short teaching before page 2 53 to 55.5, page 2 on your own 55.5 to 77.5 (the last 3 minutes are the standing buffer: if earlier parts ran long, spend the buffer first, then the choice questions, and never the checks, the exit ticket or Looking back), check together 77.5 to 80. Talk time 80 to 83.5 (one minute of pair talk, no breakout). Exit ticket 83.5 to 86.5. Looking back 86.5 to 90 (2 minutes of writing). This page ends at question 2. After question 2 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation have no fixed time slot: any child may choose them at any time, or take them home. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and pairs are not split unless both children agree. Online: page 1 is pair work, so allow about 2 minutes to move into and out of breakout rooms (or run page 1 as a whole-group screen-share if there are only 8 children).
