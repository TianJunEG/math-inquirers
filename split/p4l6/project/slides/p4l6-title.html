LESSON 6 • WEEK 3

Angle Turns

Angles

TODAY WE WILL

Estimate and measure angles, find every angle in a fan, work out clock angles, turn on the 8-point compass and find missing angles in squares and rectangles.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Estimate and measure angles with a protractor, find every angle in a fan of rays, work out angles between clock hands (30° for each space) and turns of the minute hand (6° a minute), turn on the 8-point compass (45° steps) and work backwards from a turn, and find unknown angles in squares and rectangles. Thinking strategies today: ESTIMATE FIRST (compare with 90° and 180°), COUNT THE TURN (12 clock spaces, 8 compass steps), WORK IN ORDER (every angle in a fan), WORK BACKWARDS (undo a turn). Materials: A protractor and a ruler for each child, a paper clock with a split pin (or a real clock), a square of paper for checking right angles, mini whiteboards, a compass card on the floor or wall. Online: children use their own protractor and a paper clock at home; the screen is North for the turning games, and children stand and turn on camera. Time plan: warm-up game 0 to 5, quick questions 5 to 8, explore 8 to 18, teaching 18 to 30 (click to reveal each step and answer; the "your turn" slides are optional, skip them if short of time), brain break 30 to 34, worksheet page 1 from 34 to 50 and check together 50 to 53, short teaching before page 2 from 53 to 58, worksheet page 2 from 58 to 76 and check together 76 to 80, talk time 80 to 85, exit ticket 85 to 90. The Challenge slides and the Think Harder pages are for early finishers and for take-home: they have no fixed time slot. Online: page 1 is pair work, so allow about 2 minutes to move into and out of breakout rooms (or run page 1 as a whole-group screen-share if there are only 8 children).
