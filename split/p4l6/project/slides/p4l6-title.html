Angle Turns

Angles

TODAY WE WILL

Estimate and measure angles, find every angle in a fan, work out clock angles, turn on the 8-point compass and find missing angles in squares and rectangles.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Estimate and measure angles with a protractor, find every angle in a fan of rays, work out angles between clock hands (30° for each space) and turns of the minute hand (6° a minute), turn on the 8-point compass (45° steps) and work backwards from a turn, and find unknown angles in squares and rectangles. Thinking strategies today: ESTIMATE FIRST (compare with 90° and 180°), COUNT THE TURN (12 clock spaces, 8 compass steps), WORK IN ORDER (every angle in a fan), WORK BACKWARDS (undo a turn). Materials: A protractor and a ruler for each child, a paper clock with a split pin (or a real clock), a square of paper for checking right angles, mini whiteboards, a compass card on the floor or wall. Online: children use their own protractor and a paper clock at home; the screen is North for the turning games, and children stand and turn on camera. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
