Q1: the hour hand moves too

Minute hand: pointing at 6.

Half an hour has passed, so the hour hand is halfway from 3 to 4.

Hour hand to 4: half a space = 15°. 4 to 6: 2 spaces = 60°.

15° + 60° = 75°

Q2: count in order

Each small angle: 120° ÷ 4 = 30°.

1 part: 4 angles. 2 parts: 3. 3 parts: 2. 4 parts: 1.

4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 10 angles.

3 parts = 90°: 2 right angles ✓
