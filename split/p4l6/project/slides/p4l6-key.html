29.5–30.5 MIN

Key idea: count the turn

A full turn is 360°: 4 right angles, 8 compass steps of 45°, 12 clock spaces of 30°. Estimate first, then measure.

Angles side by side add up. The corner of a square or rectangle is 90°.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Reading the wrong protractor scale (40° written as 140°) instead of starting from the 0 on the arm and checking with an estimate. (2) Counting the numbers on the clock instead of the spaces between them, or measuring the bigger way round (210° instead of 150° at 7 o’clock). (3) Compass turns: mixing up clockwise and anticlockwise, or counting the starting direction as the first 45° step.
