Smaller or bigger than a right angle? Estimate to the nearest 10°.

A: about 30°

B: about 100°

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards: children write an estimate for each before you click. Benchmarks: a right angle is 90°, a straight line is 180°, half a right angle is 45°. A is less than half a right angle: about 30°. B is just more than a right angle: about 100°. C is between a right angle and a straight line, nearer the straight line: about 145°. They measure with a protractor on the worksheet; here we only estimate. Estimates within 15° are good.
