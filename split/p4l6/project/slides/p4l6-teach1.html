Measure with a protractor

Estimate first: smaller than a right angle, so less than 90°.

Step 1. Centre on the vertex.

Step 2. Baseline along one arm.

Step 3. Count up from the 0 on that arm.

65°. Not 115°: our estimate says less than 90°.

Arms too short? Draw them longer with a ruler. The angle does not change.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Hold up a real protractor. Ask first: "Is this angle bigger or smaller than a right angle?" (smaller: estimate about 60°). Click through the steps. Think-aloud: "The bottom arm points to 0 on the INSIDE scale, so I count up the inside scale: 10, 20 … 60, 65. The outside scale says 115 at the same place, but 115 is more than a right angle and my estimate says less. So the angle is 65°." Online: children measure the angle on their screen with a real protractor if the screen is large enough, or on the worksheet later. Point out: the length of the arms does not change the angle.
