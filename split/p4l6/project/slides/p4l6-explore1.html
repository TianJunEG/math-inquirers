Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Take a paper clock with two hands.
  2. Set it to each o’clock time: 1 o’clock, 2 o’clock … 12 o’clock.
  3. Which times make a right angle between the hands? Check with a paper corner.
  4. Which time makes a straight line? What is the angle at 1 o’clock?

Go further (any time): What if the clock shows half past three (3:30)? Is the angle still 90°? Why not?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8 to 13. Pairs use a paper clock (online: a paper clock at home or a real clock held to the camera). Ask them to predict first: which times make a right angle? Most children say 3 o’clock; fewer think of 9 o’clock. They check each time with the corner of a square of paper. GO FURTHER (on the slide from minute 8, so nobody waits): What if the clock shows half past three (3:30)? Is the angle still 90°? Why not? Answer: No. It is 75°: at 3:30 the hour hand has moved halfway from 3 to 4, which is 15° past the 3. The minute hand points at 6 (180°) and the hour hand at 105°.
