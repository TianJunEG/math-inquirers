Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Take a paper clock with two hands.
  2. Set it to each o’clock time: 1 o’clock, 2 o’clock … 12 o’clock.
  3. Which times make a right angle between the hands? Check with a paper corner.
  4. Which time makes a straight line? What is the angle at 1 o’clock?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8 to 13. Pairs use a paper clock (online: a paper clock at home or a real clock held to the camera). Ask them to predict first: which times make a right angle? Most children say 3 o’clock; fewer think of 9 o’clock. They check each time with the corner of a square of paper. Early finishers: what is the angle at 1 o’clock? At 2 o’clock? Can you work it out without a protractor?
