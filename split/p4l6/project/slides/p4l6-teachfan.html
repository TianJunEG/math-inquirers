Find every angle: work in order

∠POQ means the angle at O between OP and OQ. How many angles can you find?

Step 1. Side by side: ∠POQ = 20°, ∠QOR = 50°

Step 2. Two together: ∠POR = 20° + 50° = 70°

3 angles. Angles side by side add up.

More rays? Count the small ones, then pairs side by side, then three together … until you reach the biggest.
