53–54 MIN

Clock angles: 12 spaces of 30°

Step 1. 360° ÷ 12 = 30° for each space.

Step 2. 2 o’clock: 2 spaces. 2 × 30° = 60°.

Step 3. 10 o’clock: the SHORTER way is 2 spaces: 60°.

Step 4. Think Harder: minute hand, 6° a minute.

Think Harder: 6:05 to 6:20 is 15 minutes: 15 × 6° = 90°

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q3). Link back to explore: 3 o’clock was 90° = 3 spaces, so one space is 30°. Click 1: 2 o’clock. Click 2 shows 10 o’clock: "Is it 10 spaces? No: go the shorter way, from 10 to 12, 2 spaces." Then the minute hand: it goes all the way round (360°) in 60 minutes, so 360 ÷ 60 = 6° a minute, or 30° every 5 minutes. Count on with a paper clock: 6:05 to 6:20 is 3 jumps of 5 minutes, 3 × 30° = 90°.
