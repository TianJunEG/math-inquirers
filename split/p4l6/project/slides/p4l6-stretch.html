Stretch

1. Mei faces W. She turns clockwise until she faces SE. How many degrees does she turn?

2. Four rays come out of one point, making 3 angles side by side: 25°, 40° and 25°. How many angles are there altogether? What is the biggest?

3. Ravi faces N and turns anticlockwise to face S. Then he turns anticlockwise again to face E. How many degrees has he turned altogether?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 225° (W → NW → N → NE → E → SE: 5 steps of 45°). 2) 6 angles: 25°, 40°, 25°, 65°, 65°, 90°; the biggest is 90°. 3) 270° (N to S anticlockwise is 180°, S to E anticlockwise is 90°).
