Think harder

1. Siti faces SE. She turns 225° clockwise. Which direction does she face now?

2. What is the angle between the clock hands at 10 o’clock? At 2 o’clock? What do you notice?

3. How many degrees does the minute hand turn from 7:15 to 7:50?

4. A robot starts facing N. Ben wants it to turn from N to W using two DIFFERENT cards from: 45° clockwise, 90° clockwise, 135° anticlockwise, 180°. Find ALL the pairs that work.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers (same four questions as the printed Think harder page): 1) N (225° is 5 steps of 45°: S, SW, W, NW, N) 2) 60° and 60°. Both are 2 spaces apart the shorter way. 3) 210° (35 minutes × 6°) 4) Two ways: 45° clockwise and 135° anticlockwise; or 90° clockwise and 180°.
