20.5–23 MIN

Explore: talk about it

1. Which o’clock times make a right angle?

2. Which time makes a straight line? What turn is that?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 20.5 to 23. Pencils down (or when the 60-second timer closes the rooms), then gather the pairs. Two debrief questions only. Answers: 1) 3 o’clock and 9 o’clock (3 spaces apart either way). 2) 6 o’clock: a straight line, a half turn, 180°. The third question (the angle at 1 o’clock, 30°: a full turn is 360° and the clock face has 12 equal spaces, so 360 ÷ 12 = 30; check: 3 o’clock is 3 spaces, 3 × 30 = 90) is not asked; the first teaching slide after the break uses it. Many children say 12 o’clock is a straight line too: the hands are on top of each other, so the angle is 0°. Keep the 30° idea: we use it after the break.
