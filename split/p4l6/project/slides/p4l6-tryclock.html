Your turn: clock angles

Count the spaces the shorter way. 1 space = 30°.

a The angle between the hands at 9 o’clock?

90°

b The angle between the hands at 6 o’clock?

180°

c The minute hand turns from 4:00 to 4:40. How many degrees?

240°

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) From 9 to 12 is 3 spaces: 90°. b) 6 spaces: 180°, a straight line. c) 40 minutes × 6° = 240°, or 8 jumps of 5 minutes × 30°. Note: c is more than a half turn; that is fine for a turn of the hand.
