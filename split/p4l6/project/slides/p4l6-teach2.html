25.5–28 MIN

Compass turns: count 45° steps

Face NW. Turn 135° clockwise. Which way do you face?

Step 1. From one point to the next is 45°: 8 steps make 360°.

Step 2. 135° = 45° + 45° + 45°: that is 3 steps.

Step 3. Count 3 steps clockwise from NW: N, NE, E.

Face E.

Another: face E, turn 225° anticlockwise. 5 steps: NE, N, NW, W, SW.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Children stand facing the front (North) and act it out. Think-aloud: "I face North-West. Each step on the compass is 45 degrees, so 135 degrees is 3 steps. Clockwise is the way clock hands go: North, North-East, East. I face East." Common mistake: counting NW itself as the first step. Point with a finger: move first, then count. Then the second example: 225° is 5 steps; anticlockwise from E: NE, N, NW, W, SW. Getting back from SW to E is 225° clockwise, or 135° anticlockwise, the shorter way.
