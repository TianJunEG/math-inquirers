ADDED ITEM fan (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Triangles by size"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: List every way. SAY: Name the base points B, C, D, E. A triangle needs the top point and two base points. List the pairs in order. ASK: How do you know you have not missed one or counted one twice? LISTEN FOR: An ordered list BC, BD, BE, CD, CE, DE, or a count by size 1, 2, 3. Listen for a rule such as "choose 2 base points". Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) 3 triangles of size 1, 2 of size 2, 1 of size 3. (b) 6 triangles altogether. WHY: Pairs of base points: BC, BD, BE, CD, CE, DE gives 6. By size: 3 + 2 + 1 = 6. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): The top point is joined to 5 points on the base line. How many triangles? Answer: 10 (4 + 3 + 2 + 1).
