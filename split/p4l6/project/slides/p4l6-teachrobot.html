Robot turns: one card at a time

A robot faces E. Cards: 45° anticlockwise, then 90° anticlockwise, then 180°.

Step 1. E → 45° anticlockwise → NE

Step 2. NE → 90° anticlockwise (2 steps) → NW

Step 3. NW → 180° (4 steps) → SE. The robot faces SE.

Work backwards: Mei turned 90° clockwise and now faces S. Undo it: turn 90° anticlockwise from S → E. She started facing E.

Find ALL pairs: list every pair of cards, then test each one. Do not stop at the first that works.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4d works backwards; Q6 is the robot). Act it out: one child is the robot, the group reads the cards. To undo a turn, turn the same amount the other way, like taking off shoes after socks. Check Mei: start E, 90° clockwise is S ✓. For "find all pairs": with three cards A, B, C the pairs are A and B, A and C, B and C. Example (not on the worksheet): cards 45° clockwise, 90° anticlockwise, 180°, from N to E. 45 + 180: SW, not this one. 45 cw and 90 acw: NW, not this one. 90 acw and 180: E ✓. Only one pair works.
