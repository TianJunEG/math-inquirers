Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 4 to 6 (one short round, about 2 minutes: the Warm-up check came first). Robot turns. Everyone stands and faces the front of the room: that is North (online: the screen is North). Read each call; children turn, then point and say the direction they face. Click to show it. Start every call from North. Then play faster rounds without the slide: "From where you are now: quarter turn anticlockwise! Half turn! 45° clockwise!" Children who end up facing the wrong way watch a partner and try again. Ask: "How many quarter turns make a full turn?" (4) "How many 45° turns make a quarter turn?" (2) "Is a half turn clockwise different from a half turn anticlockwise?" (no: you end up facing the same way). Listen for children who say "clockwise is the way the clock hands go". Online: on a mirrored self-view a turn looks reversed, so say "turn to your right" for clockwise, and tell children to ignore their own picture.
