Minutes 5 to 8. Whiteboards up together, then click. 1) 90°. 2) 180°: two right angles. 3) 4, so a full turn is 4 × 90° = 360°. 4) W: stand up and do it if anyone hesitates. 5) A 3/4 turn is 3 × 90° = 270°, which is more than 250°. Ask: "How many degrees in a 1/4 turn? A 1/8 turn?" (90°, 45°: we need 45° today for the compass).
