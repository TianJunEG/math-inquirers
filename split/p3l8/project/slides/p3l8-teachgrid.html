Parallel and perpendicular on a square grid

Step 1. PQ goes 3 squares right and 1 square up, again and again.

Step 2. Parallel: use the SAME steps from R: 3 right, 1 up.

Step 3. Perpendicular: swap the numbers and turn: 1 right, 3 down from S. Check it with your checker ✓

Lines that go along the grid lines are easy: across is perpendicular to up and down.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5 asks for parallel and perpendicular lines on a grid; these lines use different steps). Click 1: count the steps along PQ. Click 2: from R, copy the same steps: the new line never meets PQ. Click 3: swap 3 and 1 and turn: 1 right and 3 down. Put a paper checker on S to show the right angle. Think-aloud: "Parallel lines go the same way, so they have the same steps. For perpendicular, I swap the two numbers and turn the line round." Watch for children who swap but do not turn (1 right, 3 up): check it with the checker, it is not a right angle.
