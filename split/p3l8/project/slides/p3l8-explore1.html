Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Take any scrap of paper, even a torn one.
  2. Fold it once. Then fold the fold exactly onto itself.
  3. The folded corner is your right-angle checker.
  4. Hunt: find 3 right angles, 1 smaller angle and 1 bigger angle.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Predict first: "If I fold a torn, wiggly piece of paper twice, will the corner be a right angle?" Then everyone folds a checker (show it under the visualiser or on camera). Children hunt round the room (or at home) and record on a whiteboard: 3 right angles, 1 smaller, 1 bigger. Hints: "Put the corner of the checker into the angle. Do both arms line up?" "If one arm is hidden under the checker, the angle is smaller." Finished early: find an angle that is exactly 2 right angles (a straight line).
