What makes an angle bigger?

Which angle is bigger, P or Q? Guess first.

Step 1. Put the checker on P. The second arm is under the checker: P is smaller than a right angle.

Step 2. Put the checker on Q. The second arm is outside the checker: Q is bigger than a right angle.

Q is bigger, even with short arms. Size = how far the arms open.

Teach. Ask first: "Which angle is bigger?" Many children choose P because its arms are long. Do not correct yet. Click 1: the checker on P (one arm along the bottom arm): the other arm is hidden under the checker, so P is smaller than a right angle. Click 2: the checker on Q: the other arm is outside, so Q is bigger than a right angle. Click 3: the conclusion. Think-aloud: "An angle shows how much one arm turns to reach the other. Long arms or short arms, the turn is the same. To compare, I always line the checker up with one arm and look where the other arm goes."
