Angle and Line Detectives

Angles, Perpendicular and Parallel Lines

TODAY WE WILL

Check angles with a right-angle checker, count angles without missing any, and spot perpendicular and parallel lines.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Compare angles with a right angle using a folded-paper checker, count the angles at a point and the right angles in shapes and figures in an organised way, find a pattern in the counts, and find and draw perpendicular and parallel lines. Heuristics taught today: (1) count in an organised way (small angles first; visit every corner or meeting point), (2) look for a pattern. Materials: scrap paper to fold a right-angle checker (one per child), a ruler, mini whiteboards, square grid paper. Online: each child folds a checker on camera and does the right-angle hunt at home; annotate the grid slides with the pen tool. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before page 2 and a check-together slide after each page, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90. Stretch and Challenge are for early finishers.
