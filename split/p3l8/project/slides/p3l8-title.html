LESSON 8 • WEEK 4 • 0–1.5 MIN

Angle and Line Detectives

Angles, Perpendicular and Parallel Lines

TODAY WE WILL

Check angles with a right-angle checker, count angles without missing any, and spot perpendicular and parallel lines.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Compare angles with a right angle using a folded-paper checker, count the angles at a point and the right angles in shapes and figures in an organised way, find a pattern in the counts, and find and draw perpendicular and parallel lines. Heuristics taught today: (1) count in an organised way (small angles first; visit every corner or meeting point), (2) look for a pattern. Materials: scrap paper to fold a right-angle checker (one per child), a ruler, mini whiteboards, square grid paper. Online: each child folds a checker on camera and does the right-angle hunt at home; annotate the grid slides with the pen tool. Print the workbook before the lesson. Pairs work in one breakout room for 10 minutes, then return for the check slide. Time plan (90 minutes, with a named 3-minute float inside talk time): opening 0 to 1.5, sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 4.5 (own paper, nobody marked, one show-me for both), warm-up game 4.5 to 7.5 (two rounds) and three quick questions 7.5 to 10.5, explore 10.5 to 24 (a Go further what-if is on the explore slide from the start; pairs go to pre-assigned breakout rooms), teaching 24 to 34.5 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 34.5 to 39.5, worksheet 39.5 to 70.5 (page 1 in pairs, short teaching, page 2 on their own, a 60-second self-check slide after each page; after question 3 of page 1 any child may choose Think harder or the Investigation; from minute 67.5 everyone chooses), talk time 70.5 to 78.5 (volunteers first, pass is fine), exit ticket 78.5 to 84.5, look back 84.5 to 87.5, stamp and close 87.5 to 90. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child; the tutor never announces who is on which page and invites the whole group, never one child. If the class is behind, children finish the last question of each page at home; the check slides cover it.
