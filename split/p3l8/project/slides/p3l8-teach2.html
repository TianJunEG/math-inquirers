Right angles inside a shape

How many right angles are inside this L-shape?

Step 1. Put a dot where you start. Go round and check every corner with your checker: 5 corners are right angles inside.

Step 2. The dent corner: inside the shape, the angle is bigger than a right angle (3 right angles put together!).

6 corners, but only 5 right angles inside.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Ask first: "How many corners? How many right angles inside?" Many children say 6 and 6. Click 1: the start dot and the 5 square corners, checked one by one. Click 2: the dent corner: put the checker INSIDE the shape at that corner: the inside angle is much bigger (it goes three-quarters of the way round). Click 3: the answer. Think-aloud: "I put a dot where I start so I visit every corner once. I check each one: is the inside a right angle? A dent corner points into the shape, so inside it is bigger."
