Key idea: count in order, look for a pattern

Visit every corner or meeting point, and count small angles first. When the counts grow, look for a pattern.

An angle’s size is how far it opens, not how long its arms are. Check with your checker.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key ideas together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Thinking a turned right angle (Q1 a) is not a right angle, or that longer arms make a bigger angle (Q1 d and f). Use the checker every time. (2) Counting every corner as a right angle in Q2: dent corners are bigger inside. The roof top of the house IS a right angle: check it. (3) Counting angles at random in Q3: count by size. (4) In Q6, counting only the 4 small squares' corners and missing angles, or counting a straight line as a right angle.
