Answers: 1) For example a kite-like shape: draw a right angle along the grid lines with arms of different lengths, then join the ends through a fourth point placed outside so no other corner is square. Check every corner. 2) No. If two corners on the bottom side were right angles, both other sides would go straight up and be parallel, so they would never meet to close the triangle. 3) 4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 10.
