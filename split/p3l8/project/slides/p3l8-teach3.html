Perpendicular and parallel lines

Perpendicular lines meet at a right angle.

Parallel lines stay the same distance apart. They never meet, even if we make them longer.

Step 1. List every pair: a and b, a and c, b and c.

Step 2. a and b: perpendicular. a and c: perpendicular.

Step 3. b and c: parallel.

The letter F: 1 pair of parallel lines and 2 pairs of perpendicular lines.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach the two words with arms: one arm up, one arm out sideways: perpendicular. Both arms up: parallel. On the parallel picture, the dotted ends show the lines carrying on: they never meet.
