27–29 MIN

Count the angles: small ones first

4 lines start at one point. How many angles can you find?

Step 1. Made of 1 small angle: 3

Step 2. Made of 2 small angles: 2

Step 3. Made of 3 small angles: 1

3 + 2 + 1 = 6 angles

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach heuristic 1: count in an organised way. Ask first: "How many angles?" Most children say 3. Click through the sizes. Think-aloud: "There are 3 small angles between neighbouring lines. But two small angles side by side also make an angle: the first two together, the last two together: 2. And all three together make 1 big angle. I count by size, smallest first, so I never miss one or count one twice: 3 + 2 + 1 = 6." This is a parallel example: the worksheet has 6 lines.
