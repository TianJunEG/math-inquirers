1. A long rectangle is cut into 6 squares in a row by 5 lines. How many right angles can you find?

2. Can a 6-sided shape have 6 right angles inside it? What is the most right angles a 6-sided shape can have inside?

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use as a pair challenge or in talk time. Ask for the reason every time. Answers: 1) 24 (4 at the corners; each of the 5 lines makes 2 right angles at the top and 2 at the bottom: 4 + 5 × 4 = 24). 2) No. The most is 5, for example an L shape. More are in the question bank.
