Right angles where lines meet

How many right angles can you find in this picture?

Step 1. List every point where lines meet: 4 corners and 2 middle points.

Step 2. Each corner has 1 right angle: 4.

Step 3. Each middle point has 2 right angles, one on each side of the line: 2 + 2 = 4.

4 + 4 = 8 right angles
