Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Everyone stands. For each clock children make the hands with their arms (one arm straight up for the minute hand at 12, the other arm for the hour hand), then say "right angle", "smaller" or "bigger". Click to show the answer (clicks go clock by clock). Answers: 3 o’clock right angle; 1 o’clock smaller; 5 o’clock bigger; 9 o’clock right angle; 10 o’clock smaller; 4 o’clock bigger. Ask: "Why are 3 o’clock and 9 o’clock both right angles?" (The hour hand is a quarter of the way round, one way or the other.) "Which is bigger: 4 o’clock or 5 o’clock?" (5). Watch for children who think 9 o’clock is bigger because 9 is a bigger number.
