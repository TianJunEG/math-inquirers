Challenge: walk-through

Q1: try smaller ones, find a pattern

1 square: 4 right angles. 2 squares: 8.

3 squares: 12. Each new line adds 4.

6 squares have 5 lines: 4 + 5 × 4

= 24 right angles ✓

Q2: draw it and check

Try it: draw 6 square corners on grid paper. What do you always get?

You always get a dent (a corner that is bigger inside).

An L shape: 6 corners, 5 right angles.

So the most is 5 ✓

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use after pairs have tried. Q1: when a problem is big, try smaller ones and look for a pattern: 4, 8, 12 … goes up by 4 because each new line meets the top and the bottom, with 2 right angles at each. Q2: let children try to draw 6 square corners along the grid lines: they always get a dent. A proof is not expected: children see that 6 square corners always give a dent, and the best they can draw is the L shape with 5 square corners and 1 dent.
