Which letters have BOTH a pair of parallel lines and a pair of perpendicular lines?

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Minutes 80 to 86. Answer: F and H. F: the top and middle lines are parallel, and each meets the upright line at a right angle. H: the two upright lines are parallel, and the middle line is perpendicular to both. V: two lines that meet, not at a right angle. Z: the top and bottom are parallel, but the slanted line is not perpendicular to them. Extension: how many right angles are in a square drawn as a noughts-and-crosses grid?
