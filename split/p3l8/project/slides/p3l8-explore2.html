Explore: talk about it

1. Why is the folded corner a right angle, even with torn paper?

2. Is an angle bigger if its arms are longer?

3. Where did you find angles bigger than a right angle?

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Gather the group. Answers: 1) The first fold makes a straight line (a straight angle). Folding that line exactly onto itself cuts the straight angle into 2 equal parts, and each part is a right angle. The wiggly edges do not matter. 2) No. The size of an angle is how far the arms open (how much one arm turns to reach the other), not how long the arms are. Show two pencils opened the same amount, then slide one pencil out: the angle does not change. 3) For example a door opened wide, an open laptop, a clock at 5 o’clock, the corners of a hexagon tile.
