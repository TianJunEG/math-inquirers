3 MIN CHECK

Warm-up check

Two new puzzles. Whiteboard: your answer AND one reason. Hold up together.

Q1. A tutor wants to split 31 children into equal teams, with more than 1 child in each team and more than 1 team. Is that possible? Why?

No. 31 is prime: its only factors are 1 and 31, so the only equal groups are 1 team of 31 or 31 teams of 1.

Q2. Which has more factors, 36 or 37? How do you know?

36 has 9 factors (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36). 37 is prime and has only 2 (1 and 37). A bigger number does not mean more factors.
