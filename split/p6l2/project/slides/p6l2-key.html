Key idea: exactly two factors

A prime has exactly 2 factors: 1 and itself. Test 2, 3, 5, 7, … until d × d is bigger than the number.

n mod d is the remainder. n mod d = 0 means d is a factor of n.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Saying 1 is prime, or that 2 is not prime because it is even. (2) Missing factors by not working in pairs, or counting the middle factor of a square number twice (6 in 36). (3) Stopping a factor tree at a number that is not prime (72 = 2 × 2 × 18), or reading mod as division (thinking 10 mod 4 is 2.5 instead of the remainder 2).
