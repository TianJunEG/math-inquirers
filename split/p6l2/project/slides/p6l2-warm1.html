Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Pairs race; show one card at a time and click to reveal its pairs (clicks go card 1 to card 6). Ask: "How do you know you have them all?" Listen for working in order (1, 2, 3, 4 …) and stopping when the pairs meet. Card 2: 4 × 4 is one pair, so 16 has 5 factors (1, 2, 4, 8, 16). Card 5: 29 has only the pair 1 × 29, so it is PRIME: that is today's word. Try 2, 3, 4, 5 on 29 to prove it. Card 4: 8 factors; card 3: 6 factors; card 1: 6; card 6: 8.
