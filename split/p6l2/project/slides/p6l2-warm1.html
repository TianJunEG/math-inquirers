6.5–10 MIN

Warm-up: Factor pair hunt

Find every pair of whole numbers that multiply to make the number. One card has only ONE pair.

Card 1

12

1×12, 2×6, 3×4

Card 2

16

1×16, 2×8, 4×4

Card 3

24

1×24, 2×12, 3×8, 4×6

Card 4

29

only 1×29

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6.5 to 10. Pairs work together (no racing, no points); show one card at a time and click to reveal its pairs (clicks go card 1 to card 4; four cards only, so the warm-up fits). Ask: "How do you know you have them all?" Listen for working in order (1, 2, 3, 4 …) and stopping when the pairs meet. Card 2: 4 × 4 is one pair, so 16 has 5 factors (1, 2, 4, 8, 16). Card 4: 29 has only the pair 1 × 29, so it is PRIME: that is today's word. Try 2, 3, 4, 5 on 29 to prove it. Card 3: 24 has 8 factors; card 1: 12 has 6 factors.
