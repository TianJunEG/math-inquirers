Factors come in pairs

Find all the factors of 18.

pair
factors
1 × 18
1 and 18
2 × 9
2 and 9
3 × 6
3 and 6

Work in order: 1, 2, 3, 4 … Test each number. Write BOTH numbers of each pair.

4 and 5 do not divide 18. The next pair would be 6 × 3, which we already have, so we stop.

18 has 6 factors: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Think-aloud: "Factors of 18. 1 × 18, so 1 and 18. 2 × 9, so 2 and 9. 3 × 6. Does 4 go into 18? No. 5? No. The next is 6, and I already have 6. The pairs have met, so I stop." A prime has exactly two factors: 1 and itself. 18 is not prime. 1 is not prime (one factor only). A square number such as 36 has the pair 6 × 6, and 6 is counted once, so 36 has 9 factors.
