Story break: Kamal al-Din al-Farisi

Kamal al-Din al-Farisi • about 1260 to about 1320 • Persia (Tabriz, now in Iran)

Kamal al-Din al-Farisi lived about 700 years ago in Persia, near Tabriz in what is now Iran. His teacher told him to study a famous book on light by Ibn al-Haytham. He studied it so well that his teacher asked him to write a revised version, and he wrote that some of the old ideas were wrong. To study rainbows he tested light in a clear sphere filled with water, inside a dark room. He also worked with amicable numbers, two numbers where the proper factors of each add up to the other, such as 220 and 284. He gave a new proof of a rule by Thabit ibn Qurra for finding them, and he used prime factors and the sieve of Eratosthenes to check that 1151 is prime. Historians still discuss how complete his proof was.

Try it (optional, no pressure)

Write 284 as a product of primes. Then list its factors smaller than 284 and add them. What number do you get?
