Goldbach pairs: sum of two primes

Write 20 as a sum of two primes, in every way.

Step 1. List the primes below 20: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19.

Step 2. For each prime p, is 20 − p prime? 3 + 17 yes. 7 + 13 yes. Others: 20 − 5 = 15, 20 − 11 = 9 no.

20 = 3 + 17 = 7 + 13 (2 ways)

Only go as far as half of 20: after 10, the pairs repeat in reverse (17 + 3).

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 57 to 59. Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4). Goldbach wrote in 1742 that every even number above 2 is the sum of two primes. No one has found a counterexample, and no one has proved it. Check primes up to 10 only: 3, 7 (5 gives 15, 2 gives 18). For odd totals: one of the two numbers must be even, and the only even prime is 2.
