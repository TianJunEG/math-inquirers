Count the factors of a square

How many factors do 16 and 36 have? What do you notice?

Step 1. 16: 1 × 16, 2 × 8, 4 × 4. Factors 1, 2, 4, 8, 16: 5 factors.

Step 2. 36: 1 × 36, 2 × 18, 3 × 12, 4 × 9, 6 × 6. 9 factors.

Step 3. Both counts are ODD: the middle pair is a number times itself.

Page 2, Q5 asks for numbers with exactly 3 factors. Which squares might work?

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5). Do NOT give the Q5 answer. Say: "36 has 9 factors; 16 has 5. A number with exactly 3 factors must be a square, because the count is odd. Which squares have only 3? Try 4, 9, 16, 25, 36 yourself." Children find 4, 9, 25, 49 and notice they are squares of primes.
