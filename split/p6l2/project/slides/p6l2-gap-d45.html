ADDED ITEM d45 (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Tests for 5 and 9 together"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: List every way. SAY: A multiple of 45 is in the 5 table and in the 9 table. What does the 5 table tell you about the last digit? What does the 9 table tell you about the digit sum? ASK: How many choices are there for the last digit? For each choice, what must the second digit be? LISTEN FOR: Last digit 0 or 5. Digit sum 6 + a + 3 + b = 9 + a + b must be in the 9 table, so a + b is 0, 9 or 18. With b = 0: a = 0 or 9. With b = 5: a = 4. Listen for an organised list of the cases. Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: 6 030, 6 930 and 6 435. WHY: The last digit is 0 or 5. If it is 0, the second digit a has 9 + a a multiple of 9, so a = 0 or 9: 6 030 and 6 930. If it is 5, 14 + a is a multiple of 9, so a = 4: 6 435. Check: 6 435 = 45 × 143.
