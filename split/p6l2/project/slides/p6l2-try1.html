c Is 119 prime?

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) 5 + 7 = 12, so 3 divides it: 57 = 3 × 19. b) Not even, 7 + 1 = 8 so not 3, not 5, 7 × 10 = 70 so 71 is not 7 times anything; 8 × 8 = 64 and 9 × 9 = 81 > 71, so test up to 7 only: prime. c) 7 × 17 = 119. Many children say 119 is prime: it is the classic trap.
