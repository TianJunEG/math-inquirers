2. Find the smallest number that has exactly 6 factors.

3. Write 100 as the sum of two primes in every possible way.

4. Write 36 as the sum of two primes in every way. Then find ALL the numbers from 1 to 50 with exactly 3 factors. What is the next one after 50?

Answers (the same four questions as the printed Think harder page): 1) 7: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 (8 × 8 counts once). 2) 12 (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12). Every number below 12 has fewer than 6 factors. 3) 6 ways: 3 + 97, 11 + 89, 17 + 83, 29 + 71, 41 + 59, 47 + 53. 4) 36 = 5 + 31 = 7 + 29 = 13 + 23 = 17 + 19 (4 ways). Exactly 3 factors: 4, 9, 25, 49 (squares of primes). Next after 50: 121 (11 × 11).
