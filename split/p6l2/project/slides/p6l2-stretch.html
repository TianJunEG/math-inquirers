For early finishers

Stretch

1. Twin primes differ by 2, like 11 and 13. Find all the twin prime pairs below 50.

2. Which number below 30 has the most factors? How many?

3. A number between 20 and 40 leaves remainder 1 when divided by 2, by 3 and by 4. Find both possible numbers.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 6 pairs: 3 and 5, 5 and 7, 11 and 13, 17 and 19, 29 and 31, 41 and 43. 2) 24, with 8 factors (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24); 20 and 28 have 6. 3) 25 and 37: each is 1 more than a multiple of 12 (24 + 1 and 36 + 1).
