Seven different digits adding to 42 must be 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 (the biggest seven digits add to exactly 42), so there is no 0. The number is between 6 950 000 and 7 049 999, so it starts 6 9 with 5, 7 or 8 next; the smallest is 6 95 then 3, 4, 8, 7 (it must end in an odd digit).
