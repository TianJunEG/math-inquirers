Think harder: Regroup first, then work out

Tool: Try something simpler

(a) Find 8 × 17 × 125. Group the numbers first.

(b) Do not work out the answers. Which of these are odd?

(i) 3 × 7 × 11 × 5 (ii) 13 × 14 + 15 (iii) 25 + 36 + 47 (iv) 1 001 × 2 002 − 3

(a) 17 000.

(b) (i), (ii) and (iv) are odd. (iii) is even.

ADDED ITEM parity (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Regroup first, then work out"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Try something simpler. SAY: Look for two numbers that make a friendly number. Then say whether each part is odd or even before you add. ASK: Which two numbers in (a) make 1 000? What rule do you use for odd times odd, even times anything, and odd plus even? LISTEN FOR: 8 × 125 = 1 000 first. For (b), listen for "odd x odd is odd", "even x anything is even", "even + odd is odd", "odd + odd is even". Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) 17 000. (b) (i), (ii) and (iv) are odd. (iii) is even. WHY: 8 × 125 = 1 000, then 1 000 × 17 = 17 000. (i) all odd factors: odd. (ii) 13 × 14 is even, plus 15 is odd. (iii) 25 + 36 is odd, plus 47 is even. (iv) 1 001 × 2 002 is even, minus 3 is odd. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): Find 4 × 29 × 25.
