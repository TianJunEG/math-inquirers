Key idea: test both sides, check every clue

Place value tells you what each digit is worth. To get closest to a target, test the best number just below AND just above.

Riddles: start with the clue that fixes the most digits. Check every clue at the end.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Leaving out zeros that hold a place (340 006 for 3 040 006), or mixing up the value of a digit (3000) with how many thousands a number has (4053). (2) Looking only on one side of the target, for example trying only numbers that start with 6 for "closest to 6 000 000". (3) Working from left to right and ignoring the order of operations (6 + 4 × 5 read as 50 instead of 26).
