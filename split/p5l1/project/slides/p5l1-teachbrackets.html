Brackets change the answer

Brackets first. Then × and ÷ from left to right. Then + and − from left to right.

No brackets: 7 + 5 × 4 − 3 = 7 + 20 − 3

24

(7 + 5) × 4 − 3 = 12 × 4 − 3

45

7 + 5 × (4 − 3) = 7 + 5 × 1

12

Same numbers, same signs, three different answers!
