Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) 12 ÷ 4 + 2 = 5. b) 8 ÷ 4 + 2 = 4, and 20 − 4 = 16. c) Many ways: (8 − 6) × 3 × 4, 6 × (3 × 4 − 8), (6 ÷ 3) × (4 + 8), 3 × (6 + 8 ÷ 4). Collect several and check each one aloud. Strategy for targets: think of a multiplication that makes the target (24 = 2 × 12 = 3 × 8), then try to build the parts.
