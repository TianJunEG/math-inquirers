Worked example: closest to 5 000 000

Use all seven cards once each. Make the number closest to 5 000 000.

A 4 or a 5 must go first. Test BOTH.

Step 1. Just above: 5, then the smallest digits: 5 012 478. 5 012 478 − 5 000 000 = 12 478

Step 2. Just below: 4, then the biggest digits: 4 875 210. 5 000 000 − 4 875 210 = 124 790

12 478 is the smaller gap, so 5 012 478 is closest.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 30 to 32.5. Test both sides: today's main strategy. Ask first: "Which card goes first?" Some children say only 5. Click step 1, then ask "Are we sure? What about just below?" Click step 2. Think-aloud: "After a 5 I want the number as SMALL as possible, so the next digits go up: 0, 1, 2, 4, 7, 8. After a 4 I want it as BIG as possible, so the digits go down: 8, 7, 5, 2, 1, 0. Then I compare the gaps by subtracting." Point out that this time the number just above wins, but on the worksheet it may be the other way: always test both.
