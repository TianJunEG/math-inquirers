Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6.5 to 9.5. Million Maker. Children draw seven boxes (M, HTh, TTh, Th, H, T, O) on a whiteboard. Shuffle digit cards 0 to 9 and turn over one at a time (online: hold it up to the camera). After EACH card, children write it in one empty box at once and cannot move it. After seven cards, each child reads their number aloud; the one closest to 5 000 000 wins. Play ONE round only (the clock has room for one). Click to show the thinking question. Talk: "A 4 or a 5 belongs in the millions box: they are next to the target. After a 4, you want BIG digits next (4 9 8 …). After a 5, you want SMALL digits next (5 0 1 …)." Listen for children who say "it depends whether I start with 4 or 5". That is today's big idea: test both sides of a target.
