Minutes 6 to 9. Read each aloud, whiteboards up together, then click to show. 1) Watch for 340 006: the zeros hold the hundred thousands, thousands, hundreds and tens places. 2) The 7 is in the hundred thousands place. 3) Dividing by 1000 moves every digit three places to the right. 4) The hundreds digit is 5, so round up: 849 thousands becomes 850 thousands. 5) × and ÷ first: 8 + 20 − 3 = 25. Ask: "Who got 27 or 57? What did you do first?"
