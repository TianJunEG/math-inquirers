Numbers to 10 million and the four operations

TODAY'S TOOL

Guess and check

Focus habit: Check

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 2 (opening, inside the 90-minute clock). Objective: Read, write and compare numbers up to 10 million; solve digit-card and digit-riddle puzzles with place value (testing the best number just below AND just above a target); estimate to check large calculations; and use the order of operations and brackets to hit target numbers. Thinking strategies taught today: TEST BOTH SIDES of a target, USE THE CLUES IN ORDER and check every clue, ESTIMATE by rounding, and WRITE THE RANGE FIRST before a "find all". Materials: Digit cards 0 to 9 (one set per child), a 7-column place-value mat (M, HTh, TTh, Th, H, T, O), mini whiteboards, a calculator for the explore (every child needs one; a phone calculator is fine), workbook. Online: children cut out paper digit cards and draw a 7-box mat before the lesson and use them in the lesson (nothing is held up to the camera); the tutor writes on the slides; pairs use breakout rooms for the explore and page 1. Time plan (the exact minutes of this lesson; every lesson adds up to 90): opening 0 to 2, sneak-peek puzzles 2 to 6.5 (one check for both puzzles, no volunteer round, nobody is marked), warm-up 6.5 to 12.5 (one game round, four quick questions), explore 12.5 to 26.5 (pair work 6 minutes at most, then two questions together; a Go further what-if is on the explore slide for any pair that finishes), teaching 26.5 to 37.5 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 37.5 to 42, workbook page 1 42 to 56.5 (the questions without a star; after question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder), check together 56.5 to 60.5, short teaching before page 2 60.5 to 63.5, workbook page 2 63.5 to 71.5 (alone: start with the questions named at the top, then choose; starred parts are take-home; nobody has to finish), check together 71.5 to 74.5, talk time 74.5 to 80 (you may say pass; volunteers first; the first step of the Investigation is take-home), exit ticket 80 to 84.5, reflection 84.5 to 87 (pass is always OK), stamp typed in the chat and goodbye 87 to 88, and the buffer 88 to 90 (floating time: spend it first if you run late, never fill it on purpose). Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child and have no clock time. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and never splits a pair unless both children agree. Invite the WHOLE group, never one child: "when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation." SPINE: Today's tool is Guess and check. Guess, check, adjust using how far off you were; stop when every condition holds. Look for where you use it. Point at the card on page 1 of the workbook. HABIT: Focus habit: Check. Ask the child to test a second case before saying it works. Say: "Will it work with a different number? Test it." Do not give the answer first: ask the prompt, wait, and give a hint only if the child is stuck.
