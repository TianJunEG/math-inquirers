LESSON 1 • WEEK 1

Million Puzzles

Numbers to 10 million and the four operations

TODAY WE WILL

Read and build numbers up to 10 million, solve digit puzzles, estimate to catch mistakes and use brackets to hit a target.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read, write and compare numbers up to 10 million; solve digit-card and digit-riddle puzzles with place value (testing the best number just below AND just above a target); estimate to check large calculations; and use the order of operations and brackets to hit target numbers. Thinking strategies taught today: TEST BOTH SIDES of a target, USE THE CLUES IN ORDER and check every clue, ESTIMATE by rounding, and WRITE THE RANGE FIRST before a "find all". Materials: Digit cards 0 to 9 (one set per child), a 7-column place-value mat (M, HTh, TTh, Th, H, T, O), mini whiteboards, a calculator for the explore only, workbook. Online: children cut out paper digit cards and draw a 7-box mat before the lesson and hold them up to the camera; the tutor writes on the slides; pairs use breakout rooms for the explore and page 1. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 9, explore 9 to 21, teaching 21 to 33 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 33 to 37, worksheet page 1 37 to 49, check together 49 to 51, short teaching before page 2 51 to 57, worksheet page 2 57 to 72 (alone), check together 72 to 76, talk time 76 to 82, exit ticket 82 to 90. Stretch and challenge are for children who finish early, or for home.
