LESSON 1 • WEEK 1

Million Puzzles

Numbers to 10 million and the four operations

TODAY WE WILL

Read and build numbers up to 10 million, solve digit puzzles, estimate to catch mistakes and use brackets to hit a target.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read, write and compare numbers up to 10 million; solve digit-card and digit-riddle puzzles with place value (testing the best number just below AND just above a target); estimate to check large calculations; and use the order of operations and brackets to hit target numbers. Thinking strategies taught today: TEST BOTH SIDES of a target, USE THE CLUES IN ORDER and check every clue, ESTIMATE by rounding, and WRITE THE RANGE FIRST before a "find all". Materials: Digit cards 0 to 9 (one set per child), a 7-column place-value mat (M, HTh, TTh, Th, H, T, O), mini whiteboards, a calculator for the explore only, workbook. Online: children cut out paper digit cards and draw a 7-box mat before the lesson and hold them up to the camera; the tutor writes on the slides; pairs use breakout rooms for the explore and page 1. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
