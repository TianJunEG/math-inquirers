Explore: how long is a million seconds?

EXPLORE

  1. Guess first: is a million seconds about an hour, a day, a week, a month or a year?
  2. Work out how many seconds there are in 1 hour.
  3. Work out how many seconds there are in 1 day.
  4. How many days is 1 000 000 seconds? Compare with your guess.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs. Everybody writes a guess first and does not change it (most children guess a week, a month or a year). Let them work with pencil first; allow a calculator only for checking. Early finishers: how long is 10 million seconds? More or less than a school term?
