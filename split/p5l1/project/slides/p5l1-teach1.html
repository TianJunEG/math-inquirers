Big numbers: groups of three

4 053 000

Step 1. Read in groups of three from the right: four million, fifty-three thousand.

Step 2. The digit 3 is in the thousands place. Its value is 3000.

Step 3. But how many thousands make 4 053 000? 4 053 000 ÷ 1000 = 4053 thousands.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Build 4 053 000 on the place-value mat. Click through the steps. Think-aloud: "The space after every three digits from the right is a comma in words: four million, fifty-three thousand. The 0 in the hundred thousands place still has to be written: it holds the place." Step 3 is the trap: the DIGIT in the thousands place is 3, but the number has 4053 thousands altogether (4 million is 4000 thousands, plus 53 thousands). Ask: "How many hundreds make 4 053 000?"
