Estimate to catch mistakes

Mei's calculator says 5896 × 41 = 2 417 336. Could that be right?

Step 1. Round each number: 5896 is about 6000, and 41 is about 40.

Step 2. 6000 × 40 = 240 000. So the answer should be about 240 000.

2 417 336 is about 10 times too big. It must be wrong! (Mei pressed an extra digit: the real answer is 241 736.)

Round to an easy number with one non-zero digit, work it out in your head, then compare.
