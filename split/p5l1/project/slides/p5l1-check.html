Whiteboards. Answer both, then be ready to say WHY.

1. Cards 3, 6, 7 and 9, each used once, make a 4-digit number. Which number is closest to 7000? Say why you tested the way you did.

6973 (27 below 7000). Starting with 7 gives 7369, which is 369 away.

2. Put ONE pair of brackets in 4 + 6 × 5 − 3 to make 47. How do you know no other place works?

(4 + 6) × 5 − 3 = 47. Every other single pair gives 16 or 31.
