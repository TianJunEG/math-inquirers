Find ALL: write the range first

A number rounded to the nearest hundred is 5000. Its digits add up to 6. Find all the numbers.

Step 1. Range: from 4950 up to 5049.

Step 2. 4950 to 4999: 4 + 9 + 5 = 18 already. Too big: none!

Step 3. 5000 to 5049: 5 + 0, so the last two digits add to 1: 5001 and 5010.
