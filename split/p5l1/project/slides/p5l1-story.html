Story break: Dorothy Vaughan

1910–2008 • United States • A maths teacher who led a team of human computers at NASA's early research centre.

Dorothy Vaughan was born in 1910 and studied mathematics at Wilberforce University in Ohio, finishing her degree in 1929. She taught maths at a high school in Virginia. In 1943 she began work at the Langley laboratory of NACA, the agency that later became NASA. She joined West Area Computing, a group of African American women known as computers because they did long calculations by hand, using slide rules and simple calculating machines. Later she became the head of this group. When electronic computers arrived, she taught herself the FORTRAN programming language and taught it to her staff too. She worked at Langley until 1971, nearly thirty years.
