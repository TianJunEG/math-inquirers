Stretch

1. Which is the best estimate of 4108 × 29: 12 000, 120 000 or 1 200 000? Explain.

2. Siti saves $250 every week. How many weeks will it take her to save $1 000 000? About how many years is that?

3. Use four 4s with +, −, ×, ÷ and brackets to make 3, then 5, then 7.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 120 000, because 4000 × 30 = 120 000 (exactly 119 132). 2) 4000 weeks (1 000 000 ÷ 250); with 52 weeks in a year that is about 77 years. 3) (4 + 4 + 4) ÷ 4 = 3; (4 × 4 + 4) ÷ 4 = 5; 4 + 4 − 4 ÷ 4 = 7. Other ways are possible: check each with the order of operations.
