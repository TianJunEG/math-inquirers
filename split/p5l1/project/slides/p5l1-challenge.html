E1. Put brackets into 1 + 2 × 3 + 4 × 5 + 6 to make the answer as big as possible. You may use more than one pair. What is the biggest answer?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) 231: (1 + 2) × (3 + 4) × (5 + 6) = 3 × 7 × 11. With no brackets it is only 33. 2) 6 953 487. The seven digits must be 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9, because the biggest seven different digits add to exactly 42. The number is between 6 950 000 and 7 049 999; 7 0… needs a 0, so it starts 6 9 then 5, 7 or 8. The smallest: 6 95, then 3, 4, 8, 7 so that it ends in an odd digit.
