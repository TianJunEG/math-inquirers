Think harder

1. Each picture stands for 2 children. Apple has 3 pictures, banana has 5 and orange has 2. How many more children chose banana than orange?

2. 25 children voted: apple 8, banana 6, grape 4, and the rest chose orange. Draw the orange row. Which fruit won?

3. The key is missing. Apple has 4 pictures and 12 children chose apple. Banana has 3 pictures. How many chose banana?

Take-home: The key is lost! Cats have 3 pictures and 6 children chose cats. What is 1 picture worth? Then dogs have 5 pictures: how many children chose dogs?

For any pair that chooses to try more (these are on the slide only; Think harder T1 to T4 are in the workbook). Answers: 1) 6 more. Banana 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 children; orange 2, 4 children; 10 − 4 = 6 (or: 3 extra pictures, count in 2s: 2, 4, 6). 2) Orange 7 (8 + 6 + 4 = 18, count on from 18 to 25: 7). Apple wins with 8. 3) 9. Count in 3s: 3, 6, 9, 12 is 4 pictures, so 1 picture = 3 children. Banana: 3, 6, 9.
