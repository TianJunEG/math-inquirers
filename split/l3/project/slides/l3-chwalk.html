Q1: take away the extra 6

Apple has 6, so 24 − 6 = 18 for banana and orange.

Orange has 6 more than banana. Take away the extra 6: 18 − 6 = 12.

Share 12 equally: banana 6. Give orange the 6 back: 12.

Banana 6, orange 12. Check: 6 + 6 + 12 = 24 ✓

Q2: count in 2s

2, 4, 6 … 20 is 10 counts: 10 pictures in all.

A has 3. B has 3 + 2 = 5. Together: 8 pictures.

C has 10 − 8 = 2 pictures: 2, 4.

C: 4 children
