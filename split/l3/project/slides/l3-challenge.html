Challenge

1. 24 children voted. Apple got 6 votes. Orange got 6 more votes than banana. How many votes did banana and orange get?

2. 1 picture = 2 children. 20 children voted for three fruits. A has 3 pictures. B has 2 more pictures than A. How many children chose C?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use as a pair challenge or in talk time. Ask for the reason every time. More are in the question bank. Answers: 1) Banana 6 and orange 12. 2) 4 children (20 children is 10 pictures in all; A 3 + B 5 = 8, so C has 2 pictures = 4 children).
