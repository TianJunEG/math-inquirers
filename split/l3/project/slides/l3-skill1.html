Think harder: twice as many

1. Mei has 4 stickers. Ben has twice as many stickers as Mei.

a) How many stickers does Ben have?

b) How many stickers do Mei and Ben have in all?

c) Kumar has 3 more than Ben. How many does Kumar have?

Item 1 (Bar model: twice as many; tool of the day idea: Draw it). SAY: "Draw one block for each sticker. Ben has two rows of Mei's blocks." ASK: "How did you show "twice as many" in your drawing?" LISTEN FOR: Two equal groups of 4. Ask for the number sentence: 4 + 4 = 8. ANSWER: a) 8 stickers. b) 12 stickers. c) 11 stickers.
