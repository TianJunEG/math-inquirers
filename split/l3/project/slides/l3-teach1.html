25–27 MIN

Read the key, then compare

Key: ● = 1 child

Our favourite colours

Step 1. Read the key: ● = 1 child. Most: blue (7). Fewest: green (3). Same: red and yellow (4).

Step 2. How many more blue than green? Match each green with a blue. 4 blues are left: 7 − 3 = 4.

Step 3. How many altogether? Add every row: 7 + 3 = 10, 4 + 4 = 8, 10 + 8 = 18 children.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Point to the key first: "Before I count anything, I read the key. One dot stands for one child." Click 1: most, fewest, same. Click 2 and 3: how many more. Put a finger on each green dot and the blue dot above it: "This green has a partner … this one … this one. These 4 blues have no partner, so blue has 4 more." Think-aloud from the lesson script: "How many more chose blue than green? I do not add. I compare. Blue has 7 and green has 3. The difference is 7 − 3 = 4." Click 4: altogether. "I look for a 10: 7 + 3 = 10. Then 4 + 4 = 8. 10 + 8 = 18." Watch for children who add (7 + 3 = 10) when asked "how many more".
