Find the missing row

20 children voted. Pizza 8, noodles 5. The rest chose rice. How many chose rice?

Step 1. Add the rows we know: 8 + 5 = 13.

Step 2. Count on from 13 to 20: 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20. That is 7.

Step 3. Draw 7 for rice. Check: 8 + 5 + 7 = 20 ✓

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. This graph has a row missing, but we know how many children voted. Click 1: add the rows we know. Click 2: count on from 13 to 20 on fingers (7 fingers). Click 3: the rice row appears. Click 4: check. Think-aloud: "Everybody voted once, so all the rows together must make 20. Pizza and noodles make 13. How many more to make 20? I count on: 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20. Seven." Ask: "Which food won now?" (pizza, 8).
