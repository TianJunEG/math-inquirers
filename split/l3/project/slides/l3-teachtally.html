Tally marks: bundles of 5

Make one mark for each vote. The 5th mark goes across the first 4.

Step 1. Count the bundles in 5s: 5, 10.

Step 2. Count on the single marks: 11, 12. 12 votes.

Your turn: how many votes?

8 votes
