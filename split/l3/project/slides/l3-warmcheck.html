Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 7 to 10 (3 minutes). Two questions on the key idea in a new setting. Children write their answer and how they know on a whiteboard and show it on a count of three; click to show each answer. Ask one or two children "How do you know?". Quietly note who got both right and gave a reason: those children may start the worksheet on the Think Harder page (then Challenge and the Open investigation) while the group works on the core page. Tell them privately at minute 40; do not announce names or scores. Everyone still joins Explore and the Mini-lesson. Answers: 1) 4 more (7 − 3). Compare the two rows: do not add. 2) Not necessarily.
