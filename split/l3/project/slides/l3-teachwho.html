Who voted? What else matters?

P1B voted for ice cream for their class party. Can we say the whole school likes ice cream best?

Step 1. Who voted? Only the children in P1B.

Step 2. Other classes did not vote. They might choose something else. We can only say: "P1B likes ice cream best."

Step 3. Think about more than votes. Will the ice cream melt before the party? Is there a freezer?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.
