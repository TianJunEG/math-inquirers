Two numbers: total and difference

Two numbers add up to 500. One is 100 more than the other. What are they?

Step 1. Take away the extra 100: 500 − 100 = 400.

Step 2. Two equal bars make 400, so each is 200.

Step 3. Give back the 100: 200 and 300. Check: 200 + 300 = 500 ✓
