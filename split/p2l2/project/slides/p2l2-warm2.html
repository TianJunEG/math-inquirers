Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 8. Whiteboards up together, then click. For 3 ask: "How did you take 250 from 700?" (700 − 200 = 500, then − 50 = 450). For 5: "Which is easier: count on from 120 to 500, or take away?" (count on: 120 → 200 is 80, 200 → 500 is 300, so 380). Answers: 35, 750, 450, 248, 380.
