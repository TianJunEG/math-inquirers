Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 1 to 4. Warm-up check: two questions on the key idea of today, in a NEW setting (not recall). Give about 1 minute each. Whiteboards up together, ask one child "How do you know?", then click to show each answer. Q1 answer: A = 4 (446 + 237 = 683). Listen for: Starts at the ones: 6 + 7 = 13, carry 1; tens: A + 3 + 1 = 8, so A = 4. Accept try-and-check if the child then checks by adding. Q2 answer: B = 160. 290 is 10 less than 300, so B must be 10 more than 150. Listen for: Reads “=” as “is the same as”. A child who adds 450 and subtracts 290 is correct; ask whether there is a quicker way. FAST TRACK: a child who gets BOTH right AND can say why starts on the THINK HARDER pages and the Open investigation (workbook Q8) when the worksheet begins at minute 36, in their own breakout room or with you checking in. A right answer without a reason stays with the group. If unsure, keep the child in the group. Nobody is labelled: say "Some of you will start on a different page today; everyone gets a turn at both." Children can move at any time. Everyone joins explore, the mini-lesson, the check-together slides and talk time.
