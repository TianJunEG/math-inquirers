Magic squares: find the total first

Step 1. Find a line with all three numbers: 220 + 270 + 260 = 750. That is the magic total.

Step 2. Left column: 220 + A + 240 = 750. 220 + 240 = 460, so A = 750 − 460 = 290.

Step 3. Diagonal: 220 + 250 + A = 750, so A = 280. Keep going: total minus the two you know.

Check every row, column and diagonal at the end.
