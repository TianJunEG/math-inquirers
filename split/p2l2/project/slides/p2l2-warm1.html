Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6 to 8.5. Partners. Say a number; children call out the partner (online: unmute together or type in the chat). Click to reveal each answer, left column first. Spare calls if the children want more: 30, 85, 99 for 100; 600, 240, 505 for 1000. Do not run over: three calls are enough. Ask after 125: "How did you find 875 so fast?" Listen for: "1 and 8 make 9 hundreds, 2 and 7 make 9 tens, 5 and 5 make 10 ones." That is the key to today's explore. Watch for: children who say 985 for 125 (making every column 10). The columns are 9, 9 and 10 because of the carries. A game of about 2.5 minutes: stop it on the clock even if the game is not finished.
