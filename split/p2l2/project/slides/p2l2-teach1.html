Minutes 23.5 to 27.5. Teach. Ask first: "Which column should we start with?" Some children start at the hundreds: 4 + 2 = 6, not 7, and they get stuck. Show why the ones go first: a carry from the ones changes the tens, and a carry from the tens changes the hundreds. Think-aloud: "7 plus something ends in 3. 7 + 6 = 13. The box is 6, and I carry 1 ten. Tens: something + 8 + 1 ends in 2 …" Guess and check works too: "Try 2 in the tens box: 2 + 8 + 1 = 11. Ends in 1, not 2. Try 3."
