Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Pick any 3-digit number, like 358.
  2. Find its partner that makes 999. Check by adding.
  3. Do it for three more numbers.
  4. Look at each column. What do you notice?

Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8 to 14. Pairs choose their own 3-digit numbers and find partners to 999, checking with a column sum. Do not explain the pattern; let them find it. Any child who would like more: "Now find partners to 1000. What changes?" (the ones add up to 10 and the tens and hundreds to 9, because of the carry).
