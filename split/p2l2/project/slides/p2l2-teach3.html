"=" means "is the same as"

245 + 130 = 200 + □

Step 1. 245 is 45 MORE than 200.

Step 2. So □ must be 45 more than 130.

Step 3. □ = 175. Check: both sides are 375 ✓

You can also work out one side: 245 + 130 = 375, then 375 − 200 = 175.
