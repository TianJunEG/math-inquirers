1. A magic triangle has 3 corner circles and 3 circles in the middle of the sides. Put the numbers 110, 120, 130, 140, 150 and 160 in the circles, once each, so that every side adds up to 420. Draw the triangle. Which numbers are in the corners?

2. Find the missing digits: 5 + 7 + 38 = 132. There are three numbers, so the carry from the ones may be more than 1.

3. Mei did 523 − 267 and wrote 266. She checked by adding 266 + 267 = 533. Was her answer right? What mistake did she make? What is the right answer?

Answers: 1) Corners 140, 150, 160. Between 140 and 150: 130. Between 150 and 160: 110. Between 160 and 140: 120 (each side: 140 + 130 + 150 = 420, 150 + 110 + 160 = 420, 160 + 120 + 140 = 420). 2) 57 + 37 + 38 = 132. Ones: 7 + 7 + 8 = 22, so write 2 and carry 2 tens. Tens: 5 + 3 + 3 + 2 = 13. The first box is 7 and the second box is 3. 3) No. Her check gives 533, not 523, so 266 is wrong. The right answer is 256 (ones: 13 − 7 = 6; tens: the 2 tens lent 1 ten to the ones, so 1 ten is left and we borrow again: 11 − 6 = 5; hundreds: 4 − 2 = 2). Check: 256 + 267 = 523. Mei’s tens digit is 1 too big: she forgot that the tens had already lent 1 ten to the ones. 4) (Question: True or false? Say how you know without working out both sides. (a) 346 + 199 = 345 + 200 (b) 270 + 130 = 260 + 150 (c) 618 + 99 = 600 + 117) (a) True: 346 is 1 more than 345 and 199 is 1 less than 200. (b) False: 270 is 10 more than 260, but 130 is 20 less than 150, so the sides are 400 and 410. (c) True: 618 is 18 more than 600 and 99 is 18 less than 117 (both sides are 717).
