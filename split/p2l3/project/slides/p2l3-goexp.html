What if the farmer counts 20 legs? How many different mixes of hens and goats are there now?

Write or draw your answer on paper.

Minutes 8 to 14. Go further (a second what-if question, so nobody waits). Show this slide with the explore slide or post the question in the chat when the pairs go to their breakout rooms. It is for ANY child who finishes the explore task or wants more: say it to the whole group as a choice, never as a prize, and never name who tries it. Pairs stay together unless both children agree to split. Answer: 6 mixes: 10 hens; 8 hens and 1 goat; 6 hens and 2 goats; 4 hens and 3 goats; 2 hens and 4 goats; 5 goats.
