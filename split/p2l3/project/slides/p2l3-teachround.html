Leftovers still need a seat

13 children go boating. Each boat holds 4 children. How many boats are needed?

Step 1. Count in 4s: 4, 8, 12. 3 boats carry 12 children.

Step 2. 13 − 12 = 1 child is left. That child needs a boat too!

4 boats are needed (not 3).

Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5). Click: the boats picture, the steps, the answer. Say: "In sharing, the leftover is left over. But here every child must ride, so the leftover child needs one more boat." Ask: "How many empty seats are in the 4th boat?" (3). Compare: "17 sweets among 3 children: how many EACH?" (5, 2 left over) and "13 children, boats of 4: how many BOATS?" (4). Read the question to know what to do with the leftover.
