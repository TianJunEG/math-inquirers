Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. A pen has only hens (2 legs) and goats (4 legs).
  2. The farmer counts 16 legs altogether.
  3. Use counters or drawings to find a mix of hens and goats.
  4. Find EVERY mix. Record each one.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs use counters: 2 counters stand for a hen's legs, 4 for a goat's, or they draw circles for heads and sticks for legs. Ask for a prediction first: "Is there only one answer?" Do not show a method yet; let them try. Push early finishers: "How do you know you have them all?" Online: children draw on paper and hold it up. The 5 mixes are in the next slide's notes.
