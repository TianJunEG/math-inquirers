Explore: talk about it

1. How many different mixes did you find?

2. How do you know you have them all?

3. What happens when you swap 2 hens for 1 goat?

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Gather the pairs and take answers. Write the mixes on the board IN ORDER of goats: 0 goats and 8 hens; 1 goat and 6 hens; 2 goats and 4 hens; 3 goats and 2 hens; 4 goats and 0 hens. That is 5 mixes (4 if the pen must have both kinds; accept either if the child says which). Answers: 1) 5 mixes. 2) The goats go 0, 1, 2, 3, 4; 5 goats would need 20 legs, too many. 3) The legs stay at 16: 2 hens have 4 legs, the same as 1 goat. Say: "Going in order made sure we did not miss one. Today we use tables to go in order."
