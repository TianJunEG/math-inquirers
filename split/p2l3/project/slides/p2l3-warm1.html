Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 4 to 6 (a 2-minute game: one or two rounds). Legs and wheels count. Point to one card at a time. Children count in 2s, 3s, 4s or 5s (not in 1s) and show the number on fingers or a whiteboard, then you click to show the answer (clicks reveal the cards in order: cars, bicycles, tricycles, dogs, hens, mixed). Answers: 4 cars 16 wheels; 5 bicycles 10 wheels; 3 tricycles 9 wheels; 5 dogs 20 legs; 6 hens 12 legs; 2 cars and 1 bicycle 10 wheels (8 + 2). Ask: "Which times table did you use for the cars?" (4s). "For the tricycles?" (3s). For the last card: "Why can we not count all of them in 4s?" (the bicycle has only 2 wheels). Watch for children counting every wheel in 1s; ask them to point to each car and count "4, 8, 12, 16".
