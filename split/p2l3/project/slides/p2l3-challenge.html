1. Mr Lim has between 20 and 30 sweets. In groups of 4, 3 are left over. In groups of 5, 2 are left over. How many sweets does he have?

2. A pen has hens and goats. There are 2 more hens than goats. There are 22 legs altogether. How many hens and how many goats?
