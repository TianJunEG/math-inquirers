Your turn: how many of each?

Make a table. Start with all of one kind, then swap.

a Hens and goats: 4 animals, 10 legs.

3 hens, 1 goat

b Bicycles and tricycles: 5 of them, 12 wheels.

3 bikes, 2 trikes

c Stools (3 legs) and chairs (4 legs): 3 seats, 10 legs.

2 stools, 1 chair

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards, then click. a) 4 hens = 8 legs; swap 1: 10 legs, so 3 hens and 1 goat. b) 5 bicycles = 10 wheels; each swap to a tricycle adds 1 wheel; 2 swaps make 12, so 3 bicycles and 2 tricycles. c) 3 stools = 9 legs; 1 swap adds 1 leg: 2 stools and 1 chair. Ask about b: "Why does each swap add only 1 this time?" (3 − 2 = 1).
