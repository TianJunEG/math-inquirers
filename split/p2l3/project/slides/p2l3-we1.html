Worked example: under the cloth

A tray of cookies has the same number in every row. How many are under the cloth?

Step 1. The top row shows 5 in each row. The side shows 4 rows.

Step 2. 4 rows of 5: 5, 10, 15, 20. 20 cookies in all.

Step 3. I can see 5 + 3 = 8 cookies.

Under the cloth: 20 − 8 = 12 cookies

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click to reveal each step. Think-aloud: "Part of this tray is under a cloth. I do not need to see every cookie. The top row shows 5 in each row. The side shows 4 rows. 4 rows of 5 is 20. I can see 5 on top and 3 more down the side, that is 8. So 20 − 8 = 12 are hidden." Ask before step 3: "Did I count the corner cookie twice?" (No: 5 on top, then only the 3 BELOW it.) Common mistake: 5 + 4 = 9 seen, which counts the corner twice.
