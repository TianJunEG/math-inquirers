Think harder

1. A shop has some bicycles (2 wheels) and some tricycles (3 wheels), at least one of each. There are 17 wheels altogether. Find ALL the ways. Start with 1 tricycle, then 2 tricycles, and so on. Make a table or use counters. How do you know you have found them all?

2. A farm has 9 animals, each a hen (2 legs) or a goat (4 legs). Mei counts 25 legs. Siti counts 26 legs. Only one of them can be right. Who? How many hens and goats?

3. Ali shares 34 marbles equally among 4 friends, giving as many as he can. How many does each friend get? How many are left over?

Question 4 is on your Think harder page.

These are the SAME four questions as the printed Think harder page: the slide shows 1 to 3 and question 4 is on the page only. Never announce who is working on which page. Answers: 1) 3 ways: 7 bicycles and 1 tricycle (14 + 3); 4 bicycles and 3 tricycles (8 + 9); 1 bicycle and 5 tricycles (2 + 15). 0, 2 or 4 tricycles leave an odd number of wheels, which bicycles cannot make, and 6 or more tricycles need 18 wheels or more. 2) Siti is right: 5 hens and 4 goats (10 + 16 = 26). Mei cannot be right: every hen has 2 legs and every goat has 4 legs, so the total is always an even number, and 25 is odd. (Start with 9 hens, 18 legs; each swap to a goat adds 2: 18, 20, 22, 24, 26.) 3) 8 each (4 × 8 = 32) and 2 left over. 4) (Question: An array of stickers has 4 rows. Ben counts 12 stickers in the first 3 rows. How many stickers are in the whole array?) 16 stickers. 3 rows have 12, so each row has 4 (12 ÷ 3 = 4); 4 rows of 4 is 16.
