For early finishers

Stretch

1. Cars (4 wheels) and motorcycles (2 wheels): 8 vehicles and 22 wheels. How many cars?

2. 24 cupcakes go in boxes of 2, 3, 4, 5 or 10, the same in every box. Which box sizes leave none over?

3. Tables have 4 legs and stools have 3 legs. Ravi counts 17 legs. How many tables and how many stools?

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 3 cars and 5 motorcycles (8 motorcycles = 16 wheels; each swap to a car adds 2; 3 swaps make 22; check 12 + 10 = 22). 2) Boxes of 2 (12 boxes), 3 (8 boxes) and 4 (6 boxes); boxes of 5 and 10 leave 4 over. 3) 2 tables and 3 stools (8 + 9 = 17); 0, 1, 3 or 4 tables leave 17, 13, 5 or 1 legs, which are not in the 3 times table.
