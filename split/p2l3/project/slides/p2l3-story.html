India • 1929-2013

Shakuntala Devi was born in Bangalore, India, in 1929. Her father saw how good she was at numbers when she was very young. At six she showed her sums at a university. In 1980, in London, two 13-digit numbers were picked for her at random. She multiplied them in her head and said the answer in 28 seconds! That feat was written in the Guinness Book of World Records. People called her the human computer.

Try it (optional)

Do these in your head like Shakuntala: 7 × 5, 9 × 4 and 6 × 10. Which answer is the biggest, and which is the smallest?
