Use arrays for × and ÷, find how many of each with a table, and share with leftovers.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read an array as rows of equal groups and write two multiplication and two division sentences for it; solve "how many of each" legs and wheels puzzles with a table; share with leftovers and decide what the leftover means; find all the arrays for a number. Tables used: 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10 only. Thinking strategies taught today: DRAW IT (arrays, plates, boats) and MAKE A TABLE (start with all of one kind, swap one at a time; guess and check). Materials: About 30 counters per pair, mini whiteboards, squared paper. Optional picture cards of hens and goats, bicycles and tricycles. Online: Each child has 30 small objects (buttons, beans or Lego bricks) and a whiteboard or paper; children hold arrays and tables up to the camera. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90. Stretch and Challenge slides are for early finishers.
