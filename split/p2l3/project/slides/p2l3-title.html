Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read an array as rows of equal groups and write two multiplication and two division sentences for it; solve "how many of each" legs and wheels puzzles with a table; share with leftovers and decide what the leftover means; find all the arrays for a number. Tables used: 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10 only. Level note: sharing with leftovers (Q4, Q5) is division with a remainder, which is a P3 topic. It is a natural lead-in, so say "3 left over" in words and do not write remainders as "r 3". Thinking strategies taught today: DRAW IT (arrays, plates, boats) and MAKE A TABLE (start with all of one kind, swap one at a time; guess and check). Materials: About 30 counters per pair, mini whiteboards, squared paper. Optional picture cards of hens and goats, bicycles and tricycles. Online: Each child has 30 small objects (buttons, beans or Lego bricks) and a whiteboard or paper; children check arrays and tables against the slide. Time plan: opening 0 to 1.5, sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 6, warm-up 6 to 10.5, explore 10.5 to 23.5, mini-lesson 23.5 to 35.5, brain break 35.5 to 38.5, worksheet page 1 38.5 to 52.5, check together, page 1 52.5 to 55, short teaching 55 to 59.5, worksheet page 2 59.5 to 71, check together, page 2 71 to 73.5, flex (spare time) 73.5 to 76.5, talk time 76.5 to 81.5, exit ticket 81.5 to 86.5, looking back 86.5 to 88.5, closing and stamps 88.5 to 90. The Think harder and Go further slides are optional, if you finish early (no clock time). Kit ready at minute 0: ask families to put every item on the desk before the lesson starts (a 30-second check at the start). SPINE: Today's tool is Make a table. Put what you know in rows and columns. Look for where you use it. Point at the card on page 1 of the workbook.
