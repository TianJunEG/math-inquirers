Show after pairs have tried the Challenge, or if they are stuck. The pictures show the problem, not the answer. Ask: What do you notice? What do we know? What do we not know yet? For Q1: "Which numbers from 21 to 29 leave 3 when we make groups of 4?" For Q2: "Try 1 goat. How many hens then?"
