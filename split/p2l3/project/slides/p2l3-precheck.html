Warm-up check

Two questions, 3 minutes. Whiteboards up together, then tell your partner HOW you know.

Q1 A toy shop has cars (4 wheels) and scooters (2 wheels). 6 toys have 16 wheels altogether. How many cars and how many scooters? How do you know?

2 cars, 4 scooters

Q2 An array of counters has 4 rows with 5 in each row. A cloth covers 2 whole rows. How many counters can you see? Write a multiplication sentence.

10 counters

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 1 to 4. Warm-up check: two questions on the key idea of today, in a NEW setting (not recall). Give about 1 minute each. Whiteboards up together, ask one child "How do you know?", then click to show each answer. Q1 answer: 2 cars and 4 scooters (8 + 8 = 16 wheels). Listen for: Start with all scooters (12 wheels) and swap one at a time (+2 wheels each swap), or guess and check in a table. Ask “what did you try first?” Q2 answer: 10 counters: 2 × 5 = 10. Listen for: Sees that 2 rows are left. They may also say the whole array is 4 × 5 = 20. Listen for “rows” and “in each row”. FAST TRACK: a child who gets BOTH right AND can say why starts on the THINK HARDER pages and the Open investigation (workbook Q8) when the worksheet begins at minute 36, in their own breakout room or with you checking in. A right answer without a reason stays with the group. If unsure, keep the child in the group. Nobody is labelled: say "Some of you will start on a different page today; everyone gets a turn at both." Children can move at any time. Everyone joins explore, the mini-lesson, the check-together slides and talk time.
