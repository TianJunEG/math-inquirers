1.5–6 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. Guess, draw, count or say "I do not know". Show your paper to your tutor only.

Q1 (start here) A tray has 3 rows of counters with 5 counters in each row. Draw it if you like. How many counters are there?

15

Q2 (think about it) A toy shop has cars (4 wheels) and scooters (2 wheels). 6 toys have 16 wheels altogether. How many cars and how many scooters? How do you know?

2 cars, 4 scooters

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 1.5 to 6. Sneak-peek puzzles: two puzzles on the key idea of today, which the children have NOT learnt yet. Say out loud (about 28 words): "Two sneak-peek puzzles on ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome, so is I do not know. Nobody is marked. Show your paper to me only." Question 1 is an access question: any child can try it with objects, a drawing or counting, so everyone has a first win (about 45 seconds). Question 2 is the thinking question (about 90 seconds). Then about 2 minutes for the answers slide and the move on. Answers are PRIVATE: each child holds their own paper to the camera for you only, or writes it on their own paper and shows only you on camera. Do not raise whiteboards together, do not read answers out, do not name children. Q1 answer: 15 counters (3 rows of 5: 5, 10, 15). Listen for: Counting in 5s, or drawing and counting. This is a first win for everyone. Q2 answer: 2 cars and 4 scooters (8 + 8 = 16). Listen for: Starting with all scooters (12 wheels) and swapping one at a time is the idea of today. A child who draws 6 vehicles and counts wheels is thinking well. Guess and check is welcome. At the end click to show the answers as "one way to think about it", without saying who had what. Ask only for volunteers: "Would anyone like to tell us how they started?" Invite the WHOLE group at once, never one child: "When you have finished Start here, you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation." Never announce who is on which page. Pairs are not split unless both children agree. Children can move either way at any time. Everyone joins explore, the mini-lesson, the check-together slides and talk time.
