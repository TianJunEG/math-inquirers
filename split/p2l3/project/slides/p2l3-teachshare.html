Sharing with leftovers

Share 17 sweets equally among 3 children.

Step 1. Give one each, round by round: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15.

Step 2. 5 rounds: each child gets 5. That uses 15.

Step 3. 17 − 15 = 2 left over.

2 is less than 3, so there is not enough for another fair round.

Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4). Act it out with 17 counters and 3 paper plates: deal one to each plate per round, counting 3, 6, 9, 12, 15. Click: the plates, then each step, then the note. Think-aloud: "After 5 rounds each child has 5 and I have used 15. 2 are left. Can I do another round? No: I need 3 for a round." Ask: "Could the leftover ever be 3 or more when we share among 3?" (No: then we could do another round.)
