Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6). Use 12 counters to build each array as you click each row. Think-aloud: "2 rows: 12 shared into 2 rows is 6 in each row. 3 rows: 3, 6, 9, 12, that is 4 in each row. 4 rows of 3. 5 rows: 5, 10, 15, I jump over 12, so no. 6 rows of 2. 7 rows would need 14 chairs for 2 in each row, so I stop." Answer: 4 ways (2 rows of 6, 3 rows of 4, 4 rows of 3, 6 rows of 2). Ask: "Are 3 rows of 4 and 4 rows of 3 different?" (Yes for chairs in a hall: the rows look different. On the worksheet we count them as different ways.)
