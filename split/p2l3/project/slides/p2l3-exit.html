A pen has hens and goats. There are 5 animals and 14 legs. How many hens and how many goats?

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Minutes 80 to 90. Answer: 3 hens and 2 goats (6 + 8 = 14 legs, 3 + 2 = 5 animals). Look for a table starting with 5 hens (10 legs). Extension: find all the arrays (at least 2 rows, at least 2 in each row) for 16 counters (3 ways: 2 rows of 8, 4 rows of 4, 8 rows of 2).
