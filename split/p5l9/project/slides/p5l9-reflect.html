Minutes 85.5 to 88. REFLECTION, about a minute and a half. Children use the small box at the bottom of the Investigation and reflection page: Something I did well; The hardest part was; What helped me (tick boxes include "I asked for help" and "I am still stuck (my tutor will check in with me)"). Pass is always OK. Never collected in public; you may read it privately later to plan the next lesson and check in with any child who ticked still stuck in the open session (a quick visit to the shared breakout room or a hand signal, parent nearby, never a private message). Never read a child's reflection aloud. It is never collected or shown in public. You may read it privately afterwards to plan the next lesson and to check in with any child who ticked "I am still stuck", in the open session (a quick visit to the shared breakout room or a hand signal, with a parent nearby, never a private message). Never read a child's reflection aloud; only a child who volunteers may share. Praise strategy, not speed: "Making a table of the first five figures got you unstuck." Then type the stamp in the chat for every child (one message) and say goodbye. If the clock is behind, the reflection becomes a one-tick version: pass or one box.
