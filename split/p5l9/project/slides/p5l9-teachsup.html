Supposition: suppose they are all the same

A car park has 24 cars and motorcycles with 70 wheels altogether. A car has 4 wheels and a motorcycle has 2 wheels. How many cars?

Step 1. Suppose all 24 are motorcycles: 24 × 2 = 48 wheels.

Step 2. We are short: 70 − 48 = 22 wheels.

Step 3. Each motorcycle changed to a car adds 4 − 2 = 2 wheels. 22 ÷ 2 = 11 cars.

11 cars and 13 motorcycles. Check: 44 + 26 = 70 wheels ✓, 11 + 13 = 24 ✓

Divide the gap by the change for ONE swap, not by the value of one thing.
