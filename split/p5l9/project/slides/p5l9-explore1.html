Explore: High Fives

EXPLORE

  1. Guess first: if all 8 of us high-five everyone else once, how many high fives is that?
  2. Start small: 2 children, then 3, then 4. Draw each child as a dot and each high five as a line.
  3. Make a table: number of children, number of high fives.
  4. Look for a pattern and use it to find the answer for 8.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs. Online: breakout rooms, each pair draws dots on a whiteboard and joins them. Predict first: most children guess 8 × 8 = 64 or 8 + 7; write guesses down. Let them draw 2, 3, 4 and 5 dots before they look for the pattern. Early finishers: a class of 30. The picture shows 1, 3 and 6 lines for 2, 3 and 4 children.
