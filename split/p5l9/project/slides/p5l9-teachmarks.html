Marks taken away

A quiz has 12 questions. Each correct answer scores 5 marks and each wrong answer loses 1 mark. Siti answers every question and scores 42. How many are correct?

Step 1. Suppose all 12 are correct: 12 × 5 = 60 marks.

Step 2. We scored less: 60 − 42 = 18 marks fewer.

Step 3. Each wrong answer loses the 5 marks AND 1 more mark taken away: 5 + 1 = 6 marks. 18 ÷ 6 = 3 wrong.

9 correct, 3 wrong. Check: 45 − 3 = 42 ✓

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5). This is supposition with a twist: a wrong answer costs more than missing out on the 5 marks, because 1 mark is also taken off. Ask after step 2: "What is the difference between a correct answer and a wrong answer?" (6 marks: from +5 to −1.) Check with the other fact: 9 × 5 = 45, minus 3 × 1 = 3, gives 42.
