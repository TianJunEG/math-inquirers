Use after pairs have tried. Q1: in a diamond the rows go up 1, 3, 5 … to the widest row and back down. The widest row of figure 6 has 2 × 6 − 1 = 11 dots. The top half, including the widest row, is a square number of dots (6 × 6 = 36 for figure 6) and the bottom half is the square number before it (5 × 5 = 25). Figure 6: 36 + 25 = 61. Q2: blank questions score nothing and lose nothing, so only the 22 answered ones matter: suppose all 22 correct (88 marks), the gap is 20, each wrong answer costs 5 (the 4 you do not get and the 1 taken away), so 4 wrong and 18 correct.
