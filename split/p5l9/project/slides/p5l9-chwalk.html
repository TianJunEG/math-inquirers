Q2: suppose all answers are correct

Answered: 25 − 3 = 22 questions

All 22 correct: 22 × 4 = 88 marks

88 − 68 = 20 marks lost; each wrong loses 4 + 1 = 5

20 ÷ 5 = 4 wrong, so 18 correct. Check: 72 − 4 = 68 ✓
