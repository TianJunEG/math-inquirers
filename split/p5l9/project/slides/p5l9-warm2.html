Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 9 to 12. Whiteboards up together, then click. Ask after 1: "What is the rule? add 4." After 2: "What do you notice about 1, 4, 9, 16, 25?" (square numbers: 1 × 1, 2 × 2 ...). After 3: "multiply by 3 each time", so it is not the same kind of pattern as 1. After 4: the 20th even number is 2 × 20 = 40, a rule in words: 2 × the place. Answers: 23 and 27; 36; 162; 40. If you are short of time, skip question 4.
