ADDED ITEM woven (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Two sequences woven together"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Spot the pattern. SAY: Look at every other number. Underline the 1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th. Then underline the 2nd, 4th, 6th. What is each list doing? ASK: Which list does the 15th number belong to? How many steps from the first number of that list is it? LISTEN FOR: Odd places: 3, 7, 11, 15 (add 4). Even places: 50, 46, 42 (take away 4). 15th is the 8th odd-place number: 3 + 7 × 4 = 31. 16th is the 8th even-place number: 50 − 7 × 4 = 22. Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) 38 and 19. (b) 31. (c) 22. WHY: Odd places go up by 4: 3, 7, 11, 15, 19, ... so the 15th number (the 8th odd-place number) is 3 + 4 × 7 = 31. Even places go down by 4: 50, 46, 42, 38, ... so the 16th number (the 8th even-place number) is 50 − 4 × 7 = 22. The list continues 15, 38, 19, 34, ... so the next two numbers are 38 and 19.
