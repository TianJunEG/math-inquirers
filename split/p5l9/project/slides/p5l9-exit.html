EXIT TICKET

Figure 1 of a pattern has 6 dots. Each figure has 4 more dots than the figure before. (a) How many dots are in figure 20? (b) Which figure has 102 dots?

Write your answers on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 80 to 85.5. Answers: (a) 82 (dots = 4 × figure number + 2; 4 × 20 + 2 = 82, or 6 + 19 × 4). (b) Figure 25 (102 − 2 = 100, 100 ÷ 4 = 25). Extension: a square grid of matchsticks: 1 by 1 uses 4 sticks, 2 by 2 uses 12. How many for 3 by 3 and 10 by 10? (24 and 220.)
