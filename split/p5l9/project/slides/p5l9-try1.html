Your turn: say the rule

A dot pattern has 7, 12, 17, 22, … dots in figures 1, 2, 3, 4.

a What does each figure add?

5 more dots

b Rule in words, then figure 12?

5 × 12 + 2 = 62

Minutes 27.5 to 29. Whiteboards, then click. a) 12 − 7 = 5. b) Figure 1 has 7 = 5 × 1 + 2, figure 2 has 12 = 5 × 2 + 2: dots = 5 × figure number + 2. Figure 12: 60 + 2 = 62. (Two items only.)
