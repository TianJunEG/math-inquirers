These are the printed Go further questions B1 to B3 (the same as the workbook). Answers: B1) 61. The counts go 1, 5, 13, 25, 41, 61 (add 4, 8, 12, 16, 20). Or: each diamond is two square numbers of dots, 6 × 6 + 5 × 5 = 36 + 25 = 61. B2) 18. She answered 22 questions. All 22 correct would be 88 marks; each wrong answer costs 4 + 1 = 5 marks; 88 − 68 = 20, 20 ÷ 5 = 4 wrong. Check: 72 − 4 = 68. B3) 5. The numbers 1 to 9 use 9 digits. That leaves 91 digits: 45 two-digit numbers (10 to 54) use 90 of them, so the 100th digit is the first digit of 55.
