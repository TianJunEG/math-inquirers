Key idea: find the rule, say it in words

Ask: what stays the same and what grows? Look at the picture to see WHY. Say the rule in words: sticks = 3 × figure number + 1.

Check the rule on every figure you know. To go backwards, undo the steps in reverse order.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 35.5 to 37. Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Doubling: figure 20 is NOT twice figure 10, because the starting stick or the end chairs get counted twice. (2) Counting the jumps wrongly, 25 lots of 3 instead of 24 for figure 25 when starting from figure 1, or writing the rule as "add 3" without saying what to do with the figure number. (3) Supposition: dividing the gap by the value of one thing (22 ÷ 4) instead of the change for each swap (22 ÷ 2), and not checking BOTH facts (how many things and the total).
