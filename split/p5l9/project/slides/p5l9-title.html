Patterns and number sense

TODAY WE WILL

See how a pattern grows, say the rule for any figure in words, work backwards from a total, and solve problems by supposing.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Continue growing patterns drawn as figures, say the rule for any figure in words (for example, sticks = 3 × figure number + 1) and explain it from the picture, work backwards from a total to find the figure, and solve "two kinds of things" problems with a guess-and-check table and with supposition. Thinking strategies today: LOOK FOR A PATTERN, MAKE A TABLE, WORK BACKWARDS, GUESS AND CHECK, SUPPOSITION. Materials: matchsticks or toothpicks (about 40), square paper tiles or the square grid from the cut-out pages, mini whiteboards, workbook. Online: children have toothpicks and paper squares ready on the desk and build each figure on camera; pairs go to breakout rooms for the explore and page 1 and hold their tables up to the camera. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
