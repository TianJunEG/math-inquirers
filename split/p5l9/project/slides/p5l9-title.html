LESSON 9 • WEEK 5A

Pattern Generalisers

Patterns and number sense

TODAY WE WILL

See how a pattern grows, say the rule for any figure in words, work backwards from a total, and solve problems by supposing.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 2 (opening, inside the 90-minute clock). Objective: Continue growing patterns drawn as figures, say the rule for any figure in words (for example, sticks = 3 × figure number + 1) and explain it from the picture, work backwards from a total to find the figure, and solve "two kinds of things" problems with a guess-and-check table and with supposition. Thinking strategies today: LOOK FOR A PATTERN, MAKE A TABLE, WORK BACKWARDS, GUESS AND CHECK, SUPPOSITION. Materials: matchsticks or toothpicks (about 40), square paper tiles or the square grid from the cut-out pages, mini whiteboards, workbook. Online: children have toothpicks and paper squares ready on the desk and build each figure on camera; pairs go to breakout rooms for the explore and page 1 and hold their tables up to the camera. Time plan (the exact minutes of this lesson; every lesson adds up to 90): opening 0 to 2, sneak-peek puzzles 2 to 6.5 (one hold-up for both puzzles, no volunteer round, nobody is marked), warm-up 6.5 to 12 (one game round, four quick questions), explore 12 to 25.5 (pair work 5.5 minutes at most, then two questions together; a Go further what-if is on the explore slide for any pair that finishes), teaching 25.5 to 37 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 37 to 41, workbook page 1 41 to 55 (the questions without a star; after question 2 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder), check together 55 to 59, short teaching before page 2 59 to 62.5, workbook page 2 62.5 to 71.5 (alone: start with the questions named at the top, then choose; starred parts are take-home; nobody has to finish), check together 71.5 to 74.5, talk time 74.5 to 80 (you may say pass; volunteers first; the first step of the Investigation is take-home), exit ticket 80 to 85.5, reflection 85.5 to 88 (pass is always OK), stamp typed in the chat and goodbye 88 to 89, and the buffer 89 to 90 (floating time: spend it first if you run late, never fill it on purpose). Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child and have no clock time. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and never splits a pair unless both children agree. Invite the WHOLE group, never one child: "when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation."
