Patterns and number sense

TODAY WE WILL

See how a pattern grows, say the rule for any figure in words, work backwards from a total, and solve problems by supposing.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Continue growing patterns drawn as figures, say the rule for any figure in words (for example, sticks = 3 × figure number + 1) and explain it from the picture, work backwards from a total to find the figure, and solve "two kinds of things" problems with a guess-and-check table and with supposition. Thinking strategies today: LOOK FOR A PATTERN, MAKE A TABLE, WORK BACKWARDS, GUESS AND CHECK, SUPPOSITION. Materials: matchsticks or toothpicks (about 40), square paper tiles or the square grid from the cut-out pages, mini whiteboards, workbook. Online: children have toothpicks and paper squares ready on the desk and build each figure on camera; pairs go to breakout rooms for the explore and page 1 and hold their tables up to the camera. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 9, explore 9 to 21, teaching 21 to 33 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 33 to 37, worksheet page 1 37 to 49, check together 49 to 51, short teaching before page 2 51 to 57, worksheet page 2 57 to 72 (alone), check together 72 to 76, talk time 76 to 82, exit ticket 82 to 90. Stretch and challenge are for children who finish early, or for home.
