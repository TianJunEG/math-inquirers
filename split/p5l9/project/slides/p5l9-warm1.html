Rule: times 3, then add 1

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 4. Guess My Rule. Play without the slide first if you like: children call a number, you answer from your secret rule (times 3, then add 1), and write the pairs on the board. Round 1 uses the slide: click to show 2 to 7, 5 to 16, 10 to 31. Ask: "Is it add 5? 2 + 5 = 7 works! Now test 5." (5 + 5 = 10, not 16). This is the habit for today: TEST your rule on every case, not just the first. Then: "Say it in words: multiply by 3, then add 1." Click to reveal. Rounds 2 and 3: make up your own secret rule (one or two steps) and let the group guess; use 1 to 10 as inputs and add 0 to see what the machine does to 0. Listen for children who say "it goes up by 3" (true for the steps) but cannot say what happens to 100. That gap is today's lesson.
