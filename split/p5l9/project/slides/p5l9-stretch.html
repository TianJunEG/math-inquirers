Stretch

1. Ali saves $3 in week 1. Each week he saves $2 more than the week before. How much does he save in week 15?

2. 3, 8, 13, 18, … What is the first number in this pattern that is greater than 200?

3. A test has 10 questions. A correct answer scores 3 marks and 1 mark is taken away for each wrong answer. Siti answered every question and scored 18. How many did she get correct?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) $31 (week 15 is 14 jumps of $2 after week 1: $3 + $28). 2) 203 (add 5 each time; the 40th number is 3 + 39 × 5 = 198, which is not above 200, so the 41st, 203, is the first). 3) 7 correct and 3 wrong (all correct would be 30; each wrong answer costs 3 + 1 = 4; 12 ÷ 4 = 3 wrong; check 21 − 3 = 18).
