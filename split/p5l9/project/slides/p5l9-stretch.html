A1. Beads repeat in the order red, red, blue, yellow, yellow, yellow. What colour is the 50th bead? How many yellow beads are in the first 100?

A2. 40 square tables in one long row seat 2 × 40 + 2 = 82 people. How many can sit if the tables are put in 8 rows of 5 tables instead?

A3. A farm has chickens and goats. There are 35 heads and 94 legs. How many goats are there?

A4. Work out 1 + 3, 1 + 3 + 5 and 1 + 3 + 5 + 7. What do you notice? Use it to add the first 20 odd numbers, 1 + 3 + … + 39.

These are the printed Think harder questions A1 to A4 (the same as the workbook). Answers: A1) Red (50 = 8 × 6 + 2, so it is the 2nd bead of a group). 49 yellow beads: 16 full groups of 6 give 96 beads and 48 yellow, and beads 97 to 100 are red, red, blue, yellow. A2) 96 (each row of 5 seats 12, and 8 × 12 = 96). One long row seats only 82. Splitting the tables gives more end seats. A3) 12 goats and 23 chickens (all chickens would have 70 legs; 24 legs short; each goat adds 2 legs). Check: 48 + 46 = 94. A4) 4, 9, 16: square numbers. The first 20 odd numbers add up to 20 × 20 = 400. (Each new odd number adds an L-shaped layer to a square of dots.)
