A parallelogram with an isosceles triangle

WXYZ is a parallelogram. XY = XZ. Angle XYZ = 55°. Find angle XZY, YXZ, WXY and WXZ.

Step 1. Angle XZY = 55°. Reason: XY = XZ, so the base angles are equal.

Step 2. Angle YXZ = 180° − 55° − 55° = 70°. Reason: angles in a triangle.

Step 3. Angle WXY = 180° − 55° = 125°. Reason: neighbouring angles of a parallelogram.

Step 4. Angle WXZ = 125° − 70° = 55°. The big angle WXY is made of WXZ and ZXY.
