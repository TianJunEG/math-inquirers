Stretch

1. Rhombus ABCD has angle ADC = 130°. The diagonal AC is drawn. Find angle DAC.

2. In a parallelogram one angle is 30° less than the angle next to it. Find all four angles.

3. Two angles of a triangle add up to 110°. What is the third angle? The triangle is isosceles: list every possible set of three angles.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 25°. DA = DC (rhombus), so triangle ADC is isosceles with top angle 130° and base angles (180° − 130°) ÷ 2 = 25°. 2) 75°, 105°, 75°, 105°. Neighbouring angles add up to 180°. 180° − 30° = 150°, 150° ÷ 2 = 75°, so the smaller angle is 75° and the bigger is 105°. 3) The third angle is 180° − 110° = 70°. Isosceles: either the two angles that add up to 110° are equal (55°, 55°, 70°), or one of them equals the third angle, 70°, which makes the other 110° − 70° = 40° (70°, 70°, 40°). Two sets: 55°, 55°, 70° and 40°, 70°, 70°.
