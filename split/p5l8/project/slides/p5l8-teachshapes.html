Four-sided shapes: what do we know?

Parallelogram

Opposite angles are equal. Neighbouring angles add up to 180°.

Rhombus

A parallelogram with 4 equal sides. Neighbouring angles add up to 180°.

Trapezium

One pair of parallel sides. Angles between the parallel sides add up to 180°.

Between the parallel sides, the angles on one slanted side add up to 180°: for example 100° + 80° = 180°.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 30 to 32. Teach. Show each shape and ask: "What do you notice about the angles?" Parallelogram: opposite angles are equal (70° and 70°, 110° and 110°) and neighbouring angles add up to 180° (70 + 110). Rhombus: it is a parallelogram with all four sides equal, so the same angle facts, AND its equal sides make isosceles triangles when we draw a diagonal. Trapezium: only one pair of parallel sides (the arrows). Each slanted side joins the two parallel sides, and the two angles on it add up to 180°: 100 + 80 and 65 + 115. Check: 100 + 65 + 115 + 80 = 360, the angles in any four-sided shape. Click to show the last line.
