Your angle toolkit: facts we can give as reasons

Angles on a straight line add up to 180°.

Angles at a point add up to 360°.

Vertically opposite angles are equal.

Angles in a triangle add up to 180°.

Isosceles: the two base angles are equal.

Equilateral: all three angles are 60°.

Math Inquirers Level 5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. These six facts are the reasons children will give all lesson. Read each card and ask children to say it back before you move on. 1) Straight line: 65° and 115° make 180°. 2) Point: 100 + 100 + 90 = 290, so the missing angle is 360 − 290 = 70°. 3) Vertically opposite: lines that cross make two pairs of equal angles (show it by folding paper or by turning a pair of sticks). 4) Triangle: 50 + 60 + 70 = 180 (like the corners we tore). 5) Isosceles: two equal sides, and the two angles at the bottom (the base angles) are equal. 36 + 72 + 72 = 180 is another example; here 72 + 54 + 54 = 180. 6) Equilateral: three equal sides and three 60° angles. Say: "A reason is one of these facts. Today every angle we find gets a reason."
