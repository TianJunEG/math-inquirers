In triangle PQR, PQ = PR and angle QPR = 44°. Find angle PQR. Write the reason for each step.

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 80 to 90. Answer: 68°. Angles in a triangle add up to 180°: 180° − 44° = 136°. PQ = PR, so the base angles are equal: 136° ÷ 2 = 68°. Accept any clear reasons. Extension: draw a triangle and put the three angle labels 40°, 60° and 80° on the corners. Cut the corners off and fit them together. Now do the same with any four-sided shape.
