Angle Chasers

Angles, triangles and quadrilaterals

TODAY WE WILL

Chase unknown angles through triangles, parallelograms, rhombuses and trapezia, giving a reason for every step.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Use the angle facts (straight line 180°, point 360°, vertically opposite, triangle 180°, isosceles and equilateral triangles, parallelogram, rhombus, trapezium) to chase unknown angles through a figure, step by step, giving a reason for every step; list every case when only one angle of an isosceles triangle is given. Thinking strategies taught today: WRITE DOWN WHAT YOU KNOW, FIND ONE ANGLE AND SAY WHY, and TRY EVERY CASE. Drawings are not to scale: never measure. Materials: paper, ruler, pencil, scissors, protractor (only for the explore activity), red and blue pencils, mini whiteboards, workbook. Online: children tear or cut the corners of a paper triangle and quadrilateral on camera. The tutor screen-shares the slides and draws on the figures with the annotation tool. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before page 2 and a check-together slide after each page, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
