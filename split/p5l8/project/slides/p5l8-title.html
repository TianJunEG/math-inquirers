LESSON 8 • WEEK 4B

Angle Chasers

Angles, triangles and quadrilaterals

TODAY WE WILL

Chase unknown angles through triangles, parallelograms, rhombuses and trapezia, giving a reason for every step.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Use the angle facts (straight line 180°, point 360°, vertically opposite, triangle 180°, isosceles and equilateral triangles, parallelogram, rhombus, trapezium) to chase unknown angles through a figure, step by step, giving a reason for every step; list every case when only one angle of an isosceles triangle is given. Thinking strategies taught today: WRITE DOWN WHAT YOU KNOW, FIND ONE ANGLE AND SAY WHY, and TRY EVERY CASE. Drawings are not to scale: never measure. Materials: paper, ruler, pencil, scissors, protractor (only for the explore activity), red and blue pencils, mini whiteboards, workbook. Online: children tear or cut the corners of a paper triangle and quadrilateral on camera. The tutor screen-shares the slides and draws on the figures with the annotation tool. Time plan: sneak-peek puzzles 0 to 3 (private answers, nobody is marked), warm-up 3 to 9, explore 9 to 21 (a Go further what-if is on the explore slide for anyone who wants more), teaching 21 to 33 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 33 to 37 (with an optional 2-minute Story break about a mathematician; skip it if you are short of time), workbook page 1 37 to 49 (after question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder), check together 49 to 53, short teaching before page 2 53 to 58, workbook page 2 58 to 72 (alone: start with the questions named at the top, then choose; nobody has to finish; minutes 62 to 72 are protected open think time, 10 quiet minutes for Think harder, Go further or the Investigation, with no hints unless a child asks), check together 72 to 76, talk time 76 to 82 (you may say pass; volunteers first; then everyone tries the first step of the Investigation), exit ticket 82 to 86, reflection 86 to 88 (pass is always OK; includes "Next time I will try"), stamp and goodbye 88 to 90. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and never splits a pair unless both children agree. Invite the WHOLE group, never one child: "when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation."
