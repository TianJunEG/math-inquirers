LESSON 8 • WEEK 4B

Angle Chasers

Angles, triangles and quadrilaterals

TODAY WE WILL

Chase unknown angles through triangles, parallelograms, rhombuses and trapezia, giving a reason for every step.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 2 (opening, inside the 90-minute clock). Objective: Use the angle facts (straight line 180°, point 360°, vertically opposite, triangle 180°, isosceles and equilateral triangles, parallelogram, rhombus, trapezium) to chase unknown angles through a figure, step by step, giving a reason for every step; list every case when only one angle of an isosceles triangle is given. Thinking strategies taught today: WRITE DOWN WHAT YOU KNOW, FIND ONE ANGLE AND SAY WHY, and TRY EVERY CASE. Drawings are not to scale: never measure. Materials: paper, ruler, pencil, scissors, protractor (only for the explore activity), red and blue pencils, mini whiteboards, workbook. Online: children tear or cut the corners of a paper triangle and quadrilateral on camera. The tutor screen-shares the slides and draws on the figures with the annotation tool. Time plan (the exact minutes of this lesson; every lesson adds up to 90): opening 0 to 2, sneak-peek puzzles 2 to 6 (one hold-up for both puzzles, no volunteer round, nobody is marked), warm-up 6 to 12 (one game round, four quick questions), explore 12 to 24.5 (pair work 6.5 minutes at most, then two questions together; a Go further what-if is on the explore slide for any pair that finishes), teaching 24.5 to 36.5 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 36.5 to 41, workbook page 1 41 to 54 (the questions without a star; after question 2 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder), check together 54 to 57.5, short teaching before page 2 57.5 to 60.5, workbook page 2 60.5 to 68 (alone: start with the questions named at the top, then choose; starred parts are take-home; nobody has to finish), check together 68 to 71, talk time 71 to 76.5 (you may say pass; volunteers first; the first step of the Investigation is take-home), exit ticket 76.5 to 81.5, reflection 81.5 to 84 (pass is always OK), stamp typed in the chat and goodbye 84 to 85, and the buffer 85 to 90 (floating time: spend it first if you run late, never fill it on purpose). Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child and have no clock time. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and never splits a pair unless both children agree. Invite the WHOLE group, never one child: "when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation."
