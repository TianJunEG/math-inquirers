Think harder: Weekdays in a year

Tool: Spot the pattern

A year has 365 days. In one year, 1 January is a Tuesday.

(a) How many whole weeks are in 365 days, and how many days are left over?

(b) How many Tuesdays are in the year?

(c) What day of the week is 31 December?

(d) What day of the week is 1 January of the next year?

(a) 52 weeks and 1 day.

(b) 53 Tuesdays.

(c) Tuesday.

(d) Wednesday.

ADDED ITEM year (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Weekdays in a year"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Spot the pattern. SAY: Divide 365 by 7. The weeks use up every day of the week 52 times. Which day is the one extra day? ASK: Which day of the week is day 365? Is any other day of the week in the year 53 times? LISTEN FOR: 365 = 52 × 7 + 1. The extra day is the same weekday as 1 January, so only Tuesday occurs 53 times. Listen for the remainder idea. Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) 52 weeks and 1 day. (b) 53 Tuesdays. (c) Tuesday. (d) Wednesday. WHY: 365 ÷ 7 = 52 remainder 1. The 365th day is 364 days after the first day, and 364 = 52 × 7, so it is also a Tuesday. The next day is a Wednesday. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): In a 365-day year, 1 January is a Friday. Which weekday occurs 53 times? Answer: Friday.
