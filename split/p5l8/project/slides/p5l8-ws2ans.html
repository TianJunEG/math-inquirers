ACD = 180° − 118° − 38° = 24°. Check: angle BAC = 56° + 38° = 94° and 62° + 94° + 24° = 180° ✓. Q4: ADC = ABC = 56° (opposite angles). DA = DE: DEA = (180° − 56°) ÷ 2 = 62°. AEC = 180° − 62° = 118° (straight line). Angle DAB = 180° − 56° = 124°; DAE = 62°, so EAB = 124° − 62° = 62°. Q5a: 50° as a base angle gives 50°, 50°, 80°; 50° as the top angle gives 65°, 65°.
