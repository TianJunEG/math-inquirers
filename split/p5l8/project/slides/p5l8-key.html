35.5–36.5 MIN

Key idea: one angle, one reason

Find one angle. Say WHY. Use it to find the next one. That is an angle chase.

Do not measure the drawing. Use the facts: straight line 180°, point 360°, triangle 180°, equal base angles, and the shape facts.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 35.5 to 36.5. Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Measuring or guessing from the picture instead of using an angle fact: drawings are not to scale. (2) Calling the wrong angles equal in an isosceles triangle: the equal angles are the base angles, opposite the equal sides, not the angle between the equal sides. (3) Giving only one case when one angle of an isosceles triangle is known (the given angle could be the top angle or a base angle).
