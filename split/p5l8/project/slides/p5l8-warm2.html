Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6 to 9. Read each aloud, whiteboards up together, then click to show. Ask "What is your reason?" each time. 1) Angles on a straight line add up to 180°: 55°. 2) Angles at a point add up to 360°: 150 + 90 + 80 = 320, so 40°. 3) Angles in a triangle add up to 180°: 180 − 90 − 35 = 55°. 4) The two base angles are equal: (180 − 40) ÷ 2 = 70°. 5) Neighbouring angles of a parallelogram add up to 180°: 110°. Answers: 55°, 40°, 55°, 70°, 110°.
