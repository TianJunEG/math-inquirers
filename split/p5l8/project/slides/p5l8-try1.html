Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards: answer AND reason. a) 180° − 112° = 68° (straight line). b) 180° − 36° = 144°, 144° ÷ 2 = 72° (isosceles: base angles equal; triangle 180°). c) 180° − 65° = 115° (neighbouring angles of a parallelogram add up to 180°). Ask: "Which reason did you give?" Accept any correct fact.
