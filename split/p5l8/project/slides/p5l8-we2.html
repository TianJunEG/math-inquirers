Worked example: the rhombus and its diagonal

ABCD is a rhombus. Angle DAB = 50°. The diagonal BD is drawn. Find angle ABC, then angle ABD, then angle DBC.

Step 1. Angle ABC = 180° − 50° = 130°. Reason: neighbouring angles of a rhombus add up to 180°.

Step 2. Triangle ABD: AB = AD (all sides of a rhombus are equal). Angle ABD = (180° − 50°) ÷ 2 = 65°. Reason: base angles of an isosceles triangle.

Step 3. Angle DBC = 130° − 65° = 65°. The diagonal cuts the 130° angle into two equal parts.
