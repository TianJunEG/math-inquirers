Find every shape hiding in a picture by counting in order, describe shapes by sides and corners, and make new shapes from squares.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Name and describe 2D shapes by sides and corners, count shapes in a picture, count triangles in a figure, and compose shapes. Materials: Paper squares, scissors, pattern blocks, 2 shape riddles on cards. Online: Each child has a paper square to fold and cut on camera; shape hunt at home. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
