Minutes 5 to 7. Read each question aloud, give thinking time, take answers, then click to show the answer. Ask "How do you know?" each time. For 4, fold a paper square in front of them after they answer: two rectangles of the same size. Answers: 1) 3 2) 4 3) circle 4) 2 rectangles (if a child says 2 triangles, they folded corner to corner: that is what we do in Explore next).
