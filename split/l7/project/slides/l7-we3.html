Make shapes from 3 squares

Join 3 same-size squares. Whole sides must touch. How many different shapes?

Not allowed: squares that touch only at a corner.

Step 1. Put them all in a row: a line.

Step 2. Move one end square up: an L.

Step 3. Turn or flip the L. It is the same shape, so it is not new.

Only 2 different shapes: the line and the L.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use 3 real squares (paper or pattern blocks) under the visualiser. Click through the steps. Think-aloud: "I start with all three in a row. Now I move one end square up, so it sits on top of its neighbour: an L. What if I move the middle square up? Then it only touches at corners: not allowed. If I turn the L or flip it over, it is the same L, just turned. So with 3 squares there are only 2 shapes." Let children try to find a third one with their own squares: every try is a line or an L. The Think harder slide asks the same question with 4 squares.
