Whiteboards up together, then click. a) 3: there is no bigger square, because a bigger square needs 2 rows. b) 6: 5 small squares and 1 square made of 4 small ones (the 2 by 2 part). The extra square cannot make a second big square because the square above it is missing: show this with your 2 by 2 window. c) Open: a 2 by 2 grid (4 + 1) or a row of 5 small squares (5 + 0) both work.
