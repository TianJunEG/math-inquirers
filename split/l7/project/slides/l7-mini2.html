Counting triangles

A big triangle has one line from the top corner down to the bottom.

Count the small triangles first: 2.

Then the big ones made of more than one small triangle: 1.

How many triangles altogether?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example 2. Ask children to explain each step in their own words. Build the table: shape, sides, corners. Notice the numbers are the same. Predict the octagon. Then count triangles in figures systematically (small first, then bigger). Answer: 3 (2 small + 1 big).
