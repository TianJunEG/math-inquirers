Who am I? Cross out shapes that do not fit

I have 4 corners. My sides are not all the same length. Who am I?

Step 1. Clue 1: 4 corners. Cross out every shape that does not have 4 corners.

Step 2. Clue 2: sides not all the same length. Cross out the square.

Step 3. One shape is left. I am a rectangle. Check it with every clue ✓

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q3 is three "Who am I?" riddles). Read the riddle aloud. Click 1: "Which shapes have 4 corners? Count with me." Cross out the triangle (3), the circle (0), the pentagon (5) and the hexagon (6). Click 2: "Square or rectangle? A square has all sides the same length. My sides are not all the same, so cross out the square." Click 3: check: a rectangle has 4 corners, and its long sides and short sides are different lengths ✓. Think-aloud: "I read one clue at a time and cross out every shape that does not fit. When one shape is left, I check it against every clue."
