Count shapes in a picture: one kind at a time

Step 1. Pick one kind. Find every circle and tick it as you count: 3 circles.

Step 2. Then the next kind: triangles 1, squares 1, rectangles 4. A square goes only in the Square column.

Step 3. Add: 3 + 1 + 1 + 4 = 9 shapes. Every shape has one tick: none missed, none counted twice.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 1 (Q1 counts the shapes in a house picture; this robot is a different picture). Click 1: tick the circles one by one: the two eyes and the button. Click 2: tick the others in a new colour: the hat is a triangle, the head is a square, the mouth, the body and the two feet are rectangles. Click 3: add. Click 4: fill the table. Think-aloud: "If I jump around the picture I will miss one or count one twice. So I pick one kind of shape, find every one and tick it. Then the next kind." Say clearly: a square goes in the Square column only, even though a square is a special rectangle.
