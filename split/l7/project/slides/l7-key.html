Key idea: count in order

Count the smallest shapes first. Then count the bigger shapes made of smaller ones. Add them up.

Turning or flipping a shape does not make a new shape.

On the house picture, a square goes in the Square column only.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key ideas together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Counting only the small shapes: in the triangle-counting pictures (Q4 and the talk-time picture, a square with both lines from corner to corner) children miss the bigger triangles. Ask "Are there bigger triangles made of 2 small ones?" (2) In the house picture (Q1), counting a square window in both the Square and the Rectangle columns. Each shape goes in one column only. (3) Saying a circle has 1 side, or counting the same side twice when going round a shape: put a dot where you start. (4) Thinking a turned or flipped shape is a new one.
