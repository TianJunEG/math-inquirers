33–35 MIN

Key idea: count in order

Count small shapes first, then bigger shapes made of smaller ones.

A square is also a special rectangle: ask “why?”.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 33 to 35. Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Counting only the small triangles in figure C (answer 4 instead of 8). (2) Thinking a square is not a rectangle, or the opposite, without a reason. (3) Saying a circle has 1 side.
