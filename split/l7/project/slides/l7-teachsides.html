Sides and corners: mark where you start

Step 1. Put a dot on one corner. That is where you start.

Step 2. Go round. Count each side until you are back at the dot: 12 sides.

Step 3. Go round again. Count the corners: 12 corners.

What do you notice? Is it true for every shape? Test it on page 1.

Math Inquirers Level 1 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 1 (Q2 is a sides and corners table). Click 1: the start dot. Click 2: trace the outline with your finger and count the sides aloud with the children. Click 3: go round again for the corners (dots). Think-aloud: "This shape has lots of sides, so I could easily lose my place. I put a dot where I start, count each side, and stop when I am back at the dot." Then ask "What do you notice?" but let them test the idea on the worksheet table; do not tell them the rule. The names at the bottom are for Q2: children count the sides of each shape themselves on the worksheet. Memory help: an octopus has 8 arms (octagon).
