EXIT TICKET

How many squares are in this grid? Count every size.

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 80 to 90. Answer: 11 (8 small squares + 3 squares made of 4 small ones: a 2 by 2 window fits in 3 places). Children who write 8 counted only the small squares: ask "Are there any bigger squares hiding?" Extension: How many squares are in a grid 4 squares long and 3 squares high? (12 + 6 + 2 = 20).
