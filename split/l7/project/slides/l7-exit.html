How many squares are in this grid? Count every size.

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 80 to 90. Answer: 14 (9 small squares + 4 squares made of 4 small squares + 1 whole grid). This is the grid we counted in the worked example, so it checks the core idea. Children who write 9 counted only the small squares: ask "Are there any bigger squares hiding?" Extension: how many squares are in a grid 4 squares long and 2 squares high? (11: 8 + 3). Then 4 long and 3 high? (20: 12 + 6 + 2).
