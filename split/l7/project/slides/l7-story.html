Story break: Johannes Kepler

Optional • about 2 minutes • we can skip it

Johannes Kepler was a German astronomer. In 1611 he wrote a small booklet about snowflakes as a New Year's gift for a friend. He noticed that snowflakes have six corners, and he asked why. He thought about the six-sided cells of honeycomb and how things pack together. He could not give a final answer, so he wrote down his ideas and left the rest for others to find out. Asking "why?" is how a shape safari begins.
