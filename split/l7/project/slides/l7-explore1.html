Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Take a paper square.
  2. Fold it corner to corner and cut along the fold.
  3. What two shapes do you have?
  4. Put them together a different way. Name each new shape.

Go further: your choice, any time

What if you cut the paper square straight across the middle instead? What shapes do you get? Can you make a new shape from the pieces?
