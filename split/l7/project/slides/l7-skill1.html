Think harder: count all the cubes

1. Cubes are stacked on a mat in two rows. There are no gaps under any stack.

a) How many cubes are in the back row?

b) How many cubes are in the front row?

c) How many cubes are there in all, including the ones you cannot see?

Item 1 (Counting cubes in stacks, including hidden ones; tool of the day idea: Act it out). SAY: "Build it with real cubes if you can: stack by stack." ASK: "Which cubes can you not see? How do you know they are there?" LISTEN FOR: "The stack at the back is 3 high, so there are cubes under the top one." ANSWER: Back row: stacks of 3, 2 and 1 = 6 cubes. Front row: stacks of 2, 1 and 1 = 4 cubes. In all: 10 cubes.
