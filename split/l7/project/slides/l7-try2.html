Your turn: rectangles in strips

6

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards, then click. a) 2 + 1 = 3. b) Slide a 2-long window along 5 squares: it fits in 4 places. c) From the worked example there are 10 rectangles; the 4 single squares are squares, so 10 − 4 = 6 (3 that are 2 long, 2 that are 3 long, 1 that is 4 long). Ask a child to show b by sliding a finger along the strip.
