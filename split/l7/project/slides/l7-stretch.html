T1 Two same-size triangles can make a square. Can they also make a bigger triangle? Draw how.

T2 How many squares are in a grid 3 squares long and 2 squares high?

T3 Three squares are joined in a row to make a long rectangle. How many rectangles can you find altogether? (A square counts as a rectangle.)

T4 Draw a shape with 4 straight sides that is NOT a square. Is it a rectangle? How do you know?

For any pair that chooses to try more. These are the same four questions as Think harder T1 to T4 in the workbook (the labels match). Answers: T1) Yes. Join them along a short side (the two equal short sides touching) to make one bigger triangle. Joining along the long side makes a square instead. T2) 8 (6 small squares + 2 squares made of 4 small ones) T3) 6 (3 single squares, 2 pairs and 1 whole row). T4) Open. A long rectangle has 4 square corners, so it is a rectangle. A slanted shape with 4 sides does not have square corners, so it is not a rectangle.
