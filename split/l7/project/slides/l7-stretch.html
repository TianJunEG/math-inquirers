Stretch

1. How many squares are in a grid 3 squares long and 2 squares high?

2. How many rectangles are in a grid 2 squares long and 2 squares high? (Squares count too.)

3. Join 4 same-size squares, whole sides touching. How many different shapes can you make? (Turns and flips are the same shape.)

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) 8: 6 small squares + 2 squares made of 4 small ones (a 2 by 2 window fits in 2 places). 2) 9: 4 small squares, 2 rectangles 2 long lying down, 2 rectangles 2 long standing up, and the whole grid. 3) 5 shapes: a line of 4, a 2 by 2 square, an L, a T and a zigzag (S). Let children build them with paper squares and check each new one is not a turned or flipped copy.
