Q1: 4 lines make 5 small parts

Made of 1 part: 5. Made of 2 parts: 4

Made of 3 parts: 3. Made of 4 parts: 2

Made of all 5 parts: 1

5 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 15 triangles ✓

Q2: count each size

The big square: 1

Medium squares (the 4 quarters): 4

Tiny squares in one quarter: 4

1 + 4 + 4 = 9 squares ✓

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use after pairs have tried. Q1: the same idea as the 2-line triangle we counted (3 + 2 + 1 = 6), now with 5 small parts. Children who know the pattern 3, 6, 10 can add the next jumps: 10 + 5 = 15. Ask: "Which triangles are made of 4 parts?" (the left 4 and the right 4). Q2: the quarter that is cut up still counts as a medium square. Watch for 8 (forgetting the big square) or 5 (forgetting the tiny ones).
