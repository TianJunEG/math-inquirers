55–58 MIN

Count triangles: small ones first

How many triangles are in this picture?

Step 1. Made of 1 small part: 3

Step 2. Made of 2 small parts: 2

Step 3. Made of all 3 parts: 1

3 + 2 + 1 = 6 triangles

Math Inquirers Level 1 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4 asks for the triangles in 3 pictures; this picture is not one of them). Ask first: "How many triangles?" Take guesses. Click through the sizes. Think-aloud: "I count the small triangles first: 1, 2, 3. Now triangles made of 2 small parts: the left two together, the right two together: 2. Then all 3 parts: the whole big triangle: 1. 3 + 2 + 1 = 6." Ask "Is the middle part with the right part a triangle?" Yes. Watch for children who stop after the small ones.
