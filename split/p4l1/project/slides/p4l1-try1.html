Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards up together, then click. a) 1 × 20, 2 × 10, 4 × 5 (3 does not go in). b) 1 × 30, 2 × 15, 3 × 10, 5 × 6: 8 factors. c) 1 × 16, 2 × 8, 4 × 4: only 5 factors, because 4 × 4 uses 4 twice. Ask: "Why does 16 have an odd number of factors?" Do not explain fully yet; just notice it.
