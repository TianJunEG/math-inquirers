Factors and multiples

3 × 8 = 24

Step 1. 3 and 8 are factors of 24. They go into 24 exactly.

Step 2. 24 is a multiple of 3 and of 8. It is in their times tables.

Step 3. Is 5 a factor of 24? 24 ÷ 5 = 4 remainder 4. Not this one.

A factor is never bigger than the number. A multiple is never smaller.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Point at the array: "3 rows of 8 make 24." Click through. Think-aloud: "A factor goes INTO a number with nothing left over. A multiple is what you get when you multiply, so it is in the times table." Ask before step 3: "Is 5 a factor of 24? How could you check?" (divide, or look at the 5 times table: 20, 25; 24 is not there). Last line: a quick way to catch a mix-up. 24 has factors up to 24, and multiples from 24 upwards.
