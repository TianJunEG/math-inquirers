Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 8. Read each aloud, whiteboards up together, then click to show. Ask after 1: "Is 56 a factor of 7 too?" (No: a factor goes INTO the number; 56 is a multiple of 7.) After 3: "Is there a smaller common multiple than 24?" (12). After 4: 44 and 48 are the multiples of 4 around 45, so 45 is not one. After 5: "Did anyone find a fourth way?" (no: after 3 × 6 the next would be 6 × 3, the same pair turned round). Answers: yes, 6 12 18 24 30, 12 (or 24, 36), no, 1 × 18 / 2 × 9 / 3 × 6.
