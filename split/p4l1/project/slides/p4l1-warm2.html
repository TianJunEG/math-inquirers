Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 9 to 12.5. Three questions, about 30 seconds each: read each aloud, whiteboards up together, then click to show. No follow-up questions: if a child raises it, a factor goes INTO a number (56 is a multiple of 7), and 12 is the smallest common multiple of 4 and 6. Answers: yes, 6 12 18 24 30, 12 (or 24, 36). The other two warm-up questions (Is 45 a multiple of 4? Write 18 as a product, 3 ways) are in the Weekly Dose of Thinking Math.
