Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Fizz-Buzz. Count round the group (online: in the order of the video tiles), one number each. A multiple of 3 is "Fizz", a multiple of 4 is "Buzz", a multiple of both is "Fizz-Buzz". A child who slips starts the next round. Go to 40 if it is going well. Before you start, ask for a prediction: "Which number will be the first Fizz-Buzz?" Then click: the teal boxes are the multiples of 3, the red lines the multiples of 4, and yellow shows the numbers in both: 12 and 24. Ask: "What is the next Fizz-Buzz? How do you know without counting?" (36: they go up in 12s). Introduce the words: 12 is a MULTIPLE of 3 and of 4, so it is a COMMON MULTIPLE. That idea comes back with the buses later.
