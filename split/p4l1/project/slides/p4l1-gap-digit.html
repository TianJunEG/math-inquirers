ADDED ITEM digit (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Digit tests for 3 and 6"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Spot the pattern. SAY: Look at a multiple of 3 such as 51, 63, 84. Add their digits. What do you notice? Then try 724 and 618. ASK: If a number is in both the 2 table and the 3 table, what must be true about its last digit and its digit sum? LISTEN FOR: The digit sum of a multiple of 3 is a multiple of 3. Even last digit AND digit sum in the 3 table. Children who test all 10 digits are fine, but ask them to find a faster way. Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) 724: digit sum 13, no. 618: digit sum 15, yes. (b) The box can be 2 or 8: 582 and 588. WHY: 5 + 8 = 13, so the box digit must make 13 + box a multiple of 3: box = 2, 5 or 8. The number must also be even, so the box is 2 or 8. 582 = 6 × 97 and 588 = 6 × 98.
