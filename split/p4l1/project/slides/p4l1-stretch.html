Stretch

1. How many factors does 64 have?

2. Find all the numbers below 100 that are multiples of both 3 and 7.

3. Siti puts 45 marbles into equal groups, with none left over. Each group has more than 1 marble, and there is more than 1 group. How many groups could there be? Find all.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 7 factors: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 (8 × 8 makes it odd). 2) 21, 42, 63, 84 (count in 21s). 3) 3, 5, 9 or 15 groups (the factors of 45 apart from 1 and 45).
