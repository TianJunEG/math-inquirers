LESSON 1 • WEEK 1

Factor Hunters

Factors and multiples

TODAY WE WILL

Hunt down every factor of a number, use common factors and common multiples to solve puzzles, and crack multiple riddles.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find all the factor pairs of a number by working in order, find common factors (sharing into identical packs) and common multiples (when do buses meet again), discover which numbers have an odd number of factors, and solve multiple-based logic riddles. Thinking strategies taught today: MAKE A LIST IN ORDER, LOOK FOR A PATTERN and USE THE CLUES ONE AT A TIME. Materials: 36 square tiles or squared paper per pair, mini whiteboards, coloured pencils, a 1 to 50 number strip. Online: children cut 24 paper squares (or use squared paper) before the lesson and build rectangles on camera; the tutor marks the bus time line and the factor rainbow on the slides. Time plan: Warm-up check 1 to 4 (two questions on the key idea; children who get both right and can say why start on the Think Harder pages at minute 34), warm-up game 4 to 6 (one short round), quick questions 6 to 8, explore 8 to 18, teaching 18 to 30 (click to reveal each step and answer; the "your turn" slides are optional, skip them if short of time), brain break 30 to 34, worksheet page 1 from 34 to 50 and check together 50 to 53, short teaching before page 2 from 53 to 58, worksheet page 2 from 58 to 76 and check together 76 to 80, talk time 80 to 85, exit ticket 85 to 88, What was hard? reflection 88 to 90. The Challenge slides and the Think Harder pages are for early finishers and for take-home: they have no fixed time slot. Online: page 1 is pair work, so allow about 2 minutes to move into and out of breakout rooms (or run page 1 as a whole-group screen-share if there are only 8 children).
