LESSON 1 • WEEK 1

Factor Hunters

Factors and multiples

TODAY WE WILL

Hunt down every factor of a number, use common factors and common multiples to solve puzzles, and crack multiple riddles.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find all the factor pairs of a number by working in order, find common factors (sharing into identical packs) and common multiples (when do buses meet again), discover which numbers have an odd number of factors, and solve multiple-based logic riddles. Thinking strategies taught today: MAKE A LIST IN ORDER, LOOK FOR A PATTERN and USE THE CLUES ONE AT A TIME. Materials: 36 square tiles or squared paper per pair, mini whiteboards, coloured pencils, a 1 to 50 number strip. Online: children cut 24 paper squares (or use squared paper) before the lesson and build rectangles on camera; the tutor marks the bus time line and the factor rainbow on the slides. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
