Explore: talk about it

1. How many different rectangles did you make with 24 tiles?

2. How do you know you have found them all?

3. What do the side lengths tell us about the number 24?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Let pairs keep working, then gather. Collect the rectangles in the order children say them, then ask: "Is anything missing? Is anything there twice?" Answers: 1) 4 rectangles: 1 × 24, 2 × 12, 3 × 8, 4 × 6. 2) Try 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 rows in order. 5 rows does not work (24 is not in the 5 times table). 6 rows is the 4 × 6 turned round, so we can stop. 3) The side lengths are the factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24. With 36 tiles there are 5 rectangles, and one is a square (6 × 6). Do NOT teach the rainbow yet: the next slides do.
