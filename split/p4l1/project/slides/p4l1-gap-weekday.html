Think harder: Weekdays and remainders

Tool: Make a table

(a) Ben borrows a library book on a Monday. It is due back 45 days later. On which day of the week is it due?

(b) Kumar starts a trip on a Saturday. Which day of the week is it 30 days later?

(a) Thursday.

(b) Monday.

ADDED ITEM weekday (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Weekdays and remainders"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Make a table. SAY: A week has 7 days, so the days repeat in a cycle of 7. Write the days across the top of a table, then jump 7 days at a time. ASK: How many whole weeks are in 45 days? What is left over, and what does the leftover tell us? LISTEN FOR: 45 = 6 x 7 + 3, so it is the same as 3 days later. This is the remainder move from today's lesson. Listen for "divide by 7 and look at the remainder". Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) Thursday. (b) Monday. WHY: 45 days = 6 weeks and 3 days: Monday plus 3 days is Thursday. 30 days = 4 weeks and 2 days: Saturday plus 2 days is Monday. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): Siti starts swimming lessons on a Tuesday. Which day of the week is it 100 days later? Answer: Thursday (100 = 14 weeks and 2 days).
