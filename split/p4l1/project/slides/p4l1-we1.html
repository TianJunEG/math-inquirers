Make a list in order: today's main strategy. Click to add each row while children say it first on whiteboards. Think-aloud: "1 × 36. 2 goes into 36: 2 × 18. 3 × 12. 4 × 9. Does 5 go into 36? No, 36 is not in the 5 times table, so I skip it. 6 × 6. The next number to try is 7, then 8, then 9, but 9 is already in my list (4 × 9). The pairs have met, so I stop." Then click for the factor rainbow: each arch joins a factor pair. 6 has no partner because 6 × 6 uses it twice, so 36 has an odd number of factors (9). We come back to that before page 2.
