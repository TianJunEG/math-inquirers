Worked example: identical bags

12 apples and 18 oranges go into identical bags, with none left over. What is the greatest number of bags?

Step 1. Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12. Factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18.

Step 2. The number of bags must be a factor of BOTH. Common factors: 1, 2, 3, 6.

Step 3. Greatest: 6 bags. Each bag: 12 ÷ 6 = 2 apples, 18 ÷ 6 = 3 oranges.

Click the bags to check: 6 × 2 = 12 apples, 6 × 3 = 18 oranges.
