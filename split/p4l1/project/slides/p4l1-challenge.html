Challenge

1. There are 20 closed lockers, numbered 1 to 20. Child 1 opens every locker. Child 2 changes every 2nd locker (opens it if closed, closes it if open). Child 3 changes every 3rd locker, and so on, up to child 20. Which lockers are open at the end?

2. I am a number less than 100. When I am divided by 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6, there is always 1 left over. I am bigger than 1. Who am I?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. These are Think Harder Q7 and Q6. Answers: 1) Lockers 1, 4, 9 and 16: each locker is changed once for every factor of its number, and only numbers with an odd number of factors end up open. 2) 61: one less than the number is a common multiple of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6, which is 60.
