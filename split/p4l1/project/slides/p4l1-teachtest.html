Always true? Test it!

Ben says: "Every multiple of 4 is even." Siti says: "Every even number is a multiple of 4."

Step 1. Test Ben: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 … all even. 4 = 2 × 2, so the 4s are all in the 2s. Ben is right.

Step 2. Test Siti: 2? 6? 6 is even, but 6 is not in the 4 times table.

Siti is wrong. ONE example that does not work is enough to show it.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6b asks who is right). Ask the group to vote before clicking. Think-aloud: "To show something is ALWAYS true I need a reason, like 4 = 2 × 2. To show it is NOT always true I only need one example that breaks it." For children who are quick: every multiple of 10 is a multiple of 5, but not the other way round (15).
