31.5–32.5 MIN

Key idea: hunt in order

Factors: try 1, 2, 3 … in order until the pairs meet. Multiples: list them and look for the first number in both lists.

A factor goes into a number exactly. A multiple is in its times table.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Listing factors at random and missing one, or stopping too early (for 48, forgetting 6 × 8), instead of trying 1, 2, 3 … in order until the pairs meet. (2) Mixing up factors and multiples: saying 12 is a factor of 4. (3) For common multiples, multiplying the two numbers every time (10 × 15 = 150 minutes) instead of listing the multiples to find the first one they share (30).
