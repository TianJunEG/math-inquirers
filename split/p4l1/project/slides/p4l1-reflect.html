Minutes 86.5 to 90 (2 minutes of writing, 3.5 minutes with the routine). LOOKING BACK. Children fill in the box on the Investigation page, the page every child has (something I did well; the hardest part was; what helped me; one thing I could try next). Say one sentence: "Turn to page 3, the Looking back box. Write, tick or pass. Two minutes, no talking." The printed box says the rest. Model one yourself first: "Something I did well: I wrote the factor pairs in order. The hardest part was knowing when to stop listing factors. What helped me: writing the pairs in order and asking my partner. One thing I could try next: making a table of the factor pairs." Say: "Ticking I am still stuck is a good thing to do: it tells me where to help." Do not ask anyone to read theirs out. If a child wants to share, take one volunteer and thank them for the strategy. Never grade it, never correct it, never collect it for marks and never show it to the group. You may look at a child's page only when the child holds it to your camera. Never ask for photos or private messages. If I am still stuck is ticked, check in during the open session: a quick visit to the shared breakout room or a hand signal, with a parent nearby. Never send a DM. Praise strategies ("you drew it", "you asked for help"), not speed or scores.
