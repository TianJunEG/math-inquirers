Worked example: when do the buses meet?

Bus A leaves every 6 minutes. Bus B leaves every 8 minutes. Both leave at 7:00 am. When do they next leave together?

Bus A: 6, 12, 18, 24, 30 … Bus B: 8, 16, 24, 32 …

First number in both lists: 24. They meet again at 7:24 am.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click to draw the jumps of 6 (teal, above), then the jumps of 8 (red, below), then circle where both land. Think-aloud: "Bus A leaves at 6, 12, 18, 24 minutes past 7. Bus B at 8, 16, 24. The first number in both lists is 24, the smallest common multiple of 6 and 8." Ask: "Ravi says 6 × 8 = 48, so they meet at 7:48. Is he right?" (They DO meet at 48, but they meet first at 24. Multiplying gives a common multiple, not always the first one.)
