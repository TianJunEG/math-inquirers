Count the factors: odd or even?

Factor pairs of 100

1 × 100

2 × 50

4 × 25

5 × 20

10 × 10

Factor pairs of 18

1 × 18

2 × 9

3 × 6

18: 3 pairs, 6 factors (even). 100: 9 factors (odd)!

10 × 10 uses 10 twice, so 10 is counted once. A number times itself has an ODD number of factors.

Your turn: How many factors does 81 have?

5: 1, 3, 9, 27, 81

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5 counts factors). Click to list the pairs of 100 and of 18. Ask: "Count the factors of 100. Why is it odd when every factor has a partner?" (10 is its own partner). Numbers like 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 100 are a number times itself, called square numbers: they make square arrays, like the 6 by 6 square from the explore. 81 = 9 × 9: pairs 1 × 81, 3 × 27, 9 × 9, so 5 factors.
