Multiple riddles: one clue at a time

I am a number between 40 and 80. I am a multiple of 3 and a multiple of 5. I am even. Who am I?

Step 1. Multiple of 3 AND 5: count in 15s. 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90

Step 2. Between 40 and 80: 45, 60, 75

Step 3. Even: only 60. Check every clue: 60 = 3 × 20 = 5 × 12 ✓

Tip: list the multiples of the bigger number, then cross out any that do not fit the next clue.

Math Inquirers Level 4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6a). Use the clues one at a time and make a list. Ask before step 1: "Which numbers are multiples of both 3 and 5?" Some children will list the 3s and the 5s and circle the numbers in both; that works too. Think-aloud: "After each clue my list gets shorter. When only one number is left, I check it against EVERY clue."
