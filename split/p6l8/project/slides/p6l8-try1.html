Your turn: clocks and days

Whiteboards. Use mod.

a 10 o’clock + 27 hours. What time? (27 mod 12 = ?)

1 o’clock

b Today is Wednesday. What day is it in 20 days?

Tuesday

c It is 2 o’clock. What time was it 5 hours ago?

9 o’clock

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards, then click. a) 27 mod 12 = 3, 10 + 3 = 13, 13 − 12 = 1 o’clock. b) 20 mod 7 = 6, Wednesday + 6 = Tuesday (Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun, Mon, Tue). c) 2 − 5 = −3, below 0 so add 12: 9. Going backwards: if you go below the start, add a full turn.
