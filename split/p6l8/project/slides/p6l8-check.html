Sneak-peek puzzles

Two ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome. “I do not know” is welcome. Nobody is marked. When your tutor says, everyone says the answer together on three.

Puzzle 1: try it with objects, a drawing or counting

A clock shows 8 o’clock. What time is it 5 hours later? Use a clock drawing or count on with your fingers.

1 o’clock (8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 1). Counting on round the clock face works.

Puzzle 2: have a think

Today is Wednesday. What day of the week will it be in 100 days? What do you think? How could you find out?

Friday. 100 ÷ 7 leaves remainder 2 (98 is 14 full weeks), so move 2 days on from Wednesday: Thursday, Friday. Guessing and “I do not know” are fine.
