Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 8. Show each question, give thinking time, whiteboards up together, then click. 1) 10 o’clock. 2) 10 + 4 = 14, past 12, so 2 o’clock. 3) 3 − 5 = −2: go back 3 to 12, then 2 more to 10. 4) 12 hours is a full turn: back to 8. 5) Monday + 3 = Thursday. 10 days: one full week (7) and 3 more: Thursday again. Ask: "What do you do when you go past 12?" (start again at 1). This wrapping round is today's big idea.
