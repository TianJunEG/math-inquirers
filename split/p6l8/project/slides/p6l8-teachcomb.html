Two shifts in a row

Code with shift 15, then code the result with shift 15. What is the same as one shift?

Step 1. Take A (0): shift 15 gives 15 (P). Shift 15 again: 30, and 30 mod 26 = 4 (E).

Step 2. So A became E: that is one shift of 4. Shifts add up: 15 + 15 = 30, and 30 mod 26 = 4.

Step 3. Shifts add up like jumps on a 26-hour clock. Shift 26 is a full turn: it changes nothing.

Shift 15 then shift 15 is shift 4

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5 combines other shifts). Use a letter to show it: A → P → E, and A shift 4 → E ✓. Say: a shift is a jump on the clock, and two jumps add. Ask: "What shift undoes shift 15?" (11, since 15 + 11 = 26).
