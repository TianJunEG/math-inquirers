1. Today is Monday. What day will it be in 365 days? In 366 days?

2. Mei codes with a shift of 3. Ben codes with a shift of 29. Do their codes give the same answer? Why?

3. Work out 47 mod 12, 35 mod 12 and (47 + 35) mod 12. What do you notice?

Answers: 1) Tuesday and Wednesday (365 = 52 × 7 + 1, 366 = 52 × 7 + 2) 2) Yes: 29 mod 26 = 3, so a shift of 29 is a full turn (26) plus 3. 3) 11, 11 and 10. Adding the remainders and taking mod 12 again gives the same answer: (11 + 11) mod 12 = 22 mod 12 = 10.
