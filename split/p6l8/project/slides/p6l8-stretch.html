Think harder

1. The pattern CODECODECODE… goes on and on. What is the 100th letter?

2. It is 10 o’clock now. What time will it be 1000 hours from now (on a 12-hour clock)?

3. Find the smallest number bigger than 10 that leaves a remainder of 3 when it is divided by 4 and also when it is divided by 5.

4. (a) Work out 29 mod 12, 50 mod 7 and 100 mod 26. (b) Decode the whole message from Q4 (BPM SMG QA CVLMZ BPM UIB) with the shift you found.

The same four questions as the printed Think harder page, for anyone who wants them. Answers: 1) E (100 mod 4 = 0, so the 100th letter is the 4th letter of CODE) 2) 2 o’clock (1000 mod 12 = 4, and 10 + 4 = 14, 14 − 12 = 2) 3) 23 (3 more than a multiple of 20: 23 = 5 × 4 + 3 = 4 × 5 + 3) 4) (a) 29 mod 12 = 5, 50 mod 7 = 1, 100 mod 26 = 22.
