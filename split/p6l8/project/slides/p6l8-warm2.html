Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8 to 10. Whiteboards, then click. 1) 17 = 3 × 5 + 2. 2) 30 = 4 × 7 + 2. 3) 26 ÷ 26 = 1 remainder 0. 4) 40 = 3 × 12 + 4. 5) 7 = 2 × 3 + 1 and 10 = 3 × 3 + 1. Today we call the remainder "mod": 17 mod 5 = 2. Ask: "What are the only possible remainders when you divide by 5?" (0, 1, 2, 3, 4).
