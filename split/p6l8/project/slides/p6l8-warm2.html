Minutes 9 to 11.5. Whiteboards, then click. Three questions only. 1) 17 = 3 × 5 + 2. 2) 26 ÷ 26 = 1 remainder 0. 3) 40 = 3 × 12 + 4. Today we call the remainder "mod": 17 mod 5 = 2. Ask: "What are the only possible remainders when you divide by 5?" (0, 1, 2, 3, 4).
