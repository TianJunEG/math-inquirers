A week is a 7-day clock

Today is Thursday. What day is it in 45 days?

Step 1. A week wraps round after 7 days, like a clock after 12. So use mod 7.

Step 2. 45 ÷ 7 = 6 remainder 3, so 45 mod 7 = 3. Six whole weeks bring us back to Thursday.

Step 3. Then 3 more days: Friday, Saturday, Sunday.

In 45 days it is Sunday

Check: 6 × 7 = 42, and 42 + 3 = 45 ✓. Do not count today as day 1.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click through; ask the group to predict before each reveal. Think-aloud: "45 days is 6 whole weeks and 3 days. The whole weeks do nothing, because after 7 days it is the same day again. So only the remainder matters: 3 days after Thursday is Sunday." Watch for: counting today as day 1 (that gives Saturday). Ask: "What day was it 45 days ago?" (Thursday − 3 = Monday).
