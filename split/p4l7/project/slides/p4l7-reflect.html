Minutes 86.5 to 90 (2 minutes of writing, 3.5 minutes with the routine). LOOKING BACK. Children fill in the box on the Investigation page, the page every child has (something I did well; the hardest part was; what helped me; one thing I could try next). Say one sentence: "Turn to page 3, the Looking back box. Write, tick or pass. Two minutes, no talking." The printed box says the rest. Model one yourself first: "Something I did well: I wrote the lengths on the picture. The hardest part was finding the missing lengths on the staircase. What helped me: writing every length I could work out on the picture. One thing I could try next: pushing the steps out to make a rectangle." Say: "Ticking I am still stuck is a good thing to do: it tells me where to help." Do not ask anyone to read theirs out. If a child wants to share, take one volunteer and thank them for the strategy. Never grade it, never correct it, never collect it for marks and never show it to the group. Never ask a child to hold a page to the camera. Never ask for photos or private messages. If I am still stuck is ticked, check in during the open session: a quick visit to the shared breakout room or a hand signal, with a parent nearby. Never send a DM. Praise strategies ("you drew it", "you asked for help"), not speed or scores.
