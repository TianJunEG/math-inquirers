Borders: big rectangle take away the inside

Step 1. Whole thing: add 1 m on EACH side. 7 + 2 = 9 m long, 5 + 2 = 7 m wide.

Step 2. Path = whole take away garden: 9 × 7 − 7 × 5 = 63 − 35 = 28 m².

Step 3. Check: four strips 7 + 7 + 5 + 5 = 24, plus the 4 corner squares: 24 + 4 = 28 m² ✓
