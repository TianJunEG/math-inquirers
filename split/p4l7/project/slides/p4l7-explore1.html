Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Take 12 square tiles. Each is 1 cm by 1 cm.
  2. Use all 12 to make a rectangle. Write its length, breadth and perimeter.
  3. Make every different rectangle you can.
  4. Which has the longest perimeter? The shortest? What do you notice?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs build rectangles with 12 tiles (online: paper squares on camera, or draw on 1 cm grid paper). Ask them to predict first: "How many rectangles? Will they all have the same perimeter?" Many children think the same tiles must give the same perimeter. Let them find out. Early finishers: try 16 tiles. Which rectangle has the smallest perimeter now? (4 by 4, 16 cm.)
