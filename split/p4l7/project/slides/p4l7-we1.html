Worked example: area two ways

The same L-shape. The missing sides are 3 cm and 2 cm.

Split it: 5 × 6 = 30 and 3 × 4 = 12. 30 + 12 = 42 cm²

Take away: 8 × 6 = 48. 48 − 6 = 42 cm²

Both ways give 42 cm². Two ways that agree: a good check!

Take away is quickest when the missing piece is small. Splitting works for any shape with square corners.
