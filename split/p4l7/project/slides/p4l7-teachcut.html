Cut it and move it: what changes?

Cut a 6 cm by 2 cm rectangle in half. Stack the halves.

Area 12 cm². Perimeter 16 cm.

Area: still 12 cm². Nothing was added or lost.

Perimeter: 3 + 4 + 3 + 4 = 14 cm. It changed!

Cutting and moving keeps the area the same. The perimeter can change.
