Staircases: push every step out

Only two lengths are given. Can we still find the perimeter?

Step 1. The across sides (red) push up to the top. Together they are as long as the bottom: 8 cm.

Step 2. The down sides (green) push across to the right. Together they are as tall as the left: 5 cm.

Step 3. So the perimeter is the same as an 8 cm by 5 cm rectangle: 8 + 5 + 8 + 5 = 26 cm.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4 is a staircase with different numbers). Ask first: "Can we find the perimeter when the steps have no labels?" Click 1: trace the three red sides and push them up with your finger: "however long each step is, together they reach all the way across, 8 cm." Click 2: the same for the green sides: "together they go all the way down, 5 cm." Click 3: the rectangle. Then check by counting the grid: 2 + 1 + 3 + 2 + 3 + 2 + 8 + 5 = 26.
