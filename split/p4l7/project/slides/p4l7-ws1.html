Page 1 (Start here): with your partner

Q1 The L-shape. Missing lengths first, then the area two ways, then the perimeter.

Q2 Every rectangle with area 24 cm². List in order, starting with a length of 1 cm.

Q3 On your own, first: six tiles. Find the smallest and the largest perimeter.

Stuck? Build it with tiles, or draw it on 1 cm grid paper. Pairs: Q1 and Q2. Then Q3 alone. After question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder.
