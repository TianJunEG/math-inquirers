Key idea: split, take away, push out

Area: split into rectangles, or take the missing piece away from a big rectangle. Perimeter: push the steps out to make a rectangle.

The same area can have different perimeters. Close to a square gives the smallest perimeter.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key ideas together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Adding only the labelled sides of an L-shape, or giving a missing side the wrong length instead of using the opposite sides. (2) Thinking the same area means the same perimeter, or that cutting a corner off makes the perimeter smaller. (3) Finding the area of a border as 2 × (length + breadth) and forgetting the 4 corner squares.
