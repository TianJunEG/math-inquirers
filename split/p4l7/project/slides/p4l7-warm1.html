Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Area or Perimeter? Children make two cards (or write A and P on two halves of a whiteboard). Read each job aloud. Count 3, 2, 1 and everyone shows A or P. Then click to reveal (clicks go job 1 to job 6). Ask each time: "Why?" Listen for "it covers the floor" (area, square units like m²) and "it goes round the edge" (perimeter, units like m). Job 5 is the one to argue about: the wood goes round the photo, so it is perimeter; the glass in the frame would be area. Quick finisher: "Make up a job where you need BOTH." (A garden: grass seed for the area, a fence for the perimeter.) Watch for children who say "area is length times breadth" without knowing what it measures: ask them to point to the area on the slide.
