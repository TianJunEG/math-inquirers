Perimeter: push the steps out

Going round: 5 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 8 + 6 = 28 cm. Is there a quicker way?

Step 1. Slide the 3 cm step up to the top edge. Slide the 2 cm step across to the right edge.

Step 2. Now the outline is an 8 cm by 6 cm rectangle. Nothing was added or lost.

8 + 6 + 8 + 6 = 28 cm ✓

Careful: this works when the steps all go one way. A gap in the middle of a side has extra sides you cannot push out.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Ask first: "We know all six sides now. How do we find the perimeter?" Add them going round, starting at the top left corner: 5 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 8 + 6 = 28. Then: "Is there a quicker way?" Click 1: slide the step sides with your finger. Think-aloud: "The 3 cm side is level with the top, so if I slide it up it fills the gap in the top edge: 5 + 3 = 8. The 2 cm side slides right to sit on top of the 4 cm side: 2 + 4 = 6." Click 2: the dashed rectangle. Click 3: same answer. Click 4: the warning, which comes back before page 2. Note: the AREA did change (48 versus 42); only the outline length is the same.
