LESSON 7 • WEEK 4 • 0–1.5 MIN

Area Architects II

Area and perimeter, rectangles and squares

TODAY WE WILL

Find the area and perimeter of tricky shapes the smart way, and find out why the same area can have different perimeters.

TODAY'S TOOL

Spot the pattern

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find the area of a composite figure by splitting it into rectangles or by taking away a missing piece, find its perimeter by pushing the steps out to a rectangle, find every rectangle with a given area and compare their perimeters, and explain what changes when a shape is cut and rearranged. Thinking strategies taught today: SPLIT IT or TAKE AWAY, PUSH THE STEPS OUT, and MAKE A LIST IN ORDER. Materials: 1 cm square tiles or 24 paper squares per child, 1 cm grid paper, scissors, a 30 cm ruler, mini whiteboards, a paper strip 8 squares by 2 squares for cutting. Online: children cut out paper squares and 1 cm grid strips before the lesson and build on camera; the tutor draws on the grid figures on the slides. Time plan (90 minutes in all; every part has a budget and there is a 3-minute standing buffer inside the page-2 block). Opening 0 to 1.5. Sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 6.5 (puzzle 1 about 60 seconds, puzzle 2 about 90 seconds, then ONE check moment for both puzzles; answers ticked or said together on three; nothing held up to the camera, nobody marked). Warm-up game 6.5 to 8.5 (one short round, about 90 seconds), quick questions 8.5 to 11.5 (three questions). Explore 11.5 to 23.5 (the only breakout of the first half: about 60 seconds each way, closed by a 60-second timer; a Go further what-if is on the slide from the start; two debrief questions). Teaching 23.5 to 29 (click to reveal each step and answer; there is no "your turn" slide today). Brain break 29 to 32.5 (water and toilet first, then the game; clock check at the end of the break: if you are more than 1.5 minutes behind, skip the next optional item, never a check, the exit ticket or Looking back). Worksheet 32.5 to 78.5: page 1 (Start here, in pairs) 32.5 to 48.5, check together 48.5 to 50.5, short teaching before page 2 50.5 to 54, page 2 on your own 54 to 75 (the last 3 minutes are the standing buffer: if earlier parts ran long, spend the buffer first, then the choice questions, and never the checks, the exit ticket or Looking back), check together 75 to 78.5. Talk time 78.5 to 83.5 (one minute of pair talk, no breakout; one minute is the tool question: where did you use today's tool?). Exit ticket 83.5 to 87.5. Looking back 87.5 to 90 (common look-back: 90-second timer, then about 60 seconds to stamp journals). Pairs: Q1 and Q2. Then Q3 alone. After question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation have no fixed time slot: any child may choose them at any time, or take them home. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and pairs are not split unless both children agree. Online: page 1 is pair work, so allow about 2 minutes to move into and out of breakout rooms (or run page 1 as a whole-group screen-share if there are only 8 children). SPINE: Today's tool is Spot the pattern. Look for what repeats or changes by the same amount, then say the rule. Look for where you use it. Point at the card on page 1 of the workbook.
