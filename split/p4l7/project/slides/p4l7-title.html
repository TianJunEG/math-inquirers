LESSON 7 • WEEK 4

Area Architects II

Area and perimeter, rectangles and squares

TODAY WE WILL

Find the area and perimeter of tricky shapes the smart way, and find out why the same area can have different perimeters.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find the area of a composite figure by splitting it into rectangles or by taking away a missing piece, find its perimeter by pushing the steps out to a rectangle, find every rectangle with a given area and compare their perimeters, and explain what changes when a shape is cut and rearranged. Thinking strategies taught today: SPLIT IT or TAKE AWAY, PUSH THE STEPS OUT, and MAKE A LIST IN ORDER. Materials: 1 cm square tiles or 24 paper squares per child, 1 cm grid paper, scissors, a 30 cm ruler, mini whiteboards, a paper strip 8 squares by 2 squares for cutting. Online: children cut out paper squares and 1 cm grid strips before the lesson and build on camera; the tutor draws on the grid figures on the slides. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
