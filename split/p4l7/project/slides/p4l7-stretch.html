Early finishers / take-home

Stretch

1. A path 2 m wide goes all round a lawn 10 m by 6 m. Find the area of the path.

2. Ali joins 8 square tiles (1 cm by 1 cm) edge to edge. What is the smallest perimeter? The largest?

3. A rectangle has whole-cm sides, an area of 40 cm² and a perimeter of 26 cm. Find its length and breadth.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 80 m²: the whole thing is 14 m by 10 m = 140 m², take away the lawn 60 m² (watch for 12 m by 8 m: the path is on both sides). 2) Smallest 12 cm (a 2 by 4 rectangle, or a 3 by 3 square with one corner missing); largest 18 cm (any shape without a 2 by 2 block, such as a row of 8). 3) 8 cm by 5 cm: list the pairs for 40 (1 by 40, 2 by 20, 4 by 10, 5 by 8); only 5 by 8 has perimeter 26.
