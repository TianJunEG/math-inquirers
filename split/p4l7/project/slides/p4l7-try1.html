Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards, then click. A: the missing piece is 4 cm by 2 cm (7 − 3 = 4 across, 5 − 3 = 2 down). Take away: 35 − 8 = 27. Split: 3 × 5 + 4 × 3 = 15 + 12 = 27. B: take away the gap: 24 − 4 = 20. Or split into 3 pieces: 2 × 4 + 2 × 2 + 2 × 4 = 8 + 4 + 8 = 20. C: 100 − 9 = 91. Ask: "Which way was quicker for B?"
