A 7 cm by 5 cm rectangle has a 2 cm by 2 cm square cut from one corner. Find the area and the perimeter of the shape left.

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 85 to 90. Answer: area 35 − 4 = 31 cm²; perimeter 24 cm, the same as the 7 by 5 rectangle (push the corner back out). Children who write 20 cm for the perimeter forgot the two new sides; children who write 31 cm mixed up area and perimeter. Extension: Ravi has 24 m of fencing for a rectangular pen with whole-metre sides. One side is a wall and needs no fence. What is the largest area? (72 m²: 6 m, 12 m, 6 m.)
