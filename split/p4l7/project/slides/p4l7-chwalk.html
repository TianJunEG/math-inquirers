Q1: slide the paths to the edges

Moving the paths does not change the grass.

Grass: 10 − 1 = 9 m by 8 − 1 = 7 m

9 × 7 = 63 m²

Check: 80 − 10 − 8 + 1 = 63 ✓ (the crossing was counted twice)

Q2: the area stays the same

Rectangle: 9 × 4 = 36 cm²

The square also has 36 cm². 6 × 6 = 36, so a side is 6 cm.

Perimeter: 9 + 4 + 9 + 4 = 26 cm, then 6 × 4 = 24 cm

It went DOWN by 2 cm: a square is the most squashed-up shape.
