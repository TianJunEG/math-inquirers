Challenge

1. A garden is 10 m long and 8 m wide. Two paths 1 m wide cross it: one along its length and one across its width. What area of the garden is NOT path?

2. A rectangle 9 cm long and 4 cm wide is cut into 2 pieces. The pieces fit together exactly to make a square. How long is a side of the square? Did the perimeter go up or down?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. These are Think Harder Q6 and Q7. Answers: 1) 63 m² (slide the paths to the edges: the grass is 9 m by 7 m). 2) 6 cm (the area is 36 cm² and 6 × 6 = 36); the perimeter went down from 26 cm to 24 cm.
