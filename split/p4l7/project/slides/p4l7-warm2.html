Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 10. Whiteboards up together, then click. Insist on units: cm for perimeter, cm² for area. After 3 ask: "How did you find the breadth?" (48 ÷ 8, or which number times 8 makes 48). After 5: "Why is the side 7?" (28 ÷ 4 = 7, all four sides are equal), then 7 × 7 = 49. Answers: 42 cm², 36 cm, 6 cm, 34 cm, 49 cm².
