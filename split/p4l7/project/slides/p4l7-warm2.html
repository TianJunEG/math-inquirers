Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8.5 to 11.5. Write it, say the answer together on three, then click. Three questions, about 30 seconds each. Insist on units: cm for perimeter, cm² for area. No follow-up questions. For 3: 48 ÷ 8, or which number times 8 makes 48. Answers: 42 cm², 36 cm, 6 cm. The other two warm-up questions (perimeter of a 12 by 5 rectangle; a square with perimeter 28 cm, its area) are in the Weekly Dose of Thinking Math.
