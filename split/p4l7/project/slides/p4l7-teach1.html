Find the missing lengths first

Every corner is a square corner. Two sides have no label.

Step 1. Across: the bottom is 8 cm and the top is 5 cm. So the step across is 8 − 5 = 3 cm.

Step 2. Down: the left is 6 cm and the right is 4 cm. So the step down is 6 − 4 = 2 cm.

Opposite sides: the short pieces on one side add up to the long side opposite.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Ask first: "Can we find the area yet?" (No: we do not know every length.) Click 1: run a finger along the bottom (8 cm) and then along the top: 5 cm plus the step across. Think-aloud: "The bottom goes all the way across. The top and the step across together go the same distance. 8 − 5 = 3." Click 2: the same going down: 6 = 4 + the step, so 2 cm. Click 3: the general idea. Check by counting grid squares: 3 and 2.
