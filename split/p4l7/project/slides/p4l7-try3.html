Your turn: page 2 warm-up

Push out, cut and move, or take away.

a A staircase is 9 cm wide and 6 cm high. Perimeter?

30 cm

b A 10 cm by 2 cm strip is cut in half and stacked: 5 cm by 4 cm. Perimeter now?

18 cm

c The 10 cm by 2 cm strip is cut in half and stacked. Area now?

20 cm²

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards, then click. a) 9 + 6 + 9 + 6 = 30. b) 5 + 4 + 5 + 4 = 18 (it was 24; the area stays 20 cm²). c) 10 × 2 = 20 and 5 × 4 = 20: cutting and moving keeps the area the same.
