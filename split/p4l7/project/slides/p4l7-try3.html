Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards, then click. a) 9 + 6 + 9 + 6 = 30. b) 5 + 4 + 5 + 4 = 18 (it was 24; the area stays 20 cm²). c) The whole thing is 6 cm by 5 cm = 30; take away 12: 18 cm².
