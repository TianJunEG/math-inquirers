Your turn: perimeter

Push the steps out. Then check by adding every side.

a A 9 cm by 6 cm rectangle has a 2 cm square cut from one corner. Perimeter?

30 cm

b Figure A from before (7 cm by 5 cm, corner piece missing). Perimeter?

24 cm

c True or false: cutting a corner off a rectangle makes its perimeter smaller.

False

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards, then click. a) 9 + 6 + 9 + 6 = 30: the corner sides push back out. b) 7 + 5 + 7 + 5 = 24; check: 3 + 2 + 4 + 3 + 7 + 5 = 24. c) False: the two sides you lose are replaced by two new sides of the same length, so the perimeter stays the same (but the area gets smaller).
