Key idea: list in order, change the shape

Perimeter: push the steps out, but add the walls of a dent. Area: cut it into rectangles, or fill it in and take away.

Same perimeter does not mean same area. List rectangles in order so none are missed.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key ideas together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Mixing up area and perimeter, or writing cm for an area (it is cm²). (2) Counting squares along the edge instead of edges for perimeter: a corner square has 2 edges on the outside, so count edges, not squares. (3) Pushing out a shape with a dent and forgetting the dent's two walls. (4) In Q2, listing a garden twice (5 by 4 and 4 by 5) or missing one because the list is not in order. (5) Finding the missing sides of the L-shape wrongly: take away, do not add.
