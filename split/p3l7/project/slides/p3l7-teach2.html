Change the shape: push the steps out

Each square is 1 cm. What is the perimeter of this staircase?

Step 1. Walk round and count every edge: 12 edges, so 12 cm.

Step 2. Push the steps out: slide the top pieces up and the side pieces right. Now it is a 3 by 3 square.

Step 3. Pushing out does not change any lengths. Perimeter = 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12 cm ✓

Careful: this works for steps. A dent going into the shape has two extra walls. Look out for dents!

Teach heuristic 2: change the shape. Ask first: "What is the perimeter?" Let them count. Click 1: the 12 edges numbered (walk round with your finger). Click 2: the red arrows slide each step piece out to the dashed square. Click 3: same lengths, so same perimeter as the 3 by 3 square. Think-aloud: "Counting edges works, but it is slow and I can lose my place. When a shape goes down in steps, I can push the steps out. The pieces facing up slide up and make the top. The pieces facing right slide right and make the right side. Nothing gets longer or shorter, so the perimeter is the same as the big square."
