Worked example: the steps have no numbers!

Find the perimeter of this staircase.

Step 1. Slide every piece that faces up to the top. Together they are as long as the bottom: 8 cm.

Step 2. Slide every piece that faces right. Together they are as tall as the left side: 6 cm.

Perimeter = 8 + 6 + 8 + 6 = 28 cm

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Ask first: "Can we find the perimeter when we do not know how long each step is?" Many will say no. Click 1: the yellow pieces all face up; they slide up and make a top side as long as the bottom, 8 cm. Click 2: the red pieces face right; they slide right and make a right side as tall as the left, 6 cm. Click 3: 8 + 6 + 8 + 6 = 28 cm. Think-aloud: "I do not need the step lengths. The up-facing pieces must add up to the bottom, because the shape goes all the way across. The right-facing pieces must add up to the left side, because the shape goes all the way up." (Check: the steps are 3, 2, 3 cm across and 2, 2, 2 cm up.)
