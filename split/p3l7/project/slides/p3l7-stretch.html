For early finishers

Stretch

1. Find all the rectangles with whole-cm sides and an area of 16 cm². Which has the shortest perimeter?

2. A square has a perimeter of 32 cm. It is cut into two same-size rectangles. What is the perimeter of each rectangle?

3. Draw a figure of 7 squares (each 1 cm) with a perimeter of 12 cm.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) 16 by 1 (34 cm), 8 by 2 (20 cm), 4 by 4 (16 cm): the 4 by 4 square has the shortest perimeter. 2) Side 32 ÷ 4 = 8 cm, so each rectangle is 8 cm by 4 cm: perimeter 24 cm. 3) For example a 2 by 4 rectangle with one corner square missing, or a 3 by 3 square with two corners missing (corners pushed out: still 12 cm). Squeeze the squares together as much as you can.
