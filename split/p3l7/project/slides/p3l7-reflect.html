Look back

Two minutes. Open the last page of today’s lesson. Tick or write. You may pass on any line.

Something I did well:

Tick one: I kept trying, I asked a question, I checked my answer, I tried a new idea, or something else.

The hardest part was ...

For example: finding missing sides, listing all the rectangles, telling area from perimeter, or something else.

What helped me:

Tick one: drawing a picture, making a list, trying a number and checking, I asked for help, or I am still stuck (I can ask for a check-in in the open class, with my parent nearby).

Minutes 86 to 88. Look back: short and safe. SAY: "Turn to the last page of today and find Look back. Tick or write one thing for each line. There are no right answers, and you may pass on any line." Give a full 2 minutes. The page is never collected, marked or read out in front of the group. Children keep their page. You cannot see the paper, so say to the WHOLE group: "If you ticked still stuck, say check-in please in the open class, or ask your grown-up to tell me. I will talk it through with you in the open class, with your parent nearby." No hand signals that single a child out, no private messages. Open the next lesson with the whole group: "What is still tricky from last time?" Invite volunteers only (one or two) to share something they did well, or what helped. Praise the strategy ("you drew it and that got you unstuck"), not the speed or the answer. Then stamp the journal and close (minutes 88 to 90).
