Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8 to 11. Whiteboards. Read each question, give thinking time, then click to show the answer. Answers: 1) 56 2) 24 cm (6 + 6 + 6 + 6, or 4 × 6) 3) 24 cm² (8 × 3). Three quick questions only; the square-root question (a square has an area of 49 cm²: each side 7 cm) is on the take-home box of the Go further page in the workbook. Ask after 2 and 3: "Both answers are 24. Are they the same?" No: 24 cm is a length round the edge; 24 cm² is 24 squares of space inside. Check every child writes cm² for an area.
