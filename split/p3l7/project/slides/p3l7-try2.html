Your turn: push out, but watch for dents

Whiteboards. Draw it first.

a

A staircase is 7 cm wide and 4 cm tall. Perimeter?

22 cm

b

A 6 cm square has a 2 cm square cut from one corner. Perimeter?

24 cm

c

Now the 2 cm square is cut from the middle of a side. Perimeter?

28 cm

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards, then click. a) Push out: a 7 by 4 rectangle, 7 + 4 + 7 + 4 = 22 cm, whatever the steps are. b) A cut corner pushes out: still 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 = 24 cm (the area drops to 32 cm², but the perimeter stays the same!). c) A dent: pushing out gives 24 cm, but the dent has two walls of 2 cm inside it, so 24 + 2 + 2 = 28 cm. Ask: "Why are b and c different?" In b the cut pieces face out and slide out; in c the two walls of the dent face each other and cannot slide anywhere.
