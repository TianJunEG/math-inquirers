That is 3 rectangles. b) Areas 5, 8, 9 cm²: the 3 by 3 square is biggest. c) Length + breadth = 7: list 6 and 1 (area 6), 5 and 2 (10), 4 and 3 (12). So 4 cm and 3 cm.
