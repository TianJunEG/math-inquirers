Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Everyone stands. Read each job aloud. Children show with their hands: trace a finger round an imaginary edge for perimeter, wipe a flat palm across for area. Then click to show the answer (clicks reveal jobs 1 to 6 in order). Answers: 1 fence: perimeter. 2 tiles: area. 3 ribbon round the edge: perimeter. 4 paint: area. 5 frame: perimeter. 6 cover a book: area. Ask each time: "Does the job go ROUND the edge, or does it COVER the inside?" Quick finisher: "Which unit would you use for the fence: m or m²? For the tiles?" (m for the fence, m² for the floor). Watch for children who think a frame is area because it is a flat thing: it only goes round the edge.
