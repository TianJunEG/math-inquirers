Area: cut it up, or fill it in

Find the area of this L-shape in two ways.

Step 1. Cut it into two rectangles: 5 × 6 = 30 and 3 × 4 = 12. 30 + 12 = 42 cm².

Step 2. Or fill in the missing corner: 8 × 6 = 48. Take away the corner: 3 × 2 = 6. 48 − 6 = 42 cm².

Both ways give 42 cm². Two ways agree, so we can trust our answer.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach: change the shape for area. First ask: "Which sides do we know? Which are missing?" (The step is 3 cm across, 8 − 5, and 2 cm down, 6 − 4.) Click 1: cut along the red line into a 5 by 6 and a 3 by 4 rectangle. Click 2: the second way: fill in the corner to make an 8 by 6 rectangle, then take the corner away. Click 3: both give 42. Think-aloud: "I cannot find the area of an L-shape in one go, so I change it into rectangles. I can cut it up, or fill it in and take away. If both ways give the same answer, I know I am right." Also ask: "What is its perimeter?" (Push out: 8 + 6 + 8 + 6 = 28 cm.) Parallel example: the worksheet L-shape is 10 cm by 7 cm.
