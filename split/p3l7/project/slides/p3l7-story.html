Story break: Karen Uhlenbeck

USA (born in Cleveland, Ohio) • born 1942

Karen Uhlenbeck was born in Cleveland, in the USA, in 1942. At university she began with physics but changed to mathematics. Think of a wire loop dipped in soapy water. The soap film pulls itself into the shape with the smallest area that fits the wire. Mathematicians call such shapes minimal surfaces. Uhlenbeck worked on them with another mathematician, Jonathan Sacks, and found new ways to study when and how they exist. In 2019 she became the first woman to win the Abel Prize, one of the top prizes in maths.
