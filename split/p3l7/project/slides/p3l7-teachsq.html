Same perimeter: a square and a rectangle

A square has the same perimeter as a rectangle 9 cm by 3 cm. What is the area of the square?

Step 1. Perimeter of the rectangle: 9 + 3 + 9 + 3 = 24 cm.

Step 2. A square has 4 equal sides: 24 ÷ 4 = 6 cm each.

Step 3. Area of the square: 6 × 6 = 36 cm².

The rectangle's area is only 9 × 3 = 27 cm². Same perimeter, different area again!
