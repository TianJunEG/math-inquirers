A rectangle has a perimeter of 12 cm. Its length is 4 cm. What is its area?

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 79 to 85. Answer: 8 cm². Length + width = 12 ÷ 2 = 6, so the width is 2 cm; 4 × 2 = 8. Children who write 48 multiplied 12 × 4; children who write 8 cm found the width as 12 − 4. Extension: find all the rectangles with whole-cm sides and a perimeter of 24 cm. Which has the biggest area? (6 rectangles; 6 by 6, 36 cm²) Answers are shown only to the tutor on camera (own paper). Never mark or read out answers in front of the group. Reflection (look back) follows on the next slide: allow 3 minutes, 2 for the children plus a short share.
