A rectangle has a perimeter of 16 cm. Its length is 5 cm. What is its area?

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 80 to 90. Answer: 15 cm². Length + breadth = 16 ÷ 2 = 8, so the breadth is 3 cm; 5 × 3 = 15. Children who write 80 multiplied 16 × 5; children who write 11 cm found the breadth as 16 − 5. Extension: find all the rectangles with whole-cm sides and a perimeter of 24 cm. Which has the biggest area? (6 rectangles; 6 by 6, 36 cm²)
