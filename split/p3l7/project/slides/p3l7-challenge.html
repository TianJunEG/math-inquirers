Challenge

1. Ali joins 5 same-size squares with sides of 2 cm, whole sides touching. What is the biggest perimeter his shape can have? The smallest?

2. A rectangle 7 cm by 5 cm has a 1 cm square cut out of each of its 4 corners. Find the new area and the new perimeter.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use as a pair challenge or in talk time. Ask for the reason every time. Answers: 1) Biggest 24 cm (12 edges of 2 cm, e.g. a row of 5); smallest 20 cm (10 edges: a 2 by 2 square with one more square on a side). 2) Area 35 − 4 = 31 cm². Perimeter 24 cm, the same as before.
