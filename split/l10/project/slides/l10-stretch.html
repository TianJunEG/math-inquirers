1. A ribbon 10 cubes long is cut into pieces that are 2 cubes or 3 cubes long, with nothing left over. List every way.

2. Ben is in front of Mei. Sam is last. Ali is not first. Mei is in front of Ali. In what order do the four children stand?

3. Ali has 7 coins. They are all 10¢ and 5¢ coins and are worth 50¢. How many of each coin does he have?

Answers: 1) Two ways: five pieces of 2 cubes; or two pieces of 3 cubes and two pieces of 2 cubes (3 + 3 + 2 + 2 = 10). Three pieces of 3 leave 1 over, and one piece of 3 leaves 7, which 2s cannot fill. 2) Ben, Mei, Ali, Sam. 3) Three 10¢ and four 5¢ (30 + 20 = 50).
