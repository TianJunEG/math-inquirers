Stretch

1. Mei thinks of a number, adds 7, takes away 2 and gets 19. Ben does the same and gets 25. How much bigger is Ben's number?

2. 4, 9, 14, 19, … Which number in this pattern is closest to 50?

3. Ali has 5 coins worth 60¢. He has only 5¢, 10¢ and 20¢ coins. Find every possible set.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) 6 bigger. Work backwards: Mei 19 + 2 = 21, 21 − 7 = 14; Ben 25 + 2 = 27, 27 − 7 = 20; 20 − 14 = 6. (Clever shortcut: both did the same steps, so the answers differ by the same amount: 25 − 19 = 6.) 2) 49 (list it: 24, 29, 34, 39, 44, 49, 54; 49 is 1 away, 54 is 4 away). 3) Two sets: 20 + 10 + 10 + 10 + 10, and 20 + 20 + 10 + 5 + 5. Make a table by number of 20¢ coins: one 20¢ needs 40¢ from 4 coins (four 10¢); two 20¢ need 20¢ from 3 coins (10 + 5 + 5); three 20¢ need 0¢ from 2 coins (impossible); no 20¢: 5 coins make at most 50¢.
