T1 A pattern starts at 4 and adds 6 each time. What is the 8th number? Is 50 in the pattern?

T2 Mei thinks of a number, adds 7, takes away 2 and gets 19. Ben does the same and gets 25. How much bigger is Ben's number?

T3 Ali has 5 coins worth 60¢. He has only 5¢, 10¢ and 20¢ coins. Find every possible set.

For any pair that chooses to try more. These are the same four questions as Think harder T1 to T4 in the workbook (the labels match). Answers: T1) 46 (4, 10, 16, 22, 28, 34, 40, 46). No: the pattern goes 46, 52, so it skips 50. T2) 6 bigger. Work backwards: Mei 19 + 2 = 21, 21 − 7 = 14; Ben 25 + 2 = 27, 27 − 7 = 20; 20 − 14 = 6. (Shortcut: both did the same steps, so the answers differ by the same amount: 25 − 19 = 6.) T3) Two sets: 20 + 10 + 10 + 10 + 10, and 20 + 20 + 10 + 5 + 5. Make a table by number of 20¢ coins: one 20¢ needs 40¢ from 4 coins (four 10¢); two 20¢ need 20¢ from 3 coins (10 + 5 + 5); three 20¢ need 0¢ from 2 coins (impossible); no 20¢: 5 coins make at most 50¢. T4) Apple wins with 11, which is 2 more than banana (9).
