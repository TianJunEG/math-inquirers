How to start a puzzle

Stuck? Do not just stare. Put the puzzle on paper.

Draw it

Use it for: People in order, more than and fewer than, number machines.

List it

Use it for: Find every way: coins, pairs of numbers, shapes to count.

Make a table

Use it for: Something that grows or changes step by step.

Why? A puzzle is hard to hold in your head. On paper you can see it, check it and explain it.
