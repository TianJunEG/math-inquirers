Draw it: put people in order

Jo is taller than Ravi. Lin is shorter than Ravi. Kai is shorter than Jo but taller than Ravi. Who is the shortest?

Step 1. Draw a line: tallest at the top. Jo goes above Ravi. Leave a space.

Worked example: draw it. Ask first: "Can you hold all of this in your head?" Click step 1, then Jo and Ravi appear. Think-aloud: "I draw a line with tall at the top. Jo is taller than Ravi, so Jo is higher. I leave a space in case someone goes between." Step 2, then Lin. Step 3, then Kai: "Shorter than Jo but taller than Ravi: that is the space I left." Answer: read the line from the bottom: Lin. Check each clue against the drawing aloud. The worksheet Q6 uses older and younger: same drawing, oldest at the top.
