Whiteboards. a) Line, oldest at the top: Cy, Ann, Bo. b) Draw cubes: take away the extra 4, 6 left; share: 3 and 3; give the 4 back: 3 and 7. Check 3 + 7 = 10 and 7 is 4 more than 3. c) Draw the machine and go backwards, last step first: 11 + 2 = 13, 13 − 6 = 7. Check 7 + 6 = 13, 13 − 2 = 11.
