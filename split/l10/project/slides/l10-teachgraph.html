Picture graphs: count, compare, combine

Step 1. How many more apples than pears? Line up the rows: 3 stick out. 6 − 3 = 3.

Step 2. How many fruits altogether? 6 + 3 + 5 + 4 = 18.

Step 3. Which two make exactly 11? List the pairs with apples first: 6 + 3 = 9, no. 6 + 5 = 11 ✓ Apples and plums.

Count each row carefully. To compare, find the difference. To combine, add.

Step 3: list the pairs in order so none are missed: apples with pears (9), plums (11 ✓), figs (10); then pears with plums (8), figs (7); plums with figs (9). Only apples and plums make 11.
