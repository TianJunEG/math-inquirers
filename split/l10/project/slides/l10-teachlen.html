Longer than, then end to end

A crayon is 5 cubes long. A ruler is 7 cubes longer than the crayon.

Step 1. Ruler: start from the crayon's bar and add 7. 5 + 7 = 12 cubes.

Step 2. End to end: 5 + 12 = 17 cubes. Not 5 + 7!

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q8). Click the ruler bar, step 1, the end-to-end bar, step 2. Think-aloud: "7 longer is not the ruler's length: it is the extra bit. The ruler is the crayon's 5 and 7 more: 12. End to end means both whole lengths: 5 + 12 = 17." Common mistake: 5 + 7 = 12 given as the end-to-end answer.
