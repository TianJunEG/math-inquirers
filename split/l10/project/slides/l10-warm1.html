Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 2 to 6. Whiteboard round. Say: "Everybody writes the answer at the same time. There is no passing round and nobody is put on the spot. If you are not sure, write a guess or draw it." Ask one question at a time, give thinking time, then click to show the answer to everyone. If answers differ, say "Different ideas are a good thing to talk about." Never groan at a wrong answer. Answers: 20; 6; 10¢ + 5¢ (or 5¢ + 5¢ + 5¢ is three coins); triangle; 7.
