Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Rushing: not checking the answer against every clue. (2) Q1: finding the rule from the first pair only. (3) Q3c and Q4b: stopping at the first pair or set that works without listing them all. (4) Q5b: counting only the 3 small triangles. (5) Q7: undoing in the wrong order.
