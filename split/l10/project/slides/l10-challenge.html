C1 Triangles in a row use 3, 5, 7 … sticks. Squares in a row use 4, 7, 10 … sticks. Which numbers of sticks, up to 31, can make a row of triangles AND (with the same number of sticks) a row of squares, with none left over? What do you notice?

Q3 is open: there is no single right answer, so ask children to check each other's work. Answers: 1) 7, 13, 19, 25 and 31. They go up by 6 (the 2 and the 3 from the two patterns meet every 6). For example 7 sticks make 3 triangles or 2 squares. 2) Ali 8, Ben 12, Mei 9. 3) Open. Check that the new puzzle can be solved and that the child explains the link between the change and the new answer. Example: change "Make 45¢ with exactly 3 coins" (20¢ + 20¢ + 5¢) to "Make 45¢ with exactly 4 coins": now the only way is 20¢ + 10¢ + 10¢ + 5¢.
