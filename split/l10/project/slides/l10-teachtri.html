Count triangles: small ones first

Step 1. Count the small triangles: 1, 2.

Step 2. Now triangles made of two small ones: the big one, 3.

Step 3. 3 triangles. Small first, then bigger, so none are missed.

Your turn: I have 4 sides and 4 corners. Only the opposite sides are the same length. What am I?

A rectangle
