List it: every way, in order

Which of these coins make exactly 40¢?

Step 1. Start with the biggest coin. 50¢ is more than 40¢: too much.

Step 2. Next, 20¢: 20 + 10 = 30, too little. 20 + 10 + 10 = 40 ✓

Step 3. Without 20¢: 10 + 10 = 20, too little. So the only way is 20¢ + 10¢ + 10¢.

Going in order means you do not miss a way, and you know when to stop.

Worked example: list it. Think-aloud: "I could grab coins at random, but then I might miss a way. So I go in order, biggest coin first. 50¢ is already too much. With the 20¢: 20 and 10 is 30, not enough; 20 and both 10s is 40, yes. Now without the 20¢: only the two 10s, that is 20, too little. I have tried everything, so there is only one way."
