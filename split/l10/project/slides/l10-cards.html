11–21 MIN

Puzzle cards: pick one

Your pair gets ONE card for 10 minutes. Then swap with another pair for 4 minutes.

Coins

Make 45¢ with exactly 3 coins from 5¢, 10¢, 20¢ and 50¢.

Patterns

5, 8, 11, 14 … What is the 10th number? Show how you know.

Logic

Mei is taller than Ben. Ben is taller than Sam. Ali is shorter than Sam. Put them in order, tallest first.

Measuring

A pencil is 6 cubes long. A pen is 4 cubes longer than the pencil. Put them end to end. How many cubes long is that?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 11 to 21. Each pair works on ONE puzzle card for 10 minutes (the printed cards are on the Puzzle cards page of the workbook; online, one card per breakout room). Then pairs swap cards with another pair for a 4-minute second round (21 to 25, with Explore: talk about it). Each pair reports back in 30 seconds. Answers: Coins) 20¢ + 20¢ + 5¢ (the only way). Patterns) 32: add 3 each time, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26, 29, 32. Logic) Mei, Ben, Sam, Ali. Measuring) 16 cubes: the pen is 10 cubes, and 6 + 10 = 16.
