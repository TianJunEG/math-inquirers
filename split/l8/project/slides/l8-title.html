Order things from clues, sort with two rules in a two-loop picture and use a grid to solve a puzzle.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Order things from clues, sort using two rules with a two-loop picture, solve a logic grid, and explain an odd one out. Materials: Two loops of string, buttons or small objects, clue cards. Online: Children sort objects from home by a rule shown on the slide and explain the rule on camera. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 72 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it (the last 4 minutes check page 2, after a 1-minute stand-up stretch), talk time 72 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90. Think Harder and Challenge are for early finishers or home.
