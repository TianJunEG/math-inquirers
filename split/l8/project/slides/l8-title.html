Order things from clues, sort with two rules in a two-loop picture and use a grid to solve a puzzle.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Order things from clues, sort using two rules with a two-loop picture, solve a logic grid, and explain an odd one out. Materials: Two loops of string, buttons or small objects, clue cards. Online: Children sort objects from home by a rule shown on the slide and explain the rule on camera. Time plan: opening 0 to 2, warm-up 2 to 8 (game 2 to 6, quick questions 6 to 8; ask questions 1 to 3, and 4 and 5 only if you are on time), sneak-peek puzzles 8 to 12, explore 12 to 26, teaching 26 to 36 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 36 to 40, worksheet 40 to 72 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it (a 30-second stand-and-stretch reset after the page-1 check, taken from that slot; the last 4 minutes check page 2, after a 1-minute stand-up stretch), talk time 72 to 79, exit ticket 79 to 85, look-back 85 to 88, then flex time and goodbye 88 to 90 (this is the buffer: if you are behind, use it; do not start anything new). Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are for every child to choose, any time or at home. Nobody is told which page they should be on.
