Order things from clues, sort with two rules in a Venn diagram and use a grid to solve a puzzle.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Order things from clues, sort using two rules with a Venn diagram, solve a logic grid, and explain an odd one out. Materials: Two loops of string, buttons or small objects, clue cards. Online: Children sort objects from home by a rule shown on the slide and explain the rule on camera. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, mini-lesson 25 to 35, brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
