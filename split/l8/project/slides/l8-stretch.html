Stretch

1. Ali, Ben, Cara and Dan each have a different pet: a cat, a dog, a fish or a rabbit. Cara has the dog or the fish. Cara does not have the dog. Dan does not have the cat or the rabbit. Ali does not have the cat. Who has which pet?

2. Five children stand in a line. Ali is in the middle. Ben is just in front of Ali. Cara is last. Dan is not first. Where is Eve?

3. Numbers 1 to 30: which do you say when counting in 2s AND when counting in 5s?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) Cara fish (dog or fish, not dog). Dan: not cat, not rabbit, fish taken, so dog. Ali: not cat, so rabbit. Ben: cat. 2) Eve is first. Ali is 3rd (the middle of 5), Ben 2nd, Cara 5th; 1st and 4th are left for Dan and Eve, and Dan is not first, so Dan 4th and Eve 1st: Eve, Ben, Ali, Dan, Cara. 3) 10, 20 and 30.
