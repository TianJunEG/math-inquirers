Put things in order: draw a ladder

Write them from longest to shortest.

Step 1. Clue 1: the pencil is longer than the crayon. Pencil goes ABOVE crayon.

Step 2. Clue 2: the ruler is longer than the pencil. Ruler goes above pencil.

Step 3. Clue 3: the eraser is shorter than the crayon. Eraser goes below crayon.

Longest to shortest: ruler, pencil, crayon, eraser

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 1 (Q1 puts children, animals and ribbons in order). Draw the ladder on the board: longer at the top, shorter at the bottom. Click 1: "Longer than" means higher up. Leave a gap above and below, because more clues may come. Click 2 and 3: place each new thing next to the one it is compared with. Think-aloud: "The ruler is longer than the pencil, so the ruler goes above the pencil. The eraser is SHORTER than the crayon, so it goes below the crayon." Common mistake: writing the names in the order they appear in the clues. For heavier and lighter, or taller and shorter, use the same ladder.
