Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key ideas together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Putting numbers in the middle of the two-loop picture that only fit one rule (for example 2 in the middle of "less than 6" and "odd"). (2) Giving up on the pets grid instead of using a cross to rule out. Before page 2 tell children: "Each child has a different pet." (3) Guessing a line-up without checking all the clues, or reading "behind" as "just behind". (4) In riddles, counting 20 or 40 as "between 20 and 40".
