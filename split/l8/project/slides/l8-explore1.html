Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 12 to 17. Pairs work with the materials: Two loops of string, buttons or small objects, clue cards. Overlap two string loops. Sort a mixed set of buttons or objects by two rules and find the ones that belong in the middle. Online: Children sort objects from home by a rule shown on the slide and explain the rule on camera. Go further (what-if) is on this slide from the start: any pair may choose it at any time, so nobody waits. The tutor does not choose who and does not announce it. Answers: Open. Check the child can say the two rules and why the middle things follow both.
