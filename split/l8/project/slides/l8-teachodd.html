Odd one out: what do the others share?

Which one does NOT belong? 5, 10, 12, 15

Step 1. 5, 10 and 15 are numbers we say when counting in 5s. 12 is not, so 12 does not belong.

Step 2. A second reason: 5 is the only number with 1 digit. So 5 can be the odd one out too!

Step 3. Say it like this: “___ does not belong because ___.”

Your turn: 4, 8, 10, 13

13: the only odd number

Math Inquirers Level 1 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6 asks for an odd one out and a second reason). Think-aloud: "I look for something that three of them share and the fourth one does not. 5, 10 and 15: I say them when I count in 5s. 12: I do not. So 12 does not belong. Can I find another reason? 5 has 1 digit; the others have 2 digits. So 5 is also an odd one out." There can be more than one right answer if the reason is true. Your turn: 13 is the only odd number (also the only one bigger than 10). Accept 10 too: it is the only one we say when counting in 5s.
