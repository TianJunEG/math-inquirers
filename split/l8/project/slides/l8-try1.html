Your turn: hats

Kim, Lee and Max each wear a different hat: red, blue or green. Draw a grid on your whiteboard.

Clue 1: Lee’s hat is not red and not blue.

Clue 2: Kim’s hat is not red.

Lee: green (clue 1 leaves only green)

Kim: blue (not red, and green is taken)

Max: red (the only hat left)

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Children draw a 3 by 3 grid (names down the side, hats across the top) and fill it in. Click for each answer after a child explains. Lee: clue 1 crosses out red and blue, so green. Then cross out green for Kim and Max. Kim: not red (clue 2) and not green, so blue. Max: red. Check each clue again. Watch for children who stop after Lee: ask "Which row has only one empty box now?"
