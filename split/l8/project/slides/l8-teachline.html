Line-ups: draw the row

Tom, Kim, Sue and Raj stand in a line. Sue is last. Tom is just in front of Sue. Kim is not first.

Step 1. Sue is last: she goes in the 4th circle.

Step 2. “Just in front of Sue” means right next to her: Tom is 3rd.

Step 3. Kim is not first, so Kim is 2nd. Raj is 1st.

Step 4. Check every clue ✓ Raj, Kim, Tom, Sue.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4 is a line-up of 4 children). Draw 4 circles on the board and label the front. Think-aloud: "I start with the clue that tells me something for sure: Sue is last. Then the clue that uses Sue: just in front of Sue means right next to her, so Tom is 3rd. Kim is not first, and the only places left are 1st and 2nd, so Kim is 2nd and Raj is 1st. Now I check every clue." Teach the words: "just in front of" or "just behind" means right next to; "in front of" or "behind" means somewhere before or after, not always next to.
