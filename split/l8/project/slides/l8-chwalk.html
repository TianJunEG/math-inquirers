Q2: Does anything stay the same? Does anything change?

2s and 3s: 2s only 7, 3s only 3, both 3 (6, 12, 18), neither 7. 2s and 4s: 2s only 5, 4s only 0, both 5 (4, 8, 12, 16, 20), neither 10. Every number we say counting in 4s we also say counting in 2s, so the 4s loop sits completely inside the 2s loop.

Q3 (open): Try it. Can your partner break your rule? Change one thing: what happens?

Open. Check by trying all 6 ways to hand out the pets. Example: Ana, Bo and Cy have a cat, a dog and a fish. Ana does not have the cat. Bo does not have the dog or the fish. Cy does not have the fish. Answer: Ana fish, Bo cat, Cy dog.

Use after pairs have tried. Reveal one answer at a time and ask a child to explain before you click. Many children stop at one answer: ask "Is there another way?" and "How do you know you found them all?" For Q3, ask two pairs to swap puzzles and solve each other's.
