Your turn: where does it go?

Loop A: bigger than 10. Loop B: numbers you say when counting in 5s.

middle

b Where does 5 go?

loop B only

c Where does 12 go?

loop A only

d Where does 7 go?

outside

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Draw the two loops on the board. Children write the answer on their own paper and say it together on three, then click. a) 15 is bigger than 10 AND we say it counting in 5s: middle. b) 5: we say it counting in 5s, but it is not bigger than 10: loop B only. c) 12 is bigger than 10, but we do not say it counting in 5s: loop A only. d) 7: neither, so outside both loops. Ask for a number that goes in the middle other than 15 (20, 25, 30 …).
