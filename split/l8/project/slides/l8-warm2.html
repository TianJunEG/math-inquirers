"More than 10?"

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 10. These follow on from Guess my number. Read each question aloud, give thinking time, take answers on whiteboards, then click. 1) More than 10 means 11 to 20; even ones are 12, 14, 16, 18, 20. 2) Of those, less than 15: 12 and 14. Point out that each clue crosses out more numbers. 3) Counting in 5s: 5, 10, 15 (20 is not less than 20); odd: 5 and 15. 4) "Is it 7?" usually crosses out only one number. "Is it more than 10?" crosses out about half the numbers, whatever the answer. Good questions cross out lots of numbers.
