Logic grids: one tick in each row and column

Zoe, Kai and Lin each have a different fruit: an apple, a banana or a pear.

Step 1. Clue: Kai has the banana. Put a ✓.

Step 2. Kai has only one fruit, so cross out the rest of his row.

Step 3. Nobody else has the banana, so cross out the rest of its column.

Each child has a DIFFERENT fruit. So every row and every column ends with exactly one ✓.

Math Inquirers Level 1 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Read the sentence and stress "different": no two children have the same fruit. Click 1: tick Kai and banana. Ask: "Can Kai also have the apple?" No, he has only one fruit. Click 2: cross out the rest of his row. Ask: "Can Zoe have the banana?" No, Kai has it. Click 3: cross out the rest of the column. Click 4: the why. Think-aloud: "Every time I put a tick, I get crosses for free: the rest of that row and the rest of that column. Every time I put a cross, I check whether a row or column now has only one empty box left."
