Worked example: who drinks what?

Tia, Sam and Raj each drink a different drink: milk, juice or water.

Clue 1: Tia does not drink milk. Clue 2: Sam does not drink milk or juice.

Step 1. Use the clues: cross out Tia–milk, Sam–milk and Sam–juice.

Step 2. Sam has only water left: ✓. Cross out the rest of the water column.

Step 3. Now Tia has only juice left: ✓. Cross out Raj–juice.

Step 4. Raj has milk ✓. Check both clues again.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click to reveal each step; ask the group what to write before each click. Think-aloud (from the tutor script): "Tia does not drink milk. Sam does not drink milk or juice. I put a cross where it cannot be. Sam cannot have milk or juice, so Sam has water. Now Tia cannot have milk and water is taken, so Tia has juice. Raj has milk." Stress that the puzzle only works because each child drinks a DIFFERENT drink. Ask: "Which row or column has only one empty box left?" That is always the next place to look.
