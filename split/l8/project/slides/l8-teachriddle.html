Number riddles: list, then cross out

I have 2 digits. My digits add up to 4. I am bigger than 20. I am odd. Who am I?

Step 1. Clue 1: list every 2-digit number whose digits add up to 4: 13, 22, 31, 40

Step 2. Clue 2: bigger than 20. Cross out 13. 22, 31, 40 are left.

Step 3. Clue 3: odd. Cross out 22 and 40. I am 31.

Step 4. Check 31 with every clue: 3 + 1 = 4, bigger than 20, odd ✓

Your turn: Between 20 and 30, odd, and you say it counting in 5s.
