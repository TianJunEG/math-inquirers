Worksheet page 1: with your partner

Q1 Order from clues. Draw a ladder: tallest, heaviest or longest at the top.

Q2 Two-loop picture, numbers 1 to 10. Test every number with BOTH rules.

Stuck? Say each clue aloud and move the names up or down your ladder. Odd numbers end in 1, 3, 5, 7 or 9. Even numbers end in 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 42 to 52. Walk around. Ask "How do you know?" before giving any help. For Q2, ask pairs to say the two questions for each number: "Is it less than 6? Is it odd?" At minute 40 read questions 1 and 2 aloud, then pairs read the rest. The workbook says: "When you finish this page you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder." Never announce who is on which page and never name a child in front of the group; invite the WHOLE group at once, for example: "When you have finished Start here, you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation." Do not split a pair unless both children agree.
