Worked example: sort into a two-loop picture

Numbers 10 to 20. Test every number with BOTH rules.

Step 1. Both rules: 10, 12, 14 go in the middle.

Step 2. Less than 15 only: 11, 13.

Step 3. Even only: 16, 18, 20.

Step 4. Neither rule: 15, 17, 19 go outside both loops.

Math Inquirers Level 1 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click to fill each part. For every number ask two questions out loud: "Is it less than 15? Is it even?" Two yeses: the middle. One yes: that loop only. Two noes: outside. Think-aloud: "12. Is it less than 15? Yes. Is it even? Yes. Both, so it goes where the loops overlap. 16. Less than 15? No. Even? Yes. So it goes in the even loop, but not the middle. 15. Less than 15? No, it is equal to 15. Even? No. So it goes outside." Ask: "Why does 12 go in the middle?" (it follows both rules). Check: 3 + 2 + 3 + 3 = 11 numbers, the same as 10 to 20.
