Go further

C1 A secret number is between 10 and 30. It is even, and you say it when counting in 3s. It is 2 more than a number you say when counting in 5s. What is it?

C2 Numbers 1 to 20. Sort them in a two-loop picture with the loops "I say it counting in 2s" and "I say it counting in 3s". How many numbers go in each part? Then use the loops "counting in 2s" and "counting in 4s". What is different, and why?

Make up your own

C3 Make up your own "Who has which pet?" puzzle with three friends and three pets. Write clues so that there is only ONE answer. Swap with a partner and check that they find your answer.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For any pair that chooses Go further. Use it as a pair choice, or with volunteers in talk time (never call a child by name). Ask for the reason every time. Q3 is open: there is no single right answer, so ask children to check each other's work. Answers: 1) 12. 2) 2s and 3s: 2s only 7, 3s only 3, both 3 (6, 12, 18), neither 7. 2s and 4s: 2s only 5, 4s only 0, both 5 (4, 8, 12, 16, 20), neither 10. Every number we say counting in 4s we also say counting in 2s, so the 4s loop sits completely inside the 2s loop. 3) Open. Check by trying all 6 ways to hand out the pets. Example: Ana, Bo and Cy have a cat, a dog and a fish. Ana does not have the cat. Bo does not have the dog or the fish. Cy does not have the fish. Answer: Ana fish, Bo cat, Cy dog.
