Challenge

1. A secret number is between 10 and 30. It is even, and you say it when counting in 3s. It is 2 more than a number you say when counting in 5s. What is it?

2. Numbers 1 to 20. Sort them into a Venn diagram with the loops "I say it counting in 2s" and "I say it counting in 3s". How many go in each part?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use as a pair challenge or in talk time. Ask for the reason every time. Pairs try first, then the "see it" slide, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) 12. 2) Counting in 2s only: 7. Counting in 3s only: 3. Both: 3 (6, 12, 18). Neither: 7.
