Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome. "I do not know" is welcome. Nobody is marked.

1. A 10 by 10 square has 100 small squares. Colour in 35 of them. How many are NOT coloured? What percentage is not coloured?

65 squares, so 65%

2. A jacket costs $100. The shop takes 10% off, and then takes another 10% off the NEW price. Is that the same as 20% off? What is the final price?

No. $81, not $80.

Show your answer only to your tutor: on your own paper, or all hold paper to the camera together. Then a volunteer can tell us how they thought.

Minutes 0 to 3. SNEAK-PEEK PUZZLES. Show puzzle 1, give about a minute, click to show the short answer, then puzzle 2. Say it out loud: these are ideas we have not learnt yet; guessing is welcome; "I do not know" is welcome; nobody is marked. Puzzle 1 is an access puzzle: any child can try it with objects, drawing or counting, so everyone can start well. Puzzle 2 is a thinking puzzle. Answers are shown PRIVATELY (own paper shown only to you as you walk round; online, everyone holds paper to the camera at the same moment, or shows it to you only, on camera). Never boards in front of the group, never read out answers with names, no tally, no ranking. Never record scores. Answers and what to listen for. Q1: 65 squares, which is 65%. Percent means out of 100.; Q2: No. $100 → $90 → $81. 20% off $100 would be $80. The second 10% is taken from $90, not from $100. Minutes 0 to 3. SNEAK-PEEK PUZZLES. Puzzle 1 is one that any child can try with objects, drawing or counting, so everybody can start well. Puzzle 2 is a thinking puzzle. Then ask for VOLUNTEERS to say how they thought (put a hand up or use a reaction); never choose a child because you saw a wrong answer. Never record scores. Choosing a start: every child has the same neutral line on workbook page 1: "After question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder." Invite the WHOLE group, never one child: "when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation." Never single out a child by name and never announce who is on which page. Pairs are not split unless BOTH children agree. Any child may swap at any time.
