B1. A price tag says $109, GST included. GST is 9%. Ali says the GST is 9% of $109, which is $9.81. Is he right? How much GST is in the price?

B2. An $80 lamp is on sale in two shops. Shop P takes 25% off. Shop Q takes 10% off, and then a further 15% off the new price. In which shop is the lamp cheaper, and by how much?

B3. Mrs Tan spent 40% of her money on groceries and 25% of the remaining money on a gift. She had $54 left. How much money did she have at first?

Make up your own. Make up a price that goes up by a percentage and then down by the same percentage. Predict the final price, then test it. Think for 30 seconds before anyone speaks.

These are the printed Go further questions B1 to B3 (the same as the workbook). Answers: B1) No. $109 is 109% of the price before GST, so 1% is $1 and the GST is $9. (9% of $109 is wrong because GST is 9% of the price BEFORE GST.) B2) Shop P, by $1.20. P: $80 − $20 = $60. Q: $80 − $8 = $72, then 15% of $72 = $10.80 off, $61.20. B3) $120. Work backwards: $54 is 75% of the remainder, so the remainder is $72. $72 is 60% of her money, so 10% is $12 and 100% is $120.
