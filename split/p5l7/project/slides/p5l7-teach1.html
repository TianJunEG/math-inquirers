Build any percentage from 10%, 5% and 1%

10%

divide by 10

5%

half of 10%

1%

divide by 100

10% of $80 = $8

5% of $80 = $4

1% of $80 = $0.80

Percent means "out of 100", so 1% is one of the 100 equal parts.

Math Inquirers Level 5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Point to the bar: "100% is the whole thing. Cut it into 10 equal parts: each part is 10%." Then the three tools. Click to show each example with $80 (a different amount from the worksheet): 10% of $80 = $8; 5% is half of that, $4; 1% is $80 ÷ 100 = $0.80. Ask: "How can we make 15% from these?" (10% + 5% = $8 + $4 = $12.) "35%?" (3 × 10% + 5%.) Think-aloud: "I never learn a new rule for each percentage. I build it from 10%, 5% and 1%." Money check: 1% of $80 is 80 cents, so write $0.80, not $0.08.
