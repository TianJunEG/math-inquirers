Percentage of the REMAINDER

Ali had $80. He spent 25% of it. Then he spent 40% of the money LEFT. How much is left at the end? What percentage of $80 is that?

Step 1. 25% of $80 = $20 spent. Left: $60. The NEW whole is $60.

Step 2. 40% of $60: 10% = $6, so 40% = $24. Left: $60 − $24 = $36.

Step 3. $36 out of $80 = 45 out of 100. Do NOT add 25% and 40%!

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4 has a remainder). Draw the bar of $80 in 4 equal parts: each part is $20, which is 25%. Click 1: one part spent, $60 left, and that $60 is the NEW whole. Click 2: the 40% is of $60, not of $80. 10% of $60 is $6, so 40% is $24. $36 is left. Click 3: the trap. 25% + 40% = 65% suggests 35% is left, which would be $28. Wrong. The real fraction left is $36 out of $80: 36 ÷ 80 = 0.45, so 45%. Ask: "Why can we not add 25% and 40%?" (they are percentages of different wholes: $80 and $60). Check: spent $20 + $24 = $44, which is 55% of $80, and 55% + 45% = 100%.
