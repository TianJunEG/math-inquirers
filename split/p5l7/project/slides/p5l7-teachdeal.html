Groups in a class: make a table

50 pupils. 40% are boys. 30% of the boys and 10% of the girls have a bicycle. What percentage of the class have a bicycle?

Step 1. Boys: 40% of 50 = 20. Girls: 50 − 20 = 30.

Step 2. Bicycles: 30% of 20 = 6, 10% of 30 = 3, 6 + 3 = 9.

9 out of 50 = 18 out of 100 = 18%

Use this after worksheet page 2, for children who go on to the Think harder page (A2 compares two deals for 4 boxes). Ask first: "Which sounds better, a free pen or 30% off?" Let children vote, then work out both. Deal A: pay for 2 pens, $20. Deal B: $30 less 30% ($9) is $21. So the free pen wins here, by $1. Step 2: a free pen is $10 off $30, which is one third: about 33% off. On the worksheet the numbers are different and the answer is different: do not assume "buy and get free" always wins.
