Which deal is better? Work out the real price

Pens cost $10 each. Deal A: buy 2, get 1 free. Deal B: 30% off every pen. You want 3 pens.

Deal A: buy 2, get 1 free

Pay for 2 pens: 2 × $10 = $20

Deal B: 30% off

3 pens = $30. 30% of $30 = $9 off. Pay $21

Step 1. Compare the two prices: $20 and $21. Deal A is cheaper, by $1.

Step 2. Deal A is really how many percent off? $10 free out of $30 = about 33% off.

Work out the real price for each deal, then compare. Do not just compare the numbers on the signs!

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q7 compares two deals for 4 boxes). Ask first: "Which sounds better, a free pen or 30% off?" Let children vote, then work out both. Deal A: pay for 2 pens, $20. Deal B: $30 less 30% ($9) is $21. So the free pen wins here, by $1. Step 2: a free pen is $10 off $30, which is one third: about 33% off. On the worksheet the numbers are different and the answer is different: do not assume "buy and get free" always wins.
