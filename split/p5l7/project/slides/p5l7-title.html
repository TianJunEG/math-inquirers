Percentage Puzzles

Percentage: discounts, GST, increase and decrease

TODAY WE WILL

Build any percentage from 10%, 5% and 1%, work out discounts and GST, and catch the percentage traps.

TODAY'S TOOL

Draw it

Focus habit: Wonder

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 2 (opening, inside the 90-minute clock). Objective: Find any percentage of an amount by building it from 10%, 5% and 1%; work out a discount and then 9% GST on the sale price; find a percentage of a remainder; spot the classic percentage traps. A puzzle that goes further (Think harder page only): find a percentage increase or decrease by comparing the change with the ORIGINAL amount. Thinking strategies taught today: ASK "PERCENTAGE OF WHAT?", BUILD FROM 10%, 5% AND 1%, and DRAW A BAR MODEL. GST in Singapore is 9%. Materials: mini whiteboard and marker, workbook, pencil, a hundred-square page or squared paper. A calculator only for checking at the end of each page. Online: children keep the workbook and a whiteboard in front of the camera. The tutor screen-shares the slides and draws bar models with the annotation tool. Time plan (the exact minutes of this lesson; every lesson adds up to 90): opening 0 to 2, sneak-peek puzzles 2 to 6.5 (one check for both puzzles, no volunteer round, nobody is marked), warm-up 6.5 to 12 (one game round, four quick questions), explore 12 to 24.5 (pair work 6 minutes at most, then two questions together; a Go further what-if is on the explore slide for any pair that finishes), teaching 24.5 to 35.5 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35.5 to 40, workbook page 1 40 to 54 (the questions without a star; after question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder), check together 54 to 57.5, short teaching before page 2 57.5 to 59, workbook page 2 59 to 70.5 (alone: start with the questions named at the top, then choose; starred parts are take-home; nobody has to finish), check together 70.5 to 73.5, talk time 73.5 to 79 (you may say pass; volunteers first; the first step of the Investigation is take-home), exit ticket 79 to 84.5, reflection 84.5 to 87 (pass is always OK), stamp typed in the chat and goodbye 87 to 88, and the buffer 88 to 90 (floating time: spend it first if you run late, never fill it on purpose). Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child and have no clock time. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and never splits a pair unless both children agree. Invite the WHOLE group, never one child: "when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation." SPINE: Today's tool is Draw it. Sketch and label a model; let the picture show what stays the same. Look for where you use it. Point at the card on page 1 of the workbook. HABIT: Focus habit: Wonder. Invite a question or a what-if before any answer is given. Say: "What could we ask? What if we changed one thing?" Do not give the answer first: ask the prompt, wait, and give a hint only if the child is stuck.
