Percentage Puzzles

Percentage: discounts, GST, increase and decrease

TODAY WE WILL

Build any percentage from 10%, 5% and 1%, work out discounts and GST, and catch the percentage traps.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find any percentage of an amount by building it from 10%, 5% and 1%; work out a discount and then 9% GST on the sale price; find a percentage of a remainder; spot the classic percentage traps. Preview of P6 (Think Harder page only): find a percentage increase or decrease by comparing the change with the ORIGINAL amount. Thinking strategies taught today: ASK "PERCENTAGE OF WHAT?", BUILD FROM 10%, 5% AND 1%, and DRAW A BAR MODEL. GST in Singapore is 9%. Materials: mini whiteboard and marker, workbook, pencil, a hundred-square page or squared paper. A calculator only for checking at the end of each page. Online: children keep the workbook and a whiteboard in front of the camera. The tutor screen-shares the slides and draws bar models with the annotation tool.
