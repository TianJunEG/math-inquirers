LESSON 7 • WEEK 4A

Percentage Puzzles

Percentage: discounts, GST, increase and decrease

TODAY WE WILL

Build any percentage from 10%, 5% and 1%, work out discounts and GST, and catch the percentage traps.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find any percentage of an amount by building it from 10%, 5% and 1%; work out a discount and then 9% GST on the sale price; find a percentage of a remainder; find a percentage increase or decrease by comparing the change with the ORIGINAL amount; spot the classic percentage traps. Thinking strategies taught today: ASK "PERCENTAGE OF WHAT?", BUILD FROM 10%, 5% AND 1%, and DRAW A BAR MODEL. GST in Singapore is 9%. Materials: mini whiteboard and marker, workbook, pencil, a hundred-square page or squared paper. A calculator only for checking at the end of each page. Online: children keep the workbook and a whiteboard in front of the camera. The tutor screen-shares the slides and draws bar models with the annotation tool. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before page 2 and a check-together slide after each page, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
