Discount first, then GST

A fan costs $120 before GST. It is sold at 15% off. Then 9% GST is added to the SALE price. What do you pay?

Step 1. 15% of $120 = $12 + $6 = $18. Sale price = $120 − $18 = $102.

Step 2. GST is 9% of the SALE price $102. 10% = $10.20, 1% = $1.02. 9% = $10.20 − $1.02 = $9.18.

Step 3. Pay = $102 + $9.18 = $111.18.

GST = $9.18. You pay $111.18.
