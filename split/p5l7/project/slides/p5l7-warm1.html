Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Hundred-Square Snap. Show the three squares one at a time (cover the other two with your hand or paper online). Children write on whiteboards: the percentage shaded and the percentage NOT shaded, then hold them up together. Click to show the answers. Square 1: 30 shaded (3 full rows) so 30% shaded, 70% not shaded. Square 2: 45 shaded (4 rows and 5 more) so 45% and 55%. Square 3: 64 shaded (6 rows and 4 more) so 64% and 36%. Ask: "Who found the not-shaded part without counting? How?" (100 take away the shaded part.) "Why is the whole 100?" (percent means out of 100). Listen for: children counting every small square. Praise rows of 10: each row is 10%.
