Key idea: percentage of WHAT?

Build any percentage from 10%, 5% and 1%. Before every step, ask: percentage of what?

Take the discount off first, then work out GST on the sale price. After a change, the new amount is the new whole.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 34 to 35.5. Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Adding percentages of different wholes: 20% of the money and then 25% of the REMAINDER is not 45% of the money. (2) Thinking that up 25% and then down 25% brings the price back to the start. (3) Working out a percentage increase or decrease using the new amount instead of the ORIGINAL amount, or working out GST on the price before the discount.
