Percentage change: compare with the ORIGINAL

A ticket costs $15. The price DOUBLES to $30. Then it goes back from $30 to $15. Find each percentage change.

Step 1. Up: the change is $15. The ORIGINAL price is $15. $15 out of $15 = 100% increase.

Step 2. Down: the change is $15. The ORIGINAL price is now $30. $15 out of $30 = 50% decrease.

Same $15 change, different percentages: each is compared with the amount you START from.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use this after worksheet page 2, for children who go on to the Think harder page (A1), or at home. Percentage change goes beyond the page-1 and page-2 work, so say so: it is a puzzle that goes further, and the Percentage change = change out of the amount you START with line is all a child needs. Draw the two bars: $15 and $30. Click 1: the rise is $15, the starting price is $15, so $15 out of $15 is the whole thing again: 100% increase (doubling is a 100% increase, not 200%). Click 2: the fall is $15, but now the starting price is $30. $15 out of $30 is half: 50%. Say in words: "The percentage change is the change out of the STARTING amount." Ask: "Why are the two percentages not the same, when both changes are $15?" (the starting amount is different each time).
