Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) 25% is a quarter: $20 off, $60. b) 10% of $200 = $20, 1% = $2, so 9% = $18. c) 10% of $50 = $5 off, sale price $45. GST: 10% of $45 = $4.50, 1% = $0.45, so 9% = $4.05. Pay $45 + $4.05 = $49.05. Watch for children who work out GST on $50 instead of $45.
