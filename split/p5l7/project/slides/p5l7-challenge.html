Challenge

1. A price tag says $109, GST included. Ali says the GST is 9% of $109, which is $9.81. Is he right? How much GST is in the price?

2. Mrs Tan spent 40% of her money on groceries and 25% of the remaining money on a gift. She had $54 left. How much money did she have at first?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. These are Think Harder Q5 and Q7 (Q6, the two lamp shops, is only in the workbook). Answers: 1) No. $109 is 109% of the price before GST, so 1% = $1 and the price before GST was $100. The GST is $9, not $9.81. 2) $120. Work backwards: $54 is 75% of the money left after groceries, so that was $72; $72 is 60% of her money, so 10% is $12 and 100% is $120.
