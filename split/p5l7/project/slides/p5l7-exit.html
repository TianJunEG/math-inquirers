A $30 toy is sold at 20% off. Then 9% GST is added to the sale price. How much do you pay?

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 82 to 90. Answer: $26.16. 20% of $30 = $6, sale price $24. 9% of $24: 10% = $2.40, 1% = $0.24, so 9% = $2.16. $24 + $2.16 = $26.16. Extension: a shop raises every price by 25%, then has a "20% off everything" sale. Is the sale price more than, less than or the same as the old price? Try $40, $100 and $64. (The same: up 25% then down 20% always gives back the old price. $40 to $50 to $40; $100 to $125 to $100; $64 to $80 to $64. Siti on the worksheet fell for the opposite trap: it is NOT the same as up 5%.)
