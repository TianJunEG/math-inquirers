1. A $90 jacket is 10% off. Then the sale price is a further 10% off. What do you pay? Is it the same as 20% off?

2. A jar has 60 sweets. 25% are red. 40% of the REST are green. How many are green?

3. A $80 bag goes up by 50%, then down by 50%. What is the final price?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) $72.90. 10% of $90 = $9 off, so $81; then 10% of $81 = $8.10 off, so $72.90. It is NOT the same as 20% off ($72), because the second 10% is of $81, not of $90. 2) 18 green. Red: 25% of 60 = 15. Rest: 45. 40% of 45 = 18. 3) $60. Up 50%: $80 + $40 = $120. Down 50% of $120 = $60 off, so $60.
