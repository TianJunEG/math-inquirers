A1. At a hawker centre, a plate of chicken rice went up from $4 to $5. A month later, it went back down from $5 to $4. (a) Find the percentage increase from $4 to $5. (b) Find the percentage decrease from $5 to $4. (c) Both changes are $1. Why are the two percentages different? Hint: percentage change = the change out of the amount you START with.

A2. A box of cereal costs $15 in two shops. Shop X: "Buy 3, get 1 free!" Shop Y: "30% off every box!" Mei buys 4 boxes. (a) How much does she pay at each shop? Which is the better buy, and by how much? (b) Shop X's deal is really how many percent off?

A3. Siti says, "15% of $80 is $8 + $4 = $12." Explain her method. Use it to find 15% of $46.

Answers: A1) (a) 25% ($1 out of $4) (b) 20% ($1 out of $5) (c) Each change is compared with its own starting amount: $1 out of $4 is a bigger part than $1 out of $5. (This is a puzzle that goes further than the lesson.) A2) (a) Shop X $45 (she pays for 3 boxes); Shop Y $42 (30% of $60 = $18 off). Shop Y is the better buy, by $3. (b) 25% off (1 box free out of 4, $15 off $60) A3) She found 10% ($8) and 5% (half of 10%, $4) and added them. 15% of $46 = $4.60 + $2.30 = $6.90. A4) Saved $30 (10% = $25, 2% = $5); sale price $220.
