Worked example: 45% and 98% of $60

Find 45% of $60 and 98% of $60. Build them from 10%, 5% and 1%.

Step 1. 10% of $60 = $6 (÷ 10). 5% = half of $6 = $3.

Step 2. 45% = 4 × 10% + 5% = $24 + $3 = $27.

Step 3. 98% is almost all of it. Find what is missing: 100% − 98% = 2%. 1% = $0.60, so 2% = $1.20.

Step 4. 98% = $60 − $1.20 = $58.80.

45% of $60 = $27. 98% of $60 = $58.80.

Math Inquirers Level 5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click to reveal each step and let children say the step first. Think-aloud: "10% is one tenth: $6. 5% is half of 10%: $3. 45% is four 10%s and one 5%: four sixes are 24, add 3: $27." Then: "For 98% I could add lots of pieces, but 98% is only 2% away from the whole. I take the 2% off: 1% is 60 cents, so 2% is $1.20. $60 take away $1.20 is $58.80." Check: 45% + 55% should make $60: 55% of $60 is $33, and $27 + $33 = $60. Ask: "When is take-away the clever way?" (when the percentage is close to 100%).
