Groups in a class: make a table

50 pupils. 40% are boys. 30% of the boys and 10% of the girls have a bicycle. What percentage of the class have a bicycle?

Step 1. Boys: 40% of 50 = 20. Girls: 50 − 20 = 30.

Step 2. Bicycles: 30% of 20 = 6, 10% of 30 = 3, 6 + 3 = 9.

9 out of 50 = 18 out of 100 = 18%

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6 has a table for a class). Fill the table row by row, click by click. Boys: 40% of 50: 10% is 5, so 40% is 20. Girls: the rest, 30. Bicycles: boys 30% of 20 = 6 (10% is 2, so 30% is 6); girls 10% of 30 = 3. Whole class: 6 + 3 = 9. Tip for the table: fill in the easy parts first. Last step: 9 out of 50. To turn it into a percentage: multiply top and bottom by 2 to get 18 out of 100. Ask: "Why can we not just add 30% and 10%?" (they are percentages of different groups: 20 boys and 30 girls).
