Whiteboards up together, then click. a) 6 × 4 first: 5 + 24 = 29. b) Brackets first: 11 × 4 = 44. Same numbers as a, different answer! c) Only ÷ and ×, so left to right: 12 × 2 = 24 (not 48 ÷ 8 = 6). d) Only −, so left to right: 20 − 5 = 15 (not 30 − 5 = 25).
