52–53.5 MIN

Bar model: 4 times as many

Ali has 4 times as many stickers as Ben. They have 1 345 stickers altogether. How many stickers does Ali have?

Step 1. Ali has 4 units and Ben has 1 unit: 5 units altogether.

Step 2. 5 units = 1 345, so 1 unit = 1 345 ÷ 5 = 269.

Step 3. Ali: 4 units = 269 × 4 = 1 076. Check: 1 076 + 269 = 1 345 ✓

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6). Strategy: DRAW A BAR MODEL. Draw Ben's bar first (1 unit), then Ali's bar with 4 units the same size. The brace shows the total. Ask: "How many units altogether? What is one unit worth?" Show the division on the board: 1 345 ÷ 5 = 269 (13 hundreds ÷ 5 = 2 hundreds r 3 hundreds; 34 tens ÷ 5 = 6 tens r 4 tens; 45 ÷ 5 = 9). Extra question: "How many MORE stickers does Ali have?" (3 units = 807).
