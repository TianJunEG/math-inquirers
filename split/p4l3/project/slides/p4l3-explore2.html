1. How many different answers did you find?

2. Where do the brackets go to make the biggest answer? The smallest?

3. Some brackets do not change the answer at all. Which ones? Why?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Let pairs keep working, then gather the answers on the board. Answers: 1) Only 3 different answers: 16 (no brackets), 28 and 10. 2) Biggest: (6 + 4) × 3 − 2 = 10 × 3 − 2 = 28. Smallest: 6 + 4 × (3 − 2) = 6 + 4 × 1 = 10. 3) Brackets round 4 × 3, round 6 + 4 × 3, or round 4 × 3 − 2 all give 16, because the × is done first anyway.
