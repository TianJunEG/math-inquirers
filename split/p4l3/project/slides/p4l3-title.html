Operation Detectives

Four operations

TODAY WE WILL

Use the order of operations, find missing signs and brackets to hit a target, and work backwards through number chains.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Use the order of operations (brackets first, then × and ÷ from left to right, then + and − from left to right), find missing signs and brackets to hit a target, work backwards through a chain of operations, and solve "times as many" problems with a bar model. Thinking strategies taught today: ESTIMATE, TEST AND ADJUST (guess and check), WORK BACKWARDS and DRAW A BAR MODEL. Materials: mini whiteboards and markers; paper number cards 0 to 9; sign cards +, −, ×, ÷ and two pairs of bracket cards per child; scrap paper for bar models. Online: children write on whiteboards and hold them up to the camera; before the lesson they cut out the sign and bracket cards so they can move them on camera in the explore task. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
