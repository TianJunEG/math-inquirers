Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. Guesses and "I do not know" are welcome. Nobody is marked.

Write your answer on your own paper. When your tutor says, hold it to the camera. Only your tutor looks.

1

Try it

Ali has 2 bags with 4 sweets in each bag, and 3 loose sweets. Draw it. How many sweets has he got? Write a number sentence that matches your drawing.

11 sweets: 2 × 4 + 3 = 11.

2

Think

Put ONE pair of brackets in 2 + 3 × 4 so that the answer is 20.

(2 + 3) × 4 = 20. Without brackets it is 14.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 1.5 to 6.5 (5 minutes). SNEAK-PEEK PUZZLES. Say: "These are ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome, drawing is welcome and so is saying I do not know. Nobody is marked, and nobody's answer is shown to the group." Puzzle 1 is a TRY IT puzzle: any child can start it with objects, a drawing or counting, so everyone has a first win. Puzzle 2 needs a little thinking. Give about 60 seconds for puzzle 1 and about 90 seconds for puzzle 2. Children show their answers PRIVATELY: written on their own paper and held to the camera at the same moment for everyone (or shown only to you on camera). Make ONE hold-up moment for both papers, after puzzle 2. Never use private chat or DMs. Never ask for boards up in front of the group, never read out whose answer is whose, and never say who got it right. Then click to show each answer and talk it through for everyone ("some of you tried ..."), naming no child. Nobody is called on by name, and passing is always fine. ANSWERS: 1) 11 sweets: 2 × 4 + 3 = 8 + 3 = 11. The bags are counted first (×), then the loose sweets are added (+). 2) (2 + 3) × 4 = 5 × 4 = 20. With no brackets the answer is 14, because 3 × 4 is done first. A GOOD REASON: A story helps: the bags are counted first, then the loose ones are added. Brackets change which part is done first. Use what you see only to plan your help. It does not decide who is on which page and it is not recorded as a mark. ON PAGE 1 (printed): "Pairs: Q1 and Q2. Then Q3 alone. After question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder." Make the invitation to the WHOLE group ("when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation"), so nobody is singled out. Never message a child privately, never name a child, and never announce who is on which page. Everyone stays in the same room and every child turns to the page they choose. If a pair would be split, ask both children first; never leave a child alone on page 1 (join them to another pair or to yourself).
