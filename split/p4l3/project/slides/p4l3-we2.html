Worked example: brackets to hit a target

Put in ONE pair of brackets to make 29: 2 + 3 × 4 + 5

Step 1. No brackets: 2 + 12 + 5 = 19. Too small: we need to multiply a bigger number.

Step 2. Try (2 + 3) × 4 + 5 = 5 × 4 + 5 = 25. Closer, but not 29.

Step 3. Try 2 + 3 × (4 + 5) = 2 + 3 × 9 = 2 + 27 = 29 ✓

2 + 3 × (4 + 5) = 29

Brackets round 3 × 4 change nothing: × is done first anyway.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Strategy: test and adjust. Ask first: "Without brackets, what is the answer? Is 29 more or less?" Think-aloud: "19 is too small. Brackets let me add BEFORE I multiply, which makes the multiplication bigger. Round 2 + 3 gives 25. Round 4 + 5 gives 29. Found it." Then ask: "Which brackets would make the biggest answer of all?" ((2 + 3) × (4 + 5) = 45 with two pairs; with one pair, 29.)
