Explore: where do the brackets go?

EXPLORE

  1. Write 6 + 4 × 3 − 2 on your whiteboard and work it out.
  2. Put ONE pair of brackets somewhere. Work it out again.
  3. Move the brackets to other places. Write every answer.
  4. How many different answers? Which is the biggest? The smallest?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8 to 13. Pairs work with number, sign and bracket cards (online: paper cards on camera, or write on whiteboards). Ask them to predict first: how many different answers? Many children guess 5 or 6, one for each place. Let them try any placement; the brackets must go round at least two numbers. Early finishers: can TWO pairs of brackets make a new answer? ((6 + 4) × (3 − 2) = 10, the same as one of the others.)
