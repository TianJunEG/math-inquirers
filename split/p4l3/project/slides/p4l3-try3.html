Your turn: one pair of brackets

10 − 2 × 3 + 1 = 5 with no brackets. Where do the brackets go?

a 10 − 2 × 3 + 1 = 25

(10 − 2) × 3 + 1

b 10 − 2 × 3 + 1 = 2

10 − 2 × (3 + 1)

c 10 − 2 × 3 + 1 = 3

10 − (2 × 3 + 1)

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) 8 × 3 + 1 = 25. b) 10 − 2 × 4 = 10 − 8 = 2. c) This one is tricky: 10 − 7 = 3, because the brackets make us take away the whole 7. Ask: "In c, why is the answer smaller than with no brackets?" (we take away the 1 as well instead of adding it).
