Ben says 20 − 8 + 2 = 10 because you add first. Mei says the answer is 14. Who is right? Why?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 70 to 80. Pairs talk for 2 minutes, then share. Answer: Mei is right. With only + and − we work from left to right: 20 − 8 = 12, then 12 + 2 = 14. Ben worked out 8 + 2 first; to do that we would need brackets: 20 − (8 + 2) = 10. Second question if time: "How did you know which sign to try first in the missing-sign puzzles?" (estimate: a big target needs ×, a small one may need ÷ or −). Praise the reasons, not only the answers.
