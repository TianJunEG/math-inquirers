Q1: start with all +

All +: 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 = 21. That is 14 too many.

A − instead of + takes the number away TWICE: from +2 to −2 is 4 less.

So the numbers we flip must add up to 7: 2 + 5 or 3 + 4.

1 − 2 + 3 + 4 − 5 + 6 = 7 and 1 + 2 − 3 − 4 + 5 + 6 = 7 ✓

$24 is the half left after the book: $24 × 2 = $48

Check: 48 → 24 → 18 → 9 ✓ She had $48.
