Order of operations

The order

1. Brackets first

2. × and ÷, from left to right

3. + and −, from left to right

40 − 8 × 3 + 6 ÷ 2

Step 1. No brackets. × and ÷ first: 8 × 3 = 24, 6 ÷ 2 = 3.

Step 2. Now 40 − 24 + 3. Go from left to right: 40 − 24 = 16.

Step 3. 16 + 3 = 19.

40 − 8 × 3 + 6 ÷ 2 = 19

Not this one: 40 − 8 = 32, then 32 × 3 = 96 … Going only from left to right gives the wrong answer.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Read the order together. Children work out the example on whiteboards first, then click through the steps. Think-aloud: "No brackets, so I look for × and ÷ first: 8 × 3 is 24 and 6 ÷ 2 is 3. Now I only have + and −, so I go from left to right: 40 − 24 is 16, and 16 + 3 is 19." The last click shows the common mistake: doing everything from left to right. Ask: "Which part did this child do first? Why is that wrong?"
