Estimate first. Then test. Each one has only one answer.

9 × 3 − 2

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) 6 + 3 × 2 = 6 + 6 = 12. Children who write 6 × 3 − 2 get 16: test it! Many children try (6 + 3) × 2 in their heads: that is 18, and there are no brackets. b) 36 is much bigger than 6, so try ÷: 36 ÷ 4 = 9, then 9 − 3 = 6. c) 25 is bigger than all three numbers, so try ×: 9 × 3 = 27, 27 − 2 = 25.
