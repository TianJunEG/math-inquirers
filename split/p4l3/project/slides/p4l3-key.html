Key idea: order matters

Brackets first. Then × and ÷, then + and −, each from left to right.

To hit a target: estimate first, test, then adjust. A big target usually needs a ×.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Working strictly from left to right and ignoring the order: 40 − 8 × 3 done as 32 × 3. (2) Thinking addition always comes before subtraction (or × before ÷) instead of working left to right: 20 − 8 + 2 answered 10, 72 ÷ 8 × 3 answered 3. (3) Working backwards but using the same operation instead of the opposite one (for − 18, taking away 18 again instead of adding 18).
