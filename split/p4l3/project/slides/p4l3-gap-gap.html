Optional, if you finish early (no clock time): show it while some pairs are working or have finished, or use it live as an extra warm-up or tutor-bank question; never at a fixed minute, and it never takes time from the plan. ADDED ITEM gap (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Gaps that grow"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Make a table. SAY: Make a table with three columns: the day, the number of coins, and the gap from the day before. ASK: What is the gap on Day 2? On Day 3? What will the gap be on Day 6? LISTEN FOR: Gaps 2, 3, 4, 5, then 6, 7, 8, 9, 10. Listen for "the gap goes up by 1" and a table that keeps going without skipping a row. Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) 23 coins. (b) 57 coins. WHY: Gaps are 2, 3, 4, 5, so Day 6 adds 6: 23. Then +7 = 30, +8 = 38, +9 = 47, +10 = 57 on Day 10. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): Mei folds paper stars. Day 1: 4, Day 2: 6, Day 3: 9, Day 4: 13. The number added goes up by 1 each day. How many on Day 8? Answer: 39 (the days go 4, 6, 9, 13, 18, 24, 31, 39).
