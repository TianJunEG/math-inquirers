1. Work out 7 × (40 − 4 × 8).

2. 20 __ 4 __ 3 = 8. Write +, −, × or ÷ in the gaps. There are TWO ways. Find both.

3. I think of a number. I divide it by 3, then add 14. The answer is 50. What was my number?

Math Inquirers Level 4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 56 (inside the brackets, × first: 4 × 8 = 32, 40 − 32 = 8; then 7 × 8 = 56). 2) 20 − 4 × 3 = 20 − 12 = 8, and 20 ÷ 4 + 3 = 5 + 3 = 8. 3) 108 (work backwards: 50 − 14 = 36, 36 × 3 = 108).
