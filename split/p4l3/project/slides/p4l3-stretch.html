Think harder

1. There are 2 340 red and blue beads. There are 4 times as many blue beads as red beads. How many red beads are there?

2. Put ONE pair of brackets in 3 + 5 × 6 − 2 to make the answer 46.

3. Write +, −, × or ÷ in each gap to make this true: 12 __ 4 __ 5 = 15

4. Think of a number. Multiply it by 5. Add 35. Divide by 5. Take away the number you started with. The answer is always 7. Explain why it always works. A bar model can help.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers (same four questions as the printed Think harder page): 1) 468 (5 units = 2 340, 1 unit = 468). 2) (3 + 5) × 6 − 2 = 8 × 6 − 2 = 46. 3) 12 ÷ 4 × 5 = 15 (the only way). 4) Multiplying by 5 and then dividing by 5 gets back to your number. The 35 divided by 5 adds 7. Taking away your number leaves the 7. So the answer is always 7.
