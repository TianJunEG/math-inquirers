Worked example: missing signs

Write +, −, × or ÷ in each circle to make the number sentence true.

Step 1. 37 is big. Adding all three makes only 16, so we need a ×.

Step 2. Try 8 × 5 = 40. Then 40 and 3 make 37: 40 − 3 = 37 ✓

Step 3. Check another way: 8 + 5 × 3 = 8 + 15 = 23. Not this one.

8 × 5 − 3 = 37

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Strategy: ESTIMATE, TEST, ADJUST. Ask first: "Is 37 big or small compared with 8, 5 and 3? What does that tell us?" Click through: step 1, step 2, the signs appear in the circles, step 3, the answer. Think-aloud: "If I add everything I only get 16, so I need a times. 8 × 5 is 40, that is just 3 too many, so take away 3." Remind them to CHECK in the right order: 8 × 5 − 3 means 40 − 3, not 8 × 2.
