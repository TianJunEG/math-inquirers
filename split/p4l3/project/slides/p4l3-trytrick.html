Your turn: a number trick

Think of a number. Double it. Add 10. Halve it. Take away the number you started with.

a Start with 4.

4 → 8 → 18 → 9 → 5

b Start with 9.

9 → 18 → 28 → 14 → 5

c Why is it always 5?

Double, then halve: back to your number. Half of 10 is 5 more.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5 is a different trick). Everyone picks their own secret number and follows the steps on a whiteboard. On "Show me!", every board says 5. Ask: "How did that happen?" Draw it as a bar: your number is one bar; double it gives two bars; add 10; halve it gives one bar and 5; take away your bar and 5 is left. Try a big number to test it (100: 200, 210, 105, 5).
