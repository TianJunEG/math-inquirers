Brain break

Odd or even? Stand up if the answer is even, crouch down if it is odd: 3 + 4 × 2, (3 + 4) × 2, 20 ÷ 4 + 1, 20 ÷ (4 + 1).

Then: a drink of water and back to our seats.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Five minutes. Read each sentence, count down from 3, everyone moves. Answers: 3 + 4 × 2 = 11 (odd, crouch); (3 + 4) × 2 = 14 (even, stand); 20 ÷ 4 + 1 = 6 (even, stand); 20 ÷ (4 + 1) = 4 (even, stand). Online: children stand up and crouch on camera.
