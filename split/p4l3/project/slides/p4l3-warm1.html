Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 4 to 6 (one short round, about 2 minutes: the Warm-up check came first). Target Numbers. Children copy the four cards 2, 4, 5 and 9 onto their whiteboards. Point to one target at a time: "Make 13. Go!" The first child to show a correct number sentence wins a point; then ask for a SECOND way. Click to show one way for each target (clicks reveal 13, 22, 36, 81 in order). Other ways: 13 = 2 × 9 − 5; 22 = 2 × 9 + 4; 81 needs brackets: (4 + 5) × 9. Listen for children who write 2 + 4 × 5 and say 30: ask them to say which part they did first. That question leads straight into today's lesson. Finisher: "Can you make 100?" (Let them try; it cannot be done with these cards and signs, which is a good thing to notice.)
