Step 1. Where the lines cross, the values are the same: Week 4, 16 cm each.

Step 2. The gap between the lines is the difference. Week 1: 10 − 4 = 6 cm.

Step 3. The steeper line grows faster: P grows 4 cm a week, Q grows 2 cm.

Week 5: how much taller is P?
