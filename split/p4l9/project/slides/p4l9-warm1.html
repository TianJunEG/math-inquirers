Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Which graph tells the story? The graphs have no numbers on purpose: we read the SHAPE. Read story 1 aloud. Children point at (or write the letter of) the matching graph, then say why before you click. Clicks reveal the answers in order. Answers: 1) Graph C: the water goes up steadily. 2) Graph A: people arrive for lunch, then leave. 3) Graph D: the candle gets shorter steadily. 4) Graph B: nothing changes, so the line is flat. Ask: "Could the hawker centre graph be another shape on a rainy day?" "Make up a story for a line that goes DOWN, then UP." (e.g. water in a bathtub as it drains and is filled again). Listen for "steep" and "flat": we use those words all lesson.
