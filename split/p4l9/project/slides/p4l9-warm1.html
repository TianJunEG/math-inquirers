Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6.5 to 8.5 (one short round, about 90 seconds: the Sneak-peek puzzles came first). Which graph tells the story? The graphs have no numbers on purpose: we read the SHAPE. Read story 1 aloud. Children point at (or write the letter of) the matching graph, then say why before you click. Clicks reveal the answers in order. Answers: 1) Graph C: the water goes up steadily. 2) Graph A: people arrive for lunch, then leave. 3) Graph D: the candle gets shorter steadily. 4) Graph B: nothing changes, so the line is flat. Listen for "steep" and "flat": we use those words all lesson.
