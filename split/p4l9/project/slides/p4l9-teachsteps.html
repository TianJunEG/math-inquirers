Same jump each time: fill the gaps

Ben swims the same number of laps more each day. Day 1: 8 laps. Day 4: 20 laps.

Step 1. Day 1 to Day 4 is 3 jumps, not 4.

Step 2. 20 − 8 = 12. 12 ÷ 3 = 4 laps each jump.

Step 3. Day 2: 12, Day 3: 16, Day 5: 24.

Laps in the 5 days?

80

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4, Siti's pages). Ask first: "How many jumps from Day 1 to Day 4?" Many children say 4. Count the gaps on the graph: 1 to 2, 2 to 3, 3 to 4: 3 jumps. Click the steps, then the full line appears. Check: 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 all go up by 4. Total: 8 + 12 + 16 + 20 + 24 = 80 (or 5 days × the middle day, 16).
