Reading a line graph

Step 1. Read the title and the axes: books borrowed, Monday to Friday.

Step 2. Find the scale. Each numbered line is worth 10. The faint lines are 5.

Step 3. Tuesday: up to the point, across to the side: 40. Monday is halfway between 20 and 30: 25.

Always find what one line is worth first.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Click through the steps, then the red and teal reading lines. Think-aloud: "First the title and the axes, so I know what is counted and when. Then the scale: 0, 10, 20 on the numbered lines, so each numbered line is worth 10, and the faint line between is 5. Tuesday's point sits on the 40 line. Monday's point is halfway between 20 and 30: 25." Ask children to read Wednesday (35), Thursday (60) and Friday (50) on whiteboards. Common mistake: reading each line as 1.
