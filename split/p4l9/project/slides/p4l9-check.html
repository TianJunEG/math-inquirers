1. A line graph has gridlines at 0, 50, 100, 150 and 200. A point is exactly halfway between the 100 line and the 150 line. What number does it show?

125. Halfway between 100 and 150 is 100 + 25.

2. Shop A's line graph goes from 40 to 60 and looks very steep. Shop B's graph also goes from 40 to 60 but looks almost flat. Give a reason why this can happen.

The scales are different. For example, A's graph may start at 40 and have big gaps for each 5, while B's starts at 0 with small gaps. The same numbers can look different, so read the scale first.

A GOOD REASON: Read the scale before you read a point. The same numbers can look steep or flat if the scale changes.
