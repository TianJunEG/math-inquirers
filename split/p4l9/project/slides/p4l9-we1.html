Worked example: the biggest change

Mon → Tue: up 15 (40 − 25)

Tue → Wed: down 5 (40 − 35)

Wed → Thu: up 25 (60 − 35)

Thu → Fri: down 10 (60 − 50)

Biggest increase: Wed → Thu, up 25

The steeper the line, the bigger the change. Up or down, say which!
