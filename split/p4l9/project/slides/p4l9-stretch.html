Stretch

1. Week 1: 48, Week 2: 56, Week 3: 51, Week 4: 66. Between which two weeks was the biggest change?

2. Each line on a scale is worth 5. A point is 7 lines above 0. Another point is 4 lines higher. What do they show?

3. Sales for 4 days add up to 200. Each day is 6 more than the day before. What were the sales on Day 1?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) Week 3 to Week 4: up 15 (the other changes are up 8 and down 5). 2) 35 and 55. 3) 41 (41, 47, 53, 59: take away the extras 6 + 12 + 18 = 36, then 164 ÷ 4 = 41).
