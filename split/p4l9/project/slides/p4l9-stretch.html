Think harder

1. A table shows that Class 4A has 28 boys and 6 more girls than boys. Class 4B has 63 pupils, with 5 more girls than boys. Which class has more girls?

2. A graph's scale starts at 100 and each line is worth 25. Ali's point is 3 lines above the start and Mei's point is 7 lines above the start. How much more is Mei's number than Ali's? Mei says hers is more than double Ali's. Is she right?

3. A stall's line graph starts on Monday with 40 cups sold. The line goes up by 15 cups each day until Wednesday, then down by 15 cups each day. How many cups were sold on Friday? Which day sold the same as Thursday?

4. Ali’s height: age 7, 120 cm; age 8, 127 cm; age 9, 131 cm; age 10, 137 cm. In which year did he grow the most? How much did he grow altogether from age 7 to age 10?

Answers (same four questions as the printed Think harder page): 1) Neither: both have 34 girls. (4A: 28 + 6 = 34. 4B: 63 − 5 = 58, 58 ÷ 2 = 29 boys, 29 + 5 = 34 girls.) 2) Ali 175 and Mei 275, so Mei has 100 more. She is not right: double Ali's is 350, and 275 is less than that. (Her point is more than twice as many lines up, 7 against 3, but the scale starts at 100, so counting lines gives the wrong picture.) 3) Monday 40, Tuesday 55, Wednesday 70, Thursday 55, Friday 40. Friday: 40 cups. Thursday (55) matches Tuesday (55). 4) From age 7 to 8 (7 cm). Altogether 17 cm.
