Q1: bars, take away the extras

A + B + C = 100 − 22 = 78

Take away C’s extra 10: 68 = 4 equal bars

One bar: 68 ÷ 4 = 17

B 17, A 34, C 27, D 22. Check: 100 ✓

Q2: watch the gap

Hour 0: gap 80 − 20 = 60 litres

Each hour the gap closes by 6 + 9 = 15

60 ÷ 15 = 4 hours

A: 80, 74, 68, 62, 56. B: 20, 29, 38, 47, 56 ✓
