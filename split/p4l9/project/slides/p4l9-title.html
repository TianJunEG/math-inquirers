LESSON 9 • WEEK 5 • 0–1.5 MIN

Data Detectives

Tables and line graphs

TODAY WE WILL

Read line graphs and tables, find missing values, compare two lines and catch a graph that misleads.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read values and trends from line graphs (using the scale), find the biggest increase or decrease, find missing values in tables and graphs from totals and patterns, compare two lines on one graph, and explain why a graph with a different scale can mislead. Thinking strategies today: READ THE SCALE FIRST, LIST THE CHANGES, and WORK FROM THE TOTAL. Materials: Mini whiteboards, a ruler and two coloured pencils per child, printed blank graph grids (or squared paper), the queue table for the explore task. Online: children use a printed blank grid or squared paper and check it against the graphs the tutor plots on the slides; while children call out where each point goes (nothing is held up to the camera). Time plan (90 minutes in all; every part has a budget and there is a 2-minute standing buffer inside the page-2 block). Opening 0 to 1.5. Sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 6.5 (puzzle 1 about 60 seconds, puzzle 2 about 90 seconds, then ONE check moment for both puzzles; answers ticked or said together on three; nothing held up to the camera, nobody marked). Warm-up game 6.5 to 8.5 (one short round, about 90 seconds), quick questions 8.5 to 11.5 (three questions). Explore 11.5 to 26 (the only breakout of the first half: about 60 seconds each way, closed by a 60-second timer; a Go further what-if is on the slide from the start; two debrief questions). Teaching 26 to 32.5 (click to reveal each step and answer; the one "your turn" slide is optional: skip it if you are behind). Brain break 32.5 to 36 (water and toilet first, then the game; clock check at the end of the break: if you are more than 1.5 minutes behind, skip the next optional item, never a check, the exit ticket or Looking back). Worksheet 36 to 80: page 1 (Start here, in pairs) 36 to 54, check together 54 to 56, short teaching before page 2 56 to 60, page 2 on your own 60 to 77.5 (the last 2 minutes are the standing buffer: if earlier parts ran long, spend the buffer first, then the choice questions, and never the checks, the exit ticket or Looking back), check together 77.5 to 80. Talk time 80 to 83.5 (one minute of pair talk, no breakout). Exit ticket 83.5 to 86.5. Looking back 86.5 to 90 (2 minutes of writing). This page ends at question 2. After question 2 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation have no fixed time slot: any child may choose them at any time, or take them home. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and pairs are not split unless both children agree. Online: page 1 is pair work, so allow about 2 minutes to move into and out of breakout rooms (or run page 1 as a whole-group screen-share if there are only 8 children).
