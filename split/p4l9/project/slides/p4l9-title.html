LESSON 9 • WEEK 5

Data Detectives

Tables and line graphs

TODAY WE WILL

Read line graphs and tables, find missing values, compare two lines and catch a graph that misleads.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read values and trends from line graphs (using the scale), find the biggest increase or decrease, find missing values in tables and graphs from totals and patterns, compare two lines on one graph, and explain why a graph with a different scale can mislead. Thinking strategies today: READ THE SCALE FIRST, LIST THE CHANGES, and WORK FROM THE TOTAL. Materials: Mini whiteboards, a ruler and two coloured pencils per child, printed blank graph grids (or squared paper), the queue table for the explore task. Online: children use a printed blank grid or squared paper and hold it up to the camera; the tutor plots on the slide graphs while children call out where each point goes. Time plan: Warm-up check 1 to 4 (two questions on the key idea; children who get both right and can say why start on the Think Harder pages at minute 34), warm-up game 4 to 6 (one short round), quick questions 6 to 8, explore 8 to 18, teaching 18 to 30 (click to reveal each step and answer; the "your turn" slides are optional, skip them if short of time), brain break 30 to 34, worksheet page 1 from 34 to 50 and check together 50 to 53, short teaching before page 2 from 53 to 58, worksheet page 2 from 58 to 76 and check together 76 to 80, talk time 80 to 85, exit ticket 85 to 88, What was hard? reflection 88 to 90. The Challenge slides and the Think Harder pages are for early finishers and for take-home: they have no fixed time slot. Online: page 1 is pair work, so allow about 2 minutes to move into and out of breakout rooms (or run page 1 as a whole-group screen-share if there are only 8 children).
