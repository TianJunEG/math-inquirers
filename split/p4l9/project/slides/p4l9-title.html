LESSON 9 • WEEK 5

Data Detectives

Tables and line graphs

TODAY WE WILL

Read line graphs and tables, find missing values, compare two lines and catch a graph that misleads.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read values and trends from line graphs (using the scale), find the biggest increase or decrease, find missing values in tables and graphs from totals and patterns, compare two lines on one graph, and explain why a graph with a different scale can mislead. Thinking strategies today: READ THE SCALE FIRST, LIST THE CHANGES, and WORK FROM THE TOTAL. Materials: Mini whiteboards, a ruler and two coloured pencils per child, printed blank graph grids (or squared paper), the queue table for the explore task. Online: children use a printed blank grid or squared paper and hold it up to the camera; the tutor plots on the slide graphs while children call out where each point goes. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
