Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Take the cards 2, 4, 6 and 8.
  2. Make 4-digit numbers between 4000 and 6000.
  3. Write each one on your whiteboard.
  4. Which one is closest to 5000?

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs work with their digit cards (online: their paper cards). Ask them to predict first: "How many numbers do you think there are?" Pairs make every 4-digit number between 4000 and 6000 from 2, 4, 6, 8 (each card once), record each one, then decide which is closest to 5000. Hints: "Which card must go first?" "Keep the 4 in front and change the hundreds digit in order." Finished early: "How many numbers can you make altogether?" (24).
