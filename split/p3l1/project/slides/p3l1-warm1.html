5–8 MIN

Warm-up: Biggest number wins

HOW TO PLAY

  1. I draw a digit card and read it out.
  2. Write it in Th, H, T or O straight away.
  3. No moving it later!
  4. After four digits, the biggest number scores a point.

Round 2: the SMALLEST number wins. Careful: a 4-digit number cannot start with 0.

Sample round: the digits come out 6, 2, 9, 4

Best for biggest: 9642

Best for smallest: 2469

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 8. Biggest number wins. Children draw a Th H T O board on their whiteboard. Draw a digit card (or roll a 10-sided die) four times; after each digit children must write it in one box straight away and cannot move it. Biggest number scores a point. Play 2 or 3 rounds, then a round where the SMALLEST number wins (0 cannot go in the thousands box). The sample round shows the idea: click to show the best possible numbers for 6, 2, 9, 4 (9642 biggest, 2469 smallest). Ask after each round: "Where did you put the 9? Why?" (the thousands place is worth the most). "You got a 1 first. Where should it go when we want the biggest number?" (the ones place: it is small). Listen for the words thousands, hundreds, tens, ones.
