For early finishers

Stretch

1. What is the smallest 4-digit number with all different digits that add up to 10?

2. How many times do you write the digit 5 when you write all the numbers from 1 to 100?

3. Continue the pattern: 1, 3, 7, 15, 31, ___, ___

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 1027 (start 1, then 0; the last two digits must add to 9 and cannot be 0 or 1, so 2 then 7). 2) 20 (ten times in the ones place: 5, 15, …, 95; ten times in the tens place: 50 to 59; 55 has two 5s). 3) 63, 127 (double and add 1; or the jumps double: 2, 4, 8, 16, then 32 and 64).
