Challenge

1. A 4-digit number has all different digits. Its thousands digit is the sum of its other three digits. What is the greatest such number? What is the smallest?

2. Mei uses the cards 1, 2, 3, 4 once each to make 4-digit numbers. She lists them all from smallest to greatest. What is the 10th number in her list?

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) Greatest 9810 (9 = 8 + 1 + 0). Smallest 3012 (the smallest sum of three different digits is 0 + 1 + 2 = 3). 2) 2341 (6 numbers start with 1; then 2134, 2143, 2314, 2341).
