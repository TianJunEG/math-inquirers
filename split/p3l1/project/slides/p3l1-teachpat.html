Look for a pattern: find the jumps

5000, 4990, 4970, 4940, ____, ____

Step 1. Find each jump: 5000 → 4990 is − 10, 4990 → 4970 is − 20, 4970 → 4940 is − 30

Step 2. The jump grows by 10 each time. So the next jumps are − 40, then − 50.

Step 3. 4940 − 40 = 4900, 4900 − 50 = 4850

Always check the rule on EVERY jump, not just the first one.
