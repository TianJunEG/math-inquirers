Story break: Rozsa Peter

Hungary (Budapest) • 1905 to 1977

Rózsa Péter was born in Budapest, in Hungary, in 1905. At university she began with chemistry, then changed to mathematics. She became one of the people who started the study of recursion. A recursive rule builds each new number from the number before it, so a pattern can keep going as far as you like. She taught at a university in Budapest and wrote a book for ordinary readers, called Playing with Infinity. She said she loved maths mainly because it is beautiful. In 1973 she became the first woman elected to the Hungarian Academy of Sciences.
