Answers: 700; 3045 (watch for 345: there are no hundreds, so we write 0 in the hundreds place); 5000; 5950 (100 less: the hundreds digit goes down, and 0 hundreds becomes 9 hundreds); 7098 (same thousands and hundreds, compare the tens: 9 tens is more than 8 tens).
