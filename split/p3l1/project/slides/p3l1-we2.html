Closest to 4000: check both sides

Cards 1, 4, 7, 3. Which number is closest to 4000?

Step 1. Just ABOVE 4000: start with 4, then the rest as small as possible: 4137. That is 137 more.

Step 2. Just BELOW 4000: start with 3, then the rest as big as possible: 3741. That is 259 less.

Step 3. Compare: 137 is less than 259.

4137 is the closest to 4000.
