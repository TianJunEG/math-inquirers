Number Sleuths

Numbers to 10 000

TODAY WE WILL

Build 4-digit numbers, make the greatest and smallest, list every number in order and crack clue puzzles.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read and build 4-digit numbers by place value; make the greatest, smallest, odd, even and closest numbers from digit cards; list every number in order (heuristic: make a list in order); solve clue puzzles and continue number patterns (heuristic: look for a pattern). Materials: Digit cards 0–9 (two sets per child), a place value mat (Th H T O), mini whiteboards, a 10-sided die or a bag of digit cards 0–9 for the warm-up game. Online: Children cut out the digit cards at the back of the workbook (or write 0–9 on small paper squares) and draw a Th H T O mat on paper. The tutor draws the digits on camera. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
