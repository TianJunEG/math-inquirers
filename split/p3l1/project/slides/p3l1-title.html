PRIMARY 3 • LESSON 1 • WEEK 1 • 0–1.5 MIN

Number Sleuths

Numbers to 10 000

TODAY WE WILL

Build 4-digit numbers, make the greatest and smallest, list every number in order and crack clue puzzles.

TODAY'S TOOL

List every way

Focus habit: Explain

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read and build 4-digit numbers by place value; make the greatest, smallest, odd, even and closest numbers from digit cards; list every number in order (heuristic: make a list in order); solve clue puzzles and continue number patterns (heuristic: look for a pattern). Materials: Digit cards 0–9 (two sets per child), a place value mat (Th H T O), mini whiteboards, a 10-sided die or a bag of digit cards 0–9 for the warm-up game. Online: Children cut out the digit cards at the back of the workbook (or write 0–9 on small paper squares) and draw a Th H T O mat on paper. The tutor draws the digits on camera. Time plan (90 minutes, with a named 3-minute float inside talk time): opening 0 to 1.5, sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 5 (own paper, nobody marked, one show-me for both), warm-up game 5 to 8 (two rounds) and three quick questions 8 to 11, explore 11 to 24 (a Go further what-if is on the explore slide from the start; pairs go to pre-assigned breakout rooms), teaching 24 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 71 (page 1 in pairs, short teaching, page 2 on their own, a 60-second self-check slide after each page; after question 3 of page 1 any child may choose Think harder or the Investigation; from minute 68 everyone chooses), talk time 71 to 79 (volunteers first, pass is fine), exit ticket 79 to 85, look back 85 to 88, stamp and close 88 to 90. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child; the tutor never announces who is on which page and invites the whole group, never one child. If the class is behind, children finish the last question of each page at home; the check slides cover it. SPINE: Today's tool is List every way. List all the possibilities in an organised order and say how you know you have them all. Look for where you use it. Point at the card on page 1 of the workbook. HABIT: Focus habit: Explain. Ask for the reason in the child's own words, said or written. Say: "How do you know? Tell it as if you were teaching a friend." Do not give the answer first: ask the prompt, wait, and give a hint only if the child is stuck.
