PRIMARY 3 • LESSON 1 • WEEK 1 • 0–2 MIN

Number Sleuths

Numbers to 10 000

TODAY WE WILL

Build 4-digit numbers, make the greatest and smallest, list every number in order and crack clue puzzles.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read and build 4-digit numbers by place value; make the greatest, smallest, odd, even and closest numbers from digit cards; list every number in order (heuristic: make a list in order); solve clue puzzles and continue number patterns (heuristic: look for a pattern). Materials: Digit cards 0–9 (two sets per child), a place value mat (Th H T O), mini whiteboards, a 10-sided die or a bag of digit cards 0–9 for the warm-up game. Online: Children cut out the digit cards at the back of the workbook (or write 0–9 on small paper squares) and draw a Th H T O mat on paper. The tutor draws the digits on camera. Time plan: opening 0 to 2, sneak-peek puzzles 2 to 5 (own paper, nobody marked), warm-up game 5 to 8 and quick questions 8 to 10, explore 10 to 25 (a Go further what-if is on the explore slide from minute 10), teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 (page 1 in pairs, short teaching, page 2 on their own, a check-together slide after each page; after question 3 of page 1 any child may choose Think harder or the Investigation; from minute 67 everyone chooses), talk time 70 to 80 (volunteers first, pass is fine), exit ticket 80 to 86, look back 86 to 88, stamp and close 88 to 90. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child; the tutor never announces who is on which page and invites the whole group, never one child. If the class is behind, children finish the last question of each page at home; the check slides cover it.
