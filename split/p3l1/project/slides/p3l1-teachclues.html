What number am I? One clue at a time

Clue 1: I am between 2000 and 3000.

Clue 2: My ones digit is 3 times my thousands digit.

Clue 3: My tens digit is 1 less than my ones digit.

Clue 4: My hundreds digit is my tens digit take away my thousands digit.

Th = 2

O = 3 × 2 = 6

T = 6 − 1 = 5

H = 5 − 2 = 3. I am 2356.
