Renaming: 12 hundreds is 1 thousand 2 hundreds

What number is 3 thousands, 12 hundreds, 5 tens and 14 ones?

Step 1. 3 thousands = 3000. 12 hundreds = 1200.

Step 2. 5 tens = 50. 14 ones = 14.

Step 3. Add them: 3000 + 1200 + 50 + 14 = 4264

Check: 4264 has 4 thousands, 2 hundreds, 6 tens and 4 ones.
