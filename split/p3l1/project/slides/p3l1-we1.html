Odd or even? Choose the ones digit FIRST

Cards 2, 9, 6, 0. Make the greatest ODD number and the smallest EVEN number.

Step 1. Odd numbers end in 1, 3, 5, 7 or 9. The only odd card is 9, so 9 goes in the ones place.

Step 2. Greatest odd: the other cards from biggest, then 9: 6 2 0 9

Step 3. Smallest even: start 2, then 0. The ones digit must be even, so 6 goes last: 2 0 9 6

Greatest odd 6209 Smallest even 2096 (2069 is odd)

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click through the steps. Think-aloud: "Odd numbers end in an odd digit. The only odd card is 9, so I put 9 in the ones place FIRST. Then I make the rest as big as I can: 6, 2, 0. So 6209." Ask before step 3: "Smallest number is 2069. Why is that not the answer?" (it ends in 9, so it is odd). Common error: making 9620 first and then trying to fix it.
