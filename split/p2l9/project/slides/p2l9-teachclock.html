Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5 clocks). Use the cardboard clocks. Step 1: "The short hand tells the hour. It has gone past 7 but not reached 8." Step 2: count round in 5s together, pointing at each number. Click the answer. Click the day line: "From midnight until noon is a.m. (the morning). From noon until midnight is p.m. (afternoon and night)." Your turn: breakfast is in the morning, so 7:35 a.m. Ask: "What would you be doing at 7:35 p.m.?" Common mistake: reading the hour from the number the short hand is nearest (8) instead of the one it has passed (7).
