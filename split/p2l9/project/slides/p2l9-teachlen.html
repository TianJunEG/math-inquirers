Ribbons: overlaps and cuts

Glue 52 cm and 37 cm, with a 4 cm overlap.

Step 1. End to end: 52 + 37 = 89 cm.

Step 2. The overlap is counted twice: 89 − 4 = 85 cm.

Cut a 12 m rope into 3 equal pieces.

Step 3. 4 + 4 + 4 = 12, so each piece is 4 m.

Step 4. 3 pieces need only 2 cuts.

Your turn: Glue 30 cm and 25 cm with a 5 cm overlap. How long?

50 cm

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4 ribbons). Use two paper strips and a real overlap if you can. Click 1 shows the overlap. Step 1: "End to end means add." Step 2: "Look at the yellow part: those 4 cm are under the red ribbon AND inside the yellow ribbon. We counted them twice, so we take them away once: 89 − 4 = 85." Then the rope (click): "3 equal pieces that make 12: try 3 + 3 + 3 = 9, too small; 4 + 4 + 4 = 12 ✓. How many cuts? Count the red lines: 2. The last piece does not need a cut." Your turn: 30 + 25 = 55, 55 − 5 = 50 cm. Common mistakes: adding the overlap (89 + 4) and saying 3 cuts for 3 pieces.
