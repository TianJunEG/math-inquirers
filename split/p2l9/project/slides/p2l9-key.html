Key idea: same on both sides

A level balance means both sides are equally heavy, so you can swap.

For jugs, write every pour in a table: how much is in each jug.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Balance puzzles: adding the masses on the two sides instead of using "both sides are the same". (2) Q1c: forgetting to take the apple away before sharing among the lemons. (3) Jug puzzles: writing "pour" without how much is in each jug, so the steps cannot be checked; or "pouring half". (4) Time: counting on past the hour as if there were 100 minutes in an hour (4:50 + 35 minutes written as 4:85 or 5:85).
