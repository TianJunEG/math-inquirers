Think harder: what day is it?

1. Today is Thursday.

a) What day will it be in 3 weeks?

b) What day will it be in 25 days?

c) What day will it be in 30 days?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional slide for pairs who finish early or ask for more. Show it only on request and never announce who is working on it. Children write the answer on their own paper and say it together on three; nothing is held up to the camera. Answers and tutor lines are for you only. Item 1 (Calendar: weeks and days; tool of the day idea: Spot the pattern). SAY: "The week is a repeating pattern of 7. Take out whole weeks first." ASK: "How many whole weeks fit into 25 days? How many days are left over?" LISTEN FOR: "Remainder" language: 25 days is 3 weeks and 4 days. ANSWER: a) Thursday (3 weeks is 21 days, 3 full cycles). b) Monday (25 = 3 weeks and 4 days; Thursday + 4 days is Monday). c) Saturday (30 = 4 weeks and 2 days).
