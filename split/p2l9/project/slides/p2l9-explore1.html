Explore: balance lab

EXPLORE

  1. Hang two paper cups from a coat hanger. Check it is level when the cups are empty.
  2. Put a glue stick in one cup. Guess: how many 20¢ coins will balance it?
  3. Add coins one at a time until the hanger is level. Then try a spoon.
  4. Which is heavier, the glue stick or the spoon? How do you know?

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8 to 14. Pairs build or use their coat-hanger balance. Online: children hold their balance up to the camera; if a child has none, they balance two objects in their hands and guess which is heavier, then check on a kitchen scale with an adult. Say: "Before you add any coins, write your guess." Walk round and ask: "How do you know it is level?" "What happens if you add one more coin?" Do not teach the swapping idea yet: let children notice that the number of coins tells us how heavy the object is.
