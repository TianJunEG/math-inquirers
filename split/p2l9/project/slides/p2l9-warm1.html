Warm-up: which unit?

Write m, cm, kg, g, ℓ or minutes on your whiteboard. Then say why.

A classroom door: how tall?

m

A pencil: how long?

cm

A big bag of rice: how heavy?

kg

A strawberry: how heavy?

g

A bucket of water: how much water?

ℓ

Brushing your teeth: how long?

minutes

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 4 to 6 (shortened to two minutes because of the Warm-up check: one or two rounds). Which unit? Point to one card at a time. Children write the unit on whiteboards and hold them up on three. Then click to show the unit (clicks reveal door, pencil, rice, strawberry, bucket, brushing teeth in order). Answers: door m (about 2 m), pencil cm, bag of rice kg, strawberry g, bucket of water ℓ, brushing teeth minutes (about 2 minutes). Ask "Why not grams for the rice?" (the number would be very big; kilograms are the bigger unit) and "Why not metres for the pencil?" (a pencil is much shorter than 1 m). Quick finisher: "Name something you would measure in litres at home." (a bottle of water, a pot of soup, a fish tank). Watch for children who mix up length and mass: "Is a heavy thing always long?"
