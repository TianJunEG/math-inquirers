Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6 to 8.5. Which unit? Point to one card at a time. Children write the unit on whiteboards and hold them up on three. Then click to show the unit (clicks reveal door, rice, brushing teeth in order: three prompts only; pencil, strawberry and bucket are spare). Answers: door m (about 2 m), pencil cm, bag of rice kg, strawberry g, bucket of water ℓ, brushing teeth minutes (about 2 minutes). Ask "Why not grams for the rice?" (the number would be very big; kilograms are the bigger unit) and "Why not metres for the pencil?" (a pencil is much shorter than 1 m). Quick finisher: "Name something you would measure in litres at home." (a bottle of water, a pot of soup, a fish tank). Watch for children who mix up length and mass: "Is a heavy thing always long?" A game of about 2.5 minutes: stop it on the clock even if the game is not finished.
