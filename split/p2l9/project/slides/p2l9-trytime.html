Whiteboards, then click. a) The short hand has passed 3; the long hand on 9 is 45 minutes (count in 5s): 3:45. b) 6:35 to 7:00 is 25 minutes; 40 − 25 = 15 more: 7:15 p.m. c) The buses are 20 minutes apart; the next bus after 8:45 is 8:50, so he waits 5 minutes.
