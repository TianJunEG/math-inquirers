PRIMARY 2 • LESSON 9 • WEEK 5

Measure Masters

Length, mass, volume and time

TODAY WE WILL

Solve balance, jug and ribbon puzzles, and work out times and how long things take.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Solve balance-scale puzzles in g and kg by swapping things that balance; solve pouring puzzles with litre jugs by keeping a table of every pour; join and cut lengths in m and cm (overlaps, pieces and cuts); tell the time to 5 minutes with a.m. and p.m. and find start times, end times and how long, also across the hour. Materials: A coat hanger, two paper cups and string (a home-made balance), 20¢ coins or erasers, a glue stick and a spoon; two plastic cups for pouring; a cardboard clock with hands; mini whiteboards. Online: Children build the coat-hanger balance before the lesson and hold it up to the camera. Share the slide balances, jugs and clocks and annotate on screen. Each child needs a cardboard or paper-plate clock. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (balance puzzles and jug puzzles; click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before page 2 (ribbons, clocks, how long) and a check-together slide after each page, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
