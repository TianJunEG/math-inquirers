PRIMARY 2 • LESSON 9 • WEEK 5

Measure Masters

Length, mass, volume and time

TODAY WE WILL

Solve balance, jug and ribbon puzzles, and work out times and how long things take.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Solve balance-scale puzzles in g and kg by swapping things that balance; solve pouring puzzles with litre jugs by keeping a table of every pour; join and cut lengths in m and cm (overlaps, pieces and cuts); tell the time to 5 minutes with a.m. and p.m. and find start times, end times and how long, also across the hour. Materials: A coat hanger, two paper cups, string, a glue stick, a spoon and about 20 20¢ coins (a home-made balance; backup: a ruler balanced on a pencil); for the jug puzzles two cups or bottles of different size (about 3 and 5 units), a jug of dry rice or sand (or water), and tape marking 3 and 5 units; a cardboard clock with hands; mini whiteboards. Online: Children build the coat-hanger balance before the lesson and hold it up to the camera. Share the slide balances, jugs and clocks and annotate on screen. Each child needs a cardboard or paper-plate clock. For the pouring puzzles use dry rice or sand rather than water when online, and let children act out each pour with the two containers. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 8, explore 8 to 20 (explore2 talk is 6 minutes), teaching 20 to 32 (balance puzzles and jug puzzles; click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 32 to 36, worksheet page 1 36 to 50 then check together 50 to 52, short teaching 52 to 57, worksheet page 2 57 to 69 then check together 69 to 72, talk time 72 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
