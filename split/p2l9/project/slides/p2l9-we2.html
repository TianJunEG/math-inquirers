Jug puzzle: keep a table

A 4 ℓ jug and a 9 ℓ jug have no marks. Get exactly 1 ℓ.

You can:

fill a jug from the tap,

empty a jug into the sink,

pour from one jug into the other until one is full or the other is empty.

1 ℓ is left in the 9 ℓ jug. 9 − 4 − 4 = 1 ✓

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example (a jug puzzle). Use two real cups if you have them. Read the rules on the right first. Click to reveal each row; before each click ask "What could we do next?" Think-aloud: "I want exactly 1 ℓ. I fill the 9 ℓ jug. I pour into the 4 ℓ jug until it is full: 9 − 4 = 5 left. I empty the 4 ℓ jug and pour again: 5 − 4 = 1. I write every pour in a table so I can check it, and so I can find my way back if I get lost." Stress: after each step write how much is in EACH jug. Common mistake: "pour half". We cannot pour half: there are no marks, so a pour only stops when one jug is full or the other is empty.
