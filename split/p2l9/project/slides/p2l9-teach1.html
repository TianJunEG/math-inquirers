A level balance: swap it

A level balance means both sides are equally heavy.

Step 1. 1 pineapple balances 3 oranges. Each orange is 200 g.

Step 2. Swap each orange for a 200 g weight. The balance stays level.

Step 3. 200, 400, 600. The pineapple is 600 g.

Why? Things that balance are equally heavy, so we can swap them.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach: the idea and why. Hold up the coat-hanger balance: "Level means both sides are equally heavy." Click 1: "1 pineapple balances 3 oranges. Each orange is 200 g. How heavy is the pineapple?" Let children guess first. Click 2: "If one orange is just as heavy as a 200 g weight, I can take the orange off and put the weight on instead. Is it still level?" (Yes.) Click 3 shows the weights. Click 4: count on in 200s: 200, 400, 600. Click 5: the why. Think-aloud: "The scale is level, so both sides are the same. I swap each orange for what it balances. The scale stays level, so the pineapple is 600 g." Common mistake: adding the two sides together (pineapple + oranges). Ask: "Does a level balance tell us the two sides are the same or that we add them?"
