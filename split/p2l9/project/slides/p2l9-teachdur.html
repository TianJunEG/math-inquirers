How long? Count on to the o’clock first

A show starts at 2:45 p.m. and lasts 35 minutes. When does it end?

Step 1. 2:45 to 3:00 is 15 minutes.

Step 2. 35 − 15 = 20 more minutes: 3:20 p.m.

9:50 a.m. to 11:05 a.m.: how long?

Step 3. 9:50 → 10:00 is 10 min. 10:00 → 11:00 is 1 hour. 11:00 → 11:05 is 5 min.

1 hour and 10 + 5 = 15 minutes: 1 hour 15 minutes

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5b and Q5c). Draw the time line on the board. Click the jumps, then step 1 and step 2. Think-aloud: "An hour has 60 minutes, not 100, so I cannot just add 45 + 35. I jump to the o’clock first: 2:45 to 3:00 is 15 minutes. I still need 35 − 15 = 20 minutes: 3:20 p.m." Second example: "How long from 9:50 a.m. to 11:05 a.m.? I jump to the next o’clock: 10 minutes. Then whole hours: 10:00 to 11:00 is 1 hour. Then the minutes left: 5. Altogether 1 hour 15 minutes." Common mistakes: writing 2:80; counting 9:50 to 11:05 as 2 hours because the hour changes twice.
