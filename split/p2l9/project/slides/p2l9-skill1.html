Item 1 (Picture equations: shapes stand for numbers; tool of the day idea: Guess and check). SAY: "Find the line that tells you the most. Which shape can you find first?" ASK: "Which line did you use first and why? How can you check?" LISTEN FOR: Three triangles = 27 is the start. Then use it in line 2. ANSWER: a) 9 (27 split into 3 equal parts). b) 6 (21 - 9 = 12, shared by 2). c) 7 (13 - 6). d) 22.
