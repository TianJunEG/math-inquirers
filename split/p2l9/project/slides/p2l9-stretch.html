For early finishers

Stretch

1. A box with 5 same pencils is 150 g. The empty box is 50 g. How heavy is one pencil?

2. You have a 4 ℓ jug and a 5 ℓ jug. How can you get exactly 3 ℓ? List each pour.

3. A train leaves at 9:35 a.m. The trip takes 50 minutes. When does it arrive? Ali gets to the station 15 minutes before it leaves. When is that?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) 20 g (the pencils are 150 − 50 = 100 g; 5 pencils share 100 g: 20 + 20 + 20 + 20 + 20 = 100). 2) (4 ℓ jug, 5 ℓ jug): fill 4 (4, 0); pour into 5 (0, 4); fill 4 (4, 4); pour into 5 until full, only 1 ℓ fits (3, 5): 3 ℓ in the 4 ℓ jug. 3) Arrives 10:25 a.m. (9:35 to 10:00 is 25 minutes, 25 more). Ali arrives at 9:20 a.m. (15 minutes before 9:35).
