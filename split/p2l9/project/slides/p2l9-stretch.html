1. 2 apples and 1 lemon balance 1 pomelo. 1 pomelo balances 5 lemons. How many lemons balance 1 apple? How many lemons balance 1 pomelo and 1 apple together?

These are the SAME four questions as the printed Think harder page: the slide shows 1 to 3 and question 4 is on the page only. Never announce who is working on which page. Answers: 1) 1 apple balances 2 lemons. Swap the pomelo for 5 lemons: 2 apples and 1 lemon balance 5 lemons. Take 1 lemon off both sides: 2 apples balance 4 lemons, so 1 apple balances 2 lemons. A pomelo and an apple balance 5 + 2 = 7 lemons. 2) 1 hour 25 minutes (10:45 to 11:00 is 15 minutes, 11:00 to 12:00 is 1 hour, 12:00 to 12:10 is 10 minutes). 3) Apple 100 g, pomelo 300 g (5 apples balance 500 g). 4) (Question: You have a 2 ℓ jug and a 7 ℓ jug with no marks. How can you get exactly 3 ℓ? List each pour.) Fill 7, pour into 2, empty 2, pour again: 3 ℓ is left in the 7 ℓ jug.
