Explore: talk about it

1. How many coins balanced your glue stick? Your spoon?

2. A glue stick balances 8 coins. A spoon balances 5 coins. Which is heavier?

3. Your partner used erasers and got a different number. Why?

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 14 to 20. Let pairs keep testing, then gather to answer these one at a time. What we want them to notice: when the balance is level, the two sides are equally heavy, so the coins tell us how heavy the object is in "coins". Answers: 1) Open: compare with other pairs. 2) The glue stick: it needs more of the same coin to balance. 3) Erasers and coins are different units (an eraser is not as heavy as a coin), so the numbers are different. To compare, use the same unit. Follow-up: "If the glue stick balances 8 coins and 2 glue sticks are on one side, how many coins balance them?" (16).
