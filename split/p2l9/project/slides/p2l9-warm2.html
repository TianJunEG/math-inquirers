Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8.5 to 10.5. Read each question aloud, give thinking time, take answers, then click to show. Ask "How do you know?" each time. Answers: 1) 75 cm (30 + 40 = 70, 5 more). 2) 3 ℓ (count on from 2 to 5). Spare questions in the guide table: the minute hand on 6 is 30 minutes past; lunch at 12:30 is pm (draw a day line from midnight to midnight and mark noon); 2 bags of 5 kg are 10 kg. Ask quick questions 1 and 3 only (about 2 minutes): children answer by thumbs, hands, not by naming a child. The other questions are spare.
