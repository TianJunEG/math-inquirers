Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6 to 8 (2 minutes: ask questions 1, 3 and 5; 2 and 4 are spare). Children answer by thumbs, hands, not by naming a child. Read each question aloud, give thinking time, take answers, then click to show. Ask "How do you know?" each time. Answers: 1) 75 cm (30 + 40 = 70, 5 more). 2) 30 minutes (count in 5s from 12 to 6: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30; or half past). 3) 3 ℓ (count on from 2 to 5). 4) p.m.: 12 noon to 12 midnight is p.m., so half past twelve at lunchtime is p.m. Many children say a.m.; draw a day line from midnight to midnight and mark noon. 5) 10 kg (5 + 5).
