Your turn: a 2 ℓ jug and a 5 ℓ jug

Draw a table. Write how much is in each jug after every step.

a Get exactly 3 ℓ.

5 − 2 = 3

b Get exactly 1 ℓ.

5 − 2 − 2 = 1

c Get exactly 4 ℓ in the 5 ℓ jug.

2 + 2 = 4

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards, then click. Write the jugs as (2 ℓ jug, 5 ℓ jug). a) Fill 5 (0, 5); pour into 2 (2, 3): 3 ℓ in the 5 ℓ jug. b) Carry on: empty 2 (0, 3); pour into 2 (2, 1): 1 ℓ. c) Fill 2 (2, 0); pour into 5 (0, 2); fill 2 (2, 2); pour into 5 (0, 4): 4 ℓ. Point out that c ADDS jugs (2 + 2) while a and b TAKE AWAY (5 − 2). Ask: "Which way would get 6 ℓ?" (none: no jug holds 6 ℓ).
