Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 1 to 4. Warm-up check: two questions on the key idea of today, in a NEW setting (not recall). Give about 1 minute each. Whiteboards up together, ask one child "How do you know?", then click to show each answer. Q1 answer: 6 plums (2 apples, and each apple is 3 plums). Listen for: Swaps each apple for 3 plums. A drawing of the scale is a good reason. Q2 answer: Fill the 5 ℓ jug. Pour 2 ℓ into the small jug (3 ℓ left). Empty the small jug and pour 2 ℓ again. 1 ℓ is left in the big jug. Listen for: Checked by a search of all pours: 1 ℓ is possible. Any correct list of pours is fine. A table of pours is a good habit. FAST TRACK: a child who gets BOTH right AND can say why starts on the THINK HARDER pages and the Open investigation (workbook Q8) when the worksheet begins at minute 36, in their own breakout room or with you checking in. A right answer without a reason stays with the group. If unsure, keep the child in the group. Nobody is labelled: say "Some of you will start on a different page today; everyone gets a turn at both." Children can move at any time. Everyone joins explore, the mini-lesson, the check-together slides and talk time.
