Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. Guess, draw, count or say "I do not know". Show your paper to your tutor only.

Q1 (start here) You have a 3 l jug and a 5 l jug. You fill the 5 l jug and pour from it into the 3 l jug until the 3 l jug is full. How much water is left in the 5 l jug? (Try it with two cups and rice, or draw it.)

2 l

Q2 (think about it) A melon balances 2 apples. One apple balances 3 plums. How many plums balance the melon? How do you know?

6 plums

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 1 to 4. Sneak-peek puzzles: two puzzles on the key idea of today, which the children have NOT learnt yet. Say out loud: "These are ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome and so is I do not know. Nobody is marked." Question 1 is an access question: any child can try it with objects, a drawing or counting, so everyone has a first win (about 1 minute). Question 2 is the thinking question (about 2 minutes). Answers are PRIVATE: each child holds their own paper to the camera for you only, or sends a private chat message. Do not raise whiteboards together, do not read answers out, do not name children. Q1 answer: 2 l (5 - 3 = 2). Listen for: One pour is enough. This is a first win for everyone and previews the pour table. Q2 answer: 6 plums (3 + 3). Listen for: Listen for swapping: the melon can be swapped for 2 apples, and each apple for 3 plums. Drawing the scale is a good method. At the end click to show the answers as "one way to think about it", without saying who had what. Ask only for volunteers: "Would anyone like to tell us how they started?" You may quietly invite a child to try Think harder by PRIVATE chat message. Never announce who is on which page. Pairs are not split unless both children agree. Children can move either way at any time. Everyone joins explore, the mini-lesson, the check-together slides and talk time.
