Your turn: whiteboards

Find the scale with a number first. Then swap.

a 1 bag of flour balances 4 apples. Each apple is 100 g. How heavy is the flour?

400 g

b 2 cups balance 1 jug. The jug is 300 g. How heavy is 1 cup?

150 g

c 1 box balances 2 balls. 1 box and 1 ball balance 600 g. How heavy is 1 ball?

200 g

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards face down until 3, 2, 1 (or show only the tutor; never read a board out), then click. a) 100, 200, 300, 400: 400 g. b) Two cups share 300 g equally: 150 + 150 = 300, so 150 g. c) Swap the box for 2 balls: 3 balls balance 600 g, so 1 ball is 200 g (200 + 200 + 200 = 600) and the box is 400 g. Check: 400 + 200 = 600 ✓. For c, a child who writes 300 has shared 600 between the box and the ball as if they were the same: ask "Is the box as heavy as one ball?"
