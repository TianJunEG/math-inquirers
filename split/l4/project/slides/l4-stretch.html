For early finishers

Stretch

1. star, circle, circle, triangle, star, circle, circle, triangle … What is the 23rd shape?

2. Squares in a row need 4, 7, 10, … sticks. With 20 sticks, how many squares can you make? How many sticks are left over?

3. A, B, B, C, A, B, B, C, … How many Bs are in the first 18 letters?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) circle. The core has 4 shapes, so count in 4s: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20. The 20th is a triangle, the end of a core; 21st star, 22nd circle, 23rd circle. 2) 6 squares, 1 stick left over. The table goes 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19; 7 squares would need 22, which is too many; 20 − 19 = 1. 3) 9 Bs. Each core A, B, B, C has 2 Bs. 16 letters are 4 cores: 2, 4, 6, 8 Bs. The 17th is A and the 18th is B: 9.
