For children who finish early (these are on the slide only; Think Harder T1 to T4 are in the workbook). Answers: 1) circle. The core has 4 shapes, so count in 4s: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20. The 20th is a triangle, the end of a core; 21st star, 22nd circle, 23rd circle. 2) 9 Bs. Each core A, B, B, C has 2 Bs. 16 letters are 4 cores: 2, 4, 6, 8 Bs. The 17th is A and the 18th is B: 9. 3) Yes. Count in 5s: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25. Each of these ends the core, so the 25th is the last shape of the core, like the 5th.
