For any pair that chooses to try more. These are the same four questions as Think harder T1 to T4 in the workbook (the labels match). Answers: T1) triangle T2) 10. The core circle, triangle, triangle, square has 2 triangles. Count the cores in 4s: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, so there are 5 cores. Count the triangles in 2s: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10. T3) blue T4) 6 squares and 1 stick left over. The table goes 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19; 7 squares would need 22, which is too many; 20 − 19 = 1.
