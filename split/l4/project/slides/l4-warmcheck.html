7–10 MIN

Warm-up check

Whiteboards: answer and say how you know

1. Beads repeat: red, blue, blue, red, blue, blue ... What colour is the 12th bead? How do you know?

Blue: 12 ends a repeat

2. A row of squares is made with sticks: 1 square uses 4 sticks, 2 squares use 7, 3 squares use 10. How many sticks for 4 squares? Why?

13 sticks (3 more each time)

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 7 to 10 (3 minutes). Two questions on the key idea in a new setting. Children write their answer and how they know on a whiteboard and show it on a count of three; click to show each answer. Ask one or two children "How do you know?". Quietly note who got both right and gave a reason: those children may start the worksheet on the Think Harder page (then Challenge and the Open investigation) while the group works on the core page. Tell them privately at minute 40; do not announce names or scores. Everyone still joins Explore and the Mini-lesson. Answers: 1) Blue. The pattern repeats every 3 beads, so the 3rd, 6th, 9th and 12th beads end a repeat, and each repeat ends with blue. 2) 13. Each new square shares a side, so it needs only 3 more sticks (10 + 3).
