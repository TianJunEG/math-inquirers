Story break: Ingrid Daubechies

Optional • about 2 minutes • we can skip it

Ingrid Daubechies grew up in Belgium. As a girl she liked weaving, which makes patterns from threads, and she liked finding out how things work. When she could not sleep, she worked out the powers of 2 in her head: 2, 4, 8, 16 and on. She was amazed at how fast they grow. Today she is a maths professor, and her ideas help computers keep pictures in small files.
