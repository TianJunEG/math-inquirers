Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are for every child to choose, any time or at home. Nobody is told which page they should be on. SPINE: Today's tool is Spot the pattern. Look for what repeats or grows. Look for where you use it. Point at the card on page 1 of the workbook. HABIT: Focus habit: Wonder. Invite a question or a what-if before any answer is given.
