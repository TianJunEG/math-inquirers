Think harder: days of the week

1. Today is Wednesday.

a) What day will it be in 2 days?

b) What day will it be in 7 days?

c) What day will it be in 9 days?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional slide for pairs who finish early or ask for more. Show it only on request and never announce who is working on it. Children write the answer on their own paper and say it together on three; nothing is held up to the camera. Answers and tutor lines are for you only. Item 1 (Calendar: the week repeats every 7 days; tool of the day idea: Spot the pattern). SAY: "The days of the week are a repeating pattern, like our colour patterns. The core has 7 days." ASK: "What happens after Sunday? What does 7 days later always give?" LISTEN FOR: "It comes back to the same day." Children who count 9 one by one are fine; then ask them to find a shortcut. ANSWER: a) Friday. b) Wednesday (a whole week later). c) Friday (7 days brings you back to Wednesday, then 2 more days).
