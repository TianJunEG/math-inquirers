Go further

1. Beads repeat in a pattern of 5: red, red, blue, blue, green. How many green beads are in the first 47 beads?

2. Dots: 1, 3, 6, 10, 15 … What are the next two numbers? What is the 10th number?

Make up your own

3. Make up your own repeating pattern with a core of 5 beads (use up to 3 colours). Ask a partner: what colour is the 33rd bead? Write the answer and how you know.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For any pair that chooses Go further. Use it as a pair choice, or with volunteers in talk time (never call a child by name). Ask for the reason every time. Q3 is open: there is no single right answer, so ask children to check each other's work. Answers: 1) 9 green beads. Green is the 5th bead of each core, so greens are at 5, 10, 15 … 45: count in 5s, that is 9 numbers. Beads 46 and 47 are red. 2) 21 and 28. The 10th is 55 (add 2, 3, 4, … each time). 3) Open. Check: 30 ends a core of 5 (count in 5s), so the 33rd bead is the 3rd bead of the core. Example: red, red, blue, blue, green gives blue.
