Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click through. Think-aloud: "I do not know how many squares, but I know the rule: add 3. I keep the table going: 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22. I stop when I reach 22. It is under 7 squares." Check: the first square takes 4 sticks, and each of the other 6 squares takes 3 more: 4 + 18 = 22. Ask: "What if I had 23 sticks?" (still 7 squares, and 1 stick left over).
