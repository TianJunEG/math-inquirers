Q1: where do the greens sit?

The core has 5 beads. Green is the last one.

Greens are at 5, 10, 15 … 45: count in 5s.

Jumps: +2, +3, +4, +5. They grow by 1.

15 + 6 = 21, 21 + 7 = 28

28 + 8 = 36, 36 + 9 = 45

10th: 45 + 10 = 55
