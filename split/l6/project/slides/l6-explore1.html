Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Take 20¢, 10¢ and 5¢ coins.
  2. Make 20¢ in as many ways as you can.
  3. Record each way on your whiteboard.
  4. Ask: have I got them all?

Go further: your choice, any time

What if we want 30¢ instead? How many ways can you find? How will you know you have them all?

Math Inquirers Level 1 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs work with the materials: Play coins (5¢, 10¢, 20¢, 50¢), about 6 of each per pair, mini whiteboards. Pairs make 20¢ in as many ways as they can with 20¢, 10¢ and 5¢ coins, recording each on a whiteboard. Online: Print the cut-out coin sheet at the back of the workbook, or use real coins at home. Go further (what-if) is on this slide from the start: any pair may choose it at any time, so nobody waits. The tutor does not choose who and does not announce it. Answers: 6 ways: 20+10; 20+5+5; 10+10+10; 10+10+5+5; 10+5+5+5+5; six 5¢. Start with the biggest coin and work down.
