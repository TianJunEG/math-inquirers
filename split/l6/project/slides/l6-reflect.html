Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 88 to 90 (2 minutes, after the exit ticket). Children tick or write the Look-back box on the Choose and look-back page of their workbook. A pass is always allowed. It is never marked, never collected in front of the group and never read out; the tutor may look at it afterwards. If a child ticks "I am still stuck", the tutor looks at the box after class and checks in at the start of the next lesson, in the open session (a quick visit to the shared breakout room or a hand signal, with a parent nearby). Do not ask children to share their boxes. Praise the strategy ("Drawing it got you unstuck"), not the speed. Say: "Tick what was hard and what helped you. Write or draw one thing you did well. You can pass."
