Optional: if you finish early

Exactly 3 coins

Make 25¢ with exactly 3 coins (5¢, 10¢, 20¢).

Step 1. Try a 20¢: 20 + 5 = 25, but that is only 2 coins. Not 3.

Step 2. Try two 10¢: 10 + 10 + 5 = 25, and it is 3 coins ✓

Step 3. Try one 10¢: 10 + 5 + 5 = 20, too little. Only one way: 10 + 10 + 5.

Check TWO things every time: the total, and the number of coins.
