Fewest coins: biggest coin you can, again and again

Pay 65¢ with the fewest coins (5¢, 10¢, 20¢, 50¢).

Step 1. Biggest coin that fits: 50¢. 15¢ is left.

Step 2. Biggest coin that fits 15¢: 10¢. 5¢ is left.

Step 3. Then 5¢. Nothing left. 50 + 10 + 5 = 65: 3 coins.

Math Inquirers Level 1 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q3). Ask after step 3: "Could 2 coins make 65¢?" (No: the two biggest, 50 + 20, make 70, and no pair makes exactly 65.)
