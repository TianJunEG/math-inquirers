Challenge

1. You have one 5¢, one 10¢, one 20¢ and one 50¢ coin. How many different amounts can you make using any of them?

2. A sweet costs 15¢ and a biscuit costs 20¢. Ben spends exactly 90¢ and buys some of each. How many sweets and biscuits does he buy?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use as a pair challenge or in talk time. Ask for the reason every time. More are in the question bank. Answers: 1) 15 different amounts. 2) 2 sweets and 3 biscuits.
