Think harder

1. How many ways can you make 40¢ with 20¢, 10¢ and 5¢ coins?

2. Pay exactly $12 using exactly 3 notes or coins from $1, $2, $5 and $10. Find every way.

3. What is the fewest number of coins for 95¢ (5¢, 10¢, 20¢, 50¢)? How do you know fewer is not possible?

For any pair that chooses to try more (these are on the slide only; Think harder T1 to T4 are in the workbook). Answers: 1) 9 ways (two 20¢: 1 way; one 20¢: 3 ways; no 20¢: 5 ways). 2) 2 ways: $10 + $1 + $1, and $5 + $5 + $2. 3) 4 coins: 50 + 20 + 20 + 5. Three coins cannot work: with a 50¢ coin you need 45¢ from two coins (no pair makes 45¢), and without it three coins make at most 60¢.
