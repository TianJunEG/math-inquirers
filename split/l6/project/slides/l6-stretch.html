For early finishers

Stretch

1. How many ways can you make 40¢ with 20¢, 10¢ and 5¢ coins?

2. Mei has 6 coins, all 5¢ and 10¢, worth 45¢. How many of each?

3. What is the fewest number of coins for 95¢ (5¢, 10¢, 20¢, 50¢)?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 9 ways (two 20¢: 1 way; one 20¢: 3 ways; no 20¢: 5 ways). 2) Three 10¢ and three 5¢. 3) 4 coins: 50 + 20 + 20 + 5 (3 coins cannot work: you need a 5¢, and no two coins make 90¢).
