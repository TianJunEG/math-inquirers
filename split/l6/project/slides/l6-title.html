LESSON 6 • WEEK 3

Coin Combos

Money

TODAY WE WILL

Make an amount in every way, work from the biggest coin down, and say how we know we have them all.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find all the ways to make an amount, work systematically, and use reasoning to rule out cases. Materials: Play coins (5¢, 10¢, 20¢, 50¢), about 6 of each per pair, mini whiteboards. Online: Print the cut-out coin sheet at the back of the workbook, or use real coins at home. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
