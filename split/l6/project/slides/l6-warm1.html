Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Money Shop, cents only. Hand out play coins (5¢, 10¢, 20¢, 50¢). Point to one item at a time: "The pencil costs 80¢. Pay exactly with your coins." Any way that pays exactly is a good way. Then ask: "Can you find a way with fewer coins?" Do not ask who used the fewest. Click to show one way with the fewest coins: Eraser 65¢ = 50¢ + 10¢ + 5¢ (3 coins). Pencil 80¢ = 50¢ + 20¢ + 10¢ (3 coins). Sticker 30¢ = 20¢ + 10¢ (2 coins).
