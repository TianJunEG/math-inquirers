Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. The Coin Shop. Hand out play coins (5¢, 10¢, 20¢, 50¢). Point to one item at a time: "The pencil costs 15¢. Pay exactly. Can you pay with fewer coins than your partner?" Children lay their coins out, then you click to show the fewest-coins way for that item (clicks reveal Sweet, Pencil, Eraser, Stickers, Ruler, Juice box in order). Fewest coins: Sweet 5¢ = 5¢ (1 coin). Pencil 15¢ = 10¢ + 5¢ (2). Eraser 20¢ = 20¢ (1). Stickers 35¢ = 20¢ + 10¢ + 5¢ (3). Ruler 45¢ = 20¢ + 20¢ + 5¢ (3). Juice box 50¢ = 50¢ (1). Ask: "Someone paid 15¢ with three 5¢ coins. Is that right?" (Yes, it is exactly 15¢, just not the fewest.) "Why does the 50¢ coin win for the juice box?" Quick finisher: "Buy the pencil AND the eraser. How much? Fewest coins?" (35¢: 20¢ + 10¢ + 5¢, 3 coins). Watch for: children who count the number of coins instead of the value.
