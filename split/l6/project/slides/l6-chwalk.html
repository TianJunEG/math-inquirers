Q1: count by number of coins

1 coin: 5, 10, 20, 50 → 4 amounts

2 coins: 15, 25, 55, 30, 60, 70 → 6

3 coins: 35, 65, 75, 80 → 4

4 coins: 85 → 1. Total: 15 ✓

Q2: try biscuits 1, 2, 3 …

1 biscuit: 70¢ left. Not a 15¢ amount ✗

2 biscuits: 50¢ left ✗

3 biscuits: 30¢ left = 2 sweets ✓

4 biscuits: 10¢ left ✗. Answer: 2 sweets, 3 biscuits
