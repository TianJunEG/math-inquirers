Ways to make 25¢ (coins: 20¢, 10¢, 5¢)

Start with the biggest coin and work down.

With one 20¢: 20 + 5.

With no 20¢ but 10¢: 10 + 10 + 5, 10 + 5 + 5 + 5.

No 10¢ or 20¢: 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 + 5.

How many ways altogether?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example 2. Ask children to explain each step in their own words. Start with the biggest coin and work down. Use a table: how many 20¢, 10¢, 5¢. Show why we know when we have them all. Answer: 4 ways.
