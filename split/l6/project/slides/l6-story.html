Story break: Isaac Newton

Optional • about 2 minutes • we can skip it

Isaac Newton is famous for his big ideas in science. But for many years he also had a job at the Royal Mint, which is where English coins were made. He started there in 1696, when old silver coins were being worn out and replaced with new ones. The people at the Mint cared about every coin having the right weight and the right amount of metal. Newton worked there until 1727.

Isaac Newton

England • 1642-1727
