Swap coins to reach the total

5 coins, only 5¢ and 10¢, worth 35¢. How many of each?

Step 1. Start with all 5¢: five 5¢ = 25¢. We need 10¢ more.

Step 2. Each swap of a 5¢ for a 10¢ adds 5¢. 25 → 30 → 35: 2 swaps.

Step 3. So two 10¢ and three 5¢. Check: 20 + 15 = 35, 5 coins ✓

Optional teaching for early finishers, before Think Harder T2 (Sam's 90¢). Act it out with play coins: lay out five 5¢, then swap one at a time and count the total aloud.
