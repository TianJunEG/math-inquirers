7–10 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. A guess is a great start. Your own paper: show only your tutor.

Anyone can try this one: use counters, draw or count

1. Ben has a 20¢ coin, a 10¢ coin and a 5¢ coin. Draw them. Can he pay 25¢ exactly?

Yes: 20¢ + 5¢

Now think about this one

2. Show one way to make 15¢ with 5¢ and 10¢ coins. Can you find a different way?

10¢ + 5¢, or 5¢ + 5¢ + 5¢

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 7 to 10 (3 minutes). SAY: "These two are sneak-peek puzzles. They are ideas we have not learnt yet, so a guess is a great start, and I do not know is welcome. Nobody is marked." Question 1 is an access question: every child can try it with objects, a drawing or counting, so everyone starts with a win. Question 2 makes you think. Answers are shown privately: face to face, each child holds their own paper low so only the tutor sees it; online, children send the answer to the tutor in a private chat message (a parent may type for a child who cannot type yet). Never use boards held up in front of the group, and never read out or compare anyone's answer. Do not write scores down or announce them. Click to show each answer to the whole group only after everyone has had a try, and say "Lots of us had a different idea, and that is fine." Quietly note who found question 2 easy; you may invite a child to the Think harder page by a PRIVATE message at minute 40, never by name in front of the group. Everyone joins Explore and the Mini-lesson. Answers: 1) Yes: 20¢ + 5¢. 2) 10¢ + 5¢, and 5¢ + 5¢ + 5¢. Those are the only two ways.
