Challenge

1. In a multiplication square, the top row of a 2 by 2 block shows 42 and 48. The row below shows 49 and 56. What are the two row numbers and the two column numbers?

2. Ben writes the answers of the 6 times table from 6 × 1 to 6 × 20. How many of the answers end in 8?

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) Rows 6 and 7, columns 7 and 8. Start with 49: it can only be 7 × 7. 2) 4 answers: 18, 48, 78, 108. The last digits 6, 2, 8, 4, 0 repeat every 5, so 8 comes once in every 5 answers.
