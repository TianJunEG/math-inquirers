Notice it: the last digits go 8, 6, 4, 2, 0 and then repeat.

Test it: 8 × 11 = 88 and 8 × 12 = 96. Do they carry on the pattern? (8, 6: yes!)

Explain why: adding 8 is the same as adding 10 and taking away 2, so the ones digit goes down by 2.

Math Inquirers Level 3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach the first heuristic: LOOK FOR A PATTERN. Say: "Mathematicians notice a pattern, test it, then explain why it works. That is our slogan!" Click 1 shows the last digits. Ask: "What do you notice?" before click 2. Click 3: test the pattern on numbers beyond the table. Click 4: explain why: 8 = 10 − 2, so every time we add 8 the ones digit goes down by 2 (and the tens digit goes up). Think-aloud: "Look at the 8 times table: 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48 … The last digits go 8, 6, 4, 2, 0, then again. So the pattern repeats every 5." Do not teach the 9s patterns here: children discover them in worksheet Q1.
