For early finishers

Stretch

1. Which is more, 7 × 8 or 6 × 9? By how much?

2. The last digit of 9 × 9 is 1. What is the last digit of 9 × 9 × 9 × 9?

3. Ben multiplies a number by 6. The answer ends in 4. What could the last digit of his number be? Find all.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) 7 × 8 = 56 is 2 more than 6 × 9 = 54. 2) 1: the last digits go 9, 1, 9, 1 (9 × 9 × 9 × 9 = 6561). 3) 4 or 9: 6 × 4 = 24 and 6 × 9 = 54; check every digit 0 to 9, no other one gives a last digit of 4.
