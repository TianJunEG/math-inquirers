30.5–32.5 MIN

Use facts you know: split the array

Step 1. 6 × 8 is 6 rows of 8. Cut off 5 rows.

Step 2. 5 rows of 8: 5 × 8 = 40

Step 3. 1 more row of 8: 1 × 8 = 8

Step 4. Add the parts: 40 + 8 = 48. So 6 × 8 = 48.

Fives are easy facts. Split a hard fact into a five fact and a small fact, then ADD.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach: use facts you know. Show the array on dot paper (or draw it). Cover the bottom row with a ruler: "How many dots above the ruler? 5 rows of 8 is 40. How many below? One row of 8. Altogether 40 + 8 = 48." Click through the steps. Common slip: giving 40 as the answer and forgetting to add the second part. Ask: "Where else could I cut it?" (3 rows and 3 rows: 24 + 24 = 48).
