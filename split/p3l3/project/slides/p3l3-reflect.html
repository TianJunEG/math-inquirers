What was hard?

One minute. Open the last page of today's lesson. Tick or write. There are no right answers.

1. The hardest part was ...

For example: remembering the facts, spotting the pattern, explaining why, or something else.

2. I got unstuck by ...

For example: drawing a picture, making a list, trying a number and checking, asking or listening.

3. Next time I will try ...

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 88 to 90. Look-back. Say: "Turn to the last page of today's lesson and find What was hard? Tick or write one thing for each sentence. There are no right answers." Take one volunteer to share (the hardest part, or what got them unstuck). Never collect it, mark it or grade it. Praise the strategy ("you drew it and that got you unstuck"), not the speed or the answer. If a child ticked "I am still stuck", plan two minutes of catch-up at the start of the next lesson.
