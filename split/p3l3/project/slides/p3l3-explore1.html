Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Draw 10 dots round a circle. Label them 0 to 9, like a clock.
  2. Write the 6 times table. Circle the last digit of each answer.
  3. Join the last digits in order: 6 → 2 → 8 → 4 → 0 → back to 6.
  4. Now do the same for the 4s and the 8s. Compare the shapes.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs work with: dot paper or plain paper, coloured pencils, a ruler. Each pair draws three digit circles. Model the first two lines of the 6s on the slide: 6 × 1 = 6, so start at 6; 6 × 2 = 12, last digit 2, so draw a line from 6 to 2. Then pairs finish the 6s and do the 4s and the 8s with a different colour each. Online: children draw on paper and hold the circles up to the camera. Early finishers: try the 7s and the 3s (they make the same 10-pointed star, which uses every digit).
