1.5–5 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. Have a go: guessing and “I do not know” are welcome. Nobody is marked.

Write both answers on your own paper. When your tutor says “show me”, hold the paper up once for both.

Puzzle 1. Here is the 7 times table: 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, 56, 63, 70. Which of these numbers are in it: 49, 54, 63?

Try it with objects, a drawing or counting.

Puzzle 2. You know 6 × 7 = 42. Use it to work out 6 × 14 and 6 × 6. Say how you did it.

Think about it. Show how you thought.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 1.5 to 5. Sneak-peek puzzles: two small puzzles on ideas we have not learnt yet. Give about 1 minute for each puzzle, then say "show me" ONCE for both: children hold up their paper with both answers in one camera hold-up (about 30 seconds). SAY: "These are ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome and so is I do not know. Nobody is marked." Puzzle 1 is an ACCESS puzzle: any child can try it with objects, a drawing or counting, so everyone has a first win. Puzzle 2 is a thinking puzzle. Children write both answers on their own paper and show them only to the tutor on camera (or everybody holds paper to the camera at the same moment, once for both puzzles). No private messages. Never answers read out, never "who got it?". Do not show the answers on the slide now. Puzzle 1 answer (tutor only): 49 and 63. Listen for: Look along the table: 49 and 63 are there; 54 is not. Puzzle 2 answer (tutor only): 84 and 36. Listen for: 14 is double 7, so double 42 = 84. 6 × 6 is one 6 less than 6 × 7: 42 − 6 = 36. Come back to puzzle 2 at talk time: "Now we know the idea, how could it help?" (volunteers only). Invite the WHOLE group once, never one child; this is an invitation, not a ranking, and any child may choose Think harder or the Investigation after question 3 of page 1. Never announce who is on which page, and never split pairs unless both children agree.
