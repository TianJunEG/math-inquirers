Worked example: the last digit

What is the last digit of 8 × 23? Do not work out the whole answer.

Step 1. Split 23 into 20 and 3. 8 × 20 = 160 ends in 0.

Step 2. So only 8 × 3 decides the last digit. 8 × 3 = 24 ends in 4.

Step 3. The last digit of 8 × 23 is 4.

Step 4. Check: 8 × 23 = 160 + 24 = 184. It ends in 4 ✓

Think-aloud: "8 × 20 ends in 0, so only 8 × 3 matters. 8 × 3 = 24, so 8 × 23 ends in 4. Check: 8 × 23 = 184. Yes." Then ask: "What is the last digit of 8 × 33? 8 × 43?" (still 4).
