Times Table Treasure

Tables of 6, 7, 8 and 9

TODAY WE WILL

Find hidden patterns in the 6, 7, 8 and 9 times tables, use them to check answers, and solve times-table puzzles in order.

TODAY'S TOOL

Spot the pattern

Focus habit: Wonder

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find and explain patterns in the 6, 7, 8 and 9 times tables, use last-digit patterns, split an array into facts we know, and solve grid and list puzzles in a careful order. Heuristics taught: look for a pattern; make a systematic list. Materials: Multiplication square (1 to 10), dot paper, coloured pencils, a ruler, mini whiteboards. Online: Tutor draws on the digit-circle slide. Each child draws 10 dots round a circle (like a clock) labelled 0 to 9, and has dot paper and coloured pencils. Time plan (90 minutes, with a named 3-minute float inside talk time): opening 0 to 1.5, sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 5 (own paper, nobody marked, one show-me for both), warm-up game 5 to 8 (two rounds) and three quick questions 8 to 11, explore 11 to 25.5 (a Go further what-if is on the explore slide from the start; pairs go to pre-assigned breakout rooms), teaching 25.5 to 36.5 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 36.5 to 41.5, worksheet 41.5 to 70.5 (page 1 in pairs, short teaching, page 2 on their own, a 60-second self-check slide after each page; after question 3 of page 1 any child may choose Think harder or the Investigation; from minute 67.5 everyone chooses), talk time 70.5 to 78.5 (volunteers first, pass is fine), exit ticket 78.5 to 84.5, look back 84.5 to 87.5, stamp and close 87.5 to 90. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child; the tutor never announces who is on which page and invites the whole group, never one child. If the class is behind, children finish the last question of each page at home; the check slides cover it. SPINE: Today's tool is Spot the pattern. Look for what repeats or changes by the same amount, then say the rule. Look for where you use it. Point at the card on page 1 of the workbook. HABIT: Focus habit: Wonder. Invite a question or a what-if before any answer is given. Say: "What could we ask? What if we changed one thing?" Do not give the answer first: ask the prompt, wait, and give a hint only if the child is stuck.
