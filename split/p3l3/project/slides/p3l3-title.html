Find hidden patterns in the 6, 7, 8 and 9 times tables, use them to check answers, and solve times-table puzzles in order.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find and explain patterns in the 6, 7, 8 and 9 times tables, use last-digit patterns, split an array into facts we know, and solve grid and list puzzles in a careful order. Heuristics taught: look for a pattern; make a systematic list. Materials: Multiplication square (1 to 10), dot paper, coloured pencils, a ruler, mini whiteboards. Online: Tutor draws on the digit-circle slide. Each child draws 10 dots round a circle (like a clock) labelled 0 to 9, and has dot paper and coloured pencils. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
