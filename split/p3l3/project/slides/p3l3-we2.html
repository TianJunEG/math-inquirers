Worked example: 9 × 7 two ways

Find 9 × 7 using facts you know.

Step 1. Way 1: split 9 rows of 7 into 5 rows and 4 rows. 5 × 7 = 35, 4 × 7 = 28

Step 2. Add the parts: 35 + 28 = 63

Step 3. Way 2: 10 rows of 7 is 70. Take away one row of 7: 70 − 7 = 63

Step 4. Both ways give 63 ✓ So 9 × 7 = 63.

Click through. Way 2 is the "one too many" trick: 9 of something is 10 of it, take one away. Think-aloud: "For 9 × 7 I can do 10 sevens, which is 70, and take one 7 away: 63. I check it the other way: 35 + 28 is also 63." Two ways that agree is a strong check.
