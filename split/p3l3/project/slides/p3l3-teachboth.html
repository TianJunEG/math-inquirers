Two clues: list one, test the other

I am a 2-digit number. I can be divided exactly by 7 and by 3. My digits add up to 3.

Step 1. List the 2-digit numbers you can divide by 7: 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, 56, 63, 70, 77, 84, 91, 98

Step 2. Test each one: which can be divided by 3? 21, 42, 63, 84

Step 3. Add the digits: 2 + 1 = 3 ✓ (42 → 6, 63 → 9, 84 → 12) I am 21.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet Q6. Strategy: list the numbers for ONE clue in order, then test the other clues one at a time. Ask: "Why list the 7s, not the 3s?" (There are fewer of them, so the list is shorter.) Children may notice 21, 42, 63, 84 go up in 21s. Do not do Q6 (6 and 8) here.
