Rows and columns use numbers from 1 to 10.

Step 1. 63 can only be 7 × 9. 72 can only be 8 × 9. They share the first column, so it is 9.

Step 2. Then the rows are 8 (8 × 9 = 72) and 7 (7 × 9 = 63).

Step 3. 42 = 7 × 6, so the second column is 6.

Step 4. Fill the gap: 8 × 6 = 48. Check every number ✓

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4 is a 3 by 3 grid puzzle). Ask first: "Which number can be made in only ONE way with numbers from 1 to 10?" Children list factor pairs: 72 = 8 × 9 only; 63 = 7 × 9 only; 42 = 6 × 7 only. Click order: steps 1 to 3, then the finished grid fades in, then step 4. On the worksheet, do NOT tell them where to start; the hint says to look for a number made in only one way.
