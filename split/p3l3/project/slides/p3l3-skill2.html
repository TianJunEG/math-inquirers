Item 1 (Counting multiples in a range; tool of the day idea: Try something simpler). SAY: "Count to the top, then take away the ones that come too early." ASK: "How do you find how many 4s fit into 150? Which numbers did you take away?" LISTEN FOR: "Divide by 4, ignore the remainder". ANSWER: a) 37 (150 / 4 is 37 remainder 2). b) 12 (49 / 4 is 12 remainder 1). c) 37 - 12 = 25.
