5–8 MIN

Warm-up: Buzz!

Count round the group. Say “Buzz” instead of any number in the 7 times table.

Round 2: Buzz on the 6s, up to 60. Round 3: Buzz on the 8s, up to 80.

7s Buzz numbers: 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, 56, 63, 70

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 8. Buzz! Count round the group (online: in the order of the names on screen) from 1 to 70. Any number in the 7 times table is replaced by "Buzz". If someone says the number instead of Buzz, or Buzzes on the wrong number, the count starts again from 1 (keep it light). Click to show all the 7s Buzz numbers at the end and read them together. Then play again with 6s (to 60) and 8s (to 80). Ask: "Which numbers were Buzz in BOTH the 6s round and the 8s round?" (24 and 48). "Was any 7s Buzz number also a 6s Buzz number?" (42). Watch for children who count on their fingers from 0 each time: encourage them to add 7 to the last Buzz.
