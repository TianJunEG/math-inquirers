Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 8. Buzz! Count round the group (online: in the order of the names on screen) from 1 to 40. Any number in the 7 times table is replaced by an open palm held up to the camera (and the word "Buzz" if you like): the 7s strip stays on the screen, so nobody has to remember the table. Play it light and kind. If someone misses a Buzz or says the number, say "That is a tricky one, let us check on the strip" and carry on from that number; never start again from 1 and never name who missed. Click to show the 7s Buzz numbers at the end and read them together. Only the 7s are played here (the 6s and 8s rounds are not needed today). Ask volunteers: "What do you notice about the Buzz numbers?" (they go up by 7 each time; the last digits are 7, 4, 1, 8, 5). Watch for children who count on their fingers from 0 each time: encourage them to add 7 to the last Buzz.
