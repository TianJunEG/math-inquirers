Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Name that unit. Read each card aloud; children write the unit on whiteboards and hold them up together, then click to show (the cards reveal in order). Ask each time: "Why not the other units?" A bus is 12 m (12 cm is a finger, 12 km is a long trip). A pencil is 15 cm. An MRT ride of about 15 km. Rice 5 kg. An eraser about 20 g. Medicine 5 ml. A fish tank about 40 ℓ. A watermelon about 3 kg. Quick finisher: "Put these in order: 1 m, 90 cm, 1 km." (90 cm, 1 m, 1 km). Watch for children who mix up mass and volume units (kg for a drink).
