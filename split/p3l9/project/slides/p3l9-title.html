LESSON 9 • WEEK 5

Measure and Money Masters

Length, mass, volume, money and time

TODAY WE WILL

Change to one unit, plan a budget, and draw a time line for durations and the 24-hour clock.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Change compound units (m and cm, kg and g, ℓ and ml, $ and ¢) to one unit to compare, add and subtract; plan a budget; find durations, start and end times by drawing a time line, in 12-hour and 24-hour time. Heuristics taught: (1) change to one unit; (2) draw a time line and jump to the next o’clock. Materials: Party menu cards (one per pair), play money, a metre ruler or measuring tape, a kitchen scale and a measuring jug if available, a clock with movable hands, mini whiteboards. Online: Show the menu card on screen; children use paper and pencil. Pairs work in breakout rooms and type their best party plan in the chat. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (change to one unit, then the time line; click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
