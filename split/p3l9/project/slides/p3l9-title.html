Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Change compound units (m and cm, kg and g, ℓ and ml, $ and ¢) to one unit to compare, add and subtract; plan a budget; find durations, start and end times by drawing a time line, in 12-hour and 24-hour time. Heuristics taught: (1) change to one unit; (2) draw a time line and jump to the next o’clock (taught in a short lesson after page 1). Materials: Party menu cards (one per pair), play money, a metre ruler or measuring tape, a kitchen scale and a measuring jug if available, a clock with movable hands, mini whiteboards. Online: Show the menu card on screen; children use paper and pencil. Pairs work in breakout rooms and type their best party plan in the chat. Time plan (90 minutes, with a named 3-minute float inside talk time): opening 0 to 1.5, sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 4.5 (own paper, nobody marked, one show-me for both), warm-up game 4.5 to 7.5 (two rounds) and three quick questions 7.5 to 10.5, explore 10.5 to 25.5 (a Go further what-if is on the explore slide from the start; pairs go to pre-assigned breakout rooms), teaching 25.5 to 34 (change to one unit; the time line is taught after page 1; click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 34 to 39, worksheet 39 to 70 (page 1 in pairs, short teaching, page 2 on their own, a 60-second self-check slide after each page; after question 3 of page 1 any child may choose Think harder or the Investigation; from minute 67 everyone chooses), talk time 70 to 78.5 (volunteers first, pass is fine), exit ticket 78.5 to 84.5, look back 84.5 to 87.5, stamp and close 87.5 to 90. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child; the tutor never announces who is on which page and invites the whole group, never one child. If the class is behind, children finish the last question of each page at home; the check slides cover it. SPINE: Today's tool is Make a table. Make a table, fill it row by row and look down the columns. Look for where you use it. Point at the card on page 1 of the workbook.
