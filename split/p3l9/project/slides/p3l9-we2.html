Worked example: when does it end?

A movie starts at 2:50 p.m. and lasts 1 h 45 min. When does it end?

Step 1. Split the 45 minutes: 10 minutes takes us to 3:00, and 35 minutes are left.

2:50 + 10 min = 3:00, + 1 h = 4:00, + 35 min = 4:35 p.m.
