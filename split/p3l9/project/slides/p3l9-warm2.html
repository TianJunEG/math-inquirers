Answers: 1) 140 cm (100 + 40). 2) 3000 g. 3) 2050 ml: watch for 250 ml, the most common mistake; 2 ℓ is 2000 ml and 50 more. 4) 80 minutes (60 + 20), not 120. 5) 14:00 (2 + 12).
