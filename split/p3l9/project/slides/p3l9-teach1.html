Change to ONE unit

1 m = 100 cm • 1 kg = 1000 g • 1 ℓ = 1000 ml • $1 = 100¢

2 m 35 cm

1 kg 250 g = 1000 g + 250 g = 1250 g

1 ℓ 300 ml = 1000 ml + 300 ml = 1300 ml

$3.45 = 300¢ + 45¢ = 345¢

Careful! 2 m 5 cm = 205 cm, not 25 cm. 3 kg 40 g = 3040 g.

Why? In one unit we can compare, add and take away like normal numbers.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach heuristic 1: change to one unit. Hold up a metre ruler: "This is 1 m, 100 cm." Point to the bar: 2 m is 200 cm, and 35 cm more is 235 cm. Click the three rows: each time, the big unit becomes the small unit and we add the rest. Click "Careful!": "How do I write 2 m 5 cm in cm? Some people write 25. Is that right? 2 m alone is 200 cm, so it is 205." Same for 3 kg 40 g = 3040 g, not 340 g. Click "Why?": "Once everything is in the same unit, the numbers behave like the numbers we already know." Going back: 1650 g = 1000 g + 650 g = 1 kg 650 g.
