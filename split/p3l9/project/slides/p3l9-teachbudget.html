Budgets: is there enough money?

Ben has $15. A kite costs $6.85, a ball $4.50 and a cap $3.95. Can he buy all three?

Step 1. Estimate: about $7 + $4.50 + $4 = $15.50. Very close, so work it out exactly.

Step 2. Change to cents: 685¢ + 450¢ + 395¢ = 1530¢ = $15.30

Step 3. Compare: $15.30 is more than $15. No: he is 30¢ short.

Spend the most with 2 things? Try the dearest first: kite + ball = $11.35 (change $3.65). Kite + cap = $10.80, ball + cap = $8.45.
