Q1: what changed?

Only water was poured out: 1200 g − 750 g = 450 g.

That was half the water, so all the water is 450 + 450 = 900 g.

Bottle = 1200 g − 900 g = 300 g.

Check: 300 + 450 = 750 g ✓

Q2: take away the extras

Each file = a pen + 60¢. Two files have 2 × 60¢ = $1.20 extra.

$9.20 − $1.20 = $8.00 is 5 equal pen bars.

800¢ ÷ 5 = 160¢. One pen costs $1.60.

A file: $2.20. Check: $4.80 + $4.40 = $9.20 ✓
