For early finishers

Stretch

1. A night train leaves at 22:40 and arrives at 01:15 the next morning. How long is the trip?

2. Siti’s home is 1 km 250 m from school. She walks there and back every day. How far does she walk in 2 days?

3. Ali needs 2 ℓ of milk. A 250 ml carton costs $1.10 and a 1 ℓ carton costs $3.60. What is the cheapest way?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) 2 h 35 min: 20 min to 23:00, 1 h to 00:00 (midnight), 1 h to 01:00, 15 min to 01:15. 2) 5 km: one day is 1250 + 1250 = 2500 m; two days 5000 m = 5 km. 3) Two 1 ℓ cartons: $7.20 (one 1 ℓ and four small cartons cost $3.60 + $4.40 = $8.00; eight small cartons cost $8.80).
