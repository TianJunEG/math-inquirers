54–55 MIN

Draw a time line: jump to the next o’clock

How long is it from 9:45 a.m. to 11:20 a.m.?

15 min + 1 h + 20 min = 1 h 35 min

Why not 11.20 − 9.45? An hour has 60 minutes, not 100. Time is not like normal numbers.

Jump to the next o’clock, then jump whole hours, then the minutes left.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach heuristic 2: draw a time line. Ask first: "How long is it from 9:45 to 11:20?" Collect guesses (someone may say 1 h 75 min or 2 h 25 min). Click the three jumps one at a time. Think-aloud: "From 9:45 I jump to the next o’clock: 15 minutes to 10:00. Then a whole hour to 11:00. Then 20 minutes to 11:20. Add the jumps: 15 + 20 = 35 minutes, and 1 hour." Click the "Why not" box: "If we subtract like normal numbers we treat an hour as 100 minutes. That is the trap." Show it on a real clock if you have one.
