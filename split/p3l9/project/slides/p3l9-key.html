Key idea: one unit, and a time line

Change to one unit before you compare, add or take away.

1 m = 100 cm • 1 kg = 1000 g • 1 ℓ = 1000 ml • $1 = 100¢ • 1 h = 60 min

After the break and page 1: we learn to draw a time line for time problems.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) 2 m 5 cm written as 25 cm, or 3 kg 40 g as 340 g. (2) Subtracting times like normal numbers (11.20 − 9.45), or counting 100 minutes in an hour. (3) 24-hour clock: adding 12 to am times or to 12:30 pm; reading 17:00 as 7 pm (4) Budgets: adding the dollars and forgetting to carry the cents (85¢ + 90¢ = $1.75).
