The 24-hour clock

Step 1. a.m.: same numbers, 2 digits for the hour. 6:05 a.m. → 06:05

Step 2. p.m.: add 12 to the hour. 4:20 p.m. → 16:20

Step 3. Back again: take away 12. 19:30 → 7:30 p.m.

Careful: 12:45 p.m. stays 12:45 (it is already after 12 noon). Midnight is 00:00.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4). "The 24-hour clock does not start again at 12 noon: it keeps counting, 13, 14, 15 … up to 23, then midnight is 00:00." Point along the strip. Click each step. Think-aloud: "4:20 p.m. is in the afternoon, so I add 12: 4 + 12 = 16, so 16:20. Going back: 19 − 12 = 7, so 7:30 p.m." Click the careful box: the hour from 12 noon to 1 p.m. is already 12, so we do not add 12. Quick check: "What is 15:00?" (3 p.m.) "What is 9:10 p.m.?" (21:10).
