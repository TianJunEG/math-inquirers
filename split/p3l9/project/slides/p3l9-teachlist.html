Timetables and going backwards

Trains leave every 15 minutes from 09:50. Mei reaches the platform at 10:27. How long does she wait?

09:50

10:05

10:20

10:35

List the times. The first one after 10:27 is 10:35. Wait: 3 min to 10:30 + 5 min = 8 min.

A film lasts 1 h 25 min and ends at 20:10. When did it start?

20:10 back 10 min → 20:00

20:00 back 1 h → 19:00

19:00 back 15 min → 18:45

Go backwards on the time line: back to the o’clock first.
