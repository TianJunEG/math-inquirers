Warm-up: arm bars

Show the two bars with your hands. Then draw them in 10 seconds.

1. Ali has 5 more than Ben.

2. Mei has 3 times as many as Siti.

3. Ravi and Ben have 20 altogether.

4. Siti has 4 fewer than Mei.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 8 (after the warm-up check). Arm bars. Read each sentence aloud. Children hold their two hands apart, one above the other, to show the two bars (online: in front of the camera), then draw the bars on whiteboards in 10 seconds. Click to show a model after each one (clicks reveal 1, 2, 3, 4 in order). Ask: "Whose bar is longer? How do you show the 5?" For 2: "How many equal parts does Mei have?" (3). For 3: "Altogether means we show the total with a bracket." For 4: "Fewer: Siti's bar is shorter. The dashed piece shows what she does not have." Watch for: children who draw "4 fewer" as a longer bar, and children who draw 3 times as many with 4 parts.
