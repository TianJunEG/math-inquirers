Challenge: walk-through

Q1: draw the bars BEFORE and AFTER

Before: Ben 1 unit, Ali 3 units, so 4 units = 120.

1 unit = 120 ÷ 4 = 30. Ben 30, Ali 90.

Ali gives 20: Ali 90 − 20 = 70, Ben 30 + 20 = 50.

Check: 70 + 50 = 120 ✓ (the total stays the same)

Q2: take away the extra

Take the $5 off the box: $25 − $5 = $20.

Now 4 equal pen bars make $20.

1 pen = $20 ÷ 4 = $5. The box is $10.

Check: $10 + 3 × $5 = $25 ✓
