Challenge: walk-through

Q1: draw the bars BEFORE and AFTER

Before: Ben 1 unit, Ali 3 units, so 4 units = 120. Ben 30, Ali 90.

After: Ali has 2 parts, Ben 1 part. The total is still 120, so 3 equal parts: 120 ÷ 3 = 40.

Now Ben has 40 and Ali has 80.

Ben went from 30 to 40, so Ali gave 10. Check: 80 + 40 = 120 ✓

Q2: compare the two bills

Bill 2 has 2 more pens than Bill 1.

It costs $35 − $25 = $10 more. So 2 pens cost $10.

1 pen = $10 ÷ 2 = $5.

3 pens cost $15, so the box is $25 − $15 = $10. Check: $10 + 5 × $5 = $35 ✓
