Before and after: what stays the same?

Mei has $30 and Siti has $12. Their aunt gives each girl $5. How much more does Mei have than Siti before? How much more after?

Step 1. Before: $30 − $12 = $18. After: Mei $35, Siti $17, and $35 − $17 = $18.

Step 2. Both get the same, so the difference stays the same: $18.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach the second heuristic: BEFORE AND AFTER, look for what stays the same. Before worksheet Q6. Ask: "When both girls get $5, what happens to the gap between them?" Show with two cube towers, add 2 cubes to each: the gap does not change. Then click the model. Check aloud: Siti $12 + $5 = $17, Mei $30 + $5 = $35, and $35 − $17 = $18, the same gap as before. Compare with Q5: when one gives to the other, the TOTAL stays the same; when both get the same, the DIFFERENCE stays the same.
