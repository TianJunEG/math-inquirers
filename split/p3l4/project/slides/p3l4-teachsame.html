Before and after: what stays the same?

Mei has $30 and Siti has $12. Their aunt gives each girl the same amount. Now Mei has twice as much as Siti. How much did each girl get?

Step 1. Both get the same, so the difference stays the same: $30 − $12 = $18.

Step 2. Mei 2 units, Siti 1 unit, so 1 unit = $18. Siti now has $18: she got $6.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach the second heuristic: BEFORE AND AFTER, look for what stays the same. Before worksheet Q6. Ask: "When both girls get $5, what happens to the gap between them?" Show with two cube towers, add 2 cubes to each: the gap does not change. Then click the model. Check aloud: Siti $12 + $6 = $18, Mei $30 + $6 = $36, and $36 is twice $18. Compare with Q5: when one gives to the other, the TOTAL stays the same; when both get the same, the DIFFERENCE stays the same.
