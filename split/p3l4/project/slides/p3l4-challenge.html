1. Ali and Ben have 120 stickers altogether. Ali has 3 times as many as Ben. Ali gives some stickers to Ben, and then Ali has only twice as many as Ben. How many stickers does Ali give to Ben?

2. A pencil box and 3 pens cost $25 altogether. A pencil box and 5 pens cost $35 altogether. How much does one pen cost? How much does the pencil box cost?

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) 10. At first Ben 1 unit, Ali 3 units; 4 units = 120, so Ben 30 and Ali 90. The total is still 120, but now Ali has 2 parts and Ben 1 part, so 3 equal parts = 120 and each part is 40: Ben 40 and Ali 80. Ben went from 30 to 40, so Ali gave 10. 2) Pen $5, box $10. The second bill is $10 more because it has 2 more pens, so 2 pens cost $10 and 1 pen costs $5. Then 3 pens cost $15 and the box costs $10.
