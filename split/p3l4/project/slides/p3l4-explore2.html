3. Could you make "3 times as tall" with 22 cubes? Why or why not?

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Gather the pairs. Take ideas, then show with cubes. Answers: 1) Take away the extra 4 (16 left), share 16 into two equal towers of 8, then put the 4 back: 8 and 12. Check 8 + 12 = 20 and 12 − 8 = 4. 2) 5 and 15: together the towers make 4 equal parts of 5 (1 part and 3 parts). 3) No: we need 4 equal parts, and 22 cannot be shared into 4 equal parts (5 each leaves 2 over). Then say: "Cubes are great, but big numbers need too many cubes. Today we draw the towers as bars: bar models."
