Your turn: part-whole

Draw one bar for the whole first.

a A rope is 900 cm long. 275 cm is cut off. How long is the rest?

625 cm

b Ali has 1200 stamps: 450 from Singapore, 380 from Malaysia, the rest from Japan. How many from Japan?

370

c A shop sold 368 cups on Saturday and 245 cups on Sunday. How many cups in all?

613

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards: draw the bar, then the answer. a) 900 − 275 = 625 cm. b) 1200 − 450 − 380 = 370. c) Here the parts are known and the whole is the ?: 368 + 245 = 613. Ask: "Where does the ? go this time?"
