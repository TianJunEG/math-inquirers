A bar for each. Who has more? By how much?

a Mei has 125 beads. Ali has 60 more. How many beads does Ali have?

185

b Ben has 342 marbles. Ravi has 87 fewer. How many do they have altogether?

597

c Box A has 500 g of rice. Box B has 135 g less. How much more rice is in Box A?

135 g

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) 125 + 60 = 185. b) Ravi 342 − 87 = 255, altogether 342 + 255 = 597 (two steps). c) A trick question: the model shows the difference is already given, 135 g. Nobody needs to work out Box B. Praise children who spotted it from their model.
