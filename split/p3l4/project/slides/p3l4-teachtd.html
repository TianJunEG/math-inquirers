Total and difference

Mei and Siti have 90 beads altogether. Mei has 16 more beads than Siti. How many beads does each girl have?

Step 1. Take away the extra 16: 90 − 16 = 74. Now the two bars are equal.

Step 2. Share: 74 ÷ 2 = 37. Siti has 37.

Step 3. Mei: 37 + 16 = 53. Check: 37 + 53 = 90 ✓
