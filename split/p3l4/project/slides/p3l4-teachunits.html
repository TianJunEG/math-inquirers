Times as many: equal units

Ben has 4 times as many stamps as Ali. They have 175 stamps altogether. How many stamps does Ben have?

Step 1. Ali 1 unit, Ben 4 units: 1 + 4 = 5 units = 175

Step 2. 1 unit = 175 ÷ 5 = 35

Step 3. Ben = 4 units = 4 × 35 = 140. Check: 35 + 140 = 175 ✓

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 1 (Q3 is times as many). "4 times as many" means Ben's bar is 4 of Ali's bars. Ask before step 1: "How many units altogether?" Many children say 4. Point to the model and count: Ali's 1 and Ben's 4 make 5. Then share 175 into 5 equal units (175 ÷ 5 = 35).
