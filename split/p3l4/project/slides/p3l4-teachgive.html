Before and after: give to make equal

Ali has 64 cards. Ben has 30 cards. How many cards must Ali give Ben so that they have the same number?

Step 1. Difference: 64 − 30 = 34. Ali gives HALF of it: 34 ÷ 2 = 17

Step 2. Check: Ali 64 − 17 = 47, Ben 30 + 17 = 47 ✓ The total, 94, stays the same.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet Q5. Act it out first with cubes: 6 and 2. "How many must I move to make them equal?" Many say 4 (the difference). Try it: 6 − 4 = 2 and 2 + 4 = 6, they just swapped!
