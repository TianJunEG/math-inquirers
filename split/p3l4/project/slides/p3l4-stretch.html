For early finishers

Stretch

1. A bag of rice and a bag of flour weigh 9 kg altogether. The rice is 3 kg heavier. How heavy is each bag?

2. Ben had 3 times as many marbles as Ravi. Ben gave Ravi 20 marbles, and then they had the same. How many did Ben have at first?

3. A bag of sugar and 2 bags of flour weigh 1700 g. Each bag of flour is 400 g heavier than the sugar. How heavy is the sugar?

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) Flour 3 kg, rice 6 kg (9 − 3 = 6, 6 ÷ 2 = 3). 2) 60: the difference is 2 units and Ben gave half of it, so 1 unit = 20; Ravi had 20, Ben 60 (then 40 and 40). 3) 300 g: take away the two extras, 1700 − 800 = 900 for 3 equal units, 900 ÷ 3 = 300 (flour 700 g each).
