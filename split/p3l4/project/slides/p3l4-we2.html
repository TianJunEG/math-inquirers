Comparison model

Ravi has 236 cards. Siti has 167 more cards than Ravi. Ben has 95 fewer cards than Siti. How many cards does Ben have?

Ben has 308 cards. Check: 403 − 308 = 95 ✓

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click to build each bar. Before each click ask: "Is this bar longer or shorter? Than whose bar? By how much?" Think-aloud: "Siti has 167 MORE than Ravi, so her bar is Ravi's bar and an extra piece of 167: 236 + 167 = 403. Ben has 95 FEWER than Siti, so I compare Ben with Siti, not with Ravi. His bar is Siti's bar with 95 missing: 403 − 95 = 308." Common mistake: 236 − 95 (comparing with the wrong person). Point at the words "than Siti".
