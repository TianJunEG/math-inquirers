Part-whole model

A hall has 1500 seats. 645 are red, 480 are blue and the rest are green. How many seats are green?

Step 1. One whole cut into parts: draw ONE bar for 1500. Label the parts.

Step 2. Whole take away the parts: 1500 − 645 = 855, 855 − 480 = 375

Step 3. 375 seats are green. Check: 645 + 480 + 375 = 1500 ✓

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach the first heuristic: DRAW A MODEL. Click 1 builds the bar, then the steps. Ask before the bar appears: "Is this one thing cut into parts, or two things being compared?" (one whole: the hall). Think-aloud: "It is one whole cut into parts, so I draw ONE bar for 1500 and cut it into 645, 480 and a question mark. Whole take away the parts: 1500 − 645 = 855, 855 − 480 = 375. Check: 645 + 480 + 375 = 1500." Point out that the ? part looks a bit smaller than 480 on the model: a quick sense check.
