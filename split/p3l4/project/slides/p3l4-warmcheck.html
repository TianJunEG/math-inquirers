Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. Have a go: guessing and “I do not know” are welcome. Nobody is marked.

Show your answer privately: hold your paper up to your tutor, or send a private message.

Puzzle 1. Ali has 70 stickers and Ben has 30. Draw two bars, or use counters. How many stickers must Ali give Ben so that they have the same number?

Try it with objects, a drawing or counting.

Puzzle 2. A pen and a book cost $9 together. The book costs $5 more than the pen. How much is the pen?

Think about it. Show how you thought.

Minutes 2 to 5. Sneak-peek puzzles: two small puzzles on ideas we have not learnt yet. SAY: "These are ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome and so is I do not know. Nobody is marked." Puzzle 1 is an ACCESS puzzle: any child can try it with objects, a drawing or counting, so everyone has a first win. Puzzle 2 is a thinking puzzle. Children show their answers PRIVATELY: on their own paper held up to the tutor, or by private chat message. Never boards held up together, never answers read out, never "who got it?". Do not show the answers on the slide now. Puzzle 1 answer (tutor only): 20. Listen for: The difference is 40. Ali gives half of it: 20. Then both have 50. Puzzle 2 answer (tutor only): $2. Listen for: Take away the extra $5: 9 − 5 = 4. Two equal bars share 4, so the pen is $2 (the book is $7). Come back to puzzle 2 at talk time: "Now we know the idea, how could it help?" (volunteers only).
