Your turn: units

Draw the units. Count ALL the units.

a Mei has twice as many pens as Siti. They have 36 altogether. How many pens does Siti have?

12

b A cake costs 3 times as much as a drink. Together they cost $20. How much is the cake?

$15

c Ali has 5 times as many cards as Ben. Ali has 48 MORE than Ben. How many does Ben have?

12

Whiteboards. a) 3 units = 36, 1 unit = 12. b) 4 units = $20, 1 unit = $5, cake 3 units = $15. c) Different! The difference is 5 − 1 = 4 units = 48, so 1 unit = 12. Ask: "Why did we use 4 units in c, not 6?"
