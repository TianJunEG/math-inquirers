Model Makers

Word Problems: Bar Models

TODAY WE WILL

Draw bar models for word problems: parts of a whole, comparing, times as many, and before and after.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Draw bar models for part-whole, comparison, times-as-many and before-and-after word problems, and use them to solve two-step problems and check the answers. Heuristics taught: draw a model; before and after (look for what stays the same). Materials: Multilink cubes (about 30 per pair), strips of squared paper, scissors, mini whiteboards and markers. Online: Tutor builds the bars on the slides. Each child uses Lego bricks, coins or paper strips at home, and a whiteboard or paper to draw bars. Print the workbook before the lesson. Pairs work in one breakout room for 10 minutes, then return for the check slide. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90. If the class is behind, children finish the last question of each page at home; the check slides cover it.
