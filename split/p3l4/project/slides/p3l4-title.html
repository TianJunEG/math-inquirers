PRIMARY 3 • LESSON 4 • WEEK 2 • 0–1.5 MIN

Model Makers

Word Problems: Bar Models

TODAY WE WILL

Draw bar models for word problems: parts of a whole, comparing, times as many, and before and after.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Draw bar models for part-whole, comparison, times-as-many and before-and-after word problems, and use them to solve two-step problems and check the answers. Heuristics taught: draw a model; before and after (look for what stays the same). Materials: Multilink cubes (about 30 per pair), strips of squared paper, scissors, mini whiteboards and markers. Online: Tutor builds the bars on the slides. Each child uses Lego bricks, coins or paper strips at home, and a whiteboard or paper to draw bars. Print the workbook before the lesson. Pairs work in pre-assigned breakout rooms (auto-move in, auto-close with a timer) for about 9.5 minutes of page-1 work, then return for the 60-second self-check slide. Time plan (90 minutes, with a named 3-minute float inside talk time): opening 0 to 1.5, sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 5 (own paper, nobody marked, one show-me for both), warm-up game 5 to 8 (two rounds) and three quick questions 8 to 11, explore 11 to 25 (a Go further what-if is on the explore slide from the start; pairs go to pre-assigned breakout rooms), teaching 25 to 34.5 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 34.5 to 39.5, worksheet 39.5 to 71.5 (page 1 in pairs, short teaching, page 2 on their own, a 60-second self-check slide after each page; after question 3 of page 1 any child may choose Think harder or the Investigation; from minute 68.5 everyone chooses), talk time 71.5 to 79 (volunteers first, pass is fine), exit ticket 79 to 85, look back 85 to 88, stamp and close 88 to 90. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child; the tutor never announces who is on which page and invites the whole group, never one child. If the class is behind, children finish the last question of each page at home; the check slides cover it.
