Key idea: draw a model

Draw a bar for each amount. Label what you know and put ? on what you want to find.

Then ask: what stays the same? The total, or the difference?

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Comparing with the wrong person (Ben has 79 fewer than ALI, not Mei), or drawing "more than" as a shorter bar. (2) Times as many: dividing the total by 3 instead of by 3 + 1 = 4 units. (3) Make them equal: giving the whole difference instead of half of it.
