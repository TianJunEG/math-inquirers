Worked example: going down, with gaps

40, 36, __, 28, __, 20 Find the rule. Fill the gaps.

Step 1. The numbers get smaller, so the rule is take away.

Step 2. Use two neighbours we know: 40 to 36. Count back: 39, 38, 37, 36. Take away 4.

Step 3. Fill the gaps: 36 − 4 = 32, then 28 − 4 = 24.

Step 4. Check it lands on the numbers we were given: 32 − 4 = 28 ✓ 24 − 4 = 20 ✓

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click through the steps. Stress step 2: only use two numbers that sit next to each other. 36 and 28 are not neighbours, there is a gap between them. Think-aloud: "The numbers are getting smaller, so my rule is take away. From 40 to 36 I count back 4. Take away 4. 36 − 4 = 32. Then 32 − 4 = 28, and 28 is already there, so my rule works. 28 − 4 = 24 and 24 − 4 = 20, which is there too. My rule is take away 4." Common slip: writing "add 4" because the jump is 4. Ask: "Up or down?"
