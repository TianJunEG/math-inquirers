Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 10. Read each question aloud, give thinking time, take answers, then click to show the answer. For 4 ask: "Are we going up or down? So do we add or take away?" For 5, children can hop it on the number strip: 4 jumps of 5 land on 20, 4 jumps of 3 land on 12. Answers: 1) 8 2) 15 3) 12 4) 16 5) jumps of 5 (20 is further than 12).
