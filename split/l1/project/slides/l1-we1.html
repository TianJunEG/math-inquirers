Worked example: is 30 in the pattern?

2, 8, 14, 20, 26 … Is 30 in this pattern?

Step 1. The rule is add 6. Keep jumping: 26 + 6 = 32.

Step 2. The pattern goes 26, then 32. It jumps right over 30. So 30 is NOT in the pattern.

Step 3. Is 38 in it? 32 + 6 = 38. Yes!

A rule lets us test any number. We do not guess: we keep jumping.
