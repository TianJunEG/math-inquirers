Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Take your number strip (0 to 50), already cut out and taped end to end.
  2. Frog 1 starts on 0 and jumps in 4s. Mark each landing with a red cross.
  3. Frog 2 starts on 0 and jumps in 5s. Mark each landing with a blue circle.
  4. Write down the numbers that have BOTH a cross and a circle.

Go further: your choice, any time

What if frog 3 jumps in 10s from 0? Which numbers do frog 1 (4s) and frog 3 both land on?
