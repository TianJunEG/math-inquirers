Use two neighbours to find the rule. Then check it.

a 50, 45, __, 35, __

40, 30

b 3, __, 11, 15, __

7, 19

c 27, 25, 23, __, __

21, 19

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Own paper, shown only to the tutor. a) Take away 5 (50 to 45): 45 − 5 = 40, 35 − 5 = 30. b) The neighbours we know are 11 and 15: add 4. 3 + 4 = 7, check 7 + 4 = 11, then 15 + 4 = 19. c) Take away 2: 21, 19. Ask a child who got b right to explain which two numbers they used.
