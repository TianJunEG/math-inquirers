Item 1 (Sequences: each number is made from the two before; tool of the day idea: Spot the pattern). SAY: "Some patterns do not add the same number each time. Look at the gaps." ASK: "What is the gap between 1 and 2? Between 2 and 3? Does the gap stay the same? What do you notice about the gaps and the numbers?" LISTEN FOR: "The gaps are the numbers again" or "5 is 2 + 3". Accept "add the last two". Children who only say "it goes up more" need the gap row filled in first. ANSWER: a) The gaps are 1, 1, 2, 3, 5: they are the pattern numbers again. Each number is the two numbers before it added together (3 + 5 = 8). b) 13 and 21.
