Think harder

1. Ali counts from 3 in 4s: 3, 7, 11, … Will he say 39? Will he say 40?

2. 5, 10, 15, 22, 25, 30. One number is wrong. Which number does not belong? What should it be?

3. An add pattern has 11 as its 3rd number and 19 as its 5th number. What is the rule? What is the 1st number?

For any pair that chooses to try more (these are on the slide only; Think harder T1 to T4 are in the workbook). Answers: 1) Yes to 39, no to 40 (3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35, 39, 43: it lands on 39 and jumps over 40). 2) 22 does not belong. It should be 20 (the pattern adds 5). 3) Add 4: from the 3rd to the 5th number is two equal jumps that make 8, and 4 + 4 = 8. The 2nd number is 11 − 4 = 7 and the 1st is 7 − 4 = 3. Check: 3, 7, 11, 15, 19.
