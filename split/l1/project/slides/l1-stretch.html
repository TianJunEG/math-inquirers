For early finishers

Stretch

1. Ali counts from 3 in 4s: 3, 7, 11, … Will he say 39? Will he say 40?

2. 50, 46, 42, … What is the first number less than 10?

3. An add pattern has 11 as its 3rd number and 19 as its 5th number. What is the rule? What is the 1st number?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) Yes to 39, no to 40 (3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35, 39, 43: it lands on 39 and jumps over 40). 2) 6 (… 18, 14, 10, 6; 10 is not less than 10). 3) Add 4: from the 3rd to the 5th number is two equal jumps that make 8, and 4 + 4 = 8. The 2nd number is 11 − 4 = 7 and the 1st is 7 − 4 = 3. Check: 3, 7, 11, 15, 19.
