The rule is the jump

A number pattern follows a rule. The rule tells us how much we jump from one number to the next.

Check EVERY pair of neighbours. The rule is add 6, so the next number is 20 + 6 = 26.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Point at 2 and 8 and ask: "How far is it from 2 to 8?" Count the hops on the strip with the children: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8. Six hops. Click to show +6. Then ask the same for 8 to 14 and 14 to 20, clicking after each. Click 4 shows the next jump and 26. Think-aloud: "The numbers are 2, 8, 14, 20. They go up. How much from 2 to 8? Six. From 8 to 14? Six. From 14 to 20? Six. I check every pair of neighbours and it is always six. So my rule is add 6. The next number is 20 + 6 = 26." Watch for children who count the numbers in between (3, 4, 5, 6, 7 is five numbers) instead of counting the hops (six). Show them: put a finger on 2, then say a number for every hop.
