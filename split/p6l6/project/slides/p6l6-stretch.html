For early finishers

Stretch

1. One letter is picked at random from the word MATHEMATICS. What is the probability that it is M?

2. A program rolls a dice 1200 times and counts the numbers bigger than 4. About how many should it count? The program says 391. Is something wrong? Explain.

3. Roll two dice. What is the probability that at least one of them shows a 6?

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For early finishers (these are not on the Think Harder page). Answers: 1) 2/11 2) About 400 (2 of the 6 results are bigger than 4, and 1/3 × 1200 = 400). 391 is close, so nothing is wrong: a random simulation is never exactly the expected number. 3) 11/36 (6 with the red die, 6 with the blue die, minus the 6 + 6 counted twice: 6 + 6 − 1 = 11)
