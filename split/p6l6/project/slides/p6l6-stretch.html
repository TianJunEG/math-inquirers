For early finishers

Stretch

1. One letter is picked at random from the word MATHEMATICS. What is the probability that it is M?

2. A program rolls a dice 1200 times. About how many even numbers should it roll?

3. Roll two dice. What is the probability that at least one of them shows a 6?

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For early finishers (these are not on the Think Harder page). Answers: 1) 2/11 2) About 600 (1/2 × 1200) 3) 11/36 (6 with the red die, 6 with the blue die, minus the 6 + 6 counted twice: 6 + 6 − 1 = 11)
