Think harder

1. A spinner has 10 equal parts numbered 1 to 10. What is the probability of spinning a prime number?

2. Roll two dice and add. What is the probability that the total is 9?

3. A bag has 5 red counters and some blue counters. The probability of picking red is 1/4. How many blue counters are there?

4. (a) A bag has 4 red and 6 blue counters. How many blue counters must be added so that P(red) = 1/5? (b) In the Difference Game (Ali wins on 0, 1, 2; Ben on 3, 4, 5), change who wins on which differences so that the game is fair.

The same four questions as the printed Think harder page, for anyone who wants them. Answers: 1) 4/10 = 2/5 (2, 3, 5, 7) 2) 4/36 = 1/9 (3+6, 4+5, 5+4, 6+3) 3) 15 (5 out of 20 is 1/4) 4) (a) 10 blue (4 red out of 20: 4/20 = 1/5). (b) Any split with 18 outcomes each, e.g. Ali on 1, 3, 5 (odd) and Ben on 0, 2, 4 (even); or Ali on 1, 2 and Ben on 0, 3, 4, 5; or Ali on 0, 1, 5 and Ben on 2, 3, 4.
