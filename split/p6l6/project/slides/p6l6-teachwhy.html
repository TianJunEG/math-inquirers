The five bars are real results of a 600-roll program (counting 6s). Ask: "Run 3 gave 85. Is the dice unfair?" (No: 85 is unusual but possible; about 1 run in 20 is 85 or less.) Exact facts for the tutor: for 600 rolls, about 91% of runs give between 85 and 115 sixes; exactly 100 happens only about 4 times in 100 runs.
