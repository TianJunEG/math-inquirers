36.5–37.5 MIN

Key idea: count the equally likely outcomes

Probability = ways it can happen ÷ equally likely outcomes. A game is fair when every player has the same probability of winning.

Expected number = probability × tries. 1000 random games land close to it, but not exactly.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 36.5 to 37.5. Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Treating outcomes as equally likely when they are not (two coins: “two heads, two tails, one of each” as thirds; dice totals 2 to 12 as elevenths). (2) Thinking a random result should come out exactly as expected, or that a coin “is due” a head after several tails. (3) Not simplifying, or writing the number of ways instead of the fraction (6 instead of 6/36 = 1/6).
