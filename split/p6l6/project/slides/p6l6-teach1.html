Probability is a fraction

P(event) = number of ways it can happen ÷ number of equally likely outcomes

Roll a dice. P(more than 4)? 5 or 6: 2 ways out of 6. P = 2/6 = 1/3

0 means impossible. 1 means certain. Every probability is between 0 and 1.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 27 to 28.5. Teach. Think-aloud: “Roll one dice. More than 4 means 5 or 6: that is 2 ways out of 6 equally likely outcomes. So the probability is 2/6, which is 1/3.” Stress EQUALLY LIKELY: "Each face of a fair dice has the same chance. That is why we can just count." Ask: "Is P(win the lottery) = 1/2 because you either win or you don't?" (No: the two outcomes are not equally likely.)
