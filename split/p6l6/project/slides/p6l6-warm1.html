Minutes 6.5 to 10. Where on the line? Pairs place four events on a 0 to 1 probability line and give each one’s fraction: roll a 7, flip heads, pick a vowel from SINGAPORE, roll 1 to 6. Pairs agree on a spot and a fraction for each card (no timer), then click to drop the marker (markers appear A to D; four events only, so the warm-up fits). Answers: A 0 (impossible). B 1/2. C 4/9 (I, A, O, E are 4 of the 9 letters). D 1 (certain). Ask: "Is C more or less than an even chance?" (4/9 is a little less than 1/2, because 4.5/9 would be 1/2.) "Can a probability be 6/5?" (No: never more than 1, never less than 0.) Key words for today: equally likely outcomes, fair, expect.
