Teach the code: share your screen and run it 2 or 3 times. Point out "pick random 1 to 6" is one dice roll; adding two of them is two dice (NOT "pick random 2 to 12", which would make every total equally likely: a good question to ask!). Think-aloud: “In 1000 games I expect B to win about 2/3 × 1000, which is 666 2/3. A count cannot be 666 2/3, and every run is random, so the program will say something close, like 670 or 649, but almost never exactly 667.” The three runs on the slide are real results from 1000-game runs; yours will differ.
