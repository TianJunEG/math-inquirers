Q5: about 19 runs in 20 land between 145 and 190 doubles. Full key: (a) Differences: 0 appears 6 times, 1: 10, 2: 8, 3: 6, 4: 4, 5: 2 (36 in all). (b) Ali 24/36 = 2/3, Ben 12/36 = 1/3 (c) Not fair: Ali is twice as likely to win, even though each has 3 differences. (Making it fair is now in Think harder 4.) | Gaps: 6, 6, a = b. (a) About 167: P(double) = 6/36 = 1/6, and 1/6 × 1000 = 166 2/3. (b) A count must be a whole number, and each run is random, so it lands near 167 (about 19 runs in 20 between 145 and 190), not exactly on it.
