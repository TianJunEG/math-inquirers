Worked example: a spinner

Step 1. 6 equal parts, so 6 equally likely outcomes.

Step 2. Yellow is 2 parts: P(yellow) = 2/6 = 1/3.

Step 3. Spin 600 times: expect 1/3 × 600 = 200 yellows.

Step 4. ABOUT 200, not exactly: each spin is random.

Expected number = probability × number of tries
