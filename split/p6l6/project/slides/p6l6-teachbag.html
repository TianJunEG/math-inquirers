42.5–44.5 MIN

Change the bag, change the chance

2 red, 4 blue: P(red) = 2/6 = 1/3

Add red until P(red) = 1/2: red must equal blue. Add 2 red: 4/8 = 1/2

Start again. Add blue until P(red) = 1/4: 2 must be 1/4 of the total, so the total is 8. Add 2 blue: 2/8 = 1/4

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 42.5 to 44.5. Teach before worksheet page 1 (Q3 changes a bag of 4 red and 6 blue: do not do those numbers here). Use real counters in a bag if you can. Think-aloud: "For P(red) = 1/4, the red counters are 1 part out of 4 parts. 2 red is one part, so 4 parts is 8 counters. I have 6, so I add 2 blue." Check by writing the new fraction and simplifying. Counters with a thick outline are the new ones.
