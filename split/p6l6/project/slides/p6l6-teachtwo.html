Two dice: 36 equally likely outcomes

6 outcomes for the red die × 6 for the blue = 36 (a nested loop!).

A total of 7 happens 6 ways: P(7) = 6/36 = 1/6

A total of 2 happens 1 way: P(2) = 1/36

Dice Race: A’s totals happen 1 + 2 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 12 ways. B’s happen 24 ways.

P(A) = 12/36 = 1/3, P(B) = 24/36 = 2/3: not fair

Math Inquirers Level 6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Use a red die and a blue die so that 1 + 6 and 6 + 1 are clearly different outcomes. Think-aloud: “Two dice. The red die has 6 outcomes and for each one the blue die has 6, so 6 × 6 = 36, just like last lesson’s nested loop. A total of 7 happens 6 times: 1 + 6, 2 + 5, 3 + 4, 4 + 3, 5 + 2, 6 + 1. So P(7) = 6/36 = 1/6. A total of 2 happens once: 1/36.” Then link to the Explore: "A has more TOTALS, but B has more OUTCOMES." Common wrong idea: "there are 11 totals, so each is 1/11". Totals are not equally likely; the 36 outcomes are.
