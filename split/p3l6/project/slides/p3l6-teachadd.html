Add fractions: draw a bar

Ali drinks 13 of a bottle of water, then 16 of the bottle more. How much has he drunk?

Step 1. Make the parts the same size: 13 = 26

Step 2. 26 + 16 = 36 = 12. Add the top numbers only: the parts are still sixths.

Step 3. Left in the bottle: 66 − 36 = 36 = 12

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4 and Q5). Heuristic: DRAW A BAR. Click 1: the bar cut into sixths; the two teal parts are the third (the 1/3 label sits across both), the yellow part is the extra sixth. Think-aloud: "1/3 and 1/6 are different-sized parts, so I change 1/3 into sixths: 2/6. Now 2 sixths and 1 sixth make 3 sixths. The parts are still sixths, so the bottom number stays 6." Show the mistake: "Ben says 1/3 + 1/6 = 2/9. Is a ninth bigger or smaller than a sixth? He drank MORE than 1/3, but 2/9 is less than 1/3. So 2/9 cannot be right."
