The fraction wall

Every row is ONE WHOLE cut into EQUAL parts. More parts means smaller parts.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. This is the wall the children built. Run a finger along each row: "Every row is the same whole. Halves: 2 equal parts. Twelfths: 12 equal parts." Ask: "Which row has the biggest pieces? The smallest?" Click: the rule. Say: "The bottom number tells how many equal parts the whole is cut into. More parts, smaller parts. So 1/12 is the smallest piece on the wall, even though 12 is the biggest number."
