LESSON 6 • WEEK 3

Fraction Walls

Fractions

TODAY WE WILL

Build a fraction wall, find equal fractions, compare fractions by making the parts the same size, and add related fractions.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Use a fraction wall to find equivalent fractions, compare and order fractions by making the parts the same size, and add and subtract like and related fractions within one whole. Heuristics taught today: DRAW A MODEL (fraction wall or bar) and MAKE THE PARTS THE SAME SIZE (change one fraction to an equal fraction before comparing or adding). Materials: 7 paper strips per child, all the same length (24 cm works well), ruler, coloured pencils, scissors, mini whiteboards. Online: send the printed fraction strip page instead of asking children to measure and cut strips; marks every 12, 8, 6, 4, 3 and 2 cm give halves, thirds, quarters, sixths, eighths and twelfths. Time plan: warm-up check 2 to 5, then the warm-up game and quick questions to minute 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90, with the one-minute look-back "What was hard?" on the last slide. Stretch and Challenge slides are for early finishers. If the class is behind, children finish the last question of each page at home; the check slides cover it.
