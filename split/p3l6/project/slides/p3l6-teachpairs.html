Partners that make one whole

Which fraction joins 310 to make one whole?

Step 1. One whole = 1010. 1010 − 310 = 710 fills the gap.

Step 2. Which joins 25? Change it first: 25 = 410, so the partner is 610 (or 35).

A whole bar has no empty parts. The partner fills exactly the gap.
