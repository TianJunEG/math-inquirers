Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Take 7 paper strips, all the same length.
  2. Keep one whole. Fold or mark the others into 2, 4, 8, 3, 6 and 12 equal parts.
  3. Label every part and stack the strips into a wall.
  4. Ask: which pieces line up exactly?

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Predict first: "Which is bigger, one sixth or one eighth of the same strip?" Pairs fold halves, quarters and eighths (fold in half again and again). For thirds, sixths and twelfths, mark every 8 cm, 4 cm and 2 cm on a 24 cm strip (or roll the strip into an S until the three parts match). Children label every part (1/2, 1/4 …) and stack the strips. Stress: the parts in a row must be equal, or they are not halves, thirds … Hints: "Which pieces line up with the dashed line at one half?" Finished early: "Find three different pieces that together cover exactly one half." (e.g. 1/4 + 1/8 + 1/8, or 1/3 + 1/6, or 1/4 + 1/6 + 1/12). Online: children hold their wall up to the camera.
