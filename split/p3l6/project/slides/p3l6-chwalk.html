Use after pairs have tried. Q1: work in order from the biggest piece, as in the coin lesson. With a half: the other half needs 4 pieces; 1/4 + 1/8 + 1/8 is only 3 pieces, and 1/4 + three eighths is too much, so only four eighths. Without a half: four quarters is only 4 pieces; three quarters + two eighths works; two quarters + eighths needs 4 eighths, which is 6 pieces.
