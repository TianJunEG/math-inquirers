Challenge

1. Fill one whole bar using exactly 5 pieces. You may use 12, 14 and 18 pieces, as many of each as you like. Find all the ways.

2. Find a fraction that is bigger than 23 but smaller than 34. Hint: cut every part into smaller equal parts. Can you find another one?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) 2 ways: 1/2 + 1/8 + 1/8 + 1/8 + 1/8, and 1/4 + 1/4 + 1/4 + 1/8 + 1/8. 2) 17/24 (2/3 = 16/24 and 3/4 = 18/24). Another: 25/36 (24/36 and 27/36). Many answers are possible.
