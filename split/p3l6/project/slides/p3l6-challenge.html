Challenge

1. Fill one whole bar using exactly 5 pieces. You may use 12, 14 and 18 pieces, as many of each as you like. Find all the ways.

2. Siti and Ravi each have the same size bottle of juice. Siti drinks 23 of her bottle. Ravi drinks 712 of his. Who drinks more? How much more?

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) 2 ways: 1/2 + 1/8 + 1/8 + 1/8 + 1/8, and 1/4 + 1/4 + 1/4 + 1/8 + 1/8. 2) Siti, by 1/12 of a bottle.
