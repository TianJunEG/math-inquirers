HEURISTIC: MAKE THE PARTS THE SAME SIZE

Step 1. Same bottom number: count the parts. 37 < 57

Step 2. Same top number: bigger parts win. 25 > 29 (fifths are bigger than ninths)

Step 3. Different parts: change them. 56 = 1012, 34 = 912, so 56 > 34
