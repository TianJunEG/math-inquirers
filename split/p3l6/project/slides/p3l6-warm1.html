Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Fraction Flash. Point to one shape at a time. Children count the equal parts (bottom number) and the shaded parts (top number), write the fraction, hold it up, then click to reveal (clicks go left to right). Answers: circle 3/8; rectangle 4/6, which is the same as 2/3 (the 4 shaded parts fill two of the three columns); hexagon 5/6; bar 7/10. Ask every time: "How do you know the parts are equal?" (same size triangles, same size squares). Ask: "What fraction is NOT shaded?" (5/8, 2/6, 1/6, 3/10).
