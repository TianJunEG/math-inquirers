Sneak-peek puzzles

Ideas we have not learnt yet. Have a go: guessing and “I do not know” are welcome. Nobody is marked.

Write your answer on your own paper. Show it to your tutor on camera when asked.

Puzzle 1. Draw a rectangle. Cut it into 4 equal parts and shade 1 part. What fraction did you shade?

Try it with objects, a drawing or counting.

Puzzle 2. Mei says, "1/4 of a pizza is bigger than 1/3 of the same pizza, because 4 is bigger than 3." Is she right? Draw it.

Think about it. Show how you thought.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 2 to 5. Sneak-peek puzzles: two small puzzles on ideas we have not learnt yet. SAY: "These are ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome and so is I do not know. Nobody is marked." Puzzle 1 is an ACCESS puzzle: any child can try it with objects, a drawing or counting, so everyone has a first win. Puzzle 2 is a thinking puzzle. Children write answers on their own paper and show them only to the tutor on camera (or everybody holds paper to the camera at the same moment). No private messages. Never answers read out, never "who got it?". Do not show the answers on the slide now. Puzzle 1 answer (tutor only): 1/4. Listen for: 4 equal parts, 1 shaded: one quarter. Puzzle 2 answer (tutor only): No: 1/3 is bigger. Listen for: More equal parts means each part is smaller. Quarters are smaller than thirds. Come back to puzzle 2 at talk time: "Now we know the idea, how could it help?" (volunteers only). Invite the WHOLE group once, never one child; this is an invitation, not a ranking, and any child may choose Think harder or the Investigation after question 3 of page 1. Never announce who is on which page, and never split pairs unless both children agree.
