Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 2 to 5. Warm-up check: two quick questions on today's key idea in a new setting. Whiteboards, on their own, boards up together, then click to show the answer and ask one child to say why. Never mark or label children in front of the group. Q1: No: 1/3 is bigger. Listen for: More equal parts means each part is smaller. Quarters are smaller than thirds. Q2: 6 eighths. Listen for: 3/4 = 6/8. Double the top and the bottom, or look at the wall: two eighths fill one quarter. FAST TRACK: a child who gets both right AND can say why starts on the Think Harder pages and the Investigation (last workbook page of the lesson) at minute 40, while the group works the core pages. Tell them by name in the chat or a small breakout room. Different children can have different pages, nobody is labelled, and the check decides again next lesson.
