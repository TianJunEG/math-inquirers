26.5–28.5 MIN

Equal fractions: same amount, different pieces

HEURISTIC: DRAW A MODEL

Step 1. On the wall 13 lines up with 2 sixths and with 4 twelfths: 13 = 26 = 412

Step 2. Pattern: top and bottom are multiplied by the SAME number: × 2 gives 26, × 4 gives 412

Step 3. So 25 = 410 too. No wall needed!

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Heuristic: DRAW A MODEL. Click through the steps. Think-aloud: "1/3 on the wall lines up with 2 sixths and with 4 twelfths. Same amount, different pieces. From 1/3 to 2/6 the top and the bottom were both doubled: each third is cut into 2, so there are twice as many parts and twice as many shaded. From 1/3 to 4/12, both times 4. So 2/5 = 4/10 without a wall." Ask: "Why must we multiply the top AND the bottom?" (cutting every piece into 2 doubles the shaded pieces and all the pieces).
