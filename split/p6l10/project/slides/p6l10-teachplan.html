Clock Jumper (Lesson 8)

Puzzle: it is 9 o’clock. What time is it h hours from now?

By hand: add, then remove whole 12s. 0 left means 12 o’clock.

15 is the edge case: 9 + 15 = 24, and 24 mod 12 is 0. Without the if block it says “0 o’clock”.

Math Inquirers Level 6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5 is the planning table, Q6 the blocks and tests). Walk through a FOURTH idea so the starters stay fresh. Think-aloud: "My puzzle: 9 o’clock plus h hours. By hand: 9 + 5 = 14, take away 12: 2 o’clock. 9 + 15 = 24, take away two 12s: 0, which on a clock is 12. 9 + 30 = 39, take away three 12s: 3. I write these tests BEFORE I run anything."
