Challenge: walk-through

Q1: count, then take away

Multiples of 2: 50. Multiples of 3: 33.

Multiples of 6 were counted twice: 16.

2 or 3: 50 + 33 − 16 = 67. Not 2 or 3: 100 − 67 = 33

Check: 2 left in every 6 (1 and 5): 16 × 2 = 32, plus 97 → 33 ✓

Q2: make it balance

× 3 − 8 = same: 3 lots of n, minus 8, is 1 lot of n

So 2 lots of n = 8, and n = 4

Check: 4 × 3 − 8 = 4 ✓

× 3 + 8: 2 lots of n = −8, so n = −4. Check: −12 + 8 = −4 ✓
