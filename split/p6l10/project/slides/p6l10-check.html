Warm-up check

Two new puzzles. Whiteboard: your answer AND one reason. Hold up together.

Q1. The sequence 4, 9, 14, 19, … goes on. Is 2026 a term? How do you know?

No. The rule is 5n − 1, so 2026 + 1 = 2027 would have to be a multiple of 5, and it is not. (Every term ends in 4 or 9.)

Q2. Make 2-letter codes from A, B, C and D using different letters, where AB and BA count as different codes. How many codes are there?

12. 4 choices for the first letter and 3 for the second: 4 × 3 = 12. List: AB, AC, AD, BA, BC, BD, CA, CB, CD, DA, DB, DC.
