Sneak-peek puzzles

Two ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome. “I do not know” is welcome. Nobody is marked. When your tutor says show, everyone holds their paper to the camera at the same moment.

Puzzle 1: try it with objects, a drawing or counting

Count on in 5s from 4: 4, 9, 14, 19, … Write the next three numbers.

24, 29 and 34. Any child can count on.

Puzzle 2: have a think

The list 4, 9, 14, 19, … carries on forever. Is 2026 in the list? What do you think? How could you tell?

No. Every number in the list ends in 4 or 9 (it is 1 less than a multiple of 5), and 2026 ends in 6.

Math Inquirers Level 6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.
