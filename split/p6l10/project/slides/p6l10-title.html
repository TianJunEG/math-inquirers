Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Use ideas from all nine lessons (sequences, primes, negative numbers, algebra, counting, probability, angles, clock arithmetic, averages) in a mixed challenge round, then plan, build, test and present a small puzzle program, working out the maths by hand first and explaining why it works. Materials: Laptop or desktop with Scratch 3 (scratch.mit.edu), workbook (challenge round on page 1, planning sheet on page 2), pencil, mini whiteboard, the starter projects as .sb3 files (L10A_Mystery_Machine.sb3, L10B_Prime_or_Not.sb3, L10C_Lucky_Seven.sb3, L10D_Clock_Jumper.sb3; children use File, Load from your computer; each one already works, children change one thing), or build them from the slides, certificates, stamps. Online: Challenge round in teams of 2 in breakout rooms. Building: everyone in the main room with cameras on, asking for help in the chat. Showcase: in two rounds of 4, each child shares their screen for 2 minutes 15 seconds; the audience TYPES two stars and a question for each presenter in the chat (stars are typed, not spoken). A child may show a screenshot and the tutor runs the file. Children may paste a screenshot into the chat instead and the tutor runs their file, which removes screen-share switching. Block colours on our slides are simplified; in Scratch, find blocks by the category name on the left, not by colour. Time plan (this lesson changes the usual routine; no relay and no separate talk time): opening 0 to 2, sneak-peek puzzles 2 to 6, challenge round 6 to 19.5 (workbook page 1 in teams of 2, Q1 and Q3; check together; ask how we started as the first line of the check), starter projects 19.5 to 26.5 (only Starter A is run live; click to reveal), brain break 26.5 to 30, plan and build 30 to 58 (teaching for the planning sheet, then workbook page 2 and Scratch; Think harder and Go further are open to everyone), flex 58 to 60.5 (unscripted, lost first), showcase 60.5 to 82.5 (two rounds of 4, 2 minutes 15 seconds each plus a 20-second switch, stars typed in the chat, rubric in the showcase notes), exit slip with the talk question 82.5 to 86, looking back 86 to 88, certificates (sent as PDFs) and the last stamp 88 to 90. Build target: a working copy of one starter with ONE change is full marks; an own idea is optional. Children chose their starter and their twist at the end of Lesson 9. Before the lesson: build the starter projects once (A Mystery Machine, B Prime or Not?, C Lucky Seven and D Clock Jumper) and share them as .sb3 files (children use File, Load from your computer; no account needed) with shared project links as a backup. Print or fill in certificates, and decide a random showcase order. Showcase switching: ask the next child to have their screen ready while the one before answers questions; a child may paste a screenshot of the program into the chat and the tutor runs their file, which removes the switching time. SPINE: Today you choose the tool. Point at the row of eight tools on page 1 of the workbook. HABIT: Focus habit: Look back. Ask what was learnt and what they would change, after the work is done. Say: "What did you learn? What would you do differently next time?" Do not give the answer first: ask the prompt, wait, and give a hint only if the child is stuck.
