Think harder

1. A row of squares uses 4, 7, 10, 13, … matchsticks. Which pattern number uses exactly 100 matchsticks?

2. Find all the prime numbers between 80 and 100.

3. A regular polygon has an angle of 150° inside each corner. How many sides does it have?

4. Cards 1, 2, 3, 4 make 2-digit numbers (no card twice). (a) How many numbers? How many are even? (b) One of the numbers is picked at random. What is the probability it is a multiple of 3?

Math Inquirers Level 6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers (the same four questions as the printed Think harder page): 1) Pattern 33 (3n + 1 = 100) 2) 83, 89 and 97 3) 12 (the turn at each corner is 180° − 150° = 30°, and 360 ÷ 30 = 12) 4) (a) 12 numbers (4 × 3); 6 are even (3 + 3). (b) 4/12 = 1/3 (12, 21, 24, 42).
