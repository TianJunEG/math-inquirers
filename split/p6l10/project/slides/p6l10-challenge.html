For early finishers

Challenge

1. How many of the numbers from 1 to 100 are NOT multiples of 2 or of 3?

2. A machine does × 3, then − 8. Mei puts a number in and gets the SAME number out. What is it? What if the machine does × 3, then + 8?

Math Inquirers P6 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pair challenge for early finishers, or take home. Ask for the reason every time. Answers: 1) 33 (multiples of 2: 50, of 3: 33, of both (6): 16; 100 − (50 + 33 − 16) = 33) 2) 4 (4 × 3 − 8 = 4). With + 8 it is −4 (−4 × 3 + 8 = −4).
