Minutes 35 to 60. Five minutes on Q4 and Q5, then build for about 20 minutes (test as you go; a working copy of one starter with one change is full marks). Anyone who wants to start from their own idea may, but only after a starter works. Circulate: Ask: “What answer should your program give for this input? How do you know?” Ask: “What happens with an edge case: 1, 0, or the biggest number?” Ask: “In one sentence, what maths is your program about?” Hints: Hint 1: “Start from a starter project and change one thing: the rule, the numbers, or the question.” Hint 2: “Write the maths on your planning sheet first. Then find one block for each line of your plan.” Hint 3: “If the program disagrees with you, trace it in a table: which value goes in, what does each block do?” Anyone stuck after 5 minutes: build a starter exactly, then change ONE thing (the rule, the range, the total).
