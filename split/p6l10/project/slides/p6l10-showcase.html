Math Inquirers Level 6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 62 to 85, in TWO ROUNDS OF 4 so nobody waits more than about 12 minutes: round 1 is 62 to 73, round 2 is 74 to 85. Each child has 2 minutes 30 seconds plus a 20-second switch (4 × 2:50 = 11:20 a round; 8 children fit in the slot). The audience job: write one question for each presenter. Post the stars first, then the questions. A child who is not ready can show a screenshot and you run their file, or show their plan only. Use a timer, volunteers first (never name a child to go next); a child may pass to the second round. The next child has their screen ready while the one before answers questions; a child may paste a screenshot into the chat and the tutor runs their file instead of sharing a screen. A child who did not finish shows their plan and the tests they worked out by hand: that still earns full marks for maths and testing. SHOWCASE RUBRIC (1 to 3 points each; note it privately, share only the stars and the wish). Maths: 1 = gives an answer; 2 = correct answer with working; 3 = correct, and can say why the rule works. Testing: 1 = ran it once; 2 = a test with an answer known in advance; 3 = a known-answer test and an edge case, or a bug found and fixed. Code: 1 = it runs; 2 = under 15 blocks with sensible variable names; 3 = a twist that changes the puzzle (new rule, score, levels). Explaining: 1 = reads the blocks aloud; 2 = says what the program does; 3 = explains the maths and answers a question from the group. Questions to ask each presenter: "Which test did you do first?" "What would happen with 0 or 1?" "Where is the maths in your blocks?" Write one specific sentence of praise for each child for the certificate moment.
