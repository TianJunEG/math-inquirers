Story break: Ada Lovelace

Ada Lovelace • 1815 to 1852 • United Kingdom (England)

Ada Lovelace was born in London in 1815. She studied maths with private tutors, including Augustus De Morgan, and kept learning on her own. In 1843 she translated a French article about Charles Babbage's design for a machine called the Analytical Engine, which was never finished. She added her own notes, and they make up most of the published paper. In one note she showed, step by step, how the machine could work out a list of numbers called Bernoulli numbers. This table is often called the first computer program, but historians still debate that name and how much of the planning was hers. She also checked working carefully, found mistakes and asked for them to be corrected. And she imagined the machine working with symbols such as musical notes, not just numbers.
