Q1: each new hexagon adds 5 sticks (it shares one side), so 5n + 1; 5 × 10 + 1 = 51; 5n + 1 = 101 gives n = 20; the code starts at 6 and adds 5 fourteen times: 6 + 70 = 76, which is 5 × 15 + 1, the chain of 15. Q2: a 2-digit number with exactly 3 factors is the square of a prime: 25 or 49; digits add to 13: 49. 84 = 2 × 42 = 2 × 2 × 21 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 7. For 40 test every prime up to 20: 3 + 37, 11 + 29, 17 + 23 work; 2, 5, 7, 13, 19 do not (38, 35, 33, 27, 21 are not prime). Q3: a) −4 + 9 = 5, 5 − 12 = −7. b) 3n − 10 = n, so 2n = 10, n = 5 (or trial: 5 × 3 − 10 = 5). c) 360 ÷ 24 = 15. d) 100 hours = 8 × 12 + 4, so 4 hours on from 9: 1 o’clock. e) 4 × 3 = 12 numbers; even ones end in 2 or 4: 3 + 3 = 6. f) 12, 21, 24, 42 are multiples of 3: 4 of 12 = 1/3.
