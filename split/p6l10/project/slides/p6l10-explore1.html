Challenge round: how it works

TEAMS OF 2

  1. Teams of 2. Open the workbook at page 1: the challenge round.
  2. Three puzzles from across the program. Maths first.
  3. Score: 1 point for each answer, 1 for a check (a different way or in code), 1 for a clear reason.
  4. After 12 minutes we check together and add up the points.
