LESSON 4 • WEEK 2

Triangle Areas

Area of a triangle

TODAY WE WILL

Find the base and the height of any triangle, explain why triangles with the same base and height have the same area, and solve shaded-area puzzles.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 2 (opening, inside the 90-minute clock). Objective: Find the area of a triangle as 1/2 × base × height, choosing a base and the height that meets it at a right angle (also when the height is outside the triangle); use “same base, same height” to explain equal areas; find areas of composite figures and shaded parts by splitting into triangles or by taking away. Thinking strategies today: FIND THE BASE AND ITS HEIGHT, SAME BASE AND SAME HEIGHT, and SPLIT OR TAKE AWAY. Materials: Square grid paper (cut-out page at the back of the workbook) with an 8 cm by 6 cm rectangle already drawn on it the day before, scissors, ruler, set square or the corner of a card, mini whiteboard and marker, workbook, pencil. Online: The tutor shares the slides and clicks to draw heights and reveal each step. For the explore, children draw and cut their rectangle with the camera up as usual, and hold the cut pieces up to the camera to show they fit (or use the interactive version on the slide). Whiteboards are held up together for the quick questions. Time plan (the exact minutes of this lesson; every lesson adds up to 90): opening 0 to 2, sneak-peek puzzles 2 to 6.5 (one hold-up for both puzzles, no volunteer round, nobody is marked), warm-up 6.5 to 12.5 (one game round, four quick questions), explore 12.5 to 25.5 (pair work 6.5 minutes at most, then two questions together; a Go further what-if is on the explore slide for any pair that finishes), teaching 25.5 to 36.5 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 36.5 to 41, workbook page 1 41 to 53.5 (the questions without a star; after question 2 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder), check together 53.5 to 57, short teaching before page 2 57 to 60, workbook page 2 60 to 70 (alone: start with the questions named at the top, then choose; starred parts are take-home; nobody has to finish), check together 70 to 73, talk time 73 to 78.5 (you may say pass; volunteers first; the first step of the Investigation is take-home), exit ticket 78.5 to 82, reflection 82 to 84.5 (pass is always OK), stamp typed in the chat and goodbye 84.5 to 85.5, and the buffer 85.5 to 90 (floating time: spend it first if you run late, never fill it on purpose). Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child and have no clock time. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and never splits a pair unless both children agree. Invite the WHOLE group, never one child: "when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation."
