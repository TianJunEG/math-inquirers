LESSON 4 • WEEK 2

Triangle Areas

Area of a triangle

TODAY WE WILL

Find the base and the height of any triangle, explain why triangles with the same base and height have the same area, and solve shaded-area puzzles.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find the area of a triangle as 1/2 × base × height, choosing a base and the height that meets it at a right angle (also when the height is outside the triangle); use “same base, same height” to explain equal areas; find areas of composite figures and shaded parts by splitting into triangles or by taking away. Thinking strategies today: FIND THE BASE AND ITS HEIGHT, SAME BASE AND SAME HEIGHT, and SPLIT OR TAKE AWAY. Materials: Square grid paper (cut-out page at the back of the workbook), scissors, ruler, set square or the corner of a card, mini whiteboard and marker, workbook, pencil. Online: The tutor shares the slides and clicks to draw heights and reveal each step. For the explore, children draw and cut their rectangle with the camera pointing down at the desk, and hold up the pieces to show they fit. Whiteboards are held up together for the quick questions. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
