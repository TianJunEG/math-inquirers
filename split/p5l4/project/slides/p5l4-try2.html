Your turn: same area?

Work out base × height first.

a Triangle P: base 6 cm, height 10 cm. Triangle Q: base 12 cm, height 5 cm. Same area?

Yes: 30 and 30

b A rectangle is 8 cm by 5 cm. A triangle uses the 8 cm side as its base and its top corner on the opposite side. Area?

20 cm²

c Triangle R has base 10 cm and area 40 cm². What is its height?

8 cm

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) 1/2 × 6 × 10 = 30 and 1/2 × 12 × 5 = 30: the same area, although the shapes are very different. b) Half of 8 × 5 = 40, so 20 cm². c) 1/2 × 10 × height = 40, so 5 × height = 40 and the height is 8 cm.
