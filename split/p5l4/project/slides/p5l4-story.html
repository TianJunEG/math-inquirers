Story break: Liu Hui

3rd century CE • China (kingdom of Wei)

Liu Hui lived in China in the 3rd century, in the kingdom of Wei. Very little is known about his life, but we know his work. In the year 263 he wrote a commentary on The Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art, an older book of problems and rules. Its first chapter has rules for finding the areas of fields of different shapes, including triangles. The old book gave the rules without showing why they work, so Liu Hui explained them. He often cut shapes into pieces and moved the pieces around. In one famous example he cut a right-angled triangle into coloured pieces and fitted four copies into a rectangle. His original pictures are lost, so historians have redrawn them.

TRY IT (OPTIONAL)

A right-angled triangle has two short sides of 6 cm and 8 cm. Two copies fit together to make a 6 cm by 8 cm rectangle. What is the area of one triangle?
