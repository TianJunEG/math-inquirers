A triangle has an area of 42 cm². Its base is 12 cm. What is its height?

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 78.5 to 82. Answer: 7 cm (1/2 × 12 × height = 42, so 6 × height = 42) Extension: On a grid, draw as many different triangles as you can with area 6 cm² using a base of 4 cm. Where can the top corner go? (Anywhere on the line 3 cm above the base.)
