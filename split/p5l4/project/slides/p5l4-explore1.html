Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Draw a rectangle 8 cm by 6 cm on grid paper.
  2. Mark any point on the top side. Join it to the two bottom corners.
  3. Cut out the triangle and the two leftover pieces.
  4. Do the two pieces cover the triangle exactly? Try again with a different point.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs draw the rectangle on grid paper (cut-out page at the back of the workbook) and cut out the three pieces (online: camera pointing down at the desk). Predict first: what fraction of the rectangle is the triangle? Does it matter where the point is? Early finishers: put the point at the end of the top side. What kind of triangle is it now? Is it still half? (Yes: a right-angled triangle, half of the rectangle.)
