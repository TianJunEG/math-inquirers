Your turn: whiteboards

Write the area. Remember the 1/2.

a A triangle has a base of 10 cm and a height of 7 cm.

35 cm²

b A triangle has a base of 9 cm and a height of 12 cm. Its slanting sides are 15 cm and 20 cm.

54 cm²

c A triangle has an area of 40 cm² and a base of 8 cm. What is its height?

10 cm

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) 1/2 × 10 × 7 = 35. b) 1/2 × 9 × 12 = 54; 15 and 20 are the slanting sides. c) 1/2 × 8 × height = 40, so 4 × height = 40, height = 10 (work backwards: double 40 is 80, and 80 ÷ 8 = 10).
