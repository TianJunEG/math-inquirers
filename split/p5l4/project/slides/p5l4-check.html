2–6.5 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Guessing is welcome. Nobody is marked.

1. Draw a rectangle 6 squares wide and 4 squares tall on squared paper. Count all the squares in it. Draw a line from one corner to the opposite corner and cut along it. Do the two pieces match exactly? What fraction of the rectangle is one piece?

Yes, they match: 1/2 each, 12 squares

2. ABCD is a rectangle 12 cm long and 5 cm tall. P is a point on the top side AB. Join P to C and to D. Does the area of triangle PCD change if P slides along AB? What is the area?

No. 30 cm² every time.

Show your paper ONCE, after both puzzles: only to your tutor, or all hold paper to the camera together.

The two triangles match exactly (turn one round and it fits on the other), so each is 1/2 of the rectangle: 12 squares.; Q2: No, 30 cm². The base DC = 12 cm and the height from P down to DC is always 5 cm, so the area is 1/2 × 12 × 5 = 30 cm² wherever P is. A guess of "it changes" is fine: we test it in the explore.
