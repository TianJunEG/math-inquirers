Key idea: 1/2 × base × height

Area of a triangle = 1/2 × base × height. The height meets the base at a right angle, even when it is outside the triangle.

Same base and same height: same area, whatever the shape. Slanting sides are not needed.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Using a slanting side as the height (1/2 × 14 × 13 for a 13-14-15 triangle). (2) Forgetting the 1/2 and giving the area of the whole rectangle. (3) For a height outside the triangle, measuring the base all the way to the foot of the height (adding the dashed extension to the base).
