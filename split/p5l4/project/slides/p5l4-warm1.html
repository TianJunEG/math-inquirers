Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6.5 to 9. Half or not? Children vote with thumbs for each rectangle before you click. Answers: Rectangle 1 is half (a diagonal cuts a rectangle into two equal triangles). Rectangle 2 is half too: the top corner is on the top side, so the triangle has the whole bottom side as its base and the whole height of the rectangle as its height. Many children say no because the picture looks "pointy"; do not explain yet, collect reasons. Rectangle 3 is NOT half: the corner of the triangle is in the middle of the right side, so the triangle is 1/4 of the rectangle (base 12 cm, height 4 cm; 1/2 × 12 × 4 = 24 cm² out of 96 cm²). Say: "Hold on to your reasons. We will explain why."
