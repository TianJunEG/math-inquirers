The height can be OUTSIDE the triangle

The base is 11 cm. The top corner is past the end of the base. The height is 12 cm. What is the area?

Step 1. The top corner is past the end of the base. Extend the base with a dashed line.

Step 2. Drop the height from the top corner to the dashed line at a right angle: 12 cm.

Step 3. The base is still 11 cm. The dashed part is NOT part of the triangle.

1/2 × 11 × 12 = 66 cm²

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Ask: "Where is the height now? There is no square corner inside the triangle." Click order: step 1, the dashed extension and the height appear, steps 2 and 3, the answer. Think-aloud: "A height does not have to be inside. I extend the base with a dashed line, then draw a line from the top corner straight down to it, at a right angle. That is the height: 12 cm. But the base is only the 11 cm side. The area is half of 11 times 12: 66 cm². The 13 cm and 20 cm sides are not needed." Show it with a paper triangle leaning over. Common mistake: using the base plus the dashed part (16 cm).
