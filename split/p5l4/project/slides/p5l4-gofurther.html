B1. A right-angled triangle has sides of 15 cm, 20 cm and 25 cm. What is its height when the 25 cm side is the base?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional, if you finish early (no clock time): show it only while some pairs are working or have finished, never as a group slide at a fixed minute. These are the printed Go further questions B1 to B3 (the same as the workbook). Answers: B1) 12 cm (area 1/2 × 15 × 20 = 150 cm², and 1/2 × 25 × 12 = 150) B2) 50 cm² both times: it is always half of the big square (box it in a rectangle and take away three right-angled triangles: 160 − 50 − 12 − 48 = 50, and 140 − 50 − 12 − 28 = 50) B3) 18 cm² (the height is 9 cm because 1/2 × 12 × 9 = 54; ABD has base 4 cm and the same height) Make up your own (printed under Go further): Draw a rectangle, label its sides, and join points to make a shaded triangle. Work out its area, then swap with a partner. Think for 30 seconds before anyone speaks. Open-ended: check that a partner can solve it and that exactly one answer fits.
