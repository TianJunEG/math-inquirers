Area of a triangle: 1/2 × base × height

A right-angled triangle has sides of 5 cm, 12 cm and 13 cm. What is its area?

Step 1. Find the square corner. The two sides that make it are the base (12 cm) and the height (5 cm).

Step 2. The 13 cm side is slanting. It is not a height, so we do not use it.

Step 3. Area = 1/2 × 12 × 5 = 30 cm². Two of these triangles make a 12 by 5 rectangle: 60 cm².

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Ask first: "Which length is the height?" Let children argue; then show the square corner. Click order: step 1, step 2, step 3 (the dashed copy makes the rectangle). Think-aloud: "The height must meet the base at a right angle. Here the square corner tells me: 12 is the base and 5 is the height. 13 leans over, so it is not a height. Half of base times height: half of 60 is 30." Link back to the explore: the triangle is half of the rectangle 12 by 5. Common error: using 13 as the height (1/2 × 12 × 13 = 78: wrong).
