1. ABCD is a rectangle with AB = 12 cm and BC = 10 cm. M is the middle of BC. N is the middle of CD. What is the area of triangle AMN?

2. PQRS joins the middle points of the sides of a square of side 10 cm. What is the area of PQRS?

Answers: 1) 45 cm² (rectangle 120; white triangles ABM 30, MCN 15, NDA 30; 120 − 75 = 45). 2) 50 cm² (square 100; four white corner triangles of 12.5 each make 50; PQRS is half of the square).
