Minutes 76 to 82. Pairs talk for 2 minutes, then share. Answer: No. Two triangles with the same base and the same height have the same area, even if one leans over and has much longer sides. The area depends on the base and the height, not on the slanting sides. A long, thin triangle can have a smaller area than a short, wide one. Ask: "Can you draw a triangle with one very long side and a tiny area?" (Yes: a long base with a tiny height.) Praise reasons, not only answers. Protected think time: before anyone speaks, everyone thinks silently for 30 seconds (show the clock). Then pairs talk.
