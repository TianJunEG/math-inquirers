Minutes 21.5 to 25.5. Gather the pairs. Hold up the pieces on camera: the two blue pieces fit exactly on the yellow triangle. Answers: 1) Yes. Each blue piece is half of a smaller rectangle (cut it along the dashed line from the point down), so together the blue pieces equal the triangle. 2) 24 cm²: the rectangle is 8 × 6 = 48 cm² and the triangle is half. (Not asked together, but say it if nobody does) Does it matter where the point is? No: the base is always 8 cm and the height is always 6 cm, wherever the point is.
