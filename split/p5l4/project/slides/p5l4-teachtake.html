Take away the white triangles

ABCD is a rectangle 10 cm by 6 cm. E is on AB with AE = 6 cm. F is on BC with BF = 2 cm. What is the area of the shaded triangle DEF?

Step 1. Rectangle: 10 × 6 = 60 cm².

Step 2. White: ADE = 1/2 × 6 × 6 = 18, EBF = 1/2 × 4 × 2 = 4, FCD = 1/2 × 4 × 10 = 20.

Step 3. Shaded = 60 − 18 − 4 − 20 = 18 cm².

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4). Ask: "The shaded triangle has no square corner and no side along the rectangle. Can we find its base and height directly?" (Hard.) "What do we know? The three white triangles each have a square corner." Click through. Think-aloud: "I find the whole rectangle, then take away the three white triangles, each with a right angle at a corner of the rectangle." Check: 18 + 18 + 4 + 20 = 60. Common error: forgetting one of the three white triangles.
