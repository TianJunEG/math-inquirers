Split a figure into triangles

ABCD has a diagonal AC of 10 cm. The height from B to AC is 4 cm and the height from D to AC is 6 cm. What is its area?

Step 1. The diagonal AC is the base of BOTH triangles: ABC and ACD.

Step 2. ABC = 1/2 × 10 × 4 = 20 cm². ACD = 1/2 × 10 × 6 = 30 cm².

Step 3. Add the two parts: 20 + 30 = 50 cm².

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q3). Ask: "How can we cut this figure into triangles we know?" Click: the diagonal is already there. Think-aloud: "AC is shared. Above it, triangle ABC has height 4 cm; below it, triangle ACD has height 6 cm. I find each area and add. I could also say: the two heights together make 10 cm, so the area is 1/2 × 10 × (4 + 6) = 50." Mention the second way only if children find it; check that it gives the same answer.
