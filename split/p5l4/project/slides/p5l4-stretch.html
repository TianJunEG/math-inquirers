Stretch

1. A right-angled triangle has sides of 6 cm, 8 cm and 10 cm. What is the height when the 10 cm side is the base?

2. ABCD is a rectangle 14 cm by 6 cm. P is any point on AB. What is the area of triangle PDC? What is the total area of the other two triangles?

3. A triangle has a base of 8 cm and a height of 5 cm. What is its area? What is the area if the base is doubled? If both the base and the height are doubled?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 4.8 cm (area = 1/2 × 6 × 8 = 24 cm²; 1/2 × 10 × height = 24, so height = 24 × 2 ÷ 10 = 4.8). 2) Triangle PDC = 1/2 × 14 × 6 = 42 cm² (half of 84); the other two triangles together are 42 cm². 3) 20 cm²; 40 cm² (twice as big); 80 cm² (four times as big).
