Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 9 to 12.5. Whiteboards: read each question aloud, give thinking time, hold up boards together, then click. Answers: 1) 42 cm² (7 × 6). 2) 21 cm² (half of 42). 3) 18 (9 × 4 = 36, half is 18). 4) 7 cm (7 × 7 = 49). Ask after 3: "What are we really doing when we halve? A triangle is half of a rectangle: which rectangle?" Listen for "the base times the height".
