Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Teams of two (online: type in the chat). Show one card at a time by pointing; click to reveal its answer (clicks go card 1 to card 6). Ask for the rule every time. Card 3 and card 5 are traps: the difference is NOT the same each time (square numbers go up by 3, 5, 7, 9; in card 5 each term is the sum of the two before, the Fibonacci numbers). Say: "Today we focus on the friendly kind, where the difference stays the same, like cards 1, 2 and 6." Card 6: 2.75 + 0.75 = 3.5, then 4.25.
