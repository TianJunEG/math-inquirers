1. Find the 100th term of 12, 19, 26, 33, …

2. Is 200 a term of 2, 9, 16, 23, …? Explain.

3. Matchstick houses in a row: 1 house uses 6 sticks, 2 houses use 11, 3 houses use 16. How many houses can you make with 100 sticks?

4. Write the difference, the rule for the nth term and the 100th term of (a) 9, 11, 13, 15, … and (b) 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, …

Math Inquirers Level 6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers (the same four questions as the printed Think harder page): 1) 705. The rule is 7n + 5, and 7 × 100 + 5 = 705. 2) No. The rule is 7n − 5. The 29th term is 198 and the 30th term is 205, so 200 is skipped. 3) 19 houses. The rule is 5n + 1: 19 houses use 96 sticks (4 left over) and 20 houses would need 101. 4) (a) Difference 2; 2n + 7; 207. (b) Difference 0.5; 0.5n (or n ÷ 2); 50.
