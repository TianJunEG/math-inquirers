1. The 5th term is 23 and the 6th term is 27. The difference is always the same. What is the 1st term? What is the rule?

2. The 1st term is 4 and the 10th term is 40. What is the difference?

3. Write a sequence whose 1st term is 10 and whose 100th term is 1000.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 7: go back 4 jumps of 4 from 23 (23 − 16 = 7); rule 4n + 3. 2) 4: from the 1st to the 10th term is 9 jumps, and 36 ÷ 9 = 4 (rule 4n). 3) 10, 20, 30, 40, … (rule 10n): 99 jumps must add 990, so each jump is 10.
