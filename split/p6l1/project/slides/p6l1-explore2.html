Minutes 16.5 to 25. Pairs keep working, then gather. Two questions (pattern 10 is read from the table: 21). Answers: 1) 201: each new triangle adds 2 sticks, so 3 + 2 × 99 = 201; or "2 sticks per triangle plus 1 extra", 2 × 100 + 1 = 201. Both are right: celebrate both methods. 2) Pattern 37: work backwards, 75 − 1 = 74, 74 ÷ 2 = 37.
