What if ...?

What if each shape in the row were a SQUARE, sharing one side with the next? Predict how many sticks 10 squares in a row need. Then test pattern 3 by drawing.

31 sticks. The first square uses 4 sticks and each new square adds 3: 4 + 3 × 9 = 31, or 3 × 10 + 1.
