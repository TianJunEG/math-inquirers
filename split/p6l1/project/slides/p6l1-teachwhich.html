Is it in the sequence? Work backwards

2, 5, 8, 11, … The rule is 3n − 1.

Step 1. Is 62 a term? 3n − 1 = 62, so 3n = 63 and n = 21. Yes: the 21st term.

Step 2. Is 100 a term? 3n − 1 = 100, so 3n = 101. But 101 is not in the 3 times table. No.

Step 3. Shortcut: every term is 1 less than a multiple of 3.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 1 (Q1d and Q3). This is the PSLE "think of a number" idea run backwards: undo the − 1, then undo the × 3. Check step 1 forwards: 3 × 21 − 1 = 62. For step 2, ask: "Which terms are near 100?" (98 is the 33rd, 101 would need n = 33⅔.)
