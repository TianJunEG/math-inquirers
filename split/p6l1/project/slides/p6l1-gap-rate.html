Go further: Meeting and catching up

Tool: Draw it

(a) Town A and town B are 60 km apart. Ali cycles from A towards B at 15 km/h. At the same time Ben cycles from B towards A at 25 km/h. After how many hours do they meet?

(b) Siti leaves a station at 9:00 and travels at 40 km/h. Kumar leaves the same station at 9:30 on the same road at 60 km/h. At what time does Kumar catch Siti?

(a) 1.5 hours.

(b) 10:30.

ADDED ITEM rate (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Meeting and catching up"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Draw it. SAY: Draw the road as a line. In (a) both children close the gap, so add their speeds. In (b) Kumar closes the gap, so take the speeds away from each other. ASK: How far apart are they at the start, and by how many km does the gap change each hour? LISTEN FOR: (a) Closing speed 15 + 25 = 40 km/h, so 60 ÷ 40 = 1.5 hours. (b) Siti has a 20 km start, closing speed 60 − 40 = 20 km/h, so 1 hour after 9:30. Listen for "the gap" and "closing speed". Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) 1.5 hours. (b) 10:30. WHY: (a) 15 + 25 = 40 km each hour, 60 ÷ 40 = 1.5 hours. (b) At 9:30 Siti is 40 × 0.5 = 20 km ahead. Kumar gains 60 − 40 = 20 km each hour, so he catches her 1 hour later, at 10:30. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): Two towns are 90 km apart. Mei cycles at 20 km/h and Ravi at 25 km/h towards each other. After how long do they meet? Answer: 2 hours.
