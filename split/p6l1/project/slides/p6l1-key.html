Key idea: the rule for the nth term

The difference tells you the times table. Compare with it to find what to add or take away.

From the 1st term to the nth term there are n − 1 jumps. Test your rule in code.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Using only the difference as the rule (writing 3n for 5, 8, 11, …) and not comparing with the 1st term. (2) Off by one: doing 100 jumps from the 1st term (5 + 3 × 100) instead of 99, in the maths and in the repeat block. (3) Putting "change" before "say" in a loop, so the sprite never says the 1st term.
