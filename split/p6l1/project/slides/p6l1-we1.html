Worked example: the 100th term

Find the 100th term of 5, 8, 11, 14, …

Step 1. The difference is 3, so compare with the 3 times table: 3, 6, 9, 12.

Step 2. Each term is 2 more than 3 × n. So the nth term is 3n + 2.

Step 3. Check the 4th term: 3 × 4 + 2 = 14 ✓

100th term = 3 × 100 + 2 = 302
