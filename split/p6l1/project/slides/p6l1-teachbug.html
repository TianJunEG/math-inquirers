5 terms, only 4 jumps. The 1st term is there before any jump.

Step 1. Ben wants the 20th term of 2, 6, 10, … His code: set n to 2, repeat 20, change n by 4.

Step 2. It says 2 + 20 × 4 = 82. But the rule 4n − 2 gives 78.

Step 3. Fix: 1st term to 20th term is 19 jumps, so repeat 19 (2 + 19 × 4 = 78 ✓).
