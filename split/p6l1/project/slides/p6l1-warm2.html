Q1 and Q2 are the seed of today: 2, 4, 6, 8 is the 2 times table, so the 10th term is 2 × 10 = 20. 3, 5, 7, 9 is always 1 more than the 2 times table, so the 10th term is 21. Ask: "How did you get Q2 without counting on?" Q3: 12 + 6 × 5 = 42 (6 weeks, 6 jumps of $5, because Ali starts with $12 before any saving).
