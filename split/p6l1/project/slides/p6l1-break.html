Brain break

Pattern moves: clap, clap, stomp, clap, clap, stomp … Do 12 moves together. Then predict: what is the 20th move? The 30th?

Then: a drink of water, stretch your fingers, back to the screen.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Five minutes, away from the keyboard. Answers: the pattern repeats every 3 moves. 20 = 6 × 3 + 2, so the 20th move is the 2nd in its group: clap. 30 = 10 × 3, so the 30th move ends a group: stomp.
