Sneak-peek puzzles

Two ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome. “I do not know” is welcome. Nobody is marked. Show your answer only to your tutor: own paper held up to them, or a private chat message.

Puzzle 1: try it with objects, a drawing or counting

Matchsticks make triangles in a row: 1 triangle uses 3 sticks, 2 triangles use 5 sticks, 3 triangles use 7 sticks. Draw or build 4 triangles in a row. How many sticks?

9 sticks. Each new triangle adds 2 more sticks (3, 5, 7, 9). Any child can draw it and count.

Puzzle 2: have a think

A taxi charges $3 to start plus $2 for every km. What is the fare for 20 km?

$43. Each km adds $2, so 2 × 20 + 3 = 43. (Not 2 × 20 = 40: the $3 start charge is there from the beginning.) A guess or “I do not know” is fine; we will learn how today.
