3 MIN CHECK

Warm-up check

Two new puzzles. Whiteboard: your answer AND one reason. Hold up together.

Q1. A taxi charges $3 to start plus $2 for every km. The fares are $5 for 1 km, $7 for 2 km and $9 for 3 km. What is the fare for 20 km?

$43. Each km adds $2, so the fare is 2 × 20 + 3 = 43. (Not 2 × 20 = 40: the $3 start charge is there from the beginning.)

Q2. Is 50 a term of 7, 12, 17, 22, …? How do you know?

No. The rule is 5n + 2. 50 − 2 = 48 is not a multiple of 5. (Every term ends in 2 or 7, and 50 ends in 0.)
