Whiteboards: answer and say how you know

1. A box has 20 sweets. 12 are red and the rest are green. How many are green?

8

2. True or false: 7 + 5 = 6 + 6. How do you know?

True: both sides make 12

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 7 to 10 (3 minutes). Two questions on the key idea in a new setting. Children write their answer and how they know on a whiteboard and show it on a count of three; click to show each answer. Ask one or two children "How do you know?". Quietly note who got both right and gave a reason: those children may start the worksheet on the Think Harder page (then Challenge and the Open investigation) while the group works on the core page. Tell them privately at minute 40; do not announce names or scores. Everyone still joins Explore and the Mini-lesson. Answers: 1) 8. 12 + 8 = 20 (or 12 + 8: 2 + 8 makes 10, and 10 more makes 20). 2) True. Both sides make 12. (7 goes down 1 and 5 goes up 1, so the total stays the same.)
