Item 1 (Picture equations with two symbols; tool of the day idea: Guess and check). SAY: "Look for what is the same in both lines and what is different." ASK: "What is the extra thing in line 1? How much extra is the total?" LISTEN FOR: "One more triangle, 3 more". Guess-and-check is fine: ask them to check both lines. ANSWER: a) Line 1 has one more triangle than line 2, and 11 is 3 more than 8, so triangle = 3. b) 3 + circle = 8, so circle = 5. Check: 3 + 3 + 5 = 11.
