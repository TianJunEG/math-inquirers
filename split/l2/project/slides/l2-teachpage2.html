Two helpers for page 2

Magic triangle: put 1 to 6 in the circles so each side adds up to 12.

Step 1. Each corner is on TWO sides. 12 is a big total, so big corners: 4, 5, 6.

Step 2. 4 + ? + 5 = 12, so ? = 3. 5 + ? + 6 = 12, so ? = 1.

Step 3. 6 + ? + 4 = 12, so ? = 2. Every number once ✓

Two numbers add up to 8. One is 2 more than the other. What are they?

Step 1. Take away the extra 2. 6 cubes are left.

Step 2. Share the 6 equally: 3 and 3.

Step 3. Give the 2 back to one side: 3 and 5 ✓ (3 + 5 = 8, and 5 is 2 more than 3)

Teach before worksheet page 2, about 2 minutes in all (this slide replaces two separate slides). LEFT, magic triangle (Q5): here the total is 12, a big total, so the BIG numbers go in the corners. On the worksheet the totals are 9 and 10, which are small: ask "Small totals... so which numbers should go in the corners?" but do not tell them. The hint on the worksheet page says the same thing. Click order: step 1, corners appear, step 2, step 3, the finished triangle. Check each side aloud: 4 + 3 + 5, 5 + 1 + 6, 6 + 2 + 4. RIGHT, two numbers (Q6): use real cubes. Take 2 cubes away and put them aside, share the rest into two equal towers, then add the 2 to one tower. Children who guess-and-check are fine too: ask them to check both clues.
