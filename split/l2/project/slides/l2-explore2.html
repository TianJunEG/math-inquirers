Explore: talk about it

1. How many pairs did you find?

2. How do you know you have all of them?

3. What happens to the second number when the first goes up by 1?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Let pairs keep working and move around the room (or breakout rooms). Gather them to answer these. Answers: 1) 11 pairs from 0 + 10 to 10 + 0, or 6 different bonds if 3 + 7 and 7 + 3 are the same. 2) List in order: the first number goes up by 1 and the second goes down by 1. 3) It goes down by 1.
