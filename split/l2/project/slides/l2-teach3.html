"=" means "is the same as"

6 + 2 = 5 + __

Step 1. Work out the side you know: 6 + 2 = 8

Step 2. The other side must also be 8.

Step 3. 5 + 3 = 8, so the missing number is 3. Check: 8 and 8 ✓

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Imagine a see-saw: both sides must balance. Ask first: "What do you think goes in the blank?" Many children say 8 because "the answer comes next". Click through the steps. Think-aloud: "6 + 2 is 8, so the other side must be 8. What goes with 5 to make 8? 3."
