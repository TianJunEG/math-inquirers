Story break: Robert Recorde

Optional • about 2 minutes • we can skip it

Robert Recorde was a doctor from Tenby, in Wales. He also wrote maths books, as talks between a teacher and a pupil. In 1557 he used the sign = in a book. He was tired of writing the words "is equal to" again and again. He chose two straight lines of the same length, because, he said, no two things can be more equal. Today the sign tells us that both sides have the same amount.

Robert Recorde

Wales • about 1510-1558
