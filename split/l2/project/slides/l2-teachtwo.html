Two numbers: total and difference

Two numbers add up to 8. One is 2 more than the other. What are they?

Step 1. Take away the extra 2. 6 cubes are left.

Step 2. Share the 6 equally: 3 and 3.

Step 3. Give the extra 2 back to one side: 3 and 5. Check: 3 + 5 = 8, and 5 is 2 more than 3 ✓

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6). Use real cubes. Take 2 cubes away and put them aside, share the rest into two equal towers, then add the 2 to one tower. Children who guess-and-check are fine too: ask them to check both clues.
