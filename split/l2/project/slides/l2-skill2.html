Optional slide for pairs who finish early or ask for more. Show it only on request and never announce who is working on it. Children write the answer on their own paper and say it together on three; nothing is held up to the camera. Answers and tutor lines are for you only. Item 1 (Mental addition: pair numbers to make 10; tool of the day idea: Try something simpler). SAY: "Do not add from left to right. Hunt for two numbers that make 10." ASK: "Which two numbers did you join first? Is there another pair?" LISTEN FOR: "8 and 2 make 10." Accept any order that works. ANSWER: a) 8 + 2 = 10 and 3 + 7 = 10, so 20 marbles. b) 9 + 1 = 10 and 6 + 4 = 10, so 10 + 10 + 10 = 30. Item 2 (Mental addition: add 9 by adding 10; tool of the day idea: Try something simpler). SAY: "Adding 10 is easy. Then fix it." ASK: "Why do we take 1 away? What is 19 close to?" LISTEN FOR: "9 is 1 less than 10" and "19 is 1 less than 20". ANSWER: a) 45. b) 44 (10 is 1 too many, so take 1 away). c) 52 + 20 = 72, then take away 1: 71.
