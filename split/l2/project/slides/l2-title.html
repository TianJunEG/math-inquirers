Number Bond Inquiry

Number Bonds

TODAY WE WILL

Find all the ways to make 10, use them to make 20 and learn what the equals sign really means.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Know the bonds of 10, use them to find bonds of 20, balance equations, and reason about missing numbers. Materials: Ten-frames, red and yellow counters, a cup, a paper triangle with 6 circles. Online: Slide ten-frame with a hidden-counter reveal; each child uses 10 coins or paper pieces of two colours. Time plan: opening 0 to 2, warm-up 2 to 8 (game 2 to 6, quick questions 6 to 8; ask questions 1 to 3, and 4 and 5 only if you are on time), sneak-peek puzzles 8 to 12, explore 12 to 26, teaching 26 to 36 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 36 to 40, worksheet 40 to 72 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it (a 30-second stand-and-stretch reset after the page-1 check, taken from that slot; the last 6 minutes check page 2, after a 1-minute stand-up stretch), talk time 72 to 79, exit ticket 79 to 85, look-back 85 to 88, then flex time and goodbye 88 to 90 (this is the buffer: if you are behind, use it; do not start anything new). Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are for every child to choose, any time or at home. Nobody is told which page they should be on.
