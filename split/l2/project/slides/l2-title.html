Number Bond Inquiry

Number Bonds

TODAY WE WILL

Find all the ways to make 10, use them to make 20 and learn what the equals sign really means.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Know the bonds of 10, use them to find bonds of 20, balance equations, and reason about missing numbers. Materials: Ten-frames, red and yellow counters, a cup, a paper triangle with 6 circles. Online: Slide ten-frame with a hidden-counter reveal; each child uses 10 coins or paper pieces of two colours. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 72 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it (the last 4 minutes check page 2, after a 1-minute stand-up stretch), talk time 72 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90. Think Harder and Challenge are for early finishers or home.
