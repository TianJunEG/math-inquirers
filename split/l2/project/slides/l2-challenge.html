Make up your own

3. Change the magic triangle: use the numbers 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 instead of 1 to 6. Can you make every side add to the same total? Try it. Which totals can you make?

For any pair that chooses Go further. Use it as a pair choice, or with volunteers in talk time (never call a child by name). Ask for the reason every time. Q3 is open: there is no single right answer, so ask children to check each other's work. Answers: 1) Corners 2, 4, 6. Between 2 and 4: 5. Between 4 and 6: 1. Between 6 and 2: 3. (It is the only set of corners that works.) 2) Ben 7, Mei 4. (20 − 9 = 11 are shared; take away Ben's extra 3: 8, share 4 and 4; Ben has 4 + 3 = 7.) 3) Open. Yes, it still works. Totals 12, 13, 14 and 15 are possible. Example for 12: corners 2, 3, 4 with 7 between 2 and 3, 5 between 3 and 4 and 6 between 4 and 2.
