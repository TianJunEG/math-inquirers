Stretch

1. 13 + 7 = 10 + __ = 4 + __

2. □ + □ + 4 = 20, and both boxes are the same number. What is in the box?

3. How many pairs make 20 if both numbers are from 5 to 15? (7 + 13 and 13 + 7 count once.)

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 10 and 16 (all three are 20). 2) 8 (20 − 4 = 16, and 8 + 8 = 16). 3) 6 pairs: 5+15, 6+14, 7+13, 8+12, 9+11, 10+10.
