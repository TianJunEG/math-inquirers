Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Take a ten-frame and 10 counters of two colours.
  2. Fill the ten-frame. Write the number of each colour, like 6 + 4.
  3. Change some counters to make a new pair. Write it down.
  4. Keep going until you think you have found them all.

Go further: your choice, any time

What if we use 12 counters? Make 12 with two colours in as many ways as you can. What do you notice?

Math Inquirers Level 1 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs work with the materials: Ten-frames, red and yellow counters, a cup, a paper triangle with 6 circles. Pairs make 10 on a ten-frame with two colours in as many ways as they can and record each pair. Then ask: how do you know you have them all? Online: Slide ten-frame with a hidden-counter reveal; each child uses 10 coins or paper pieces of two colours. Go further (what-if) is on this slide from the start: any pair may choose it at any time, so nobody waits. The tutor does not choose who and does not announce it. Answers: 7 different pairs if 3 + 9 and 9 + 3 count as the same: 0 + 12, 1 + 11, 2 + 10, 3 + 9, 4 + 8, 5 + 7, 6 + 6. The first number goes up by 1 as the second goes down by 1.
