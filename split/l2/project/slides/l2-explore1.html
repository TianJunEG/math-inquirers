Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Take a ten-frame and 10 counters of two colours.
  2. Fill the ten-frame. Write the number of each colour, like 6 + 4.
  3. Change some counters to make a new pair. Write it down.
  4. Keep going until you think you have found them all.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs work with the materials: Ten-frames, red and yellow counters, a cup, a paper triangle with 6 circles. Pairs make 10 on a ten-frame with two colours in as many ways as they can and record each pair. Then ask: how do you know you have them all? Online: Slide ten-frame with a hidden-counter reveal; each child uses 10 coins or paper pieces of two colours.
