Key idea: bonds and equals

List bonds in order so none are missed. Bonds of 10 help with bonds of 20.

"=" means both sides have the same value.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key ideas together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Reading "=" as "the answer comes next" (5 + 3 = 4 + __ answered 8). (2) Counting 3 + 7 and 7 + 3 as different pairs when asked for different pairs. (3) Magic triangle: trying numbers at random instead of thinking about the corners.
