Key idea: bonds and equals

Bonds of 10 are the building blocks for bonds of 20.

“=” means both sides have the same value.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 33 to 35. Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Reading "=" as "the answer comes next" (5 + 3 = 4 + __ answered 8). (2) Counting 3+7 and 7+3 as different bonds when asked for different pairs. (3) Magic triangle: trying numbers at random instead of looking at the corners.
