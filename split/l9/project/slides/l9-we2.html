Worked example: not starting at 0

A key lies from the 2 cm mark to the 9 cm mark. How long is the key?

Step 1. The key does not start at 0, so 9 is NOT the answer.

Step 2. Count the spaces from 2 to 9: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

Step 3. The key is 7 cm long. Check: 9 − 2 = 7 ✓

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example (a ruler that does not start at 0, as if the end of the ruler were broken). Ask first: "How long is the key?" Many children say 9. Click 1: "9 is where the key ends, not how long it is. It started at 2, not 0." Click 2 and the jumps: count the spaces aloud together. Click 3: "Another way: from 0 to 9 is 9 spaces, but the first 2 spaces are not under the key. 9 − 2 = 7." Check by sliding a real key or pencil so it starts at 0. Think-aloud: "Where does it start? Where does it end? I count the spaces in between."
