Think harder: the clock

1. Look at the clock.

a) Write the time.

b) What time is it 1 hour later?

c) The minute hand moves from the 12 to the 4. Count in 5s. How many minutes pass?

Optional slide for pairs who finish early or ask for more. Show it only on request and never announce who is working on it. Children write the answer on their own paper and say it together on three; nothing is held up to the camera. Answers and tutor lines are for you only. Item 1 (Time: o'clock, half past and minutes in fives; tool of the day idea: Act it out). SAY: "Use a paper clock. Move the minute hand and count in fives." ASK: "Where is the minute hand at half past? How many minutes from the 12 to the 6?" LISTEN FOR: Each number on the clock is 5 minutes. ANSWER: a) 7:30 (half past 7). b) 8:30. c) 20 minutes (5, 10, 15, 20).
