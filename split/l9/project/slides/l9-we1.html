Worked example: compare two lengths

A red rope is 8 cubes long. A blue rope is 5 cubes long.

Step 1. Line up the left ends. Red sticks out by 3 cubes: 8 − 5 = 3. Red is 3 cubes longer.

Step 2. End to end: 8 + 5 = 13 cubes.

Step 3. Cut 3 cubes off red: 8 − 3 = 5. Now red is the same as blue.

Math Inquirers Level 1 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. Before each click ask the group to predict. Step 1 then the yellow highlight: "I line up the left ends, like a race start. Red goes further. The part that sticks out is the difference: 3 cubes. 8 take away 5 is 3." Step 2 then the long bar: "End to end means one after the other, so I add: 8 + 5 = 13." Step 3 then the cut bar: "If I cut 3 cubes off red, 8 − 3 = 5, the same as blue." Common mistake: answering "8" to "how much longer" (the length, not the difference). Point at the highlighted part.
