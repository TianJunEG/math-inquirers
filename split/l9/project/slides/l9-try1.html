Your turn: whiteboards

Draw two bars. Line up the left ends.

a A ribbon is 9 cubes. A string is 6 cubes. How much longer is the ribbon?

3 cubes

b Put the ribbon and the string end to end. How long?

15 cubes

c A 10-cube rope is cut 4 cubes shorter. How long is it now?

6 cubes

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards up together, then click. a) 9 − 6 = 3. b) 9 + 6 = 15. c) 10 − 4 = 6. Ask a child to show the part that sticks out on their bars for a. A child who writes 9 for a has given the length, not the difference.
