Quick questions

1. A ribbon is 7 cubes. A string is 4 cubes. Which is longer?

2. How many cubes longer is it?

3. Crayon 5 cubes, pen 9 cubes, pencil 7 cubes. Shortest first?

4. How do we compare two lengths fairly?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 10. Read each question aloud, give thinking time, take answers, then click to show the answer. Ask "How do you know?" each time. Answers: 1) The ribbon (7 cubes is more than 4 cubes). 2) 3 cubes (7 − 4 = 3; or count on from 4 to 7). 3) Crayon (5), pencil (7), pen (9). 4) Line up one end of each object at the same starting line, then look at the other end. Watch for children who say "the pen is longest because it has the biggest number" without saying the units are the same: praise it, then add "because they were all measured in the same cubes".
