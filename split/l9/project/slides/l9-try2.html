Your turn: how long?

Count the cm spaces between the two ends.

a How long is the eraser?

4 cm

b A pencil lies from the 6 cm mark to the 11 cm mark.

5 cm

c A clip lies from the 1 cm mark to the 4 cm mark.

3 cm

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) The eraser goes from 2 to 6: 4 spaces, 4 cm (6 − 2 = 4). b) 11 − 6 = 5 cm. c) 4 − 1 = 3 cm. A child who writes 6, 11 or 4 is reading the end mark only: ask "Where does it start?" Let them check b and c on a real ruler.
