Change the unit: both ways

1 clip is as long as 2 erasers.

Clips to erasers: a rope is 4 clips long.

Step A. Each clip is 2 erasers: 2, 4, 6, 8. The rope is 8 erasers long.

Erasers to clips: a book is 6 erasers long.

Step B. Put the erasers in groups of 2: 2, 4, 6. That is 3 groups, so the book is 3 clips long.

To a smaller unit: the number gets bigger. To a bigger unit: the number gets smaller.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4 goes both ways). Left side, click 1: draw 2 erasers under each clip. Click 2: count in 2s along the erasers: 2, 4, 6, 8. Think-aloud: "4 clips, and every clip is 2 erasers, so 2 and 2 and 2 and 2: 8 erasers." Right side, click 3: ring the erasers in 2s. Click 4: "6 erasers make 3 groups of 2, so 3 clips. I check: 2 + 2 + 2 = 6." Click 5: the summary. Ask: "Changing to erasers, did the number get bigger or smaller? Why?" The teaching slides and the worksheet both use 1 clip = 2 erasers, so the worksheet Q4 is the same method (5 clips; 12 erasers).
