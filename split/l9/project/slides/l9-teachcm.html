Centimetres: measure with a ruler

A ruler is a row of equal units called centimetres (cm). Each space between two marks is 1 cm.

Step 1. Put one end of the crayon at 0.

Step 2. Read the number at the other end: 7. The crayon is 7 cm long.

Check: count the spaces, not the marks: 7 spaces, 7 cm.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Give out rulers. "Cubes are our own unit. Everyone in the world uses the same unit: the centimetre. A ruler is a row of centimetres stuck together." Show that each space from one mark to the next is 1 cm (a fingertip wide). Click 1 and 2. Click 3 shows the jumps: "The number 7 tells us 7 spaces from 0." Click 4: "We count spaces, not marks. If I counted the marks from 0 to 7 I would get 8, which is wrong." Some rulers have a blank bit before 0: show where 0 is on yours.
