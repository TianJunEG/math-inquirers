LESSON 9 • WEEK 5

Measure It!

Length

TODAY WE WILL

Measure with equal units and a cm ruler, compare lengths, and change from one unit to another.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Compare and order lengths, measure with equal units and with a centimetre ruler (also when the object does not start at 0), and change a length from one unit to another in both directions. Materials: Linking cubes or paper cube strips, paper clips, erasers, objects to measure, a cm ruler for each child, mini whiteboards. Online: Print the cube strip at the back of the workbook; measure household objects. Time plan: opening 0 to 2, warm-up 2 to 8 (game 2 to 6, quick questions 6 to 8; ask questions 1 to 3, and 4 and 5 only if you are on time), sneak-peek puzzles 8 to 12, explore 12 to 27, teaching 27 to 37 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 37 to 41, worksheet 41 to 72 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it (a 30-second stand-and-stretch reset after the page-1 check, taken from that slot; the last 4 minutes check page 2, after a 1-minute stand-up stretch), talk time 72 to 79, exit ticket 79 to 85, look-back 85 to 88, then flex time and goodbye 88 to 90 (this is the buffer: if you are behind, use it; do not start anything new). Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are for every child to choose, any time or at home. Nobody is told which page they should be on.
