Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 88 to 90 (1 minute, after the exit ticket). Children tick or write the "What was hard?" box in their workbook. Ask one or two children to share one line; never mark or grade it. Praise the strategy ("Drawing it got you unstuck"), not the speed. Say: "Tick what was hard, tick what helped you get unstuck, and write one thing you will try next time."
