Story break: Al-Biruni

Optional • about 2 minutes • we can skip it

Al-Biruni lived about a thousand years ago. He was born in Khwarazm, in Central Asia, and he loved to measure things. He wanted to know how big the Earth is. He measured the distance between two spots near a hill, and the angles up to the top, to find how tall the hill was. Then he climbed it and looked at the horizon. With a little maths he found a size for the Earth that is close to today's value.

Al-Biruni

Central Asia (Khwarazm) • 973-1048

Try it (if you like)

Al-Biruni needed careful measuring. A rod is 10 cm long. A table is 6 rods long. How long is the table in cm?
