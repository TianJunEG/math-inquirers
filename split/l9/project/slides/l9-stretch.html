For early finishers

Stretch

1. A crayon lies from the 3 cm mark to the 11 cm mark on a ruler. How long is it?

2. A pencil is 3 erasers long. A ruler is 2 pencils long. A desk is 4 rulers long. How many erasers long is the desk?

3. A ribbon 15 cubes long is cut into 3 equal pieces. How long is each piece? How many cuts?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) 8 cm, not 11 (count the spaces from 3 to 11, or 11 − 3 = 8). 2) 24 erasers (a ruler is 3 + 3 = 6 erasers; a desk is 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 = 24). 3) 5 cubes each (5 + 5 + 5 = 15) and 2 cuts: 3 pieces need only 2 cuts. Let them check 3 with a cube strip and scissors or by drawing.
