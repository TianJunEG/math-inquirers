Stretch

1. A ribbon 15 cubes long is cut into 3 equal pieces. How long is each piece? How many cuts?

2. A string is 6 paper clips long. 1 paper clip is as long as 2 cubes. Ben says the string is 12 cubes long. Mei says it is 8 cubes long. Who is right? How do you know?

3. A pencil is 8 cm long. It starts at the 3 cm mark on a ruler. At which mark does it end?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early (these are on the slide only; Think Harder T1 to T4 are in the workbook). Answers: 1) 5 cubes each (5 + 5 + 5 = 15) and 2 cuts: 3 pieces need only 2 cuts. 2) Ben. Six clips of 2 cubes each: 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 12 cubes. 3) The 11 cm mark (3 + 8 = 11).
