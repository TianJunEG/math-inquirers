Think harder

T1 A ribbon lies from the 3 cm mark to the 11 cm mark on a ruler. How long is it?

T2 A red ribbon lies from the 2 cm mark to the 9 cm mark. A blue ribbon lies from the 5 cm mark to the 11 cm mark. Which ribbon is longer? By how many cm?

T3 1 clip is as long as 3 cubes. A pen is 12 cubes long. How many clips long is the pen? A ruler is 5 clips long. How many cubes long is the ruler?

T4 A pencil is 3 erasers long. A ruler is 2 pencils long. A desk is 4 rulers long. How many erasers long is the desk?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For any pair that chooses to try more. These are the same four questions as Think harder T1 to T4 in the workbook (the labels match). Answers: T1) 8 cm (count the spaces from 3 to 11, or 11 − 3) T2) Red is longer: red 7 cm, blue 6 cm, so red is 1 cm longer. T3) 4 clips. 15 cubes. T4) 24 erasers (a ruler is 3 + 3 = 6 erasers; a desk is 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 = 24)
