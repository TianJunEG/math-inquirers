33–35 MIN

Key idea: equal units

Use equal units, no gaps, no overlaps, and start at zero.

A bigger unit gives a smaller number.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 33 to 35. Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Leaving gaps or overlapping the cubes. (2) Not starting at zero. (3) Thinking more units means a bigger unit.
