Challenge

1. A train has 3 carriages. Each is 6 cubes long. There is a gap of 1 cube between carriages. How long is the whole train? What if there are 5 carriages?

2. A ribbon 30 cubes long is cut into three pieces. The first is 4 cubes longer than the second. The third is 4 cubes shorter than the second. How long is each piece?

Make up your own

3. Make up your own measuring puzzle that uses TWO different units (for example clips and cubes). Write the puzzle and its answer. Give it to a partner and check their answer.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who have finished the topic. Use as a pair challenge or in talk time. Ask for the reason every time. Q3 is open: there is no single right answer, so ask children to check each other's work. Answers: 1) 20 cubes (18 + 2 gaps). With 5 carriages: 30 + 4 gaps = 34 cubes. There is always 1 gap fewer than carriages. 2) 14, 10 and 6 cubes. 3) Open. Check that the link between the units is stated and the answer works. Example: 1 clip is as long as 4 cubes. A snake is 5 clips long. How many cubes long is it? Answer: 20 cubes.
