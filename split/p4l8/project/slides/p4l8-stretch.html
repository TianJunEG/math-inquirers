Think harder

1. A cuboid is 3 cm long, 2 cm wide and 1 cm high. Its net is drawn on 1 cm squared paper. How many squares does the net cover?

2. Ben puts a dice on the table. The top face shows 3 and the front face shows 5. What numbers are on the bottom face and on the back face?

3. A dice is on a table. Mei walks round it and can see the top and the four side faces. The numbers she can see add up to 19. What number is on the bottom face?

4. There are 5 different shapes made of 4 squares joined edge to edge (a line, a square, an L, a T and a zigzag). Which one has exactly 1 line of symmetry?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers (same four questions as the printed Think harder page): 1) 22 squares: 2 faces of 3 by 2 (6 each), 2 faces of 3 by 1 (3 each) and 2 faces of 2 by 1 (2 each). 12 + 6 + 4 = 22. 2) Bottom 4 (3 + 4 = 7). Back 2 (5 + 2 = 7). 3) 2. All six faces add up to 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 = 21, and 21 − 19 = 2. 4) Only the T. The line has 2, the square has 4, the L and the zigzag have 0.
