Stretch

1. Which shape made of 5 squares joined edge to edge has exactly 2 lines of symmetry?

2. Ben folds a net into a cube. Each face is a different colour. How many faces touch the red face along an edge? Could 5 faces touch it?

3. Shade 2 squares of a 3 by 3 grid so that the pattern has exactly 2 lines of symmetry. Find one way.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) The row of 5 squares (one line along the row and one up-down through the middle square). The plus has 4, the T-like shapes have 1. 2) 4 faces touch it; 5 is impossible because the face opposite red never touches it. 3) Answers vary: the top-middle and bottom-middle squares (up-down and across lines), the left-middle and right-middle squares, or two opposite corners (the two diagonals).
