This dice net shows 3, 2 and 1. Opposite faces add up to 7. Which number goes in the square marked "?"

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 80 to 90. Answer: 4. The 3, 2, 1 and ? squares make a staircase: the 3 and the ? are the two ends of the step, so they are opposite, and 3 + 4 = 7. Children who write 6 or 5 chose a square next to it. Extension: fill in the other two squares. The square right of the ? is opposite the 1, so 6; the bottom-right square is opposite the 2, so 5.
