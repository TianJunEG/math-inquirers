Opposite faces: look for a line of 3

In a line of 3 squares, the two ends are opposite. The middle one folds between them.

Step 1. B, C, D is a line of 3: B and D are opposite.

Step 2. A, B, F is a line of 3 going down: A and F are opposite.

Step 3. C, D, E is a line of 3: C and E are opposite.

Opposite pairs: B and D, A and F, C and E.

Check: each letter is used once, and a pair never touches along an edge once folded.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before the worksheet (Q2 has a different net). Use the paper model: lay it flat, fold it, and point to the faces. Click 1: "Look along the row: B, C, D. When I fold, C is the bottom and B and D stand up on opposite sides. So B and D face each other." Click 2: the same going down: A, B, F. Click 3: C, D, E. Do NOT say "squares next to each other are opposite": opposite faces have exactly ONE square between them in a line. Ask: "Which net squares are NOT in a line of 3? Can you still find their partner?" (The last pair is what is left over.)
