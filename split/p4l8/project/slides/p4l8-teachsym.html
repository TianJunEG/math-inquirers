Complete the figure: count from the line

Make the dashed line a line of symmetry.

Step 1. The roof corner is 4 squares LEFT of the line. Put a dot 4 squares RIGHT.

Step 2. The bottom corner is also 4 squares left. Put a dot 4 squares right, same height.

Step 3. Join the dots in the same order. The two halves now match.

Flip, do not slide: the far corners swap sides.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4 has a rocket and a fish; this is a house). Click 1: run a finger along the row of squares from the line to the roof corner: 4. "A partner is the same distance on the other side." Click 2: the bottom corner. Click 3: join. Think-aloud: "Every corner needs a partner at the same height and the same distance from the line. I count squares; I do not guess." Common mistake: copying the half by sliding it to the right (it would look the same but not flipped). Ask: "If the left roof slopes down to the left, which way does the right roof slope?"
