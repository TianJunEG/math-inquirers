Key idea: fold it in your head

A net needs one base, one lid and four walls, with nothing in the same place twice. In a line of 3 squares, the two ends are opposite.

A line of symmetry is a fold line: the two halves match exactly.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key ideas together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Thinking any 6 squares make a cube, and ticking shapes with a 2 by 2 block, 5 squares in a row, or two squares that close the same end. (2) Saying two squares next to each other are opposite, instead of the ends of a line of 3. (3) Saying a rectangle has 4 lines of symmetry (the diagonal does not work), or completing a symmetric figure by sliding the half across instead of flipping it.
