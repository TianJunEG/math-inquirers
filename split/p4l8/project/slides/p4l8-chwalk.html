Q1: count the hidden faces

Two dice: 21 + 21 = 42 (1 + 2 + … + 6 = 21 each)

Hidden: the 2 glued faces, both the same number

42 − 36 = 6 for the 2 hidden faces

6 ÷ 2 = 3 on each glued face ✓

Q2: the middle, then a team of 4

The middle square is on all 4 lines: it must be shaded.

Every other square has 3 partners: the 4 corners go together, the 4 edge squares go together.

5 = 1 + 4: shade the middle and one team.

Two teams, so 2 ways: an X or a plus.

Use after pairs have tried. Q1: the trick is to count the hidden faces, not the visible ones. Check: if the glued number were 2, the hidden total would be 4 and the visible total 38. Q2: a square on a line of symmetry stays put when we fold along that line; the middle square lies on all four, so it must be shaded for any pattern to be symmetric under every line (or be empty). A corner square flips to another corner, so if one corner is shaded all four must be: that is a team of 4. The edge-middle squares are another team of 4.
