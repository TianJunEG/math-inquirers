Your turn: does it fold into a cube?

Fold it in your head. Write YES or NO and say why.

a A row of 4 with one square above the first and one below the last.

YES

b A 2 by 3 rectangle of squares.

NO

c A column of 4 with two squares sticking out in a row.

NO

Math Inquirers Level 4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards (YES or NO), then click. a) YES: base the second square of the row; the other squares fold as walls; the square above the first swings to the back and the square below the last swings to the front. b) NO: it has a 2 by 2 block; 4 squares around one corner would be needed, but only 3 faces meet at a corner of a cube, so two squares overlap. c) NO: the two squares in a row stick out and one lands on top of the other (both want the same face). Ask a child to show each with a finger fold.
