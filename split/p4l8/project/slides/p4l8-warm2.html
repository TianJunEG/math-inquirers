Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 10. Hold up a real cube or dice if you can. Whiteboards up together, then click. After 3 ask: "Where do the corners meet? How many faces meet at one corner?" (3.) After 4: "Why must opposite faces add up to 7 on a dice?" (It is how dice are made: 1 and 6, 2 and 5, 3 and 4.) After 5: "Show me the two folds." Answers: 6, 12, 8, 4, 2.
