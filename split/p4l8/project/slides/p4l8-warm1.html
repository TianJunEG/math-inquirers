Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6.5 to 8.5 (one short round, about 90 seconds: the Sneak-peek puzzles came first). Fold It in Your Head. Show one letter at a time (the clicks reveal H, T, E, L, U, Z in order). Count 3, 2, 1: fingers up for the number of lines of symmetry. Then a child shows with a finger where the fold line goes. Answers: H 2 (up-down and across), T 1 (up-down), E 1 (across), L 0, U 1 (up-down), Z 0. A line of symmetry is a fold line where the two halves match exactly. Z is the surprise: it looks balanced, but folding it never makes the halves match (it turns upside down onto itself, which is a different idea). Hold up a small mirror along a line if you have one: if the picture in the mirror looks like the whole letter, it is a line of symmetry.
