LESSON 8 • WEEK 4 • 0–1 MIN

Symmetry and Nets

Symmetry, nets of a cube

TODAY WE WILL

Find out which shapes fold into a cube, find opposite faces on a dice net, and complete symmetric figures.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Decide whether a shape made of 6 squares folds into a cube by folding it in your head, find which faces of a net are opposite (and use it for dice), complete a figure so that a given line is a line of symmetry, find all the lines of symmetry of a shape, and shade squares to make a pattern symmetric. Thinking strategies taught today: FOLD IT IN YOUR HEAD (base, walls, lid), TEST EVERY CASE, and COUNT THE SQUARES FROM THE LINE. Materials: squared paper (2 cm squares), scissors, sticky tape, 6 paper squares per child, a dice, a small mirror (optional), mini whiteboards, coloured pencils. Online: children cut out 6 paper squares before the lesson and fold on camera; the tutor clicks to reveal each fold on the slides and shades the grids. Time plan: opening 0 to 1, sneak-peek puzzles 1 to 4 (two ideas we have not learnt yet; answers shown to the tutor only, on camera, nobody marked), warm-up game 4 to 6 (one short round), quick questions 6 to 8, explore 8 to 18 (a Go further what-if is on the slide from minute 8), teaching 18 to 30 (click to reveal each step and answer; the "your turn" slides are optional, skip them if short of time), brain break 30 to 34, worksheet page 1 (Start here, in pairs) 34 to 50 and check together 50 to 53, short teaching before page 2 53 to 58, worksheet page 2 (on your own) 58 to 76 and check together 76 to 80, talk time 80 to 85, exit ticket 85 to 88, looking back (reflection) 88 to 90 (2 minutes). After question 3 every child may choose: carry on, or try the Think harder page. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation have no fixed time slot: any child may choose them at any time, or take them home. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and pairs are not split unless both children agree. Online: page 1 is pair work, so allow about 2 minutes to move into and out of breakout rooms (or run page 1 as a whole-group screen-share if there are only 8 children).
