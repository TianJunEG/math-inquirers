LESSON 8 • WEEK 4 • 0–1.5 MIN

Symmetry and Nets

Symmetry, nets of a cube

TODAY WE WILL

Find out which shapes fold into a cube, find opposite faces on a dice net, and complete symmetric figures.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Decide whether a shape made of 6 squares folds into a cube by folding it in your head, find which faces of a net are opposite (and use it for dice), complete a figure so that a given line is a line of symmetry, find all the lines of symmetry of a shape, and shade squares to make a pattern symmetric. Thinking strategies taught today: FOLD IT IN YOUR HEAD (base, walls, lid), TEST EVERY CASE, and COUNT THE SQUARES FROM THE LINE. Materials: squared paper (2 cm squares), scissors, sticky tape, 6 paper squares per child, a dice, a small mirror (optional), mini whiteboards, coloured pencils. Online: children cut out 6 paper squares before the lesson and fold on camera; the tutor clicks to reveal each fold on the slides and shades the grids. Time plan (90 minutes in all; every part has a budget and there is a 2.5-minute standing buffer inside the page-2 block). Opening 0 to 1.5. Sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 6.5 (puzzle 1 about 60 seconds, puzzle 2 about 90 seconds, then ONE hold-up moment for both papers; answers shown to the tutor only, on camera, nobody marked). Warm-up game 6.5 to 8.5 (one short round, about 90 seconds), quick questions 8.5 to 11.5 (three questions). Explore 11.5 to 25.5 (the only breakout of the first half: about 60 seconds each way, closed by a 60-second timer; a Go further what-if is on the slide from the start; two debrief questions). Teaching 25.5 to 34 (click to reveal each step and answer; the one "your turn" slide is optional: skip it if you are behind). Brain break 34 to 37.5 (water and toilet first, then the game; clock check at the end of the break: if you are more than 1.5 minutes behind, skip the next optional item, never a check, the exit ticket or Looking back). Worksheet 37.5 to 80: page 1 (Start here, in pairs) 37.5 to 56.5, check together 56.5 to 59, short teaching before page 2 59 to 62.5, page 2 on your own 62.5 to 77.5 (the last 2.5 minutes are the standing buffer: if earlier parts ran long, spend the buffer first, then the choice questions, and never the checks, the exit ticket or Looking back), check together 77.5 to 80. Talk time 80 to 84 (one minute of pair talk, no breakout). Exit ticket 84 to 86.5. Looking back 86.5 to 90 (2 minutes of writing). After question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation have no fixed time slot: any child may choose them at any time, or take them home. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and pairs are not split unless both children agree. Online: page 1 is pair work, so allow about 2 minutes to move into and out of breakout rooms (or run page 1 as a whole-group screen-share if there are only 8 children).
