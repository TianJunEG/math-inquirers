Shade one more square

Make the dashed line a line of symmetry. Check every shaded square has a partner.

Step 1. Top row: the left corner and the right corner are the same distance from the line. They are partners already.

Step 2. Second row: the shaded square is 1 square left of the line. Its partner, 1 square right of the line, is not shaded.

Step 3. Shade it. Now every shaded square has a partner. Only 1 way works.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6 has a different pattern and two lines). Method: take each shaded square in turn, flip it over the line, and check the partner is shaded. A square with an unshaded partner is the clue: that partner is the one to shade. Ask: "Could I shade a different square instead?" (No: any other square would have no partner.) Q6 on the worksheet has two dashed lines, so there are two ways: one for each line.
