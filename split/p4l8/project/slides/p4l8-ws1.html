Page 1 (Start here): with your partner

Q1 Six shapes of 6 squares. Which fold into a cube? Say why the others do not.

Q2 A lettered net. Which letter is opposite which?

Q3 Dice nets. Opposite faces add up to 7.

Stuck? Cut the shape out and fold it, or colour the base first. After question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder.

Minutes 37.5 to 56.5. Pairs do Q1 to Q3. A child who is slow on Q1 (mental folding) still has Q2 and Q3 as the carry-on questions. Walk around. Ask "How do you know?" before giving any help. For Q1 ask pairs to fold each shape in their heads first, then check one with paper. For Q3(b) ask: "Which squares are the ends of a line of 3?" CHOOSING (printed on page 1: "After question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder."): invite the WHOLE group ("when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation"); never message a child privately, never name a child, never announce who is on which page. If one child of a pair wants to move on, do not split the pair unless both agree; if a pair does lose a member, join the other child to another pair or to yourself straight away. Children on Think harder keep working through the page check; reading along is optional.
