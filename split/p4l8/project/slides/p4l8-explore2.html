Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 22 to 25.5. Pencils down (or when the 60-second timer closes the rooms), then gather the shapes on the board. Two debrief questions only. Ask "Is this one the same as that one, just turned or flipped?" Answers: 1) There are 11 different nets of a cube altogether (turned or flipped copies count as the same); most groups find 4 to 7. 2) Two squares landed on the same face: for example a 2 by 2 block (4 squares meeting at a corner, but only 3 faces meet at a cube corner), 5 squares in a row, or two squares closing the same end. The third question (do all the shapes that work have 4 squares in a row? No: the staircase and the 3-3 net have a row of 3 and no row of 4) is not asked. Do NOT give the method yet if nobody finds it; the teaching part comes next.
