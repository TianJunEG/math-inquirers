Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Take 6 paper squares.
  2. Join them edge to edge (whole sides touching). Tape them.
  3. Try to fold your shape into a cube: no gaps, no overlaps.
  4. Draw every shape that works. Did any fail? Why?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs join 6 paper squares and try to fold them into a cube (online: fold on camera, or draw the shape on squared paper and fold in their heads). Ask them to predict first: "Will every shape work?" Let them find out. Early finishers: find a shape that does NOT fold into a cube, and say why it fails.
