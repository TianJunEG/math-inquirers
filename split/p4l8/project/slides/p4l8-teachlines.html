Find ALL the lines of symmetry

Step 1. A rectangle: up-down and across. That is 2 lines.

Step 2. Is the corner-to-corner line a line of symmetry? Fold along it: the long side lands on the short side, so the halves do NOT match. Not this one.

Step 3. A square has 4 lines: up-down, across and both corner-to-corner lines.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5). Use a paper rectangle: fold it up-down and across (the halves match) and then fold it corner to corner (the corners do not meet: the long edge lies beside the short edge). Systematic method: try up-down, across, then the two corner-to-corner lines, and mark each that works. Mention: a shape can have 0 lines (like the letter Z); that is a perfectly good answer.
