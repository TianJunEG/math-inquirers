Explore: talk about it

1. How many ways did you find for 20¢?

2. How do you know you have all of them?

3. Are 10 + 5 + 5 and 5 + 10 + 5 different?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Let pairs keep working and move around the room (or breakout rooms). Gather them to answer these. Answers: 1) 4 ways with 20¢, 10¢ and 5¢ coins. 2) Start with the biggest coin and work down. 3) No. The same coins, in a different order.
