ANA'S PUZZLE

I think of a number. I multiply by 5. Then I subtract 4. My answer is 41.

Ana wrote: 41 ÷ 5 … hmm, it does not come out evenly. So she wrote 41 − 4 = 37.

What went wrong? Vote:

👍 A: She undid the steps in the wrong order.

❤️ B: She subtracted 4 again, but subtracting is undone by adding.

😮 C: Nothing is wrong.

Both A and B are true! Right way: 41 + 4 = 45, 45 ÷ 5 = 9. Check: 9 × 5 = 45, 45 − 4 = 41. ✓
