Go further: a three-step mystery

CHALLENGE

I think of a number. I double it. I add 7. Then I multiply by 3. My answer is 57.

Type your number. Brave extra: could my answer ever be 39 if I multiply by 4 and add 6? Type yes or no, and why.

57 ÷ 3 = 19. 19 − 7 = 12. 12 ÷ 2 = 6. Check: 6 × 2 = 12, 12 + 7 = 19, 19 × 3 = 57. ✓

Extra: 39 − 6 = 33, and 33 ÷ 4 is not a whole number, so no whole number works.
