Routes on a grid: add as you go

Walk from A to B along the lines, going only right or up.

Along the bottom and up the left side there is only 1 way to each point.

Any other point is reached from the LEFT or from BELOW: add those two numbers.

1 + 2 = 3, 3 + 3 = 6, 4 + 6 = 10 routes to B

Math Inquirers Level 6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5 uses a 3 by 3 grid with a puddle: do not solve it here). Clicks: the 1s on the edges, the first note, the other numbers, the second note, the answer. Check by listing: a route is 3 rights and 2 ups in some order, e.g. RRRUU, RRURU …; there are 10 of them. Ask: "If a point were blocked by a puddle, what number would you write there?" (0: no routes go through it) but let them use this on the worksheet themselves.
