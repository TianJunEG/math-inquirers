Explore: the bubble tea shop

EXPLORE

  1. A bubble tea shop has 3 teas (milk, green, taro) and 2 toppings (pearls, jelly).
  2. On paper, list every order of 1 tea and 1 topping. How do you know you have them all?
  3. The shop adds sizes M and L. Predict how many orders now, then check.
