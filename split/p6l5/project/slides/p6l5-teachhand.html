Handshakes: count each pair once

5 friends. Every pair shakes hands once.

A shakes 4 new hands, B 3 new, C 2, D 1, E 0.

4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 10 handshakes

Or: 5 × 4 = 20 counts every handshake twice (A–B and B–A). 20 ÷ 2 = 10

Count the lines in the picture: 10 ✓

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4). Think-aloud: “5 friends shaking hands. Friend A shakes 4 hands. B has already shaken A’s, so 3 new ones. C: 2 new. D: 1 new. E: none left. 4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 10. Or: 5 people times 4 hands is 20, but each handshake has two people in it, so I counted every one twice: 20 ÷ 2 = 10.” Ask: "With 6 friends?" (5 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 15, or 6 × 5 ÷ 2). Do not do 7 friends: that is the worksheet.
