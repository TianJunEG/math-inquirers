Think harder

1. A set lunch is 1 main, 1 drink and 1 dessert. There are 4 mains and 3 drinks. There are 60 different set lunches. How many desserts are there?

2. How many 3-digit numbers can you make from the cards 1, 2, 3 and 4, using each card at most once? How many of them are even?

3. In a league of 6 teams, every team plays every other team twice (once at home, once away). How many matches are played?

4. (a) Mei packs 4 T-shirts, 3 pairs of shorts and 2 caps. She wants at least 40 outfits with a cap. What is the fewest number of extra pairs of shorts she must buy? (b) A party has 45 handshakes. How many people are there?

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

The same four questions as the printed Think harder page, for anyone who wants them. Answers: 1) 5 (4 × 3 = 12 and 60 ÷ 12 = 5) 2) 24 (4 × 3 × 2); 12 are even (the last card is 2 or 4: 2 choices, then 3 × 2 for the others) 3) 30 (6 × 5: each ordered pair is one home match) 4) (a) 2 extra pairs: with 5 pairs, 4 × 5 × 2 = 40; with 4 pairs only 4 × 4 × 2 = 32. (b) 10 people (9 + 8 + … + 1 = 45, or 10 × 9 ÷ 2 = 45).
