Think harder

1. A hexagon has 6 corners. How many diagonals does it have?

2. How many rectangles of any size can you find in a strip of 4 squares in a row?

3. 5 children line up for a photo. In how many different orders can they stand? In how many of these is Ali at the front?

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Extra questions for any pair that wants them (these are not on the Think harder page). Answers: 1) 9 (6 corners make 6 × 5 ÷ 2 = 15 lines; 6 of them are sides, so 9 are diagonals) 2) 10 (4 of length 1, 3 of length 2, 2 of length 3, 1 of length 4) 3) 120 (5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1); 24 with Ali at the front (4 × 3 × 2 × 1)
