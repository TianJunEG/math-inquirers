15–25 MIN

Explore: talk about it

1. How many orders with 3 teas and 2 toppings? With sizes M and L too?

2. How did you make sure you had every order, with none twice?

3. In your code, which loop goes round faster?

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Gather pairs (or bring them back from breakout rooms). Ask each, take two or three answers, then share one child’s screen to run their code. Answers: 1) 6 orders; with sizes, 12 (each of the 6 orders comes in 2 sizes: 6 × 2). 2) Fix the tea, go through every topping, then move to the next tea: a list in order, like a tree. 3) The inner loop: it runs all the way round (2 toppings) for every single turn of the outer loop (each tea). Watch for children who add (3 + 2 = 5): ask them to point to all 6 orders on their list.
