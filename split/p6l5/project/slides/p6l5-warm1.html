Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6.5 to 10.5. Count it, don’t list it. Four quick counting puzzles (two-digit numbers, multiples of 5, lamp posts, numbers with a 7). Pairs say how they counted. No racing and no points. Click once per card to show its answer (cards go left to right, top row first). Four puzzles only, so the warm-up fits. Answers: 90 two-digit numbers (99 − 10 + 1: counting from 10 to 99 includes both ends); 20 multiples of 5; 11 lamp posts (100 ÷ 10 = 10 gaps, plus 1); 19 numbers with a 7 (7, 17, …, 97 is 10; 70 to 79 is 10; 77 is in both lists, so 10 + 10 − 1). Ask after each: "How did you count without listing?" Praise the "+ 1" fix (fence posts: 11 lamp posts) and the "counted twice" fix (77): both come back today.
