Math Inquirers Level 6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 8. Count it, don’t list it. Six quick counting puzzles (pages 15 to 40, two-digit numbers, multiples of 5, lamp posts, numbers with a 7, even numbers). Pairs say how they counted. No racing and no points. Click once per card to show its answer (cards go left to right, top row first). Answers: 26 pages (40 − 15 = 25 counts the gaps, so add 1); 90 two-digit numbers (99 − 10 + 1); 20 multiples of 5; 11 lamp posts (100 ÷ 10 = 10 gaps, plus 1); 19 numbers with a 7 (7, 17, …, 97 is 10; 70 to 79 is 10; 77 is in both lists, so 10 + 10 − 1); 25 even numbers. Ask after each: "How did you count without listing?" Praise the "+ 1" fix (fence posts) and the "counted twice" fix (77): both come back today.
