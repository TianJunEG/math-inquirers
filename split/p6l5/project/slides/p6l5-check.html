3 MIN CHECK

Warm-up check

Two new puzzles. Whiteboard: your answer AND one reason. Hold up together.

Q1. A café meal is 1 bread (3 kinds), 1 filling (4 kinds) and 1 drink (2 kinds). How many different meals are there?

24 meals: 3 × 4 × 2. Choices made one after another multiply. Tree or list: fix the bread, then the filling, then the drink.

Q2. 5 chess clubs each play every other club once. How many games? Why is the answer not 5 × 4?

10 games. 5 × 4 = 20 counts every game twice (A against B is the same game as B against A), so halve it: 20 ÷ 2 = 10.
