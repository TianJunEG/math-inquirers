Sneak-peek puzzles

Two ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome. “I do not know” is welcome. Nobody is marked. Show your answer only to your tutor: own paper held up to them, or a private chat message.

Puzzle 1: try it with objects, a drawing or counting

You have 2 tops (red, blue) and 3 pairs of shorts (black, white, grey). Draw or list every different outfit (1 top and 1 pair of shorts). How many outfits?

6 outfits: red-black, red-white, red-grey, blue-black, blue-white, blue-grey. A drawing or an organised list works.

Puzzle 2: have a think

5 chess clubs each play every other club once. How many games is that? Is it 5 × 4?

10 games. 5 × 4 = 20 counts every game twice (A against B is the same game as B against A), so halve it. Guessing is fine.
