40.5–42.5 MIN

Digit cards: order matters, and watch the 0

3-digit numbers from 2, 4, 7: 3 × 2 × 1 = 6

247, 274, 427, 472, 724, 742

2-digit numbers from 0, 1, 2: the tens digit cannot be 0.

Tens: 2 choices (1 or 2). Ones: 2 left (0 is allowed here). 2 × 2 = 4

10, 12, 20, 21 ✓

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 40.5 to 42.5. Teach before worksheet page 1 (Q2, digit cards). Click through. Think-aloud: "Hundreds: any of 3 cards. Tens: one card is used, so 2 left. Ones: 1 left. 3 × 2 × 1 = 6. I list them in order to check: 247, 274, 427, 472, 724, 742." Then the 0 trap: "02 is not a 2-digit number, so the tens has only 2 choices, but the ones can be 0." Ask: "How many 3-digit numbers from 0, 1, 2?" (2 × 2 × 1 = 4: 102, 120, 201, 210).
