Go further: Ratio and the difference

Tool: Draw it

PREVIEW: a Primary 6 topic, beyond Level 5. A taste, not a test.

Kumar and Wei collect marbles in the ratio 7 : 4. Kumar's collection is bigger than Wei's by 24 marbles.

(a) How many parts is the difference between the two collections?

(b) How many marbles is one part?

(c) Find the number of marbles that each boy has.

(a) 3 parts.

(b) 8 marbles.

(c) Kumar 56 marbles and Wei 32 marbles.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

ADDED ITEM r7 (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Ratio and the difference"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Draw it. PREVIEW LABEL: say "This is a first taste of a topic from a later level. We are not testing it; we are noticing how it works." SAY: Draw two bars: Kumar 7 units, Wei 4 units. The extra 3 units on Kumar's bar are the 24 marbles. ASK: How many units is the 24? How many marbles is each unit? Can you check with the 7 : 4 and the difference of 24? LISTEN FOR: 7 − 4 = 3 units is 24 marbles, so 1 unit is 8. Kumar 7 × 8 = 56, Wei 4 × 8 = 32. Check: 56 − 32 = 24 and 56 : 32 = 7 : 4. Listen for "the difference in parts is the difference in marbles". Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) 3 parts. (b) 8 marbles. (c) Kumar 56 marbles and Wei 32 marbles. WHY: 3 parts = 24 marbles, so 1 part = 8 marbles. Kumar: 7 × 8 = 56. Wei: 4 × 8 = 32. Check: 56 − 32 = 24 and dividing both by 8 gives 7 : 4. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): Mei and Siti have stickers in the ratio 5 : 2. Mei has 36 more than Siti. How many does each have? Answer: Mei 60 stickers, Siti 24 stickers.
