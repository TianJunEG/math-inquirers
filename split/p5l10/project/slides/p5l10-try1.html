Whiteboards, then click. a) 19 − 4 = 15, 15 ÷ 3 = 5. Check 5 × 3 + 4 = 19. b) 9 + 5 = 14, 14 is half, so 28. Check 28 ÷ 2 = 14, 14 − 5 = 9. c) 14 + 6 = 20; 20 is 2/3 of the money (1/3 was spent); 1/3 = 10, so 30. Check 30 − 10 = 20, 20 − 6 = 14.
