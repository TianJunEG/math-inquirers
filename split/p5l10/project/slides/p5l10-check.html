1. Ali spends half of his money on a book. Then he spends half of the money he has LEFT on lunch. He has $6 left. How much did he have at first? Which strategy did you pick?

$24. Work backwards: 6 × 2 = 12 before lunch, 12 × 2 = 24 before the book.

2. How many squares of ALL sizes are in a grid of squares that is 2 squares tall and 3 squares wide? How did you count so that none is missed?

8: 6 small squares (1 by 1) and 2 squares of size 2 by 2.

Everyone does the check. Then some of us start on page 1 and some start on the Think Harder pages. Both are good choices, and you can swap.

Show the two questions; children answer on whiteboards and hold them up; ask one child per question "How do you know?" and then click to show the short answer. Answers and what to listen for: Q1: $24. Work backwards: 6 × 2 = 12 before lunch, 12 × 2 = 24 before the book.
