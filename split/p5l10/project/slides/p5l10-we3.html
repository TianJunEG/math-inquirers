Draw a model: units and an extra

Ali and Ben have $86 altogether. Ben has $6 more than 2/3 of Ali’s money. How much does each have?

Step 1. Take away the extra $6: 86 − 6 = 80. Now there are 3 + 2 = 5 equal units.

Step 2. 5 units = 80, so 1 unit = 80 ÷ 5 = $16.

Step 3. Ali: 3 × 16 = $48. Ben: 2 × 16 + 6 = $38.

Check BOTH clues: 48 + 38 = 86 ✓ 2/3 of 48 = 32, and 32 + 6 = 38 ✓

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example: a model. "Ali is 3 units because Ben has 2/3 of Ali (2 units), so Ali is split into 3 equal units. Ben has 2 of those units and an extra $6." Draw the bars on the board while children say what to draw. Click step 1: remove the extra before sharing equally. Step 2: 80 ÷ 5 = 16. Step 3: Ali 48 and Ben 38. Check both clues, not just the total. Common mistake: 86 ÷ 5 without taking away the $6 first (17.2, not a whole number: a sign something went wrong). Worksheet Q3 is the same kind with Mei and Ravi.
