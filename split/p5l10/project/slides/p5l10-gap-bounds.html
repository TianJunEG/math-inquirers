How many whole numbers between 300 and 600 have digits that add up to 6?

Write them from smallest to greatest.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional, if you finish early (no clock time): show it while some pairs are working or have finished, or use it live as an extra warm-up or tutor-bank question; never at a fixed minute, and it never takes time from the plan. ADDED ITEM bounds (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Counting numbers in bounds"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: List every way. SAY: Split into cases by the first digit: 3, 4 or 5. For each case, what must the other two digits add up to? ASK: Why is 600 not in the list? How many pairs of digits make 3? Make 2? Make 1? LISTEN FOR: First digit 3: other digits add to 3, so 4 numbers. First digit 4: add to 2, so 3 numbers. First digit 5: add to 1, so 2 numbers. 4 + 3 + 2 = 9. Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: 9 numbers: 303, 312, 321, 330, 402, 411, 420, 501, 510. WHY: First digit 3: 03, 12, 21, 30, so 303, 312, 321, 330. First digit 4: 02, 11, 20. First digit 5: 01, 10. A total of 4 + 3 + 2 = 9. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): How many whole numbers between 200 and 500 have a digit sum of 5? Answer: 9.
