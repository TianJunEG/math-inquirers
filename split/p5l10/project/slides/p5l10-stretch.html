For early finishers

Stretch

1. Ben has 14 coins, all 50¢ and 20¢ coins, worth $4.90. How many of each?

2. What is the smallest whole number bigger than 1 that leaves a remainder of 1 when divided by 2, by 3, by 4, by 5 and by 6?

3. 1/2 of a number is 5 more than 1/3 of the same number. What is the number?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 7 fifty-cent coins and 7 twenty-cent coins (suppose all 14 are 20¢: $2.80; $2.10 short; each swap adds 30¢; 210 ÷ 30 = 7; check 350 + 140 = 490). 2) 61 (1 more than the smallest number that 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 all divide into: 60). 3) 30 (a half minus a third is 1/6 of the number, and that is 5; check 15 and 10).
