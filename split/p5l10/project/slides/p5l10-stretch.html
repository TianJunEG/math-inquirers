These are the printed Think harder questions A1 to A4 (the same as the workbook). Answers: A1) $60. Work backwards: $24 is 2/3 of what was left, so that was $36; $36 is 3/5 of her money, so 1/5 is $12 and she started with $60. Check: 60 − 24 = 36, 36 − 12 = 24. A2) 15 numbers: 104, 113, 122, 131, 140, 203, 212, 221, 230, 302, 311, 320, 401, 410, 500. A3) 72 and 73 (145 is 1 more than twice 72). A4) Day 8. After 7 days and nights it is at 7 m.
