Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 14 to 21. Gather pairs, then take one card at a time. Answers: A) 4: work backwards, 5 × 3 = 15, 15 − 7 = 8, 8 ÷ 2 = 4; check forwards. B) Ben has the fish, Ali the cat, Mei the dog (Ben has no cat and no dog, so fish; Ali has no dog, so cat; Mei has the dog). C) 5 squares: 4 small and 1 big; most children first say 4. D) 13 and 7 (guess and check, or: take away the 6, share 14 into 7 and 7, give the 6 back to one). Write the starts on the board as children say them: "work backwards", "tick and cross grid", "count by size", "guess and check". Do NOT give a method before they have tried; the next slides name them.
