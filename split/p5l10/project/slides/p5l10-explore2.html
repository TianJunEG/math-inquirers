GO FURTHER: A SECOND WHAT-IF (OPTIONAL, FOR ANY PAIR)

Card A has a twin: a number is TRIPLED, then 4 is taken away, then the answer is divided by 2, and the result is 7. What was the number? Then make up your own card of this kind whose answer is 10.

Math Inquirers Level 5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 14 to 21. Gather pairs, then take one card at a time. Answers: A) 4: work backwards, 5 × 3 = 15, 15 − 7 = 8, 8 ÷ 2 = 4; check forwards. B) Ben has the fish, Ali the cat, Mei the dog (Ben has no cat and no dog, so fish; Ali has no dog, so cat; Mei has the dog). C) 5 squares: 4 small and 1 big; most children first say 4. D) 13 and 7 (guess and check, or: take away the 6, share 14 into 7 and 7, give the 6 back to one). Write the starts on the board as children say them: "work backwards", "tick and cross grid", "count by size", "guess and check". Do NOT give a method before they have tried; the next slides name them. GO FURTHER what-if (open to every pair from minute 9; nobody waits): 6. Work backwards: 7 × 2 = 14, 14 + 4 = 18, 18 ÷ 3 = 6. Check: 6 × 3 = 18, 18 − 4 = 14, 14 ÷ 2 = 7. Any new card is fine if its working backwards gives a whole number and it checks when run forwards.
