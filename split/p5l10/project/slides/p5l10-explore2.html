GO FURTHER: A SECOND WHAT-IF (OPTIONAL, IF YOU FINISH EARLY)

Solve a SECOND puzzle card (one your pair did not get), and say how you started. Then: card A has a twin: a number is TRIPLED, then 4 is taken away, then the answer is divided by 2, and the result is 7. What was the number? Then make up your own card of this kind whose answer is 10.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 23.5 to 27.5. Pencils down: gather the pairs, then take one card at a time. Answers: A) 4: work backwards, 5 × 3 = 15, 15 − 7 = 8, 8 ÷ 2 = 4; check forwards. B) Ben has the fish, Ali the cat, Mei the dog (Ben has no cat and no dog, so fish; Ali has no dog, so cat; Mei has the dog). C) 5 squares: 4 small and 1 big; most children first say 4. D) 13 and 7 (guess and check, or: take away the 6, share 14 into 7 and 7, give the 6 back to one). Write the starts on the board as children say them: "work backwards", "tick and cross grid", "count by size", "guess and check". Do NOT give a method before they have tried; the next slides name them. TWO questions together only (about a minute each), then move on. GO FURTHER what-if (open to every pair as soon as it has finished; nobody waits): 6. Work backwards: 7 × 2 = 14, 14 + 4 = 18, 18 ÷ 3 = 6. Check: 6 × 3 = 18, 18 − 4 = 14, 14 ÷ 2 = 7. Any new card is fine if its working backwards gives a whole number and it checks when run forwards.
