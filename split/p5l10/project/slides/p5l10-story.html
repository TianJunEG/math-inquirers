Story break: George Polya

1887–1985 • Hungary, Switzerland and the United States • A teacher who wrote a book about how to solve problems.

George Polya was born in Budapest, Hungary, in 1887. He later taught in Zurich, Switzerland, and then at Stanford University in the United States. In 1945 he published a book called How to Solve It, which has sold over a million copies. In it he gave four steps for any problem: understand the problem, make a plan, carry out the plan, and look back at your work. Looking back means checking your answer. He also described helpful ideas, called heuristics, such as drawing a picture or working backwards from the answer. Teachers around the world still use his ideas.
