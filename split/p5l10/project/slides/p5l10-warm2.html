Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 10. Whiteboards up together, then click. Answers: 1) 48 ÷ 4 = 12, × 3 = 36. 2) 10 × 6 ÷ 2 = 30 cm². 3) 6 ℓ in 2 min is 3 ℓ per minute; 45 ÷ 3 = 15 minutes. 4) 180° − 65° = 115°. 5) add 3: 17. Ask after 3: "Which start did you use: a table, a rate per minute, or something else?"
