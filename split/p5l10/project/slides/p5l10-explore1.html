Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs choose 2 of 4 puzzle cards (online: the tutor puts each card on the shared screen). Card A: a number is doubled, 7 is added, the answer is divided by 3, giving 5; find the number. Card B: Ali, Ben and Mei have a cat, a dog and a fish; Ali has no dog; Ben has no cat and no dog. Card C: how many squares of all sizes are in a 2 by 2 grid? Card D: two numbers add to 20 and differ by 6. Predict first: which card is the hardest? Children write the answer and how they started. Early finishers: make a puzzle card for the next pair.
