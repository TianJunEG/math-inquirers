Minutes 13 to 27.5 (the whole explore; this slide 13 to 23.5). Each pair solves ONE of the 4 puzzle cards and teaches it (online: send one card per breakout room as an image; the second card is the Go further what-if). Pair work is at most 6 minutes. Card A: a number is doubled, 7 is added, the answer is divided by 3, giving 5; find the number. Card B: Ali, Ben and Mei have a cat, a dog and a fish; Ali has no dog; Ben has no cat and no dog. Card C: how many squares of all sizes are in a 2 by 2 grid? Card D: two numbers add to 20 and differ by 6. Predict first: which card is the hardest? Children write the answer and how they started. Anyone who wants more (open to every pair): solve a second card, or make a puzzle card for the next pair. PROTECTED THINK TIME: before the pairs touch anything, say "Think for 30 seconds before anyone speaks" and show the clock; then pairs talk and start. PAIR WORK IS CAPPED AT 6 MINUTES: say "Pencils down" at minute 23.5 and bring the group back (the minutes before that include the 30 seconds of thinking, finding materials and, in lessons that use them, the move to and from breakout rooms). GO FURTHER (say it as soon as a pair has finished the task): any pair that finishes early may try the what-if question on the next slide. It is open to every pair, not a reward. Answer: 6. Work backwards: 7 × 2 = 14, 14 + 4 = 18, 18 ÷ 3 = 6. Check: 6 × 3 = 18, 18 − 4 = 14, 14 ÷ 2 = 7. Any new card is fine if its working backwards gives a whole number and it checks when run forwards.
