Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 2 (opening, inside the 90-minute clock). Objective: Choose a way to start a puzzle (draw a model, list in order, make a table, work backwards, guess and check or suppose, use a logic grid), solve competition-style problems, check every answer against every clue, and explain why the chosen start works. Materials: puzzle cards for 4 stations (backwards, logic, counting, guess and check), mini whiteboards, scrap paper, a 3 by 4 grid drawn on paper, certificates, stamps. Online: each breakout pair gets one puzzle card on screen and reports back how they started; logic grids and models are shared on screen and annotated together; children check their working against the slide and explain it aloud. Time plan (the exact minutes of this lesson; every lesson adds up to 90): opening 0 to 2, sneak-peek puzzles 2 to 6.5 (one check for both puzzles, no volunteer round, nobody is marked), warm-up 6.5 to 13 (one game round, four quick questions), explore 13 to 27.5 (pair work 6 minutes at most, then two questions together; a Go further what-if is on the explore slide for any pair that finishes), teaching 27.5 to 38 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 38 to 42, workbook page 1 42 to 55.5 (the questions without a star; after question 2 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder), check together 55.5 to 58.5, short teaching before page 2 58.5 to 60.5, workbook page 2 60.5 to 68 (alone: start with the questions named at the top, then choose; starred parts are take-home; nobody has to finish), check together 68 to 71, talk time 71 to 76.5 (you may say pass; volunteers first; the first step of the Investigation is take-home), exit ticket 76.5 to 81, reflection 81 to 83.5 (pass is always OK), certificates and goodbye 83.5 to 87, and the buffer 87 to 90 (floating time: spend it first if you run late, never fill it on purpose). Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child and have no clock time. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and never splits a pair unless both children agree. Invite the WHOLE group, never one child: "when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation."
