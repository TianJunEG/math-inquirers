TODAY WE WILL

Choose a start for a puzzle: draw a model, work backwards, use a logic grid, list in order or guess and check. Then check every clue.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Choose a way to start a puzzle (draw a model, list in order, make a table, work backwards, guess and check or suppose, use a logic grid), solve competition-style problems, check every answer against every clue, and explain why the chosen start works. Materials: puzzle cards for 4 stations (backwards, logic, counting, guess and check), mini whiteboards, scrap paper, a 3 by 4 grid drawn on paper, certificates, stamps. Online: each breakout pair gets one puzzle card on screen and reports back how they started; logic grids and models are shared on screen and annotated together; children hold their working up to the camera. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (how to choose a start: backwards, logic grid, model; click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 88, certificates 88 to 90.
