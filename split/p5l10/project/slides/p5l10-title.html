Choose a start for a puzzle: draw a model, work backwards, use a logic grid, list in order or guess and check. Then check every clue.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Choose a way to start a puzzle (draw a model, list in order, make a table, work backwards, guess and check or suppose, use a logic grid), solve competition-style problems, check every answer against every clue, and explain why the chosen start works. Materials: puzzle cards for 4 stations (backwards, logic, counting, guess and check), mini whiteboards, scrap paper, a 3 by 4 grid drawn on paper, certificates, stamps. Online: each breakout pair gets one puzzle card on screen and reports back how they started; logic grids and models are shared on screen and annotated together; children hold their working up to the camera. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 9, explore 9 to 21, teaching 21 to 33 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 33 to 37, worksheet page 1 37 to 49, check together 49 to 51, short teaching before page 2 51 to 57, worksheet page 2 57 to 72 (alone), check together 72 to 76, talk time 76 to 82, exit ticket 82 to 88, certificates 88 to 90. Stretch and challenge are for children who finish early, or for home.
