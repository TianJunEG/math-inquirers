ADDED ITEM evencap (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Greatest possible with a rule"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Guess and check. SAY: Start with the 6 biggest numbers and find their total. Is it even? If not, what is the smallest change you can make? ASK: Why does swapping one number change odd to even? How do you know that no total greater than 56 works? LISTEN FOR: 12 + 11 + 10 + 9 + 8 + 7 = 57, odd. Change one number by 1 (replace 7 by 6): 56. Any greater total is 57 only, which is odd. Listen for "the biggest possible is 57 and it is odd, so the next one down". Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: 56. WHY: The greatest total possible is 57, and it is odd. The next total below is 56. Replacing 7 by 6 gives 12 + 11 + 10 + 9 + 8 + 6 = 56, which is even. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): Mei chooses 5 different whole numbers from 1 to 10. She wants the total to be odd. What is the greatest total? Answer: 39.
