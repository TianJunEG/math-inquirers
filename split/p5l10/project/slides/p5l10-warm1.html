Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6.5 to 10. Mix-up relay: SIX cards from our earlier topics (angles and patterns are in the quick questions). Read a card and ask for a volunteer; that child answers and invites the next volunteer. A child may say pass once, or choose a green (quick) or orange (think-about-it) card. Keep a private list so every child gets a turn before anyone has a second; do not call on a child by name who has not volunteered. Click to reveal each answer in order. A wrong answer gets a friendly "try again", never a groan. Answers: 1) the 5 is in the hundred thousands place: 500 000. 2) 4/6 + 1/6 = 5/6. 3) 8 × 5 ÷ 2 = 20 cm². 4) 5 × 4 × 3 = 60 cm³. 5) $6 ÷ 4 = $1.50 each, × 10 = $15. 6) 10% is $8, so 20% is $16. Volunteers who have not yet answered go first. Ask "Which lesson does this remind you of?" for each card.
