Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 4. Relay review: one card from each of our earlier topics. Read a card, say a child's name; that child answers and names the next child. Click to reveal each answer in order. A wrong answer gets a friendly "try again", never a groan. Answers: 1) the 5 is in the hundred thousands place: 500 000. 2) 4/6 + 1/6 = 5/6. 3) 8 × 5 ÷ 2 = 20 cm². 4) 5 × 4 × 3 = 60 cm³. 5) $6 ÷ 4 = $1.50 each, × 10 = $15. 6) 10% is $8, so 20% is $16. 7) base angles are equal: (180° − 40°) ÷ 2 = 70°. 8) 3 × 10 + 1 = 31. Ask "Which lesson does this remind you of?" for each card.
