Your toolbox: how to START

Stuck? Do not stare. Pick a start and put the puzzle on paper.

Draw a model

Parts and units, amounts that are shared or compared.

Work backwards

A number is changed in steps and you know the end. Undo the LAST step first.

Logic grid

Who has what? Ticks and crosses; a tick means crosses in the rest of its row and column.

List in order

"Find all", counting shapes of every size. Go in order so nothing is missed.

Make a table

Something grows or changes step by step.

Guess and check / suppose

Two kinds of things with a total.

Then check against EVERY clue and explain why your start works.
