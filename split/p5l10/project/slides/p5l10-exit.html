EXIT TICKET

Ravi spends 1/2 of his money on a ticket, then $5 on a snack, and has $7 left. How much money did he start with? Say which start you used.

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your last stamp in your inquiry journal.

Minutes 76.5 to 81. Answer: $24. Work backwards: 7 + 5 = 12; 12 is half of his money, so 12 × 2 = 24. Check: 24 ÷ 2 = 12, 12 − 5 = 7. Extension: write your own logic puzzle with four people and four things. Does it have exactly ONE answer?
