Work backwards: undo the last step first

Siti spent 1/4 of her money, then $10, then half of what was left. She has $13 left. How much did she start with?

Step 1. Undo the half: $13 is half of what was left, so before that she had 13 × 2 = $26.

Step 2. Undo the $10: 26 + 10 = $36.

Step 3. $36 is 3/4 of her money (1/4 was spent). 1/4 = 36 ÷ 3 = $12, so all of it is 4 × 12 = $48.

$48. Check forwards: 48 − 12 = 36, 36 − 10 = 26, 26 ÷ 2 = 13 ✓

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example: working backwards with fractions. Ask first: "What do we know? Only the END." Draw the steps as a machine going forwards, then go back along the machine with the OPPOSITE of each step, starting at the end. Think-aloud: "Half of what was left is $13, so what was left is $26. Before the $10 lunch she had $36. Now the tricky bit: she spent a quarter, so $36 is three quarters. If 3 quarters is $36, 1 quarter is $12, and four quarters is $48." Always check by running forwards. Common mistake: taking 3/4 of $36 or adding 1/4 of 36. Worksheet Q1 uses the same idea with different numbers.
