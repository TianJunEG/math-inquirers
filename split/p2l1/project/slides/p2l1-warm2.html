Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 10. Read each aloud, whiteboards up together, then click to show. Ask after 3: "Why did the hundreds digit change?" (9 tens and 1 more ten make 10 tens, which is another hundred). After 4: "Which digit changed? Why not the ones?" Answers: 462, 80, 306, 305, 999.
