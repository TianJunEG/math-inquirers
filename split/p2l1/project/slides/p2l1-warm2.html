Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6 to 8 (2 minutes: ask questions 1, 3 and 5; 2 and 4 are spare). Children answer by thumbs, hands or private chat, not by naming a child. Read each aloud, whiteboards face down until 3, 2, 1 (or show only the tutor; never read a board out), then click to show. Ask after 3: "Why did the hundreds digit change?" (9 tens and 1 more ten make 10 tens, which is another hundred). After 4: "Which digit changed? Why not the ones?" Answers: 462, 80, 306, 305, 999.
