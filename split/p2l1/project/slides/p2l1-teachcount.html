Counting past a hundred

Say each number aloud. Watch what happens to the hundreds digit.

Count on in 10s: 475, 485, ___, ___

495, 505

Count back in 10s: 628, 618, 608, ___, ___

598, 588

Count on in 100s: 63, 163, 263, ___

363

Past 9 tens, the hundreds digit goes up. Back past 0 tens, it goes down.

Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5). Children say the next numbers on whiteboards before each click. The trap is in the second row: many children say 608, 698. Show it on the chart: going up a row from 608 is 598. Think-aloud: "608 is 6 hundreds and 0 tens. To take 10, I break a hundred: 5 hundreds and 9 tens, 598."
