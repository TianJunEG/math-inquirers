Key idea: list in order

Fix the hundreds digit, then swap the others. Each card starts the same number of numbers, so you know when you have them all.

Zero can never be the hundreds digit. Biggest digit first makes the biggest number.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Writing 6 hundreds, 15 tens and 2 ones as 6152 instead of regrouping 15 tens as 1 hundred and 5 tens (752). (2) Listing digit-card numbers at random, so one is missed or written twice, or counting 059 as a 3-digit number. (3) Counting back in 10s across a hundred: 512, 502, 592 instead of 492.
