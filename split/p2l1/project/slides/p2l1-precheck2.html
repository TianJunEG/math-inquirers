Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 2 to 4. Question 2, the thinking question (about 2 minutes). Same rules as question 1: guessing and "I do not know" are welcome; answers are shown to you only on each child's own paper; nothing is read out; nobody is marked or named. Q2 answer: 4 numbers: 407, 470, 704, 740. Listen for: 0 cannot be the hundreds digit, so only 4 and 7 can start. Listing in order: 4 starts 2 numbers, 7 starts 2 numbers. A child who says 6 is using 3 x 2 x 1 without the zero rule: a good idea, not a mistake to correct yet. At the end click to show the answer as "one way to think about it", without saying who had what. Ask only for volunteers: "Would anyone like to tell us how they started?" Then move on to the warm-up game.
