Patterns on a 1000 chart

Each row has 10 numbers.

Step 1. One square RIGHT: 1 more. 283 → 284

Step 2. One square DOWN: 10 more. 283 → 293

Your turn: Which number is directly below 296? Directly above 281?

306 and 271

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4 has chart pieces). Look for a pattern: point along a row (+1 each time) and down a column (+10 each time; the ones digit stays the same). Ask: "What is directly below 296 (one square down)? It is not on our piece!" Go down a column: 296, 306, because 10 more than 296 crosses the hundred. Directly above 281 (one square up) is 10 less: 271.
