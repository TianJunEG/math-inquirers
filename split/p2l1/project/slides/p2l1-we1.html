Worked example: every number from 1, 6, 9

Use each card once. Make every 3-digit number. How many are there?

1 first

169

196

6 first

619

9 first

961

Fix the hundreds digit. Swap the other two. Each card starts 2 numbers: 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 numbers.

Biggest: biggest digit first, 961. Smallest: smallest digit first, 169.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Make a list in order: this is today's main strategy. Click to reveal each number while children say it first. Think-aloud: "I keep 1 in the hundreds place. The 6 and 9 can go 6, 9 or 9, 6: 169, 196. Nothing else starts with 1. Now 6 first …" Ask: "How do we KNOW there are no more?" (every card has had its turn at the front, and each one only starts 2 numbers). Then the biggest and smallest, read straight from the list.
