For early finishers

Stretch

1. How many 3-digit numbers have all three digits the same?

2. Use the digit cards 3, 0 and 0. How many 3-digit numbers can you make?

3. I am a 3-digit number. You say me when you count in 10s from 5. My hundreds digit is 7. My tens digit is 1 less than my hundreds digit. Who am I?

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 9 (111, 222, … 999; 000 is not a 3-digit number). 2) Only 1: 300 (003 and 030 are not 3-digit numbers). 3) 765 (counting in 10s from 5 always ends in 5; tens digit 7 − 1 = 6).
