Digit riddles: list, then check

I am a 3-digit number. My hundreds digit is 2. My tens digit is 3 more than my ones digit. My digits add up to 11.

Step 1. List the tens and ones that fit: ones 0, tens 3; ones 1, tens 4; ones 2, tens 5; ones 3, tens 6 …

Step 2. Check the total: 2 + 3 + 0 = 5, 2 + 4 + 1 = 7, 2 + 5 + 2 = 9, 2 + 6 + 3 = 11 ✓

I am 263.
