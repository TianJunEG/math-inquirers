Careful: the zero card

Make every 3-digit number with 0, 3 and 6.

Step 1. 3 first: 306, 360. 6 first: 603, 630.

Step 2. 0 first: 036 is just 36. It has only 2 digits: not this one!

So only 4 numbers. Zero can never be the hundreds digit. Smallest: 306.

Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before the worksheet (Q3 has a zero card). Ask first: "Will 0, 3, 6 make 6 numbers like before?" Let children argue. Show 036 on a calculator or say it aloud: "thirty-six". Click through. Smallest is 306, not 036. Biggest is 630. Ask a volunteer who already knows why to explain "zero can never go first" in two sentences (a job for anyone who would like it, never a named child).
