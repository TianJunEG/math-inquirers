LESSON 1 • WEEK 1

Digit Detectives

Numbers to 1000

TODAY WE WILL

Build 3-digit numbers, find every number from digit cards by listing in order, and spot patterns on a 1000 chart.

TODAY'S TOOL

List every way

Focus habit: Wonder

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read and build 3-digit numbers by place value (also with more than 9 tens), make every number from a set of digit cards by listing in order, find the biggest and smallest, and use patterns on a 1000 chart to count on and back in 1s, 10s and 100s. Thinking strategies taught today: MAKE A LIST IN ORDER and LOOK FOR A PATTERN. Materials: Digit cards 0 to 9 (one set per child), place-value mat (hundreds, tens, ones), base-ten blocks or paper strips and squares, mini whiteboards, the 1000-chart pieces on the workbook page (rows of 10). Online: children cut out paper digit cards before the lesson and move them on camera; the tutor writes on the 1000-chart pieces on the slides. Time plan: opening 0 to 1.5, sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 6, warm-up 6 to 10.5, explore 10.5 to 23.5, mini-lesson 23.5 to 35.5, brain break 35.5 to 38.5, worksheet page 1 38.5 to 52.5, check together, page 1 52.5 to 55, short teaching 55 to 59.5, worksheet page 2 59.5 to 71, check together, page 2 71 to 73.5, flex (spare time) 73.5 to 76.5, talk time 76.5 to 81.5, exit ticket 81.5 to 86.5, looking back 86.5 to 88.5, closing and stamps 88.5 to 90. The Think harder and Go further slides are optional, if you finish early (no clock time). Kit ready at minute 0: ask families to put every item on the desk before the lesson starts (a 30-second check at the start). SPINE: Today's tool is List every way. Write each way down, in order, so none is missed. Look for where you use it. Point at the card on page 1 of the workbook. HABIT: Focus habit: Wonder. Invite a question or a what-if before any answer is given. Say: "What could we ask? What if we changed one thing?" Do not give the answer first: ask the prompt, wait, and give a hint only if the child is stuck.
