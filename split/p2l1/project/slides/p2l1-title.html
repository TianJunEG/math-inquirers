LESSON 1 • WEEK 1

Digit Detectives

Numbers to 1000

TODAY WE WILL

Build 3-digit numbers, find every number from digit cards by listing in order, and spot patterns on a 1000 chart.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read and build 3-digit numbers by place value (also with more than 9 tens), make every number from a set of digit cards by listing in order, find the biggest and smallest, and use patterns on a 1000 chart to count on and back in 1s, 10s and 100s. Thinking strategies taught today: MAKE A LIST IN ORDER and LOOK FOR A PATTERN. Materials: Digit cards 0 to 9 (one set per child), place-value mat (hundreds, tens, ones), base-ten blocks or paper strips and squares, mini whiteboards, a 1000 chart page (rows of 10). Online: children cut out paper digit cards before the lesson and move them on camera; the tutor writes on the 1000-chart pieces on the slides. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
