Challenge: walk-through

Q1: one place at a time

Hundreds: every number 300 to 399 → 100

Tens: 330, 331 … 339 → 10

Ones: 303, 313 … 393 → 10

100 + 10 + 10 = 120. 333 has three 3s!

Q2: smallest digits in the biggest places

Hundreds: 1 and 2 → 100 + 200 = 300

Tens: 3 and 4 → 30 + 40 = 70

Ones: 5 and 6 → 11

300 + 70 + 11 = 381, e.g. 135 + 246 ✓

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use after pairs have tried. Q1: a list in order by PLACE stops us missing any 3s; many children say 111 or 102 because they count 333 once. Q2: the hundreds digits matter most, so they get the smallest cards; any way of placing 1, 2 / 3, 4 / 5, 6 gives 381 (136 + 245 also works). Check by adding: 135 + 246 = 381.
