Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Let pairs keep working, then gather them. Collect the numbers on the board in the order children say them, messy on purpose, then ask "Is anything missing? Is anything there twice?" Answers: 1) 6 numbers: 358, 385, 538, 583, 835, 853. 2) List them in order: 2 numbers start with 3, 2 start with 5, 2 start with 8. 3) 853 is biggest (biggest digit in the hundreds place), 358 is smallest.
