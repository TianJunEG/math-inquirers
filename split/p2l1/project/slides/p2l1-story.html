Story break: Aryabhata

Optional • about 2 minutes • skip any time

India • 476 - about 550 (sources differ)

Aryabhata lived in India about 1,500 years ago. In the year 499 he wrote a short book of maths and astronomy, all in verses. One rule in his book says that each place in a number is ten times the place before it. So ten ones make a ten, and ten tens make a hundred. Historians are not sure exactly where he was born. He wrote his numbers using letters, not the digits we use today.

Try it (optional)

Aryabhata’s rule: each place is ten times the place before it. In the number 538, which digit is in the tens place, and what is it worth? What is ten times 50?
