Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 1 to 4. Warm-up check: two questions on the key idea of today, in a NEW setting (not recall). Give about 1 minute each. Whiteboards up together, ask one child "How do you know?", then click to show each answer. Q1 answer: 4 numbers: 407, 470, 704, 740. Listen for: 0 cannot be the hundreds digit (047 is not a 3-digit number). Listing in order: 4 starts 2 numbers, 7 starts 2 numbers, 0 starts none. A child who says 6 is using the 3 x 2 x 1 idea without the zero rule. Q2 answer: No. 13 tens is 130, so the number is 500 + 130 = 630. Listen for: 13 tens is 1 hundred and 3 tens; a box holds only one digit, so ten tens swap for a hundred. Listen for “regroup” or “130”. FAST TRACK: a child who gets BOTH right AND can say why starts on the THINK HARDER pages and the Open investigation (workbook Q8) when the worksheet begins at minute 36, in their own breakout room or with you checking in. A right answer without a reason stays with the group. If unsure, keep the child in the group. Nobody is labelled: say "Some of you will start on a different page today; everyone gets a turn at both." Children can move at any time. Everyone joins explore, the mini-lesson, the check-together slides and talk time.
