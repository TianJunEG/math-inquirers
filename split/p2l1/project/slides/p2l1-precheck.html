Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 1 to 4. Sneak-peek puzzles: two puzzles on the key idea of today, which the children have NOT learnt yet. Say out loud: "These are ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome and so is I do not know. Nobody is marked." Question 1 is an access question: any child can try it with objects, a drawing or counting, so everyone has a first win (about 1 minute). Question 2 is the thinking question (about 2 minutes). Answers are PRIVATE: each child holds their own paper to the camera for you only, or writes it on their own paper and shows only you on camera. Do not raise whiteboards together, do not read answers out, do not name children. Q1 answer: 325. Listen for: Hundreds first, then tens, then ones. A drawing of 3 flats, 2 rods and 5 units is welcome. This is a first win for everyone. Q2 answer: 4 numbers: 407, 470, 704, 740. Listen for: 0 cannot be the hundreds digit, so only 4 and 7 can start. Listing in order: 4 starts 2 numbers, 7 starts 2 numbers. A child who says 6 is using 3 x 2 x 1 without the zero rule: a good idea, not a mistake to correct yet. At the end click to show the answers as "one way to think about it", without saying who had what. Ask only for volunteers: "Would anyone like to tell us how they started?" Invite the WHOLE group at once, never one child: "When you have finished Start here, you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation." Never announce who is on which page. Pairs are not split unless both children agree. Children can move either way at any time. Everyone joins explore, the mini-lesson, the check-together slides and talk time.
