Place value: more than 9 tens

What number is 5 hundreds, 12 tens and 4 ones?

Step 1. 5 hundreds = 500

Step 2. 12 tens = 10 tens + 2 tens. 10 tens make 1 hundred, so 12 tens = 120.

Step 3. 500 + 120 + 4 = 624. Check: 6 hundreds, 2 tens, 4 ones ✓

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Build it with base-ten blocks (online: paper strips and squares). Ask first: "Can we write 5, 12, 4 as 5124?" (No: that is a 4-digit number. A box can only hold one digit.) Click through the steps. Think-aloud: "12 tens is too many for the tens box. Ten of them swap for one hundred. Now I have 6 hundreds, 2 tens and 4 ones: 624." Go the other way too: "624 is 6 hundreds and 2 tens. It is ALSO 5 hundreds and 12 tens."
