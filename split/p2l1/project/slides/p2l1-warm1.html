I turn over one digit card at a time. Write it in the H, T or O box straight away. No moving it later! Biggest number wins.

Where would you put a 9? A 0? What about a 5: keep it, or wait for a bigger card?

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Place It Right. Children draw three boxes on a whiteboard: H, T, O. Shuffle digit cards 0 to 9 and turn over one at a time (online: hold it up to the camera). After EACH card, children write it in one empty box at once and cannot move it. After three cards, read the numbers aloud: biggest wins. Play 3 rounds: two for the biggest, one for the smallest. Click to show the thinking question. Talk: "A 9 goes in the hundreds: it is worth 900 there. A 0 goes in the ones. A 5 is tricky: are there more big cards or small cards left?" Listen for children who say "the hundreds digit matters most". That idea comes back in talk time (401 or 399).
