Warm-up: Place It Right

I turn over one digit card at a time. Write it in the H, T or O box straight away. No moving it later! Biggest number wins.

Where would you put a 9? A 0? What about a 5: keep it, or wait for a bigger card?

Bonus, if you like: write the biggest and the smallest 3-digit number whose digits add up to 9.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6 to 8.5. Place It Right. Children draw three boxes on a whiteboard: H, T, O. Shuffle digit cards 0 to 9 and turn over one at a time (online: read the digit aloud; nothing is held up to the camera). After EACH card, children write it in one empty box at once and cannot move it. After three cards, read the numbers aloud: biggest wins. Play 2 rounds (about 2.5 minutes): one for the biggest, one for the smallest. Click to show the thinking question. Talk: "A 9 goes in the hundreds: it is worth 900 there. A 0 goes in the ones. A 5 is tricky: are there more big cards or small cards left?" Listen for children who say "the hundreds digit matters most". That idea comes back in talk time (401 or 399). Bonus bubble (a choice for anyone who would like more): biggest 900, smallest 108 (the hundreds digit cannot be 0). A game of about 2.5 minutes: stop it on the clock even if the game is not finished.
