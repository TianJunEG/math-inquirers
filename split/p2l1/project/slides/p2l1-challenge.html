1. Ben writes all the numbers from 300 to 399. How many times does he write the digit 3?

Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. These are Think Harder Q7 and Q5. Answers: 1) 120 (100 in the hundreds place, 10 in the tens place from 330 to 339, 10 in the ones place 303, 313 … 393). 2) 381, for example 135 + 246 (1 and 2 in the hundreds, 3 and 4 in the tens, 5 and 6 in the ones).
