Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Take the digit cards 3, 5 and 8.
  2. Make a 3-digit number using each card once. Write it down.
  3. Make as many different numbers as you can.
  4. Circle the biggest and the smallest. Have you got them all?

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs work with digit cards 3, 5 and 8 (online: paper cards on camera). Ask them to predict first: how many numbers? Most children guess 3 or 9. Let them record numbers in any order at first; the teaching part shows how to list them in order. Early finishers: add a 0 card and use 0, 3, 5. Why are there fewer numbers?
