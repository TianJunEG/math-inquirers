Math Inquirers Level 3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Let pairs keep working, then gather them and collect sums on the board. Answers: 1) There are many: 168, if 173 + 286 and 286 + 173 count once. 2) Exactly once, every time. 3) The digits of the answer always add up to 18 (4 + 5 + 9 = 18, 5 + 6 + 7 = 18). Do not explain why in full; just say: "Mathematicians can prove this is ALWAYS true. Notice it, test it, explain why!" Anyone ready for more: "Can the answer be less than 400?" (No: the smallest answer is 459.)
