Story break: Joan Clarke

England (London, Cambridge, Bletchley Park) • 1917 to 1996

Joan Clarke was born in London in 1917. She won a scholarship to Newnham College, Cambridge, to study mathematics, and she earned a double first, the top marks. Cambridge did not give women full degrees then, so hers was not officially awarded until 1948. In 1940 she joined the codebreakers at Bletchley Park and worked in Hut 8, on secret messages sent by the German navy. She used careful counting and chance to cut down how many possibilities had to be tested. Later she became deputy head of Hut 8.
