Magic squares: find a full line first

Step 1. Full line: 80 + 10 + 60 = 150. EVERY line must make 150.

Step 2. Diagonals: 80 + 50 + ? = 150, so 20. 60 + 50 + ? = 150, so 40.

Step 3. Now every line has one gap: 150 − 80 − 40 = 30, 150 − 60 − 20 = 70, 150 − 40 − 20 = 90.

Check every row, column and diagonal: 150 ✓
