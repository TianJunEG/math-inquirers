Work backwards: number pyramids

Each brick is the sum of the two bricks under it.

Step 1. Top: 2000 = ? + 1200. Undo: 2000 − 1200 = 800.

Step 2. 800 = 450 + ?. Undo: 800 − 450 = 350.

Step 3. 1200 = 350 + ?. Undo: 1200 − 350 = 850.

Check by adding up: 450 + 350 = 800 ✓
