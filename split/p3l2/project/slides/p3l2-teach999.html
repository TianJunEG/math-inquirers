A pattern for taking from 1000

1000 − 372 = ?

Step 1. 999 − 372: each place is 9 take away a digit, so no exchanging! 9 − 3, 9 − 7, 9 − 2 → 627

Step 2. 1000 is 1 more than 999, so the answer is 1 more: 628

Your turn: 1000 − 456 = ?

544

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5). Ask: "Why is 999 − 372 so easy?" (9 is the biggest digit, so we never need to exchange). Check: 628 + 372 = 1000. Your turn: 999 − 456 = 543, plus 1 = 544. Q5 asks children to notice the same pattern with 9999, so do not show 4-digit examples.
