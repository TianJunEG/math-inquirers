Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find missing digits in 4-digit addition and subtraction by working column by column from the ones; check subtraction by adding and work backwards in magic squares and number pyramids (heuristic: work backwards); use guess, check and improve for digit-card and shape puzzles (heuristic: guess and check). Materials: Digit cards 0–9 (one set per child), mini whiteboards, a printed 3 × 3 grid, counters or small paper squares to move around. Online: Children use the cut-out digit cards at the back of the workbook (or paper squares numbered 0–9) and draw the grids on paper. Share the screen for the column sums and write on them. Time plan (90 minutes, with a named 3-minute float inside talk time): opening 0 to 1.5, sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 5 (own paper, nobody marked, one show-me for both), warm-up game 5 to 8 (two rounds) and three quick questions 8 to 11, explore 11 to 25 (a Go further what-if is on the explore slide from the start; pairs go to pre-assigned breakout rooms), teaching 25 to 34 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 34 to 39, worksheet 39 to 71 (page 1 in pairs, short teaching, page 2 on their own, a 60-second self-check slide after each page; after question 3 of page 1 any child may choose Think harder or the Investigation; from minute 68 everyone chooses), talk time 71 to 79 (volunteers first, pass is fine), exit ticket 79 to 85, look back 85 to 88, stamp and close 88 to 90. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child; the tutor never announces who is on which page and invites the whole group, never one child. If the class is behind, children finish the last question of each page at home; the check slides cover it. SPINE: Today's tool is Guess and check. Make a sensible guess, check it against every clue, improve it. Look for where you use it. Point at the card on page 1 of the workbook.
