Addition and Subtraction

TODAY WE WILL

Crack missing digits one column at a time, check subtraction by adding, and solve magic squares and shape puzzles.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Find missing digits in 4-digit addition and subtraction by working column by column from the ones; check subtraction by adding and work backwards in magic squares and number pyramids (heuristic: work backwards); use guess, check and improve for digit-card and shape puzzles (heuristic: guess and check). Materials: Digit cards 0–9 (one set per child), mini whiteboards, a printed 3 × 3 grid, counters or small paper squares to move around. Online: Children use the cut-out digit cards at the back of the workbook (or paper squares numbered 0–9) and draw the grids on paper. Share the screen for the column sums and write on them. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90. If the class is behind, children finish the last question of each page at home; the check slides cover it.
