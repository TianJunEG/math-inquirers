Teach the heuristic WORK BACKWARDS: subtraction and addition undo each other. Click 1 turns the subtraction into an addition (the picture changes). Think-aloud: "Subtracting with missing digits on top is hard, because I would have to exchange without knowing the digit. So I undo it: the answer plus the number taken away gives back the first number." Common error: looking for a digit that can be taken from 2 to leave 7. There is none; it must be 12 − 5 = 7.
