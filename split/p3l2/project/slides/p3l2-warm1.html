Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 8 (after the warm-up check). Make 1000. Point to one card at a time; children write the missing number on their whiteboards and hold them up together; click to reveal (order: 350, 720, 105, 999, 460, 75). Answers: 650, 280, 895, 1, 540, 925. Ask: "How did you work out 720?" Good ways: count on to the next hundred (720 → 800 is 80) then to 1000 (200 more), so 280; or use bonds of 10 for each place. For 105 watch for 995 (it is 895: 105 + 895 = 1000). Quick finisher: "Make 10 000 with 3500." (6500)
