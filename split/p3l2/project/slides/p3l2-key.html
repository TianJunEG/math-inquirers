Key idea: one column at a time

Start at the ones and carry to the left. Check subtraction by adding. Stuck? Guess, check, then improve.

A puzzle is cracked when you can say why it is the ONLY answer.

Math Inquirers P3 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Forgetting the carried 1 when working out the missing digit in the next column. (2) In subtraction, looking for a digit with 3 − □ = 8 (there is none) instead of regrouping: 13 − 5 = 8. (3) Magic squares: guessing numbers instead of finding a full line first to get the total.
