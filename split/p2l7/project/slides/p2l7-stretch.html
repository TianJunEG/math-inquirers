Answers: 1) 1/12, 1/5, 1/3, 1/2 2) No. Eighths are smaller than fifths (the more equal parts, the smaller each part). So 3 eighths is less than 3 fifths: 3/5 is bigger than 3/8. 3) No. 3 eighths and 2 eighths make 5 eighths: 5/8. The parts are still eighths, like 3 apples and 2 apples make 5 apples. Adding the bottom numbers would change the size of the parts. 4) (Question: Ali says: “Half of my pizza is bigger than half of your pizza.” Can he be right? Explain.) Yes, if his pizza is bigger. Halves of different-size wholes are different sizes.
