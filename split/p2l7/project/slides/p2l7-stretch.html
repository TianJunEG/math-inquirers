For early finishers

Stretch

1. Ali's ribbon and Mei's ribbon are each cut into thirds. Mei's 13 is longer than Ali's 13. How can that be?

2. Write all the fractions with bottom number 5 that are bigger than 25 and smaller than 1 whole.

3. A bar has 12 equal parts. Ben shades 112, then 212 more, then 312 more. How much is not shaded?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) Mei's whole ribbon is longer than Ali's. A third of a long ribbon is longer than a third of a short one: fractions only compare like this when the wholes are the same size. 2) 3/5 and 4/5. 3) 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 twelfths shaded, so 6/12 is not shaded.
