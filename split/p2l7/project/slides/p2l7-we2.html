Compare: one part of the same whole

Which is bigger: 12, 13 or 16?

Step 1. Same whole: all the bars are the same length.

Step 2. 12 is 1 of 2 parts. 16 is 1 of 6 parts.

Step 3. More equal parts: each part is smaller.

Smallest to biggest: 16, 13, 12

A pizza shared by 6, not 2: each slice is smaller.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click through. Hold up the folded strips from Explore. Think-aloud: "All the bars are the same length, so it is the same whole. One half is 1 of 2 parts. One sixth is 1 of 6 parts. When I cut the same whole into more parts, each part gets smaller. So one sixth is the smallest and one half is the biggest." Story: "One pizza for 2 children, or one pizza for 6 children: when do you get a bigger slice?" Common mistake: "1/6 is bigger because 6 is bigger than 2". Answer with the strips, not just words. Only compare like this when the wholes are the same size.
