Fair Shares

Fractions

TODAY WE WILL

Cut wholes into equal parts, name and compare fractions, and add fractions to share things fairly.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Recognise equal parts and name fractions (halves, thirds, quarters, up to twelfths); compare unit fractions and like fractions; add and take away like fractions within one whole; solve fair-sharing stories with a bar. Materials: 4 paper strips of the same length per child (A4 cut lengthwise), paper circles, scissors, crayons, mini whiteboards. Online: each child has 4 paper strips, a crayon and scissors on camera; the tutor shows fraction bars on the slides. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 8, explore 8 to 20 (explore2 talk is 6 minutes), teaching 20 to 32 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 32 to 36, worksheet page 1 36 to 50 then check together 50 to 52, short teaching 52 to 57, worksheet page 2 57 to 69 then check together 69 to 72, talk time 72 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
