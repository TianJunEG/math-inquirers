Fair Shares

Fractions

TODAY WE WILL

Cut wholes into equal parts, name and compare fractions, and add fractions to share things fairly.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Recognise equal parts and name fractions (halves, thirds, quarters, up to twelfths); compare unit fractions and like fractions; add and take away like fractions within one whole; solve fair-sharing stories with a bar. Materials: 4 paper strips of the same length per child (A4 cut lengthwise), paper circles, scissors, crayons, mini whiteboards. Online: each child has 4 paper strips, a crayon and scissors on camera; the tutor shows fraction bars on the slides. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 (page 1 in pairs, short teaching before page 2, a check-together slide after each page), talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
