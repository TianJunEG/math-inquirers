Add fractions with the same bottom number

29 + 49 = ?

Step 1. Colour 29: 2 ninths.

Step 2. Colour 49 more: 4 more ninths.

Step 3. Count: 6 ninths. The parts are still ninths, so the bottom number stays 9.

29 + 49 = 69

How much more makes 1 whole?

3 more ninths. 69 + 39 = 99 = 1 whole

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4 adds and takes away fractions). Click 1: colour 2 ninths. Click 2: colour 4 more. Click 3: count the coloured parts: 6. Think-aloud: "Two ninths and four ninths. Ninths are the name of the parts, like apples. 2 apples and 4 apples are 6 apples. 2 ninths and 4 ninths are 6 ninths. The parts do not change size, so it stays ninths." Then the side question: 9 ninths make the whole bar, so 3 more ninths are needed. Taking away works the same way: 6/9 − 2/9 = 4/9. Big mistake to stop: 2/9 + 4/9 = 6/18. Show that 6/18 would mean the parts became smaller, which did not happen.
