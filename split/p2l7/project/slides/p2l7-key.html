34.5–35.5 MIN

Key idea: equal parts

A fraction names EQUAL parts of a whole. The bottom number: how many equal parts. The top number: how many we take.

Same whole: more equal parts means smaller parts. So 18 is smaller than 14.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 34.5 to 35.5. Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Naming a fraction when the parts are not equal (Q1 B and D): ask "Are all 4 parts the same size?" (2) Thinking 1/6 is bigger than 1/4 because 6 is bigger than 4 (Q3): fold or draw the strips. (3) Adding the bottom numbers too: 2/5 + 1/5 written as 3/10 (Q4). The parts are still fifths, so only the top numbers are added. (4) Writing shaded over not shaded (3/5 instead of 3/8).
