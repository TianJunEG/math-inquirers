Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 1 to 4. Warm-up check: two questions on the key idea of today, in a NEW setting (not recall). Give about 1 minute each. Whiteboards up together, ask one child "How do you know?", then click to show each answer. Q1 answer: Ali has more. The same prata cut into fewer parts gives bigger parts. Listen for: Bigger bottom number means smaller parts when the whole is the same. A drawing of two bars is a good reason. A child who says 1/8 because 8 is bigger is not there yet. Q2 answer: 3/8 (3 + 2 = 5 eighths eaten, 8 − 5 = 3 eighths left). Listen for: Counts eighths: the bottom number stays 8. A child who writes 5/16 has added the bottom numbers. FAST TRACK: a child who gets BOTH right AND can say why starts on the THINK HARDER pages and the Open investigation (workbook Q8) when the worksheet begins at minute 36, in their own breakout room or with you checking in. A right answer without a reason stays with the group. If unsure, keep the child in the group. Nobody is labelled: say "Some of you will start on a different page today; everyone gets a turn at both." Children can move at any time. Everyone joins explore, the mini-lesson, the check-together slides and talk time.
