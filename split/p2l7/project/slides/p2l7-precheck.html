Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 1.5 to 6. Sneak-peek puzzles: two puzzles on the key idea of today, which the children have NOT learnt yet. Say out loud (about 28 words): "Two sneak-peek puzzles on ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome, so is I do not know. Nobody is marked. Say your answer together on three." Question 1 is an access question: any child can try it with objects, a drawing or counting, so everyone has a first win (about 45 seconds). Question 2 is the thinking question (about 90 seconds). Then about 2 minutes for the answers slide and the move on. Answers are PRIVATE: each child writes on their own paper and says the answer together on three; nothing is held up to the camera. Do not raise whiteboards together, do not read answers out, do not name children. Q1 answer: 1/2 (one half). Listen for: Equal parts matter. Folding on camera is welcome. This is a first win for everyone. Q2 answer: Ali (1/5). The same prata cut into fewer parts gives bigger parts. Listen for: Many children say 1/8 because 8 is bigger. Draw two strips, one cut in 5 and one cut in 8, and compare. A drawing is a good answer. At the end click to show the answers as "one way to think about it", without saying who had what. Ask only for volunteers: "Would anyone like to tell us how they started?" Invite the WHOLE group at once, never one child: "When you have finished Start here, you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation." Never announce who is on which page. Pairs are not split unless both children agree. Children can move either way at any time. Everyone joins explore, the mini-lesson, the check-together slides and talk time.
