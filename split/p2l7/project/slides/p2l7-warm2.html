Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8.5 to 10.5. Read each question aloud, give thinking time, take answers on whiteboards, then click to show. Ask "How do you know?" each time. Answers: 1) one quarter, written 1/4 ("1 of 4 equal parts") 2) 3. Spare questions in the guide table: how many halves make 1 whole (2); a cake cut into 5 equal pieces, 1 piece is one fifth, 1/5; 2 of 7 equal parts shaded is 2/7. Show how we write it: the bottom number is how many EQUAL parts the whole has, the top number is how many we take. Watch for children who write shaded over not shaded: ask "How many parts are there altogether?" Ask quick questions 1 and 3 only (about 2 minutes): children answer by thumbs, hands, not by naming a child. The other questions are spare.
