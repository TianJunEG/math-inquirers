Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6 to 8 (2 minutes: ask questions 1, 3 and 5; 2 and 4 are spare). Children answer by thumbs, hands, not by naming a child. Read each question aloud, give thinking time, take answers on whiteboards, then click to show. Ask "How do you know?" each time. Answers: 1) one quarter, written 1/4 ("1 of 4 equal parts") 2) 2 3) 3 4) one fifth, 1/5 5) 2/7. For 5, show how we write it: the bottom number is how many EQUAL parts the whole has (7), the top number is how many we take (2). Watch for children who write 2/5 (shaded over not shaded): ask "How many parts are there altogether?"
