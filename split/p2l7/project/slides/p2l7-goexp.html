Explore: go further

Finished early or curious? Try this. It is your choice.

Fold a strip into 6 equal parts. How many sixths make a half? How many sixths make a third?

Write or draw your answer on paper.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8 to 14. Go further (a second what-if question, so nobody waits). Show this slide with the explore slide or post the question in the chat when the pairs go to their breakout rooms. It is for ANY child who finishes the explore task or wants more: say it to the whole group as a choice, never as a prize, and never name who tries it. Pairs stay together unless both children agree to split. Answer: 3 sixths make a half and 2 sixths make a third. Take it at the debrief (minute 14) only if a pair offers it.
