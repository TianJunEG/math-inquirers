Fair-sharing stories: draw a bar

A cake is cut into 10 equal pieces. Ben eats 4 pieces. Siti eats 3 pieces. What fraction of the cake is eaten? What fraction is left?

Step 1. Draw a bar of 10 equal parts. Colour Ben's 4: 410.

Step 2. Colour Siti's 3: 310.

Eaten: 410 + 310 = 710

Left: 3 white parts, 310

Check: 710 eaten and 310 left make 1010, the whole cake ✓

Think-aloud: "The cake has 10 equal pieces, so I draw a bar of 10 parts: each part is one tenth. Ben eats 4: I colour 4. Siti eats 3: I colour 3 more in a new colour. Eaten: 4 tenths and 3 tenths make 7 tenths. Left: the white parts: 3 tenths." Ask: "Who ate more? How much more?" (Ben, 1/10 more). Always check that eaten and left make the whole.
