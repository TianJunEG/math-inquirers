Explore: fold fair shares

EXPLORE

  1. Take 4 paper strips that are the same length.
  2. Keep one whole. Fold one in half, one into quarters and one into eighths.
  3. Open them out. Write the fraction on every part.
  4. Line them up. Then try to fold a strip into 3 equal parts!

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8 to 14. Each child folds their own strips; pairs compare. Show how to fold in half by matching the ends. Quarters: fold in half, then in half again. Eighths: in half three times. Children write 1/2, 1/4 or 1/8 on each part and line the strips up under each other, left ends together. Thirds are hard on purpose: there is no "fold in half" trick. Let them try (roll the strip into an S and flatten it, then check the 3 parts are the same length). Online: the same with paper strips on camera.
