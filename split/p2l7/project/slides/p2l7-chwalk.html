Q1: list them in order

Same whole: bigger bottom number, smaller part.

Smaller than 14: bottom number more than 4.

Bigger than 17: bottom number less than 7.

So 5 or 6: 15 and 16 ✓

Q2: take away the extra

12 − 4 = 8 pieces for Ali and Ben.

Take away Ali's extra 2: 6 pieces left.

Share 6 fairly: 3 and 3. Ben 3, Ali 3 + 2 = 5.

Ben gets 312. Check: 4 + 5 + 3 = 12 ✓
