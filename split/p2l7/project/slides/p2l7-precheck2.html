Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 2 to 4. Question 2, the thinking question (about 2 minutes). Same rules as question 1: guessing and "I do not know" are welcome; answers are shown to you only on each child's own paper; nothing is read out; nobody is marked or named. Q2 answer: Ali (1/5). The same prata cut into fewer parts gives bigger parts. Listen for: Many children say 1/8 because 8 is bigger. Draw two strips, one cut in 5 and one cut in 8, and compare. A drawing is a good answer. At the end click to show the answer as "one way to think about it", without saying who had what. Ask only for volunteers: "Would anyone like to tell us how they started?" Then move on to the warm-up game.
