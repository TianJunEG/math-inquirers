TALK TIME

Is 12 always bigger than 14? Can you think of a time when it is not?

Sentence starters:

“I noticed that …” “I tested it by …” “I know because …” “Pass.”

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 72 to 80. Pairs talk for 2 minutes, then share. Answer: when the wholes are the same size, yes, 1/2 is bigger. But half of a small cupcake is less than a quarter of a big cake! Fractions only compare like this when the wholes are the same. Praise the reasons, not only the answers. Talk time rules: you choose volunteers first ("Who would like to share?"); never cold-call a child by name; any child may say pass; no public tallies, scores or rankings.
