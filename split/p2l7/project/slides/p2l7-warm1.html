Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Fair or not fair? Point to one snack at a time: "Ali and Mei share this. Is it fair? Thumbs up or thumbs down." Then click to show the answer (clicks go pizza, prata, toast, chocolate, cake, pancake). Ask every time: "How do you know?" Listen for "the parts are the same size" (fair) and "one part is bigger" (not fair). Answers: pizza fair (cut through the middle: 2 halves); prata not fair (the cut is not through the middle); kaya toast fair (corner to corner makes 2 same-size triangles: fold one onto the other to check); chocolate bar not fair (3 different widths); cake fair (3 equal parts: thirds); pancake not fair (all cuts go through the middle, but 2 pieces are small and 2 are big). The pancake is the tricky one: cutting through the middle is not enough, the pieces must be the same size. Today's big word: EQUAL parts.
