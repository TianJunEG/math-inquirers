Story break: Ahmes the scribe

Optional • about 2 minutes • skip any time

Egypt • about 1550 BC (date on the papyrus copy; sources differ)

Ahmes was a scribe in ancient Egypt, more than 3,500 years ago. He wrote out a long maths roll on papyrus. It has 84 problems and tables for dividing numbers and handling fractions. Egyptian maths often wrote fractions with a 1 on top, like 1/3 and 1/4. Ahmes did not say the ideas were his own. He said he was copying an older work. The roll is now kept in the British Museum in London, and is called the Rhind Papyrus.

Try it (optional)

Share 1 loaf of bread equally among 4 children. What fraction of the loaf does each child get? What fraction do 3 of the children get together?
