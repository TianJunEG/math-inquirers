Worked example: the total stays the same

Ali and Ben had 96 cards altogether. Ali gave Ben 14 cards. Then Ali had 1/3 as many cards as Ben. How many cards did Ali have at first?

Step 1. Giving cards does not change the total: 96 before and 96 after.

Step 2. After: Ali 1 unit, Ben 3 units. 4 units = 96, so 1 unit = 24.

Step 3. Ali has 24 after. He gave away 14, so he had 24 + 14 = 38 at first. Check: Ben 58 + 14 = 72 = 3 × 24 ✓

Math Inquirers Level 5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Ask first: "Did the total change when Ali gave Ben some cards?" Draw the AFTER model first: it is the part with the fraction. Click order: step 1, step 2, then the bars fill in with the numbers, then step 3. Think-aloud: "Ali has 1/3 as many as Ben, so Ali is 1 unit and Ben is 3 units. The total is 4 units and that is 96, so a unit is 24. Ali has 24 AFTER giving away 14. To go back to BEFORE I add the 14 back: 38." Stress going backwards from after to before. Check: before 38 + 58 = 96.
