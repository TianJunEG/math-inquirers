Key idea: find what stays the same

Ask: what stays the same? The total (one gives to the other), the difference (both get or spend the same), or one amount (only the other changes).

Draw that part the same in the Before and After models. Fraction of a remainder: cut the parts that are left into equal units.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Taking the second fraction of the whole amount instead of the remainder (1/4 of the remainder read as 1/4 of all the money). (2) Thinking the difference goes down by 10 when one person gives the other 10 (it goes down by 20: one loses 10, the other gains 10). (3) Keeping the units from the Before model when the thing that stays the same is a different amount, so the units in the two models are not the same size.
