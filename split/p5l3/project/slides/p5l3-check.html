2–6.5 MIN

Sneak-peek puzzles

Guessing is welcome. Nobody is marked.

1. Ben is 8 and his father is 32. (a) How old is Ben's father when Ben is 12? (b) How much older than Ben is his father now? How much older when Ben is 12?

(a) 36 (b) 24 older, then 24 older

2. Ali has 40 cards and Mei has 20. Ali gives Mei some cards and then they have the same number. How many cards did Ali give? What stays the same?

10 cards. The total stays 60.

Show your paper ONCE, after both puzzles: only to your tutor, or all hold paper to the camera together.

Minutes 2 to 6.5. SNEAK-PEEK PUZZLES. Show puzzle 1 and give about a minute; click to show the short answer later. Then show puzzle 2 and give about a minute. Open with one line only: "Guessing is welcome. Nobody is marked." Puzzle 1 is an access puzzle: any child can try it with objects, drawing or counting, so everyone can start well. Puzzle 2 is a thinking puzzle. ONE hold-up for both puzzles: after puzzle 2, everyone shows their paper at the same moment (own paper shown only to you as you walk round; online, all hold paper to the camera together, or show it to you only, on camera). Then click through the two short answers and say each with one short reason. NO volunteer round here (volunteers come in the talk time). Never boards in front of the group, never read out answers with names, no tally, no ranking. Never record scores. Answers and what to listen for. Q1: (a) 36. (b) 24 years older now, and 24 years older when Ben is 12: the gap does not change.; Q2: 10. The TOTAL stays 60, so each has 30 afterwards and Ali gives 40 − 30 = 10. Counters or a drawing help.
