Your turn: fraction of the remainder

Draw a bar. Cut the parts into equal units.

a Ben spent 1/2 of his money on a game and 1/4 of the remainder on lunch. He had $18 left. How much at first?

$48

b Mei read 3/10 of a book on Saturday and 2/7 of the remaining pages on Sunday. 25 pages were left. How many pages in the book?

50 pages

c In (a), what fraction of Ben's money was left?

3/8
