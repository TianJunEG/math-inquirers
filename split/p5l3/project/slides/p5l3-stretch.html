A1. Ravi spent 1/4 of his money on Monday and 2/5 of the remainder on Tuesday. He spent $66 altogether. How much money did he have at first?

A2. A box had red beads and blue beads. 3/7 of the beads were red. Then 30 red beads were added. Now 3/5 of the beads are red. How many beads were in the box at first? How many are in the box now?
