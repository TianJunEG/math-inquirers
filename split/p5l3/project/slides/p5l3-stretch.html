For early finishers

Stretch

1. Ali spent 3/8 of his money on a ticket and $15 on food. Then he had 1/4 of his money left. How much money did he have at first?

2. Mei and Siti have 60 stickers. Mei has 2/3 as many as Siti. How many stickers must Siti give Mei so that they have the same number?

3. A tank is 1/3 full of water. After 20 litres are added it is 1/2 full. How many litres does the full tank hold?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) $40 (3/8 + 1/4 = 5/8 is spent or left, so $15 is 3/8 of the money; 1 unit = $5; 8 units = $40). 2) 6 stickers (Mei 24, Siti 36; 30 each). 3) 120 litres (1/2 − 1/3 = 1/6 of the tank is 20 litres).
