Ali had 35 stickers and Siti had 25. How many stickers must Ali give Siti so that Ali has 1/2 as many stickers as Siti?

Draw a model on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 77 to 83. Answer: 15 (the total stays 60; 3 units = 60, so Ali has 1 unit = 20 at the end: 35 − 20 = 15) Extension: Make up your own before-and-after story where the total stays the same and the answer is 24. Swap with a partner and solve each other’s story with a model.
