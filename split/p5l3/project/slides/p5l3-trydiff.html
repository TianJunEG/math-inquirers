Your turn: the difference stays the same

Find the difference first. It does not change.

a Ravi has $30 and Ali has $10. Their aunt gives each of them the same amount. Then Ali has 1/2 as much as Ravi. How much did she give each?

$10

b Ben has $52 and Siti has $20. Each of them spends the same amount. Then Siti has 1/3 as much as Ben. How much did each spend?

$4

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) The difference is $20 and stays $20. After: Ali 1 unit, Ravi 2 units, so the difference is 1 unit = $20; Ali has $20 after, so she gave 20 − 10 = $10 each (check: $40 and $20). b) The difference is $32. After: Siti 1 unit, Ben 3 units, so 2 units = $32 and 1 unit = $16; Siti has $16 after, so she spent 20 − 16 = $4 (check: Ben 52 − 4 = 48 = 3 × 16).
