For early finishers

Challenge

1. Ali and Ben had $90 altogether. Ali spent 1/3 of his money and Ben spent $10. Then they had the same amount of money. How much did Ali have at first?

2. Mei is 1/4 as old as her father. In 12 years she will be 1/2 as old as her father. How old is Mei now?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. These are NOT on the workbook. Answers: 1) $48 (Ali has 3 units and keeps 2 after spending 1 unit; Ben has $90 − 3 units, and after spending $10 he has $80 − 3 units, which equals the 2 units Ali keeps; so 5 units = $80, 1 unit = $16; Ali $48, Ben $42; both have $32 left). 2) 6 years old (the difference is 3 units now and 1 unit in 12 years, so 3 old units = 1 new unit; Mei now 1 unit = 6, father 24; in 12 years 18 and 36).
