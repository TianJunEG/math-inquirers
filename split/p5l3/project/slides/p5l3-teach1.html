26.5–29 MIN

Worked example: fraction of the remainder

Mei spent 2/5 of her money on a bag and 1/2 of the remainder on shoes. She had $27 left. How much money did she have at first?

Step 1. Money = 5 parts. The bag is 2 parts. The remainder is 3 parts.

Half of 3 parts is awkward: cut every part in 2.

Step 2. Cut every part in 2: 10 equal units. Bag 4, shoes 3 (half of the remainder), left 3.

Step 3. 3 units = $27, so 1 unit = $9 and 10 units = $90. Check: 36 + 27 + 27 = 90 ✓

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 26.5 to 29. Teach. Read the problem twice. Ask: "Is the 1/2 half of her money or half of what is left?" Click order: step 1, the cut bar, steps 2 and 3. Think-aloud: "2/5 on the bag means I draw 5 parts and shade 2. That leaves 3 parts. Half of the REMAINDER is half of 3 parts, and I cannot halve 3 parts neatly, so I cut every part into 2 pieces. Now every unit is the same size. The bag is 4 units, the remainder is 6 units, shoes are 3 units, and 3 units are left." Then: "3 units are $27 so one unit is $9; the whole bar is 10 units, $90." Check by going forwards: bag 2/5 of 90 = 36; remainder 54; shoes 27; left 27. Common error: taking 1/2 of the WHOLE for the shoes.
