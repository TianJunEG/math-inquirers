1. What stayed the same when Ali gave counters to Mei?

2. What stayed the same when both children got 2 more?

3. When did Mei have 1/2 as many as Ali?

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Let pairs keep working, then gather them. Build one table on the board and ask for each row. Answers: 1) The total: 24 every time Ali gives Mei counters (16 and 8, 15 and 9 …). 2) The difference: 12 every time both get 2 more (20 and 8, 22 and 10 …). 3) After Ali gives 2 (16 and 8), and after both get 6 more (24 and 12). Ask: "Why does the total stay the same when Ali gives and the difference stay the same when both get?" Do NOT tell: the next slides name these two ideas.
