LESSON 3 • WEEK 2

Before and After

Fraction word problems

TODAY WE WILL

Draw before-and-after models, find what stays the same, and solve fraction stories with units.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 2 (opening, inside the 90-minute clock). Objective: Solve fraction word problems with units and before-and-after models: fraction of a remainder, giving between two people (the total stays the same), both getting or spending the same amount (the difference stays the same), and one amount that does not change. Thinking strategies today: DRAW A MODEL and ASK "WHAT STAYS THE SAME?" Materials: Mini whiteboard and marker, 24 counters or paper squares (two colours), paper strips for bar models, workbook, pencil. Online: The tutor shares the slides and builds each bar model click by click. Children draw their models on whiteboards and hold them up to the camera together. For the explore, children move 18 + 6 paper squares on the desk with the camera up as usual and hold the table up to the camera; or use the interactive version on the slide. Time plan (the exact minutes of this lesson; every lesson adds up to 90): opening 0 to 2, sneak-peek puzzles 2 to 6.5 (one hold-up for both puzzles, no volunteer round, nobody is marked), warm-up 6.5 to 13 (one game round, four quick questions), explore 13 to 26.5 (pair work 6 minutes at most, then two questions together; a Go further what-if is on the explore slide for any pair that finishes), teaching 26.5 to 38.5 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 38.5 to 42.5, workbook page 1 42.5 to 56.5 (the questions without a star; after question 2 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder), check together 56.5 to 60, short teaching before page 2 60 to 61.5, workbook page 2 61.5 to 69.5 (alone: start with the questions named at the top, then choose; starred parts are take-home; nobody has to finish), check together 69.5 to 71.5, talk time 71.5 to 77 (you may say pass; volunteers first; the first step of the Investigation is take-home), exit ticket 77 to 83, reflection 83 to 85.5 (pass is always OK), stamp typed in the chat and goodbye 85.5 to 86.5, and the buffer 86.5 to 90 (floating time: spend it first if you run late, never fill it on purpose). Think harder, Go further and the Investigation are open to every child and have no clock time. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and never splits a pair unless both children agree. Invite the WHOLE group, never one child: "when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation."
