LESSON 3 • WEEK 2

Before and After

Fraction word problems

TODAY WE WILL

Draw before-and-after models, find what stays the same, and solve fraction stories with units.

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Solve fraction word problems with units and before-and-after models: fraction of a remainder, giving between two people (the total stays the same), both getting or spending the same amount (the difference stays the same), and one amount that does not change. Thinking strategies today: DRAW A MODEL and ASK "WHAT STAYS THE SAME?" Materials: Mini whiteboard and marker, 24 counters or paper squares (two colours), paper strips for bar models, workbook, pencil. Online: The tutor shares the slides and builds each bar model click by click. Children draw their models on whiteboards and hold them up to the camera together. For the explore, children move 18 + 6 paper squares on the desk with the camera up as usual and hold the table up to the camera; or use the interactive version on the slide. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 9, explore 9 to 21, teaching 21 to 33 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 33 to 37, worksheet page 1 37 to 49, check together 49 to 51, short teaching before page 2 51 to 57, worksheet page 2 57 to 72 (alone), check together 72 to 76, talk time 76 to 82, exit ticket 82 to 90. Stretch and challenge are for children who finish early, or for home.
