Preview: Sharing in a ratio

Tool: Draw it

PREVIEW: a Primary 6 topic, beyond Level 5. A taste, not a test.

Mei and Siti share 45 stickers in the ratio 4 : 5. Mei gets the 4 and Siti gets the 5.

(a) How many equal parts are there?

(b) How many stickers is one part?

(c) How many stickers does each girl get?

(d) How many more stickers does Siti get than Mei?

(a) 9 parts.

(b) 5 stickers.

(c) Mei 20 stickers and Siti 25 stickers.

(d) 5 more stickers.

ADDED ITEM r3 (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Sharing in a ratio"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Draw it. PREVIEW LABEL: say "This is a first taste of a topic from a later level. We are not testing it; we are noticing how it works." SAY: Draw a bar for Mei with 4 boxes and a bar for Siti with 5 boxes, the same size. The 45 stickers fill all the boxes. ASK: How can you check your answer? What do you notice about the difference between the two shares and one part? LISTEN FOR: 4 + 5 = 9 parts, 45 ÷ 9 = 5. Mei 4 × 5 = 20, Siti 5 × 5 = 25, total 45. The difference 5 is 1 part because 5 − 4 = 1. Listen for the check: the two shares add up to 45. Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) 9 parts. (b) 5 stickers. (c) Mei 20 stickers and Siti 25 stickers. (d) 5 more stickers. WHY: One part is 45 ÷ 9 = 5 stickers. Mei: 4 × 5 = 20. Siti: 5 × 5 = 25. The shares add to 45, and their ratio 20 : 25 is 4 : 5 when both are divided by 5. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): Ravi and Aisha share 56 marbles in the ratio 3 : 5. How many marbles does each get? Answer: Ravi 21 marbles, Aisha 35 marbles.
