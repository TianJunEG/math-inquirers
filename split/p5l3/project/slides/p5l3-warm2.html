9.5–13 MIN

Quick questions

1. What is 2/5 of 45?

18

2. 3/4 of a number is 27. What is the number?

36

3. What is 1/3 of 3/4?

1/4

4. Ali has 12 more stickers than Ben. They each get 5 more. How many more does Ali have now?

Still 12

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 9.5 to 13. Whiteboards: read each question aloud, give thinking time, hold up boards together, click to show. Answers: 1) 18 (45 ÷ 5 = 9, 9 × 2). 2) 36 (3 units = 27, 1 unit = 9, 4 units = 36). 3) 1/4 (1/3 of 3 quarters is 1 quarter). 4) Still 12: both get the same, so the gap does not change. Question 4 is one of the two big ideas of today (the other is that the total stays the same when cards move between two children); do not explain yet, just ask "why?" and collect ideas. If you are short of time, skip question 4.
