6–9 MIN

Quick questions

1. What is 2/5 of 45?

18

2. What is 1/4 of 36?

9

3. What is half of 1/2?

1/4

4. Ali has 12 more stickers than Ben. They each get 5 more. How many more does Ali have now?

Still 12

5. Ali has 30 cards and Ben has 20. Ali gives Ben 5. How many cards do they have altogether now?

Still 50

Math Inquirers Level 5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6 to 9. Whiteboards: read each question aloud, give thinking time, hold up boards together, click to show. Answers: 1) 18 (45 ÷ 5 = 9, 9 × 2). 2) 9 (36 ÷ 4). 3) 1/4 (half of a half). 4) Still 12: both get the same, so the gap does not change. 5) Still 50: the cards only moved from one child to the other. Questions 4 and 5 are the two big ideas of today; do not explain yet, just ask "why?" and collect ideas.
