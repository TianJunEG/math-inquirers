Warm-up: What's left?

A jar has 120 marbles. I turn over three cards, one at a time. After each card, write how many marbles are LEFT. Read every card carefully: "of the jar" or "of what is left"?

Each unit is 10 marbles. Card 1 gives away 3 units.

Card 1 Give away 1/4 of the jar.

90 left

Card 2 Give away 1/3 of what is left.

60 left

Card 3 Give away 5/6 of what is left.

10 left

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. What is left? Children have mini whiteboards. Say "Card 1" and read it; give thinking time; children write the number left and hold up boards together; click to show the answer. Then Card 2 and Card 3. Talk: Card 2 says "of what is left", so we take 1/3 of 90, not 1/3 of 120 (90 − 30 = 60). Card 3: 5/6 of 60 is 50, so 10 are left. Ask: "Did anyone take 1/3 of 120? What would that give? (40; the story would be different.)" The bar shows the first card: 120 is cut into 12 units of 10, and 1/4 is 3 units. Listen for children who draw the remainder as a new bar. Answers: 90, 60, 10. This is the idea of the whole lesson: after each step the amount we start from changes.
