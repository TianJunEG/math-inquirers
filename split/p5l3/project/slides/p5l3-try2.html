Draw the After model first.

a Ali has 45 stickers and Mei has 15. How many must Ali give Mei so that they have the same number?

15

b Ben and Ali have 72 marbles. Ben gives Ali 6, and then Ben has 1/2 as many as Ali. How many did Ben have at first?

30

c Ali has 40 stickers and Mei has 24. Ali gives Mei 8. How many does each have now?

32 each

Whiteboards. a) The total is 60, so 30 each; Ali gives 45 − 30 = 15 (check: 30 and 30). b) After: Ben 1 unit, Ali 2 units, 3 units = 72, 1 unit = 24, so Ben has 24 after and had 24 + 6 = 30 at first. c) Total 64, Ali 32 and Mei 32 after giving 8.
