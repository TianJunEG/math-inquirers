Worked example: the difference stays the same

Mei is 8 and her mother is 36. In how many years will Mei be 1/3 as old as her mother?

Step 1. Both get older by the same number of years, so the difference stays the same: 36 − 8 = 28.

Step 2. Later: Mei 1 unit, Mum 3 units. The difference is 2 units = 28, so 1 unit = 14.

Step 3. Mei will be 14, so 14 − 8 = 6 years later. Check: 14 and 42, and 14 is 1/3 of 42 ✓

Later, Mei is 1 unit and Mum is 3 units, so the gap is 2 units. 2 units are 28, so 1 unit is 14. Mei is 14 later; she is 8 now; 6 years."
