Worked example: one amount stays the same

2/5 of the pupils in a class are girls. After 4 more girls join, half of the class are girls. How many pupils were in the class at first?

Step 1. The boys did not change: 3 units before and 3 units after.

Step 2. After, half are girls, so girls = boys = 3 units. The girls grew by 1 unit: that is the 4 new girls.

Step 3. 1 unit = 4, so at first 5 units = 20 pupils. Check: 8 girls, 12 boys; 12 and 12 later ✓

Math Inquirers P5 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5, the bead box). Ask first: "Which group did NOT change?" The boys. So the boys get the same number of units in the Before and the After model. Click order: step 1, the After model, then steps 2 and 3. Think-aloud: "Before, 2/5 girls means girls 2 units and boys 3 units. After, half are girls, so girls and boys are equal: 3 units each. The girls went from 2 units to 3 units, so the 4 new girls are 1 unit. The class was 5 units: 20." Common mistake: making the total the same in both models. Here the total changes (20 to 24); only the boys stay the same.
