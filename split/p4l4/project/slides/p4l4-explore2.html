1. How many pizzas did they eat? Write it in two ways.

2. How many slices make exactly 3 pizzas?

3. What if each pizza had 6 slices and they ate 11?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Gather the ideas. Write 11/4 and 2 3/4 side by side and tell children the names: 11/4 is an IMPROPER fraction (the top is bigger than the bottom, so it is more than 1); 2 3/4 is a MIXED number (a whole number and a fraction). They are the same amount. Answers: 1) 11/4 = 2 3/4. 2) 12 slices (12/4 = 3). 3) 11/6 = 1 5/6: one whole pizza and 5 sixths.
