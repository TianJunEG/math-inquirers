EXPLORE

  1. Cut paper circles into 4 equal slices (quarters).
  2. Ravi's group eats 11 slices. Take 11 quarter-slices.
  3. Put the slices together to build whole pizzas.
  4. Write how many pizzas they ate in two different ways.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8 to 13. Pairs use paper quarter-slices (online: cut paper circles on camera). Ask them to predict first: more than 2 pizzas or less? More than 3? Let them write the amount any way they like; listen for "eleven quarters" and "two and three quarters". Early finishers: what if every pizza had 6 slices and they ate 11 slices?
