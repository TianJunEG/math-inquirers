Improper fraction to mixed number

Write 73 as a mixed number.

Step 1. 3 thirds make 1 whole. Fill the first bar: 3 thirds used.

Step 2. Fill the second bar: 6 thirds used. 1 third is left.

Step 3. Put the last third in the third bar: 2 wholes and 1 third.

73 = 213

7 ÷ 3 = 2 remainder 1

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Strategy: DRAW A MODEL. Ask first: "Is 7/3 more or less than 1? How do you know?" (the top is bigger than the bottom). Click order: step 1, first bar fills, step 2, second bar fills, step 3, the last third, the answer. Think-aloud: "The denominator says 3 pieces make a whole. I have 7 pieces. 3 fill one bar, 3 more fill another, and 1 is left over. That is 2 and 1 third." Show the shortcut at the end: 7 ÷ 3 = 2 remainder 1; the remainder is the number of thirds left over.
