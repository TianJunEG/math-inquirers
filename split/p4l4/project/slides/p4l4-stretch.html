For early finishers

Stretch

1. How many halves are there in 6 1/2?

2. Which is closer to 1: 7/8 or 9/10? Explain.

3. Ben has 48 marbles. 5/8 of them are green. How many marbles are NOT green?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 13 (6 wholes = 12 halves, plus 1). 2) 9/10: it is only 1/10 short of 1, and 7/8 is 1/8 short; a tenth is a smaller piece than an eighth. 3) 18 (1 unit = 48 ÷ 8 = 6; green 5 units = 30; not green 3 units = 18).
