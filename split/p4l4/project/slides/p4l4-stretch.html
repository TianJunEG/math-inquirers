Answers (same four questions as the printed Think harder page): 1) Ben. 7/4 = 14/8 = 1 6/8, which is more than 1 5/8. 2) 25 (1 unit = 5, blue = 15, not blue = 5 units = 25). 3) 1/2, 7/12, 2/3, 5/6 (6/12, 7/12, 8/12, 10/12). 4) 17/6 = 2 5/6. Between 2 and 3: 13/6, 14/6, 15/6, 16/6, 17/6. Odd numerators: 13/6, 15/6, 17/6. 15/6 = 2 1/2 is exactly in the middle and 13/6 is closer to 2, so only 17/6 is closer to 3.
