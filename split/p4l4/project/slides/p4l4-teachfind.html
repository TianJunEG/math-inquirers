Find ALL: make the denominators the same

Fractions with denominator 8 between 14 and 34

14 = 28 and 34 = 68

In between: 38, 48, 58

28 and 68 are EQUAL to the ends: they do not count.

3 fractions. Make a list in order, so none is missed.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 58.5 to 59. Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6). Strategy: MAKE A LIST IN ORDER. Change the end fractions to eighths, then list every eighth in between. The ends themselves do not count, because "between" means bigger than one and smaller than the other. The "Who am I?" riddle is now on the Think harder page (stretch 4): it uses the same idea, list everything that fits the first clues and then cross out with each new clue (example: improper fraction, denominator 4, between 1 and 2, odd numerator, closer to 2 than to 1: 5/4, 6/4, 7/4; odd: 5/4, 7/4; closer to 2: 7/4).
