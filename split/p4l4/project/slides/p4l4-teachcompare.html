Comparing fractions: three tools

Same denominator

23 or 59 ?

23 = 69, and 69 is more than 59

23 is bigger

Compare with a half

38 or 47 ?

Half of 8 is 4: 38 is less than a half. Half of 7 is 3½: 47 is more.

47 is bigger

Same numerator

25 or 29 ?

Both have 2 pieces. Fifths are bigger pieces than ninths.

25 is bigger

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4). Three tools, three examples. Click each column: the reason, then the answer. Tool 1: change one fraction so the denominators match (2/3 = 6/9: multiply top and bottom by 3). Show it with paper strips: a strip in thirds and a strip in ninths, same length. Tool 2: compare each with a half, the "half of the denominator" test from the warm-up. Tool 3: same numerator, so look at the size of the pieces: sharing among 5 gives bigger pieces than sharing among 9. Ask: "Which tool is quickest for 5/6 and 3/4?" (same denominator: twelfths).
