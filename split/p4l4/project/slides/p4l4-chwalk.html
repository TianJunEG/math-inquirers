Q1: cut both bars into sixths

A half = 36. A third = 26.

The difference is 16, and that is 3.

The number is 66: 6 × 3 = 18.

Check: half of 18 is 9, a third is 6. 9 is 3 more ✓

Q2: cut each fifth in half

Monday 25 = 410. The rest is 610.

Tuesday: half of the rest = 310.

Left: 310 = 45 pages, so 110 = 15.

The book is 10 × 15 = 150 pages ✓

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use after pairs have tried. Q1: guess and check also works (try 12: 6 and 4, only 2 more; try 18: 9 and 6, 3 more). The sixths idea shows WHY: the gap between a half and a third is always one sixth. Q2: check forwards: Monday 2/5 of 150 = 60, rest 90, Tuesday 45, left 45 ✓. A common mistake is to say Tuesday is half of the whole book.
