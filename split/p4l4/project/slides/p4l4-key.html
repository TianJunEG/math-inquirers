Key idea: fractions can be more than 1

The denominator tells how many pieces make 1 whole: 73 = 213

Fraction of a set: the denominator gives the number of units. Find 1 unit, then take the units you need.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Changing a mixed number to an improper fraction by multiplying the whole number by the numerator, or forgetting to add the numerator (3 2/5 written as 6/5 or 15/5). (2) Thinking the bigger denominator means the bigger fraction (3/7 > 3/5 "because 7 is bigger than 5"). (3) Finding a fraction of a set by dividing by the numerator, or stopping at one unit (3/8 of 24 answered 8 or 3).
