57.5–58.5 MIN

Find the whole from a part

23 of the marbles in a bag are blue. There are 24 blue marbles. How many marbles are in the bag?

Step 1. The whole bag is 3 units. 2 units are blue.

Step 2. 2 units = 24, so 1 unit = 24 ÷ 2 = 12.

Step 3. 3 units = 3 × 12 = 36.

36 marbles. Check: 36 ÷ 3 = 12, 2 × 12 = 24 ✓

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5). This time we know a PART, not the whole. Draw the whole bar in 3 units and shade 2 units; the 24 goes under the shaded part, the question mark over the whole. Think-aloud: "2 units are 24, so 1 unit is 12. The bag is 3 units: 36." Watch for children who find 2/3 of 24 (= 16): ask "Is the bag bigger or smaller than the blue marbles?"
