Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Focus habit: Explain
SPINE: Minutes 87.5 to 90. Start the timer (90 seconds). Say: "Pick one problem from today. Question 1: did I answer the question? Question 2: does my answer make sense? Question 3: can I check it another way? Tick, write one line, or pass. If you are still stuck, tick the box and I will come." Children tick the Look back box on the Choose and look-back page of their workbook, or just tick on paper. A pass is always allowed. It is never marked, never collected in front of the group and never read out; the tutor may look at it afterwards. If a child ticks "I am still stuck", check in during the open session next lesson (a quick visit to the shared breakout room or a hand signal, with a parent nearby; never a direct message). Do not ask children to share. Praise a check you saw, not speed. Then stamp the journals. HABIT: Focus habit: Explain. Ask for the reason in the child's own words, said or written. Say: "How do you know? Tell it as if you were teaching a friend." Do not give the answer first: ask the prompt, wait, and give a hint only if the child is stuck.
