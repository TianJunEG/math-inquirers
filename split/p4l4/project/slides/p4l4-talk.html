Minutes 70 to 80. Pairs talk for 2 minutes, then share. Answer: 2 1/3 is more. 9/4 = 2 1/4, so both have 2 wholes; a third is bigger than a quarter (sharing a pizza among 3 gives bigger slices than among 4). Mei looked only at the 9, but quarters are small pieces. Second question if time: "Ravi says 3/7 is bigger than 3/5 because 7 is bigger than 5. What would you tell him?" (fifths are bigger pieces, so 3/5 is bigger). Praise the reasons, not only the answers.
