Minutes 79.5 to 83.5. Pair talk is 1 minute (no breakout rooms: children tell the grown-up beside them, or write one sentence), then share. Answer: 2 1/3 is more. 9/4 = 2 1/4, so both have 2 wholes; a third is bigger than a quarter (sharing a pizza among 3 gives bigger slices than among 4). Mei is a made-up character, not a child in the group: say "her idea", never name a real child. Mei looked only at the 9, but quarters are small pieces. Not asked in class (moved to the Weekly Dose of Thinking Math): "Ravi says 3/7 is bigger than 3/5 because 7 is bigger than 5. What would you tell him?" (fifths are bigger pieces, so 3/5 is bigger). Praise the reasons, not only the answers. TALK TIME RULES: ask for volunteers first; a child may always pass; never call on a child by name to answer; no public tallies, scores or rankings; praise the reason, not the child. If a question uses a mistaken idea (a character who gets it wrong), present it as the character's idea and ask "What would you say to them?" so no child owns the mistake.
