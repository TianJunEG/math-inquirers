Q3 Fraction of a set. Use the bar model.

Stuck? Fold a paper strip, or draw the bars. After question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder.

Q2 C is 10 quarters: children may write 2 2/4 or 2 1/2; both are right. CHOOSING (printed on page 1: "After question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder."): invite the WHOLE group ("when you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation"); never message a child privately, never name a child, never announce who is on which page. If one child of a pair wants to move on, do not split the pair unless both agree; if a pair does lose a member, join the other child to another pair or to yourself straight away.
