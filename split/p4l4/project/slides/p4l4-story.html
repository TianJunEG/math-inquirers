Story break: Mahavira

India (Karnataka, possibly Mysore) • about 800 - about 870 (he worked around 850)

Mahavira lived in southern India about 1,200 years ago, around the year 850. We know almost nothing else about his life, not even exactly where he was born. We do know he wrote a maths book, the Ganita Sara Sangraha, while King Amoghavarsha ruled the land. One chapter explains how to work with fractions, including how to split a fraction into unit fractions, which have 1 on top. For example, the book shows that 2/17 can be written as 1/12 + 1/51 + 1/68. Splitting a fraction into smaller, friendlier pieces is a clever way to understand it.
