Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards. a) 28 ÷ 4 = 7, 3 × 7 = 21. b) 42 ÷ 6 = 7, 5 × 7 = 35. c) 1 unit = 36 ÷ 4 = 9; she gives away 9 and keeps 3 units = 27. Ask: "Could you do c in one step?" (she keeps 3/4 of 36 = 27).
