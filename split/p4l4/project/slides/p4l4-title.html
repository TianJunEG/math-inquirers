LESSON 4 • WEEK 2 • 0–1.5 MIN

Fraction Frontier

Fractions

TODAY WE WILL

Change between improper fractions and mixed numbers, find a fraction of a set with a bar model, and compare fractions with different denominators.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Change between improper fractions and mixed numbers using fraction bars and number lines, find a fraction of a set (and the whole set from a part) with a bar model, and compare fractions with different denominators by making the denominators the same, comparing with one half, or comparing pieces when the numerators are the same. Thinking strategies taught today: DRAW A MODEL (fraction bars, number lines, bar models), MAKE A LIST IN ORDER and COMPARE WITH A BENCHMARK (one half). Materials: paper strips (one per child, to fold into halves, quarters and eighths), paper circles to cut into quarters, 30 counters, mini whiteboards and markers. Online: before the lesson children cut out 3 paper circles and cut each into 4 equal slices; they fold paper strips on camera and hold up mini whiteboards. Time plan (90 minutes in all; every part has a budget and there is a 2-minute standing buffer inside the page-2 block). Opening 0 to 1.5. Sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 6.5 (puzzle 1 about 60 seconds, puzzle 2 about 90 seconds, then ONE hold-up moment for both papers; answers shown to the tutor only, on camera, nobody marked). Warm-up game 6.5 to 8.5 (one short round, about 90 seconds), quick questions 8.5 to 11.5 (three questions). Explore 11.5 to 24 (the only breakout of the first half: about 60 seconds each way, closed by a 60-second timer; a Go further what-if is on the slide from the start; two debrief questions). Teaching 24 to 32 (click to reveal each step and answer; the one "your turn" slide is optional: skip it if you are behind). Brain break 32 to 35.5 (water and toilet first, then the game; clock check at the end of the break: if you are more than 1.5 minutes behind, skip the next optional item, never a check, the exit ticket or Looking back). Worksheet 35.5 to 79.5: page 1 (Start here, in pairs) 35.5 to 53, check together 53 to 55.5, short teaching before page 2 55.5 to 59, page 2 on your own 59 to 77 (the last 2 minutes are the standing buffer: if earlier parts ran long, spend the buffer first, then the choice questions, and never the checks, the exit ticket or Looking back), check together 77 to 79.5. Talk time 79.5 to 83.5 (one minute of pair talk, no breakout). Exit ticket 83.5 to 86.5. Looking back 86.5 to 90 (2 minutes of writing). After question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation have no fixed time slot: any child may choose them at any time, or take them home. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and pairs are not split unless both children agree. Online: page 1 is pair work, so allow about 2 minutes to move into and out of breakout rooms (or run page 1 as a whole-group screen-share if there are only 8 children).
