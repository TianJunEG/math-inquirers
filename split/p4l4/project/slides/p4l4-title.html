LESSON 4 • WEEK 2 • 0–1 MIN

Fraction Frontier

Fractions

TODAY WE WILL

Change between improper fractions and mixed numbers, find a fraction of a set with a bar model, and compare fractions with different denominators.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Change between improper fractions and mixed numbers using fraction bars and number lines, find a fraction of a set (and the whole set from a part) with a bar model, and compare fractions with different denominators by making the denominators the same, comparing with one half, or comparing pieces when the numerators are the same. Thinking strategies taught today: DRAW A MODEL (fraction bars, number lines, bar models), MAKE A LIST IN ORDER and COMPARE WITH A BENCHMARK (one half). Materials: paper strips (one per child, to fold into halves, quarters and eighths), paper circles to cut into quarters, 30 counters, mini whiteboards and markers. Online: before the lesson children cut out 3 paper circles and cut each into 4 equal slices; they fold paper strips on camera and hold up mini whiteboards. Time plan: opening 0 to 1, sneak-peek puzzles 1 to 4 (two ideas we have not learnt yet; answers shown to the tutor only, on camera, nobody marked), warm-up game 4 to 6 (one short round), quick questions 6 to 8, explore 8 to 18 (a Go further what-if is on the slide from minute 8), teaching 18 to 30 (click to reveal each step and answer; the "your turn" slides are optional, skip them if short of time), brain break 30 to 34 (an optional 2-minute Story break about a mathematician, minutes 32 to 34, skippable), worksheet page 1 (Start here, in pairs) 34 to 50 and check together 50 to 53, short teaching before page 2 53 to 58, worksheet page 2 (on your own) 58 to 76 and check together 76 to 80, talk time 80 to 85, exit ticket 85 to 88, looking back (reflection) 88 to 90 (2 minutes). After question 3 every child may choose: carry on, or try the Think harder page. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation have no fixed time slot: any child may choose them at any time, or take them home. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and pairs are not split unless both children agree. Online: page 1 is pair work, so allow about 2 minutes to move into and out of breakout rooms (or run page 1 as a whole-group screen-share if there are only 8 children). A slide marked ON DEMAND is not part of the teaching before page 2: show it only while children work on page 2, when a child chooses that choice question, and make the offer to the whole group. The page itself carries the same hint.
