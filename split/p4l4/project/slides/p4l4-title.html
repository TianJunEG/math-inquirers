LESSON 4 • WEEK 2

Fraction Frontier

Fractions

TODAY WE WILL

Change between improper fractions and mixed numbers, find a fraction of a set with a bar model, and compare fractions with different denominators.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Change between improper fractions and mixed numbers using fraction bars and number lines, find a fraction of a set (and the whole set from a part) with a bar model, and compare fractions with different denominators by making the denominators the same, comparing with one half, or comparing pieces when the numerators are the same. Thinking strategies taught today: DRAW A MODEL (fraction bars, number lines, bar models), MAKE A LIST IN ORDER and COMPARE WITH A BENCHMARK (one half). Materials: paper strips (one per child, to fold into halves, quarters and eighths), paper circles to cut into quarters, 30 counters, mini whiteboards and markers. Online: before the lesson children cut out 3 paper circles and cut each into 4 equal slices; they fold paper strips on camera and hold up mini whiteboards. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
