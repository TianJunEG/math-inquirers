Fraction of a set: use a bar model

Mei has 30 stickers. 35 of them are stars. How many star stickers does she have?

Step 1. The denominator is 5: cut the bar into 5 equal units.

Step 2. 5 units = 30, so 1 unit = 30 ÷ 5 = 6.

Step 3. 3 units = 3 × 6 = 18.

18 stars

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Strategy: DRAW A BAR MODEL. The whole bar is ALL the stickers. The denominator tells how many equal units to cut it into; the numerator tells how many units we want. Think-aloud: "30 stickers in 5 equal units: each unit is 6. Stars are 3 units: 18." Ask: "How many stickers are NOT stars?" (2 units = 12). Watch for children who divide by 3 (the numerator).
