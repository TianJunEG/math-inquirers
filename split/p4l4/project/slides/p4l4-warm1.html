Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 4 to 6 (one short round, about 2 minutes: the Warm-up check came first). More or less than a half? Point to one card at a time. Children show thumbs up (more than a half), thumbs down (less) or a flat hand (exactly a half), then you click to reveal (clicks go 3/8, 5/9, 6/12, 7/10, 2/5, 4/7). Ask each time: "How do you know?" The quick test: find half of the denominator and compare it with the numerator. 3/8: half of 8 is 4, and 3 is less than 4. 5/9: half of 9 is 4 and a half, and 5 is more. 6/12: half of 12 is 6, exactly a half. Listen for children who say "5/9 is less because 9 is big". That idea comes back when we compare fractions later.
