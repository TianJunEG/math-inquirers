Challenge

1. Half of a number is 3 more than a third of the number. What is the number?

2. Siti read 2/5 of her book on Monday. On Tuesday she read half of the pages that were left. She still has 45 pages to read. How many pages are in the book?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. These are Think Harder Q6 and Q7. Answers: 1) 18 (half = 3 sixths, a third = 2 sixths, so 1 sixth = 3). 2) 150 pages (Monday 4 tenths, Tuesday 3 tenths, 3 tenths left = 45).
