Mixed number to improper fraction

Write 423 as an improper fraction.

Step 1. Each whole is 3 thirds. 4 wholes = 4 × 3 = 12 thirds.

Step 2. Add the 2 thirds: 12 + 2 = 14 thirds.

423 = 143 Check: 14 ÷ 3 = 4 remainder 2 ✓

Math Inquirers Level 4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example. Ask: "How many thirds are in ONE whole? In 4 wholes?" Children count the shaded thirds on the bars: 3, 6, 9, 12, then 13, 14. Think-aloud: "4 wholes, each cut into 3, is 12 thirds. 2 more thirds make 14 thirds." Watch for the common mistake 4 × 2 = 8 (multiplying the whole number by the numerator): ask them to point to 8 thirds on the bars.
