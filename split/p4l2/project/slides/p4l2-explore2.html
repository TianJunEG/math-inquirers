1. Which of your numbers round to 50 000 to the nearest thousand?

2. How do you know you have found them all?

3. Which one is closest to 50 000? How far away is it?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Let pairs keep working, then gather. Collect numbers on the board and test each one together. Answers: 1) 49 527, 49 572, 49 725, 49 752. 2) A number that rounds to 50 000 must be from 49 500 to 50 499. With no 0 card, nothing starts 50, so it must start 49, and the hundreds digit must be 5 or 7 (2 and 4 are too small). Each of those gives 2 numbers. 3) 49 752, only 248 away.
