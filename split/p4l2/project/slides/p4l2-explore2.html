1. Which of your numbers round to 50 000 to the nearest thousand?

2. How do you know you have found them all?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 21 to 24.5. Pencils down (or when the 60-second timer closes the rooms), then gather. Two debrief questions only. Collect numbers on the board and test each one together. Answers: 1) 49 527, 49 572, 49 725, 49 752. 2) A number that rounds to 50 000 must be from 49 500 to 50 499. With no 0 card, nothing starts 50, so it must start 49, and the hundreds digit must be 5 or 7 (2 and 4 are too small). Each of those gives 2 numbers. The third question (which is closest to 50 000? 49 752, only 248 away) is not asked; the next slides teach the halfway mark properly.
