Siti says 49 999 rounds to 50 000 to the nearest 10, 100 AND 1000. Ravi says 45 000 rounds to 40 000 to the nearest ten thousand. Who is right?

Sentence starters:

“I noticed that …” “I tested it by …” “I know because …”

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 70 to 80. Pairs talk for 2 minutes, then share. Answer: Siti is right: 49 999 is only 1 away from 50 000, so it rounds to 50 000 each time (49 999 → 50 000 to the nearest 10 because 9 ones round up). Ravi is wrong: 45 000 is exactly halfway between 40 000 and 50 000, and halfway rounds up, to 50 000. Praise the reasons, not only the answers.
