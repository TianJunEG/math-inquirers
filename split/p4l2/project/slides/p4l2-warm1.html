Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6.5 to 8.5 (one short round, about 90 seconds: the Sneak-peek puzzles came first). Place It Right with 5 digits. Children draw five boxes: TTh, Th, H, T, O. Shuffle digit cards 0 to 9 and turn over one at a time (online: read the digit aloud; nothing is held up to the camera). After EACH card, children write it in one empty box at once and cannot move it. After five cards, read the numbers aloud: biggest wins. Round 2: closest to 50 000 wins. Click to show the thinking question. Talk: "A 4 or a 5 in the ten thousands place. If you have a 4, you want the other digits BIG (49 9..). If you have a 5, you want them small (50 1..)." 49 999 is 1 away and 50 100 is 100 away. Listen for children who compare the distances; that is rounding thinking, and it comes straight back in the explore.
