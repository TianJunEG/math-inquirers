Stretch

1. What is the largest whole number that rounds to 30 000 to the nearest ten thousand?

2. Find the rule and write the next two numbers: 100 000, 97 500, 95 000, ___, ___

3. Use the digit cards 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9 once each. Make the number closest to 60 000.

Answers: 1) 34 999 (35 000 is halfway and rounds up to 40 000). 2) Take away 2500: 92 500, 90 000. 3) 59 731 (269 away; the best above 60 000 is 61 357, which is 1357 away).
