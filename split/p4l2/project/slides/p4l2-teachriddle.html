Number riddles: strongest clue first

I am a 5-digit number. Rounded to the nearest thousand, I am 20 000. My digits add up to 5. My ones digit is 3.

Step 1. Rounding clue: I am from 19 500 to 20 499.

Step 2. Starting 19: 1 + 9 = 10, already more than 5. So I start 20 …

Step 3. 2 + 0 + ? + ? + 3 = 5, so the hundreds and tens are 0.

I am 20 003. Check every clue ✓
