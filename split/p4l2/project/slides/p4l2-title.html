LESSON 2 • WEEK 1

Big Number Puzzles

Numbers to 100 000

TODAY WE WILL

Round big numbers on a number line, work backwards from a rounded number, and solve 5-digit riddles and patterns.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Round numbers to the nearest 10, 100 and 1000 using a number line, work backwards from a rounded number to find the smallest and largest possible numbers, use rounding to estimate and check, and solve 5-digit number riddles, digit-card puzzles and number patterns. Thinking strategies taught today: DRAW A NUMBER LINE, WORK BACKWARDS, MAKE A LIST IN ORDER and USE THE CLUES ONE AT A TIME. Materials: Digit cards 0 to 9 (one set per child), mini whiteboards, a place-value chart (ten thousands to ones), a blank number line strip. Online: children cut out paper digit cards before the lesson and move them on camera; the tutor marks the number lines on the slides. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
