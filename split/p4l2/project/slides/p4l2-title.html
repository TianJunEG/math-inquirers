LESSON 2 • WEEK 1

Big Number Puzzles

Numbers to 100 000

TODAY WE WILL

Round big numbers on a number line, work backwards from a rounded number, and solve 5-digit riddles and patterns.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Round numbers to the nearest 10, 100 and 1000 using a number line, work backwards from a rounded number to find the smallest and largest possible numbers, use rounding to estimate and check, and solve 5-digit number riddles, digit-card puzzles and number patterns. Thinking strategies taught today: DRAW A NUMBER LINE, WORK BACKWARDS, MAKE A LIST IN ORDER and USE THE CLUES ONE AT A TIME. Materials: Digit cards 0 to 9 (one set per child), mini whiteboards, a place-value chart (ten thousands to ones), a blank number line strip. Online: children cut out paper digit cards before the lesson and move them on camera; the tutor marks the number lines on the slides. Time plan: warm-up game 0 to 5, quick questions 5 to 8, explore 8 to 18, teaching 18 to 30 (click to reveal each step and answer; the "your turn" slides are optional, skip them if short of time), brain break 30 to 34, worksheet page 1 from 34 to 50 and check together 50 to 53, short teaching before page 2 from 53 to 58, worksheet page 2 from 58 to 76 and check together 76 to 80, talk time 80 to 85, exit ticket 85 to 90. The Challenge slides and the Think Harder pages are for early finishers and for take-home: they have no fixed time slot. Online: page 1 is pair work, so allow about 2 minutes to move into and out of breakout rooms (or run page 1 as a whole-group screen-share if there are only 8 children).
