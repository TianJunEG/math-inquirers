LESSON 2 • WEEK 1 • 0–1.5 MIN

Big Number Puzzles

Numbers to 100 000

TODAY WE WILL

Round big numbers on a number line, work backwards from a rounded number, and solve 5-digit riddles and patterns.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Round numbers to the nearest 10, 100 and 1000 using a number line, work backwards from a rounded number to find the smallest and largest possible numbers, use rounding to estimate and check, and solve 5-digit number riddles, digit-card puzzles and number patterns. Thinking strategies taught today: DRAW A NUMBER LINE, WORK BACKWARDS, MAKE A LIST IN ORDER and USE THE CLUES ONE AT A TIME. Materials: Digit cards 0 to 9 (one set per child), mini whiteboards, a place-value chart (ten thousands to ones), a blank number line strip. Online: children cut out paper digit cards before the lesson and move them on camera; the tutor marks the number lines on the slides. Time plan (90 minutes in all; every part has a budget and there is a 3-minute standing buffer inside the page-2 block). Opening 0 to 1.5. Sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 6.5 (puzzle 1 about 60 seconds, puzzle 2 about 90 seconds, then ONE hold-up moment for both papers; answers shown to the tutor only, on camera, nobody marked). Warm-up game 6.5 to 8.5 (one short round, about 90 seconds), quick questions 8.5 to 11.5 (three questions). Explore 11.5 to 24.5 (the only breakout of the first half: about 60 seconds each way, closed by a 60-second timer; a Go further what-if is on the slide from the start; two debrief questions). Teaching 24.5 to 29.5 (click to reveal each step and answer; there is no "your turn" slide today). Brain break 29.5 to 33 (water and toilet first, then the game; clock check at the end of the break: if you are more than 1.5 minutes behind, skip the next optional item, never a check, the exit ticket or Looking back). Worksheet 33 to 80: page 1 (Start here, in pairs) 33 to 50.5, check together 50.5 to 52.5, short teaching before page 2 52.5 to 56.5, page 2 on your own 56.5 to 76.5 (the last 3 minutes are the standing buffer: if earlier parts ran long, spend the buffer first, then the choice questions, and never the checks, the exit ticket or Looking back), check together 76.5 to 80. Talk time 80 to 83.5 (one minute of pair talk, no breakout). Exit ticket 83.5 to 86.5. Looking back 86.5 to 90 (2 minutes of writing). Pairs: questions 1 and 2. Then question 3 on your own. After question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation have no fixed time slot: any child may choose them at any time, or take them home. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and pairs are not split unless both children agree. Online: page 1 is pair work, so allow about 2 minutes to move into and out of breakout rooms (or run page 1 as a whole-group screen-share if there are only 8 children).
