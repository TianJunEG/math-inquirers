28.5–29.5 MIN

Key idea: find the halfway mark

To round, find the two round numbers and the halfway mark. Halfway or more rounds up.

Working backwards: the smallest number IS the halfway mark below. The largest is 1 less than the halfway mark above.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Forgetting to carry when the digit to change is a 9 (63 950 to the nearest hundred written as 63 900 instead of 64 000). (2) Working backwards with the wrong limits: saying the largest number that rounds to 63 000 is 63 500 instead of 63 499. (3) Digit-card puzzles: putting 0 in the ten thousands place, or making the biggest number first and hoping it is even, instead of choosing the ones digit first.
