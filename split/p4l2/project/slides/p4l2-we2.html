Work backwards: what rounds to 25 000?

Rounded to the nearest thousand, a number is 25 000. What are the smallest and largest whole numbers it can be?

Step 1. Halfway marks: 24 500 and 25 500. Halfway rounds UP.

Step 2. Smallest: 24 500. Largest: 25 499 (25 500 would round to 26 000).

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Work backwards from the rounded number. Ask first: "Could the number be 25 600? 24 400? 25 500?" Let children argue, then click the band. Think-aloud: "Everything from the halfway mark below, 24 500, up to just before the halfway mark above rounds to 25 000. 25 500 itself is halfway, and halfway rounds up, to 26 000. So the largest whole number is 25 499." Common mistake: saying 25 500 is the largest.
