Worked example: round 36 482 to the nearest 1000

Step 1. It is between 36 000 and 37 000.

Step 2. Halfway is 36 500. 36 482 is just BEFORE halfway.

Step 3. So it is nearer 36 000. To the nearest 100: between 36 400 and 36 500; 82 is past 50, so 36 500.

36 482 ≈ 36 000 (nearest 1000) 36 482 ≈ 36 500 (nearest 100)
