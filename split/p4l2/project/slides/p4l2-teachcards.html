Digit cards: choose the ones digit first

Use 0, 2, 4, 7 and 9 once each.

Step 1. Largest ODD number: the ones digit must be 7 or 9. Keep 9 for the front, so 7 goes last: 94 207.

Step 2. Smallest EVEN number: 0 cannot go first, so start 20. The last digit must be even: only 4 is left. 20 794.

Step 3. Closest to 50 000: try 49 … (biggest after: 49 720) and 70 … (70 249). 49 720 is only 280 away.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Step 2: "Smallest: the smallest digit that is not 0 goes first, then 0. The ones digit must be even, and the only even card left is 4." Step 3: compare the best number on each side of 50 000.
