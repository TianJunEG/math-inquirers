Digit cards: choose the ones digit first

Use 0, 2, 4, 7 and 9 once each.

Step 1. Largest ODD: the ones digit must be 7 or 9. Keep 9 in front, 7 last: 94 207.

Step 2. Smallest EVEN: 0 cannot go first, so 2 then 0. The only even card left is 4: 20 794.

Step 3. Closest to 50 000: 49 720 (280 away) beats 70 249 (20 249 away).

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6). Think-aloud for step 1: "Odd means the ones digit is odd. I decide that FIRST, then put the biggest of the rest in front." Step 2: "Smallest: the smallest digit that is not 0 goes first, then 0. The ones digit must be even, and the only even card left is 4." Step 3: compare the best number on each side of 50 000. Ask: "Why not 4 … something?" (we need a 9 in the thousands to get close: 49 720).
