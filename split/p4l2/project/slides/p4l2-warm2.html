Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6 to 8. Read each aloud, whiteboards up together, then click. After 2: watch for 6045 or 600 045; say it as "60 thousand, 0 hundreds, 4 tens, 5 ones". After 3: the thousands digit was 9, so the ten thousands digit changes too. After 4: the 0 holds the hundreds place. After 5: 36 815 is between 36 000 and 37 000; ask "Is it nearer 36 000 or 37 000?" Do not teach the halfway rule yet. Answers: 7000, 60 045, 60 600, 43 058, 37 000.
