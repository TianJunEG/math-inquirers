Two clues: where do they overlap?

A whole number rounds to 4250 to the nearest 10, AND to 4200 to the nearest 100. Find all the numbers.

Step 1. Nearest 100 is 4200: from 4150 to 4249. Nearest 10 is 4250: from 4245 to 4254.

In both: 4245, 4246, 4247, 4248, 4249 (5 numbers)
