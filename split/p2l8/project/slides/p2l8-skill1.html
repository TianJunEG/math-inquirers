Think harder: cubes in a corner

1. Cubes are stacked in the corner of a room, touching both walls. The bottom layer is 3 rows of 3. The next layer is 2 rows of 2. The top layer is 1 cube.

a) The bottom layer has ___ cubes.

b) The whole stack has ___ cubes.

c) ___ cubes touch the left wall.

Item 1 (Cubes in the corner of a room; tool of the day idea: Act it out). SAY: "Build it layer by layer with cubes, then look at one wall." ASK: "Which cubes touch the wall in the bottom layer? In the next layer?" LISTEN FOR: "3, then 2, then 1." ANSWER: a) 9. b) 14 (9 + 4 + 1). c) 6 (3 in the bottom layer, 2 in the next and 1 on top).
