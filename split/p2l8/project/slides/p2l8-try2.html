Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards face down until 3, 2, 1 (or show only the tutor; never read a board out), then click. a) 4 + 1 = 5. b) 6 small + 2 squares made of 4 (the 2 by 2 window fits in 2 places) = 8. There is no 3 by 3 square: the grid is only 2 high. c) 8 small + 3 of size 2 by 2 = 11. Ask a child to show the window sliding along c.
