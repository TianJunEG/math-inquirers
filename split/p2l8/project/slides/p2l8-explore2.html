Explore: talk about it

1. How many quarter circles cover a semicircle exactly?

2. Find 3 different ways to make a whole circle from your pieces.

3. How many straight sides has a quarter circle? Are they the same length?

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 14 to 20. Let pairs keep building, then gather them. Answers: 1) 2. 2) 2 semicircles; 4 quarter circles; 1 semicircle and 2 quarter circles. 3) 2 straight sides, and yes, they are the same length (each goes from the middle of the circle to its edge). They meet at a square corner. Ask: "What fraction of the circle is a semicircle? A quarter circle?" (1/2 and 1/4: last lesson!).
