52–54 MIN

Count cubes layer by layer

How many cubes are in this stack? Some are hidden!

Step 1. Lift off the top layer in your head: 3 cubes.

Step 2. Bottom layer: 3 long and 2 deep. 3 + 3 = 6. The back 3 hold up the top layer, so they must be there!

3 + 6 = 9 cubes

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4 counts the cubes in three stacks). Build this stack with real cubes first, then show the picture. Ask: "How many cubes can you see? How many are there really?" Many children say 6 or 7. Click 1: the top layer. Click 2: the bottom layer: point to the right side, where you can see the end of the back row. Click 3: the stack taken apart. Think-aloud: "I count layer by layer. The top layer has 3. The bottom layer is a rectangle 3 long and 2 deep: 6. Cubes cannot float, so there must be cubes under the top ones. 3 + 6 = 9." On the worksheet the rule is the same: every cube sits on the table or on another cube, and there are no gaps at the back.
