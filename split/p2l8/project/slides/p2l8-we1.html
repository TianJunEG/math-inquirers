Worked example: find the pieces

This mushroom is made of pieces. How many of each kind? Tick each piece as you count.

Step 1. 1 straight side and 1 curved side: half circle. 1 of them.

Step 2. 2 straight sides with a square corner and 1 curved side: quarter circles. 2 of them.

Step 3. 4 straight sides, all the same length: square. 1 of them.

1 + 2 + 1 = 4 pieces

Notice: the 2 quarter circles together would make 1 more half circle.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click to reveal each step. Think-aloud: "I count one kind of piece at a time and tick it, so I never miss one or count one twice. The cap has one straight side along the bottom and a curved side: a half circle. The grass pieces each have two straight sides meeting in a square corner and a curved side: quarter circles, even though they face different ways. The stem has 4 straight sides, all the same: a square." Ask: "Does turning a quarter circle make it a different shape?" No. This is the same thinking as the robot on the worksheet.
