Shape Builders

2D and 3D Shapes

TODAY WE WILL

Build shapes from semicircles and quarter circles, count every hidden square, triangle and cube, and find which nets fold into a cube.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Name and describe semicircles and quarter circles and use them to make and break up shapes; count every square and triangle in a figure by working in order; count the cubes in a stack, including hidden ones, layer by layer; decide which nets fold into a cube and find opposite faces. Materials: paper circles (2 per child), scissors, about 12 linking cubes per child (or sugar cubes), printed cube nets from the back of the workbook, mini whiteboards. Online: children cut paper circles and build stacks on camera; the tutor shows the stacks and nets on the slides; a cereal box cut open shows a net. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 (page 1 in pairs, short teaching before page 2, a check-together slide after each page), talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
