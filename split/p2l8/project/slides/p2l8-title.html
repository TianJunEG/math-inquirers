Shape Builders

2D and 3D Shapes

TODAY WE WILL

Build shapes from semicircles and quarter circles, count every hidden square, triangle and cube, and find which nets fold into a cube.

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Name and describe semicircles and quarter circles and use them to make and break up shapes; count every square and triangle in a figure by working in order; count the cubes in a stack, including hidden ones, layer by layer; decide which nets fold into a cube and find opposite faces. Materials: paper circles (2 per child), scissors, about 12 linking cubes per child (or sugar cubes), the cube-net cut-out page (nets A to F, near the back of the workbook, cut out before the lesson), mini whiteboards. Online: children cut paper circles and build stacks on camera; the tutor shows the stacks and nets on the slides; a cereal box cut open shows a net. No linking cubes at home? Use sugar cubes, dice or small boxes, or draw each stack layer by layer. Time plan: Sneak-peek puzzles 1 to 4, warm-up game 4 to 6, quick questions 6 to 8, explore 8 to 20 (explore2 talk is 6 minutes), teaching 20 to 32 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 32 to 36, worksheet page 1 36 to 50 then check together 50 to 52, short teaching 52 to 57, worksheet page 2 57 to 69 then check together 69 to 72, talk time 72 to 77, Investigation share 77 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 87, Looking back 87 to 89, closing and stamps 89 to 90.
