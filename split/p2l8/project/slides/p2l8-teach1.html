Semicircles and quarter circles

Step 1. Cut a circle in half through its middle: 2 semicircles. A semicircle has 1 straight side and 1 curved side.

Step 2. Cut a semicircle in half again: 2 quarter circles. A quarter circle has 2 straight sides and 1 curved side.

The 2 straight sides of a quarter circle are the same length, and they meet at a square corner.

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Hold up a paper circle. Click 1: fold and cut through the middle. "Each half is a semicircle: semi means half. How many sides? One straight side, one curved side." Click 2: cut one semicircle again: "Each piece is a quarter of the circle: a quarter circle. Two straight sides and one curved side." Click 3: put the two straight sides of a quarter circle against a ruler: the same length, and they make a square corner like the corner of a page. Think-aloud: "To name a piece I count its straight sides and look for a curved side. One straight and one curved: semicircle. Two straight that meet at a square corner, and one curved: quarter circle."
