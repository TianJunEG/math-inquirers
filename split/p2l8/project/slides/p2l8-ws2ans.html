Q3 B: one cube is completely hidden at the back, bottom, left. C: count layers: 9 + 4 + 1 = 14. b) a 3 by 3 by 3 cube has 3 layers of 9: 27; 27 − 14 = 13. Q4a: B (2 by 3) has a 2 by 2 block; D has two squares that land on the same face. (Think harder 3: E folds into a cube; F has a row of 5, which wraps round onto itself.) Q4b: the extra square must go under the row (any of the 4 places), so the two loose squares become the top and the bottom. Above the row, or at the ends, it does not work. The dice net (opposite faces add up to 7) is on the Go further page: in a straight row, the 1st and 3rd squares are opposite, and so are the 2nd and 4th.
