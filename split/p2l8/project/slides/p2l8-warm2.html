Quick questions

1. How many faces does a cube have?

2. How many flat faces does a cylinder have?

3. Cut a circle into 2 equal parts. What is each part called?

4. How many quarter circles make 1 whole circle?

5. Which solid has a tip and can roll?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 5 to 10. Read each question aloud, give thinking time, take answers, then click. Answers: 1) 6 2) 2 (the top and the bottom; the side is curved) 3) a semicircle (half circle) 4) 4 5) a cone. For 1, hold up a cube and count the faces with the children, putting a finger on each: front, back, left, right, top, bottom. Ask: "How do you know you did not count a face twice?" (go in pairs: front and back, left and right, top and bottom).
