Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8.5 to 10.5. Read each question aloud, give thinking time, take answers, then click. Answers: 1) 6 2) a half circle. Spare questions in the guide table: a cylinder has 2 flat faces; 4 quarter circles make 1 whole circle; a cone has a tip and can roll. For 1, hold up a cube and count the faces with the children, putting a finger on each: front, back, left, right, top, bottom. Ask: "How do you know you did not count a face twice?" (go in pairs: front and back, left and right, top and bottom). Ask quick questions 1 and 3 only (about 2 minutes): children answer by thumbs, hands, not by naming a child. The other questions are spare.
