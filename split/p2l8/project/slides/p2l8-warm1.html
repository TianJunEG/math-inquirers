Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 6 to 8.5. Point to one object at a time: "What solid is it the same shape as?" Children make the shape with their hands (fists together for a cube, a long box shape for a cuboid, a tube for a cylinder, hands meeting in a point for a cone), then say the name. Click to show (clicks go dice, tissue box, tin can, party hat: four solids only; the drum and the traffic cone are spare). Ask: "How do you know it is a cube and not a cuboid?" (all 6 faces are the same square). "Which ones can roll?" (cylinder and cone). "Which can you stack?" (cube, cuboid, cylinder standing up). Watch for children who call the cube a square: a square is flat, a cube is a solid with 6 square faces. A game of about 2.5 minutes: stop it on the clock even if the game is not finished.
