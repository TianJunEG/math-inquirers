Think harder

1. How many rectangles can you find in a row of 4 squares joined side by side? (A square is a rectangle too. Count all sizes.)

2. A stack is 3 cubes long, 3 cubes wide and 2 cubes high. How many cubes?

3. How many triangles are in a triangle with 3 lines from the top corner to the bottom?

Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Open to any child who chooses it. Answers: 1) 10 (4 of length 1, 3 of length 2, 2 of length 3, 1 of length 4). 2) 18 (each layer 3 + 3 + 3 = 9, two layers). 3) 10: made of 1 part 4, of 2 parts 3, of 3 parts 2, all 4 parts 1 (4 + 3 + 2 + 1).
