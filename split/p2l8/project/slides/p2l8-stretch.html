Think harder

1. A stack of cubes has a bottom layer 4 cubes long and 3 cubes deep, a middle layer 3 cubes long and 2 cubes deep, and 1 cube on top. How many cubes are in the stack? Draw the layers.

2. How many rectangles can you find in a grid 2 squares long and 2 squares high? Remember that a square is a rectangle too. Count them by size.

3. Cut out nets E and F from the cut-out page and try folding them. Which one folds up into a cube? How do you know?

These are the SAME four questions as the printed Think harder page: the slide shows 1 to 3 and question 4 is on the page only. Never announce who is working on which page. Answers: 1) 19 (12 + 6 + 1) 2) 9: four small squares (1 by 1), two long ones (1 high and 2 long), two tall ones (2 high and 1 long), and the whole grid (2 by 2). 4 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 9. 3) E folds into a cube (it is a staircase of squares, one of the 11 cube nets). F does not: the row of 5 squares wraps round and the 1st and 5th squares land on the same face. 4) (Question: Six squares are joined in a straight line. Can they fold into a cube? Explain.) No. When you fold the line round, the 5th square lands on the same face as the 1st and the 6th on the 2nd, and the two ends are left open.
