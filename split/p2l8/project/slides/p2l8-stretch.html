Stretch

1. How many quarter circles make 2 whole circles and 1 semicircle?

2. A stack is 3 cubes long, 3 cubes wide and 2 cubes high. How many cubes?

3. How many triangles are in a triangle with 3 lines from the top corner to the bottom?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

For children who finish early. Answers: 1) 10 (4 + 4 + 2). 2) 18 (each layer 3 + 3 + 3 = 9, two layers). 3) 10: made of 1 part 4, of 2 parts 3, of 3 parts 2, all 4 parts 1 (4 + 3 + 2 + 1).
