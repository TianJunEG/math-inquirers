How many cubes are in this stack? Every cube sits on the table or on another cube.

Write your answer on a slip of paper. Show it to your tutor. Then collect your stamp in your inquiry journal.

Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 80 to 87. Answer: 7 (bottom layer 3 + 3 = 6, top layer 1). Children who write 4 counted only the front faces and the top cube; ask "What is under the top cube? What is behind the front row?" Extension: How many more cubes would make the stack 2 layers of 6? (5 more).
