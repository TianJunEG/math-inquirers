Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 1 to 4. Warm-up check: two questions on the key idea of today, in a NEW setting (not recall). Give about 1 minute each. Whiteboards up together, ask one child "How do you know?", then click to show each answer. Q1 answer: 13 cubes (5 x 2 = 10, and 3 more on top). Listen for: Counts layer by layer. A child who counts only the cubes they can see is missing hidden cubes. Q2 answer: 5 quarter circles (2 + 2 + 1). Listen for: Each semicircle is 2 quarter circles. Listen for “a semicircle is 2 quarters”. FAST TRACK: a child who gets BOTH right AND can say why starts on the THINK HARDER pages and the Open investigation (workbook Q8) when the worksheet begins at minute 36, in their own breakout room or with you checking in. A right answer without a reason stays with the group. If unsure, keep the child in the group. Nobody is labelled: say "Some of you will start on a different page today; everyone gets a turn at both." Children can move at any time. Everyone joins explore, the mini-lesson, the check-together slides and talk time.
