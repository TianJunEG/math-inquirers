Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 1 to 4 (this slide is question 1, minutes 1 to 2; click to the next slide for question 2). Sneak-peek puzzles: two puzzles on the key idea of today, which the children have NOT learnt yet. Say out loud: "These are ideas we have not learnt yet. Guessing is welcome and so is I do not know. Nobody is marked." Question 1 is an access question: any child can try it with objects, a drawing or counting, so everyone has a first win (about 1 minute). Question 2 is the thinking question (about 2 minutes). Answers are PRIVATE: each child holds their own paper to the camera for you only, or writes it on their own paper and shows only you on camera. Do not raise whiteboards together, do not read answers out, do not name children. Q1 answer: 12 cubes (6 in each layer, 2 layers). Listen for: Counting one layer first, then doubling, is a good method. This is a first win for everyone. Q2 answer: 5 (each semicircle is 2 quarters: 2 + 2 + 1). Listen for: Listen for &quot;a semicircle is half a circle, which is 2 quarters&quot;. Drawing the pieces and cutting them is fine. At the end click to show the answers as "one way to think about it", without saying who had what. Ask only for volunteers: "Would anyone like to tell us how they started?" Invite the WHOLE group at once, never one child: "When you have finished Start here, you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation." Never announce who is on which page. Pairs are not split unless both children agree. Children can move either way at any time. Everyone joins explore, the mini-lesson, the check-together slides and talk time.
