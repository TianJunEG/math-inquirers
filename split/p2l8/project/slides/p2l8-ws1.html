Q1 The robot. Count one kind of piece at a time and tick it. Then make whole circles from pieces. (Part c is a ★ take-home star.)

Q2 Count every square (by size). (The triangles, part b, are a ★ take-home star.)

Stuck? Use your paper pieces, or colour each shape you count.

Minutes 38.5 to 52.5. Pairs work together. Ask "How do you know you have them all?" before giving any help. For Q2a, give pairs a paper window to slide (cut a 2 by 2 and a 3 by 3 hole in card). Q1(c) and Q2(b) (triangles) are marked ★ take-home on the page: children do them at home. Say once, to everyone: "After question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder." Never say who is on which page. Invite the WHOLE group at once, never one child: "When you have finished Start here, you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation." Pairs are not split unless both children agree.
