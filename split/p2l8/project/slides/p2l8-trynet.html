Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Whiteboards (or thumbs up and down), then click. a) Yes: this is one of the 11 cube nets. b) No: the 1st and the 3rd square of the row are opposite each other, so the two squares on top of them both fold onto the same face (the lid), and the bottom is open. c) E: C, D and E are in a straight line, so when D is the bottom, C and E are the two walls facing each other. Also A is opposite D, and B is opposite F. This is the idea for the dice net in Q5.
