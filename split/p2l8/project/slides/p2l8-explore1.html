8–14 MIN

Explore: circle pieces

EXPLORE

  1. Take 2 paper circles that are the same size.
  2. Fold one in half and cut along the fold.
  3. Fold the other in half, then in half again. Open it and cut along both folds.
  4. Use your pieces to make new shapes. Name them!

Math Inquirers P2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 8 to 14. Each child cuts their own circles (draw round a cup if there are no ready-made circles). One circle gives 2 semicircles; the other gives 4 quarter circles. Children then put pieces together: whole straight sides must touch. Things to make: a whole circle in different ways, a semicircle from 2 quarter circles, a "fan", a "wave" (two semicircles side by side, one turned over). Online: the same on camera.
