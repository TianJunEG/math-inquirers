Q1: count one step at a time

Tall step: 3 high and 3 deep: 3 + 3 + 3 = 9

Middle step: 2 high and 3 deep: 2 + 2 + 2 = 6

Short step: 1 high and 3 deep: 3

9 + 6 + 3 = 18 cubes ✓

Q2: count by size

1 by 1: 4 in a row, 3 rows: 12

2 by 2: the window fits 3 across, 2 down: 6

3 by 3: it fits 2 across, 1 down: 2

4 by 4: too big. 12 + 6 + 2 = 20 ✓
