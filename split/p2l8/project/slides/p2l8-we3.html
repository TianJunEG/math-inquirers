Count triangles: sort them by their bottom line

How many triangles are in this picture?

Step 1. Triangles that sit on the bottom line: 3

Step 2. Triangles that sit on the line across: 3

3 + 3 = 6 triangles

No other line can be the bottom of a triangle here, so we have them all.

Math Inquirers Level 2 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click through. Think-aloud: "Every triangle has a bottom side. In this picture a bottom side must lie on the bottom line or on the line across. On the bottom line: the left part, the right part, and the whole big triangle: 3. On the line across: the small left one, the small right one and the small triangle at the top made of both: 3. 3 + 3 = 6." Ask: "Is the shape under the line across a triangle?" No: it has 4 sides.
