Minutes 5 to 8. One question from earlier lessons each. Whiteboards up together, then click, and ask "Which lesson is this from?" 1) 9:24: the first common multiple of 6 and 8 is 24 (Factor Hunters). 2) 46 000: the hundreds digit is 6, so round up (Big Number Puzzles). 3) 24 ÷ 4 = 6, 6 × 3 = 18 (Fraction Frontier). 4) 6 tenths × 4 = 24 tenths = 2.4 (Decimal Detectives). 5) N to E is 90°, to S is 180°, to SW is 45° more: 225° (Angle Turns).
