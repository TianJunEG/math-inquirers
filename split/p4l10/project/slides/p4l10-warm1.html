Warm-up: who am I?

Clue 1: I am a 2-digit number.

Clue 2: I am in the 7 times table.

Clue 3: My digits add up to 10.

Clue 4: I am odd.

I am 91. (28 is even.)

Every clue crosses numbers out. Which clue did the most work?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Who am I? Children have a 1 to 100 grid (or a whiteboard list). Click one clue at a time; after each, children cross out every number that no longer fits. Clicks: clue 1, clue 2, the 7 times table shades (14, 21, 28 … 98), clue 3, 28 and 91 are circled, clue 4, the answer, the question. Clue 3 check: 1 + 4, 2 + 1, 2 + 8 = 10 ✓, 3 + 5, 4 + 2, 4 + 9, 5 + 6, 6 + 3, 7 + 0, 7 + 7, 8 + 4, 9 + 1 = 10 ✓, 9 + 8. So 28 and 91; clue 4 leaves 91. Second riddle if time: I am a 2-digit number in the 9 times table. My tens digit is 3 more than my ones digit. Who am I? (63: the 9 times table gives 18, 27, 36, 45, 54, 63, 72, 81, 90, 99; only 63 has tens 3 more than ones.) Ask: "Which clue did the most work?" (clue 2: it left only 13 numbers).
