Suppose they are all the same

15 bicycles and tricycles have 38 wheels altogether. How many tricycles?

Step 1. Suppose ALL 15 are bicycles: 15 × 2 = 30 wheels.

Step 2. We need 38. 38 − 30 = 8 wheels short.

Step 3. Swap a bicycle for a tricycle: 1 more wheel. 8 swaps.

8 tricycles, 7 bicycles. Check: 14 + 24 = 38 wheels, 15 cycles ✓

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click the steps, then 8 cycles get a third wheel, then the answer. Think-aloud: "Two facts: 15 cycles and 38 wheels. I keep the 15 and pretend they are all bicycles. That gives 30 wheels: 8 too few. Every time I change a bicycle into a tricycle I add exactly 1 wheel, so I change 8 of them." Check BOTH facts: 7 + 8 = 15 cycles, 7 × 2 + 8 × 3 = 38 wheels. Point out: for chickens and goats, each swap adds 2 legs, so you divide the shortfall by 2. Worksheet Q2 is a heads-and-legs puzzle.
