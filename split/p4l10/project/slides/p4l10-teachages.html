Ages: everyone gets older together

Mei and her aunt are 40 years old altogether. In 4 years, her aunt will be twice as old as Mei. How old is Mei now?

Step 1. In 4 years, BOTH are 4 years older: 40 + 4 + 4 = 48.

Step 2. Then: 3 equal bars = 48. 1 bar = 16.

Step 3. Mei will be 16, so now she is 16 − 4 = 12.

Mei is 12, her aunt is 28. Check: 12 + 28 = 40; 16 and 32 ✓

Math Inquirers Level 4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6). Ask first: "In 4 years, how much older is the TOTAL?" Many say 4: but two people each get 4 years older, so the total grows by 8. Click step 1, the bars, steps 2 and 3, the answer. Think-aloud: "The twice-as-old fact is about the future, so I draw the bars in 4 years: Mei one bar, her aunt two bars, 48 altogether. 48 ÷ 3 = 16. That is Mei in 4 years, so now she is 12." Common mistake: forgetting to go back to NOW at the end.
