TODAY WE WILL

Choose a way into tricky puzzles: guess and check, suppose, work backwards, and logic. Then check every fact.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Choose a way into a non-routine problem (guess and check with a table, suppose they are all the same, work backwards, a logic grid, draw it) and use it on competition-style puzzles, checking every answer against every fact. Thinking strategies today: GUESS AND CHECK WITH A TABLE, SUPPOSE (heads and legs), WORK BACKWARDS, LOGIC GRID, DRAW IT. Materials: Four puzzle cards (A to D) for the explore stations, mini whiteboards, counters or cubes, certificates, stamps. Online: pairs go to breakout rooms with one puzzle card each and report back; the tutor ticks and crosses the logic grid on the slide as children call out. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before each page and a check-together slide after it, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 88, certificates 88 to 90 (last slide).
