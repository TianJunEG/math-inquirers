Challenge Day

Mixed heuristics

TODAY WE WILL

Choose a way into tricky puzzles: guess and check, suppose, work backwards, and logic. Then check every fact.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Choose a way into a non-routine problem (guess and check with a table, suppose they are all the same, work backwards, a logic grid, draw it) and use it on competition-style puzzles, checking every answer against every fact. Thinking strategies today: GUESS AND CHECK WITH A TABLE, SUPPOSE (heads and legs), WORK BACKWARDS, LOGIC GRID, DRAW IT. Materials: Four puzzle cards (A to D) for the explore stations, mini whiteboards, counters or cubes, certificates, stamps. Online: pairs go to breakout rooms with one puzzle card each and report back; the tutor ticks and crosses the logic grid on the slide as children call out. Time plan: warm-up game 0 to 5, quick questions 5 to 8, explore 8 to 18, teaching 18 to 30 (click to reveal each step and answer; the "your turn" slides are optional, skip them if short of time), brain break 30 to 34, worksheet page 1 from 34 to 50 and check together 50 to 53, short teaching before page 2 from 53 to 58, worksheet page 2 from 58 to 76 and check together 76 to 80, talk time 80 to 85, exit ticket 85 to 90, certificates 88 to 90 (last slide). The Challenge slides and the Think Harder pages are for early finishers and for take-home: they have no fixed time slot. Online: page 1 is pair work, so allow about 2 minutes to move into and out of breakout rooms (or run page 1 as a whole-group screen-share if there are only 8 children).
