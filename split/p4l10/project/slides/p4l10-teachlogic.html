Logic grid: tick and cross

Ben, Siti and Ravi each play a different sport. Clue 1: Siti does not play football or badminton. Clue 2: Ben does not play football.

Step 1. Clue 1: cross Siti’s football and badminton. Only swimming is left: tick it. Nobody else swims: cross.

Step 2. Clue 2: cross Ben’s football. Ben’s only box left is badminton.

Step 3. Ravi: football is the only box left. Every row and column has one tick ✓

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q4 is a 4 by 4 grid). Click: step 1, then its ticks and crosses; step 2, then its marks; step 3, then the last tick. Think-aloud: "Each person has exactly one sport, and each sport has exactly one person. So when I tick a box, I can cross out the rest of that row AND that column." Answer: Ben badminton, Siti swimming, Ravi football. On the worksheet, children write ✓ for yes and X for no.
