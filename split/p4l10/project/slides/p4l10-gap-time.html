Think harder: Timetables, trying both orders

Tool: List every way

Kumar leaves home at 2:40 pm. He must go to the post office and the library, in either order, and then walk home. He stays 10 minutes at each place.

The post office closes at 3:30 pm. The library opens at 3:10 pm. Walking times: home to post office 20 minutes, home to library 30 minutes, post office to library 15 minutes (each way).

(a) Which place should he visit first?

(b) At what time does he get home?

(a) The post office first.

(b) 4:05 pm.

LISTEN FOR: Library first: arrives 3:10, leaves 3:20, reaches the post office at 3:35, too late. Post office first: 3:00, leaves 3:10, library at 3:25, leaves 3:35, home at 4:05. Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) The post office first. (b) 4:05 pm. WHY: Post office first: arrive 3:00, leave 3:10, walk 15 minutes to the library at 3:25 (open), leave 3:35, walk 30 minutes home: 4:05 pm. Library first fails because he arrives at the post office at 3:35. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): Change the post office closing time to 3:40 pm. Which orders work now, and which gets Kumar home sooner? Answer: Both orders work. Post office first gets home at 4:05 pm; library first: library 3:10 to 3:20, post office 3:35 to 3:45, home 4:05 pm.
