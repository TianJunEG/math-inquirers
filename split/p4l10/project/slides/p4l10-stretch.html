Think harder

1. There are 20 bicycles and tricycles with 47 wheels altogether. How many tricycles are there?

2. Two next-door even numbers (like 6 and 8) multiply to make 624. What are they?

3. Ravi spent $15 on a book. Then he spent half of the money left on a bag. He had $18 left. How much money did he have at first?

4. Ali, Ben, Mei and Siti are 8, 9, 10 and 11 years old, in some order. Ali is 2 years older than Siti. Ben is older than Mei. Siti is older than Mei. How old is each child?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers (same four questions as the printed Think harder page): 1) 7 tricycles (and 13 bicycles). All bicycles: 40 wheels, 7 short, each swap adds 1. 2) 24 and 26 3) $51 (18 × 2 = 36, 36 + 15 = 51) 4) Ali 11, Ben 10, Siti 9, Mei 8. Ali is 2 more than Siti, so (Siti, Ali) is (8, 10) or (9, 11). Mei is younger than both Siti and Ben. If Siti were 8, Mei would have to be younger than 8. So Siti is 9 and Ali is 11, and Mei is 8. Ben is the one left, 10.
