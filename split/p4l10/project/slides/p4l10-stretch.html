1. A farm has ducks and goats: 16 heads and 44 legs. How many goats?

2. I think of a number, multiply it by 4 and add 6. I get 50. What is my number?

3. Two next-door numbers multiply to make 380. What are they?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) 6 goats (and 10 ducks): all ducks 32 legs, 12 short, each goat adds 2. Check 20 + 24 = 44. 2) 11 (50 − 6 = 44, 44 ÷ 4 = 11). 3) 19 and 20 (20 × 20 = 400 is a bit too big; 19 × 20 = 380 ✓).
