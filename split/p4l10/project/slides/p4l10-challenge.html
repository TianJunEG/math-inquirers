Challenge

1. A hawker centre has 15 tables. Some seat 4 people and some seat 6. There are 76 seats altogether. How many tables seat 6?

2. Mei gave Ali half of her stickers and 2 more. Then she gave Ben half of the stickers left and 2 more. She had 5 left. How many did she have at first?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. These are Think Harder Q5 and Q6. Answers: 1) 8 tables seat 6 (and 7 seat 4). 2) 32 stickers.
