Challenge: walk-through

Q1: suppose all seat 4

All 15 seat 4: 15 × 4 = 60 seats

76 − 60 = 16 seats short

A 6-seat table adds 2: 16 ÷ 2 = 8

8 seat 6, 7 seat 4. Check: 48 + 28 = 76 ✓

Q2: work backwards

Left after Ben: 5. Add the 2 back: 7 = half

Before Ben: 7 × 2 = 14

Before Ali: (14 + 2) × 2 = 32

Check: 32 → gives 18 → 14 → gives 9 → 5 ✓

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use after pairs have tried. Q1 is a heads-and-legs puzzle in disguise: tables are the heads, seats are the legs. Q2: "half and 2 more" is undone in two steps, last first: add the 2 back (that is the half she kept), then double. Many children add 2 and then halve, or take 2 away: check forwards to catch it. 32: Ali gets 16 + 2 = 18, 14 left. Ben gets 7 + 2 = 9, 5 left ✓.
