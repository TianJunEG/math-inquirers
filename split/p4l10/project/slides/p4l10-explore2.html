Minutes 15 to 25. Gather pairs and collect ways of starting: write them on the board in children's words ("I guessed and changed it", "I pretended they were all beetles", "I started at 43"). Answers: 1) Suppose all 10 are beetles: 60 legs, 8 short; each spider instead of a beetle adds 2 legs, so 4 spiders. Or guess and check with a table. 2) Open. 3) No: A only 14 × 15 = 210 (13 × 14 = 182, 15 × 16 = 240); C undoing gives one number; D each clue leaves one choice. The next slides name the four ways in.
