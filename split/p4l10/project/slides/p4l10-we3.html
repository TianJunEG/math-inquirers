Work backwards: start at the end

Siti spent half her money on a book. Then she spent $4 on lunch. She had $11 left. How much did she have at first?

Step 1. Start at the end and undo the last step: − $4 becomes + $4. $11 + $4 = $15.

Step 2. Undo "half" by doubling: $15 × 2 = $30.

At first: $30

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Think-aloud: "I know the END, $11, not the start. So I walk backwards through the machine, undoing each step, LAST step first. She took away $4, so I add it back: $15. She halved her money, so I double: $30." Check forwards: half of $30 is $15, $15 − $4 = $11 ✓. Common mistakes: undoing in the wrong order (11 × 2 = 22, 22 + 4 = 26: check forwards and it fails), or taking away 4 again. Worksheet Q3 has three steps to undo.
