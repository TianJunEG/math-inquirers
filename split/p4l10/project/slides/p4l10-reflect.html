What was hard?

One minute. Finish these three lines on the last page of today's lesson. There are no wrong answers and nobody marks it.

1. The hardest part was …

2. I got unstuck by …

3. Next time I will try …

Ideas for line 2: drawing it, a list or table, a smaller number, asking my partner, reading it again.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 88 to 89 (1 minute, then certificates). REFLECTION. Children fill in the three lines in the What was hard? box at the bottom of the last workbook page for this lesson (hardest part; how I got unstuck; next time). Model one yourself first: "The hardest part for me was choosing which way in to use. I got unstuck by trying a table first." Then one child shares one line (not always the fastest child). Never grade it, never correct it, never collect it for marks. Praise the strategy ("you drew it", "you tried a smaller number"), not the speed or the score. Use it to see which strategies each child reaches for.
