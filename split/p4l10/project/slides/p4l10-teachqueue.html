Draw it: queues

Ali is 5th from the front and 8th from the back of a queue. How many people are in the queue?

Step 1. 5th from the front: 4 people in front of Ali. 8th from the back: 7 behind him.

Step 2. 4 + 1 + 7 = 12 people. Not 5 + 8 = 13: that counts Ali twice!

12 people
