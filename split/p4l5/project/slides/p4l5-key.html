Key idea: line up the points

Line up the decimal points and compare place by place: ones, tenths, hundredths, thousandths. More digits does not mean bigger.

To round, find the two neighbours and the halfway mark. To round backwards, find the smallest and largest numbers that round to it.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Say the key idea together. Common mistakes to watch for during the worksheet: (1) Comparing decimals by the number of digits (3.405 > 3.45 because 405 > 45) instead of lining up the points. (2) Reading a number line by counting marks without first working out what one step is worth (0.1, 0.01 or 0.001). (3) Rounding backwards: giving 7.45 as the largest number that rounds to 7.4 (it rounds to 7.5), or forgetting 7.35 to 7.39, which are below 7.4.
