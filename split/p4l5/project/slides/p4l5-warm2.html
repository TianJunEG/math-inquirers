Minutes 8.5 to 11.5. Three questions, about 30 seconds each: read each aloud, whiteboards up together, then click to show. No follow-up questions. 1) 7 hundredths. 2) Watch for 0.64: the 4 must be in the thousandths place, so the hundredths place needs a 0. 3) Many children say 0.75 because 75 > 8. Write 0.80 under 0.75: 8 tenths beats 7 tenths. Finish with the line: "0.8 is the same as 0.80. 0.80 is bigger than 0.75." It comes back in the teaching. The other two warm-up questions (round 4.68 to 1 decimal place; how much more than $3.05 is $5?) are in the Weekly Dose of Thinking Math.
