Read each aloud, whiteboards up together, then click to show. 1) 7 hundredths. 2) Watch for 0.64: the 4 must be in the thousandths place, so the hundredths place needs a 0. 3) Many children say 0.75 because 75 > 8. Write 0.80 under 0.75: 8 tenths beats 7 tenths. Do not settle it fully: it comes back in the teaching. 4) 4.68 is between 4.6 and 4.7, nearer 4.7. 5) Count on: $3.05 → $4 is 95¢, $4 → $5 is $1, so $1.95.
