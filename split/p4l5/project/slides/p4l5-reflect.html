Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 88 to 90 (2 minutes). LOOKING BACK. Children fill in the box on the Investigation page, the page every child has (something I did well; the hardest part was; what helped me). Say: "You can write, tick or pass. Ticking I am still stuck is a good thing to do: it tells me where to help." Model one yourself first: "Something I did well: I wrote the extra zeros. The hardest part was comparing decimals with different numbers of digits. What helped me: adding zeros so they had the same length." Do not ask anyone to read theirs out. If a child wants to share, take one volunteer and thank them for the strategy. Never grade it, never correct it, never collect it for marks and never show it to the group. You may look at a child's page only when the child holds it to your camera. Never ask for photos of work. If I am still stuck is ticked, check in during the open session: a quick visit to the shared breakout room or a hand signal, with a parent nearby. Praise strategies ("you drew it", "you asked for help"), not speed or scores.
