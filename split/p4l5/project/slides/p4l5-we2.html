Rounding: find the neighbours

Round 6.47 to 1 decimal place. Then to the nearest whole number.

Step 1. 1 decimal place: the neighbours are 6.4 and 6.5. Halfway is 6.45.

Step 2. 6.47 is past halfway, so it rounds UP to 6.5.

Step 3. Nearest whole number: neighbours 6 and 7, halfway 6.5. 6.47 is below it: 6.

Math Inquirers Level 4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Click through the steps. Think-aloud: "Rounding to 1 decimal place means: which tenth is it nearest to? 6.47 is between 6.4 and 6.5. The halfway mark is 6.45. 6.47 is past it, so 6.5." Then the whole number: "Which whole numbers is it between? 6 and 7. Halfway is 6.5, and 6.47 has not reached it, so it rounds to 6." Rule we use: exactly halfway rounds UP (6.45 rounds to 6.5). Ask: "Why does 6.47 round up one time and down the other?" (we are looking for different neighbours).
