LESSON 5 • WEEK 3 • 0–1.5 MIN

Decimal Detectives

Decimals

TODAY WE WILL

Zoom in on number lines, order decimals to 3 places, round and work backwards from a rounded number, and solve money and mass puzzles.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read decimals to 3 places on number lines, compare and order them by place value, round to the nearest whole number, 1 or 2 decimal places and work backwards from a rounded number (smallest and largest), and solve money and mass puzzles with bar models. Thinking strategies today: ZOOM IN (what is one step worth?), LINE UP THE POINTS, MAKE A LIST IN ORDER, DRAW A BAR MODEL. Materials: Digit cards 0 to 9 and a decimal point card for each child, a place-value chart (ones, tenths, hundredths, thousandths), mini whiteboards, a metre ruler, play money. Online: children cut out paper digit cards and a paper decimal point before the lesson and move them on camera; the tutor marks the zoom-in number lines on the slides. Time plan (90 minutes in all; every part has a budget and there is a 2.5-minute standing buffer inside the page-2 block). Opening 0 to 1.5. Sneak-peek puzzles 1.5 to 6.5 (puzzle 1 about 60 seconds, puzzle 2 about 90 seconds, then ONE hold-up moment for both papers; answers shown to the tutor only, on camera, nobody marked). Warm-up game 6.5 to 8.5 (one short round, about 90 seconds), quick questions 8.5 to 11.5 (three questions). Explore 11.5 to 25 (the only breakout of the first half: about 60 seconds each way, closed by a 60-second timer; a Go further what-if is on the slide from the start; two debrief questions). Teaching 25 to 33 (click to reveal each step and answer; the one "your turn" slide is optional: skip it if you are behind). Brain break 33 to 36.5 (water and toilet first, then the game; clock check at the end of the break: if you are more than 1.5 minutes behind, skip the next optional item, never a check, the exit ticket or Looking back). Worksheet 36.5 to 79.5: page 1 (Start here, in pairs) 36.5 to 55, check together 55 to 57, short teaching before page 2 57 to 62, page 2 on your own 62 to 77.5 (the last 2.5 minutes are the standing buffer: if earlier parts ran long, spend the buffer first, then the choice questions, and never the checks, the exit ticket or Looking back), check together 77.5 to 79.5. Talk time 79.5 to 83 (one minute of pair talk, no breakout). Exit ticket 83 to 86.5. Looking back 86.5 to 90 (2 minutes of writing). After question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder. Think harder, Go further and the Investigation have no fixed time slot: any child may choose them at any time, or take them home. The tutor never announces who is on which page, and pairs are not split unless both children agree. Online: page 1 is pair work, so allow about 2 minutes to move into and out of breakout rooms (or run page 1 as a whole-group screen-share if there are only 8 children).
