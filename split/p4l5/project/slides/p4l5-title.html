LESSON 5 • WEEK 3

Decimal Detectives

Decimals

TODAY WE WILL

Zoom in on number lines, order decimals to 3 places, round and work backwards from a rounded number, and solve money and mass puzzles.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Objective: Read decimals to 3 places on number lines, compare and order them by place value, round to the nearest whole number, 1 or 2 decimal places and work backwards from a rounded number (smallest and largest), and solve money and mass puzzles with bar models. Thinking strategies today: ZOOM IN (what is one step worth?), LINE UP THE POINTS, MAKE A LIST IN ORDER, DRAW A BAR MODEL. Materials: Digit cards 0 to 9 and a decimal point card for each child, a place-value chart (ones, tenths, hundredths, thousandths), mini whiteboards, a metre ruler, play money. Online: children cut out paper digit cards and a paper decimal point before the lesson and move them on camera; the tutor marks the zoom-in number lines on the slides. Time plan: warm-up 0 to 10, explore 10 to 25, teaching 25 to 35 (click to reveal each step and answer), brain break 35 to 40, worksheet 40 to 70 with short teaching before page 2 and a check-together slide after each page, challenge for early finishers from 65, talk time 70 to 80, exit ticket 80 to 90.
