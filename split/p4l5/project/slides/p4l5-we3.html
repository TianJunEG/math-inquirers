Rounding backwards: the detective question

A number has 2 decimal places. Rounded to 1 decimal place, it is 3.8. What is the smallest it can be? The largest?

Step 1. Halfway marks: 3.75 and 3.85. The shaded numbers all round to 3.8.

Step 2. 3.75 rounds UP to 3.8: it is the smallest. 3.85 rounds up to 3.9: not this one!

Smallest 3.75, largest 3.84

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

This is the detective question: work backwards from the rounded number. Click to shade the band, then the steps. Ask before step 2: "Is 3.85 in or out?" Many children say in. Test it: 3.85 is exactly halfway, so it rounds UP to 3.9. So the largest with 2 decimal places is 3.84. Ask: "How many numbers with 2 decimal places round to 3.8?" (3.75 to 3.84: 10 numbers).
