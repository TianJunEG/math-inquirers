Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Target 5. Children draw □.□□ on a whiteboard. Shuffle digit cards 0 to 9 and turn over one at a time (online: hold it up to the camera). After EACH card, children write it in one empty box at once and cannot move it. After three cards, everyone reads their number aloud; the number closest to 5 wins (work out how far each is from 5). Play 3 rounds: target 5, then target 2.5, then the smallest number possible. Click to show the thinking question. Talk: "A 4 or a 5 belongs in the ones box: 4.9 something or 5.0 something is close to 5. A 9 in the ones box is a disaster. A 9 in the hundredths box hardly matters: it is only 9 hundredths." Listen for children who say "the ones digit matters most, then the tenths". That is the key idea of today.
