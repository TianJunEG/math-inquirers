Challenge

1. A number with 3 decimal places rounds to 2.46 (to 2 decimal places). Its digits add up to 20. What is the number?

2. 3 apples and 1 pear weigh 0.95 kg. 1 apple and 1 pear weigh 0.45 kg. All the apples weigh the same. What is the mass of the pear?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then use the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) 2.459 (it is from 2.455 to 2.464; 2 + 4 + 5 + 9 = 20; 2.46_ would need an 8, but 2.468 rounds to 2.47). 2) 0.2 kg (the extra 2 apples weigh 0.95 − 0.45 = 0.5 kg, so 1 apple is 0.25 kg and the pear is 0.45 − 0.25 = 0.2 kg).
