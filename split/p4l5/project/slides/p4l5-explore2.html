2. Which number is the smallest? The largest?

3. Which number is closest to 5? How do you know?

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 15 to 25. Let pairs keep working, then gather them. Write their numbers on the board in the order they say them, messy on purpose, then ask "Is anything missing? Is anything there twice?" Answers: 1) 12 numbers. The three digits can be arranged 6 ways (258, 285, 528, 582, 825, 852) and the point can go in 2 places: 2.58, 2.85, 5.28, 5.82, 8.25, 8.52, 25.8, 28.5, 52.8, 58.2, 82.5, 85.2. 2) Smallest 2.58, largest 85.2. 3) 5.28: it is only 0.28 away from 5. 5.82 is 0.82 away and 2.85 is 2.15 away. Ask: "Why is a number with 2 decimal places always smaller than one with 1 decimal place here?" (it has only one digit in front of the point).
