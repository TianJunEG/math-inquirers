Minutes 21.5 to 25. Stop the hands-on after about 6 minutes (or when the 60-second timer closes the rooms) and finish ordering here, in the debrief. Two debrief questions only. Write their numbers on the board in the order they say them, messy on purpose, then ask "Is anything missing? Is anything there twice?" Answers: 1) 12 numbers. The three digits can be arranged 6 ways (258, 285, 528, 582, 825, 852) and the point can go in 2 places: 2.58, 2.85, 5.28, 5.82, 8.25, 8.52, 25.8, 28.5, 52.8, 58.2, 82.5, 85.2. 2) Smallest 2.58, largest 85.2. The third question (closest to 5: 5.28, only 0.28 away) is not asked; it comes back in the teaching.
