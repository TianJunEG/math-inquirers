Bar model: take away the extra, then share

Ravi and Ben have $6.80 altogether. Ravi has $0.60 more than Ben. How much does each boy have?

Step 1. Take away the extra: $6.80 − $0.60 = $6.20

Step 2. Two equal bars: $6.20 ÷ 2 = $3.10 (Ben)

Step 3. Ravi: $3.10 + $0.60 = $3.70

Check: $3.10 + $3.70 = $6.80, and $3.70 is $0.60 more than $3.10 ✓

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q5). Draw the two bars first: "Who has more? Whose bar is longer? By how much?" Think-aloud: "If I cut off Ravi's extra 60 cents, the two bars are the same. Together they are $6.80 minus $0.60, which is $6.20. Shared equally: $6.20 divided by 2. 6 divided by 2 is 3, 20 cents divided by 2 is 10 cents: $3.10." Show the division as 6.20 ÷ 2 = 3.10 too. Always check BOTH clues at the end.
