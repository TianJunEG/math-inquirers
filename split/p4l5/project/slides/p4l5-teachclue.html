Mystery decimal: list the cases, check every clue

I have 2 decimal places. Rounded to 1 decimal place, I am 3.2. My hundredths digit is 6 more than my tenths digit.

Step 1. Rounds to 3.2, so I am from 3.15 to 3.24. My tenths digit is 1 or 2.

Step 2. Tenths 1: hundredths 1 + 6 = 7. 3.17 is from 3.15 to 3.24 ✓

Step 3. Tenths 2: hundredths 2 + 6 = 8. 3.28 rounds to 3.3: not this one!

I am 3.17. Only one number fits every clue.

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Optional stretch teaching (the mystery decimal with 3 places is Think harder Q4). Strategy: use the rounding clue to make a short list, then test every case against every clue. Ask after step 1: "Why only tenths 1 or 2?" (3.15 to 3.19 have tenths 1; 3.20 to 3.24 have tenths 2). Stress step 3: the case that breaks a clue must be crossed out out loud, so children see why the answer is the only one.
