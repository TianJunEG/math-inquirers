Story break: Simon Stevin

Flanders (born in Bruges, now Belgium) • 1548-1620

Simon Stevin was born in Bruges, in what is now Belgium, in 1548. He worked on many things, from the science of water to the science of weights. In 1585 he published a small book called The Tenth about decimal fractions. Stevin did not invent decimals, because others had used them before him. What he did was explain them clearly for the people who needed them. He said they would help surveyors, carpet-makers, wine-gaugers, mint-masters, stargazers and every kind of merchant. He showed how to split a whole into tenths, hundredths and thousandths. We still use decimals this way for money and measuring.

TRY IT (OPTIONAL)

Write 3 + 4/10 + 5/100 as a decimal. Then add 2.8 to your answer.
