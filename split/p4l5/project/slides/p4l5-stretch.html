1. Siti buys 4 notebooks that cost the same. She pays with a $10 note and gets $2.60 change. How much is one notebook?

2. Find ALL the digits that the letter A can be: 3.A5 < 3.42

3. Mei is 1.38 m tall. Ben is 0.15 m taller than Mei. Ali is 0.09 m shorter than Ben. How tall is Ali?

Math Inquirers Level 4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Answers: 1) $1.85 ($10 − $2.60 = $7.40, $7.40 ÷ 4 = $1.85). 2) 0, 1, 2 and 3 (3.35 < 3.42 but 3.45 > 3.42). 3) 1.44 m (Ben 1.38 + 0.15 = 1.53 m, Ali 1.53 − 0.09 = 1.44 m).
