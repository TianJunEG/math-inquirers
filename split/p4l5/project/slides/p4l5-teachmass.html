Mass puzzles: take away the container

The 5 cups all have the same mass. What is the mass of one cup?

Step 1. The cups alone: 2.3 kg − 0.55 kg = 1.75 kg (write 2.30 − 0.55)

Step 2. One cup: 1.75 kg ÷ 5 = 0.35 kg

Check: 5 × 0.35 = 1.75, and 1.75 + 0.55 = 2.3 ✓

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach before worksheet page 2 (Q6). Ask: "Why can't we just divide 2.3 by 5?" (the tray is on the scale too). Think-aloud: "First I take the tray away: 2.30 minus 0.55. I line up the points and write 2.3 as 2.30. That is 1.75 kg for 5 cups. Then 1.75 divided by 5: 175 hundredths divided by 5 is 35 hundredths, 0.35 kg."
