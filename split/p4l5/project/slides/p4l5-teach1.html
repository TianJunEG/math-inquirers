Zoom in: what is one step worth?

Step 1. Top line: 2 to 3 in 10 steps, so one step is 0.1.

Step 2. Zoom in: 2.3 to 2.4 in 10 steps, so one step is 0.01.

6 steps past 2.3: 2.36

Math Inquirers P4 Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Start with the top line: "How many steps from 2 to 3? Ten. So each step is one tenth, 0.1." Point along: 2.1, 2.2, 2.3. Then the zoom: "The gap from 2.3 to 2.4 is cut into ten tiny steps. Each one is a tenth of a tenth: one hundredth, 0.01." Click step 1, step 2, then the answer. Think-aloud: "The arrow is 6 tiny steps past 2.3. So it is 2.3 and 6 hundredths: 2.36." Ask: "Can we zoom in again, between 2.36 and 2.37?" (yes: 10 steps of 0.001; halfway is 2.365). Use a metre ruler too: 1 m = 100 cm, so 2.36 m is 2 m 36 cm.
