Children mark in a different colour; ask ONE volunteer to explain only Q3 (run the machine backwards: undo the last step first), about 35 seconds a question. Q1a: OUT is always 3 more, so n + 3 (a one-step rule). Q1d: IN goes up 1, OUT goes up 4, so × 4; 4 × 1 = 4, OUT 1, so − 3; IN 20: 80 − 3 = 77. Q2c: (n + 3) × 4 is 4n + 12, so P is always 9 more than Q (32 − 23 = 9 for IN 5): they can never match. Q3: a) 43 + 5 = 48, ÷ 6 = 8; b) 74 − 4 = 70, ÷ 7 = 10; c) 45 ÷ 5 = 9, + 3 = 12; d) 15 − 9 = 6, × 4 = 24.
