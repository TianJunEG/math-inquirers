Explore: build a secret machine

EXPLORE AT THE COMPUTER

  1. Build the machine on the right. (* means ×. Find "ask" in Sensing and the green blocks in Operators.)
  2. Change the rule to your OWN secret two-step rule.
  3. Share your screen. Your partner calls IN numbers; you type them in.
  4. Your partner writes your rule with n. Fewest IN numbers wins. Then swap.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 10 to 15. Screen-share Scratch for 2 minutes and build the machine together: "ask" is in Sensing (light blue), "answer" is the round block under it, the * and + blocks are in Operators (green); put the * block INSIDE the + block. Make a variable called out. Type 4: it says 14. Then pairs go to breakout rooms. Each child changes the rule to a secret two-step rule (one × and one + or −), shares their screen, and types in the IN numbers the partner calls out. The partner tries to write the rule with n using as few IN numbers as possible. Early finishers: put the ask and say inside a "forever" loop so the machine keeps going; or make a three-step machine. Optional Python version (same machine, for children who know Python): n = int(input("IN? ")) | out = n * 3 + 2 | print("OUT:", out) Typing 4 prints OUT: 14; typing 10 prints OUT: 32.
