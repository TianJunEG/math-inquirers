Minutes 86 to 88 (2 minutes), after the exit slip. Everyone has the box: it is on the Finish page, a page every child reaches. Say: "Two minutes, just for you. Finish: something I did well, the hardest part was, and tick what helped. You can write pass. If you are still stuck, tick that box and I will check in with you in the open session, in the shared room, with your parent nearby." Never collect it in front of the group and never read an answer out. You may look at a child's box on camera in the open session if the child offers it. Praise the strategy, not the speed: say "you you tried a number and checked, and that is what got you unstuck", never "you were quick". If a child ticks I am still stuck, check in during the open session the same day: visit the shared breakout room or use a hand signal, with a parent nearby. Never by private message. If a child writes "everything" or "I am bad at maths", say quietly in the open session: "Thank you for telling me. Let's find one small thing that worked today," and point to a specific item they got right. If a child writes that nothing was hard, ask what would make it harder.
