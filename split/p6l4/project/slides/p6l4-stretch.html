1. IN 3 gives OUT 11. IN 7 gives OUT 23. The rule is "× a number, then + a number". Find it.

2. set n to 2, then repeat 3 times: set n to (n × 2) + 1. Then say n. What does it say?

3. Solve (n + 6) × 4 = 60.

Optional, if you finish early (no clock time): show it only while some children are working, never as a group slide at a fixed minute. Answers: 1) OUT = 3n + 2 (IN up 4, OUT up 12, so × 3; 3 × 3 = 9, + 2 = 11; check 3 × 7 + 2 = 23). 2) 23 (2 → 5 → 11 → 23). 3) n = 9 (60 ÷ 4 = 15, 15 − 6 = 9; check 15 × 4 = 60).
