1. A machine does × 3, then − 4. Which IN number comes out as the SAME number?

2. Two machines are joined: the OUT of the first goes into the second. Each one does × 2, then + 1. IN 5 gives OUT 23. Which IN gives OUT 63?

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Pairs try first, then the "see it" slide if stuck, then the walk-through. Answers: 1) 2 (2 × 3 − 4 = 2; the equation is 3n − 4 = n). 2) 15 (backwards through the second machine: 63 − 1 = 62, ÷ 2 = 31; then through the first: 31 − 1 = 30, ÷ 2 = 15. Check: 15 → 31 → 63).
