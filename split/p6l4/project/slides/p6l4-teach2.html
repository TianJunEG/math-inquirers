Order matters: use brackets

IN 4: (4 + 2) × 5 = 30

IN 4: 4 × 5 + 2 = 22

Step 1. Same steps, different order, different OUT.

Step 2. A: OUT = (n + 2) × 5. The brackets say “do this first”.

Step 3. B: OUT = 5n + 2. Times comes before add, so no brackets are needed.

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Teach. Ask first: "Will both machines give the same OUT for IN 4?" Thumbs up or down, then click. Remind them of the order rule from primary school: brackets first, then × and ÷, then + and −. (n + 2) × 5 can also be written 5(n + 2). Do NOT say whether A and B can ever match: that is worksheet Q2(c).
