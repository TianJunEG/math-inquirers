Optional, if you finish early

Think harder: Percentage change, with units

Tool: Draw it

(a) Ravi spends 25% of his money and $60 is left. Find the amount he began with.

(b) Mei has 50 stickers. Ali has 20% more stickers than Mei. Ben has 20% fewer stickers than Mei. Find the difference between Ali's and Ben's stickers.

(a) $80.

(b) 20 more stickers.

ADDED ITEM pct (a slide-only addition for the skill gap "Percentage change, with units"; it is not in the printed workbook). TOOL: Draw it. SAY: Draw a bar for the money Ravi had at first. The part he spent is 25%, so 4 units make the whole and 1 unit is spent. ASK: What percentage is left, and how many units is that? How many dollars is one unit? For (b), what are Ali's and Ben's stickers as percentages of Mei's? LISTEN FOR: (a) 75% is left, 3 units = $60, 1 unit = $20, whole = 4 x $20 = $80. (b) Ali has 120% of 50 = 60 and Ben has 80% of 50 = 40, so 20 more; the gap is 40% of 50. Listen for "percent of what?": the 100% is Mei's stickers. Everyone writes the answer on their own paper, then say the answer together on three, then click the answer box. Nobody holds anything up, nobody is marked, and passing is always fine. ANSWER: (a) $80. (b) 20 more stickers. WHY: (a) 100% - 25% = 75% = $60, so 25% = $20 and 100% = $80. (b) Ali: 50 + 10 = 60. Ben: 50 − 10 = 40. Difference 60 − 40 = 20. SAME SKILL, NEW NUMBERS (tutor bank, say it aloud or write it on the board): Siti uses up 40% of her savings and $90 remains. How much were her savings at the start? Answer: $150.
