Worked example: gaps in the table

IN n
2
5
10
OUT
3
12
27

Step 1. IN goes up by 3 (2 to 5). OUT goes up by 9 (3 to 12). 9 ÷ 3 = 3, so × 3.

Step 2. Test: 3 × 2 = 6. OUT is 3, so take away 3.

Step 3. Rule: OUT = 3n − 3. Check IN 10: 3 × 10 − 3 = 27 ✓
