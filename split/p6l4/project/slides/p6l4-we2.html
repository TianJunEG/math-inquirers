30.5–32 MIN

Run the machine backwards

A machine does × 5, then − 4. OUT is 31. What was IN? That is the equation 5n − 4 = 31.

Step 1. Undo the LAST step first: the opposite of − 4 is + 4. 31 + 4 = 35

Step 2. Then undo × 5 with ÷ 5. 35 ÷ 5 = 7. So n = 7.

Check: 7 × 5 − 4 = 31 ✓

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 30.5 to 32. Click through. Analogy: "You put on socks, then shoes. To undo, shoes come off first." Think-aloud: "The machine did times 5 and then took away 4. The last thing it did was take away 4, so I undo that first: 31 + 4 = 35. Then undo times 5: 35 ÷ 5 = 7. Check forwards: 7 × 5 = 35, 35 − 4 = 31." A second one if time: (n + 2) × 5 = 45: undo × 5 first (9), then − 2: n = 7.
