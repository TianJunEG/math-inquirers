Warm-up: guess my rule

I am a machine. Here is what came out. What is my rule?

Round A

3 → 8    7 → 12    20 → 25

add 5: n + 5

Round B

2 → 10    5 → 25    9 → 45

times 5: 5n

Round C

2 → 3    6 → 11    10 → 19

× 2, then − 1: 2n − 1

Round D

1 → 5    4 → 14    10 → 32

× 3, then + 2: 3n + 2

Math Inquirers P6 • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 0 to 5. Guess my rule. Play each round live first: children call out an IN number, you say the OUT (use the rule on the card), and they guess. Then click to show the rule. Ask: "Which IN numbers helped most?" Round C and D need two steps. Check the rule on EVERY pair: Round D, 1 × 3 + 2 = 5, 4 × 3 + 2 = 14, 10 × 3 + 2 = 32. Some children say "add 4" for 1 → 5: it fails at 4 → 14. Write each rule with the letter n: n + 5, 5n (5 × n, we leave out the × sign), 2n − 1, 3n + 2. Round D is the machine they will build in the explore.
