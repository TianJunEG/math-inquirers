3 MIN CHECK

Warm-up check

Two new puzzles. Whiteboard: your answer AND one reason. Hold up together.

Q1. A phone plan costs $10 a month plus $3 for every GB of data. Write the cost for n GB, then find the cost for 7 GB.

Cost = 3n + 10. For 7 GB: 3 × 7 + 10 = $31.

Q2. One month the same plan cost $52. How many GB were used?

14 GB. Undo the + 10 first: 52 − 10 = 42. Then undo the × 3: 42 ÷ 3 = 14. Check: 3 × 14 + 10 = 52.
