# Independent audit: Primary 1 early-finisher puzzle pilot (school/p1)

Audited 23 Oct 2026, branch `claude/great-fermi-1hv4vc`, committed PDFs in `school/p1/out/`. Audit only: no pack file was changed.

Method: every pupil page (20), front matter (4) and key (8) rendered with `pdftoppm` (70, 80, 110 and 150 dpi) and read as a Primary 1 pupil, as a teacher with 30 pupils, and as a Singapore P1 teacher. Pages 1, 6, 12, 14, 17 and 18 were also converted to 1-bit and checked (the builder's own `out/bilevel_check/` was used for P1-01). Every puzzle was solved from the printed page, not from the generator (by hand, and by brute-force scripts for the grids, magic square, mazes and colour pages). A pack was also built to a scratch folder with a 62-character school name to test the footer. Checked against `README.md`, `docs/school_books/plan.md`, `docs/terms/owner_decisions.md` addenda 5 to 10 and the 23 Oct price hold, `docs/moe/syllabus_by_level.md`, `docs/moe/verification_report.md`, `term/l1/WORDING_GUIDE.md` and `docs/books/p1_school_paper_patterns.md`.

## 3. Findings

Severity: High = pupil cannot tell right from wrong, content not suitable for P1, or an owner rule broken; Medium = real classroom or teacher problem, fix before pilot; Low = polish.

### High

**F1. P1-04 and P1-16: the check does not check the answer.** Location: check strip on both pages, teacher notes "Grid. One tick in each row and column", key entries P1-04 and P1-16 ("exposes most mistakes"). Evidence: I enumerated all 120 ways to place the five numbers; 120 of 120 give one tick per row and column, and only 1 is right. Swapping Ali and Mei (or cup and kite) passes the check. A pupil who ticks one box per row, in any order, is told "yes, that is true". This breaks owner addendum 8 (the pupil can tell whether they are right). On P1-16 the last answer ($38, from the grid) has no check at all. Fix: make the check content-based. Easiest: put a letter on each number chip (as P1-07 does with shirts) and have the pupil read the letters down the rows to spell a word; or change the check to "Put your numbers back in the clues. Is every clue true?" with a tick box per clue. Keep "one tick per row and column" only as a hint, not as the check.

**F2. P1-12 and P1-14: colour-by-answer slip detection is 72% and 76%, and judging by picture alone it cannot reach the 98% target; rework the check.** Evidence (my own enumeration, same result as the builder): P1-12, slips +-1 and +-10 over all 25 boxes, 72 of 100 change the picture; slips in the 16 shaded boxes are caught 64 of 64, slips in the 9 unshaded boxes only 8 of 36. P1-14, +-1 and +-2: 110 of 144; slips of +-4 (the realistic times-table slip, such as 4 x 3 = 8 or 4 x 3 = 16): only 36 of 72, because 8, 12 and 20 are all in the shaded set. A wrong answer in an unshaded box leaves the picture looking right, so the pupil is told "all correct" while holding a wrong number on the line. The decoys placed near the shaded values (README note 1) make the picture sensitive in the shaded boxes but not in the others. An "answer bank" strip does not fix it: I tested it and it catches only 48 to 61%, because the decoys are all in the bank. My judgement: rework, do not accept. Options in order of preference: (a) give every box one of three answers and a different mark for each (shade, dots, blank-with-cross or stripes), so any answer that matches none of the three is visibly wrong; the three target values in both pages are far enough apart (35, 46, 58; 8, 12, 20) that +-1, +-2, +-10 slips never land on another target; (b) convert both pages to code-word pages (the pack has 8 already, so (a) is better for variety); (c) accept the rate but say so in the teacher notes. Do not ship the pack claiming "every page checks itself" with the current devices.

**F3. P1-14 uses the division sign for Primary 1 pupils.** Location: 10 of 36 boxes (for example 14 ÷ 2, 16 ÷ 2, 20 ÷ 4). Evidence: `docs/moe/syllabus_by_level.md` lists "the sign ÷" under Primary 2 only; the P1 line says only "sign x"; `docs/books/p1_school_paper_patterns.md` section 6.1 says "Operator is 'x'; no division symbol appears" in the P1 papers read. The page title is "Times and Share" but there is no sharing picture or sentence, so a P1 pupil sees a symbol they have not been taught. A Singapore P1 teacher would not hand this page out. Also 36 computations is long for a 5 to 15 minute task (F22). Fix: replace the ÷ boxes by sharing words or pictures ("12 shared equally into 3 groups"), or remove division from the page and move division to a separate small page. Re-tag the page honestly ("Multiplication within 40" if division is dropped).

**F4. P1-20: the check is circular.** Location: clue "18 children chose in all" and check "Is the total 18?". Evidence: the pupil is meant to find the Pear row from the total (3 + 3 + 6 + 4 = 16, so Pear = 2). If the pupil does that, the total is 18 by construction. I tested single slips in the Orange row (+-1, +-2) with Pear derived from the total: 4 of 4 still pass the check. Only drawing mistakes (miscounting circles drawn) are caught. The key note ("if a pupil's total is wrong, one of the first two clues was misread") is therefore false for a pupil who uses the total clue. Fix: replace the total clue by a third difference clue that fixes Pear (for example "Pear has 1 fewer than Banana" gives Pear 2) and keep "18 children chose in all" as the check only (it then is independent). I verified that Apple 3, Banana 3 and the clues Orange = Apple + 3, Grape = Banana + 1, Pear = Banana - 1 give 3+3+6+4+2 = 18, so the data need no change.

**F5. P1-04 and P1-16 do not tell a 6 to 7 year old how to use a logic grid.** Location: instruction "Tick the grid." Evidence: no worked cell, no word about crosses or about which row/column a clue points to. The teacher note says pupils "mark a cross where a clue rules a number out", but that is only in the key, and the plan's own rule says a needed instruction must be printed on the page (3 lines max). P1 pupils have little or no experience of logic grids; a bare 5 x 5 grid with four clues needs at least one example line or a cross legend. Fix: add a one-line legend ("Put a cross where a clue says no. Put a tick where it must be yes.") and pre-fill one cell, or pre-fill one clue's cells. Also state the chain explicitly on P1-04 ("Write the numbers in order first").

### Medium

**F6. Tier labels are not honest on four pages.** P1-06 (2 stars) is a +5 chain in a 5 x 5 maze and easier than P1-08 (1 star), which needs 25 additions within 20; P1-19 (2 stars) is counting five kinds of shape and is the easiest page in the pack (no steps, no reasoning), easier than P1-17 (ruler) at 1 star; P1-07 (1 star) and P1-02 (2 stars) are about the same difficulty (P1-02 needs only ones digit and a range). Evidence: step counts per page; plan rule "stretch = same topic, more steps or clues". Fix: swap P1-06 to 1 star and P1-19 to 1 star; move one honest two-star in their place (for example a two-clue version of P1-19 with a total-by-shape clue, or the P1-02 version with 4 clues). Add a short "how a star is decided" line in the teacher notes. Then rebuild one or two pages so the split stays 10 and 10.

**F7. Teacher key states wrong or unverifiable facts.** (a) P1-16 note says "'Costs $5 more than' needs a subtraction"; the page says "$2 more than the book". (b) P1-12 note lists decoys "34, 36, 45, 47": 36 and 47 do not occur among the answers. (c) P1-14 note lists decoys "7, 9, 10, 14 and 16": 9, 10 and 14 do not occur (14 is a question, not an answer). (d) P1-08 note says decoy boxes make "10, 11, 13 or 14"; the page also has 8 (6+2) and 16 (8+8, 9+7). (e) P1-04 note says pupils mark crosses (not on the page, see F5). Fix: generate each note from the page data (see section 4); re-read the key line by line.

**F8. P1-01: no legend for the rods and the rods are very tight.** The page never says "a long rod is 1 ten, a dot is 1 one", yet the pupil must know that. The ten-square rods are drawn about 2 mm apart; at 100 dpi 1-bit the inner squares break up and the rods nearly merge, so counting 9 against 8 rods is hard (I viewed it at 150 dpi, and at 100 dpi 1-bit). The hint "Is it a toy to fly?" also fits KITE (4 letters; boxes are 6, so it is guessable only if the pupil counts boxes). Fix: add a legend row (one rod = 10, one dot = 1), put 4 to 5 mm between rods, draw each rod as a plain bar with 9 inner ticks, change the hint to "Is it a space toy?" or "Is it in space?".

**F9. P1-03: the "numbers" and "letters" labels are under the wrong place.** Two rows of boxes sit under the arrow, with "numbers" at the far left and "letters" at the far right, both below the lower row. A pupil cannot tell which row is which, or that the pairs should be placed one above the other. Fix: label the rows at the left ("number", "letter") and align each letter box under its number box (or write the letter beside each number box).

**F10. Strand balance is thin outside Number.** Strand counts (from the key): Whole Numbers 7, Add/Subtract 5, Multiplication/Division 2, Money 2, Length 1, Time 1, 2D shapes 1, Picture graph 1. Number and Algebra is 16 of 20 pages (80%); the plan's 40-page map halves to about 2 pages each for the ten units (counting 2, comparing 2, patterns 2, ordinals 2, add/subtract 3, mult/div 2, money 2, length and time 2, shapes 2, graphs 1). Add/subtract has 5 pages against 3, and shapes have 1 against 2; ordinals and shape work are under-served. P1-11 uses shape icons, but it is an arithmetic page. Fix: swap one add/subtract page (P1-12 after rework or P1-10) for a second geometry page (for example forming figures from shapes or a shape-picture count with a total clue). Keep P1-19 at 1 star (F6) and make the new shapes page the two-star.

**F11. Money wording: the key writes "80c", "5c" and so on.** Location: key P1-15 answers "1) 80c, 2) 60c ...", teacher note "5c, 10c, 20c, 50c". The owner's 23 Oct decision (and `p1_school_paper_patterns.md`) says "50¢", never "50c". The pupil page is correct (coins show numbers and the answer box has "¢"). Fix: change the key to "80¢" etc. and scan all key text for the pattern `\dc\b`.

**F12. P1-02: reading and layout.** The clue "My tens digit is more than 2. I am less than 38. My ones digit is 6." wraps so that "6." stands alone on a line (also on clue 4: "more than 4."). Large empty bands above and below the riddles. Fix: keep each clue on one line (break after the second sentence) or use two short lines; use the white space to give each riddle a bigger answer box.

**F13. P1-09: words and boxes.** "Every column makes 15." uses "column", a word P1 pupils may not read; use "across" and "down" ("Every line across makes 15. Every line down makes 15."). The eight small total boxes are about 11 x 9 mm, below the pack's own 12 mm minimum for answer boxes. The page works well otherwise; the unique-solution proof is solid.

**F14. P1-06: label and tier.** See F6 for the tier. The check "If you get stuck, go back" is fair, but the START tag sits on the top border of the 20 box, and "FINISH" on the top border of the 60 box, so some pupils will start in the box above. Fix: put START and FINISH in a small black label inside the box at the left, or draw an arrow into the box.

**F15. P1-13: the three answer boxes have no labels.** "Write: bags, dots in each bag, total." tells the order, but the boxes show only "x" and "=". Pupils who write dots first (5 x 2) get the right total, and the key says 2 x 5; teacher marking and the key disagree even though both are correct. Fix: print "bags", "dots", "total" above the first row of boxes, or state "Write: __ bags x __ dots = __ dots".

**F16. P1-17: print and syllabus notes.** The 0 and 12 on the ruler are crossed by the ruler's border line (at 150 dpi, 1-bit too), and the 1 mm ticks are very dense for a 6-year-old (at 100 dpi they merge). `p1_school_paper_patterns.md` section 6.2 found no cm or ruler marks in any P1 paper read (units only), although cm is in the syllabus; teachers may say pupils have not met cm rulers yet. Strips and ruler scale together on a copier, but the page says nothing about printing at 100% (the teacher note says copies at other scales stay right: true for the picture, false if a pupil lays a real ruler on it). Fix: move the 0 and 12 labels below the border, draw mm ticks at half height with a longer 5 mm tick, and add "Print at 100%" in the teacher notes.

**F17. P1-18: clock legibility and wording.** In clock 4 (7:35) the hour and minute hands are 17 degrees apart and overlap for most of their length; in clock 2 (7:50) the minute hand runs through the numeral 10. Both stay readable at 150 dpi but are hard at copier resolution for a 6-year-old. The question "What time is it 1 hour later?" is awkward; the school-paper frames are "The time is ___", "It is ___" and "lasts for ___". Also the hint "Is it a clock helper?" for TIMER is vague. Fix: thicker, differently shaped hour hand (short with a bar), keep the minute hand to the minute ring, avoid clock times where the hands overlap (change 7:35 to 7:30 or 3:35), and use "What is the time 1 hour later?" or "It is ___ 1 hour later". Note the page crosses the hour twice (7:50 to 8:20, 7:35 to 8:05) which is two-step for half-hour additions: acceptable as the two-star, but say so in the teacher note.

**F18. Key: no solved pictures, and routes as coordinates.** P1-06 and P1-08 routes are given as "(row, column from top-left)" lists; P1-12 and P1-14 show a small bitmap but still collide with the text (the picture on the P1-12 and P1-14 entries sits across the "Teacher note" line). A teacher spot-checking 30 pupils wants a thumbnail of each solved page (route drawn, grid ticked, picture shaded). Seven of the eight key pages are half empty (3 entries per page with large gaps, for example page 3 and page 8). Fix: generate a reduced solved copy of each page into the key (the generator already holds the solution), two or four per key page, and remove the page breaks that leave half pages empty.

**F19. Teacher notes are thin for a teacher using this with a class.** Missing: expected time (the plan says 5 to 15 minutes; P1-14 and P1-12 take longer, see F22), when in the P1 year each topic is usually taught (money, time, length, graphs and shapes are late-year in school papers, so a teacher in Term 1 should not hand out P1-15 to P1-20), what to do when a pupil's check fails (go back, ask a friend, show the teacher), recommended first pages, and a statement of how the stars were decided. Fix: add a half page (a term/ topic table; "when the check fails"; time per page).

### Low

**F20. Wording guide and register.** "Work out each sum/box" (P1-12, P1-14): the guide says "Find the value of", not "Work out". "Put the numbers in order" (P1-03): guide pattern is "Arrange the numbers in order." "Orange has [] more than Pear": guide frame is "more ___ than". Hints such as "Is it a shape for love?" (heart) and "Is it a toy to fly?" are oddly phrased; "Is it a toy that flies?" or no hint at all for picture pages. Code words and hints are otherwise suitable (ROCKET, CLOUD, BRIDGE, FLOWER, TIGER, CASTLE, PLANET, PIANO, PENCIL, SHAPE, SPRING, TIMER), all within P1 reading and spelling.

**F21. P1-07: half the page is empty.** The queue answer boxes use only the left half; the pupils' name boxes could be bigger or the queue drawn horizontally. The page also solves by elimination for Ravi (4th), which is fine for 1 star.

**F22. Workload.** P1-14 has 36 sums, P1-12 25, P1-08 25. For a 6 to 7 year old this is 15 to 25 minutes, above the 5 to 15 minute design. Fewer boxes (a 5 x 5 for P1-14 as for P1-12) would fit.

**F23. Front matter placeholders.** "(c) 2026 Preciouslyours Pte Ltd" (entity and year not approved); the licence text is a draft; "the licence is for the edition bought" implies a sale while the price is on hold (no price is printed: correct); "Ask the publisher for a quote" has no contact; "Edit the pages" is forbidden but the school name line is a handwriting blank. On the committed PDFs the footer licence line is a blank ("________ (school name)") on all 20 pupil pages and 8 key pages: a school that photocopies without building with `--school` must hand-write the name 20 times. The footer is 8 pt, small at copier quality. Fix: keep as open questions 2 and 9; build a per-school PDF as the delivered product and keep the blank one as the master.

**F24. Alignment wording in the key.** The key states "Mathematics Syllabus, Primary, 2021 edition; Primary 1, pages 31 and 32" and gives outcome numbers. Owner addendum 7 allows a modest claim only after verification and off the pages; this is in the teacher key, not on the pupil pages, so it is allowed, but the wording "syllabus outcomes ... the puzzle practises" is stronger than "matched to the topics". Also the sentence "Teachers may wish to tell the pupils these topics are covered in class work" after the list of uncovered topics is unclear and should go.

**F25. Mazes: the check can be passed by breaking the rule.** P1-06: FINISH is reachable in 4 plain steps ignoring "count on by 5" (the legal route is 8 steps); P1-08 similarly. The check is "must reach FINISH", so a pupil who does not follow the rule is not told. In practice pupils follow the numbers, but the K6 device only works when the pupil also writes the numbers or when decoys are on every wrong first step. Fix: add "Write the numbers you step on" with a row of boxes, and set the check to "The last number is 60" or "You step on 9 boxes" (P1-06) and "10 boxes" (P1-08).

**F26. Key jargon.** "K1", "K4", "K6", "K7" codes, "Script checks passed: 3 of 3", and "tri", "pent" appear in the teacher key; a teacher cannot use them. "Pentagon" is not in the P1 syllabus (listed in the syllabus summary as not named); the page shows an unnamed icon, which is fine, but the key should say "pentagon icon" or "five-sided icon".

**F27. Prices and options on P1-16.** A pen at $30 and a cup at $8, a book at $4 and a ball at $15 are odd shopping prices; use classroom-credible prices ($3, $5, $7, $12, $20). Low priority; the maths is correct.

**F28. Outcome numbers are not verified.** The key assigns numbers such as "3.1, 3.2 (concept of multiplication, multiplying within 40)", "2.4 (add more than two 1-digit numbers)", "1.4, 1.5 (compare)". `docs/moe/verification_report.md` gives different numbers for P1 (multiplication within 40 = 3.3, division within 20 = 3.4; place value and number words = 1.2, 1.3; adding and subtracting within 100 and algorithms = 2.5, 2.6; mental calculation = 2.7), and `syllabus_by_level.md` lists nine addition/subtraction items against seven numbers, so some numbers in the key are guesses (for example 2.4, and 1.4/1.5). The teacher cannot trust "outcome 3.2". Fix: check each number against the syllabus PDF, or drop the numbers and keep topic names and page numbers (Medium if the key is shown to a school).

## 4. What to change in the shared generator

1. **Self-check library (`gen/common.py`).** Add a "false-pass" test next to the slip test: for each page, enumerate wrong complete answers and report how many still pass the check. Today the grid, picture and total checks report "unique solution" but not "wrong answer passes". Run it for K2, K3, K4, K6. This would have flagged F1, F2 and F4. For the grid (K4), replace "one tick per row and column" by a letter or code on each column.
2. **Colour-by-answer.** Replace the shade/no-shade design by three answer values with three marks, or give each box its own letter; drop "decoys near shaded values" as the only device. Keep the slip detector but include +-4 and +-5 slips for times-table pages and a "same set" slip test.
3. **Graph detective.** Make the check independent: the data clue set must fix every row without the total; use the total only in the check.
4. **Key text from data.** Generate decoy lists, "make 10, 11, 13 or 14", "$5 more than" and similar notes from the page data (the F7 mistakes all come from typed notes). Add a validator rule that every number quoted in a note exists in the page data.
5. **Key thumbnails.** The generator already stores the solution (routes, grids, shaded sets); render a small solved page per entry and lay out two to four entries per page, without leaving half pages.
6. **Validator (`validate.py`).** Add: (a) regexp for `\d+c\b` in all text (F11); (b) a banned-symbol list per level (no ÷ at P1) (F3); (c) a banned-word list for P1 text (column, row, diagonal) and a simple P1 word list in place of the 8-word sentence rule alone; (d) check that every instruction term has a legend on the page (rods, ticks, crosses); (e) check that answer boxes are at least 12 mm tall (P1-09 boxes fail); (f) a difficulty score per page (number of steps) and a test that every two-star page scores above every one-star page of the same strand; (g) check that answer labels appear for multi-box answers (P1-13); (h) a numeral-clipping test for ruler and clock labels (F16, F17).
7. **Mazes.** Add an option to write the numbers on the route, and a check expressed as a last number or a count of boxes (F25).
8. **Clocks.** Reject any time where the hands are within 25 degrees of each other, and keep the minute hand inside the numeral ring.
9. **Build options.** Add `--school` as a required argument for delivered PDFs, and an option to print the name in the cover and licence page too; keep a blank master.

## 5. What was checked and found sound (so it is not re-audited)

Unique solutions: P1-02 (all five riddles, each 1 number), P1-04, P1-16 (1 of 120), P1-06 and P1-08 (1 legal route each, by my own search), P1-09 (also with repeats allowed), P1-11 (six unknowns, each fixed), P1-19 and P1-20 (counts verified from the picture). Code pages: no single slip produces another real word in my enumeration of +-1/+-2/+-5/+-10 slips (nearest near-word TASTLE, not a word). Price: none. Mi: none. Pupil-page alignment wording: none.
