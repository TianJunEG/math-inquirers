# Daily teasers (website/teasers/)

A new puzzle every day for each of the six levels, picked in the visitor's browser from the **Singapore calendar date**
(Asia/Singapore, UTC+8 with no daylight saving), so every visitor sees the same puzzle that day and it changes at 00:00
Singapore time whatever time zone the browser is in. Static only: no server, no cookies, no tracking, no timers, no scores,
no streaks, nothing saved. The existing fixed puzzle stays on the page as the no-JavaScript / failed-JavaScript fallback.

Owner decisions: **TEASER-DAILY-GO** (docs/terms/owner_decisions.md) and the follow-ups: 365 puzzles per level (one per calendar
date, MM-DD, with a spare for 29 Feb), released in monthly batches, November 2026 first, then December 2026, January 2027 ...;
**TEASER-STRICT** (Levels 3 to 6: a sub-structure at most once a quarter, only with a new story and a new frame) and **L1L2-GAP60**
(Levels 1 and 2: a sub-structure may return after at least 60 days with a new story and new numbers).

**What may be promised on the site (the honest wording rule):** "a new puzzle every day", never "a new kind of puzzle every day" and never "a new idea every day".
At Level 1 a maths idea comes back six times a year (every 61 days) with a new story and new numbers; at Level 2 the same; at Levels 3 to 6 an idea returns once
per quarter at most. Across levels the same idea may appear at several levels: in the round-2 bank **50 of the 172 sub-structures used appeared at three or more
levels** (202 of 366 puzzles; one pattern idea at all six). That is allowed (the rules are per level), but it is one more reason not to promise "a new idea".
In the round-3 bank (Nov and Dec, rebuilt) 41 of the 196 sub-structures used appeared at three or more levels (161 of 366 puzzles), and 105 at one level only. In the round-4 bank 50 of the 178 sub-structures used appear at three or more levels (180 of 366 puzzles) and 70 at one level only; under the round-4 ruling each of those uses has its own story, numbers and difficulty, and no two share numbers with the same answer. In the round-5 bank 57 of the 173 sub-structures used appear at three or more levels (202 of 366 puzzles) and 68 at one level only; under the round-5 ruling (below) each of those uses also has its own sentence frame, and a story or frame that the audit found at several levels is now at one level only.

**Owner decisions that bind the bank.** TEASER-STRICT, L1L2-GAP60 and the family cap are in force: a **family is used at most 3 times per quarter at Levels 3 to 6
(and at most 3 times in any 60 days at Levels 1 and 2)**; this cap is CONFIRMED by default (the owner's "proceed with the recommendations" instruction), no longer an open question.

**Owner ruling of fix round 4 (C3, adopted as the default until the owner says otherwise):** every puzzle has a new story AND a new frame; a parent must not see the same puzzle twice at one level;
no two puzzles at DIFFERENT levels may share the same numbers and answer, or the same story skeleton; the same sub-structure at several levels is allowed only with a different story, different numbers and a different difficulty.
Sub-structures that a parent sees as one idea (`same_idea_groups` in `taxonomy.json`: division with a remainder, days in a range, box volume, overlapping sets, ratio after a change, tracing a loop) count as ONE unit for every repeat rule.
A frame that our own books use (the verifier now reads the whole tree, PDFs included) is retired in `taxonomy.json` with the reason.

**Owner ruling of fix round 5 (C3, adopted, not asked again):** "new story AND new frame": each story or sentence frame appears at ONE level only. The 23 cross-level clusters of AUDIT_ROUND5 section 3 (74 puzzles: the weekday date, the value of a digit, solids counted together, facing and turning, two overlapping groups, who did it, the times-table count, fold and cut, the take-away game, knock-out, circle elimination, rows that grow, boat crossings, add the previous two, the tank, the bus or ferry wait, border tiles and the chessboard, a corner cut from a rectangle, and the digit-card, digit-clue, sign and seat-number puzzles) were resolved by keeping the idea at the level where it fits best and giving every other puzzle a new story and, where the structure is shared, a different sentence frame, other numbers and another difficulty (`FIXES_ROUND5.md` lists every puzzle). The same sub-structure at several levels is still allowed, only with a different story, different numbers and a different sentence frame. `verify_bank.py` enforces it: two puzzles of the same sub-structure (or of one same-idea group) at different levels with 6 or more shared four-word runs (numbers and names masked; units, function words and runs found in 8 or more questions of the bank do not count) are a FAIL, and 5 shared runs are a REVIEW line of the structure report; any two puzzles at different levels with 8 or more shared runs are a FAIL.

**Fix round 6 (AUDIT_ROUND6; adopted defaults of the owner's delegate, revocable, logged in `docs/terms/owner_decisions.md`).**
(1) *One frame, one level (H1).* `verify_bank.py` has a **sentence-frame rule**: every sentence is reduced to a function-word skeleton (numbers `#`, names `N`, content words `W`, a list of three or more numbers one token `L`), windows of one or two neighbouring sentences with ten words or more are compared for two puzzles of the same sub-structure (or same-idea group) at different levels; a best score of **0.80 or more is a REVIEW line, 0.90 or more a FAIL** unless the pair is in `frame_ledger.json` with a reason and the same wording (hash). The stock comparison sentence "X is taller than Y" and bare asks are exempt. The 22 same-frame groups (Tier A) of AUDIT_ROUND6 were rewritten at all levels but one; 15 pairs of "the same idea at another age" (Tier B: logic genres, sticker or sundae combinations, matches and the like) remain at 0.90 or more and are in the ledger. The rule is a net, not a proof: on the 39 pairs rebuilt from the audit tables it scores 18 at 0.80 or more (the other 21 tell the same story in other sentences and need a reader).
(2) *ANSWER-WINDOW (H2).* The full-year answer rule at Levels 3 to 6 is dropped. The same answer value may not recur within a level within **60 days** (counted round the year, single digits included, so a 60-day window holds at most nine single-digit answers), and the same answer with the same sub-structure never within **92 days**; Levels 1 and 2 are unchanged. The budget is in `batches/YEAR_PLAN.md`, the rule in `verify_bank.py` (`answer_window_rules`, a FAIL), the decision in `docs/terms/owner_decisions.md` as ANSWER-WINDOW.
(3) *Source lock (M2).* `source_lock.json` now holds minimum bytes per source root and per near-copy folder (97%), and `source_manifest.tsv` lists every source path with its size: a key file (PDF, book, holiday lesson, `must_exist`) that is missing or cut to under half of its size, or more than 0.5% of all paths missing or cut, makes the verification invalid (exit 2).
(4) *Text answers.* From January a month needs 3 text-answer puzzles at every level (4 at Levels 5 and 6): a FAIL for every verified month except November and December. Six text-answer asks and five new Level 5 sub-structures are coded (`kinds_r4.py`, `brute_r4.py`, `samples_r4.py`): see `TEXT_ANSWER_PLAN.md` section 6 and `FIXES_ROUND6.md`.

**The same date next year.** The bank is keyed by month and day, so a child who visits on 14 Nov 2027 meets the 14 Nov 2026 puzzle again. The
plan is to write a fresh batch (new numbers and new stories, same sub-structure rotation) before a date comes round a second time, and to
publish it under the same MM-DD key only when the previous year's puzzle for that date is retired. Until then the same date shows the same puzzle.
Within one year every number set and story is different and no answer value recurs inside its window (60 days; 92 days with the same sub-structure) (checked by `verify_bank.py`).

## How a visitor's page decides what to show

1. Work out the Singapore date from `Date.now() + 8 hours` (no `Intl`, no dependence on the browser's own time zone).
2. The page ships its own fixed puzzle (home: frog hops, ages 6-8; level pages: that level's sample). This is the fallback (owner decision TEASER-DAILY-GO).
3. The two most recent released months are built into every page, so a visitor on those dates sees the daily puzzle at once. Any other released month is fetched from `/teasers/L<level>-<MM>.json`, waiting up to 8 seconds; a late reply is swapped in only if the visitor has not touched the fixed puzzle.
4. If the date has no released puzzle (month not released, file missing, fetch failed or too slow), the fixed puzzle stays, with no date label. Nothing is fetched for an unreleased month.
5. If JavaScript is off, the fixed puzzle is what is on the page. Every visitor therefore sees the same puzzle for the same date, or the fixed one; there is no random or per-visitor pool.

The home page uses the Ages 6-8 set (there is no level picker on the home page). If a page is left open past midnight, the new day's puzzle appears when the visitor returns to the tab (focus / visibility events, no timers).

## Adding the next month (for example January)

1. Levels 1 and 2: the sub-structure of each day is fixed by `batches/YEAR_PLAN.md` (the released day 61 days earlier); write a new story and new numbers for it, with an answer that differs from every answer within 60 days. Levels 3 to 6: choose sub-structures that are unused in the quarter and at least 30 days from their last use (`taxonomy.json` lists them per level); a family at most 3 times a quarter. Answers: no answer value twice within 60 days at Levels 3 to 6 (single digits included), none with the same sub-structure within 92 days (`batches/YEAR_PLAN.md`, answer budget). Text answers: at least 3 a month at every level, 4 at Levels 5 and 6 (`TEXT_ANSWER_PLAN.md` section 6); a month with fewer fails. A story must not repeat a sentence, a frame or the numbers of anything in our books: write it, run the verifier, and rewrite on any FAIL (a REVIEW is read by the author).
2. Write `defs/m01/l1.py` ... `l6.py` (31 puzzles each). Every puzzle needs a `sub=` that exists in `taxonomy.json` for that level; a new sub-structure needs a syllabus citation, a level range, a solver in `kinds_r2.py`, an independent checker in `brute_r2.py` and a sample in `samples_r2.py` first.
3. `python3 website/teasers/build_bank.py 01`, then `verify_bank.py 01` **on the full working tree** (a month that is not released yet is checked as a draft against every released month; the sentence-frame rule, the answer window and the text-answer gate apply to it) (it refuses an incomplete tree; it also needs `pdftotext`) until 0 FAIL and the REVIEW lines are explained; then `verify_bank.py --selftest`, `year_model.py` and `test_flag_off.py`. Re-run the verifier on the full tree again just before the release: the holiday, term and extension books keep growing.
4. An independent audit of the batch, with a second reader who hand-solves at least 15 puzzles a level (new items first; `second_reader_round6.py` is the model), then add `"01"` to `released` in `manifest.json`, rebuild and re-test.
5. Releasing to the live site is a separate step: set `"site_enabled": true`, run the generators and `build_site.py`, commit the rebuilt `website/dist`.

## Rules every puzzle follows

- Original wording and story; not copied from the holiday program, term lessons, extension workbooks, school booklets or external sources. `verify_bank.py` fails on any 7-word shared run, on a masked-skeleton match with shared numbers, and (round 2) on a **shared story**: the content words of the question (names, number words and very common words removed) found in one source sentence or two neighbouring sentences with the same objects and the same numbers, or the same specific objects (snail and wall, frog and well), or the same wording with other numbers.
- Round-5 owner ruling (see above): each story or sentence frame at ONE level only; the cross-level sentence-frame rule of `verify_bank.py` (round 6: the function-word skeleton rule and the ledger).
- Round-4 owner ruling (see above): a new story AND a new frame; no repeated idea at one level (`same_idea_groups`); across levels no shared numbers with the same answer and no shared story skeleton; a frame that our books use is retired in `taxonomy.json` (`retired`, and `retired_frames` for the wording patterns). The honest wording rule stays "a new puzzle every day".
- Flag off until launch: `website/mi_signup.html` and `website/level_pages/*.html` stay in their committed no-teaser state while `site_enabled` is false (`test_flag_off.py`). A preview is built in a scratch copy of `website/`, never in place.
- Structure policy (owner). **Levels 3 to 6 (TEASER-STRICT):** a sub-structure at most once per calendar quarter and never within 30 days of itself across a quarter boundary (checked circularly over the year); a family at most 3 times a quarter; **ANSWER-WINDOW (round 6):** the same answer value not twice within 60 days (single digits included) and, with the same sub-structure, not twice within 92 days (the full-year answer rule is gone). **Levels 1 and 2 (L1L2-GAP60):** a sub-structure at least 60 days after its last use, counted round the year in both a 365-day and a 366-day year (so 31 Oct and 1 Nov of the next year are neighbours, and 28 Feb to 1 Mar is counted); a family at most 3 times in any 60 consecutive days and never on two consecutive days; an answer not repeated within 60 days and a single-digit answer at most twice in any 60 days. No sub-structure that the level's fixed page puzzle uses; no repeated frame; no two puzzles of a level with most of their numbers or the same story in common.
- A sub-structure is a maths idea a parent would see as different. Sub-structures that a parent would call the same puzzle (three division stories, two ways to calculate a bracket expression, add or take off a percentage ...) were merged in round 2, so the taxonomy counts only ideas that look different.
- One answer, proved by brute force over the whole search space; the author solver and the independent checker must agree.
- Singapore wording and spelling (metres, litres, colour, Primary, HDB, hawker centre, MRT, $ and cents; GST at 9%); coins 5, 10, 20, 50 cents only; no brand names. No coin or note images.
- Reading load and sentence length limited per level (checked); topics only from that level's syllabus (cited in `taxonomy.json`); level-fit corrections of round 2: perimeter, line-ups, squares in a grid and durations of more than one hour start at Levels 2 or 3; fractions of a set and area with a corner cut out start at Level 3 or 4; rounding starts at Level 4.
