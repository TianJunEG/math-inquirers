Terms and refund policy

November beta · Math Inquirers by Preciouslyours Pte Ltd, Singapore

1. Who we are

Math Inquirers is run by Preciouslyours Pte Ltd (UEN 201507566K, [registered address, to update later]), “we”. You are the parent or guardian registering a child, “you”. Math Inquirers is an independent maths enrichment program. It is not run by, approved by or affiliated with the Ministry of Education or the Gifted Education Programme.

2. What the beta is

  • Ten live online lessons of 90 minutes, two a week for five weeks, in a small group of up to 8 children, taught by a tutor over video.
  • It is a beta. We are testing the lessons with real children, so we may adjust lessons, times or materials as we learn. We will tell you before any change to your group’s times.
  • We do not promise any exam result, ranking or placement. We do not identify “gifted” children.

4. Groups and seats

  • Each group has up to 8 children. Seats go in order of registration. When a group is full we may open another group and offer you a place there or on a waiting list.
  • A group runs with at least 4 children. If fewer register, we will offer you another group or time, or a full refund.

7. Term-time program (from launch)

These rules apply to the regular school-term program, not to the November beta. They are a draft until the term price is set.

  • Terms. A term has 10 lessons of 90 minutes, about one a week. Places open every term. A child can join any term, and any class that has a free seat, part-way through if needed.
  • Fee. The fee is for the whole term: [S$ per term]. A child who joins part-way pays a prorated fee for the lessons remaining.
  • Two ways to pay. (a) Pay the full term at the start and get a 5% discount. (b) Pay in three instalments, with no discount: the first before lesson 1, the second after lesson 3, and the third after lesson 6. [Instalment amounts, for example 30% / 30% / 40%] We send the schedule with your payment link, and payment is online through Stripe, by card or PayNow.
  • Missed lessons and exam periods. A child who skips a lesson, for example during school exams, can watch the recording and use the online practice, and may attend the same lesson in another class or in a holiday class if a seat is free. There are no refunds for skipped lessons.
  • Holiday courses. A child who has done a holiday course carries on with the next term of the school year: after a December course, Term 1; after a one-week March course, Term 2; after a June course, Term 3.
  • Withdrawing. The refund rules in section 5 apply: notice of one month before the term starts gets a refund of the lessons not attended, and none after that, but the child can move the remaining lessons to a later term.

Families who finish the beta and return the feedback form receive a code for the introductory price on the regular program [amount and closing date to be set after the beta]. The code is issued after we receive the feedback form.

9. What we need from you

  • A device with a camera, microphone and a stable internet connection, and the materials listed before the first lesson.
  • A parent or guardian nearby, who lets your child find the answers. Please do not give answers during the lesson.
  • Join only through the link we send, and do not share it.

10. Safety and conduct

  • Lessons use one approved video platform. The tutor does not send private messages to children. Check-ins and invitations go to the whole group or to the parent.
  • We may remove a child or a parent from a lesson for behaviour that harms the group, and we will tell you why.

11. Recording, children’s work and your data

Lessons are recorded so that a child who misses a lesson can catch up. We also keep photos of your child’s work and short progress notes, and your delivery name, address and phone number so that we can post the workbook. How we record, what we keep, who can see it and for how long is in the Privacy and recording notice. You agree to it when you register.

12. Materials and recordings

The workbooks (printed or PDF), slides, practice questions and recordings are for your family’s personal use. The printed workbook is yours to keep and use, and you may print the PDF copy for your own child. Please do not copy, sell, post online or share them with other families, and do not record or screenshot lessons yourself.
