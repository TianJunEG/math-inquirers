2. What the beta is

  • Ten live online lessons of 90 minutes, two a week for five weeks, in a small group of up to 8 children, taught by a tutor over video.
  • It is a beta. We are testing the lessons with real children, so we may adjust lessons, times or materials as we learn. We will tell you before any change to your group’s times.
  • We do not promise any exam result, ranking or placement. We do not identify “gifted” children.

3. Fee and payment

  • The beta fee is S$25 a lesson, S$250 for all 10 lessons for one child, which includes access to the lesson recordings and online practice for your child’s level.
  • Payment is due before the first lesson, by [PayNow or bank transfer; details sent on WhatsApp]. [Instalment option, if any]

  • Each group has up to 8 children. Seats go in order of registration. When a group is full we may open another group and offer you a place there or on a waiting list.

6. Founding-family price

Families who finish the beta and return the feedback form receive a code for the introductory price on the regular program (currently S$500 instead of S$650, valid until 12 March 2027). The code is issued after we receive the feedback form.

7. What we need from you

  • A device with a camera, microphone and a stable internet connection, and the materials listed before the first lesson.
  • A parent or guardian nearby, who lets your child find the answers. Please do not give answers during the lesson.
  • Join only through the link we send, and do not share it.

8. Safety and conduct

  • Lessons use one approved video platform. The tutor does not send private messages to children. Check-ins and invitations go to the whole group or to the parent.
  • We may remove a child or a parent from a lesson for behaviour that harms the group, and we will tell you why.

9. Recording, children’s work and your data

Lessons are recorded so that a child who misses a lesson can catch up. We also keep photos of your child’s work and short progress notes. How we record, what we keep, who can see it and for how long is in the Privacy and recording notice. You agree to it when you register.

10. Materials and recordings

The workbooks, slides, practice questions and recordings are for your family’s personal use. Please do not copy, sell, post online or share them, and do not record or screenshot lessons yourself.
