Privacy and recording notice

November beta · Preciouslyours Pte Ltd (Math Inquirers) · written to follow the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)

Why you are reading this

We teach children under 13, so we ask a parent or guardian to agree for them. When you register you tick boxes to agree to the points below. You can say no to the optional one. The others are needed to join a live recorded group.

What we collect, and why

WhatWhy
Parent name, mobile number, emailTo register your child, tell you about times and changes, take payment and reply to you.
Child’s name, level and ageTo place your child in the right group and give feedback to you.
Lesson recordingsSo a child who misses a lesson can catch up.
Photos of your child’s work, answers to practice questions and short progress notesTo give your child feedback, show you how they are doing and improve the lessons.
Your feedback formTo improve the program. We report feedback without your child’s name.

We do not collect your child’s photo or school, and we do not ask for NRIC numbers. [Confirm nothing else is collected]

How lessons are recorded

  • We record the tutor and the shared lesson screen. Children’s cameras are not included in the recording, and children’s names are not shown. [Confirm the platform setting before the first lesson]
  • A child’s voice may be heard if they speak. The tutor repeats answers in their own words wherever possible.
  • Children may keep their cameras off in the live lesson. A parent who does not want their child heard on the recording can ask us to mute the child; please tell us when you register.
  • We tell the group at the start of each lesson that it is being recorded.

Who can see it

  • Recordings: only the enrolled families of the same group, through a private link. We never post them publicly.
  • Your child’s work and progress notes: you and your child’s tutor. Other families never see them.
  • Our service providers who store or deliver the data (video platform, file storage, messaging). They may store data outside Singapore. We choose providers that protect personal data to a standard comparable to the PDPA. Online payments from the regular program are handled by HitPay; during the beta we confirm PayNow payments manually. Parent and child profiles are stored with Supabase. Lessons are held on Google Meet; recordings and files are stored on Google Drive; we message you on WhatsApp.
  • We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

DataKept for
Lesson recordings30 days after your group’s last lesson, then deleted
Photos of work and progress notes90 days after the program ends, then deleted
Registration and payment records7 years, as required for business and tax records
Feedback formsKept without your child’s name

Your rights

  • You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you or your child, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We will reply within 30 days.
  • You can withdraw your agreement at any time. If you withdraw the recording or children’s work agreement, your child can no longer join a recorded group, and we will refund the lessons not yet held under our terms.
  • Contact our data protection contact: Jasmine Hon, [pypteltd@gmail.com, to be changed later].

Keeping it safe

Recordings and work are stored privately, with access limited to the people above. Our tutors agree to keep children’s data confidential and not to make their own recordings or photos. If something goes wrong with personal data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Wording used on the registration form

  1. Contact (needed): I agree to Math Inquirers (Preciouslyours Pte Ltd) using my details to arrange the class and contact me about it.
  2. Terms (needed): I have read the terms and refund policy and agree to them.
  3. Recording (needed): I agree that live lessons are recorded as described in this notice, and that recordings are shared only with the enrolled families of my child’s group.
  4. Children’s work (needed): I agree that Math Inquirers keeps photos of my child’s work, practice answers and short progress notes, as described in this notice. I am the parent or legal guardian of the child.
  5. Feedback (optional): I agree that my feedback may be used, without my child’s name, to improve and describe the program.
