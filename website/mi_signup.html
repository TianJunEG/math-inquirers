How children learn

Four habits of a young mathematician

Every lesson follows the same four steps, so children learn how to think, not just what to write.

1

Notice the pattern

Look closely before rushing to an answer.

2

Test it with objects

Counters, coins and strips to check an idea.

3

Check your answer

Does it make sense? Try another way.

4

Explain why

Say or draw why it works. This is where learning sticks.

Placement

Which level is right for my child?

Levels are named by age, and each spans three ages and overlaps the next, so children can join at the level that suits how they are learning now. Start with the level that matches your child's school year:

  • Ages 6-8 (builds on Primary 1 maths)
  • Ages 7-9 (builds on Primary 2 maths)
  • Ages 8-10 (builds on Primary 3 maths)
  • Ages 9-11 (builds on Primary 4 maths)
  • Ages 10-12 (builds on Primary 5 maths)
  • Ages 11-13 (maths + coding bridge, after PSLE)

Take the free 30-minute diagnostic for the level that matches your child's school year (the Math Inquirers Placement Check for that level). Then:

  • The check is scored out of 10. 7 to 10: enrol at that level.
  • 4 to 6: enrol at that level (the tutor will note that support may help), or choose one level down.
  • 0 to 3: we recommend one level down.
  • If it was too easy (9 or 10 out of 10), your child may also try the diagnostic one level up.

You have the final say. You can change the level before lesson 1 at no cost, and the price is the same at every level. Ages 6-8 is the lowest level: if your child finds it hard, they start with the free taster and a tutor chat.

Questions

Parents often ask

Who is it for?

Children aged 6 to 13 who enjoy puzzles or finish school work easily. There are six levels, by age and school year (Level 1 builds on Primary 1 maths). K2 children starting Primary 1 in 2027 are welcome in Level 1 after a free 15-minute readiness check, which we arrange after you register. Every lesson has three kinds of questions, Start here, Think harder and Go further, so every child is stretched.

Is it linked to MOE or the GEP?

No. Math Inquirers is an independent maths enrichment program. It is inspired by research-based approaches used in gifted education, such as low-floor, high-ceiling tasks and explaining why, but we do not identify or test for giftedness, and it is not a GEP preparation course.

Who teaches?

Lessons are planned and led by an experienced Singapore primary school maths teacher, using the Math Inquirers lesson plans, slides and workbook.

What if my child misses a lesson?

Every lesson is recorded, so your child can watch it later and use the online practice. Your child can make up a missed lesson once by joining the same lesson in another class of the same level, if a seat is free. Beta fees are not refunded.

How does online work?

Live video lessons with the same group of up to 8. We post the printed workbook (with the kit cut-out pages) to you before the first lesson. All your child needs is a laptop or tablet with a camera, on a table with space for the kit.

What will it cost after the beta?

We have not set the regular price yet. We plan an introductory price for the first families after the beta, including those who join the beta and give feedback. We will tell you the amount and how long it lasts after the beta, before any regular program starts.

Can we ask for a different time?

Yes. Use Request another time to tell us the level and the days and times that suit you. The times shown as "not open yet" are placeholders we may open if enough families are interested. Nothing is booked or charged when you ask.

How does the workbook reach us?

We print your child's workbook (the Inquirer Kit cut-out pages are at the back) and post it to your home in Singapore before the first lesson, included in the beta fee. We need a delivery address and postal code when you register. What if we need to cancel, or miss a lesson?

The November beta fee is not refunded. If your child cannot come to a lesson, they can make it up once by joining the same lesson in another class of the same level, if a seat is free. Every lesson is also recorded, so your child can watch it and use the online practice. The printed workbook and kit are yours to keep. If we cancel a lesson or a group and cannot reschedule, we refund every lesson not held. Message us on WhatsApp or email to arrange a make-up.
