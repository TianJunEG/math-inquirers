The 10 lessons

What your child learns

Sequences, primes, negative numbers, algebra, counting, probability, codes and data, each also explored in Scratch. Each lesson follows the same four steps: notice the pattern, test it, check the answer, explain why.

The maths is enrichment toward secondary maths, with every puzzle also explored in code. Some topics (negative numbers, primes, simple equations, angles in polygons) are met in lower-secondary maths; others (sequences and the nth term, counting, probability, clock arithmetic) are enrichment, so this is not a Secondary 1 course.

Three tiers

Start here, Think harder, Go further

Every child's workbook has all three, plus an Investigation on the Finish page. After the first few questions, any child may choose to carry on or try the next tier. Nobody is told which page they are on, and a child who finds Think harder too hard simply goes back to Start here.

At home

What your child needs

  • A laptop or desktop with Scratch 3 open in the browser (scratch.mit.edu, free, no account needed); a tablet can run Scratch but is slower
  • Pencil, coloured pencils, a ruler, a protractor and scissors
  • A mini whiteboard and marker
  • About 25 matchsticks or toothpicks, linking cubes or counters, two dice and a coin, and squared paper
  • Optional: a Python editor, for children who already know Python

These come from the lesson plans for this level.

Try one now

A puzzle to try

Think harder · Lesson 1
2, 9, 16, 23 …

Is 200 a term of 2, 9, 16, 23, …? Explain.

Show the answer

No. The rule is 7n − 5. The 29th term is 198 and the 30th term is 205, so 200 is skipped.

In class, the answer is only half the job

Children then explain how they know, and hear other ways of seeing the same puzzle. That is where the thinking grows.

Placement

Is Ages 11-13 right for my child?

Levels are named by age, and each spans three ages and overlaps the next, so children can join the level that suits how they are learning now. This level is for ages 11-13 and builds on Primary 6 maths, after the PSLE.

Take the free 30-minute diagnostic for the level that matches your child's school year (the Math Inquirers Placement Check for that level). Then:

  • The check is scored out of 10. 7 to 10: enrol at this level.
  • 4 to 6: enrol at this level (the tutor will note that support may help), or choose one level down (Ages 10-12).
  • 0 to 3: we recommend one level down (Ages 10-12).

You have the final say. You can change the level before lesson 1 at no cost, and the price is the same at every level.
