Three tiers

Start here, Think harder, Go further

Every child's workbook has all three, plus an Investigation on the Finish page. After the first few questions, any child may choose to carry on or try the next tier. Nobody is told which page they are on, and a child who finds Think harder too hard simply goes back to Start here.

In the kit

Cut-outs in the workbook

  • Play coins and play notes, with $1 coins
  • Paper cube strips
  • Ten-frames and 20 paper counters (10 red, 10 yellow)
  • Number strips 0 to 50 and 0 to 20
  • 24 shape and sorting tiles (4 shapes, 3 colours, 2 sizes)

Nothing needs to be bought for the kit. The kit is the cut-out pages at the back of your child's workbook. We post the printed workbook to your home before the first lesson (Singapore addresses), and your child cuts out the pieces and keeps them in one envelope.

At home

What your child needs

  • A laptop or tablet with a camera, on a table with space for the kit
  • Pencil, crayons, scissors and a cm ruler
  • A mini whiteboard and marker
  • Everyday things the lessons use, such as paper cups, straws or lolly sticks (never real matches), paper clips, erasers and buttons (small objects only with a grown-up nearby)

These come from the lesson plans for this level.

Placement

Is Ages 6-8 right for my child?

Levels are named by age, and each spans three ages and overlaps the next, so children can join the level that suits how they are learning now. This level is for ages 6-8 and builds on Primary 1 maths.

Take the free 30-minute diagnostic for the level that matches your child's school year (the Math Inquirers Placement Check for that level). Then:

  • The check is scored out of 10. 7 to 10: enrol at this level.
  • 4 to 6: enrol at this level (the tutor will note that support may help), or choose one level down.
  • 0 to 3: we recommend one level down.
  • If it was too easy (9 or 10 out of 10), your child may also try the diagnostic one level up (Ages 7-9).

You have the final say. You can change the level before lesson 1 at no cost, and the price is the same at every level. Ages 6-8 is the lowest level: if your child finds it hard, they start with the free taster and a tutor chat.
