How Math Inquirers fits school maths

Math Inquirers is independent enrichment. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by MOE, the Gifted Education Programme or any other ministry. Choose where your child goes to school: each level card shows how its ten lessons compare with that system's maths at the same age.

Singapore: compared with the MOE 2021 Primary Mathematics syllabus (updated October 2025), lesson by lesson. Where the syllabus names a topic in the same year, the lesson is marked as taught at school; harder questions inside a lesson can still go further (see the tier map below).

Lessons use puzzles and explaining, not school-style practice, so “goes a little further” means the topic is usually met at school a year or more later. Level 1 is for ages 6 to 8, Level 2 for 7 to 9, Level 3 for 8 to 10, Level 4 for 9 to 11, Level 5 for 10 to 12 and Level 6 for 11 to 13. Hover or tab to a lesson for details. The tenth lesson of each level is a mixed review and is not compared.

This is our own comparison, made from public curriculum documents, and some placements could not be confirmed.

How the three tiers fit the syllabus

Every lesson has three tiers of questions. Start here is the core idea. Think harder is the same idea in an unfamiliar setting. Go further is the challenge: mastery, then extension. Each level is compared with the school year it is named after (Level 1 with Primary 1, and so on).
