# Conditions that apply to a printed educational workbook (read 23 Oct 2026; not legal advice)

Sources: MAS page "Using Images of Singapore Currency" (https://www.mas.gov.sg/currency/using-images-of-singapore-currency, last updated 24 Aug 2026) and Gazette Notification No. 2078 of 11 August 2006 (Currency Act, section 20(1)), read in full: `source_mas/gazette_2078_of_2006_reproduction-of-currency.pdf`. The Currency Act 1967 itself (sections 20(1), 20(2)) was NOT read.

## 1. Is permission needed?
- MAS page: permission is NOT needed to import, manufacture, sell, circulate or distribute "a publication for educational purposes or in connection with any news, factual article or report" containing a reproduction, if the Conditions below are met.
- Gazette para 1(b) and 3: MAS grants permission to any person for "specified merchandise or product", which includes "any publication in which the reproduction of currency notes or coins is used for educational purposes, or in connection with any news or factual article or report, not being an advertisement for any merchandise product, or service", subject to the Schedule. Para 2: the permission does not apply to a person who is contravening section 20(1) or whom MAS has notified in writing. Para 4: anyone not following the Schedule must get MAS's prior permission.
- Penalty without permission: fine up to $2,000, or up to 3 months' prison, or both.
- UNCONFIRMED: whether a workbook sold to schools under a photocopy licence is a "publication used for educational purposes" (looks so) or "merchandise or product" (selling is listed on the "permission required" side). Same open point as owner decision D1. A lawyer or MAS must say.

## 2. Conditions for coins and notes (gazette Schedule 1 and 2; MAS page "Conditions")
1. Maintain, and not detract from, the dignity, integrity and image of the note or coin.
2. No risk of confusion with genuine notes or coins (MAS page: must not be used so they could be mistaken as genuine).
3. Do not distort images of the President or any national symbol.
4. Not offensive or against the public interest.
5. Printed on material that can unambiguously and easily be told apart from coins, paper notes or polymer notes.
- Coins: no size rule is stated anywhere (page or gazette).

## 3. Extra conditions for notes only (gazette Schedule 3)
(a) Not electronic (hardcopy books): at least 150% of both length and width of the genuine note, OR not more than 60% of both length and width.
(b) Enlarged or reduced: depicted proportionally (also when viewed at an angle).
(c) May be shown at angles; where the WHOLE note is shown flat to the camera it shall contain the word "SPECIMEN" in black and bold lettering diagonally across the reproduction, which shall NOT cover any part of the portrait. (The task wording said "across the whole flat note"; the gazette text is "diagonally across the reproduction" and "not cover any part of the portrait".)
(d) Not duplex printing: nothing on the reverse that could give the impression it is a genuine note (MAS page: blank on its reverse).
