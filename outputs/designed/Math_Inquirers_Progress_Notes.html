Short notes after each lesson
Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

A short note after each lesson on what each child tried, enjoyed and found tricky. It feeds the weekly message to parents. It is not a test and it does not rank or label anyone. Use usernames or first names only. Keep it to 30 seconds per child.

How to use the notes

  • Write what you saw, not a score. Quote the child's own words where you can.
  • Always start the weekly message with something the child did well.
  • If a child tried something harder, tell the family so they can enjoy it with them, and mention the next level only if the family asks.
  • If a child found a lesson tricky, say what helped and what to try at home. Do not compare children or share notes between families.
  • Notes are private to the tutor and the child's own family. Do not record anything beyond learning in class.
