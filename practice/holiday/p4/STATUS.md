# Level 4 (Primary 4, ages 9-11) holiday online practice: week 1 (lessons 1 and 2)

Build: `python3 practice/holiday/build_holiday.py p4 1` (and `p4 2`); test: `python3 practice/holiday/test_holiday.py p4 1` (and `2`). Output in `p4/NN/dist/` (git-ignored, not deployed); screenshots in `p4/NN/proof/` (390 px and 900 px, light and dark).
Week 1 = lessons 1 and 2 (Tue 24 Nov and Fri 27 Nov, `website/data/beta_schedule.json`).

Source: the workbook is generated by `p4/workbook_html.js` from `p4/content/lN.js` (`workbook()`, `gaps.invest`, `qbank.stretch[0:4]`, `qbank.challenge[-3:]`). It is code, not JSON, so each question was transcribed into `p4/NN/lesson.py` with an independent brute-force checker (`p4/p4lib.py`) and the printed strings are proved to occur in `p4/content/lN.js`. The printed key (`key`, `gaps.invest.key`, `qbank.a`) agrees with every checker.

Unavailable (not mirrored) and why:
- The common "Looking back" box (three prompts, tool ticks): generic reflection, not a question with an answer.
- Answer lines "Explain / write what you notice" are mirrored as self-check items (the child compares with the model answer); nothing is marked.
- Tables and multi-part boxes were split into several items (one screen each); the stem is repeated in each.
- Q1 of lesson 1 asks for factor pairs in boxes: online the child types the smaller number of each pair (after 1 x 48), in order.
- Q6(a) of lesson 1 / Go further 5(b) of lesson 1 and Go further 6 of lesson 2 (make up your own puzzle/riddle) are open self-checks with a model.
- No hand-drawn extras: figures (tree array, bus time line, number lines, digit cards) were redrawn as SVG at phone-readable size; the workbook's own SVG text is too small at 390 px.

Shared-file changes (backward compatible; Level 1 and ext/submit tests re-run and pass):
- `practice/ext/public/sheet.js` and `practice/submit/mark.js`: `nums()` now accepts space-grouped numbers in a comma list (`10 001, 10 010` = 10001 and 10010). Without a comma it behaves as before.
- `practice/holiday/test_holiday.py`: the "unanswered, submit anyway?" confirm is expected only when there are paper-only items (writing self-checks are answered in the test).

Open questions:
- Typed answers are plain numbers or space-grouped numbers; commas inside one number (47,290) work too, but as a list separator they split numbers.
- Lesson 1 Go further are printed as Q5 to Q7 in the workbook; ids are G5 to G7 (lesson 1) and G5 to G7 (lesson 2) to match.
