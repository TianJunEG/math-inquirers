## High
**H1. A correct list typed with commas and no spaces is marked wrong when the numbers have 3 digits** (`practice/ext/public/sheet.js` `nums()` and the same code in `practice/submit/mark.js`). The thousands-separator rule `(\d),(?=\d{3}(\D|$))` turns "250,450" into 250450. Page and server agree, so the server also marks it wrong. Reproduced on Level 2: L1 2(a), 3(a), 6(b), Investigation (b), Think harder 1; L2 5(a) corners, 5(b) corners, 6, Think harder 1, Go further 5. The instruction says only "with a comma between", and children (and parents) naturally type `250,450`. "250, 450" and "250 450" are accepted. Fix: split on commas first and merge a group only if the split parse fails to match the key (try both parses), in both files and `mark.test.mjs`; or say "comma and a space" (weaker). Level 1 (1 to 2 digit numbers) and Level 3 (4 digits) are not affected.

## Medium
**M1. P3 L2 1(a): "from left to right in the picture" is wrong/ambiguous.** The boxes are: top number (2nd column), second number (3rd column), answer (1st column, leftmost). Stored order is top to bottom (4, 7, 6); a child reading left to right by position would give 6, 4, 7 and be marked wrong. The worked steps number the boxes top to bottom. Reword ("the first box in the top number, the box in the second number, the box in the answer") or label the boxes.

**M2. Cards reveal the answers of other cards (marked items become copy-able).** Reworded stems, not in the printed workbook: P3 L1 1(e) says "Why is 5037 the closest" (answer of 1(d)); P3 L1 6(b) notice says "The counts are 1, 3, 6" (answer of 6(b)); P3 L1 Investigation 8(c) why says "18 numbers, not 24" (answer of 8(c)); P3 L2 5(b) prints "4127 + 5872 = 9999 and 2593 + 7406 = 9999" (answers of 5(a)); P3 L2 3(a) fill says "The magic total is 4500" (answer of 3(a) total); P2 L2 5(a) circles says "160 is between them" (answer of 5(a) between). Because any question can be opened first, these defeat the marking. Use wording like "your answers to 5(a)" or show the figures only after Submit.

**M3. Hints that give the answer or teach something false.**
- P1 L2 5(b) hint: "Try corners that are 1, 3 and 5" is the answer.
- P2 L2 5(b) draw hint: "The three biggest numbers ... go in the corners" gives the corners (and the answer of 5(b) corners and Think harder 1).
- P2 L2 5(a) circles hint: "The corner is on two sides, so it is the smallest of the three" is not a rule (it is true only for sum 390; in 5(b) the corners are the biggest). Misleading for a child.
- Near-giveaways on yes/no cards: P2 L1 Investigation (c) "Can you find one mirror number that is even?" and Think harder 4 "try ... swap the digits of 425"; P2 L2 4(a) hint names the diagonal.

**M4. Keyboard and screen-reader focus is lost after every action.** After Enter on a question button, "Give me a hint", "Skip to next", "Submit" or "Yes, submit", `document.activeElement` is `BODY` (the nav/card is rebuilt). A keyboard user must tab from the top, through 30+ question buttons, each time. Keep focus on the control (or move it to the card heading), and move focus to the summary after Submit.

**M5. (Code inspection; not testable in desktop Chromium) Number-pad keyboard has no comma/space on iPhone.** All `set` answers and number answers use `inputmode="numeric"`. The iOS numeric pad has digits only, so a child on an iPhone or iPad cannot type the comma or space that "type them with a comma between" needs; "10,20,30" becomes "102030" and is marked wrong. Affects 6 + 5 + 4 + 5 + 4 + 2 set items across the six lessons. Use `inputmode="text"` (or "decimal" gives a comma/period) for `set` items, and test on a real device.

**M6. Printed meaning lost: position of numbers in the figure.** P1 L2 5(a) (magic triangle) is typed as "4, 5, 6", which are simply the numbers not used in the corners: a child can score without solving, and the placement the printed answer checks (6 between 1 and 2, etc.) is not checked. Same for P2 L2 5(a) corners (110, 120, 130 are given/left-over) and P3 L2 2(a)/(b)/(c) (only the sum is asked, so the numbers made are not seen). Acceptable as self-check or paired with a placement question; flag for the owner.

## Low

- L2. Reading load for Level 1: split cards keep the whole printed stem, e.g. P1 L1 2(a) rule card: "What is the rule? Fill in the rule and the next two numbers. Now the rule for 4, 7, 10, 13 ... Write it in words ... Then check it with the answer after you submit." (44 words, repeated instruction). Investigations and 5(a) are 43 to 44 words. - L3. "Submit anyway?" always appears: self-check and paper-only items can never count as answered (paper items have no input), so a child who answers every marked question still sees "N questions have no answer". Count only marked items, or word it "N questions have no typed answer".
- L4. Open (no objective answer) items are not mapped to `marking_mode none` by `make_keys.py` (it looks for a first step starting "Open"); the lessons' own `open=True` items (P1 L1 2, P1 L2 1, P3 L1 4, P3 L2 6) are exported as `self`. Reports will show them as self-check; decide if intended.

- L6. Figure text on a 360 to 390 px phone: the digits of the 1000-chart pieces (P2 L1 4) are 11.8 px at 390 px and 10.7 px at 360 px; (a)/(b)/(c) labels of the column sums are 11.8 px (P2 L2 1, P3 L2 1; 10.5 to 10.7 px in the solution figure/at 360 px). - L7. Placeholder text ("Write here...") is `#757575` on the page background: about 4.3:1 light and 3.8:1 dark, below 4.5:1. Everything else measured at 4.74:1 or more.
- L8. Radio groups have no group name (the question is not linked) and numbered boxes are named only "Box 1 ... Box n"; screen readers hear "Yes radio" without the question. Use fieldset/legend or `aria-labelledby`.
- L9. Footer on every page says "nothing is sent anywhere". True while the flag is off; must change when SUBMIT-TO-TUTOR is enabled. Answers are stored per lesson in `localStorage` with no child identity, so a shared tablet shows the previous child's answers.
- L10. Investigation is listed before Think harder (print order is Think harder, Go further, Investigation). Declared in STATUS. P3 asks "Think harder is optional"; online all tiers show (open owner questions in STATUS).
- L11. P3 L2 Think harder 2 gives A = C = 3; earlier letter puzzles say "different letters are different digits". The printed question does not say so; consider adding "(letters may repeat here)".
- L12. A few extra reworded items: P1 L1 "Do not write 0", "up to 20"; the same wording as STATUS lists. No other item changes the printed meaning.
