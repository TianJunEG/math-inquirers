# Independent audit, round 2: Level 5 Extension Workbook

Audited 23 Oct 2026 by a second model in a fresh session. Report only: nothing in the book, data, builders or tools was edited and nothing was pushed. The only file written is this one.

Checked: `outputs/extension/L5/` print (100 pp, 1-bit, 600 ppi), print vector (100 pp), soft copy (99 pp), `build_report.json` (0 problems, `check_bw_failures` 0); `extension/data/l5_u01..u10.json` against commit 9be9d42 (the data audited in round 1; identical to 3a871e3); holiday text `p5/content/l1..l10.js`; `README.md`, `PROGRESS.md`, `TOOL_REQUESTS.md`.

Method, honestly:
- **Pages.** All 100 print pages were rendered at 100 dpi and every unit page 6-75 was viewed (two-up), plus pp. 1-5, 77. Zooms at 200-400 dpi on pp. 28, 29, 32, 33, 38, 55, 56, 57, 59 (the changed figures). Answer pages 96-99 were read as text from the vector PDF.
- **Whys (check 1), from the PDF:** I parsed all 244 answer entries (Try + numbered items) out of pp. 80-99 and compared each with the item at that position: Why text equals the Why of the question in that position for all 244; the "page N" printed under each answer equals the question's page for all 244 (3 wrapped ones read by eye: pp. 8, 8, 58). For the 218 questions whose number could be read from the question page text, rank equals the printed number; the other 16 (figure-beside-text layouts, U4 and U8) were seen on the page images. Then I read every Why on pp. 96-99 against its question by eye.
- **Hand solving (check 2):** I solved by hand every item whose text, parts or answer changed (76 items; 63 of them with a new key) and every Why or figure change (112 changed items in all, listed below). Brute-force scripts of my own for: all expressions from 3, 4, 7, 9 (U1 G03), card sums (U2 T03), pack mixes (U6 T01), 3-digit digit-sum counting (U10 G01), digit 7 count (U10 S12), rectangles in a 2 by 5 grid, riddle U1 S12 and T01. Geometry figures checked from the coordinates in the data. Result: **0 wrong keys, 0 unsolvable, 0 ambiguous new items.** `run_checks.py --level 5`: 416 answers, OK.
- **Not done:** no child trial, no printer proof, no real 600 dpi print, soft copy only compared as text.

## Verdict

**Close, but not sell-ready until three clone items and a handful of figure and Learn points are fixed (about half a day).** The root bug is gone: every Why on every answer page now matches its question, the contents and answer page numbers are right, Welcome, cover and plaque are good, and all the maths is right. But the H2 fix introduced or left three items that are the holiday item with the same numbers (one made worse than it was), the unit 8 figures are still not clean, the new supposition and angle-fact Learn boxes are one-liners, and README still claims "no shared key number". None of this is a maths error; all are cheap data edits plus a rebuild.
