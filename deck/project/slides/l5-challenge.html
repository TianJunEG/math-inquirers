Challenge

1. Ali is 5 years older than Ben. In 3 years’ time their ages will add up to 25. How old is each now?

2. A snail climbs 3 cm each day and slides back 1 cm each night. It starts at the bottom of a 9 cm wall. On which day does it reach the top?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use as a pair challenge or in talk time. Ask for the reason every time. More are in the question bank. Answers: 1) Ben 7 and Ali 12. 2) Day 4 (3, 5, 7, then 9 on the fourth day before it can slide back).
