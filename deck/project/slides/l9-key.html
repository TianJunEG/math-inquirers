Key idea: equal units

Use equal units, no gaps, no overlaps, and start at zero.

A bigger unit gives a smaller number.

COMMON MISTAKES

• Leaving gaps or overlapping the cubes.

• Not starting at zero.

• Thinking more units means a bigger unit.
