Bigger unit, smaller count

1 clip is as long as 2 erasers.

A pen is 4 clips long. How many erasers long is the pen?

Why is the number of erasers bigger?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example 2. Ask children to explain each step in their own words. Equal units, no gaps or overlaps. A bigger unit gives a smaller number. 1 clip = 2 erasers means a 5-clip desk is 10 erasers. Answer: 8 erasers. Each eraser is shorter, so you need more of them.
