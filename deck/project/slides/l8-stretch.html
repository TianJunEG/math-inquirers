65–70 MIN

Stretch

1. In a race, Sam is ahead of Tia. Tia is ahead of Raj. Mei is ahead of Sam. Who is third?

2. Tia does not drink milk. Sam drinks neither milk nor juice. Raj drinks the third drink. The drinks are milk, juice and water. Who drinks what?

3. Numbers 1 to 12. Which are multiples of 2? Which are multiples of 3? Which are both?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Minutes 65 to 70. For children who finish early; take the whole group through one together if time allows. More are in the question bank. Answers: 1) Tia (order: Mei, Sam, Tia, Raj). 2) Sam: water. Tia: juice. Raj: milk. 3) Multiples of 2: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12. Multiples of 3: 3, 6, 9, 12. Both: 6 and 12.
