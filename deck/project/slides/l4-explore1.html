Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Build 1 triangle with 3 sticks. Count the sticks.
  2. Build 2 triangles in a row so they share a side. Count the sticks.
  3. Build 3 triangles. Count the sticks.
  4. Predict the 4th before you build it.

Picture: three rows of straws forming 1, 2 and 3 triangles joined along shared sides, with a sticks count under each.

Minutes 10 to 15. Pairs work with the materials: 21 matchsticks or straws per pair, shape cut-outs. Pairs build 1, 2 and 3 triangles in a row with sticks and count. Predict the 4th before building it. Online: Drawing on paper or screen; children use straws, toothpicks or pencils at home.
