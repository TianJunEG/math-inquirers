Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Take 20¢, 10¢ and 5¢ coins.
  2. Make 20¢ in as many ways as you can.
  3. Record each way on your whiteboard.
  4. Ask: have I got them all?

Picture: a table with the coins 20¢, 10¢ and 5¢ drawn as circles, and rows for each way to make 20¢.
