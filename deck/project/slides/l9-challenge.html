Challenge

1. Ali’s pencil is 9 cubes. Mei’s is 2 cubes shorter than Ali’s. Ben’s is 3 cubes longer than Mei’s. How long are all three pencils together?

2. A ribbon 30 cubes long is cut into three pieces. The first is 4 cubes longer than the second. The third is 4 cubes shorter than the second. How long is each piece?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Use as a pair challenge or in talk time. Ask for the reason every time. More are in the question bank. Answers: 1) 9 + 7 + 10 = 26 cubes. 2) 14, 10 and 6 cubes.
