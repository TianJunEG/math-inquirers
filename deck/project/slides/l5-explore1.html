Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Hide a secret number of counters under a cup.
  2. Add 5 more counters beside the cup. Tell your partner the total.
  3. Your partner works backwards to find the secret number.
  4. Lift the cup to check. Then swap roles.

Picture: a paper cup hiding some counters, with 5 loose counters beside it and a card that reads “12 altogether”.
