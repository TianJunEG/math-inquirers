Explore: what to do

EXPLORE

  1. Estimate: how many cubes long is your pencil?
  2. Measure with cubes: no gaps, no overlaps, start at zero.
  3. Measure a book and a shoe the same way.
  4. Then measure the book in hand spans and compare with your partner.

Picture: a pencil with a row of linking cubes laid end to end beneath it, starting exactly at the pencil’s tip with no gaps.
