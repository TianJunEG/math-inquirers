Venn diagram

Numbers 1 to 10. Rule 1: even. Rule 2: bigger than 5.

Middle (both): 6, 8, 10. Only even: 2, 4. Only bigger than 5: 7, 9. Outside: 1, 3, 5.

Why does 6 go in the middle?

Math Inquirers Holiday Program • Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Worked example 2. Ask children to explain each step in their own words. Model the grid: put a tick where it fits and a cross where it cannot. Each cross helps another cell. Draw the Venn for numbers 1 to 10. Answer: 6 is even AND bigger than 5.
