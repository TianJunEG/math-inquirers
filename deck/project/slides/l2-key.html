Key idea: bonds and equals

Bonds of 10 are the building blocks for bonds of 20.

“=” means both sides have the same value.

COMMON MISTAKES

• Reading "=" as "the answer comes next" (5 + 3 = 4 + __ answered 8).

• Counting 3+7 and 7+3 as different bonds when asked for different pairs.

• Magic triangle: trying numbers at random instead of looking at the corners.
