Otter’s practice: Digit detectives

Hello! Otter worked through this unit and sent the answers in. Here is a friendly picture of how it went. There are no scores or rankings here: every child learns at their own pace, and a mistake is simply a clue about what to look at next.

What went well

Otter had a go at every kind of question, which is a good start. The next part shows where to look together.

Worth looking at together

  • Place value to 1000
    • Said 6: gave the digit, not the value (6 tens = 60).
    • Said 505: changed only the tens digit and did not carry to the hundreds.
  • Comparing and ordering
    • Treated 85 as bigger than 508: looked at the first digit only, not at how many digits.

A good way to help: ask your child to explain how they got one of these answers, and listen for the idea behind it. Please do not worry about the wrong answer itself.

Left for later

  • What comes 1 row below 347 on the chart?
  • How many 3-digit numbers can you make from 2, 5, 8?
