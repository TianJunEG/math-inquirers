# Primary 3 school papers: wording, ranges and fit against the Level 3 term plan

Written 23 Oct 2026 for the Level 3 plan (`docs/terms/level_3_year_plan.md`). It is the Primary 3 twin of `docs/books/p2_school_paper_patterns.md`. **No school question text is reproduced.** Quoted phrases are 8 words or fewer. The verbatim questions are in the private, gitignored `docs/books/private_p3_papers/non_routine_questions.md`.

## 0. What was studied, and limits

- Source: the owner's Drive folder "P3 Math": 217 Primary 3 mathematics PDFs (plus one 47.7 MB Primary 3 science textbook, not opened because it is not a mathematics paper and is over the download limit). The papers hold **207 separate tests, quizzes and assessments with at least one recorded question, from 28 school labels** (about 25 schools: Nanyang, Rosyth, Tao Nan, Henry Park, ACS Junior and Primary, Nan Hua, Raffles Girls', Methodist Girls', Catholic High, Maha Bodhi, CHIJ St Nicholas, Red Swastika, SCGS, Pei Hwa, St Hilda's, Pei Chun, Maris Stella, Ai Tong, Paya Lebar Methodist Girls', Rulang, CHIJ Toa Payoh, St Joseph's Institution Junior, Northland, River Valley and two ACS-labelled items). Dated 2019 to 2025 and five undated "PastYr" copies.
- About 25 PDFs are re-labelled copies (one paper filed as 2020, 2021 and 2022; Methodist Girls' papers filed as 2022, 2024 and "PastYr"; Rosyth weighted assessments repeated between 2021, 2022 and 2024). They were read once and repeated questions are counted once.
- **How they were read.** The 2020 to 2025 papers through the Drive text layer (OCR), which keeps words and numbers but loses every picture. The 2019 papers had only watermarks and answer keys in the Drive text, so I read them from my own OCR of the rendered pages at 100 dpi; small print and some answer keys are doubtful. Picture conventions below are inferred from stems and labels.
- **The Primary 3 syllabus changed in 2023.** The 2021 syllabus reached Primary 3 in 2023 (MOE syllabus summary, p. 30). Papers dated 2019 to 2022 follow the older syllabus; 2023 to 2025 follow the current one. The question forms barely differ, but the 2024 and 2025 papers are shorter (see section 2). Counts by year are in the catalogue.
- I did not read school mark schemes; marks are the marks printed on the papers. Slots: Singapore Term 1 is Jan to Mar, Term 2 Mar to Jun, Term 3 Jun to Sep, Term 4 Sep to Nov. A paper's inclusion here says nothing about any child's results or any exam. Nothing here is a promise.

## 1. Headline findings that change the Level 3 plan

1. **"Times as many" is a Term 1 skill, not a Term 3 one.** About 178 recorded items use it, and Weighted Assessment 1 (March to May) already asks for stories such as "7 times as many" and "3 times as many" plus a difference. The plan puts the bar-model lesson on "times as many" in Term 3 (lesson 24) and only a two-step story in lesson 6. Give lessons 3 and 6 a Start here exposure and a Think harder "times as many with a difference".
2. **Offers and unit prices are everywhere.** "1 for $2, 3 for $5", "3 for $8", "buy 3 get 1 free", "3 stickers free for every 15 books", "5 stamps for every $3": about 45 items, usually asking for the least cost or the most you can get. The plan has price per piece (lesson 10) but not offers; add them to lessons 8, 10 and 37.
3. **"Least number of boxes" (round up) is a stock item**, about 38 items, set with divisors 4 to 9 ("34 donuts, at most 4 in a box", "79 tourists, 8 to a minibus"). The plan has only a short mention in lesson 4. Give it a Think harder item in lessons 4 and 32.
4. **Gaps and posts on a line are set in every term.** "10 trees at equal distances, the 1st to the 4th is 180 cm", "the 1st to the 6th lamp post is 30 m, how far to the 25th": about 71 items, 2019 to 2025. The plan holds this in lesson 33 (Term 4); move the line version to lesson 11 (Term 2) and keep the ring in 33.
5. **Combinations appear as whole tasks from 2025.** Tao Nan's Weighted Assessment 2 (2025) is three open tasks: ice-cream flavours and toppings (12), teams of 2 from 5 (10), 3 items from 5 (10). Nanyang set "how many ways" in four Term 1 papers (2021 to 2025). The plan places counting in Term 4 only; add a list-all-the-ways routine to a Term 2 warm-up.
6. **True, false or not possible to tell is a standard format**, 34 items from 2020 to 2025: statements about a bar graph, two grid figures, a set of ropes or heights, a receipt. "Not possible to tell" is the new part. Put it in lessons 4, 14, 16 and 29.
7. **Primary 3 papers do not name the tool.** I found no "Hint:" in any stem (Primary 2 papers had them), only "Draw a timeline" (Raffles Girls', 2025) and "Study the model below" (Maha Bodhi, 2019). A lesson may name the tool at Think harder but must not rely on the paper doing it.
8. **Time is to the minute, across noon and midnight, with watches that are fast or slow.** "A flight 23 45 to 04 10", "his watch was 20 minutes fast", "4 hours after 11.28 p.m.", "3 min 15 s": the 24-hour clock is routine from 2019 (written as 14 30 or 1430). The plan's lesson 9 covers it; add fast and slow watches (about 15 items) and a drawn timeline.
9. **Area and perimeter at Primary 3 includes figures made of identical rectangles or squares**, a wire bent into a rectangle and then a square, and "same perimeter, different area" statements. The plan treats composite area as Primary 4 (lesson 14); the papers ask for the perimeter of a rectilinear figure and the area of one tile in a figure made of five, which is Primary 3 reasoning. Keep lesson 14 and note this.
10. **Newer forms in 2024 and 2025.** Rulang set an error-analysis item (explain Bala's mistake), "what is the question if the answer is 139" and "what if there are 8 shelves" (WA2 and WA3, 2024); ACS (Junior) set 25-mark "bite-sized" assessments; Raffles allowed a calculator in Weighted Assessment 1 (2024); Ai Tong, Rosyth and Nan Hua call Term 1 and Term 2 papers "reviews" or non-weighted. Lesson 39 now carries posing and error analysis.

## 2. How the papers are built

| Feature | What the papers do |
|---|---|
| Length and marks | End-of-year: 80 marks in 2019 to 2023 (Booklets A, B, C; 1 h 30 to 1 h 45; Section A 25 to 32 marks, B 28 to 35, C 16 to 22). From 2024 most schools set 50 marks in 1 h 15 to 1 h 30 (A 10 to 17 marks, B 20 to 30, C 12 to 20); Catholic High, Tao Nan, Rulang and Maha Bodhi set 50. Weighted or term assessments: 20 to 30 marks in 40 to 50 minutes (Henry Park 25, Nanyang 20, Raffles 20 with a calculator allowed in 2024, Maha Bodhi 30, ACS Junior 25 in 2025). |
| Sections | A multiple choice, options numbered (1) to (4), answer shaded on the OAS or written in a bracket; B short answer, 1 or 2 marks (2 marks for most from 2023); C problem sums, 3 or 4 marks, usually (a) and (b) with (b) built on (a). Section C questions with parts are about 21 per cent of the recorded items. |
| Instructions | "Show your working clearly and write your answers in the spaces provided." "For questions which require units, give your answers in the units stated." "The number of marks available is shown in brackets [ ] at the end of each question." "Express your answer in its simplest form." "All diagrams are not drawn to scale." |
| Answer frames | "Ans:" with a unit after the line ("cm", "km m", "$", "p.m."); a mark in brackets [3] at the right; a "Working" column or "Do not write in this space" margin; word statements required in Henry Park, St Hilda's and Rulang ("write your word statements"). |
| Skill signposts | Rosyth and Pei Chun printed skills per question in earlier years; 2024 and 2025 papers print only marks. |
| Marks by type | 1 mark: place value, a table fact, a conversion, a name of a line. 2 marks: a one-step story, a two-step item, ordering, simplest form, a drawn line. 3 marks: a two-step story. 4 marks: a three-step story or a two-part item. 5 or 6 marks appear only in Tao Nan's open tasks (2025). |
| Time of year | Term 1 (WA1, Mar to May): numbers to 10 000, add and subtract, tables, remainders, simple stories (and often money). Term 2 (WA2 or semestral 1): multiplication and division, fractions, length, graphs. Term 3 (WA3 where set): angles, parallel and perpendicular lines, mass and volume. Term 4 (end-of-year): everything, with fractions, time, area and perimeter, graphs and a long set of problem sums. School order varies. |
| Dates | 2019 to 2023: times written 7.25 p.m. with full stops; 24-hour times as 14 30 (a space) or 1430. 2024 to 2025: "3:15 pm" with a colon and no full stops; the 24-hour clock still as 14 30. Litre is written l or L (the script ℓ appears in older papers). |

## 3. Per topic

Quoted phrases are 8 words or fewer.

### 3.1 Numbers to 10 000

- **Wording:** "Write eight thousand and fifteen in numerals"; "which digit is in the hundreds place"; "What is the value of the digit"; "Arrange the numbers in increasing order"; "Complete the number pattern"; "3 tens more than ... is 3120"; "7 thousands, 2 tens and 18 ones"; "Use all the digits to form"; "I am a 3-digit number"; "Each digit can only be used once"; "1000 more than".
- **Number ranges:** 1000 to 9999; patterns with steps 10, 20, 100, 200, 400, 1000 or two alternating steps; digit cards of four digits with a 0 (smallest or greatest odd or even, below 5000, between 800 and 900); a number line marked at equal intervals with one arrow; clue riddles with two or three conditions.
- **Pictures:** Base-ten pictures (thousands, tens, ones) in a few papers; number lines; coins and notes in place-value questions; otherwise text.
- **Marks:** 1 mark (2023 and earlier: 2 marks in multiple choice) for one-step place value; 2 marks for a digit-card or clue riddle.
- **Level 3 plan:** Lesson 1 matches; riddles and digit cards were not planned there (now Think harder). Alternating patterns: lessons 1 and 17.

### 3.2 Addition and subtraction to 4 digits

- **Wording:** "Find the sum of 3687 and 1329"; "Find the difference between"; "Subtract 1265 from 5892"; "What is the missing digit"; "What is the missing number in the box"; "After selling 328 stickers ... left"; "fewer ... than"; "altogether"; "Study the model below".
- **Number ranges:** Sums to 9999, differences from 9000 or 8000 (regrouping across zeros: 9000 - 5826), stories with 3- and 4-digit numbers, missing digits in column sums, equations such as 400 + [ ] = 57 x 8.
- **Pictures:** Vertical sums with boxes or letters for missing digits; a one-bar model with a question mark; a part-whole bar (Maha Bodhi 2019).
- **Marks:** 1 mark bare sums; 2 marks stories; 3 marks two-step; 4 marks with parts.
- **Level 3 plan:** Lessons 2, 5 and 6 match. Add "1000 less than" and "3 tens more than" place-value forms to lesson 1 and a missing-digit column to lesson 2.

### 3.3 Multiplication

- **Wording:** "Find the product of 312 and 6"; "Multiply 67 by 8"; "4 groups of 7"; "has the same value as"; "7 hundreds x 3"; "such boxes"; "3 times as many as"; "What is the missing number".
- **Number ranges:** 2- and 3-digit by 1-digit (608 x 7, 258 x 6, 243 x 8), tables 6 to 9 inside problems (9 x 7, 8 x 9), products of tens and hundreds (7 tens x 3, 4 x 625, 7 x 280), money times a digit, n groups of m statements.
- **Pictures:** Arrays and groups in a few papers; star patterns that grow; otherwise text.
- **Marks:** 1 mark (multiple choice), 2 marks for stories, 3 to 4 marks in Section C.
- **Level 3 plan:** Lesson 3 matches. "Has the same value as 4 groups of 7" and "7 hundreds x 3" cards are school items; add them at Start here.

### 3.4 Division and remainder

- **Wording:** "Find the quotient and remainder of"; "What is the remainder when"; "Divide 804 by 4"; "packed equally into boxes of"; "How many were left unpacked"; "What is the least number of boxes"; "When a number is divided by 4"; "can be divided exactly by".
- **Number ranges:** Dividends to 999 in questions and to 1250 in stories; divisors 2 to 9; quotients of 3 digits; riddles of the form "a number between 20 and 30 with remainder 5 when divided by 9".
- **Pictures:** Almost none; a few pack pictures.
- **Marks:** 1 to 2 marks; Section C 3 marks for "how many boxes" after a subtraction.
- **Level 3 plan:** Lesson 4 matches. Round up and none-left true or false need Think harder items; remainder riddles at Go further.

### 3.5 Fractions

- **Wording:** "Express ... in its simplest form"; "Arrange the fractions in order"; "What is the missing numerator"; "Which fraction is equivalent to"; "How many more parts must be shaded"; "What fraction of the figure is shaded"; "What is the missing fraction in the box"; "Find the sum of".
- **Number ranges:** Denominators 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 (9 and 7 rare); sums and differences of two related fractions within one whole; equivalent fractions with a missing top or bottom; ordering three fractions; one fraction of a cake or pizza left.
- **Pictures:** Figures of equal parts (rectangles, triangles, squares, circles); number line 0 to 1 with one arrow; a fraction strip.
- **Marks:** 1 to 2 marks.
- **Level 3 plan:** Lessons 7 and 27 match the content. "More parts to shade" and "missing fraction in a sum" are the extra forms.

### 3.6 Money

- **Wording:** "Express 1850¢ in dollars and cents"; "gave the cashier $50"; "How much change did he receive"; "Which two items did she buy"; "How much more money does she need"; "Count and write the amount"; "Special offer"; "at most".
- **Number ranges:** Decimal notation to $200 in everyday stories and to $3000 in large-number stories; change from $50 and $100; notes and coins counted to $60; menus and price lists of 4 to 5 items; offers.
- **Pictures:** Specimen notes ($2, $5, $10, $50, $100) and coins (5¢ to $1); price tags; a menu table; a bus-fare table.
- **Marks:** 1 mark to count; 2 marks for change or a pair of items; 3 to 4 marks as Section C.
- **Level 3 plan:** Lesson 8 matches; add offers (see section 1) and "which two items" with a total or a change.

### 3.7 Time

- **Wording:** "What time did he start"; "How long did ... take"; "Express your answer in the 24-hour clock"; "Write the time in 12-hour clock"; "Draw the minute hand"; "The watch was 20 minutes fast"; "How many minutes are there in"; "lasted".
- **Number ranges:** To the minute; durations to 6 h 45 min; across noon and midnight (23 45 to 04 10); 24-hour clock; seconds in 3 min 15 s; start from end and duration; two-stage journeys with a wait; a slow or fast clock or watch.
- **Pictures:** One or two analogue faces; a table of programmes; a timeline the pupil draws (2025); a calendar page rarely.
- **Marks:** 1 mark conversion; 2 marks duration or start; 3 to 4 marks two-stage.
- **Level 3 plan:** Lessons 9 and 35 match; add fast and slow watches and a drawn timeline.

### 3.8 Length, mass and volume

- **Wording:** "Express 8 km 60 m in metres"; "Write 3095 g in kilograms and grams"; "What is the mass of the watermelon"; "How much more water is needed"; "Give your answer in litres and millilitres"; "heavier than"; "Compare the masses".
- **Number ranges:** km and m to 10 km; kg and g to 9 kg (compound sums such as 4 kg 750 g); l and ml to 6 l; 1 l = 1000 ml in jugs ("2 L, 1280 ml"); equal shares of 300 ml into 4 glasses; scale readings.
- **Pictures:** Kitchen scale dials (0 to 5 kg and 0 to 1000 g), beakers marked 0 to 1000 ml, rulers under a pencil (not always from 0), balances with identical objects, a map with legs in metres.
- **Marks:** 1 mark to read or convert; 2 marks to compare or two-step; 3 to 4 marks as Section C.
- **Level 3 plan:** Lessons 11, 12 and 28 match. The balance with identical objects is a school form; the lesson-28 pouring puzzles are contest items.

### 3.9 Area and perimeter

- **Wording:** "Find the area of the figure"; "Find the perimeter of the figure"; "made up of 1-cm squares"; "made up of identical rectangles"; "Which two figures have the same perimeter"; "bent into a square".
- **Number ranges:** Sides to 50 cm and perimeters to 108 m; grid figures of 12 to 24 squares; rectilinear perimeter from labelled sides; rectangles from 2 to 5 identical parts; area of one square from a perimeter.
- **Pictures:** 1-cm grid figures with shaded squares; labelled composite figures; a square next to a rectangle.
- **Marks:** 1 to 2 marks for a figure on a grid; 3 to 4 marks for a bent wire or composite item.
- **Level 3 plan:** Lessons 13 and 14 match; composite figures made of identical parts are school Primary 3 and should stay.

### 3.10 Angles, perpendicular and parallel lines

- **Wording:** "How many acute angles are there"; "Name a pair of parallel lines"; "perpendicular to line AB"; "Draw a line parallel to AB"; "Which letter has both parallel and perpendicular lines"; "greater than a right angle"; "Mark the right angles".
- **Number ranges:** 3 to 8 marked angles per figure; grid figures with 6 to 9 lettered points; letter sets (H, A, M, L, E, T; L, O, V, E; N, H, V, T); drawing with a ruler and a set square.
- **Pictures:** Always a picture; grid lines; letters drawn on a grid; arrowheads for parallel lines in Henry Park and Tao Nan.
- **Marks:** 1 to 2 marks.
- **Level 3 plan:** Lessons 15 and 23 match; the "letters with both" item is a school form.

### 3.11 Graphs

- **Wording:** "The bar graph shows"; "How many more ... than"; "Each ... stands for"; "Complete the graph"; "twice the number of"; "The scale ... was torn off".
- **Number ranges:** Bar graphs with scales of 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 or 50; 4 to 6 bars; double bars (boys and girls); picture graphs with a key of 5, 6 or 10; totals to 450.
- **Pictures:** Horizontal and vertical bar graphs; a legend for two series; a missing bar to draw; a graph with the scale removed (Methodist Girls', Pei Hwa).
- **Marks:** 1 to 2 marks; Section C parts (a) to (d).
- **Level 3 plan:** Lesson 16 matches; add the removed scale, the missing bar and a picture key of 5 or 6.

### 3.12 Patterns and clue riddles

- **Wording:** "Complete the number pattern"; "How many circles are needed to make Figure 10"; "What is the missing number in the pattern"; "The numbers in each square are related".
- **Number ranges:** Sequences with steps 10 to 1000, alternating steps, doubling; growing pictures to Figure 10 or 20; number squares with a hidden rule.
- **Pictures:** Figure sequences of circles, sticks or beads; squares of four numbers.
- **Marks:** 1 to 2 marks; 4 marks with a table.
- **Level 3 plan:** Lessons 17, 22 and 26 match.

### 3.13 Word-problem forms

- **Wording:** "fewer ... than"; "3 times as many"; "After he gave"; "in the end"; "The rest"; "at first"; "so that they have the same".
- **Number ranges:** Comparison with a difference, "times as many", sum and difference together, give-and-get before and after, two-step purchases; 3- and 4-digit numbers, money to $200.
- **Pictures:** Almost none; the papers do not draw models (two items mention one).
- **Marks:** 2 marks in Section B; 3 or 4 marks in Section C.
- **Level 3 plan:** Lessons 6, 24, 25 and 10 hold these; see the catalogue for how the forms are counted.

## 7. Paper-based wording changes for child pages

- **Section instructions:** "Choose the correct answer and write its number in the bracket"; options labelled (1) to (4), never A to D.
- **Frames:** "Ans:" with the unit after the line; the mark in brackets [3] at the right; "Show your working clearly"; a "Working" box.
- **Comparison words:** "more than", "fewer than", "heavier than", "lighter than", "times as many", "greater", "smaller".
- **Questions about number:** "Find the sum of", "Find the difference between", "Find the product of", "What is the quotient when", "What is the remainder when", "Arrange ... Begin with the smallest".
- **Time:** 12-hour times as 3:15 pm with a colon (accept 3.15 p.m.); 24-hour times as 14 30 with a space (accept 1430).
- **Money:** $3.20 and 1850¢; "gave the cashier"; "How much change did he receive?".
- **Units:** km, m, cm; kg, g; L or l, ml; "in the units stated".
- **True or false:** "Each statement below is either true, false or not possible to tell. Put a tick in the correct column."
- **Graphs:** "Each ... stands for 5"; "The number of ... is not shown".

## 8. What was not checked

- Pictures in every file (all text is OCR). Clock hands, graph bars, coin pictures and figure layouts are inferred from stems and answer keys.
- The 2019 papers were read from my own 100 dpi OCR; small print and some answer keys are doubtful, and I did not re-read every 2019 page by eye.
- School mark schemes, and whether a school's reproduction of specimen notes may be imitated (D1 stays open).
- The science textbook in the folder (47.7 MB, not a mathematics paper).
- Papers from other schools, other years or other folders; counts describe this set only.
