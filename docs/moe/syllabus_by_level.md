## Primary 1 (pp. 31-32)

**NA, Whole Numbers (p. 31)**
- Numbers to 100 (1.1 to 1.7): count a set; tens and ones; read and write in numerals and words; compare sets (1.4); compare and order numbers; number-sequence patterns (1.6); ordinals "first, second, up to tenth" with 1st, 2nd, 3rd (1.7).
- Addition and subtraction (2.1 to 2.7): concepts, signs + - =, inverse relationship, adding more than two 1-digit numbers, within 100, algorithms, mental calculation within 20, 2-digit plus ones without renaming, 2-digit plus tens.
- Multiplication and division (3.1 to 3.4): concepts, sign x, multiplying within 40, dividing within 20.

**NA, Money (p. 31):** count money in cents up to $1 and dollars up to $100.

**MG, Measurement (p. 32):** length in cm (1.1 to 1.4: measure, abbreviation, compare and order, measure and draw a line to the nearest cm). Time (2.1 to 2.4): tell time to 5 minutes, am and pm, h and min, durations of one hour or half hour.

**MG, Geometry (p. 32):** 2D shapes: identify, name, describe and classify rectangle, square, triangle, circle, half circle, quarter circle (1.1); form figures from shapes (1.2); identify the shapes in a figure (1.3); copy figures on dot or square grid (1.4).

**ST (p. 32):** read and interpret picture graphs.


**Outcome wording as printed in the PDF (added 23 Oct 2026 from pp. 31-32 of the October 2025 edition; the tags in school/p1 use these words):**
- Whole Numbers 1.1: counting to tell the number of objects in a given set

- Whole Numbers 1.3: reading and writing numbers in numerals and in words
- Whole Numbers 1.4: comparing the number of objects in two or more sets
- Whole Numbers 1.5: comparing and ordering numbers
- Whole Numbers 1.6: patterns in number sequences
- Whole Numbers 1.7: ordinal numbers (first, second, up to tenth) and symbols (1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.)
- Addition and Subtraction 2.1: concepts of addition and subtraction
- Addition and Subtraction 2.2: use of +, - and =
- Addition and Subtraction 2.3: relationship between addition and subtraction
- Addition and Subtraction 2.4: adding more than two 1-digit numbers
- Addition and Subtraction 2.5: adding and subtracting within 100
- Addition and Subtraction 2.6: adding and subtracting using algorithms
- Addition and Subtraction 2.7: mental calculation involving addition and subtraction (within 20; 2-digit number and ones without renaming; 2-digit number and tens)
- Multiplication and Division 3.1: concepts of multiplication and division
- Multiplication and Division 3.2: use of x
- Multiplication and Division 3.3: multiplying within 40
- Multiplication and Division 3.4: dividing within 20
- Money 1.1: counting amount of money (in cents up to $1; in dollars up to $100)
- Length 1.1: measuring length in centimetres
- Length 1.2: use of abbreviation cm
- Length 1.3: comparing and ordering lengths in cm
- Length 1.4: measuring and drawing a line segment to the nearest cm
- Time 2.1: telling time to 5 minutes
- Time 2.2: use of 'am' and 'pm'
- Time 2.3: use of abbreviations h and min
- Time 2.4: duration of one hour/half hour
- 2D Shapes 1.1: identifying, naming, describing and classifying 2D shapes (rectangle, square, triangle, circle, half circle, quarter circle)
- 2D Shapes 1.2: forming different 2D figures with rectangle, square, triangle, half circle, quarter circle
- 2D Shapes 1.3: identifying the 2D shapes that make up a given figure

- Picture Graphs 1.1: reading and interpreting data from picture graphs
- Problem solving (p. 13): problem-solving competency is the central focus of the curriculum; general problem-solving strategies and heuristics (guess and check, work backwards, draw a model, make a list or table, look for a pattern). School-test question types that are not one numbered outcome are tagged "problem solving".

## Primary 2 (pp. 33-34)

**NA, Whole Numbers (p. 33):** numbers to 1000 (count in tens and hundreds, place value to hundreds, words, compare and order, patterns, odd and even numbers 1.6). Add and subtract with algorithms to 3 digits; mental calculation with a 3-digit number and ones, tens or hundreds. Multiplication tables of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10; the sign ÷; x and ÷ as related; mental calculation within those tables.

**NA, Fractions (p. 33):** fraction as part of a whole (1.1), notation (1.2), compare and order unit fractions and like fractions with denominators up to 12 (1.3); add and subtract like fractions within one whole (2.1).

**NA, Money (p. 33):** dollars and cents, decimal notation, compare two or three amounts, convert between decimal notation and cents only.

**MG, Measurement (p. 34):** length in m, mass in kg and g, volume of liquid in litres, abbreviations, compare and order. Time to the minute, hours and minutes, convert hours and minutes to minutes and back.

**MG, Geometry (p. 34):** make and complete patterns with 2D shapes by size, shape, colour or orientation (1.1). 3D shapes: identify, name, describe, classify cube, cuboid, cone, cylinder, sphere.

**ST (p. 34):** picture graphs with scales.

## Primary 3 (pp. 35-36)

**NA, Whole Numbers (p. 35):** numbers to 10 000 (count in hundreds and thousands, place value, words, compare, patterns). Algorithms for add and subtract to 4 digits; mental calculation of two 2-digit numbers. Tables of 6, 7, 8, 9; division with remainder (3.3); multiplication and division algorithms up to 3 digits by 1 digit (3.4); mental calculation within the tables.

**NA, Fractions (p. 35):** equivalent fractions, simplest form, compare and order unlike fractions (denominators up to 12), equivalent fraction given a new denominator or numerator (1.1 to 1.4); add and subtract two related fractions within one whole (2.1).

**NA, Money (p. 35):** add and subtract money in decimal notation.

**MG, Measurement (p. 36):** length in km, volume in ml, compound units and conversions (km and m, m and cm, kg and g, l and ml, "numbers ... within easy manipulation"). Time: seconds, find start, finish or duration, 24-hour clock (2.1 to 2.3).

**MG, Area and Volume (p. 36), "Area and Perimeter":** concepts; area in square units, cm2 and m2 (no conversion between them); perimeter of rectilinear figure, rectangle, square; area of rectangle and square.

**MG, Geometry (p. 36):** angles: concept, right angle, greater or smaller than a right angle. Perpendicular and parallel lines: identify and draw.

**ST (p. 36):** bar graphs, including different scales on the axis.

## Primary 4 (pp. 37-40)

**NA, Whole Numbers (p. 37):**
- Numbers to 100 000 (1.1 to 1.6): place value, words, compare, patterns, rounding to the nearest 10, 100 or 1000 (1.5), the sign ≈ (1.6).
- Factors and multiples (2.1 to 2.5): relationship; test whether a 1-digit number is a factor of a number within 100; common factors of two numbers; test whether a number is a multiple of a 1-digit number; common multiples of two 1-digit numbers.
- Four operations (3.1, 3.2): multiplication algorithm up to 4 digits by 1 digit and up to 3 digits by 2 digits; division algorithm up to 4 digits by 1 digit.

**NA, Fractions (p. 37):** mixed numbers and improper fractions and their relationship (1.1); fraction as part of a set (2.1); add and subtract fractions with denominators not above 12 and "not more than two different denominators" (3.1).

**NA, Decimals (p. 38):** decimals to 3 places (notation, place value, compare, decimal-fraction conversion both ways when the denominator is a factor of 10 or 100, rounding to whole number, 1 and 2 decimal places); add and subtract decimals to 2 places; multiply and divide decimals (to 2 places) by a 1-digit whole number; divide a whole number by a whole number with a decimal quotient (3.2); round answers to a stated accuracy (3.3).

**MG, Area and Perimeter (p. 39):** find a missing side of a rectangle from its area or perimeter; the side of a square from its area or perimeter; area and perimeter of composite figures made of rectangles and squares.

**MG, Geometry (p. 39):**
- Angles: notation ∠ABC and ∠a, measure in degrees, draw an angle of given size.
- Rectangle and square: properties "excluding diagonal properties"; drawing them.
- Line symmetry: identify symmetric figures, decide if a line is a line of symmetry, complete a symmetric figure on a square grid.
- Nets: identify 2D representations of cube, cuboid, cone, cylinder, prism, pyramid; draw 2D representations of cube, cuboid, prism, pyramid; identify nets of cube, cuboid, prism, pyramid; identify the solid a net makes.

**ST (p. 40):** complete a table from data; read and interpret tables, line graphs and pie charts.

## Primary 5, Standard (pp. 41-42)

**NA, Whole Numbers (p. 41):** read and write numbers to 10 million; multiply and divide by 10, 100, 1000 and their multiples without a calculator; order of operations without a calculator (2.2); brackets without a calculator (2.3).

**NA, Fractions (p. 41):** fraction and division: divide a whole number by a whole number with a fraction quotient (1.1), express fractions as decimals (1.2). Four operations: add and subtract mixed numbers (2.1); multiply a proper or improper fraction by a whole number (2.2); multiply a proper fraction by a proper or improper fraction (2.3); multiply two improper fractions (2.4); multiply a mixed number by a whole number (2.5). No fraction division at P5.

**NA, Decimals (p. 41):** multiply and divide decimals (to 3 places) by 10, 100, 1000 and multiples (1.1); convert a measure to a larger or smaller unit in decimal form (km and m, m and cm, kg and g, l and ml) (1.2).

**NA, Percentage (p. 41):** a part of a whole as a percentage; the sign %; a percentage part of a whole; "finding discount, GST and annual interest" (1.4).

**NA, Rate (p. 41):** rate as the amount of one quantity per unit of another; find the rate, the total or the number of units from the other two. (The word "speed" is never used.)

**MG, Area and Volume (p. 42):**
- Area of triangle: concepts of base and height, area, composite figures of rectangles, squares and triangles.
- Volume of cube and cuboid: build with unit cubes (2.1), cubic units cm3 and m3 (no conversion between them), draw cubes and cuboids on an isometric grid (2.3), volume of a cube or cuboid (2.4), volume of liquid in a rectangular tank (2.5), relationship of litre or ml with cm3 (2.6).

**MG, Geometry (p. 42):** angles on a straight line, angles at a point, vertically opposite angles, finding unknown angles (1.1 to 1.4). Triangle: properties of isosceles, equilateral and right-angled triangles; angle sum of a triangle; unknown angles "without additional construction of lines". Parallelogram, rhombus, trapezium: their properties and unknown angles.

**ST:** no Statistics strand in Standard P5.

## Primary 6, Standard (pp. 43-44)

**NA, Fractions (p. 43):** divide a proper fraction by a whole number (1.1); divide a whole number or a proper fraction by a proper fraction (1.2), both without a calculator.

**NA, Percentage (p. 43):** find the whole given a part and the percentage (1.1); percentage increase or decrease (1.2).

**NA, Ratio (p. 43):** a:b and a:b:c with whole numbers ("excluding ratios involving fractions and decimals"), equivalent ratios, divide a quantity in a ratio, simplest form, ratio of two or three quantities, missing term in equivalent ratios, relationship between fraction and ratio (1.1 to 1.7).

**NA, Algebra (p. 43):** a letter for an unknown number (1.1); notation of simple expressions such as a ± 3, a × 3 or 3a, a ÷ 3 or a/3 (1.2); simplify simple linear expressions "excluding brackets" (1.3); evaluate by substitution (1.4); "simple linear equations involving whole number coefficient only" (1.5).

**MG, Area and Volume (p. 44):** area and circumference of a circle; area and perimeter of a semicircle and quarter circle; composite figures of square, rectangle, triangle, semicircle, quarter circle. Volume: find a missing dimension of a cuboid from its volume and the other dimensions, the edge of a cube from its volume, the height of a cuboid from volume and base area, the area of a face from volume and one dimension; use of the square-root and cube-root signs (2.5).

**MG, Geometry (p. 44):** unknown angles, without additional construction, in composite figures involving square, rectangle, triangle, parallelogram, rhombus, trapezium.

**ST (p. 44):** average as total value ÷ number of data; the relationship between average, total and number of data.

## Foundation track, P5 and P6 (pp. 45-50)

The Foundation syllabus revisits P1-P4 material (factors and multiples, equivalent fractions, decimal operations, area and perimeter, angle measurement, rectangle and square properties, tables, bar and line graphs at P5; rate, percentage, average, pie charts at P6). It adds nothing that is not in Standard. Our levels are mapped to the Standard track; the Foundation pages (pp. 45-50) were read only to confirm that nothing new appears there.
