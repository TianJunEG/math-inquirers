# Holiday program readiness (Levels 1 to 6), as of 23 Oct 2026

Question from the owner: "Is the holiday program all ready for all 6 levels?" Scope: the 5-week, 10-lesson, 90-minute live online program, beta from Tue 24 Nov 2026 (32 days away). Read-only audit of branch `claude/great-fermi-1hv4vc`; nothing was pushed and no lesson, term/, school/ or tool file was edited.

Evidence used: file listings and git dates; `pdfinfo` page counts of every workbook, print PDF, tutor guide and weekly pack; deck file counts per lesson; `docs/audit/` (audit, fixes, fixpass, re-audit, rebudget, timing, journey, gifted, close_gaps for each level); `website/dist` plus the live site at https://math-inquirers.pypteltd.workers.dev/ (fetched today); `website/data/beta_schedule.json` compared with `docs/schedule`, `portal/supabase/*.sql` and the live Supabase `groups` table; portal README; a Python date, weekday, overlap, break and lunch check of all 60 session dates; the portal Node test (10/10 pass) and `gen_groups_seed.py --check` (ok). I rendered and looked at a Level 6 workbook page, Level 2 workbook kit pages and print pages, and a Level 4 slide. All audit scores are desk estimates by a model; no child has done any of this material and no lesson has been run against a stopwatch.

Key: **R** Ready, **M** Minor gaps, **N** Not ready.

## 1. Verdict in one paragraph

**No, not yet.** The teaching material is in good shape for all six levels: 60 lessons, 60 decks, 6 designed workbooks with print-ready PDFs, 6 tutor guides, 6 parent packs, all audited and fixed at least twice, and every lesson has a 90-minute budget that sums to 90. The schedule is consistent across the JSON, the live sign-up page and the live Supabase `groups` rows. What is not ready is everything that turns the material into a running business: the terms and privacy pages are unreviewed drafts, the site contradicts itself on whether a printed workbook is posted, no printer is chosen, the portal and online payment (now Stripe, replacing HitPay on 23 Oct) are not live, the published slide decks are the old versions, the promised recordings and online practice do not exist yet, and the video platform and tutor routine are undecided. All of that is fixable in four weeks if the owner makes four decisions this week (see section 5). Teaching content risk is low; launch-operations risk is high.
