## 3. Reading load

Judgement: **12 words is right for 8 and 9 year olds; 18 is too long.** plan.md contradicts itself: its table (line 308) gives 12 for Primary 3, and line 248 gives 18 for check wording at P3 to P4. Reasonable reading of both: 12 for instruction and clue sentences, up to 18 only in the check strip. The builder's "18" came from the brief; in practice the pages are already mostly short. Sentences over 12 words (numerals not counted, units counted):
- P3-02 "Go to the box with the words for the number at the foot of your box." 16 words.
- P3-40 and P3-39 strip "Parts in the same place on the left and on the right have the same colour." 16 words (strip, within 18).
- P3-34 "Is each angle smaller than, equal to or greater than a right angle?" 13 words.

Everything else is 11 words or fewer. Moving the rule to 12 costs about six edits.

## 4. 1-bit photocopy legibility

Method: all 40 pages at 200 dpi greyscale, then two proxies: a "dark copier" (blur then threshold keeps strokes, thickens them) and an extreme "light copier" (blur then threshold 105, drops strokes thinner than about 0.35 mm). Also the builder's validated 150 dpi 1-bit render (max ink loss 13.3 and 12.4 percent). Every drawn page viewed at zoom in both.
- Beakers P3-21: liquid level is a 0.9 mm line and survives the light proxy; the hatching (0.3 mm every 1.5 mm, the build report says 3 mm) disappears in the light proxy but is then not needed; in the dark proxy item 4 (450 mℓ) becomes a dense stripe field but the level line stays clear. Scale numbers read. The tiny "mℓ" under each beaker is lost (the unit beside the box is large). OK.
- Dials P3-23: needle and 100 g ticks survive in both. **Labels 800, 900 and 1000 touch or overprint the dial ring on items 1, 2 and 6 (and "1000" on 6 sits on the ring)**: readable at 100 dpi, crowded in 1-bit. Items 3 to 5 clean. See M3.
- Stopwatch P3-26 and clocks P3-27: hands, ticks and numerals read; small clocks are about 33 mm drawings; 5-minute ticks heavier than the rest. OK.
- Grids P3-30 (unit lines 0.35 mm, 8 mm squares): clean in both. P3-33: figures are drawn from the same 0.35 mm unit lines; the outline is 0.8 mm. In the light proxy the interior lines turn dotted, which matters for rows 1 and 3 (area counting). The default 150 dpi 1-bit render is fine. Recommend one real copier test (a light machine).
- P3-31 labelled figures: labels 8 pt bold, clear. Pairs of labels stacked beside the notch ("4 m / 4 m", "4 cm / 4 cm") are close together; every side is labelled, so adding all labels always works.
- Angles P3-34, lines P3-35, P3-36 grid: clear. Bar graphs P3-37 and 38: bars solid; minor gridlines are 0.22 mm (0.62 pt, above the 0.57 pt rule) and vanish in the light proxy but the 10-step lines stay.
- P3-20 coins and notes: coin "cents" is set at about 8 pt (word 7.3 mm wide), readable in the dark proxy; the teacher notes already warn about a light setting.
- P3-15 shading: white separator between shaded cells about 0.7 mm; shaded cells still separate in both proxies (the P2 M2 failure is fixed).
- Flat grey: none found (validator counts text edges only; per-page 8 to 10 edge blocks). All pages black and white.
- Answer boxes: all value boxes 34.1 x 13.5 mm (43.1 x 13.5 for fractions on P3-08, 19), colour-page boxes 12 to 13 mm; counted squares 8 mm; discs 9 mm. The six letter boxes in the check strip measure 10.9 mm square (the 12 mm rule is for value boxes; one capital fits easily): L7.
