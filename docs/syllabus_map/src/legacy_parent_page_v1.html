Syllabus map by level

Compares each of the 60 Math Inquirers lessons with the primary curriculum of 12 places.

Each cell compares the lesson's topic with the grade where that place first teaches it, measured against the year the program level is named after. Level N is compared with year N.

Bands differ: China and Korea set content by two-year bands, and the Ontario, US, Australia and India mapping used three-year bands, so “same year band” is more generous there than for Hong Kong or England. A dot in a cell corner means the placement was inferred or could not be confirmed in a document the mapper opened.

This is a desk comparison, not a claim of alignment with, or endorsement by, any ministry or curriculum. Challenge Day and Showcase lessons are mixed reviews and are not mapped.

Cells counted: — same band, — taught earlier locally, — taught later locally (the program is ahead), — with no matching topic.
