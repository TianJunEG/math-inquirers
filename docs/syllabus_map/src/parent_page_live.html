Singapore: compared with the MOE 2021 Primary Mathematics syllabus (updated October 2025), lesson by lesson. Math Inquirers is independent enrichment. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by MOE or the Gifted Education Programme. Where the syllabus names a topic in the same year, the lesson is marked as taught at school; harder questions inside a lesson can still go further (see the tier map).

Math Inquirers is an enrichment program. Lessons use puzzles and explaining, not school-style practice, so “goes a little further” means the topic is usually met at school a year or more later.

Level 1 is for ages 6 to 8, Level 2 for 7 to 9, Level 3 for 8 to 10, Level 4 for 9 to 11, Level 5 for 10 to 12 and Level 6 for 11 to 13. Each lesson box shows its number and a mark: ✓ Same, ▲ Later, – Not listed, ~ Mixed. Hover or tab to a lesson for details. The tenth lesson of each level is a mixed review and is not compared.
