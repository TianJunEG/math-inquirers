How the three tiers fit the syllabus

Every lesson has three tiers of questions. Start here is the core idea. Think harder is the same idea in an unfamiliar setting. Go further is the challenge: mastery, then extension. For each level, this grid shows how each tier sits against the school year the level is named after.

How to read it. Level 1 is compared with Primary 1, Level 2 with Primary 2, and so on. Singapore is compared with the MOE 2021 Primary Mathematics syllabus (updated October 2025).

How firm each row is. Levels 1 to 3: the lesson-by-lesson audit lists, for each lesson, which Think harder and Go further items go beyond the syllabus, so these rows are well supported. Levels 4 and 5: the audit lists the items that look ahead, but gives no verdict for a whole tier, and several year placements could not be confirmed. Level 6: thin. The audit compared whole lessons with Primary 6 and Secondary 1 and did not separate the tiers, so its cells are a best reading, not a finding.

Wording counts only what the audit found; a tier marked “at school level” can still hold the odd item that goes beyond, and a tier marked “reaches further” is mostly built from school-level ideas. These are desk comparisons, not a claim of alignment with any ministry.
