How Math Inquirers fits school maths

Choose where your child goes to school. Each level card shows how its ten lessons compare with what that school system teaches at the same age.

Your child's school system

Math Inquirers is an enrichment program. Lessons use puzzles and explaining, not school-style practice, so “goes a little further” means the topic is usually met at school a year or more later.

Level 1 is for ages 6 to 8, Level 2 for 7 to 9, Level 3 for 8 to 10, Level 4 for 9 to 11, Level 5 for 10 to 12 and Level 6 for 11 to 13. Hover or tab to a lesson for details. The tenth lesson of each level is a mixed review and is not compared.

This is our own comparison, made from public curriculum documents, and some placements could not be confirmed. It is not approved or endorsed by any education ministry.
