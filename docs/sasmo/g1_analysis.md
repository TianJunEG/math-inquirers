# SASMO Grade 1 contest papers: what they test and how Level 1 prepares for it

Analysis only. No lesson files were changed. Contest problems are not reproduced here: they are referred to by paper and question number (for example 23-12 is 2023, Question 12) and by a short skill tag. Question text and answers are in the git-ignored private notes.

## 1. What was analysed, and how far to trust it

- Seven files, but **four distinct papers (2021, 2022, 2023, 2024) = 100 problems**. The "SASMO 22 Paper 2" file is the 2022 paper re-typeset and called a mock; the 2023 paper appears three times (two loose copies and inside a scan); the 2022 paper appears in the scan, the mock and a printed Grade 1 practice book. That book (63 pp, 2021 and 2022 with full solutions) is Grade 1 and added no new papers. No Grade 2 or other-grade material was used.
- Format each year: 25 questions, 90 minutes. Section A = Q1 to 15, multiple choice, 2 marks, minus 1 for a wrong answer (60 problems). Section B = Q16 to 25, whole-number answers, 4 marks, working must be shown (40 problems).
- 2021 stems were read from the practice book. Ten 2024 problems and a few others depend on pictures that I could not solve with confidence. Categories are my single-coder judgement; a problem is filed under its main skill only, so counts are indicative, not exact. Four papers is a small sample; a topic with 1 to 3 problems is "occasionally seen", not "tested".
- The contest is not the school syllabus. It uses 3- and 4-digit numbers, multiplication and clock arithmetic that the Primary 1 syllabus handles later or lightly. Level 1 teaches "Primary 1 plus thinking", so some gaps below are by design.

Difficulty band as the paper presents it: Section A is mostly fluency (12 of 13 computation items) and perception (all 14 visual and 3D items), plus short patterns, time and logic. Section B is where reasoning lives: all 7 bar-model problems, all 4 symbol equations, all 3 constraint grids and 4 of 5 counting-ways problems are Section B. Roughly 11 of the 100 problems need multiplication facts (every symbol-equation problem uses a times sign). Difficulty also rises within a section; the last two or three questions are hard even for strong Grade 1 children.

## 3. How Level 1 maps to it

Level 1 lessons: L1 skip counting and rules; L2 number bonds and "="; L3 picture graphs; L4 repeating and growing patterns; L5 working backwards, more/fewer; L6 coin combinations; L7 2D shapes and counting; L8 logic and sorting; L9 length (cubes, cm); L10 mixed puzzles. Tiers: Start here (school level), Think harder (new setting), Go further (past the syllabus).

| Type | Level 1 prepares it | Start here / Think harder | Go further would need | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Place value, digit puzzles | Odd/even in L1, L8 only. Searches of the lesson and bank text find no tens-and-ones work and no 3-digit numbers | Nothing | A whole new strand: tens and ones, digit cards, numbers to 1000 | **Real gap, largest** |
| Computation, number sentences | L2 bonds to 10 and 20, and "= means the same as" (exit ticket 20 = 13 + __) | Bonds, balance equations | Adding four 2-digit numbers by making tens; compensating (38 + 25 = 36 + 27). The programme audit already notes 2-digit add and subtract is never taught | Partial; 2-digit work is a gap |
| Sequences and rules | L1 constant-step rules; L4 growing stick patterns (+2); two shared counts | Rules, tables | Gaps that grow (+1, +2, +3), sum of the previous two, two rules interleaved | Prepared for constant steps, **extension needed** |
| Visual perception, 3D | Nothing. L9 uses cubes only as a measuring unit | Nothing | Short routine: spot the difference, shadows, stacks of cubes, folding and punching | **Real gap**, but partly a practice effect and hard to teach in words |
| Time, calendar | None (the programme audit says Level 1 never touches time) | Nothing | Days of the week cycle, "later than" by chunks, half past | **Real gap** |
| Ordering, logic | L8 orders from clues, logic grid, Venn | Orders of 4 | Circular seating, "fits inside" chains | Well prepared |
| Bar-model word problems | L5 more/fewer, sum and difference (Q3), working back; Go further has "Ali and Ben 20 marbles, Ali 4 more" | Two-step stories | "Times as many", ages "in 3 years", transfers between two groups | Prepared; extension for multiplicative and age forms |
| Counting figures | L7 counts squares and triangles by size; Go further grids | Yes | Dots and bricks counted by structure | Well prepared |
| Counting ways | L6 coin ways, L2 pairs, L7 shape counts | Yes | Numbers from digit cards; routes with two stages; handshakes | Prepared; extension light |
| Repeating patterns | L4 core, prediction by multiples | Yes | Cycles with a missing core part | Well prepared |
| Symbol equations | L5 number machines, undoing; L2 magic triangle | Missing numbers | Substituting one symbol for another; subtract equations; times facts | **Gap** (small share but all 4 marks each) |

| Multiplication facts | Skip counting in 2, 3, 5 (L1) and shared multiples | Shared multiples | Tables of 3 to 8; equal groups with remainders | **Gap**, but Primary 1 syllabus does include it |
| Money | L6 | Yes | None | Over-prepared (1 of 100) |
| Picture graphs (L3) | n/a | n/a | n/a | SASMO Grade 1 never tests them in these papers. L3 is justified by the syllabus, not by SASMO |

## 4. Gaps in order of size

1. Place value and digit puzzles (14 of 100; Section B too).
2. Two-digit mental arithmetic and multi-term sums (13; mostly easy marks in Section A, so a child who cannot do it loses cheap points).
3. Sequences with changing steps (10).
4. Visual perception and 3D (14 combined, all Section A).
5. Time and calendar (7).
6. Symbol equations plus multiplication facts (about 11 problems touch multiplication).

## 5. Recommendations

- Do not market Level 1 as SASMO preparation until at least gaps 1 to 3 are closed. It is honest to say it builds the reasoning habits the contest rewards (patterns, logic, counting, working backwards).
- Treat these as optional Go further items, not changes to Start here. Suggested homes: place value and digit cards beside L2; changing-step sequences in L1 and L4; time and calendar as a short extension in L9; symbol equations in L5; spatial routine as a warm-up in L7.
- Original practice drafts for gaps 1 to 6 are in `docs/sasmo/g1_original_problems_draft.md` (draft, not yet in the program). Check them with a tutor before use.
- Before building anything big, get one more year's paper and a few real child attempts: the evidence here is four papers and my categorisation.
