## 1. Role
1.1 The Tutor teaches Math Inquirers online lessons (90 minutes, Google Meet) to the groups the Company assigns, using only the Company's approved lesson slides, scripts, tutor guide, workbooks and tutor console.
1.2 The Tutor prepares before each lesson, starts on time, ends on time, and follows the part timings in the lesson budget. Where a lesson does not work, the Tutor records it on the observation sheet or tutor notes and tells the owner; the Tutor does not rewrite lessons for children without approval.
1.3 The Tutor keeps private notes (`ops/out/tutor_notes_sheet.pdf`) and helps write each child's end-of-program report. Reports and notes contain no scores, marks or rankings, and no child is compared with another.
1.4 Schedule: [days and groups, e.g. one tutor for Levels 1 to 6, 12 sessions a week from 24 Nov 2026]. Absence, illness or a clash is reported to the owner as early as possible, and at least [48 hours] ahead where it can be foreseen, so a cover tutor or reschedule can be arranged.

## 2. Confidentiality
2.1 The Tutor keeps confidential, during and after the engagement: children's and families' names, contact details, addresses, photos, work and progress notes; lesson recordings; the Company's slides, scripts, answers, guides, question banks, workbooks, prices, plans and customer lists; and the tutor console and any login.
2.2 The Tutor uses this information only to do the job, shares it with nobody outside the Company (including other families), and does not discuss one family's child with another family.
2.3 Answer keys, speaker notes and the tutor phone remote are never shown to children or on the shared screen.
2.4 These duties survive termination for [2 years; lawyer to confirm], and for personal data without time limit.

## 3. Child safeguarding
The Tutor must follow these rules at all times. Breaking one is a serious breach (clause 12).
3.1 **No private contact.** No private chat, direct message, call, video call, email, social media friend or follow, or gift exchange with a child. All contact with a family goes through the Company's WhatsApp Business number (+65 9880 5962) with the parent, about the program only.
3.2 **No one-to-one sessions** with a child unless the owner has agreed in writing and a parent is present on screen throughout. Make-up is by recording or a later class, not a private lesson.
3.3 **Private paper.** A child's working on their own paper is private. The Tutor never asks a child to hold paper up to the camera, never asks to see paper on screen, and never takes a photo or screen capture of a child's work.
3.4 **Stuck children.** The child marks the 'I am still stuck' box on their own look-back page. The parent photographs it and sends it to the business WhatsApp after the lesson; the Tutor replies to the parent only. Tutors never use a personal phone, personal WhatsApp or personal email for this.
3.5 **Cameras.** Children may keep cameras off and stay muted without question. The Tutor does not ask a child to turn a camera on, does not ask about the child's home or surroundings, and ends or reports the session if something concerning appears on screen.
3.6 **Recording.** Lessons are recorded by the Company's Meet account only: the Tutor, the shared screen and anyone's unmuted voice are recorded; children's cameras and names are not. The Tutor never makes their own recording, screenshot, audio or photo of a lesson or a child, and tells the Company if one is made by anyone else. The Company's WhatsApp number is not shown on recorded slides.
3.7 **Chat.** In-meeting chat is limited to the Tutor and set so children cannot send private messages; the Tutor does not reply to private messages.
3.8 **Conduct in the room.** Kind, calm and encouraging language only: no shaming, sarcasm, comparison or labels ('weak', 'slow'), no discussion of religion, politics, appearance or family finances, and no physical or personal questions. Praise effort and reasoning, not 'cleverness'. No marks, scores or ranking are shown to children.
3.9 **Concerns.** If the Tutor sees or hears anything suggesting a child may be at risk, or a rule was broken, the Tutor tells the owner **the same day** by phone or in person, writes only the facts, and does not investigate, confront or promise secrecy to the child. The owner decides next steps, including reporting to the proper authority. [Owner and lawyer: add named safeguarding lead and reporting route.]

## 4. Waiting room, links and equipment
4.1 The Tutor admits only enrolled children (and a parent where present) from the Company's list, and removes anyone else. Meet links are not posted publicly.
4.2 The Tutor teaches from a quiet, private room with a neutral background, uses a laptop (lesson screen) and a phone (remote) approved by the Company, and shares the lesson window only.
4.3 Equipment is [tutor's own / Company's]; the Company reimburses [nothing / agreed items].

## 5. Intellectual property
5.1 All lesson slides, scripts, workbooks, answer keys, question banks, puzzles, diagrams, Mi the dolphin and other branding, recordings, the tutor console, templates, and any improvement or new material the Tutor writes, edits or suggests for the program (together "Materials") belong to the Company or its licensors. The Tutor assigns to the Company all rights in anything created for the Company in the course of the work, and waives moral rights where permitted. [Lawyer: confirm wording, and treatment if contractor.]
5.2 The Tutor may use the Materials only to teach the Company's classes. No copying, posting, sharing, selling or teaching them elsewhere, and no use for the Tutor's own private tuition.
5.3 The Tutor does not copy school or contest questions into Materials, and does not use another provider's material in a Company lesson.
5.4 On termination the Tutor returns or deletes all Materials, notes and data (clause 6.5).

## 6. Data protection (PDPA)
6.1 The Tutor handles personal data only as the Company instructs and only as needed for the lessons, reports and notes, following the Company's privacy notice (`website/privacy.html`).
6.2 Notes and reports are kept in the Company's approved storage or locked folder, not on a personal phone, personal cloud, personal email or messaging app.
6.3 The Tutor uses only the Company-approved accounts (Meet, Drive, WhatsApp Business, tutor console), with a strong password and two-step verification, and does not share logins.
6.4 The Tutor reports any loss, theft or suspected breach of data (lost device, wrong message, misdirected file) to the owner immediately, and no later than [the same day].
6.5 At the end of each program and on termination, the Tutor deletes working copies and follows the retention table (recordings 30 days after the group's last lesson; delivery addresses 30 days after delivery).
6.6 The Tutor does not use children's data or photos for marketing, social media or any other purpose.

## 7. Conduct
7.1 Professional, punctual and prepared; dressed appropriately on camera; no alcohol or drugs while teaching; no smoking or eating on camera except water.
7.2 No recommending outside tutors or products to families, and no soliciting families to leave the Company or to hire the Tutor privately, during the engagement and for [6 months; lawyer to confirm] afterwards.
7.3 No statements about Math Inquirers that the Company has not approved: no claims of MOE, GEP or SASMO approval or results, and no promises of marks or admission. The program is independent enrichment.
7.4 No discussion of fees, refunds or complaints with families: the Tutor refers those to the owner.
7.5 Declare any conflict of interest, for example a child the Tutor already teaches privately.
