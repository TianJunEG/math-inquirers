## Sending rules
- Business number only; WhatsApp Business labels: Enquiry, Confirmed, Paid, Workbook posted, Active, Finished.
- One family per message unless it is a group announcement (templates 11 and 13 may go to a broadcast list so families do not see each other's numbers; do not use a WhatsApp group with all parents).
- Never send a child's photo, work or name to another family.
- Update the dates before sending; Level 5 and Level 6 start on different days (27 Nov and 26 Nov).
