# Primary 4 early-finisher puzzles: two semester booklets (plan)

Branch `claude/great-fermi-1hv4vc`. Pipeline: `school/p4` (copied from `school/p2`). Build report: `docs/school_books/p4_semester_booklets_build_report.md`. Outcome wording: `docs/school_books/p4_outcome_wording.md`.

## 0. Decisions this plan follows

- `docs/school_books/plan.md` and the owner decisions: two booklets per level by typical term (Semester 1 = Terms 1 and 2, Semester 2 = Terms 3 and 4), 20 single-sided A4 pupil pages each, 10 one-star and 10 two-star per booklet, black and white and photocopy-ready, an annual school licence, DRAFT stamps on the front matter, the publisher line "Preciouslyours Pte Ltd, trading as Math Inquirers", the registered office and contact email of the P2 front matter.
- The teacher notes say that schools vary: the pages follow the typical order, so a teacher uses any page whenever its topic has been taught.
- No Ministry of Education or SASMO affiliation or alignment claim. The only wording is the fact "The mathematics syllabus is published by the Ministry of Education, Singapore." (licence page) and, on the cover only, "Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Ministry of Education, Singapore."
- The teacher notes say "Pictures are not actual size."
- All puzzles are original. The private sources `docs/books/private_*` and `docs/sasmo/private_*` were used only to check similarity (section 6), never to write a page.
- Outcome wording comes from the Primary Mathematics Syllabus P1 to P6 PDF (2021 edition, updated Oct 2025) in the owner's uploads. Only the Primary 4 pages (printed pages 37 to 40) were read for this level. The 42 outcome lines are copied into `p4_outcome_wording.md`.
- Primary 5 content is excluded: percentage, ratio, average, area of a triangle, volume of a cube or cuboid, and any decimal or fraction work beyond the Primary 4 limits (decimals to 3 places and operations to 2 places, fractions with denominators up to 12 and at most two different denominators). Conventions: a gap in 5-digit numbers (45 280) and none in 4-digit numbers (4280); decimals without trailing zeros; fractions in simplest form; the sign ≈ and the angle sign are drawn as pictures because the fonts lack them.

### Primary 5 content left out

Percentage; ratio; average; area of a triangle; volume of a cube or cuboid; fractions with unlike denominators beyond two denominators; decimals beyond 3 places; division of decimals by a decimal; the four operations on fractions beyond addition and subtraction. Cube nets are Primary 4 and are kept; surface area is not used.

## 3. Ranges and wording

Sentences of at most 14 words, at most 4 instruction sentences, a check strip of at most 18 words. Numbers to 100 000 (a thin gap in 5-digit numbers). Decimals to 3 places. Fractions with denominators up to 12 (and 20, 25, 50 and 100, which are factors of 100, only for converting to decimals, DP1.3 and DP1.4). Shapes use cm and cm², paths and gardens m and m². Names in stories come from a short mixed list.

## 4. Mechanics for self-checking (the answer must be shown wrong, not shown right)

The strip at the foot of each page tells the pupil how to check and never gives the answer. Code words: the letters of the answers, read in the order asked, spell a word of 6 letters with distinct letters, only one arrangement of which is a word, and no single wrong placement spells another word (tested against a list of about 241 000 words). Ordering and matching pages are tested with every permutation. Routes use an induced path with dead-end boxes that also follow the rule, so the route is unique and a shortcut has the wrong number of boxes. Colour by code uses the shared library vendored in `school/p4/cbc_vendor` (five colours, a tally, and twin marks so matching parts share a colour).

## 5. Lessons carried from the P1 to P3 audits

Reading limits; no category hint in the strip; one star = one skill, two star = two steps or two clues; no coin or note art is needed at Primary 4; every page needs 12 mm answer boxes (9 mm on colour pages); a page must be solved from the rendered image, not the key (this found a real bug: see the build report).

## 6. Verification per page

For every page: a seeded generator; an independent solver that reads the printed text or drawing (not the generator's data); enumeration of the wrong answers (every single change of one answer and every wrong arrangement, at least 95% rejected); the word test; one key entry; a viewed render in colour and as a 1-bit photocopy simulation; and a hand-solve from the image. A similarity check was run against `docs/books/private_*` and `docs/sasmo/private_*` (gitignored): no page shares a 7-word run with any private entry, and none repeats a private scenario with the same numbers (details in the build report).
