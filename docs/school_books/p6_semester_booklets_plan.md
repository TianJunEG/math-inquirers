# Primary 6 semester booklets: plan and gap analysis (DRAFT)

Two booklets, Semester 1 (Terms 1 and 2, S1-01 to S1-20) and Semester 2 (Terms 3 and 4, S2-01 to S2-20). Each has 20 single-sided A4 pages (10 one-star, 10 two-star), black and white, photocopy-ready, each with a code word, route, matching, ordering, domino-loop, lettered-choice or colour-by-code check. Pipeline `school/p6`. Footer codes print both numbers, for example `S1-07 (P6-07)`.

## Source (updated after the audit)

The syllabus PDF (Primary Mathematics Syllabus 2021, updated October 2025) was supplied to the session on 23 Oct 2026 and the Primary 6 pages were read: Primary 6 Standard pp. 43 and 44, Primary 6 Foundation pp. 49 and 50, and the Primary 4 and 5 pages for placing topics. The PDF is treated as data. Only the outcome lines are used, in their wording (`docs/school_books/p6_outcome_wording.md`); the numbers on the key are the PDF's. The earlier build used our own summary, `docs/moe/syllabus_by_level.md`; its Primary 6 numbering was checked and is right. In particular the **Ratio numbering 1.1 to 1.7 is verified**: 1.1 notation of a:b and a:b:c, 1.2 equivalent ratios, 1.3 dividing a quantity in a given ratio, 1.4 simplest form, 1.5 ratio of two or three quantities, 1.6 missing term, 1.7 relationship between fraction and ratio. The strands are named in the PDF Area and Circumference of Circle 1.1 to 1.3 (our ids AV), Volume of Cube and Cuboid 2.1 to 2.5 (VO), Special Quadrilaterals 1.1 (GE) and Average of a Set of Data 1.1 and 1.2 (ST); the key prints them as "Area and Volume 1.1", "Geometry 1.1", "Statistics 1.1". The only syllabus statement on the printed pages is the factual "The mathematics syllabus is published by the Ministry of Education, Singapore." The cover says "Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Ministry of Education, Singapore." No alignment claim is made.

## Honest gaps: what a printed page cannot check

- Drawing, constructing and measuring outcomes cannot be checked by a code word. None is attempted.
- Explaining or justifying in words cannot be checked; pages check the answer only.
- Mental calculation and fluency under time cannot be checked.
- A pupil can reach a right answer by a wrong method. The check shows that something is wrong; it does not say which step.
- Two wrong answers at once were not all tested. The angle figures are now deliberately not drawn to scale and say so, so measuring does not give the answer.

## Primary 7 content excluded

Negative numbers, prime numbers, speed, probability, coordinates, simultaneous equations, expanding or factorising, brackets with letters, surface area, trigonometry, median and mode, HCF and LCM problems, ratios with fractions or decimals. Notation is held to a : b with whole numbers, one letter, no brackets, and the letter x is avoided.
