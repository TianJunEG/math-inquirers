# Primary 6 semester booklets: plan and gap analysis (DRAFT)

Two booklets, Semester 1 (Terms 1 and 2, pages S1-01 to S1-20) and Semester 2 (Terms 3 and 4, S2-01 to S2-20). Each has 20 single-sided A4 pages (10 one-star, 10 two-star), black and white, photocopy-ready, with a code word, route, matching, ordering, domino-loop, lettered-choice or colour-by-code check. Pipeline: `school/p6`. Footer codes print both numbers, for example `S1-07 (P6-07)`.

## Source and its limit

The Ministry of Education syllabus PDF is **not in this repository**. The outcome wording used comes only from our own summary in `docs/moe/syllabus_by_level.md` (Primary 6 Standard track, pages 43 and 44 of the 2021 edition, updated October 2025), restated in our own words. Wording beyond that summary cannot be copied or checked here. Outcome numbers are used where the summary gives them (Fractions 1.1 and 1.2, Percentage 1.1 and 1.2, Ratio 1.1 to 1.7, Algebra 1.1 to 1.5, Volume 2.5); the Ratio order 1.1 to 1.7 follows the order of the summary's list and must be checked against the PDF. Lines with no number in the summary carry ids of our own (AV, VO, GE, ST). The only syllabus statement on the printed pages is the factual "The mathematics syllabus is published by the Ministry of Education, Singapore." The cover says "Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Ministry of Education, Singapore." No alignment claim is made.

## Honest gaps: what a printed page cannot check

- Drawing, constructing and measuring outcomes (drawing angles, practical measuring) cannot be checked by a code word. None is attempted.
- Explaining or justifying in words (for example why an expression simplifies) cannot be checked; pages check the answer only.
- Mental calculation and fluency under time cannot be checked, and a calculator is allowed by the teacher.
- A pupil can reach a right answer by a wrong method. The check shows that something is wrong; it does not say which step.
- Angle work: the figures are drawn accurately so a pupil could measure; the page says "Do not measure". The check cannot stop this.
- Two wrong answers that cancel each other were not all tested (see the build report).

## Primary 7 content excluded

Negative numbers, prime numbers and factors beyond the P5 level, speed, probability, coordinates, simultaneous equations, expanding or factorising, brackets with letters, surface area, trigonometry, median and mode, HCF and LCM problems, ratios with fractions or decimals. Notation is held to a:b with whole numbers, one letter, no brackets, and the letter x is avoided.
