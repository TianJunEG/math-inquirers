# Primary 4 outcome lines: wording copied from the syllabus PDF

Source: Primary Mathematics Syllabus P1 to P6 (2021 edition, updated Oct 2025), Primary 4, printed pages 37 to 40, read from the PDF in the owner's uploads. The wording is copied from the PDF text. Where the PDF lists bullets under a line (multiplication 3.1, rounding decimals 1.5, nets 4.1 to 4.3 and similar), the bullets are joined in brackets or with commas and semicolons. Used by `school/p4/validate.py` (each outcome id of each page must have its wording here) and by `school/p4/gen/outcomes.py`.

This file is only a record of the syllabus wording. It makes no claim about any school or approval.

Problem solving is not a numbered outcome line: it is a process that runs across all the topics.


## Numbers up to 100 000

- NU1.1: number notation, representations and place values (ten thousands, thousands, hundreds, tens, ones)
- NU1.2: reading and writing numbers in numerals and in words
- NU1.3: comparing and ordering numbers
- NU1.4: patterns in number sequences
- NU1.5: rounding numbers to the nearest 10, 100 or 1000
- NU1.6: use of ≈

## Factors and Multiples

- FM2.1: factors, multiples and their relationship
- FM2.2: determining if a 1-digit number is a factor of a given number within 100
- FM2.3: finding the common factors of two given numbers
- FM2.4: determining if a number is a multiple of a given 1-digit number
- FM2.5: finding the common multiples of two given 1-digit numbers

## Four Operations

- FO3.1: multiplication algorithm (up to 4 digits by 1 digit; up to 3 digits by 2 digits)
- FO3.2: division algorithm (up to 4 digits by 1 digit)

## Mixed Numbers and Improper Fractions

- MI1.1: mixed numbers, improper fractions and their relationship

## Fraction of a Set

- FS2.1: fraction as part of a set

## Fractions: Addition and Subtraction

- FA3.1: adding and subtracting fractions with denominators of given fractions not exceeding 12 and not more than two different denominators

## Decimals up to 3 decimal places

- DP1.1: notation, representations and place values (tenths, hundredths, thousandths)
- DP1.2: comparing and ordering decimals
- DP1.3: expressing decimals as fractions
- DP1.4: expressing fractions as decimals when the denominator is a factor of 10 or 100
- DP1.5: rounding decimals to the nearest whole number, 1 decimal place, 2 decimal places

## Decimals: Addition and Subtraction

- DA2.1: adding and subtracting decimals (up to 2 decimal places)

## Decimals: Multiplication and Division

- DM3.1: multiplying and dividing decimals (up to 2 decimal places) by a 1-digit whole number
- DM3.2: dividing a whole number by a whole number with quotient as a decimal
- DM3.3: rounding answers to a specified degree of accuracy

## Area and Perimeter

- AP1.1: finding one dimension of a rectangle given the other dimension and its area/perimeter
- AP1.2: finding the length of one side of a square given its area/perimeter
- AP1.3: finding the area and perimeter of composite figures made up of rectangles and squares

## Angles

- AN1.1: using notation such as ∠ABC and ∠a to name angles
- AN1.2: measuring angles in degrees
- AN1.3: drawing an angle of given size

## Rectangle and Square

- RS2.1: properties of rectangle and square, excluding diagonal properties
- RS2.2: drawing rectangles and squares

## Line Symmetry

- LS3.1: identifying symmetric figures
- LS3.2: determining whether a straight line is a line of symmetry of a symmetric figure
- LS3.3: completing a symmetric figure with respect to a given line of symmetry on square grid

## Nets

- NT4.1: identifying 2D representations of cube, cuboid, cone, cylinder, prism, pyramid
- NT4.2: drawing 2D representations of cube, cuboid, prism, pyramid
- NT4.3: identifying the nets of 3D solids (cube, cuboid, prism, pyramid)
- NT4.4: identifying the solid which can be formed by a given net

## Tables, Line Graphs and Pie Charts

- ST1.1: completing a table from given data
- ST1.2: reading and interpreting data from tables/line graphs/pie charts
