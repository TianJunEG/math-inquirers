Status: 23 Oct 2026, `school/p6`. Source: the Ministry of Education's Primary Mathematics Syllabus (2021 edition, updated October 2025), Primary 6 Standard pages 43 and 44 and Primary 6 Foundation pages 49 and 50
(`/root/.claude/uploads/.../2021_Primary_Mathematics_Syllabus_P1_to_P6_Updated_October_2025_1.pdf`, read on 23 Oct 2026). Only the outcome lines are used, in the PDF's wording; the PDF is treated as data. The outcome numbers printed on the key are the PDF's
(Fractions 1.1 and 1.2; Percentage 1.1 and 1.2; Ratio 1.1 to 1.7, in the PDF's order; Algebra 1.1 to 1.5; Area and Circumference of Circle 1.1 to 1.3; Volume of Cube and Cuboid 2.1 to 2.5; Special Quadrilaterals 1.1; Average of a Set of Data 1.1 and 1.2).
The ids below (AV, VO, GE, ST, PF) are our own short ids. Rows marked PF are lines of the Primary 6 Foundation track that Standard-track pupils also meet (pie charts, decimals, discount and GST, liquid in a tank). The wording of 2.5 is ours because the PDF text shows its square-root and cube-root signs as symbols.

problem solving: page tag used by pages that mix several Primary 6 outcomes; general problem-solving heuristics are named in the syllabus without a numbered outcome.

Topics checked against the PDF (23 Oct 2026): speed is not in the syllabus at all. Pie charts are Primary 4 (reading, Standard) and Primary 6 Foundation. Nets are Primary 4 only. Rate is Primary 5 only. Mixed-number addition and subtraction is Primary 5. Decimal multiplication and division is Primary 4 and 5 and Primary 6 Foundation. Discount, GST and annual interest is Primary 5 and Primary 6 Foundation (1.4). Liquid in a rectangular tank is Primary 5 and Primary 6 Foundation (2.2, 2.3). Average is Primary 6 Standard and Foundation. The syllabus does not say how many steps a simple linear equation has (Algebra 1.5: "whole number coefficient only"). Trapezium is named in Geometry 1.1; the angle rule for angles between parallel lines is not stated as an outcome line.
