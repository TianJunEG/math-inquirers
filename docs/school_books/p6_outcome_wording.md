# Primary 6 outcome wording used by the school puzzle booklets (summary wording)

Status: 23 Oct 2026, built for `school/p6`. **This is a SUMMARY in our own words, not the text of the syllabus.** The Ministry of Education's syllabus PDF is not in this repository, so
no wording beyond `docs/moe/syllabus_by_level.md` (our own summary of pages 43 and 44 of the 2021 edition, updated Oct 2025) can be copied or checked here. Where that summary gives an outcome number
(Fractions 1.1 and 1.2; Percentage 1.1 and 1.2; Ratio 1.1 to 1.7; Algebra 1.1 to 1.5; Volume 2.5) the id below uses it. The summary lists the seven Ratio lines in one list, so the order 1.1 to 1.7 follows that list and
must be checked against the PDF. Lines whose number the summary does not give (circles and composite figures, the other volume lines, angles, average) carry ids of our own (AV, VO, GE, ST). The wording is not to be quoted as the syllabus's own.
The lines marked `RV` are Primary 4 and Primary 5 lines that Primary 6 pupils meet again in revision (the page tag begins with Revisits).
