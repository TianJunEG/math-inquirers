## What was built

Two booklets of 20 single-sided A4 pupil pages (`school/p3/out/P3_S1_Puzzles.pdf`, `P3_S2_Puzzles.pdf`), front matter of 5 pages each (cover with DRAFT stamp, publisher line and the not-affiliated sentence; annual licence page with registered office; two pages of teacher notes; contents with outcome coverage), complete packs of 25 pages, and teacher answer keys (`P3_S1_Answer_Key.pdf`, `P3_S2_Answer_Key.pdf`). Each booklet is 10 one-star and 10 two-star pages. Footers print both numbers, for example `S1-07 (P3-07)` and `S2-07 (P3-27)`. The page table (code, title, outcomes, stars, mechanism) is section 2 of the plan and section 2 of `school/p3/README.md`.

Every page: a generator with its own seed; an independent solver that reads the PRINTED text or drawing and not the generator's data (bar heights against the printed axis, clock hand end points, beaker and dial needles, the squares of a figure, printed side lengths, the arms of an angle, the end points of lines and the lettered grid, fractions by value, route cells by their printed expressions); the wrong-answer enumeration in `validate.py` (every single slip, every wrong arrangement on the ordering pages, every other way of choosing on the lettered-choice pages, at 95% or better); the word test (every code word has exactly one real-word arrangement, no single letter in another slot makes a word, no swap makes a word, tested against a **262 542-word list**: the 129 000 words of P1 and P2 plus the 400 000 most frequent English words of `wordfreq`); a key entry; a viewed render in colour and as a 1-bit copy.

Mechanism variety (the P2 audits said 23 of 40 pages were code-word pages): 19 code-word pages, 8 lettered-choice pages (patterns, true or false, equal fractions, the 24-hour clock, start and finish, same or different, angles, lines), 3 ordering pages, 5 routes (words and numbers, mental pairs, tables, simplest form, equal measures), 1 loop (fact families), 3 colour-by-code pages and 1 draw-and-read-off page (Draw the Lines).

## Weak points (honest list)

1. **Mechanism repetition is reduced, not removed.** 19 of 40 pages are still a code table of numbers; the pupil sees "look it up in the code table" often in Semester 1 (9 of 20) and Semester 2 (10 of 20).
2. **Lettered-choice pages are guessable per row** (one in three, one in two on the true-or-false page): a pupil can guess a row. The word test makes a wrong set of letters spell no word, but a pupil who guesses has no feedback until the word fails. The check strip says to read the six letters in order; it does not say which row is wrong.
3. **Draw the Lines (P3-36) does not see the drawing.** The page sees the letter where the line meets the edge. A pupil who draws a wrong line that happens to end on the right letter passes; a pupil who reads the letter without drawing also passes. Perpendicular and parallel lines 2.2 is covered in part only, and the teacher notes say so.
4. **Practical measuring is only read from drawings** (beakers P3-21, dials P3-23, stopwatch P3-26): the outcome lines on measuring length in km and time in seconds are met by conversions, compound sums and a stopwatch face; no kilometre is measured.
5. **Small drawn scales.** Beaker marks are about 3.5 mm apart (a 100 mℓ step on a 1000 mℓ scale, a 50 mℓ step on a 500 mℓ scale); dial marks every 50 g are about 3 mm apart on the ring; clock faces are about 23 mm across the ring (the drawing is 33 mm). Viewed in 1-bit at 150 dpi they read, but a pupil trial should confirm them for 8 and 9 year olds. The hatching that shows the liquid is now a 0.5 mm line every 2.4 mm (not a tint), with the level line 1.4 mm, and was viewed in colour and 1-bit.
6. **P3-30 and P3-33 (area and perimeter on unit squares)** need careful counting of edges at 8 mm squares; the perimeters are the hardest numbers to hand-count (see the hand-solve note).
7. **Right angles on P3-34 are rotated** so a right angle is not always a horizontal and a vertical line; the notes say to use the corner of a sheet of paper. Angles are never within 25 degrees of 90 degrees, apart from the right angles themselves.
8. **P3-29 Time Detective** answers are a mix of four-digit times (0805) and minutes in one code table; a time with a leading zero must be written with the zero (the table prints "0805 h"). No journey crosses midnight (changed after the audit).
9. **Colour-by-code pages.** The vendored library prints the question forms of the new bank at 4.2 mm type; with 12 mm answer boxes the parts are crowded (the P2 audits asked for larger boxes; the P3 pages use 12 mm squares, so some question wording had to stay short). The library prints "ℓ" and "mℓ" from a fallback font. A pencil copy writes Y, R, B, G or O in the part, which does not fit beside a 12 mm box on every small part: the oval and circle parts are tight (viewed).
10. **Number notation.** Four-digit numbers are written without a space (4132), as the school papers do; 10 000 would be written with a space but appears only in a topic line. The owner may prefer the space in every four-digit number (decision for the owner).
11. **Reading load.** Owner decision (23 Oct 2026): 12 words a sentence for instructions, stems and clues, up to 18 in the check strip (plan.md's own table and its line 248); `validate.py` enforces both. The brief's 18 no longer applies to instructions.
12. **Blank boxes.** As in P1 and P2, a box left empty gives no letter and the check cannot see it; the notes say so.
13. **No real photocopier test, no pupil trial.**
14. **Another process commits the working tree as "Checkpoint" commits**, so some commits may contain my files earlier than my own commit.

## Differences from the brief and decisions for the owner

- **Page numbers.** P3-01 to P3-20 are Semester 1 and P3-21 to P3-40 Semester 2, in booklet order (P2 used a separate build order). A footer reads `S2-07 (P3-27)`; in Semester 1 both numbers agree (`S1-07 (P3-07)`).
- **Decision: where the 24-hour clock is written.** Times in the 24-hour clock are printed with four digits and `h` (1535 h, 0805 h), as in the Primary 3 school papers (14 30 or 1430 are also seen); the owner may prefer "15 35".
- **Decision: litre sign.** The litre is written ℓ and the millilitre mℓ, as in the P2 booklets and the syllabus summary; school papers also write l, L and ml.
- **Decision: fractions and money in Semester 1.** Fractions (P3-15 to P3-19) and one money page sit in Semester 1; some schools teach fractions again in Term 4 and money in Term 2; the notes say to hand out any page when its topic has been taught (P2-FRACTIONS-KEEP precedent).
- **Decision: Time 2.2 is Primary 3.** P3-28 and P3-29 find a start, a finish or a duration (this was removed from P2 by P2-REMOVE-P3).
- **Decision: three colour pages with the vendored library.** The colour-by-code library has no Primary 3 bank. A bank file `banks_p3.py` (tables 6 to 9, conversions, money in decimal notation) was written in the vendored copy `school/p3/cbc_vendor/` and four small library changes were made there (below). If the owner wants them in the shared library, the coordinator has to merge them; they were NOT made in `school/colour_by_code`.
- **What I wanted from the shared library (not done there):** (1) `qcore.py`: a money blank written `$□` (the equation evaluator removes `$(v)`), and a guard for division by a blank (`ZeroDivisionError` returns false); (2) `textcheck.py`: the units km, ℓ, mℓ, ml with their factors, and a rule for "Has $a. Spends $b. Left: $□"; (3) `banks_p3.py`; (4) a larger answer box (12 mm) as a build option instead of the module constant `L.BOX_SIDE`.
- **Honest gaps** (plan, section 1): writing number words, real measuring, the idea of an angle beyond comparison, the pupil's own drawing, mental calculation as a method, kilometres as a measured length, cm to m.
- **Coins and notes** (one page, P3-20 Money Sums): original art at the real proportions of P2 (`gen/art2.py`: coins 0.80 of real diameter, notes 2:1 growing with value, "Pictures are not actual size." and "Real $2 notes are purple, $5 green, $10 red." in the teacher notes). The real-image variant stays held back pending the lawyer; nothing was changed for it.
