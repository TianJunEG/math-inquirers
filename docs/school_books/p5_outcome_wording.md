# Primary 5 outcome lines used by the school booklets (our own summary, NOT the syllabus wording)

Source: `docs/moe/syllabus_by_level.md`, section "Primary 5, Standard (pp. 41-42)", which is this repository's own summary of the Primary Mathematics Syllabus P1 to P6 (2021 edition, footer "Updated: Oct 2025"). **The syllabus PDF itself is not in the repository**, so the exact wording of the outcome lines could not be copied. Each line below is a short rewording of that summary. Outcome numbers are printed only where the summary prints them (Whole Numbers 2.2 and 2.3; Fractions 1.1 to 2.5; Decimals 1.1 and 1.2; Percentage 1.4; Volume 2.1 to 2.6; Geometry 1.1 to 1.4); other lines carry no number. Used by `school/p5/validate.py` (each outcome id of each page must have its line here) and by `school/p5/gen/outcomes.py`.

This file only records what the pages practise. It makes no claim about any school or approval.

Not in the Primary 5 Standard syllabus summary (so not used): a Statistics strand (pie charts and tables are Primary 4; average is Primary 6), ratio (Primary 6), fraction division (Primary 6), finding the whole from a percentage and percentage change (Primary 6), algebra (Primary 6), circles (Primary 6), finding a missing dimension of a cuboid from its volume (Primary 6), speed, prime numbers, negative numbers, probability, coordinates, surface area.


## Whole Numbers

- Whole Numbers: numbers to 10 million: reading and writing numbers up to 10 million
- Whole Numbers: multiply and divide by 10, 100, 1000 and multiples: multiplying and dividing by 10, 100, 1000 and their multiples, without a calculator
- Whole Numbers 2.2: order of operations, without a calculator
- Whole Numbers 2.3: use of brackets, without a calculator

## Fractions

- Fractions 1.1: dividing a whole number by a whole number, with a fraction as the quotient
- Fractions 1.2: expressing fractions as decimals
- Fractions 2.1: adding and subtracting mixed numbers
- Fractions 2.2: multiplying a proper or improper fraction by a whole number
- Fractions 2.3: multiplying a proper fraction by a proper or improper fraction
- Fractions 2.4: multiplying two improper fractions
- Fractions 2.5: multiplying a mixed number by a whole number

## Decimals

- Decimals 1.1: multiplying and dividing decimals (up to 3 decimal places) by 10, 100, 1000 and their multiples
- Decimals 1.2: converting a measure to a larger or smaller unit in decimal form (km and m, m and cm, kg and g, ℓ and mℓ)

## Percentage

- Percentage: a part of a whole as a percentage: expressing a part of a whole as a percentage, and the sign %
- Percentage: a percentage part of a whole: finding a percentage part of a whole
- Percentage 1.4: finding discount, GST and annual interest

## Rate

- Rate: rate as the amount of one quantity per unit of another quantity; finding the rate, the total or the number of units

## Area and Volume

- Area of triangle: base, height and area: area of a triangle (base and height)
- Area of triangle: composite figures: area of composite figures made of rectangles, squares and triangles
- Volume 2.1: building cubes and cuboids with unit cubes
- Volume: cubic units (cm³, m³): cubic units cm³ and m³
- Volume 2.3: drawing cubes and cuboids on an isometric grid
- Volume 2.4: volume of a cube or cuboid
- Volume 2.5: volume of liquid in a rectangular tank
- Volume 2.6: the relationship between ℓ or mℓ and cm³

## Geometry

- Geometry 1.1: angles on a straight line
- Geometry 1.2: angles at a point
- Geometry 1.3: vertically opposite angles
- Geometry 1.4: finding unknown angles
- Geometry: properties of triangles: properties of isosceles, equilateral and right-angled triangles
- Geometry: angle sum of a triangle: angle sum of a triangle; unknown angles of a triangle
- Geometry: parallelogram, rhombus, trapezium: properties of a parallelogram, rhombus and trapezium; their unknown angles

## Problem solving

- Problem solving: problem-solving competency and general heuristics (guess and check, work backwards, draw a model, make a list or table, look for a pattern); pages that practise these through stories are tagged problem solving in the key (syllabus summary, p. 13).
