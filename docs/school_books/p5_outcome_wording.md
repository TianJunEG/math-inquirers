# Primary 5 outcome lines used by the school booklets (wording copied from the syllabus)

Source: Primary Mathematics Syllabus P1 to P6 (2021 edition, footer "Updated: Oct 2025"), Primary 5 Standard, pages 41 and 42. Only the outcome-line wording and numbers are copied, as the syllabus prints them (the PDF numbers Whole Numbers 1.1 and 2.1 to 2.3, Fractions 1.1 to 2.5, Decimals 1.1 and 1.2, Percentage 1.1 to 1.4, Rate 1.1 and 1.2, Area of Triangle 1.1 to 1.3, Volume 2.1 to 2.6, Geometry 1.1 to 1.4, 2.1 to 2.3, 3.1 and 3.2). Each key entry in the booklets adds our own wording. Used by `school/p5/validate.py` (each outcome id of each page must have its line here) and by `school/p5/gen/outcomes.py`.

This file only records what the pages practise. It makes no claim about any school or approval.

Not in the Primary 5 Standard syllabus (so not used): a Statistics strand (pie charts and tables are Primary 4; average is Primary 6), ratio (Primary 6), fraction division (Primary 6), finding the whole from a percentage and percentage change (Primary 6), algebra (Primary 6), circles (Primary 6), finding a missing dimension of a cuboid from its volume (Primary 6), speed, prime numbers, negative numbers, probability, coordinates, surface area.

Problem solving: some pages are tagged "problem solving" because their questions come from the kinds found in school tests rather than from one numbered outcome. This is our own tag, not a syllabus line.
