# Primary 1 early-finisher pack: coverage gap analysis and expansion proposal

Date: 23 Oct 2026. Read-only analysis; no file under `school/` was touched. Trigger: the owner says the 20-page pack "looks good but is still short of many topics".

Sources read: `docs/moe/syllabus_by_level.md` (P1 section, pp. 31 to 32), `docs/moe/verification_report.md`, `docs/syllabus_map/README.md`, `docs/books/p1_school_paper_patterns.md`, `docs/books/p1_non_routine_catalogue.md`, `docs/school_books/plan.md` (P1 unit table, template list T01 to T12), `school/p1/README.md` (page inventory), `school/colour_by_code/README.md` and `banks_p1.py` (14 sheets).

## 0. Bottom line

- The pack covers each strand at least once, but only five areas are covered well (place value, comparing and ordering, add and subtract within 20, multiplication and sharing, o'clock and half past). Money, length, shapes and picture graphs rest on one pack page plus one colour sheet each.
- Not covered at all: reading and writing numbers in words (1.3), comparing sets by matching (1.4 as a set task), mental-strategy and fact-family work, every word problem and every two-step problem, am and pm, drawing a line to the nearest cm, forming figures from shapes, copying on a grid, classifying shapes, half and quarter circle.
- The biggest gap is not a syllabus topic but a question style: P1 school papers are mostly short stories and picture questions. The pack has no story sum at all and trains only 3 of the 14 non-routine types well (LOG, PAT, CHAL).
- Recommendation: grow to **40 pages as two packs of 20 (Pack A = today's 20 with light rebalancing, Pack B = 20 new pages), each pack 10 one-star and 10 two-star**. New pages go mostly to add and subtract (+5, mostly word problems), whole numbers (+3), then +2 each for multiplication and sharing, money, length, time, shapes and picture graphs.

## 3. Proposed size and balance

### Option chosen: 40 pages, two packs of 20

Pack A (today's P1-01 to P1-20, light rebalancing) and Pack B (new P1-21 to P1-40). Pack B can be sold, licensed and printed separately, and a school can start with A. Each pack keeps 10 one-star and 10 two-star.

## 5. Prioritised new pages (Pack B, 20 pages)

Lessons learned that every page must keep:

- Use friendly objects (fruit, animals, toys, sweets), never bare dots or empty boxes; the 7-year-old finds bare dots dull.
- Answer boxes at least 6 mm wide and tall; the current `validate.py` already requires 12 mm height, keep that.
- Rulers must not always start at 0 (use a ruler where the object starts at 2 or 3).
- Money pages show "cents" on Singapore-style coins and write the amount as 50¢, never 50c and never $0.50.
- Picture graphs use picture symbols, one picture = one, 4 to 6 rows, 0 to 10 pictures.
- Pure black and white; no category hint in the check strip; every page passes the 95% wrong-answer enumeration and the word-list slip test.
- Time words: only "o'clock" and "half past" in text; 5-minute and am / pm times as digits, "pm" with no full stops.
- Sharing convention printed on the page: say who shares ("shares with 3 friends: 4 children").

## 7. What cannot be verified from this repository

- **The syllabus PDF and its extracted text are not in the repo.** `docs/moe/` holds only `syllabus_by_level.md` and `verification_report.md`; both are our own-words summaries of the owner's PDF (read in a previous session). Every outcome number, page number and wording quoted here is taken from that summary, not from the source. Outcome wordings could not be re-read.
- Specifically unconfirmed: the exact text of each outcome in Whole Numbers 1.1 to 1.7, Addition and Subtraction 2.1 to 2.7 (the summary itself says it does not assert the individual numbers within 2.1 to 2.7), Multiplication and Division 3.1 to 3.4, Measurement 1.1 to 1.4 and 2.1 to 2.4, Geometry 1.1 to 1.4 and the single Statistics outcome.
- Items the owner's brief assumes but the summary does not list as P1 outcomes: number bonds, word problems, and "up to 2-step" problems. They come from school papers and the owner's brief, not from the syllabus text. If the syllabus says nothing about two-step at P1, then the two-step pages are paper-driven, not syllabus-driven.
- Whether the summary's statement that 3-D shapes start at P2 (p. 34) and that P1 geometry is 2D only is correct is taken on trust.
- Paper evidence is limited: the P1 papers were read as OCR text with pictures lost (patterns file, section 0), the 2023 zip and the 14 MB MCQ book were not read, and the sample is uneven across schools. "No evidence" for shapes, grids, calendars and cube views is weak evidence of absence.
- Coverage verdicts for the pack pages come from the README's inventory and tags, not from re-opening the generated PDFs; a page might test more or less than its tag says. Page content was not re-solved here.
- The pack was being edited by another agent while this was written, so the page list may have changed after this analysis.
