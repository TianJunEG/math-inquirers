## 2. The 40-page allocation

**Typical order, schools vary.** Singapore primary schools do not teach in one fixed order. The order below follows (a) the order the syllabus lists topics, (b) when each topic shows up in the school papers read for the project (`p1_school_paper_patterns.md` section 2 to 6: shapes and ordinals in Terms 1 and 2; time from Term 3 with 5-minute and am/pm in Term 4; money late Term 3 and Term 4; sharing and equal groups heavy in Terms 3 and 4; picture graphs Term 4), and (c) our own lesson sequence. Early-finisher pages are mixed-difficulty, so a teacher can hand out any page at any time; the semester is only the booklet it lives in.

Page ids: existing pages keep their id **P1-01 to P1-20** (so `validate.py` tags and the key do not change). New pages use the working ids **N01 to N22** from the coverage-gap draft and are given final ids P1-21 to P1-40 in build order. "Pos" is the position in the booklet (S1-01 to S1-20, S2-01 to S2-20). Stars: 1 or 2. Mechanic codes: K1 code word, K2 hidden picture, K3 check total, K4 grid consistency, K6 route closes, K7 put the answer back, K8 chain closes, K9 measured check (plan.md section 8.1).

Where a page below is "kept", it keeps its puzzle; "adjusted" lists the light change.

## 5. Design rules (gathered from owner feedback and the audits)

These apply to all 40 pages. Sources: `AUDIT_FIXES_3.md` owner feedback section, `p1_coverage_gap.md` section 5, the school-paper patterns, `coin_reproduction_check.md`.

1. **Objects, not bare dots or empty boxes.** Apples, stars, fish, balloons, animals, toys. Rows of 12 objects or fewer; at most one item with 13 to 20 objects (two rows). Friendly pictures also for ten-frames and counting scenes (N05).
2. **Answer boxes at least 6 mm** wide and tall (all blanks in the pack are 6 mm squares; `validate.py` keeps a 12 mm minimum height for the main boxes). Space rows so nothing is crowded; P1-16 is the densest page and is not to be copied.
3. **Rulers do not always start at 0.** An arrow marks the start of each strip: "Each strip starts at the arrow. Count the cm to the other end." Mix starts at 0 and at 1 to 4 (N15, N16, P1-17). The cm scale only; never mm.
4. **Coins show cents.** Singapore-style but ORIGINAL art, not a copy of any real design: ring, ridged hint, own motif per value, the value large with the word cents, true diameter ratios only, much larger than real size, black and white, no "SINGAPORE" lettering or national symbol. Write 50¢ (never 50c, never $0.50). Whether real designs may be used is open (D1); default is original art.
5. **Picture graphs use picture symbols**: a smiley face is 1 child, or another small picture; "Each [picture] stands for 1 [noun]", always one picture = one; 4 to 6 rows; 0 to 10 pictures per row; title above; labels left; a fruit or object icon beside each row label; "Draw a face or a circle" (the checks count symbols). No tally, no axis numbers, no half icons, no scale of 2.
6. **Code words: no wrong placement may spell another word.** The check strip says "Write the letters in order. Do they spell a word?" and nothing more (no category hint). The enumeration `validate.py` runs (single slips, swaps; 95% floor; V3) must reject every wrong arrangement that spells any word of the 129 000-word list. **Known breaches to fix before the booklets ship:** P1-04 (BELOW also BOWEL, ELBOW) and P1-07 (SNAKE also SNEAK, SKEAN). The audit left them because a unique-word page can be guessed from the letters; the fix is a design where a computed final letter or a non-letter symbol (as P1-16's total letter) breaks the symmetry. N03 and N13 must be built that way from the start. Check also V4 (at least 3 common words can be made from the table letters, so the word cannot be guessed).
7. **Time:** only "o'clock" and "half past" in text; 5-minute times as digits (4:35); "am" and "pm" with no full stops; hours and minutes abbreviated "h" and "min" only on N17. No "quarter past", "ten past".
8. **Sharing convention printed on the page:** who shares ("shares with 3 friends: 4 children share"). No division sign.
9. **Pure black and white.** Only #000 and #fff; no grey, tint, gradient or opacity; strokes at least 0.57 pt; the bilevel check must keep the ink. No Mi, no MOE, alignment or affiliation wording on pupil pages.
10. **Reading level:** instruction sentences at most 8 words and at most 3 lines; numerals for quantities; one clue per sentence; a picture or table to place children in.
11. **Self-check quality:** a check strip of at most 18 words; the check must not give the answer; wrong answers rejected at 95% or better for every single slip and every wrong arrangement; a total or answer may not be derived from the clue that the check then tests (SC-4).
12. **Original puzzles only:** own generators, own numbers; no school or book question reproduced (plan.md section 11). The catalogue examples are inspiration for types only.
13. **Outcome numbers on every page footer or key tag** must come from section 1a, and `validate.py` must find the topic phrase in `docs/moe/syllabus_by_level.md` (see section 7, item 8).
14. **Only syllabus content:** nothing from section 1b. A page with a decorative pentagon or hexagon, a ÷, a fraction of a number, a calendar or a 3-D solid fails review.
