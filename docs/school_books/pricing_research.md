## 2. Licence structures seen (norms)

- Per-building/site licence, annual, unlimited teachers (Super Teacher Worksheets, LessonUp-style). Multi-site discount of about 13% (US$375 to 325).
- Per-staff-member annual subscription (Twinkl). Marketplace model: per-teacher licence plus add-on copies (TpT).
- Perpetual-use purchase of a fixed worksheet pack with reproduction rights inside one school is the natural fit for a one-off 20-page product; annual renewal only makes sense for new content each year.
- Standard terms to specify: one school/campus, internal use, unlimited photocopying by the school's staff for its own pupils, no sharing outside, no uploading to public sites, no resale; watermark with school name on PDF.

## 3. Singapore school buying context

- Schools buy via GeBIZ quotations/ITQs (up to S$90k ex-GST) or via their own small-purchase rules (threshold unverified). Vendors need a GeBIZ Trading Partner account and, for many MOE contracts, ACRA registration. A low-value one-off licence will likely be handled as a small purchase by a HOD/department with school finance approval, but confirm.
- Enrichment/programme fees can be charged to pupils and paid from Edusave (S$230/yr in 2023; check the current figure), which suits a per-pupil printed pack priced under about S$10 to 15.
- Typical spend: no verifiable figure for department budgets found. Assume a primary maths department resource budget is in the low thousands of S$ per year (unverified); keep a whole-school licence under about S$500 to stay inside any plausible small-purchase limit.

## 4. Recommended starting price bands (SGD, ex-GST)

Basis: international site-licence anchors (about S$450 to 500 per building per year for an all-subject worksheet site, converting US$375 at roughly 1.3), scaled down for a single 20-page product; print cost inferred from Section 1.

| Product | Starting band | Suggested launch price | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20-page digital school licence, one level, one school, perpetual | S$60 to 120 | S$89 | Far below a whole-site subscription; teachers can justify it as a departmental resource |
| Whole-school multi-level bundle (P1 to P6, 6 x 20 pages) | S$250 to 450 | S$349 | About 35 to 50% discount versus 6 x S$89 = S$534; stays under likely small-purchase limits |
| Printed bulk per pupil copy (20-page, A5/A4 mono, stapled; min order 100) | S$2.50 to 4.50 | S$3.50 | Print cost likely S$0.80 to 1.50 per copy at volume (inferred, get quotes); leaves margin for design/IP |
| Home extension workbook, physical, per level | S$12 to 18 | S$15 | Local workbook range inferred from S$7.75 secondary workbooks plus premium for puzzle/enrichment positioning (L) |
| Home extension workbook, PDF soft copy, per level | S$6 to 10 | S$8 | About 50 to 60% of physical; no print/ship cost |

Optional: parent bundle P1 to P6 PDF S$35 to 45; multi-year school renewal not needed.

## 5. Assumptions

1. 20 pages of content per level; school buys per level and reproduces for its own pupils.
2. Prices ex-GST; GST registration status of the seller not assumed.
3. USD to SGD about 1.3; GBP not converted.
4. Print cost per copy is inferred from loose-page rates, not booklet quotes.
5. Home-market bands are positioned against assessment books, not tuition.

## 6. Owner should validate

1. Get 3 print quotes (100, 300, 1,000 copies; A4/A5, mono vs 2-colour cover, saddle-stitch).
2. Check current primary maths assessment book and enrichment book prices at Popular, Epigram, Kinokuniya and publisher sites.
3. Ask 3 to 5 HODs/teachers what they would pay and who approves it; confirm the school small-purchase limit and whether a GeBIZ account is needed for sub-S$X purchases.
4. Confirm Edusave usage rules for printed enrichment packs and current annual amount.
5. Check Twinkl, TpT and local competitors (e.g. Singapore tuition-centre worksheet sellers) directly for live site-licence prices.
6. Decide perpetual vs annual licence and GST/business registration implications.
7. Verify the TpT multiple-licence discount via TpT help centre.
