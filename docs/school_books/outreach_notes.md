# Outreach notes: Primary 1 sampler to schools (DRAFT)

Files: `outputs/school_sampler/` (sampler PDF, one-page school summary, draft emails). Nothing has been sent. All wording is DRAFT until the owner approves it.

## Choosing 10 to 20 schools

- Start with 10 to 20 primary schools, sent in two or three small batches, so replies can shape the next batch.
- Mix school sizes and areas; include a few where you already have a friendly contact (a former colleague, a parent who teaches).
- Use only addresses the school publishes (general office or department address on its website). Do not guess or scrape personal addresses.
- Address the role (Head of Department, Mathematics), not a named person, unless the school website names the HOD publicly.
- Send one email per school, individually. No mass cc or bcc.
- Skip schools that have asked not to be contacted, and any school you cannot find a published address for.

## Subject line

Plain and specific, no capitals, no exclamation marks, no "free", no price. Suggested: "Primary 1 maths puzzles for early finishers: a sampler for feedback". Keep it the same for every school so replies can be filed easily.

## Privacy and opt-out

- The email says who we are and has an opt-out line ("reply no thanks"). Honour any opt-out at once and keep a do-not-contact list; never email that address again.
- Keep only the data you need in the tracking table (school, role, address, dates). No personal data beyond a published role address and any name a teacher gives you.
- Send the follow-up once only. Do not send more than two emails per school.
- Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act and Spam Control Act may apply to business emails. This is a reminder, not legal advice: confirm with a lawyer or adviser before a wider campaign.

**This wording has not been approved by a lawyer. Do not give any school a booklet or licence terms until a lawyer has approved it.** The sampler and summary carry DRAFT stamps for the same reason; a final build removes them only after approval.
