## Safeguarding correction (applied last, everywhere)
No private one-to-one messages anywhere. The guide rule "No private messages to children" is kept (the Tutor Guide checklist now says so again).
- Invitations go to the WHOLE group: "When you have finished Start here you can choose Think harder, Go further or the Investigation." Nobody is singled out, nobody is named, pairs are not split unless both agree.
- A child who ticks "I am still stuck (my tutor will check in with me)" gets a check-in in the open session (a quick visit to the shared breakout room or a hand signal, with a parent nearby), then again at the start of the next lesson. Never a DM.
- Sneak-peek and exit answers: written on own paper and shown only to the tutor on camera (or everyone holds paper to the camera at the same moment). Never read out, never boards for the group, never private chat.
