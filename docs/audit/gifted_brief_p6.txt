STEP 2 - the owner's new tier principle, which every question set must satisfy:
 * EASY / CORE questions must be manageable for a student who has NOT yet learnt the topic: low floor. They can be started and usually solved with objects, drawing, counting, guess-and-check, patterns, or skills from the level below; no unexplained notation or vocabulary; concrete or pictorial where possible; the topic's formal method is NOT required.
 * THINK HARDER questions need the idea taught in this lesson, applied in a new setting.
 * CHALLENGE questions are for students who have COMPLETED the topic: they need mastery of it plus extension (multi-step, generalise, justify, combine with other topics, open-ended, problem posing, 'what if' changes).
 Within each lesson the three tiers should form a clear low-floor, high-ceiling ladder.

STEP 3 - audit: for EVERY lesson (a) classify each Core, Think Harder and Challenge item in the question bank AND on the printed workbook (core pages, Think Harder pages) as compliant / violation of the tier principle with a one-line reason (e.g. 'Core item needs the formal algorithm taught in this lesson', 'Challenge only needs one step of the taught method'); (b) score the lesson 0/1/2 on each of the 12 rubric criteria with one-line evidence; (c) list the lesson's gifted-style strengths and gaps. Write docs/audit/gifted_p6.md: summary table per lesson (tier compliance % of items, rubric total /24, verdict: gifted-style / partly / mainly enrichment-practice), an honest overall verdict for the level, and what marketing language is safe for it (from section 3 of the research). Be honest and specific; do not inflate.

STEP 4 - fix: rewrite or swap items so every lesson's tiers comply: make Core genuinely approachable without prior teaching (change wording/context, add scaffolds such as a picture/table starter or 'use cubes/coins'), keep Think Harder on the taught idea, and make Challenge truly need the completed topic plus extension (reuse/promote items between tiers where that fixes it; write new ones where needed).
