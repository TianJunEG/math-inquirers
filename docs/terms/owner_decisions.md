# Term-time program: owner decisions (22 Oct 2026)

Source: the owner's replies in the working session. Anything marked "open" is not decided.

## Decided
1. **Enrolment.** Places open every term, and a child can join any available slot mid-year and pay a **prorated fee** for the lessons remaining.
2. **Term length.** A school term is about 10 weeks, so the program runs **10 lessons per term** (40 per level per year), not 9. The term plans must be adjusted from 36 to 40 lessons per level.
3. **Exam periods.** A child who skips during exams can make the lesson up on another day or in a holiday class if there is a seat. Every lesson is also recorded.
4. **Holiday graduates follow the term calendar.** After a December holiday course they start Term 1; after a June course, Term 3; after a one-week March course, Term 2.
5. **Payment.** Price is per term. Options: (a) pay in full at the start of the term, with a **small discount**; (b) three instalments: first at the start, second after 3 lessons, third after another 3 lessons. A payment schedule is shown to the parent.
6. **Contest-style items** (digit-sum tests, gaps and posts, chance, ratio notation in Level 5, cryptarithms, pairing sums, worst-case counting): taught at **Think harder**, never Start here. In the plans, move them from Go further to Think harder where they were Go further.
7. **Level 4 holiday course:** no cryptarithm lesson is added. The term plan already holds them.
8. Payments run online from launch (originally HitPay; **replaced by Stripe on 23 Oct 2026**, see below); PayNow by hand during the beta (see terms page).
