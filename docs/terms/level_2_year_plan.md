## 1. Design principles

- **The year has one arc, matching the slogan.** Term 1 *Notice it* (structure in numbers, patterns, shapes, money, measures; the tour of P2 topics with a low floor). Term 2 *Test it* (systematic listing, data, models, logic: how to check a guess). Term 3 *Explain why* (rules of rules, equivalence, "always/sometimes/never", justify in words). Term 4 *Create and connect* (chance, problem posing, finding errors, mixed contest-style work).
- **Terms depend on each other, but entry points stay open.** Each term's first two lessons re-enter the previous term's key idea in a new setting (about 10 minutes of spiral warm-up per lesson). A new child can join at Term 1 only; a mid-year joiner gets a 2-page bridge note (open question 2).
- **Spiral revisiting is planned, not accidental.** Every topic taught in one term returns in a different dress at least once later (see matrix, "r"). Tools return too: a "toolkit card" (draw it, list it, table, work backwards, try a smaller case) gains one new move per term and every lesson names the move used.
- **Problem solving and inquiry grow by term.** T1: tutor-led moves, one explore per lesson. T2: children choose the move and check by a second method. T3: children write a one-line reason and test someone else's claim. T4: children pose, swap, and mark-up problems (including wrong solutions). The 12-criterion gaps in `gifted_p2.md` (look-back, problem posing, pacing flexibility) are built in from the start rather than patched.
- **Start here is school level or below, and truly untaught-friendly.** Term-time children are in P2 *while* learning these topics, unlike the holiday group who have finished P2. So every lesson's Start here must work for a child meeting the idea for the first time; Go further may go past the syllabus and says so in the tutor guide. Words such as "remainder", "letter for an unknown", "fraction of a set" stay out of student pages before their MOE year (audit M1, M4, M5): use boxes, "left over", "bar cut into parts".
- **Each lesson is 90 minutes, one big idea.** Same routine as the holiday course (warm-up, explore, teach, two worksheet pages, talk, exit ticket) but with the audit's re-time (about 12 minutes for each worksheet page) and at most 5 to 6 question blocks per page. Nine lessons in a school term of about 10 weeks leave one buffer week (catch-up, the end-of-term check, make-ups); no dates are hardcoded.

"Beyond MOE" = not in the P1-P3 syllabus; other-country sources are named where used. Tier cells: S = Start here, T = Think harder, G = Go further.

## 3. How the holiday lessons are reused

- **Term 1 adapts holiday lessons 1-9 in the same order** (the `p2/content/l1-l9.js` and `split/p2l1-p2l9` decks), with the audit's fixes applied first: boxes instead of letters; "left over" not "remainder"; seconds, fraction-of-a-set and nets taken out of Start here and Think harder; the re-timed 90 minutes; the digit-card and balance props added to the kit.
- **Lesson 10 (Challenge Day) is cut** as a lesson, to reach 9. Why: it is a mixed review of other lessons, which a continuing course does not need at the start of a year. Its parts move on rather than disappear: the "how to start" moves become the toolkit card used in every lesson; picture graphs with scales go to T2.1; the logic grid goes to T2.8; the format returns as T4.9. Nothing is merged; nothing else is cut.
- **Heaviest rewrites in Term 1:** lesson 8 (lowest rubric score, 11/24; nets cut), lessons 5 and 9 (overloaded; timing), and lesson 1 and 6 (fix the issues in `audit_p2.md`).
- **Terms 2-4 are new** (27 lessons). Deliberate overlaps only: T2.9, T3.7 (money contexts), T4.9 and the 10-minute spiral warm-ups. The nearest topic collisions were checked: T2.4 and T3.7 versus holiday lesson 5 (that one is undoing; these are part-whole and compare bars); T3.3 versus lesson 9 (that one uses a balance for mass; this one is symbol equations); T3.5 versus lesson 8 (that one composes and counts cubes; this one names solids and draws views).
- **Risk to flag:** a child who finishes Term 4 may then be offered the holiday Level 2 course, which repeats Term 1 content (open question 3).

**Gaps and where school practice outpaces us.** (1) Written column algorithms and mental calculation speed: school drills these; we teach understanding and one strategy per lesson, so parents should still expect school practice for fluency (the `moe_alignment` disclosure line applies). ## 5. Assessment and progress

**End-of-term check (10 questions, about 30 minutes, in the buffer week or the last 30 minutes of lesson 9).** Q1-4 Start here, retrieval of the term's core ideas; Q5-7 Think harder, new settings; Q8-9 Go further; Q10 is a reflection ("Which question was trickiest? What did you try first?"). Private paper, shown to the tutor only, not ranked or labelled as pass/fail. Each term's check also includes 2 spiral items from earlier terms. The results guide the next-term tutor note; the diagnostic cut-offs in `levels_and_placement.md` are not reused.

**Parent report line per term (fill in; observed, not promised):**
- T1: "This term [Name] explored numbers, patterns, tables, money, fractions and shapes. We saw [Name] [strength seen, e.g. draw a picture to start]. Next we will practise [one habit]."
- T2: "This term [Name] learned to test ideas: reading graphs, listing every case, and drawing bar models. We saw [specific example]. A helpful thing at home is [one activity]."
- T3: "This term [Name] practised explaining why: rules of patterns, fractions on a line, and checking claims. We saw [example of an explanation]. Next term we create and connect."

## 6. Work to turn the plan into the full set

Per lesson: tutor guide entry and script, about 25 slides (deck generator), 2-page workbook plus Think harder pages, question bank (6 Start here, 8 Think harder, 9 Go further), Weekly Dose practice page, parent-side note, kit list. Per term: end-of-term check, report line, bridge note.

| Term 1 adaptation, with audit fixes | 9 lessons | 4-6 hours each | 36-54 h |
| End-of-term checks, report lines, bridge notes | 4 terms | 6-8 hours each | 24-32 h |
| Audit passes (answers, tier compliance, timing, gifted rubric) | 4 terms | 8-12 hours each | 32-48 h |

If one writer-agent produces a term's nine lessons in parallel with an audit agent, the calendar time is shorter than the hour count; human review of each term is the real bottleneck. Suggested order: Term 1 first (reuse, lowest risk), then T2, T3, T4.
