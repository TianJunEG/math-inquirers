# UX review: holiday workbooks and decks, school puzzle book, colour-by-code, Level 1 extension

Reviewed 2026-10-23, report only (no source files touched). Method: PDFs rendered with pdftoppm and read as images. Holiday: sampled 8 pages per level for Levels 1 to 6 (cover, journal, first lesson page, mid, late, certificate); decks: 6 slides each from L1 lesson 3, P2 L4, P3 L4, P4 L1, P5 L8, P6 L5, rendered from `split/*/project/slides/*.html` at 1920x1080 with every build step visible, so stacked-reveal overlaps in that render may be artefacts of the static view; confirm in the slide player before acting on deck items marked (verify). School: all 25 pages of the P1 pack, key pages, 6 colour-by-code sheets (P1 and P2) plus a teacher copy. Extension: all 26 pages of the BW print edition. Mid-grey share measured at 100 dpi.

## P1 (fix before release)

1. **Holiday print PDFs: grey play-money and halftone tags.** `outputs/print/bw/Math_Inquirers_P1_Workbook_PRINT.pdf` p55 (17 percent mid-grey, cut-out notes and coins on flat grey with a dashed edge) and p53 (8 percent). Also the "No.1/No.2" tags and the "Start here" pill on every lesson page (e.g. p3) are dithered halftone, so small white-on-dark text turns speckled when photocopied. Change the bw theme in `levels/mklevel.py` / `wbstyles` (find the bw print stylesheet) to: white fill, 1.5 pt black outline, and solid black tag with white text; play money outline-only with the value large. Same check for P2-P6 bw files (48 pages of P5 have over 5 percent mid-grey).
2. **Holiday: two PDFs per level with unclear roles.** `outputs/print/` and `outputs/print/bw/` carry the same filenames, and `bw` is NOT bit-identical. A tutor can easily print the wrong one. Rename the bw files `..._PRINT_BW.pdf` (print_spec.json / build step in `levels/build_level.sh`), and say on the cover footer which edition it is.
3. **Holiday, last page blank in P1-P3 (and P4 has 1 trailing page).** P1 p64, P2 p74, P3 p64 contain only a stray character after the certificate. Remove the trailing page break in `levels/mklevel.py` (print would waste a sheet and break double-sided pairing).
4. **Holiday, page numbers drift.** The cut-out page that is PDF p55 prints "Page 54", and the kit is referred to as "in the kit at the back" (P1 p3) without a page number. Number every physical page, or print "Kit, page N" in the instruction. Files: `levels/mklevel.py` footer counter; wording in the Lesson 1 data.
5. **Extension: answers are for grown-ups only, so a child cannot self-check.** `extension/tools/render.py` answer pages (book pp22-25). The design says self-study with explained answers, but the answers section header reads "for grown-ups" and the item numbers do not say which workbook page they came from. Add the question page to each section heading ("Start here 1-5, workbook page 6") and a short "Check your answers" line on Check yourself pages ("answers on page 25"). Currently the Check yourself box says only "Check the answers at the back" with no page number (p11, p18).
6. **School colour-by-code: coloured regions carry printed text, so there is almost nowhere to colour.** `school/colour_by_code/out/p1/CBC-10_house_shapes_pupil.pdf` and the P1 pack p6 "Colour the Rocket": most regions are 2 to 3 cm wide and half filled with sums or shape icons; a crayon over bold 10 pt text hides it and a pencil shade makes the sum unreadable. Fix in the colour_by_code layout tool (`school/colour_by_code/` generator, `cbc_vendor` in `school/p1/`): keep text in the top third of a region and leave the rest blank, minimum region about 25 x 18 mm, or move the sum outside with a leader line. Also set the answer box at the corner, not centre.

## P2 (clear usability gaps)

7. **Colour key reads as a number row, not a key.** CBC sheets and P1 pack p6/p12/p14: the five key boxes show "1 yellow / 2 red ..." as black text in empty rectangles. A 6-year-old must read the word, find a crayon, and match. Add a small drawn swatch box to colour in beside each word (empty square labelled with the first letter, e.g. "Y"), plus "Colour the key boxes first" in the instructions. Same boxes are used on all 22 CBC sheets so one change in the shared header template fixes all.
8. **Yellow is the least visible crayon on a photocopy.** In the P1 pack the key values map 1 to yellow in nearly every sheet. Put yellow on the smallest or most generous regions, or add a "light colours will copy faintly, press firmly" note in teacher notes (`school/p1/out/pupil.html` / teacher notes section, p3-4).
9. **Line art is geometric blocks, not pictures.** CBC rocket, house, ice cream and castle are made of straight-edged compartments; interest comes only from the maths. For P1 to P2 pupils add 2 to 3 non-text details per picture (portholes, windows, flames) as outlines, and ensure the finished picture is recognisable when coloured by the key. The house sheet marks (triangle, square, diamond, star, plus) "same small mark = same colour" as a second rule; that doubles the cognitive load and the Check strip is the only place it is explained. Put the mirror/mark rule in the instructions line instead.
10. **Self-check strip text is small for early finishers.** P1 pack Check it box on pp7-26: instruction text about 8 pt, with "Is it in the sky?" prompts. Raise to 10 pt; add "Stuck? Ask a friend, then ask your teacher".
11. **School name and Name fields differ across sheets.** The P1 pack has only "Name" (no Class); CBC-10 has "Name" and "Class"; CBC sheets add "School name" at bottom left. Make all three the same (Name, Class) and keep the licence line in the foot only.
12. **P1 pack contents and teacher notes length.** pp2-4 (licence, notes, contents) are three text-only pages before the first puzzle; teacher notes are small and dense (about 9 pt). Move the licence to the back, keep one page of notes headed "5 minute guide: which puzzle for which child" with the star system and a one-line fix when the check fails. Files: `school/p1/gen` front matter, `school/p1/out/front.html`.
13. **Extension: "Hints are upside down" is a good idea but small (9.5 pt rotated).** pp7-10 Think harder / Go further: for ages 6 to 8 the rotated text is hard. Raise to 11 pt and make the dashed separator a "Hint (turn the page round)" label with a small icon. File: `extension/tools/ext.css` hint rule.
14. **Extension: only 2 of 10 units exist but the contents, pace plan and My progress list all 10.** Contents p3 and pace plan p4 mark units 3 to 10 "Not in this proof edition", which is honest but puts empty rows in front of the child. Hide absent units in the print/soft edition for any customer-facing proof; keep the placeholder rows only in the internal build (`extension/tools/build.py`).
15. **Holiday workbooks: cover and p1 identical across Levels 1 to 6.** Primary 6 gets the same confetti blocks as Primary 1; the cover is the first impression for an 11 or 12 year old. Make the cover shapes (`levels/mklevel.py` cover block) level-tinted and for P5-P6 use a geometric or code motif (graph-paper, angle arcs). Same for the deck title slide: the "!" speech bubble and "?" cloud appear identically in P2, P5 and P6 title and break slides (looks babyish at P5-P6); swap for a simple rule line or Mi-free diagram for P5-P6 in `split/*/project/slides/*title*.html` generator (`deck/gen*.js`).
16. **Holiday pages: writing space and legibility for P1.** P1 p3 "Count with the frog": answer blanks are about 1.5 cm underscores at 11.7 pt, and the lower 35 percent of the page is empty. Use the free space for bigger boxes (at least 12 mm) and a working area; ages 6 and 7 write large. P1 Think harder pages (p21, p32) have 2 ruled lines per task: 3 lines at 9 mm would be more realistic.
17. **Holiday: "Start here" has two meanings.** It is a tab/pill naming the first section of the page (P1 p3) and also an inline hint label ("Start here. 63 500 is halfway...", P4 p9). Rename the hint to "Hint" (consistent with the extension book, which uses Hint). Files: `levels/extras_data.js` / the hint rendering in `levels/mklevel.py`.
18. **Holiday: no footer page count or level on some pages; header absent on continuation pages.** P1 p32 and P3 p21 (go further / look back pages) have only a tiny "Lesson 6 . MONEY" line at 6.4 pt and no title or name field; footer text is 6.4 pt on every page. Raise the footer to 8 pt and repeat the lesson tab and Name on continuation pages.

## P3 (polish)

19. P5 p10 and P4 p9: "Start here" pill becomes italic grey instruction text, so the hierarchy changes between levels. Keep the pill.
20. P6 pages carry Python and Scratch panels in several tints; in bw they become grey boxes (P6 p14 "Python corner"). Replace with a border-only box with a monospace label.
21. Holiday certificate (pp63-ish) has an empty "Name" line with no label (the dashed line above "completed all 10 lessons"). Add "Name:" and a space for the date and tutor signature to be on one line.
22. Deck (verify): on the title slide the "0-2 MIN" pill sits on the "?" cloud (L1 lesson 3, p3l4), and a long title ("Let's Plan the Fruit Party") runs under the cloud. Reserve the cloud area or shorten decoration for long titles.
23. Deck (verify): small speaker-note text printed at the bottom of title and warm-up slides in the static HTML (4 to 6 px). If the player hides notes it is fine; if not, hide them. Digit cards slide P6 L5 overlaps its heading in the static render.
24. Deck: "Looking back / Look-back" and "Reflect" titles vary between levels ("Look-back", "Looking back", "Exit slip and reflection"). Standardise the name used on the workbook page.
25. P1 pack p25 (last puzzle, Fruit Graph): the answer area is "a)/b)" with very short boxes while the graph rows have empty circles; fine to colour but the key is not given. Add "Draw one circle for each child" with an example row that is already filled (it is: keep).
26. CBC teacher copies show answer in brackets "(yellow)" over the art: good, but also bold and underline the answer part only on the grid for easier marking; the key chart on the P1 key (p10) is repeated and fine.
27. Extension: Welcome page p2 says "Ages 6-8" on cover and "20 to 25 minutes" pace; page 4 has the pace plan as a table of page ranges; add a plain "Start on page 5" arrow on page 2 so a child need not read the table. Add a one-line motivation device (a star per session) in the page footer; presently only the end-of-unit "My progress" p19 tick-box provides reward.

## What already works well

- Holiday: strong, consistent system across all six levels: lesson tab and topic in the header, large title, Name/Group/Date strip, numbered "No." cards with a clear write area; body type about 11.7 pt on all levels; certificate and journal pages; Think harder / Go further / Look back structure repeated; P4 and P5 diagrams are clear and the graph-paper background is subtle.
- Holiday P6: Scratch and Python panels are well explained and marked optional; maths density is mature, not babyish, apart from the cover.
- Decks: strong hierarchy (large title, minute pill, colour bar per level), accessible type sizes at 1920 wide, "Talk time" and "Looking back" slides are a good consistent pattern; answer reveal slides use big type.
- School P1 pack: truly black and white with solid lines (mid-grey measured at about 4 percent, anti-aliasing only), identical layout on all pages (title, stars, Name, Topic, Instructions, boxed Check it strip, licence footer, page code), self-checking by code word is clever, instructions are one short action per line, validation_report.txt shows all 40 checks pass (unique solutions, wrong answers rejected). Teacher key lists answers, check result, and a teacher note per page and has a one-page topic coverage table. The CBC "Check your work" strip lists how many parts of each colour so a pupil can verify without a teacher; the teacher copy shows the answer and colour in brackets.
- Extension: clear four-label system (Start here, Think harder, Go further, Check yourself) explained on page 2; "Learn it" pages have goal, worked example and "How to find a rule" steps; large 13 pt text and big answer boxes; "Your turn" after each worked example; pace plan with 3 short sessions; per-answer "Why:" explanations on pp22-25; Mi is used warmly on the cover, welcome and certificate; no Mi in the school books, as required. BW print has no grey fills (only about 4 percent anti-aliasing).
