# Level 1 term books, decks and guides: design review

Report only. No source file was changed. Reviewed as a child (6-7), a parent helping at home, and a tutor teaching live online.

## What was sampled

Workbook PDFs (colour): L02 and L13 and L24 (Terms 1, 2, 3, all pages), plus L05/L08 page counts. Quest Logs: L10 (4 pages) and L20 (3 pages). Tutor guide: L17 pages 1, 2, 5, 9 (also page counts for L02-L28). Decks: L08 (12 slides), L20 (8 slides), L24 (4 slides), plus an L08 slide shrunk to 400 px wide to simulate a phone on Meet. Print books: Book 1 (colour and B&W, pages 1, 2, 3, 9, 20) and Book 3 B&W (pages 3, 4, 5, 17). Parent pieces: L10 Quest Parent Note, L13 Parent Summary, L13 Home Practice and Home Answers.

Page references below use the PDF page number. Quoted source files are in `term/l1/tools/` unless stated. Screenshots are not committed; render with `pdftoppm -r 75 -f N -l N file.pdf out`.

## P1: fix first (child cannot do the work, or the tutor cannot read it live)

1. **Slide body text is too small for a laptop on Meet and unreadable on a phone.** In `L08_Deck.pdf` slides 6 and 10 the body text is about 2% of slide height (steps, table, "What if" box), the lower 40-50% of every content slide is empty, and when shrunk to phone width (400 px) the text is about 6 px high. Same in L20 slides 5, 24, 28 (exit-ticket slide text is smaller still) and L24 slides 12 and 20 (coins and the "$1" coin about the size of a thumbnail). Fix in `term/l1/tools/b_deck.py` (and its CSS in `rebuild/slidekit.py` / `levels/restyle.py` if the sizes live there): raise body text to at least 44 px on the 1920x1080 slide, stack content to use the full height, cap a slide at one question plus one picture, and scale coin/note pictures to at least 25% of slide width. Check by exporting a slide at 400 px wide: every word should still be readable.
2. **Several steps appear at once on one slide.** `L08_Deck` slide 6 "Explore: two plates" shows four numbered steps plus a "What if?" box together. A tutor on a call cannot control the child's attention. Fix in `b_deck.py`: split into one step per slide (or use build/reveal order via `deck.json`), keep the "What if?" as its own slide.
3. **Answer blanks are too short for a 6-year-old's handwriting in Term 3 and Quest Logs.** In `L24_Workbook.pdf` pages 1-5 and `L10_Quest_Log.pdf` / `L20_Quest_Log.pdf` the answers are a dashed underscore about 15 mm wide ("The total is ______¢", "There are ______ children"). Two-digit answers and money signs will not fit. Term 1 and 2 use rounded boxes (about 10 mm square, two boxes for two digits, which also shows place value and works well). Fix in `b_print.py` (`.al` rule, currently `min-width:17mm; height:18px`) and the blank-to-box conversion: use the box component everywhere an answer is a number, one box per digit, at least 11-12 mm square; keep underscores only for words.
4. **Exit-ticket block is cramped.** `L13_Workbook.pdf` page 5 and `L24_Workbook.pdf` page 5: the E1, E2, E3 tags overlap the box above, text sits against the tag and the box edge, and boxes are about 40% shorter than the Start here boxes. The exit ticket is the one thing the child does alone, so it should be the easiest to read. Fix in `b_print.py` (`.tight2` / `.tight3` shrink steps): never apply `tight2/tight3` to the exit ticket; move the Investigation to its own page (page 5 holds Investigation + Exit ticket + Look-back, which is three tasks on one page) or move the Look-back onto the kit page's empty half.
5. **B&W print: halftone fill on the tier pills makes the text illegible.** `outputs/print/bw/term/..Book1.._Workbook_PRINT.pdf` page 3: "Start here", "Think harder", "Kit" sit on a dotted grey fill and the black text blurs into it (zoom at 150 dpi confirms). The tier is the child's main navigation cue. Fix in the B&W stylesheet / print script (`b_print.py` or the bw conversion step): in B&W render pills as white fill, 2 px black outline, black text (or black fill, white text); no hatch behind any text.
6. **B&W cover is a solid black block.** Same book, page 1. It uses a large amount of ink and shows the greyscale mascot at low contrast. Fix: B&W cover = white page, thick black rule and outline lettering; keep the mascot as outline art.
7. **Contents page text cut off.** Book 1 page 2 (colour and B&W): the "Kit pages (cut-outs)" paragraph is cut at the right edge ("keep them" ends mid-line). Fix in the book assembler (the script that writes `term_books_spec.json`, not the lesson builders): wrap the paragraph inside the print margin; check every book with `pdftotext` for lines that touch the page edge.
8. **Quest Log has no Mi on page 1.** `L10_Quest_Log.pdf` and `L20_Quest_Log.pdf` page 1: the quest is "Mi's Challenge Quest" and there is a "Mi says" box with six bullets, but the picture of Mi is missing (all regular workbook page 1s have one). Fix in `b_quest.py`: add the standard header Mi (reading pose is wrong here: use a swimming or "ready, set" pose) at the top right, and cut "Mi says" to three short bullets for the child; move the other bullets (partner, ruler, optional cave) to the grown-up note.

## P2: fix soon (confusing or inconsistent, but the lesson still runs)

1. **Instruction wording: two actions per sentence on the first page.** All workbooks page 1: "Use your number cards, your frog or a drawing. Say your answer out loud, then write it." and "After question 3 you may choose: carry on, or try Think harder." are read by a grown-up, not a child. Fix in the lesson JSON `child_idea`/intro text or `b_print.py` template: three short lines with a tick-sized icon (say it, write it, choose); use one intro sentence per line.
2. **Page numbers say different things.** Workbooks: footer "Page 1" restarts per lesson; print books add "- 3 -" at the foot as well; quest pages show "Page 1 of 4" in the pill and "Page 1" in the footer. Print book contents list page numbers that are book pages, while the footer shows lesson pages, so a parent who is told "go to page 3" gets two different pages. Fix in `b_print.py` and the book assembler: show only the book page number in print books, and "Page N of M" in lesson PDFs, in one place.
3. **Footer is about 5 pt and the Look-back box is about 6-7 pt.** `L02_Workbook` page 5, `L13_Workbook` page 5. The Look-back has 12-14 tick options in a small, wrapped block, including the line "I am still stuck (my grown-up will photograph this page for my tutor)". A 6-year-old cannot read it alone. Fix in `b_print.py` (`.lbk .r`, currently `font-size:13.5px` and smaller): at most 3 choices per row, at least 14 px text, pictures (smile faces) for "easy / ok / hard", and keep the stuck option as a separate bold line.
4. **Kit pieces are small.** `L24_Workbook.pdf` pages 6-7: 5¢ and 10¢ coins are about 7 mm across and the page is half empty; the instruction paragraph is about 7 pt. `L13` page 6: counters (R / Y letters, good) about 10 mm. Fix in `b_print.py` kit builder: scale coin and note pieces to fill the page (aim for at least 14 mm for the smallest piece), keep the cut-gap, and make the "A grown-up cuts" line 12 pt bold at the top.
5. **Page 1 and kit pages leave large empty areas while other pages squeeze.** `L02_Workbook` page 1 (bottom 15% empty), `L13` page 6, `L24` page 6 (half empty). Even out: let the build shift question boxes onto the empty space rather than using `tight` classes on crowded pages.
6. **Small pictures in questions.** `L24_Workbook.pdf` page 3 T4 (three coin sets, labels about 6 pt, Set B coins about 5 mm). Book 3 B&W page 3-5: queue figures about 4 mm tall, children alternate grey/black shirts with no other mark, so "which child" depends on tiny labels. Fix in `svg.py` / `figs2.py` / `figs3.py`: minimum picture size of 6 mm per object for a drawn count, minimum label size 12 px, and use shape or letter, not colour or shade, to tell objects apart.
7. **Colour cues in B&W.** Tens bars (blue) and ones squares (yellow) become two white outlines (Book 1 page 9 B&W), so the only difference is size. Red/yellow counters are marked R/Y (good) but ten-frame counters on pages are not. Fix in the B&W stylesheet: draw the "10" on each tens bar and an outline weight difference; keep the letter marks on every coloured counter, including worksheet pictures, not only kit pages.
8. **Mi in B&W is hard to recognise at header size.** Book 1 page 3 B&W (about 12 mm): hatched grey, it reads as a bird. Fix: provide a line-art Mi (`mascot/`) for B&W and use it at 22 mm or larger in the header; make sure it does not touch the name box (currently it overlaps the dashed boundary on page 1).
9. **Mi poses are always the same.** Every workbook page 1 uses the "reading book, pointing" pose. Fix in `b_print.py` `hdr()`: pass a pose per moment (reading for page 1, thumbs-up for Quest, thinking for Think harder, wave for Look-back); keep Mi out of the answer area (currently fine, keep).
10. **Tutor guide: first page and Safeguarding block are walls of text.** `L17_Tutor_Guide.pdf` page 1-2: the "Strand" row is a paragraph with syllabus page references and internal owner-decision text (not needed mid-lesson, and it should not sit in a document that may be shared), 7 pt grey; the green "Before the lesson" and red "Safeguarding" boxes are 12+ lines each; the Timing plan table starts at the bottom of page 2 and breaks onto page 3. Fix in `b_guide.py`: add a one-page "Run sheet" first (timings, slide names, materials, three rules, answers to exit ticket), move strand/MOE and long safeguarding to the end as an appendix, keep the timing table on one page.
11. **Tutor guide: script kind labels are tiny.** Page 5 and 9: SAY / ASK / DO labels about 6 pt, children's lines in grey about 8 pt. The time-stamped single-column layout is good, but the print size is too small to scan at arm's length. Fix in `b_guide.py`: body 10.5-11 pt, labels 8 pt bold, quoted words to say in a larger, darker type than the DO/NOTE lines.
12. **Time chips are shown to the children.** Every deck content slide has a pill such as "12-28 MIN"; Exit ticket slide says "OPTIONAL". These are tutor information. Fix in `b_deck.py`: hide the chip on the shared screen (keep it in speaker notes or in a tutor-only export).
13. **Parent notes are dense and repeat admin text.** `L10_Quest_Parent_Note.pdf` and `L13_Parent_Summary.pdf`: 8-9 pt body, the same long "if your child is stuck, send a photo" / recording / WhatsApp / privacy paragraphs in every summary, and the "Next lesson" box has two paragraphs of admin. Tone is warm and clear ("nothing is added up", "you may stop at any time") which should stay; length is the problem. Fix in `b_print.py` (`PARENT_CSS` in `hp/parent_style.css`) and the parent text in the lesson JSON: body at least 10 pt, put "Try at home (5 minutes)" first, move the repeated admin to one page in Book front matter and a single line in each summary.
14. **Home practice sheet.** `L13_Home_Practice.pdf`: the introduction is four sentences, the tier labels ("Start here" per question) add clutter on a sheet with only five questions, No.5 uses an underscore blank. Home Answers page uses about 7 pt text with 60% empty page. Fix in `b_print.py`: one-sentence intro, tier labels only as a small dot, boxes for numbers, larger text on the answers page.

## P3: polish

1. Header pill wraps to two lines when the topic is long (`L13_Workbook` page 5 "ADDITION: DOUBLES AND NEAR DOUBLES"); shorten the header topic or cut the pill text ("Choose, then look back" to "Finish").
2. Quest Log page 3 (`L10`) is half empty and page 2 of `L20` bottom 25% is empty; move the Gate 7 ruler bar to the same page as Gate 7 (it is on page 4 now) so the grown-up does not have to flip.
3. Number-line blanks in `L02_Workbook` page 1 are good but the answer-sized box could be a little larger than the digits it sits among; align its size to the digits (about 1.2x).
4. Rounded dashed "Name / Date" strip repeats on every page; keep but shorten the Date line to half length so Name has room (a 6-year-old writes large).
5. Deck: the speech bubbles of Mi on the quest slide sit close to the last bullet (L20 slide 5); move Mi to the lower right corner with at least 40 px margin.
6. Print books carry 12-14 blank pages per workbook book by design (back of kit pages); add a small "This page is left blank on purpose" line so parents do not think it is an error.
7. Term 3 (L21-L30) has no print books yet in `outputs/print/term/`; apply the fixes above in the builder first, then produce them.

## What already works well (keep)

- Workbook structure: tier pills (Start here, Think harder, Go further, Choose then look back, Kit) in a consistent top-right position with a green lesson chip and topic line; numbered tags No.S1, T1, G1, E1 give an unmistakable way to say "do question T2".
- Number answers in rounded boxes, one per digit, in Terms 1 and 2; big number line and counters pictures on L02 page 1 and L13 pages 1-2 (a good size for the child).
- One idea per box, with the question in bold and the sub-parts (a), (b) in teal; plenty of space inside the boxes on page 1 of each lesson.
- Kit pages: dashed cut lines, underlined 6 and 9 on number cards (L02), R/Y letters on counters so colour is not the only cue (L13), bold "grown-up cuts" wording, a blank back so cutting does not spoil other pages.
- B&W print versions keep question layout, answer boxes and kit cut lines identical; R/Y letters and underlined digits survive without colour.
- Tutor guide script page: time-stamped, one line per action, kind label on the left, yellow "recording" rows, pink "if stuck" rows, a "model answer" note after each task; a very good basis for live use.
- Slides: dark title slide with "today we will" box, consistent top colour bar, big counter and ten-frame pictures, short "Puzzle 1 anyone can try / Puzzle 2 think" structure, closing slide that gives the child something to tell a grown-up.
- Parent summary: "What we did / Words we used / Good questions / When your child is stuck" is the right set of headings and the tone is calm and non-competitive.
- Mi appears at the page 1 header only, to the right of the name box, never over an answer area.
