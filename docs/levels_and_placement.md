# Levels (Level 1 to Level 6), age bands, and placement by diagnostic
Levels are named Level 1 to Level 6. Each level has a three-year age band that overlaps the next (6-8, 7-9, 8-10, 9-11, 10-12, 11-13), so children who are ahead or behind their school year can join the level that suits how they learn now. Typical school year in Singapore:
| Program level | Age band (3 years) | Typical school year |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Ages 6-8 | Primary 1 |
| Level 2 | Ages 7-9 | Primary 2 |
| Level 3 | Ages 8-10 | Primary 3 |
| Level 4 | Ages 9-11 | Primary 4 |
| Level 5 | Ages 10-12 | Primary 5 |
| Level 6 | Ages 11-13 (Maths + coding bridge) | Primary 6, after PSLE |
Pages show the level name first, then the age band and the school-year hint in brackets, e.g. "Level 3 (ages 8-10, covers P3 topics)". The "covers P3 topics" hint means the school year, not the level name.
## Placement
1. Parent takes the free 30-minute diagnostic for the level matching their child's age.
2. Result: 70% or more = enrol at that level. 40-69% = enrol at that level; tutor note that support may help (or choose one level down). Under 40% = recommend one level down.
3. A child may also try the diagnostic one level up if it was too easy (90%+).
4. Parent has the final say; the level can be changed before lesson 1 at no cost. Same price at every level.
5. Level 1 (ages 6-8) is the lowest level; weaker learners there start with the free taster and a tutor chat.
