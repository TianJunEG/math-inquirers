## Claims I could not confirm
- Kids Britannica says Hopper 'coined the term bug'. Wikipedia and the Smithsonian say the word predates 1947 (Edison, 1870s) and that the log page was probably not hers. The story avoids this claim.
- Hannah Cairo: her birth year is not given in the sources opened (17 when the paper was posted in February 2025, 18 by the Davidson profile), so no year is stated. Quanta does not say where she was born; Davidson says born and raised in the Bahamas. Hardships (isolation, graduate-school rejections) are left out. A video summary claim about Stein's conjecture is not used.
- Solomon's degree year (1959 vs 'early 1960s') differs between sources, so no year is given. Her role in the turtle itself is not stated by sources; the story credits her with Logo only. The link to Muzzey Jr High (spelled Muzzy in one source) is taught-there per EdSurge and early use per Wikipedia Logo.
- Singh gives 'around 850' for Al-Kindi's manuscript; the Istanbul discovery year was not in the sources, so it is not stated. Claims that it was 'the earliest use of statistical inference' are not used.
- Quetelet's chest measurements of Scottish soldiers were not found in the MacTutor or Wikipedia pages opened, so left out. Quetelet index (BMI) not used.
