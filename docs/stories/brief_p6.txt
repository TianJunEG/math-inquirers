MATHEMATICIAN STORIES for Math Inquirers Level P6 (ages 11-13). Repo /home/claude/math-inquirers. Research task with WebSearch/WebFetch. Do NOT edit lesson, workbook, deck or guide files and do not commit; write only docs/stories/p6.json and docs/stories/p6.md.

Goal: one short "Meet a Mathematician" story for each of the 10 lessons, linked to that lesson's topic, to be shown on a slide, a workbook corner box and in the tutor script. First read this level's 10 lesson titles and topics (p6/content/l1..l10.js; for P1 hp/lessons.js) so each story really connects to what the children do that day.

Rules (important):
 * TRUE ONLY. Every fact must be supported by at least two reliable sources (encyclopaedias, university or museum pages, MacTutor biographies, Britannica, NASA, etc.). Open each source, do not rely on memory. List the source URLs.
 * Famous legends must be labelled, e.g. "A story often told about young Gauss is ... (historians are not sure it happened exactly like this)". Do not invent dialogue, quotes, childhood scenes or feelings. Short real quotes only if verified word for word and attributed with a source.
 * Choose a varied set across the level: women and men, different countries and centuries (e.g. Al-Khwarizmi, Aryabhata, Brahmagupta, Liu Hui, Zu Chongzhi, Ramanujan, Hypatia, Emmy Noether, Sophie Germain, Katherine Johnson, Maryam Mirzakhani, Euclid, Archimedes, Eratosthenes, Fibonacci, Euler, Gauss, Ada Lovelace, Mary Somerville, Terence Tao, Sofia Kovalevskaya, Pascal, Fermat, Descartes, Hardy). Prefer people with a clear, accurate link to the topic. At most 2 from the same country per level. Include at least 3 women per level where honest links exist. Avoid anyone whose story is mainly dark or unsuitable for children; avoid contested priority claims unless stated carefully.
 * Child-friendly and encouraging: show effort, curiosity, mistakes, asking why, or collaborating, not just genius. Reading level suitable for ages 11-13 (P1-P3: about 60-90 words, short sentences; P4-P6: about 100-150 words). No frightening or heavy details (war, persecution, death) beyond one calm factual line if essential.
 * Each entry must have: lesson (1-10), name, years, country_or_region, one-line "who" tag, story (the text), link_to_lesson (one sentence: how it connects to today's topic), try_it (one question or mini-task the child can do right then, correct and checkable, with its answer), source_urls (list), confidence (high/medium) and legend_note (empty or the label used). Also suggest a simple icon idea (not a likeness of the real person) for the card.
 * Spelling: British/Singapore English, "maths", "program" for the brand name. No unicode shape glyphs.
 * Do not use copyrighted passages; write in your own words.
Also write docs/stories/p6.md: a readable table of the ten stories plus a "claims I could not confirm" list and any choice you rejected and why. Final report: 8 lines.
