The school may print and photocopy pages for its own pupils and staff, keep the file on its network for its teachers, and hand out single pages. **My questions:**
1. **Annual licence term.** Is "one school year, renewable" clear enough? What happens at the end of the year (must copies be destroyed)? Should the start date be stated?
2. **Photocopy rights within one school.** Is the wording on who may copy (pupils and staff of one named school, one campus) and what they may do enough and enforceable? Does a school's "one campus" need defining?
3. **"Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Ministry of Education, Singapore."** Where should this sentence go? Today it is on the P2 cover and on the P1 licence page (we have not decided one place for both). Is the wording right, and is one placement enough?
4. **Publisher line.** "Preciouslyours Pte Ltd, trading as Math Inquirers": is this the right form? Is the copyright line "© 2026 Preciouslyours Pte Ltd" right?
5. **Registered office.** Our files show 66 Tannery Ln, Singapore 347805. Please check this is the correct address to print on the licence page, or whether the licence page needs one at all.
6. **MOE syllabus reference.** The booklets say "The mathematics syllabus is published by the Ministry of Education, Singapore", and the teacher answer keys name syllabus outcomes such as "Whole Numbers 1.2". Is it fine to refer to the MOE syllabus this way, or should the wording change?
7. **DRAFT stamps.** Each booklet carries DRAFT stamps and a temporary Gmail contact. Please tell me what must be true before I remove them (for example, your written approval).
8. **Terms and privacy.** `website/terms.html` (section 1 "not affiliated with MOE", and section 12 on personal use of workbooks) and `website/privacy.html` cover our home classes, not schools. Do they touch the school booklets, or will schools need separate terms? Does the privacy notice need a school-licence line if we only keep a school's published office email?
9. **MOE registration.** Does the Education Act (section 21) requirement to register apply to our online enrichment maths classes (groups of up to 8, more than 10 children across groups)? If you cannot advise, whom should I ask?
10. **Real coin and note images.** May the school booklets show real Singapore coin and note images (official MAS images) under the MAS educational-publication exemption (Gazette Notification No. 2078 of 2006)? We would meet the conditions MAS lists: notes shown at no more than 60% of real size with the word SPECIMEN and one-sided only, no distortion, on paper. A published Singapore school practice book appears to do this. Does a sold, photocopy-licensed workbook count as an educational publication, and does the exemption also cover copyright in MAS's image files? Until you say yes, the booklets keep our own original drawings.

12. **Recording of children in live lessons (Google Meet).** We teach groups of up to 8 children (about 6 to 13) on Google Meet and record each lesson so that an absent child can catch up. The recording follows the active speaker and the shared screen, so a child's voice is recorded and a child's face can appear if their camera is on. The tutor reviews and trims it, then it is shown only to the logged-in parents of enrolled children in that group through our parent portal, and deleted 30 days after the group's last lesson. Files are stored with Google (Drive) and Cloudflare (reviewed copy), both outside Singapore. Please read `docs/recordings_design.md` and the draft wording in its section 6.4, and tell me:
   a. **Recording children.** Is the parent's recording consent (registration item 3, redrafted) enough for children's voices and possible faces, or do I need more (for example a separate consent for each child's image, or the child's own notice)? What must I do for a family who withdraws consent during the program?
   b. **Retention.** Is "30 days after the group's last lesson" reasonable for catch-up recordings, and for the review checklist and the log of which parent watched which recording? Is a shorter period better?
   c. **Storage location and overseas transfer.** Google and Cloudflare store the files outside Singapore. What must the privacy notice and my contracts with them say under the PDPA transfer rules? Is Google (Meet/Drive) or Cloudflare a "data intermediary" for us, and what about the tutor who reviews recordings?
   d. **Other children and parents.** Enrolled families can see and hear the other children in their group on the recording. Is it enough that each parent consented and that families agree not to share or record it? Should I stay with voice only and cameras off? What do I say if a parent objects to another family's child being on the recording?
   e. **Our promise.** The terms promise recordings (for catch-up, and after withdrawal "recordings of lessons already held"). What wording on availability, on a recording we cannot publish (private content found), and on the end date is safe?

11. **Online practice answers sent to us (children under 13).** We plan online versions of our workbooks (extension books, holiday and term lessons). When a child presses "Submit", the answers (first name or nickname, level, each answer, skipped questions, and the mark our server works out again) are sent to Math Inquirers and stored in Supabase (database) via a Cloudflare page, so a tutor or I can generate reports, and the parent gets a summary. Two kinds of families: (a) families in our live groups; (b) families who only buy an extension book online and are not in any group (they would not give the live-lesson recording or children's work agreements). Please advise on, and correct, the drafts in `docs/legal/online_submission_consent_draft.md`:
    - **Consent and notice.** Is a separate parent consent line for online practice, plus the privacy page additions, enough for children under 13 under the PDPA (consent from the parent or guardian, purpose limitation, notification)? Is it right that families who only buy a book are NOT asked for the recording and children's work agreements?
    - **Retention.** Is "12 months after the last submission, or 90 days after the child's online access ends, whichever is sooner" reasonable, and is the existing privacy page promise (work and answers kept 90 days after the program ends) consistent with it? What should happen to a book order record when a parent deletes the account?
    - **Overseas transfer.** Supabase may store data outside Singapore (we aim for a Singapore region), and the Cloudflare page that receives answers runs at edge locations worldwide. Stripe handles payments. What wording and contract checks do we need for the transfer limitation obligation, and is naming the providers enough?
    - **Rights and contact.** Parent access, correction, deletion and withdrawal (including a portal button that deletes one child's practice answers). The data protection officer contact is currently a stand-in Gmail address. What must the notice say about breach notification for this data?
    - **Marketing and the trial class funnel.** The extension book (printed page and QR link, a note on the page after Submit, and a "next step" line in the parent report) invites parents, never children, to ask for a free trial class. (i) May we show a parent-facing suggestion inside the parent's own report that is based on the child's practice results (for example "a small class could help with place value") without separate marketing consent, given that it is shown only to the parent in the product? (ii) We would send email or WhatsApp messages about trial classes or other books only if the parent ticked a separate optional line (never pre-ticked, with stop at any time), or asked for a trial class. Is that enough, and do the Do Not Call Registry rules apply to a message sent to a Singapore mobile number in that case? (iii) Practice answers would not be used for advertising or compared between children. Is a plain statement of that enough?
    - **Purchase terms.** One purchase of online access is for one named child; refund revokes access. Is a short extension book terms page needed beside the live program terms, and what must it say about refunds and the printed book?
    - **Children's data on the page.** We store no timers, photos, school or NRIC, and we do not store free text typed in open boxes. Is anything else needed for a children's online service (for example age-appropriate wording)?

13. **Free preview class: open to the public, unknown children, attendance records and a 6-month exclusion.** We plan a free 60-minute online preview class on Google Meet, open to any family (children about 6 to 13, a parent must be present), to show how we teach and to invite parents to a trial class or enrolment. It is held weekly with the same content, so we want each child to attend only once in any 6 months. Please read `docs/preview_class_design.md` (sections 1, 2, 4 and 5) and the draft SQL in `portal/supabase/preview_class.sql`, and tell me:
    a. **Attendance records and the 6-month exclusion.** To enforce the rule we keep, for 7 months after a child attends, only keyed hashes (HMAC-SHA256, secret key kept in our database) of the parent's normalised email, phone number, the child's first name and birth year; contact details in clear are deleted 30 days after the class (6 months if the parent ticked the optional marketing box). Is a hash of this kind still personal data, and is this consistent with data minimisation and purpose limitation? Is a **consent** (a required tick: "keep a coded record so that this free class is offered once per child per 6 months") valid when it is a condition of a free class, or should we rely on another basis? Is 7 months reasonable?
    b. **Profiling or automated decision.** The form refuses a registration automatically when the hashes match ("not available"), and sends close matches (for example the same first name with a birth year one year apart) to a person. Does this count as profiling or an automated decision under the PDPA or any guidance, what must we tell parents, and must a person always be able to review a refusal? We also say nothing about which detail matched (so a stranger cannot test whether a family attended). Is that acceptable, and what should the refusal message say?
    c. **Children and unknown families.** We collect a child's first name and birth year from an unknown parent without an account, with consent only by the parent's tick. Is that enough for under 13s, and is "parent must be present and joins with the child" an adequate safeguard? We do not record the class, children's cameras and microphones are off, children answer by typing, the room is a waiting room that the host admits by name, and a second adult attends. Is there anything more you would require (for example a child safeguarding policy, a named designated person, or a parent declaration in the registration)?
    d. **Marketing follow-up.** After the class we want to email or WhatsApp the parent a thank-you with the free practice page, and, only if the parent ticked a separate optional box, a few follow-up messages about trial classes. (i) Is a thank-you with the practice link, sent because the parent registered, a marketing message? (ii) For Singapore mobile numbers, do the Do Not Call Registry rules apply to a WhatsApp message to someone who registered and ticked the box, and must we also check the Registry? (iii) What must every message say (our name, how to stop)? (iv) For how long may we keep the contact of a parent who ticked but never replied?
    e. **Retention and deletion.** Is "contact details 30 days after the class, the coded record 7 months, a parent's request to delete removes both (and so lets them attend again)" acceptable? What is the rule if a parent asks us to delete their record **before** the 6 months have passed, given that we promised the class once per child?
    f. **Terms and notices.** The preview needs a short page of its own (not the paid-class terms): the rule of one attendance per child per 6 months, no recording, parent present, conduct, our right to refuse or remove, and no promise about the paid classes. Please say what it must contain, and whether the free class changes the privacy notice (new purpose, new retention, Google Meet as provider outside Singapore).
