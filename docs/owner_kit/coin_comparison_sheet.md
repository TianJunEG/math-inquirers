## For each page, check
- [ ] Sizes: a bigger coin or note in real life is also drawn bigger (50c bigger than 10c; $10 note longer than $2).
- [ ] Numerals: the number on each coin or note matches its value.
- [ ] Words: "cents", "c", "$" and "dollar" are written correctly.
- [ ] Nothing looks like a copy of the real design: no portraits, no crests, no exact patterns, no signatures.
- [ ] A child could tell the coins apart from the picture alone.
- [ ] Anything that looks wrong or confusing next to real money (write the page and what).
