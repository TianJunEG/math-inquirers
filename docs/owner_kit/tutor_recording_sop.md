# Tutor SOP: recording a live lesson (one page) - DRAFT 24 Oct 2026

Applies to every lesson on Google Meet. Full design: `docs/recordings_design.md`. Settings marked (check) are confirmed in the owner's dry run before the first lesson.

**Rules that never change**
- Record only with the company Meet (the owner's Workspace account). Never make your own recording, screenshot, audio or photo.
- Use a **computer** (iPhone/iPad cannot record).
- Recording has you, the shared screen and anyone who speaks. Children's cameras stay **off**.

**Before (10 minutes early)**
1. Join the group's Meet link; ask the owner/host to make you **co-host**.
2. Close other windows and chat apps. Slide deck on, workbook pages ready.
3. Host controls: children's cameras off if the control exists (check); chat off or "no personal details in chat"; captions OFF until the owner says otherwise.
4. Check the screen: no child's name, no parent email, no notifications.

**Start**
5. Meeting tools > Recording > **Start recording**. Wait for the red recording sign. Google tells everyone.
6. Say once, in your own words: "This lesson is being recorded so anyone who misses it can catch up. The recording has my voice, the screen and the voice of anyone who speaks. Please keep your cameras off. If you would rather not be heard, stay on mute; I will repeat answers in my own words."
7. If a camera is on, politely ask the family to switch it off. Ask parents not to appear in the background.

**During**
8. Use the REC lines and timers from the guide. Repeat each child's answer in your words. Say a first name only to call on a child, never with "right" or "wrong". No one holds work to the camera. Do not ask about school, address, phone.
9. If someone says something private (address, family trouble), carry on, then write the time (mm:ss) on your notes for the cut list.

**Stop**
10. Before the goodbye chat: Meeting tools > Recording > **Stop recording** > confirm. (It also stops when everyone leaves.)
11. If you forgot to start, or it stopped early: tell the owner at once; do not re-record without them.

**After (by 12:00 the next day)**
12. The owner moves the file into the group's `01_raw` Drive folder and shares it with you **view-only**. If no file after 2 hours: tell the owner; do not start another recording.
13. Watch (1.5x to 2x is fine, but listen to all of it and scrub the picture). Tick all three:
   - No private information (address, phone, school, birth date, email, passwords, parents or siblings talking).
   - No child's face or full name; no name with a right/wrong answer.
   - On topic (no side chat, complaints, anything embarrassing).
14. Write the cut list: `mm:ss-mm:ss` and a reason without any name. Send it to the owner. If something cannot be cut, say so: the recording is not published.
15. Delete anything you downloaded. Tell the owner if a family asks you about a recording, or if someone else recorded the lesson.

**Not allowed:** private messages to a child; sharing a link; recording alone with one child unless a parent is on screen throughout (tutor agreement 3.2).
