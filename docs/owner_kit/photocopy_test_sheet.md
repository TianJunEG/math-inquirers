# Photocopier test: instructions (DRAFT for the owner)

Nothing here has been done yet. This is the real-world check number 4 in `docs/owner_checks_outstanding.md`. It takes about 30 minutes.

## What to print
1. Print `docs/owner_kit/photocopier_test_pages.pdf` (10 pages, A4, black and white, actual size). These are the real pages taken from `school/p1/out` and `school/p2/out`; nothing was changed.
2. Print `docs/owner_kit/photocopy_test_sheet.pdf` (one page). Write your results in its boxes.

## What to do
Photocopy each of the 10 pages at NORMAL toner (not light), at a school office or any copier like the ones schools use. Put the copy next to the original and look at the copy itself.

| # | Page | Look for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | P2 S1-18 Parts of a Whole (fractions) | Shaded parts stay separate; they do not merge into one block |
| 2 | P2 S2-01 Dollars and Cents | The words "cents" and "dollar" and the numbers on coins and notes can be read |
| 3 | P2 S2-04 Purse Match | Coins still separate; letters in the boxes can be read |
| 4 | P2 S1-17 Colour the House (colour by code) | The numbers in each part can be read, also under pale crayon; part edges visible |
| 5 | P2 Answer Key page (S1-17, S1-18, S1-19) | Grey areas not black blobs; small text readable |
| 6 | P1 S2-10 Coin Purses | Same as 2 and 3 |
| 7 | P1 S2-11 Fewest Coins | Same as 6 |
| 8 | P1 S1-04 Colour the House | Same as 4 |
| 9 | P1 S1-17 Finish the Figure (square grid) | Fine grid lines stay visible |
| 10 | P1 Answer Key page (S1-04, S1-05, S1-06) | Same as 5 |

P1 has no fractions page, so the P1 pages chosen are the nearest equivalents: coins (6, 7), colour by code (8), the fine square grid (9) and a key page (10).

## Afterwards
Write one sentence of what you did and found, for example "photocopied S2-01 at normal toner, cents readable", or "not done yet". A problem on any page: note the page number and what went wrong.
