# Coins and notes vs real MAS images (assistant check, 23 Oct 2026; owner allowed an online check)

Sources: MAS coins page and notes page (mas.gov.sg, circulation currency). Images compared: 5c, 10c, 50c, $1 reverse/obverse and the $2 note front. Side-by-side: outputs/owner_review/coins_ours_vs_real_MAS.png

- Sizes: real $1 (24.65 mm) is about 1.47 times the 5c (16.75 mm); ours 19.8 mm vs 13.4 mm = 1.48. Real 50c 23.00 vs $1 24.65 = 0.93; ours 18.4/19.8 = 0.93. Order and ratios match.
- Real notes are 2:1 (126x63, 133x66, 141x69); ours 27.1x13.5, 28.6x14.2, 30.3x14.8 mm = 2:1, growing with value.
- Not a copy: real coin reverses show the Esplanade (5c), public housing (10c), Changi Airport (20c), the Port of Singapore (50c), the Merlion (1 dollar) and the Coat of Arms; ours use invented symbols (leaf, star, flower, sailboat, sun). Real notes show a portrait, large numeral, cowrie shells and the lion symbol; ours show a plain outline and corner numerals.
- Differences a child could notice: real coins are gold-coloured (5c, 10c, outer ring of the $1) or silver (20c, 50c) and the $1 is not round; real $2, $5, $10 notes are purple, green and red. A black-and-white worksheet cannot show colour. Suggested optional teacher-note line (NOTE-COLOURS-ADD): "Real $2 notes are purple, $5 green, $10 red."
- Not compared: the 20c coin image (not downloaded), the $5 and $10 notes (only the $2 front was viewed).
