Math Inquirers Holiday Program

K2 Readiness Check

A 15-minute check for K2 children who want to join before starting Primary 1
Notice it. Test it. Explain why.

Why we check

Math Inquirers uses topics chosen to complement the Primary 1 syllabus and goes beyond them. K2 children have not met P1 maths yet, so some will fly and some will feel lost. The check tells us who will enjoy the group, who needs a little support first, and who will get more from a later intake. It is short and friendly: children should feel they played some number games.

How to run it

  • Time: about 15 minutes, one child at a time. On video, with the parent setting out the counters beside the child.
  • You need: about 25 counters (buttons or coins are fine), number cards 9, 14, 17 and 20, the child sheet (last page) and a pencil.
  • Parent: may sit in, but asks them not to help or prompt. Online, the parent only moves objects when asked.
  • Tone: say "Let's play some number games." Praise effort, not right answers. Never say "wrong"; say "Let's try another one."
  • Stop early if the child is upset or has scored 0 on four items in a row. Note it and move to the decision.

Scoring

2 = correct, without help.  1 = correct with a hint, or slow (counting from 1, using fingers for everything).  0 = not yet, or no answer. Total out of 24.

Decision

18–24
Ready. Joins any group like a Primary 1 child. Tell the tutor in the class list that the child is in K2 and keep an eye on Lessons 5 and 6 (working backwards and money), which are the biggest jump.
12–17
Ready with support. Joins, with three conditions: we send the K2 Starter Pack two weeks before the program (numbers to 20, writing numbers, number bonds to 10, coins); the child sits with a buddy; and the tutor sets Core questions first, with Think Harder only when Core is done.
0–11
Not yet. Recommend a later intake (the March or June holidays) and give the free home activity sheet. Say it positively: "Your child will get much more out of it after a term of Primary 1."

Override rules

  • Score 0 on item 3 (writing numbers) → Ready with support at best, because the workbook needs writing.
  • "Stays with the task" ticked "not yet" → recommend the online group with a parent nearby, or a later intake.
  • Child scores 22+ and explains reasons well → note "strong", so the tutor offers Challenge questions early.

Telling the parent

Within a day, send a short message with the result band, one thing the child did well, and the next step. Sample for "Ready with support":

"Thank you for bringing Ethan for the number games today. He counted and compared numbers confidently and loved the pattern game. Primary 1 maths will be new to him, so we will send a short Starter Pack to try at home over the next two weeks, and he will start with the core questions in class. We think he will enjoy the program. His place in Group A is confirmed."

If several K2 children sign up

If four or more K2 children register for the same period, consider a separate K2 group. Teach the same ten lessons with smaller numbers (to 20 instead of 100), more objects and fewer written questions. This can grow into a "Little Inquirers" K2 program later.
